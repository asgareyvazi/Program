    3. Drilling & Workover History

Drilling History
  RKB‐MSL: 33m

  The drilling and testing operations were completed in 64.6 days.

  BL-1P was drilled on MudLine Suspension system (MLS), with the seabed located at

  100m from rotary table. The final well architecture was the following:


   • 36” diameter hole with 30” conductor pipe to 168m

   • 26” diameter hole with 20” surface casing to 308m

   • 17-1/2” diameter hole with 13”3/8 intermediate casing to 1076m

   • 12-1/4” diameter hole with 9”5/8 production casing to 2021m


  The trajectory kicked off below the 20” shoe, and reached a maximum inclination of

  26 deg (J shape) @ approximate depth of 340m.

   All drilling operations were completed as planned. However, important mud losses

   occurred in the 17”1/2 section in the Umm Er Rhaduma, but the section was drilled

   without major problem. In addition, below the Yamama, very heterogeneous and

   quite hard formations were encountered, which resulted in erratic drilling parameters

  and very slow ROP.

   All casing operations were completed as planned, except for the 9”5/8 casing where

   the DV collar was not perfectly sealed. 4.3 days trying to restore the pressure integrity

   of the casing was not successfull. During BL-1P future tie-back operations, the

   integrity of the DV collar will be tested again, and the appropriate remedial action

   taken inplace prior to running the production completion.

  The following geological and reservoir data were acquired from the Arab reservoir:

   • 108 meters of 5-1/4” core,

   • Comprehensive open hole electrical logging,

   • Six (6) MDT PVT samples,

   • Two (2) DST with matrix acidification

   Reservoir coring in the Arab was completed successfully with 99.5% of core

   recovery.

  However, a tentative coring of the Nahr Umr shales was a failure.

All reservoir electrical logging and MDT operations were completed successfully.

Both DST #1 and DST #2 were completed successfully and confirmed the good

reservoir properties and the presence of H2S in the Arab effluent (0.6% to 1.4% in the

reservoir fluid in downhole conditions). However, it was not possible to perform any

PLT logging on DST #1 because of a valve failure in the DST string, and after this

first test, 4.4 days were spent to kill the well as important losses resulted from the

matrix acidification.

At the end of the operations, BL-1P was temporarily abandoned for later tie-back to

Balal WHP jacket.


SUMMARY OF BL‐1P TIE‐BACK and RE‐ENTRY OPERATIONS (2 nd phase in 2002)

BL-1P tie-back, re-entry and completion have been performed with the jack-up

Trident 19 from 27th November to 13th December 2002. The objective was to

recover reservoir characteristics in the 9 5/8” casing and to run a 4 ½” completion.

The tie-back, re-entry and completion operations were completed in 15.5 days.

Tie‐back operation summary

The tie-back was completed in 3 days. The main problem faced on this specific

operation was due to a funnel conception problem at the drain deck (diameter to small

for DMR pin connector size).

The cover on top of 30” casing was previously removed during WHP installation over

BL-1P well during summer 2002.

The casings tied-back were as follows :

• 30” conductor pipes (RL4 – X52 – 1” WT)

• 20” casings (RL4S – X52 – 0.625” WT)

• 13 3/8” casings (BTC – N80 – 72 lbs/ft)

• 9 5/8” casings (NK3SB – L80 – 47 lbs/ft)

The tie-back of all the casings was completed as planned. A camera from UWG in

tandem with a brush was run with a 5” DP string prior to each casing tie-back in order

to check the connection status and to clean it.

During these tie-back operations, BL-1P was equipped with a FMC wellhead and X-

mas tree

As there was an uncertainty on the position of 9 5/8” casing slip dies (on top of 1 St

magnet to recover the fourth pieces.

Prior to reaching the EZSV bridge plug, 3 cement plugs were set in 9 5/8” casing. The

first one from 200 m to 304 m MD/RT was found hard with a ROP of 3m/h.

The second cement plug set from 950 m to 1054 m MD/RT theoretically, covered the

9 5/8” DV at 1051 m witch was found leaking on BL12P operations. The top of

cement was observed at 1004 m, soft to 1048 m and hard to 1130 m.

The third cement plug planned from 1733 m to 1837 m was found at 1707 m and

drilled to 1835 m top of Bridge plug.

During the drilling of the EZSV bridge plug #2  , a trip to change the bit was

necessary. A 8 ½ super junk mill was run to mill the EZSV and to  drill the

cementbelow the EZSV.

At 1875m no progress during the drilling. The super junk mill was replaced by a 8 ½

bit ATJ-G8. The

second EZSV was drilled out easily. Drilled out cement to 1902 m where some losses

7 m3/h appeared.

A pill of ASC ( acid soluble cement ) was pumped and squeezed with success to stop

losses. The cement was drilled without losses to the float collar at 1985.5 m.

The casing was scrapped and several pills of Safe Surf and HI vis were pumped in the

well and displaced by a brine (d 1.18) SG.


Well equipped with a 4 ½” completion, a stimulation with balls was performed to

restore the productivity through the initial intervals of perforations.

8.  Well Securing & Workover Program

    1. Move and approach rig alongside BALAL platform in BALAL field.

    2.  Jack up and skid mast over well No BL-1P. Record & report, Air gap,

     RKB/THF and RKB/MSL.

    3.  Record wellhead and Tubing Ann. Pressures.

    4. Hook up lines on Ann. & TBG. P/T same.

    5.  Release pressure from CL to close 3SV, check wellhead pressure and make

       sure 3SV is functioning.

    6.  Retreive 3SV, Open SSD, Kill well by 72pcf brine & confirm well is static or

       under loss condition.

      Note:
            Keep Circulation cross wellhead during next operations.
            72pcf mud impose 200 psi over pressure to the ARAB Reservoir @

             top of perforation zone (1846m MD, 1725m TVD).

    7.  Install & close SSSV, install BPV in tubing hanger.

    8. N/D X-Mass tree, check hanger, N/U BOP and test same. (THS top flamp: 13-

       5/8” 5K)
