1. Introduction


     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers workover of well West Paydar-2, West Paydar oil field, Iran.
            The well is intended to produce oil from Asmari formation. The well is in closed
               position right now.

            Problem
              Left two fish in hole; the first including ±2830m 7/32" cable plus two heavy
             weight sinker bar (3m long) plus shooting CCL (20cm long) plus 2-1/8" inner jet
            gun (4m long) with 52 IRjets.
            The second including crossover, 2-1/2" spring jar (1.22m) plus 1-7/8" tube jar
             (1.82m) plus knuckle joint + wire line spear (0.6m).

             Note: Totally 200m of the 7/32" cable has been recovered dated 1385 / 10 / 04-05
            by wire line operation.


           Main Objective
           The primary objective of this program is to retrieve all fish in hole and then
              clean bore hole to resume oil production from Asmari formation.

             Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of
            Workover request no. 85/ 13- 0/322, dated: 1385/ 10/ 09 and the received
              information from IDSC IMS 1385/ 10/ 16 and Pishgaman Roshd Electronic Co.
              dated 1385/ 10/ 14.


           The main head lines of this program are as follow:

                1.    Move and R/U Workover rig.
                2.     Record wellhead and annuluses pressures.
                3.     Displace hole with proper mud.
                4.     Secure well, L/D wellhead, N/U BOP's.
                5.      Pull out completion string (Mill out and fish packer).
                6.     Retrieve all fish in hole/ clen hole to 3125m, MD.
                7.    Run urban completion string.
                8.    L/D BOP's, N/U wellhead.
                9.     Release the Rig.


             Note: The detailed program is illustrated here after in this document under
              the head of "Workover Program".

2. Workover Program

            1. Move workover  rig to the well  site of PYW-2, R/U and make  it ready  for
               operation, Record rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea level.

            2. The well head spools, valves & P-Seals and x-mas tree must be checked and
              serviced by well head specialist and be repaired if needed.

            3.  Connect  killing & flowing lines to 7-1/16"-5000 x-mas tree side outlets and
             connect return line to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to
            4500 psi pressure.

            4. Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 7" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x 13-
               3/8" annulus and 13-3/8" x 18-5/8" annulus.  If necessary, release  / bleed off
              pressures, monitor their build up and investigate for the probable relations.

             Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of
            pipes while relation investigation.

            5.  Bullhead the well by pumping 70pcf salt water which its full column
             imposes176psi over pressure to the reservoir @ top perforations (3045m). This
              operation should be done after installing dummy into 3SV. Shall could not able
                to bullhead, lubricate the well and make sure about well condition.

             Note: Monitor the pressures of annuluses all the time.

            6.  Rig up wireline equipment; test same and lubricators to max 4500psi pressure.
             Set no-go plug «AR - 4.312"». Test same and tubing to max 4500psi. Monitor
              tubing annulus pressure behaviour while testing inside tubing.

            7.  While Circulating cross well head, install « RN - 6-5/16"» two way back pressure
              valve into 11" x 9-5/8" neck x 7" N.VAM "SA2-FMC" tubing hanger and test to
             4500psi pressure.

             Note: Shall B.P.V fail against pressure test or could not be set in its
             position in tubing hanger, it is recommended to install plug into dummy of
          3SV landing nipple or to set 3SV in closed position prior to L/D well head.


            8.  While circulating cross tubing head spool, L/D well head to top of 13-5/8"-3000 x
             11" -5000 tubing head spool. N/U 13-5/8" BOP's stack; test same to 4500psi
              against two-way. Retrieve 6-5/16" RN two way back pressure valve from tubing
             hanger and 4.312" AR plug from no-go landing nipple.

             Note: Take care to provide 11"x13-5/8"-5000 adaptor tie down flange to
            connect BOP's to tubing head spool.

            9.  While circulating cross tubing head spool, pick up 7" landing joint, screw into
              tubing hanger. Open tie down screws and test to 4000psi pressure. Prepare an
           "HTS Pin x landing joint thread Box x/over to connect L/J to the tubing hanger.

10. Sting out anchor seal unit, model «KC-22» from « SAB3 » packer and break
    circulate 70pcf salt water.

11. While circulating cross tubing head spool, pull out completion string; check all
   components for any damage to be fixed or replaced by new one on time.

12. RIH with 8-3/8" E-mill to top of packer @ 300m. Mill out same and push it down
    to top of fish @ 508m (Consider the 14.75m tail of packer). Pull out of hole. L/D
     mill.

13. RIH with 6" x 3" taper tap to top of the felt down packer. Work on taper tap to
   engage with packer. Pull out packer and its tail. L/D them all.

14. RIH with 8" Impression block to top of fish. Apply enough weight to get clear
   mark of fish lay out condition. Pull out of hole, check in mark to make a proper
    decision for fishing operation.

15. Shall it applicable, dress proper size overshot to catch the fish. It is
   recommended to use over size guide/wall hook guide. RIH to top of fish, try to
   catch and recover same.

16. Made up wile line spear to the end of drill pipe string to engage with the 7/32"
    cable. RIH, work on same to recover all fish or at least the cable portion.

Note: The witness of wireline operation company (Pira Hafari) and that of
   perforating company (Pishgaman) should be on your fishing job.

 Note: Milling operation and spotting solvent may be necessary to pass
   through asphalt down to reach the remained fish.

17. Shall the fish be recovered, perform a 8-3/8" E.mill to top of 7" liner lap and a 6-
    1/8" E.mill to 3125m. Circulate mud and clean hole. Pull out of hole; lay down drill
    string pipes.

Note: It is recommended to spot Xylene against the asphalted interval for a
   while and then flush same to clean the hole.

18. Scrap 9-5/8" casing down to 300m.

19. Run urban completion string as per program (install plug and prong into No-go
    landing nipple, travel joint shear pins and installed dummy inside 3SV landing
    nipple) Perform all necessary pressure tests.

Note: Displace behind completion string by completion fluid (gasoil + 0.55
    lit/bbl inhibitor) prior to set the packer.

Note: Do not forget to flush control line in due time.

Note: The detailed completion program will be offered in due time.
20. Install 6-5/16" back pressure valve. Pressure test same to 1000psi. Tighten tie
   down screws, test same and BPV to 4500psi.
