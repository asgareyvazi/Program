1  Introduction


     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers the drilling of well# 31 in Kangan field, Iran. The well  is
             intended to produce gas from Deh Ram group. The well will be drilled vertically to a
             planed total depth of 2914m (-2100) «170m inside Nar Mbr. ». The exact TD will be
             determined based on the logging results. The open hole drain is considered to be
           590m.

             of well # 23 and 2060m northwest of well # 24.

8 Pre Spud


      P.P.Z prepared a pre-spud checklist and distributed it in contractor rigs. The checklist will

      be performed based on  the aforementioned  checklist. In addition  to  the  checklist,

      following matters should be checked:

   ¾ Check & observe the alignment of the rig. This check will be performed in four stages;

           rig levelling, derrick verticality, derrick alignment with rotary table, rotary table

         alignment with conductor pipe. To do this pick up 3 joints of 9 3/4” DC and check the

           verticality of the derrick.

   ¾  Mechanical facilities for mud treatment such as :

         Desander, Desilter, mud cleaner, proper size of shaker–screens, hoppers, mud guns &

          mixers, should be all available on rig and functioning properly.

   ¾  All mud tanks, mud transfer lines & connection should be checked by filling them

         with water & cross wellhead circulation.

   ¾ Mud engineer laboratory should be equipped with a complete set of testing equipment.

        The calibration should be done under supervision of well site drilling supervisor

          before starting the operation.

   ¾ Weight indicator, rotary speed gauge, any smart drilling instrumentation, Stroke meter,

          pressure gauges on manifold and in the drilling console, loss / gain gauge and any

          other gauges should be checked and calibrated.

   ¾  Stand pipe manifold valves & connection must be checked & pressure tested.

   ¾  All motors should be checked under load prior to start drilling.

   ¾ Check & control mud stock & cement stock every day & if there is any necessity  to

          request mud material, cement or any drilling tool, it should be prepared & requested at

         proper time.

   ¾  Air drilling package must be installed & ready for using in the case of need.

       Note: Fill up pre-spud check list and send it to drilling office.

1  Geological Overview


           Kangan anticline  is located in coastal Fars, southwest of Nar and west of west
            Assaluyeh anticlines and north of Kangan harbor in Boushehr province. This structure
            extended northwest – southeast direction and divided to 4 parts of A, B, C and D. The
           Kangan anticline is nearly asymmetric and has 55Km length and 6Km width base on
               structural map. The youngest and the oldest Formations on surface are Bakhtiyari
              (Pliocene) and Sarvak (Cenomanian) respectively.
           The highest dip angles of 30 and 40 degree were defined for north and south flanks of
           Kangan anticline respectively and two reverse faults cut this flanks parallel to structure
              elongation.
              Exist of sweet gas in lower part of Dashtak Fm (unit-k), Kangan Fm. and U. Dalan in
                this structure is confirmed. The horizontal and vertical closures are 325 Km2 and 825m
              respectively, base on under ground contour map over Kangan Formation. The gas water
              contact in the Kangan large anticline is variable due to petrophysical changes and
             reported as -2060m, -2100m and -2070m by ICOFC, based on gas water contact in
            Formation of western, central and eastern parts of this anticline respectively.
           The Kangan –31 is located on the south flank and eastern part about 2160m southeast
              of well # 23 and 2060m northwest of well # 24 and will be drilled to total depth of 2914
            (-2100)m in Nar member.


2  Stratigraphy of Kangan structure

           The stratigraphic succession in Kangan - 31 will be normal from surface (Gurpi Fm.)
          down to T.D. (2914m) in Nar member as follows:

     Gurpi Formation (Surface to 50m)

           The Gurpi Formation consists of glauconitic and fossiliferous, gray to dark gray
              limestone. The boundary of Gurpi and Ilam Formations is disconformable.
            Age: Campanian to maestrichtian


      Ilam Formation (50 to 138m)

           The Ilam Formation  is composed  of  microcrystalline sandy  argillaceous cream
             limestone and porous organic and fossiliferous clastic limestone. Ilam Formation is
            conformable with Laffan shale member.
            Age: Santonian to Campanian

Laffan Shale Member (138 to 178m)

     The Laffan Member is composed of interbeds of argillaceous and pyritic gray limestone
     and olive to olive brown & red brown shale. Laffan member is conformable with
      Sarvak Formation.
     Age: Coniacian to Santonian


Sarvak Formation (178 to 314m)

     The Sarvak Formation mainly consists of pyritic crystalline porous organic clastic buff
      limestone. This Formation consists of two members, Ahmadi and Maudoud.
     Ahmadi member contains of greenish gray shale and buff to light gray, dark gray
      limestone.
     Maudoud member contains of porous fossiliferous, cream to light gray limestone.
     The boundary of Sarvak and Kazhdumi Fm. is conformable.
     Age: Cenomanian to Probable Albian


Kazhdumi Formation (314 to 367m)

     The Kazhdumi Formation is composed of grey to grayish & reddish brown shale.
     Age: Albian to Cenomanian


Dariyan Formation (367 to 469m)

      This Formation mainly consists of porous light cream limestone and in middle part with
      argillaceous limestone. The boundary between Dariyan and Gadvan Fm is conformable.
     Age: Aptian

Gadvan Formation (469 to 532m)

     The Gadvan Formation is mainly composed of pyritic greenish blue, blue shale beds
     and fossiliferous gray limestone. The boundary of Gadvan and Fahliyan Formations is
      conformable. Age: Barremian to Aptian


Fahliyan Formation (532 to 687m)

      This Formation is consisted of porous cream to buff limestone and high argillaceous
      gray limestone. The boundary of Fahliyan and Hith Formation is conformable.
     Age: Neocomian


Hith Formation (687 to 754m)

      This Formation is mainly composed of anhydrite beds, white, hard and glassy with
      interbeds of crystalline and pale brown dolomite. The boundary of Hith and Surmeh Fm
        is conformable.
     Age: Upper Jurassic

                     Sequence of Drilling Operation



1.3 Sequence of Operation

     •  Make up 26” BHA as per BHA design, drill formation down to 36m (30” casing point
          in Asmari formation). Use LCM pill if encountered heavy losses. If loss couldn’t be
        cured by LCM pill use cement plug(s) to cure same.

     Note:
       - TD should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of rat hole under casing shoe.
       - The exact TD will be determined based on 30” casing tally.
       - Drop Totco prior to POOH to evaluate the verticality of hole.
       - Use following parameters to drill this hole section:
        -WOB = Maximum available
         -RPM = 60-110
        -GPM = 600-1000

     •  POOH. If observed any indication of drag, run back to bottom. Wash and ream to
       make sure that the hole condition is good.

     •  After POOH, rack back the 26in BHA and check the condition of the bit.

     •  Make up 36” BHA, comprising of 26” bit, 36” hole opener and 11” drill collars to tag
         inside 42” conductor.

     •  Enlarge hole to 36” to section TD.

     •  Perform hole condition prior to run casing

     •  Check the stinger is compatible with the shoe. Check the shoe to be stab in type.

     •  R/U 30” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

     •  Run ±36m (Approx. 4 Jts) 30", 234 ppf, X-52, RL-4 casing with stab in-type guide
        shoe to bottom as per casing program. Casing shoe should be adjusted in such a way
          to have 2 meters of rat hole and enough stick up (1.5 m) on rig floor. Fill casing by
       non LCM fresh mud joint by joint.

     Note:
       - Take care when running casing not to set down excessive weight if tight hole or
         ledges are encountered.

     •  Run stinger on 5” DP’s using the “C” Plate & 5” x 30” centralizer. Sting in. M/U &
           test cementing lines. Circulate for one casing annulus volume. While circulating,
        check the DP’s annulus for any return. Use all DP’s weight on the string, if observing
        any minor leakage through DP’s annulus.
