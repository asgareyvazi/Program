     1.1 INTRODUCTION

THIS PROGRAM COVERS WORK OVER OF WELL AGH-9, AGHAR FIELD; IRAN.THE WELL IS NOW
PRODUCING 2.27 MM.M3 /D GAS FROM UPPER DALAN FORMATION.
THIS PROGRAM IS PRAPARED ACCORDING TO LETTER No. 83/H T B/079( 83//چ ت ب 079) FROM SOUTH
ZAGROS PRODUCTION COMPANY DATED 83/07/11.
   THE RESULT OF 27-31/02/1383 INVESTIGATION OF WELL CONDITION SHOW THAT THERE IS
   PRESSURE RELATION BETWEEN 9 5/8"×7" ANNULUS AND TUBING VIA TUBING(THE LEAKAGE IS
   PROBABLY IN THE INTERVAL OF 4 JOINTS UNDER TUBING HANGER), A REMEDIAL PLAN WAS
   ARRANGED TO REACH BELLOW PURPOSES:

    •  INSTALL PLUG TO BE SURE THAT PACKER & SEAL BETWEEN PACKER AND ANCHORE
      SEAL HAS NO LEAKAGE BY DOWNHOLE SERVICES (WIRE LINE).
    •  KILL WELL BY 72 PCF MUD.
    •  N/D WELL HEAD & N/UP BOP.
    •  LAY DOWN COMPLETION STRING.
    •  REPAIR DAMAGED PART OF COMPLETION STRING.
    •  RUN GAS WELL COMPLETION STRING.
    •  DISPLACE ANNULUS BY WATER WITH CORROSION INHIBITOR VIA SSD.
    •  FLOWING WELL ON PROPER CHOKE SIZES & RECORD THE FLOWING PRESSURES.

 1.2.1. DRILLING, COMPLETION AND PRODUCTION HISTORY
   1.2.1.1. DRILLING HISTORY
 Rig was spud at 1376/01/25 on well AGH-9. Commence drilling with 26"bit, with 67 pcf mw to 102 m
 and run 20"casing to 101 m and cemented with return on surface.
 Cont. drilling with 17 1/2" bit, with 65 pcf mw (cut mud with air) to 1464 m and 13 3/8"casing was
 run to 1451 m and cemented with return on surface.
 Drilling to 3208 m with 12 1/4" bit, with 63-65 pcf MW and 9 5/8"casing was run to 3207 and
 cemented with return on surface. While drilling, due to pipe stuck @2440 m, Length of fish 250m (bit,
 drill collars) remain in the well (top of fish @ 2190 m).Due to retrieved all of the fish no success, plug
 back well to 2355m (KOP) and start side tracking from this point. During drilling this hole mud loss
 and well flow occur periodically.
 Cont. drilling with 8 1/2" bit, with 65-85 pcf mw to 4163 m and run CDR/GR, BSC/GR, CDL/CNT/GR,
 DLL/MSFL/GR logs.7"liner was run from 3086-4162 m and cemented. Dry and pressure test of 7" liner
 lap was ok. Coring f/3241-3242.5 m (Dashtak formation) was performed. Perform DST test form open
 hole before running 7" liner by setting packer at @3168 m, that 80pcf mud was observed under DCIP
 valve. During drilling this hole, mud loss and well flow was observed and we had complete loss at
 3624, 4017, 3980, 4037 m. Loss was cured by pumping pill & magneset.
 Commence drilling to 4203 m with 6 1/8" bit, with 73 pcf mud, with 2-16 bbl/hr that was cured by
 LCM-pill. Perform DST test form open hole and set packer at 4127 m (Nar formation) that the result
 was 68-72 pcf salt water. Plug back hole to 4141 m. Replace mud with high PH water and corrosion
 inhibitor.

2.7 Preparation

                   It is planned to Kill the well and cure the loss, N/U the BOP and pull out completion
                string (change destroyed equipment) & cleaned hole then run completion string as
             program. Flow the well on proper choke sizes, secure the well and finally release the
                   rig.


            2.7.1 Standards and Preparation of the Operations

                     •  Make sure that the whole operation is based on good and safe work over
                       operation practices.
                     •  After bullhead well, we require wire line lubricator, BPV plus related running
                           tools, wear bushing and plug tester, 6 1/8" j.mill and necessary X-Over are
                    on board. For more detail, refer to equipment list.
                     •  Prior to start operation, hold a safety/Pre-spud meeting with rig crews and
                         service hands to review the work over program and potential problems. Note
                      meetings in tour book. Ensure that gas monitors are set up. Ensure that all
                         safety devices are properly installed and functioning.

                 Note: Before spud the rig follow pre-Spud check list.

                 Note: Before spud the rig make sure the suitable adaptor for nipple up
                 the BOP (11"-10000) above the 11"×11"-10000 Tubing head spool are
                   available on rig site.

              2.7.2  Pre-Running Checklist

                    •   Pre Completion Meeting to be held with all Rig Personnel.
                    •  The formation could cause mud losses at any time and a small drop in mud
                      level could cause rapid flow of gas or setting the packer due to differential
                   pressure  in wrong place. So the hole must be monitored continuously. Mud
                 volume displaced must be checked against the completion displacement and
                   accurate trip records must be kept at all times.
                    •   Although there  is positive plug in string while running in, but the correct
                   crossover from tubing thread (variety size as completion string) to 5” IF drill pipe
                 must be available on the rig floor at all times when handling tubing. Also a safety
                    valve are required on the rig floor all time (7” pup joint VAM or x-over sub 7” IF
                    pin x 5" IF box excess on rig floor must be exist). If flow is detected, these must
                 be installed immediately and the well must be shut in.
                    •   Special precautions need to be taken when running the control line outside
                   tubing (due to difficulty in sealing with the annular preventor) and when running
                   wire  line. These situations must be discussed by the crew &  all responsible
                   personnel to commence running the completion.

2.8 Completion Running Procedure


This program assumes that the packer and tailpipe are run with the rest of the
completion.


                    •  Make up and  test  pre-assemblies  (in workshop  as  per  manufacturer’s
                   procedures).
                    •    All equipment to be checked for any transport damage.
                    •    All OD’s and ID'S to be checked against the checklist sheets sent from the
                  workshop. All assemblies and tubing to be drifted with respective sizes prior to
                     picking up to the rig floor. It is recommended that after testing, plugs and other
                    flow control accessories are installed in the respective flow control nipples to
                  ensure that the plugs can and will be installed properly down hole.
                    •   Prior to running the completion, RIH with 7” casing scraper to 3150 m.
                    •   Install the RN (2.562'') plug into the top No-Go nipple, then the prong and
                   pressure test to 6500psi. Leave the plug and prong set.(plug in SSD CMD 2.750
             & test string against this plug)
                    •   Check well control precautions (see top of page) are in place. Run completion
                   as per Completion Diagram (optional-2). Tail pipe length = approx. 12.61 m.
                 Note that the torque requirements for the 4 ½” string (12.6 lb/ft tubing, 12.6
                         lb/ft crossovers and completion equipment) and the 7” string (29 lb/ft tubing and
                   completion equipment) are:
                    •   Torque figures: 4-1/2” 13.5 lb/ft New Vam 75-80-85 KSI
                                       Min. 3780 ft/lbs; opt. 4200 ft/lbs; Max. 4620 ft/lbs
                     Torque figures: 7” 29 lb/ft New Vam 75-80-85 KSI
                                   Min 8460 ft/lbs; Opt. 9400ft/lbs; Max. 10340ft/lbs
                    •   Also note that the correct API modified thread lubricant must be used. Do not
                  use any more thread  lubricant than necessary, the completion eng. should
                  demonstrate correct use of thread lubricant (using paint brush, not dope brush
                 and applying to pin and only the shoulder of the box).
                    •  Make up 7” packer assembly and set the assembly in the slips.
                    •  Remove shear screws, and install the packer test clamp (segments).
                NOTE: A dog collar clamp or safety clamp should be used at all times until there
                           is sufficient weight on the slips to ensure that the tubing string is held securely in
                   the rotary table during each connection.
                    •    Fill assembly with clean water 72.4PCF none solid mud.
                    •   Pressure test assembly to 6,500 psi for 15 minutes.
                    •   Bleed off pressure slowly.
                    •   Dismantle surface test fittings and bull plug and install shear screws.
                    •   Clear the rig floor and make ready to run the rest of the 9 5/8''*7” x 4 1/2”
                   completion string.
                    •  Run the packer slowly through the BOP’s. Fill up the first 10 joints with Hi-Vis
                             pill and the rest with water to make sure that no debris pumped in. Once the
                   packer is in the casing, do not run the packer faster than 30 foot joint -from slips
                   out to slips in - per 4 minute.
                NOTE:  Monitor  the  trip  tank  continuously  while  running  completion  string
                   plugged. Mud volume  displaced must be  checked  against  the  completion
                   displacement and accurate trip records must be kept at all times. Fluid level must
                 be monitored continuously. A contractor Toolpusher or supervisor must be on the
                        rig floor at all times and must keep the ICOFC Company Man and Completion
                   Engineer notified of any changes in well status.

                    •  RIH adding additional completion equipment and 4''/4 ½” / 7” tubing as per
                   (Completion diagram)  Fill the tubing with fresh water with inhibitor every 10
                       joints.

2.9 Milling Procedure
              2.9.1 Items to be checked prior to sending tools to the rig

    1.  OD, ID and length of all bottom hole assembly items.

    2.  Ensure the cylinder is long enough to accept the body of the packer.

    3.  Ensure the length from the bottom of the milling shoe to the catch sleeve  is the correct
        length and that it will go all the way through the packer to be milled.

    4.  Ensure the assembly on the catch sleeve rotates freely.
   WARNING: The spring above the catch sleeve is very strong. Watch your fingers.

    5.  Confirm the correct catch sleeve is used.

    6.  Tighten the safety sub below the catch sleeve. If this rotates off while milling the packer,
       then the packer cannot be retrieved in one trip.

              2.9.2 Milling the packer

    1.  Make up the tool string and run in hole slowly. Care should be exercised when running the
         tools through the BOPs to ensure that the milling tool does not damage this equipment.

    2.  Run in hole at a moderate speed

    3.  Slow down as packer setting depth is reached. If the packer setting depth is known, then the
        Kelly can be made up prior to tagging the packer. Ensure there is at least 10 feet of string up
      when commencing milling operations. This is to ensure that the tool does not have to be
        released, to add a single while milling the packer. Once the catch sleeve is released, the tool
       has to come out of the hole to be redressed.
      Tag the packer with the shoe, pull back and space out. Ensure there is at least 10 feet of
         string up when commencing milling operations.

    4.  Prior to stinging into the packer, check up and down weights with the pumps on and off. Also
       check rotating weights with the pumps on and off. Pump rate should be 2 - 3 Bbls a minute
       and rotating speed should be 70 - 80 RPM.

    5.  Stop rotating and slowly go down to tag the packer with the pumps running. As the stinger
        enters the packer bore a pressure increase will be seen. Back off on the pumps to the same
       pressure that was seen, when circulating above the packer. Tag the packer with the milling
       shoe and place 5,000 lbs of string weight on the packer.

    6.  Stop pumps.

    7.  Pick up to bring the catch sleeve inside the bore of the packer. Slowly rotate the string 2
        times. This is to un-jay the catch sleeve and set it in the catch position. Slack-off and place
       5,000 lbs of string weight on the packer.

    8.  Pick up until an over-pull is seen. Pull 15,000 lbs over string weight to ensure the catch is well
        seated. If no over-pull is seen after 3 feet of picking up, go back and repeat steps 6, 7 & 8
          until an over-pull is seen.


    9.  Slack off and place 5,000 lbs of string weight on the packer.

    10. Pick back up until the milling shoe is just above the packer. Do not pick up too far as the
         string will shorten when the string is turned. This can bring the catch sleeve in contact with the
       bottom of the packer and may release the catch sleeve.

    11. Start pumps up and bring the pressure up to the same as in step 5 and 6. Start the rotary and
         build up the RPM to the same as in step 5. Ensure rotary speed and pump pressures are the
      same as in step 5

    12. Slack off and place 1,000 lbs of string weight on the packer and allow to mill off. Keep doing
         this and gradually increasing the weight until 5,000 lbs of string weight is on the packer. This
       should be done over a time frame of 30 minutes. A 9 5/8” model D packer should take around
       4 - 5 hours to mill.

      NOTE: Problems may be encountered when milling through the extrusion rings that back up
        the packing element. These rings may begin to rotate. The pumps may be stopped for 5
       minute intervals to try to burn through these rings if they do rotate. A close eye must be kept
       on the torque at this point to prevent twist off.

    13. Once the packer has fallen, stop the rotary immediately. This is to stop the safety sub from
       being accidentally being backed out and the catch sleeve releasing. Go down as far as
        possible. Pick back up as high as possible and circulate at a high rate. Repeat this 3 or 4
        times to ensure that the packer is free.

    14. Circulate at a high rate, at least bottoms up before pulling out of the hole.

    15. POOH.

    Note: If for some reason the milling tool has to be released from the packer, then following should
       be followed. Stop pumps and rotary.

    16. Pick up and pull 10,000 lbs over string weight. Rotate the string 10 times to the right. If the
         tool does not release, increase the pick up to 15,000 lbs over string weight and rotate the
         string until the tool releases.

    17. POOH.

    18. When the tools reach surface, the Baker Engineer must be on the rig floor to inspect the tools
       as they come through the rotary table.
