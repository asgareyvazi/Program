1.4.   DRILL 12-1.4” PILOT HOLE

      The objective of drilling a pilot hole is to establish whether there is a any shallow gas or
       shallow water flow hazard up to a depth of  around 600 m below seabed. The pilot hole
        will be logged using the CDR but real time data is not required; data will be downloaded
      when the BHA is on surface.

        Details regarding mud program (specified in section 6.2), BHA (specified in section 7.2.1),
        deviation surveys (specified in section 8), mud logging (specified in section 10.1), electric
       logging (specified in section 10.3), and drilling hazards (specified in section 9).

3.4.1.      Move 50 m off location (direction will be confirmed from base 48 hrs prior to drill
         pilot hole).

3.4.2.      Make up 12-1/4" pilot hole BHA as per BHA programme Section 7.2.1.

3.4.3.        Idea of the pilot hole BHA: It should be almost same as 26" BHA.

3.4.4.        Position ROV at seabed and confirm water depth and temperature.

3.4.5.       Tag sea bed , confirm water depth (cross check ROV reading).

3.4.6.         Drill pilot hole to 600 m below sea bed.

3.4.7.        Bentonite sweeps will be used as per the mud programme (Section 6.2). Keep the
         string moving all the time. ROP will be limited to around 50 m/hr.


The results of the pilot hole will determine the forward programme with respect to the 20” casing
setting depth. Ideally this should be set as deep as possible at around 600m.

       1)  If no shallow gas or shallow water flow is encountered, 20” casing will be set as
          programmed at 600 m below seabed to ensure that the maximum LOT is achieved for
            the next section.

       2)  If a gas or water flow is encountered below 300 m from seabed, the well will be killed
           with heavy mud and the 20” casing will be set above 300 m. This setting depth should
           provide a fomation strength at the 20” shoe of at least 9.8 ppg which will be sufficient
             to drill through a shallow water hazard pressured at 9.5 ppg.

       3)  If a gas or water flow is encountered before reaching 300 m below sea bed the
         Dynamic Kill or DKD process will have to be planned to enable drilling to continue
             for a further 150 m of hole. The decision to mix DKD mud will be taken at this point.
