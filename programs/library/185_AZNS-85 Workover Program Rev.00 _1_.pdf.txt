1.  General Information

 1.1 Field Introduction

Azadegan South Oil Field is located in West Karoun province on a large long-axis anticline with N-S
trending with approximate dimensions about 20km width & 60km length near to Iran-Iraq border, to the
east of Yaran Oil Field.

The trap area of the south Azadegan is limited to 43.2x17.2km The Azadegan Oil field was discovered in
1999, when Well AZN-1 was drilled vertically down to the Cretaceous Fahliyan Formation with total
depth of 4,643m. The results of this well, revealed the reservoir characteristics of the field. Following to
the result of the well No. AZN-1, another 5 exploration wells was drilled in different parts of this huge
field down to Fahliyan Formation. In the later stage the well No.10 assumed as exploration well for
evaluation of deeper formations. This well was drilled in 2008, down to the depth of 5340m (Neyriz Fm.),
mainly to evaluate the Jurassic formations.

Based on the exploration wells results, carbonate reservoir of Sarvak Formation is broadly developed in
Azadegan Field. This formation was penetrated in all of seven exploration wells (AZN-1, AZN-2, AZN-
3, AZN-4, AZN-5, AZN-6 & AZN-10). Clastic oil-bearing zones of Kazhdumi Formation developed in
main anticline of Azadegan Field, extending from north to center of South Azadegan. The Clastic oil-
bearing zones of Gadvan Formation developed only in crest area & south parts of South Azadegan Filed,
which was discovered by AZN-1 in crest & AZN-4 in south parts. The carbonates of Fahliyan Formation
also developed in Azadegan field, but the oil reservoir of this formation developed in South part of South
Azadegan.

The Azadegan Field is an elongated anticline, trending in N-S direction, belonging to "Arabian Trend".

The stratigraphy sequence of the field includes AghaJari, Gachsaran, Fahliyan, Pabdeh, Gurpi, Ilam,
Laffan, Sarvak, Kazhdumi, Dariyan, Gadvan & Fahliyan Formations, from top to bottom. This sequence
in well no AZN-10, the deepest up to now drilled well of the field, continues with Garau, Gotnia,
Najmah, Sargalu, Alan, Mus & Neyriz formations.

The Dominant lithofacies of Sarvak Formation are Rudist-bearing packstone & partially grainstone,
which contains abundant bioclasts. Some other facies like matrix-dominated wackestone & mudstone are
also found.

The AZADEGAN Oilfield is Located in Khuzestan Province & is Approximately Located 80 Kilometers
West of Ahwaz City, near to Iran-Iraq Border. It is in The Northwest of The YADAVARAN Field & in
The West of JUFEYR  Field. The Average Monthly Temperature  is 45ºc in summer, & Highest
Temperature is 52ºc. The Lowest Temperature is -4.4ºc in winter. Maximum Rainfall is 78mm in February,
Winter Season.

This Document covers the work over of well AZNS-85. Main headlines of program are perform 7" tie-
back liner lap dry test & complete the well for Fahliyan production. The well was drilled to the final
depth of 4090m (Fahliyan Formation) on 2014 by NISOC to produce oil from Fahliyan formation.

 1.7 Drilling History

The AZNS-85 (Pad 79) was drilled by rig 86 Fath, which rig moving was spudded on 15-07-1394 @
00:00.

     26" hole section drilling to 152m, run 20" casing to 150m (J-55, 133#, BTC) & cement job as
      program with 60bbl cement return, Mw 68pcf.
     17-1/2" hole was drilled to 1279m, while increase mud weight f/67 to 85pcf, run 13-3/8" casing (N-
       80, BTS, 72#, f/0 to 1099.15m & NKT, BTS, 72#, f/1099.5m to 1279m) & cement job as program
       with 180bbl cement return.
     12-1/4" hole section was drilled to 3237m with 6-150BPH loss while pumping LCM Pill & bypass
       system, take full set log, continue drilling to 3367m with 20-150BPH loss while pumping LCM Pill
    & bypass system, complete loss pump LCM Pill several time, decrease Mw f/80 to 77pcf, hole
       condition & While condition stuck string & complete loss, work on stuck & free pipes, pump
    LCM Pill & increase Mw to 70pcf, drilling to 3361m, flow the well, twist-off string, fish left hole
       f/3029.37m to 3496.85m (12-1/4" PDC bit, bit sub, 9 Jts D.C, 8" Jar, 3 Jts 8-1/2" D.C, XOS, 36 Jts
       5" DPs), fish recovered, increase Mw to 78pcf, drilling to 3950m, take log as program, run 9-5/8"
       casing (L-80, 53.5#, VAM f/0 to 1844m & 110SS, 47#, TPCQ f/1844m to 3950m) & cement job as
       program, no cement return.
     The hole section 8-1/2" was drilled to 3957m, take CBL/VDL/CCL/GR log from 9-5/8" casing,
        drilling to 4034m, increase Mw to 101pcf due to flow well, drilling to 4090m (TD), suddenly well
       flow (20BPH total gain 30BPH oil & gas in mud; 92pcf) increasing Mw to 103pcf, run 7" liner
      (VM110SS, 35#, N.VAM) to 4090m, pressure test & inject 150bbl cement in 9-5/8" casing @
       3103m, dry test 7" liner lap was OK, take CBL/VDL/CCL/GR log from 7" liner, set cement plug
       f/3882m to 3650m & test 9-5/8" casing was not OK, take CAST log f/3648m to 1200m in 9-5/8"
        casing, observe tear 9-5/8" casing f/3157m to 3140m, first tie back 7" liner to 3829m (L-80, 29#,
      HSM1), dry test 7" liner lap was not OK, second tie back 7" liner lap to 2903m (L-80, 29#,
     VAM), set cement plugs f/3879m to 3663m & f/2903m to 2763m, suspension well without
       completion string & release rig @ 1395-02-27.

5.  Sequence of Operation

 5.1 Main Objective
Base on well condition, the decision has been made to perform 7" tied-back liner lap dry test &
Main plan
1.  Held PJSM. Spud the Rig. Stand back required 5" DPs, 5" HWDPs and 6-1/2" DC.

2. Check the wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record well head pressures; check the all annulus
    pressure. Check the return fluid & Bleed off any annulus pressure to Zero upon client’s approval
   and recheck the well status.

Note: If wellhead pressure is not ZERO after bleed off, contractor shall inform client. Remedial
plan to be prepared in advance by operators & will be followed after client's confirmation.

3. RIH with tubing hanger retrieving tool & engage with TBH. Pull out with TBH to surface.
4. N/U BOP and pressure test. Perform wellhead pressure integrity test with cup tester.
5. RIH with 8-1/2" D/O BHA to top of cement plug.
6.  Drill out cement plug to 7" liner lap f/2768m to 2903m (take care of lap@ 2903m), POOH.
7. RIH with 5-7/8" clean-out BHA to top of cement @ 3663m & tag, CBU till shakers clean, POOH.
8. RIH with tandem scraper 9-5/8" * 7", scrape inside 9-5/8" casing & 7" liner (dry test packer
    setting depths), CBU & POOH.
9.  Perform BOP test.
10. RIH with wireline SBT/VDL/CCL/GR Logging tools to bottom, log up to surface (in two separate
   runs for 9-5/8" CSG & 7" Liner), POOH.
11. RIH with wireline corrosion logging tools to bottom, log up to surface (in two separate runs for 9-
    5/8" CSG & 7" Liner), POOH.
12. RIH with 9-5/8" RTTS packer & perform 7" liner lap dry test, POOH.

Note: If dry test is not OK, follow contingency plan #1.

13. Perform BOP test.
14. RIH with 5-7/8" D/O BHA (3-1/2" DPs JT by JT), drill-out cement plug inside 7" liner f/3663m
    to 3879m, continue RIH to 4070m LDC, CBU till shakers clean, POOH to surface.
15. RIH with 7" scraper, scrape inside 7" liner, CBU till shakers clean, displace inside 7" liner with
    non-solid fluid, POOH to surface.
16. Run perforation gun assembly & perforate 7" liner in Fah-2a (4031m to 4045m) & Fah-3a
   (4057m to 4061m) tentatively, POOH to surface.
17. RIH with GR-CCL log to confirm perforation intervals.
18. Perform BOP test.

Completion Fluid Mixing Procedure:

    1. Make sure that all pits and lines involved with completion fluid are thoroughly cleaned,
       flushed and free of any fluid and solid.

    Make sure that at least one large pit is kept empty for transferring ready brine into after
          given time for settling. After transferring one pit to empty pit, the first one is to be flushed
         and cleaned for accommodating the brine of next ready pit, and so on to the last pit.

    Depends on the rig facilities and structure, mud engineer on board may find it better to
           prepare the fluids pit-by-pit.

    2.  Transfer the content of CaBr2 drums into designated pits.

    Record the number of drums poured in each tank for accurate volume in it as the base of
           calculation for adding other additives into; do not rely on pit gauge, sounding or mud
          logging sensor, but have a look on them for comparison and/or correlation if found useful.

      7.1.2.4  Downhole Losses

formation losses must be taken into consideration.
Losses will be minimized/avoided by the planned use of properly sized bridging/weight material (and
Polymer) in conjunction with appropriate drilling practices. If seepage losses are noted on the well no
specific action will be taken but close monitoring of the loss rate is to be made. For moderate loss
rates addition of CaCO3 50 micron and 150 micron is recommended to ensure that there is adequate
correctly sized material to act as bridging agents.

Spotting Procedure

    1.  After spotting LCM pill, pull the bit above the calculated top of the pill. Wait 30 minutes and
       monitor the annulus.
    2.  After 30 minutes, attempt to fill the annulus, if not already full. If the hole can be filled, close
     BOP and squeeze 10bbl LCM pill into formation without exceeding 200psi.
    3.  If the hole remains stable, continue to drill ahead.
    4.  Should the LCM pill fail to reduce the losses or the hole cannot be filled, then mix and spot
       another 50bbl of LCM pill.
    5.  Attempt to fill the annulus. If successful, close the BOP and squeeze 20bbl of LCM pill into
       the formation without exceeding 200psi.
    6.  If a squeeze pressure develops, hold the pressure for 1 hour before running the drill string

12.  Health, Safety & Environments

  12.1   WELL CONTROL
 All the drilling operation shall obey the requirement of Well Control Manual of MSA & contractor’s
 well control manual; under no circumstances should the deviation from well control policy be permitted
 without prior approval through MSA. If the contractor’s well control manual or anywhere has conflict
 with Well Control Manual of MSA, take Well Control Manual of MSA as the standard.

  12.1.1 WORK Requirement of Well Control Equipment
    With shut-in casing pressure lower than 1,000psi, while snubbing with annular preventer, drill tools
      shall have 18-deg shoulder tool joints, & the snubbing velocity shall not be greater than 0.2m/s.
     After shut off the annular preventer or ram preventer, with the shut-in casing pressure not greater
     than 2,000psi, drill tools are allowed to pull up & down with the velocity less than 0.2m/s, however,
      rotating or tool joint passing through are forbidden.
    While drilling through pay zone & thereafter, BOP function (close-open) test for the BOPs shall be
     performed regularly.
     Kill manifolds shall not be used for drilling fluid fill-in.
    The maximum allowed shut-in casing pressure shall be remarkably indicated on choke manifolds.
     All gate valves of well control manifolds shall be tagged with serial numbers, & be indicated with
      on/Off.
     In case shear rams were used, after shearing the drill pipe with shear rams, retrieve the upper sheared
       drill pipe, lower a drill pipe joint with TIW valve installed into the hole & close pipe rams on it to
      prevent the possibility of shear ram’s failure.

  12.1.2  Management of Well Control Equipment
    Well control equipment used by the drilling crew shall be managed & operated by designated
     personnel with clear post responsibilities.
    Well  site  shall have  specified component storeroom & rubber element storeroom with  air
      conditioners  respectively. The temperature of storerooms  shall meet storage requirements of
     components & rubber elements.
    At least 1 set is available as back-up for every type ram assembly in the well site. Ram preventer
      side door seals & ram rubber, every type gasket & bolt etc. should be enough for backup.

Upon reaching surface casing shoe
At midpoint of the wellbore

 12.4    Primary Control Reference
It is the responsibility of the Drilling Contractor to keep the hole full at all times by using all the
available equipment. The Drilling Supervisor shall monitor that the correct preventive practices are being
implemented and/or conducted at all times.

Extreme care shall be taken to monitor mud volume, drilling breaks & gas cut mud.

The MACP (Maximum allowable casing pressure) shall be confirmed by the Drilling Supervisor, either
after setting each new casing string or, whenever the density of the drilling mud changes.

The MACP shall be clearly written on a kick control sheet that will be posted near the choke control
panel.

"In-post" policy

"In-post" will be initiated after the surface casing is cemented.

The "in-post" personnel must be trained and verified qualified by the technical staff of drilling rig.

During drilling, specific personnel shall be arranged to be "in-post", the mud logging personnel shall
monitor the level of the circulating tank etc. as per the requirements. During tripping, other auxiliary
operation or drilling suspension, specific personnel for "in-post" shall be arranged by the drilling and
mud logging personnel in the meantime.

"In-post" requirements:

For drilling operation, variation of the outlet flow, drilling time, lithology, bubble, smell, showings, pit
volume, density and mud viscosity, gas measured values and content of chloride etc. shall be carefully
monitored. The level of the circulating tank shall be observed and recorded each 15 minutes and if
special situation occurs, the measurement and record shall be intensified. If anomaly occurs, it shall be
reported to the driller in time.

For tripping operation, trip sheet shall be filled accordingly. If anomaly occurs, it shall be reported to the
driller.

For other operations, it shall be observed whether there is overflow of mud at the wellhead or mud level
from wellhead. If anomaly occurs, it shall be reported to the driller.

Treatment of loss of circulation

Before cementing non-target zone or pay zone, if loss of circulation occurs during drilling, POOH shall
be carried to run open ended drill pipe for cement plug job if condition allows. During RIH, mud shall be
filled from annulus to balance formation pressure to avoid blowout.

Mud level not at wellhead after loss of circulation. Stop drilling, pick up TDS or Kelly, stop pumps and
observe with moving the drill string and prepare well control related work. Each 10 minutes, mud with
density no lower than the drilling weight shall be reversely filled up from the annulus. Each time, the
filling up shall be 1~2m3, or continuous reserve injecting mud with small speed to maintain that the
column pressure of mud in wellbore is higher than the formation pressure. POOH to casing shoe or

13.  H2S Monitoring & Prevention Measure

  13.1    Monitoring H2S
 Somewhere on the well site is apt to accumulate H2S, such as mud pit, shale shaker, and drilling
 platform, should be installed one set of. Make sure H2S monitor and alarm system can be used. Alarm
 threshold setting for H2S warning indicator: the first warning level is 10mg/m3, unable warning sound,
  just show present value of H2S content to operators. The second warning level is 20mg/m3, when the
 H2S content in air exceed 20mg/m3, H2S warning indicator can give an alarm automatically, and
 warning sound should be heard by everyone work in well site. The audible alarm should be installed on
 Kelly board.

 Equip enough gas masks on drilling platform, shaker screen (total amount should large than 24), and
 equip 12 sets of 30min positive pressure Air Aspirators 2 sets of portable H2S monitor and appropriative
 H2S warning indicator. Arrange a person specially assigned for taking charge  all the protective
 apparatus. This man must do periodical check to keep these equipment in a good condition.

 H2S monitor should be done weekly check and compulsory examination.

 After drilling into the pay zone, strengthen the detection of H2S content in mud.

  13.2    Measures for H2S prevention
 Windsocks shall be set on the drill floor and in the monsoon direction or/and prevailing wind direction of
 Azadegan oil field, off well site. In emergency, employees must follow the evacuation procedure.

 Explosion-proof blowers shall be installed around the rig, shale shaker, and drilling fluid tanks to blow
 away toxic and flammable gas at the well site.

 The rig crew in the H2S area shall be furnished with H2S monitors and adequate protective apparatus.
 Everybody shall be able to use, maintain, and check the apparatus.

 The technicians of rig crew are responsible for H2S proof training and the tool pusher is in charge of the
 supervision and examination.

 The pH value of drilling fluid in the H2S area shall be kept over 9.5. Pay attention to measurement of
 H2S concentration in drilling fluid. The H2S concentration shall be kept at less than 50mg/cm3 by using
 sulphur removal agents and degassers.

 Personnel safety and protection shall be carried out according to SY/T5087 during operations in polluted
 areas where H2S content exceeds the critical concentration.
