
16” CASING RUNNING PROCEDURE
GOLDFINGER
POOH.  OPEN PBL SUB AND WASH 16” PROFILE @ 8526’ ON TRIP OUT.
HOLD SAFETY MEETING AND REVIEW JSA.   CLEAN PITS AND START MIXING SPACER.
RU WEATHERFORD CSG TOOLS.
P/U AUTO FILL FLOAT SHOE JT.
INSPECT SHOE TRACK WITH LIGHT FOR TRASH.
CHECK VISUALLY TO MAKE SURE AUTO FILL EQUIPMENT IS WORKING PROPERLY.
M/U 16” 97#  N80HC GB-3P-SC CASING AND RUN 29 JTS BEFORE PU THE HANGER (SEE RUNNING TALLY SHEET).
VERIFY PIPE IS FULL EA JT WHILE RUNNING.
MAKE UP FIRST JOINTS TO DIAMOND, AVERAGE THE TORQUE AND USE THAT FOR THE REMAINDER OF THE CASING.
THERE SHOULD BE    7   JTS OF 16” 97#  PLUS BAKER LOCK JOINT & 2 CENTRALIZER JOINTS
MU DRILL PIPE TO HANGER ASSY.  (VERIFY PUP JOINT ID/OD BEFORE PU HANGER) INSTALL ALLEMON TOOL TWO STANDS ABOVE HANGER.   16” SHOE IN OPEN HOLE AT STD # 81 .    SLOW DOWN WHEN HANGER REACHES WH @ STD #   .AFTER CASING ENTERS THE STACK, INSTALL STRIPPING RUBBER ON DRILL PIPE ABOVE PS-20’S TO KEEP FROM DROPPING DEBRIS IN THE HOLE.  WATCH DISPLACEMENT IN TRIP TANK.
TIH W/CASING.   CHECK MUD LEVEL IN PIPE EA STD, IF NOT VISIBLE FILL PIPE W/MUD EVERY 10 STDS.    DO NOT CIRCULATE OVER 2 BBLS/MIN BEFORE LANDING CASING UNLESS FILL IS ENCOUNTERED.  AFTER ENTERING PROFILE @ 8526’ CHECK RUNNING SPEEDS TO CONTROL LOSSES IF ANY.
5 STANDS FROM BOTTOM, DROP ALLOMON DE-ACTIVATION BALL.  MU CEMENT STAND.
LAND 16” HANGER INTO PROFILE.
89  STDS OF 5 1/2”  D.P. +    25.38’ DOWN ON CMT STD WILL LAND HANGER.
WITH BALL ON SEAT, PRESSURE UP TO 1000 PSI AND HOLD ~ 2-3 MINUTES.  INCREASE PRESSURE AND PUMP BALL THROUGH ALLEMON TOOL (2500 PSI TO BLOW BALL).  DEACTIVATE AUTO FILL FLOAT EQUIPMENT.  IF 100% CIRCULATION MAINTAINED, CIRCULATE BOTTOMS UP.  GEMOCO REP TO BE ON THE RIG FLOOR.
HOLD PRE-JOB SAFETY MEETING.  RU CMT LINES, HALLIBURTON TO PUMP 5 BBLS OF SPACER AND TEST LINES TO 5000 PSI.  DROP DART.
CONTINUE PUMPING 95 BBLS OF SPACER AND CEMENT WITH 680 SACKS, 16.7 PPG LEAD CEMENT.   KICK OUT TOP PLUG WIPER DART.   DISPLACE WITH HALLIBURTON 10 BBLS SPACER, 411 BBLS 11.2 PPG SBM.  SLOW DOWN TO 3 BPM WHEN BOTTOM DART NEARS ALLOMON TOOL AND WHEN TOP DART NEARS ALLOMON TOOL (SHOULD SEE +/- 1500 PSI WHEN DARTS PASS THROUGH)
BUMP PLUG WITH 500 PSI OVER CIRCULATING RATE.  DO NOT PUMP MORE THAN HALF THE SHOE TRACK VOLUME (SHOE TRACK VOLUME =31 BBLS).  AFTER PLUG REACHES LAUNCHER, CHECK VOLUME PUMPED TO USE AS A MARKER FOR BUMPING THE PLUG.
BLEED OFF & CHECK TO SEE THAT FLOATS ARE HOLDING.
RELEASE HANGER & SET FMC PACKOFF AS PER SERVICE HAND.  TEST PACKOFF TO 1500 PSI.
PULL OFF AND CIRCULATE BOTTOMS UP TO ENSURE NO GAS.  IF PACKOFF DOES NOT TEST, WILL HAVE TO RUN SEPARATE TRIP OPEN ENDED TO SQUEEZE.
POOH LD RUNNING TOOL.
TIH TO SET SURFACE PLUG AND PREPARE TO DISPLACE RISER & PULL BOP’S.

246 BBLS CASING CAPACITY
SHOE TRACK = 31 BBLS
THERE ARE 3.7 BBLS BETWEEN ALLOMON TOOL AND HANGER
