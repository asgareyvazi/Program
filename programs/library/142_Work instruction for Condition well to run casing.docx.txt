Work instruction for Condition well
To:  Toolpusher, mud engineer, mud logger and waste management & SAED Rep.
Make up 24”bit(Type: MS-TZC,S/N: 73969-T, with 3*16 & 1*18 Nozzle) on the BHA:
24”R.Bit
24”near bit stabilizer (w/float valve & baffle)
2jts 9-1/2”DC
24”String stabilizer
7jts 9-1/2”DC
XOS(7-5/8”Reg * 6-5/8”Reg)
2jts 8-1/2”DC
8”jar
3jts 8-1/2”DC
XOS (6-5/8”Reg * 4-1/2”IF)
Continue RIH with the BHA to 1stand 5”DP after BHA, call the Mud Logger to Calibrate Hook Load Sensor.
SLM 5”DPs while RIH.
Continue RIH to BTM, in case of any drag while RIH, just pass that point with W&R, W&R parameters are: GPM=800, RPM=70-90, WOB=0-5000lbs.
Continue RIH to BTM (1981m), W&R the last stand to the BTM several times.
Circulate to BTM’s Up, Mud Engineers is in charge of monitoring mud return density.
While circulation at the BTM, move string UP & Down.
Spot 200 bbl HI-VIS pill (water loss control).
POH to surface.
