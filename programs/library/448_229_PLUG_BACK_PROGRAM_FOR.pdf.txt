PLUG BACK PROGRAM FOR 8 ½” HOLE ON WELL FZ-1

        M/U ckeck trip BHA (8 ½” insert bit SMITH  W5293 w/o
            nozzles - bit sub - 1 std 6 ½” DC – 8 ½” SST – 2 std 6 ½” DC –
       XO – Jar – XO – 2 x 6 ½” DC – XO – 12 x 5”HWDP.
        RIH w/check trip BHA.
        Wash down last std to TD @ 2887m. Circ and cond  mud
         and Circ until shakers are clean.
       POOH w/ check trip BHA, Rack back BHA.
          P/U 290m of 2 7/8” tbg and TIH w/ CMT stinger on 5” DP to
          2887m.
          Break Circ. Circ x Cond Mud.
         R/U HALCO and pressure test CMT lines to 3000 psi.
            Set balanced CMT plug#1 from 2887m to 2599m: HALCO to
        pump 20 bbls 13 ppg DUAL spacer @max 5 BPM ahead,
           followed by 66.4 bbls 15.8 ppg CMT slurry APP. To pump 7.8
           bbls 13 ppg DUAL spacer @max 5 BPM behind. Displace
           with 137 bbls 11 ppg salt sat. mud @ 8 BPM.
           Slowly POOH w/stinger to 2271m. R/U and reverse out
           spacer and excess CMT@2260m. reverse circ stinger clean.
       POOH and rack back stinger(behind DC stands).
          Change wash pipe on TDS.
        M/U rock bit ( SMITH LW5293 w/o nozzles) – XO – XO – 1
               jt 5 HWDP – XO( 4 ½” IF Pin x 4 ½” Reg Pin) – rot scraper –
             bit sub – 3 std 5” HWDP – Jar – 1 std 5” HWDP and RIH on
          5” DP. Scrape csg at Whipstock setting depth from 2000m to
         2100m first and then from 2145m to 2175m(bridge plug
             setting depth interval.) DO NOT RUN WITH SCRAPER
       BELOW 2145m. DO NOT RUN WITH BIT/ SCRAPER
        INTO GREEN CMT WHILE SCRAPING CSG.
        Wash down and tag TOC plug with 10000 lb(watch weight
         and pressure). If TOC plug is deeper than 2311m, we may
          have to set another plug.
         P/U 5 std. Close pipe rams and R/U HALCO. Pressure test
       CMT lines to 3500psi and test CMT plug to 2500psi.
