              Drilling Special Services Engineering Department


           9 5/8″ CEMENTING PROCEDUER(KSH-A003)



1- Clean up tanks and preparing chemicals for mixing fluid
2-  Fill tanks with water (Lead : 900 bbl Fresh Water & Tail: 60 bbl Fresh Water) for mixing
    fluids (Lead and Tail)
3-  before finished running casings with Co. man approval, start mixing cement additives and
   preparing mixing fluids according to below
 Lead Cement mixing procedure:
    Add Anti Foam and allow mixing for 5 min.
    Add CR-450 to Fresh Water and allow mixing for 15 min.
    Add Bentonite to Fresh Water and allow mixing for 2 hours.
    Add CD-1B and allow mixing until the cement operation will finish.
 Tail Cement mixing procedure:
    Add Anti Foam and allow mixing for 5 min.
    Add CR-450 and allow mixing for15 min.
    Add CD-1B and allow mixing for15 min.
    Add CFL-325 and allow mixing until the cement operation will finish.
4- Before running casing, check cement head specially plunger of released cement plug and put
    the Top & Bottom plug.
5-  After running Casing, install circulating swage, start circulation at least one casing cap
   volume.
6-  Pre job safety meeting with involved personnel and arrange with mud engineer to measure
    drilling fluid & cement loss/gain during cement job.
7- Stop Circulation, Connect 9 5/8" Cement head and Cement line.
8-  Test cement line W/ 3000 psi
9- Pump Pre Flush ( 40 bbl Fresh Water ) then release bottom plug.
10- Mix and pump 1080 BBLS lead cement slurry.
11- Mix and pump 60 BBLS tail cement slurry.
12- Release Top plug while pump 2 bbl Location Water by cement unit.
13- Displace cement with ------ Bbl drilling fluid, If does not observe bumping pressure, you will
   pump additional displacement liquid (Drilling Fluid) Max 3 bbl (25% Shoe track volume).
14- Release Casing Pressure
15- Monitoring return line for checking Float Collar and Float Shoe.
16- Rig down Cement Line.
