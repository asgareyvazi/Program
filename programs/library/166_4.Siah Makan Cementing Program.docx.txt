# Cementing  Plan
Note: The following cement program is based on mud weight mentioned in mud program. The required Cementing Program for each phase will be issued in due time and this sample program is relate to Group 1 depths. (W014N, W036N) and for other wells will be issued in due time but the volume of cementing operation will be explained.
Cementing table:

# 18 5/8" Casing
For cementing operation, note the followings:

#### Conduct safety meeting prior to start operation.

#### Circulate mud and clean inside casing and annulus.

#### Flush all the lines with water. Test the lines to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.

#### Mix and pump cement as per program.  Total slurry volume is calculated based on 100% excess in open hole.

#### All samples should be stored until the success of the cement job has been verified, then they may be discarded.

#### Fill up the cementing operation sheet and send it to drilling office.

#### Ensure that the 18 5/8" casing is full of mud before pumping cement and stinger for leakage inside casing.

# 13 3/8" Casing
Important: The following program is a sample; therefore cement should be tested by using the field drilling water, cement sample and additives.
Cementing will be performed by conventional method (Plugs). Annular volume is 1029 bbl open hole with 76 bbl of cased hole volume.
The 17 1/2” hole will be drilled from 150 m to 2677 m by using Bentonite mud.
For cementing operation, note the followings:
Conduct safety meeting prior to start operation.
Circulate mud and clean casing annulus.
Flush all the lines with water. Test the lines to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.
Mix and pump cement as per program. Total slurry volume is calculated based on 100% excess in open hole and 20% excess in cased hole.
All samples should be stored until the success of the cement job has been verified, then they may be discarded.
Fill up the cementing operation sheet and send it to drilling office.

# 9 5/8" Casing
Important: The following program is a sample; therefore it should be tested by using the field drilling water, cement sample and additives as required.
12 1/4” hole section is from 2677 to 3313 m to run and set 9 5/8” casing. This hole section will be drilled by using mud weight from 130-137 pcf.
For cementing operation, note the followings:
A minimum of 10 kg dry blend and 10 kg of neat cement should be transferred from the rig to cement lab along with additive samples for QC testing right after receiving cement. During the cement job, 5 liters of a representative mix-water sample should be collected from cement mix water tanks.
Conduct safety meeting prior to start operation.
Circulate mud and clean casing annulus.
Flush all the lines with water. Test the lines to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.
Mix and pump cement as per program. Total slurry volume is calculated based on 100% excess in open hole and 20% excess in cased hole.
All samples should be stored until the success of the cement job has been verified, then they may be discarded.
Fill up the cementing operation sheet and send it to drilling office.
