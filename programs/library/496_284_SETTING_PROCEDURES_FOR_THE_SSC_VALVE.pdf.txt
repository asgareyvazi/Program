        SETTING PROCEDURES FOR THE SSC VALVE.

1.  RIH HW DRILL PIPE ABOUT 25.000 LBS
   SUFFICIENT PIPE WEIGHT MUST BE BELOW THE PACKER TO SET THE PACKER
   ELEMENT AND COMPENSATE FOR ANY ANTICIPATED PRESSURE BUILDUP.

2. MAKE UP THE SSC VALVE ON A SINGLE OR DOUBLE PIPE JOINT.

3. MAKE UP THE RTTS PACKER ON THE BOTTOM OF THE SSC VALVE .

4.  TIGHTEN THE CONNECTION AND JAM NUT ON THE RTTS PACKER

5.  RIH RTTR AND SSC VALVE AT A SAFE DEPTH @ 100 M.

6.  SET THE RTTS PACKER.

7.  ROTATE TO THE LEFT 22 TURNS TO RELEASE SEAL MANDREL.

8.  POOH/ REMOVE THIS PART OF DRILL PIPE FROM THE WELL.

9.  CLOSE THE BOP.

10. LAYDOWN STINGER/ VALVE MANDREL.



       RETRIEVING PROCEDURE FOR THE SSC VALVE .

           TO RESUME NORMAL OPERATION IN THE WELL.

1.  MAKE UP THE SEAL MANDREL ON THE DRILL PIPE

2.  OPEN THE BLOWOUT PREVENTER/ BOP.

3.  RIH THE PIPE IN WELL,BEING VERY CAUTIOUS AS THE SEAL MANDREL
   APPROACHES THE SSC VALVE. IT IS VERRY IMPORTANT THAT THE MANDREL
   NOT BE JAMMED INTO THE VALVE.

4.  INSTALL VALVE ( TO CLOSE DRILL PIPE ) AND GAUGE .

5.  ROTATE TO THE RIGHT WITH 22 TURNS TO ENGAGE AND OPEN THE SSC VALVE

6.  OBSERVE THE WELL PRESSURE AT THE TOP OF THE DRILL PIPE.

7.  UNSET THE RTTS PACKER AND IF NECESSARY, CIRCULATE THE WELL BEFORE
   REMOVING THE SSC VALVE AND PACKER.

8.  POOH AND JOB WAS COMPLETED .
