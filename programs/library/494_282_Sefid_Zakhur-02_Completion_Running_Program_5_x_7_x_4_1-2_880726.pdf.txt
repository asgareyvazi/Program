        COMPLETION STRING PROGRAM

              «Sefid Zakhur-02 / RIG 49 fath»



Important Notes

    1) Run 9 5/8" scraper to scrap packer depth at 3675 m (+/- 30 m).


A-Completion Surface Tests

    1)  Drift and measure OD, ID and length of all completion equipment and

        accessories.

    2) Check sealing area and different sections of x-mas tree, tubing hanger

      and 2 way check valve.

    3) Wash all tubing joints with fresh water on rack.

    4) Check pin and box of all tubing joints and down-hole equipment for any

      damages on threads and sealing area and clean them by diesel.

    5) Measure distance between rig floor and top of tubing head spool.

    6) Measure length of tubing hanger and its neck.

    7) Prepare proper landing joint.

    8) Use proper dope as adequate thin layer on pin section of joints, to avoid

       dropping inside string on the top of plug.

    9) To pull up tubing joints and any other down-hole tools use protector (or

       proper pup joint / sub).

    10) To pull up tools with 2 ends boxes, use 1 joint tubing or proper pup joint

            / sub.

    11) Set dummy in 3SV L/N, connect C/L and pressure test against dummy to

      5000 for 15 minutes.

    12) Operate SSD once.

    13) Pressure test SSD & SPM to 4500 psi for 15 minutes.

    14) Set plug in No Go L/N (3.313” RPT) and pressure test to 4500 psi for 15

       minutes.

    15) Set expansion joint to full open position, pressure test to 4500 psi for 15

       minutes and set shear pins.


Notes:

  •  Compare OD of plug, running/pulling tools, dummy and 2 way check valve

     with all down hole equipment, hole sizes of x-mas tree and surface

    equipment to be rigged up on the x-mas tree.
  •   If it is necessary to change the plug type mentioned in plan, compare OD of

    new one with ID of all down-hole equipment in particular SSD, packer, 3SV

    L/N and dummy.


B-Completion Running Procedure:

          •  Remove Wear Bushing.
          •  Rig up completion running tools.

     1. RIH with Mule Shoe, 1 Jt TBG, No Go L/Nipple and etc. as per attached
         tally up to 3SV L/Nipple (while Plug is set in No Go L/Nipple).

    Note: Fill up 100 m on top of Plug with Hi-Vis corrosion inhibited polymer
     pill (≥150 sec) and the rest joints with completion fluid.
   Then fill up each 10 joints tubing with 92 pcf calcium bromide
   (same weight as mud).

     2. Connect Control Line to 3SV L/Nipple and test to 5000 psi against
     Dummy for 15 minute Band Control Line straps to tighten same against
       tubing.

     3.  Continue RIH as per tally, pick up Tubing Hanger, connect Control Line
        to same and test to 4500 psi for 15 minute

     4. P/U 5” Landing Joint and connect to Tubing Hanger.

 5. R/U pump unit.

 6.  Flush and clean Tubing Head Spool with water (or air).

 7. Land Tubing Hanger so that just touch Tubing Head Spool.

 8. Space out and pull out the string 3 m up (for total stroke of 20 ft).

 9.  Pressurize tubing to 4500 psi step by step (500 psi on each step @ 3
    minute) and monitor tubing annulus at same time (open Tubing Head
    Spool side outlets).

Note: While pressurizing inside tubing, some back flow will be observed
through Tubing Head Spool side outlet Valves, it should be stopped after a
while.

 10. Release pressure and over pull the string with 20,000 lbs.

 11. Apply proper downward weight to shear off Expansion Joint Shear Pins,
    then set Tubing Hanger at its position in Tubing Head Spool.

 12. Tighten Tie Down screws.

 13. Test inside tubing to 4500 psi for 15 minute, close top Rams and test
   Tubing Hanger to 4500 psi for 15 minute

 14. Test annulus to 2000 psi for 15 minute, and back off Landing Joint.

 15. Set Two-Way Check Valve inside Tubing Hanger. Pressure test to 6500
    psi for 15 minute

 16. N/D BOP.

 17. Check Tubing Hanger for any damages. Pressure test Control Line to
   4500 psi against Dummy for 15 minute

 18. N/U X-Mas Tree as per program. Pressure test same to 10000 psi for 15
   minute

 19. Pull out Two-Way Check Valve.

 20. Connect Flow Line and Chock Manifold to X-Mas Tree and R/U
    Wireline Pressure Control Equipment. Test X-Mas Tree, Chock
    Manifold, Flow Line and Wireline Pressure Control Equipment to 10000
    psi for 15 minute.

 21. Remove dummy from 3SV L/N.

    22. Open SSD.

    23. Set dummy in 3SV L/N.

    24. Displace annulus volume with 76 pcf calcium cloride. Displacement
        direction is from tubing through annulus.

    25. Remove dummy from 3SV L/N.

    26. Close SSD.

    27. Remove prong and plug from No Go L/N.

    28. Monitor wellhead or flow line pressure.

    29. Set dummy in 3SV L/N.

    30. Flow the well through proper choke size to clean up it.

    31. Having cleaned well, record flowing pressures (wellhead, burning line, 7"
      x 9 5/8" annulus and 9 5/8" x 13 3/8" annulus) through 3 different choke
         sizes. Fill out flow sheet.

    32. Close the Well.

    33. Remove Dummy.

    34. Flush Control Line with hydraulic oil.

    35. Install 3SV and pressure test C/L to 4500 psi for 15 minutes.

    36. Release pressure above 3SV to zero.

    37. Monitor X-mas Tree pressure for any 3SV leakage.

    38. Fill up above 3SV with corrosion inhibited diesel.

    39. Close all Valves except bottom master valve and 2SV.

    40. Release Rig.
