1. RIH with 12 ¼” slick BHA #1 no jets. Logging weights above and below jars.

2.  Take slow pump rates down the drill string and up the annulus. Take slow pump rates

   down the drill string and up the choke line.

3.  Continue drill out cement and rat hole to 1879m with 9.6 ppg mud.

4.  Observe well for 1 hrs, if no loss no gain displace hole with sea water.

5.  Observe 1 hrs, if no gain contiue drilling to end of rat hole (1885m). Use high-vis pill

    to sweep the hole clean as required.

6.  Observe the well by flow checking one hour on the trip tank if all ok.

7.  Continue drilling formation from 1885m to 1903m (18m) on a one mud pit system

    pit# 2 or pit #3 and adjust mud weight with hole situation.(When drilling you must use

    the active pits not reseve pits. Pit 2 and pit 3 are active pits as they have the fast barite

   mixer in them).

8.  Circulate bottoms up and flow check on trip tank..

9. POOH and change BHA# 1 for BHA# 2 and RIH with BHA#2 to 1903m

10. Continue drilling formation from 1903m to TD @ ±2057m with BHA No. 2

11. Note: (Reference to top formation received from G&G department, SC500 top @

   2467m and possibility of gas presence @ 2327m). Adjust Mud weight to suit the well

    situation. Pump high-vis pill to sweep hole as required. (Well site geoligest shall

   confirm the final casing point)

12. Circulate bottoms up and flow check on trip tank 30min.

13. POOH with BHA #2 and RIH with BHA #3 (including Under Reamer) to TD

  NOTE: Pay close attention and do not open under reamer's arms while reamer is still

    in the casing.

14. Open the under reamer arms when the bit depth @ 1888m and open up the 12 1/4"

   hole to 14 3/4" from ±1880.5m to ±2049.5 (under reamer depth) with BHA No. 3. Use

    high-vis pills to sweep the hole as required. (Well site geoligest shall confirm the

    final casing point).

15. Circulate bottms up untill shakers are clean.

16. Wiper trip to be discussed depending on the hole condition.
