5. Cementing Procedure
5.1 Pre-Job

(1) Prepare Lead cement 105t, Tail cement 25t.

(2) Fill up Tanks with 1000bbls Fresh Water for mixing Fluid.

(3) Mix 700bbls solution in water tank for lead slurry and spacer. Mix 127bbls solution in water tank for
     tail slurry.
