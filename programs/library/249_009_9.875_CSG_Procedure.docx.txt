
9 7/8” CSG RUNNING PROCEDURE

## San Jacinto
OFFLINE:
Buckup Wea float equip on 9 7/8..
Buckup WEA cmt std and load darts. DONE.
Check to see if link tilt clamps fit rental bails.
Check bails for fit on OES equipment.
Install stop collars on pipe rack.
With Thd inspector, clean thds on pipe rack.
Clean pit and mix spacer.
Rabbit Pipe in the Derrick.
TIH HO assy.  Slip & cut drill line before going through BOP’s.   Set WB GIH.   While TIH, close blinds (if any losses) and boost riser 1.5 vol.
Stage in hole, brk circ at 12000’, 14000, and wash final 3 stds down.
Bring pumps on slow to drlg rate (900 gpm) over 15 min.  Then bring Boost on.
Circulate above well head on stages.
CIRC btms up.  Thin mud to 15/20 yp.     Be prepared for gas at surface.  Slow pumps down hole if excessive gas >3000 units.  POOH.
R/U CSG tool, c/o bails to 22’ 500T (Quality).     P/U CSG.
Mix Halco spacer.  Build _+/-125 usable bbls_ WBM for push in front of cmt.
Build WBM for  displacing 9 7/8 csg only with riser margin & inhibitors included.
P/U guide shoe, 3 JTs Csg,  FC.  Install Turbulizer & Centralizer on each jt w/stop collar across pay sands – see talley sheet.
Baker lock to top of FC.  Max flow rate thru FE is 2-3 BPM.
RIH as per CSG Tally: ______-Jts 9 7/8” total . (______-JTs left out)
FMC Hanger Assembly.
XO – 20’ 5 7/8” PUP – 5 7/8” 47.34# (.075 w) single + XO + 10’ 6 5/8 pup.
Install OES DP flow back tool.
8-STDs of Rental 27.7# 6 5/8” DP.
75 std of TO 40.9# Landing string.
CMT STD.
CSG is Auto Fill, confirm pipe is filling.
Mud Engineer to document mud losses (Running & Cementing CSG)
TIH w/CSG @ +  1.5-min/std. Slack off and adjust speed accordingly.  CSG is Auto Fill, confirm Drill Pipe is filling.  *Easy on and off slips.
Land CSG hanger in well head:
Close FC W/5-7 BPM flow rate.
Circulate csg and DP vol – hold PJSM.
R/U Halliburton CMT lines.  Test to 5000 psi.
Pump spacer with Halco.  Drop btm dart.   Mix and pump +/-640 sx Premium Blended cmt w/super CBL then _____sx of Premium + liq add’s .  Kick out top dart with  10 bbl spacer.  Turn over to rig to finish displacement:
Halco to remain lined up on cmt job for data collection.
Pump ________bbls /  __________stks of WBM to displace dart to Plugs.
Shear top plug with __________psi.  Reset one counter.
Continue displaceing with a total of  _________ bbl of WBM    _________ Stks.
Switch to SBM to displace DP volume to wellhead.
Bump plugs with 500 psi over pump pressure.
Check floats with Halco.
Set Seal Assembly:
30K DN, Rotate 4-turns to the right.
Drill string will drop 8”.
Break circulation with Halliburton.
Close Pipe rams.
Pressure up to 7000-psi for 2-minutes.
Bleed the pressure off then go to test pressure.
Open rams and go to straight pull on DP.
PU FMC spacer assy with 6 stds 5” below.  Run ____ jts HWDP on top.  GIH & set w/35k.  Pump pills and displace riser with sea water.
Test casing to  _______ psi and spot surface plug.  Pull up to top of plug and reverse out.  POOH.
