                                              Scraper and Polish Mill Assembly




                                      DESIGN AND OPERATION

                        This one trip Scraper, Top Dress and Polish mill is an important part of the liner hanger
                      “Monobore” system.
                   Due to the design, it eliminates the need for a costly two trip system where the scraper
                    and top dress would be run on separate runs.

                    The upper Scraper complete with top dress mill is designed to scrape the 9 5/8” casing
                         without the need for any rotation. It has 360 Degrees cleaning capabilities due to the
                          positioning of the blades. The scraper will clean the casing to within 30 cms of the top
                           of the liner hanger.
                    The top dress part of the scraper is a brass no-go ring that allows the scraper to be sat
                   down on top of the liner hanger without damage occurring to the liner hanger. It also
                        gives a positive indication when pulled to surface.

                    The assembly is run in hole on drill pipe until the top dress mill sits on top of the liner
                       hanger. The string is then reciprocated to clean any cement or debris that may have
                    been left down hole inside the lower PBR.

                   Once  it has been confirmed that the lower PBR is clean, the assembly can be pulled
                        out of hole.
