                Rig Move Procedures and installation

    Move to a new location
    1.  Checking and securing the safety access to the new location.
    2.  Communicating with the Company related about the underground power
       cables before burying and drilling in any anchor.
    3.  Checking the high voltage power lines on the location. Working within 5
       meters from them is prohibited.
    4.  Checking all location if need construction or preparing.
    5.  Checking and making sure that the ground under the Rig structure shall
      be level off, firm and strong. Equipment pins and safety pins shall be in
        places.
    6.  Equipment on location shall be positioned reasonably. Mud tank and
       shacks to be placed more than 30 meters away from wellhead, BOP
        controller system and generator 25 meters away from wellhead, Mud
     pump apart from Mud tank more than 5 meters.
    7.  Standing behind the rig or trucks is prohibited during the night shipment
      from one location to another.
    8. Check the tubing and casing pressure.
    9.  Checking guy lines should be installed.



               Procedures of Rig up & Rig down
R/U:
   1- Pre –safety meeting and distribute duty for crew members.
   2- Spot the rig on base flat metal.
   3- Mechanic has to check hydraulic and air system.
   4- Lifting rig carrier on base jacks (the load of rig must be on base jacks).
   5- Release guy lines for rig carrier.
   6- Distribute crew member to monitor handling lines and electric cable while
      perform RU.
   7- Mechanic raises the mast (part-1).
   8- Put safety derrick pins on the right position to secure the derrick.
   9- Pull down two small winch lines about 10m.
   10- Release brake and let swab line down smoothly.
   11- Mechanic Extend the mast (part-2), so driller must be control the brake
     and let traveling blocks go down slowly.

   13- Install support jacks.
   14- Secure carrier lines (derrick back lines) and guy lines.

R/D:
   1- Pre –safety meeting and distribute duty for crew members.
   2- Prepare monkey broad, disconnect electrical cable and derrick safety pins.
   3- Release guy lines and derrick back lines.
   4- Distribute crew member to monitor handling lines and to be sure it not
      hanging.
   5- Raise traveling blocks near crown.
   6- Mechanic retracts the mast (part-2).
   7- Release derrick safety pins.
   8- Pull up the swab line and wenches lines slowly.
   9- Release support jacks.
   10- Lower mast (part-1) down.
   11- Arrange handling lines on rig carrier.
   12- Release basic jacks.

                     Well Killing Procedure

  Killing Well With Normally Circulation:
 The killing fluid should be pumped from tubing then turn back from annulus
 between tubing and casing to circulate. This method would be favorable to
 protect pay zone.
 1. The measured killing fluid density, viscosity and usage on the well site
     should meet the demand of program.
 2. As to wells with high gas production and without sand produced the flow
     line with needle valve should be applied to control the drainage of gas.
 3. The check valve at inlet and needle valve at outlet should be installed
    when  connecting  operational  lines  for  killing  well  with  normally
     circulation.
 4.  Pressure test inlet line of killing well operation without leakage in 5
    minutes to be qualified.
 5. The well cleanup with water to release gas should be more than one and
     half round.
 6. The pump rate should be no less than 500 L/min while flushing the well
    and the pump couldn’t be stopped during circulation.
 7.  Needle valve could be applied to control the balance between inlet
    pumping rate and outlet pumping rate during circulation.
 8. The killing fluid meeting the demand of design should be used to kill
     well with normal circulation immediately after flushing well with clean
     water one round.
 9. The difference between measured killing fluid density at inlet and at
     outlet should be less than 0.02 Kg/m3 after one round well killing with
    normally circulation.
 10. Well killing is qualified when no overflow observed at both inlet and
      outlet.

  Killing Well With Reverse Circulation:

1. The killing fluid is pumping from annulus between tubing and casing and
   turn back from tubing to circulate. This method  is applicable to high
   pressure oil wells with large production  it’s difficult to apply positive
   circulation.
  2. Killing lines and working procedures for reverse circulation operation is
  the same to positive circulation operation.

                   Swabbing Procedure

 Preparation:
1. The ID of swabbing string should be of same specification.
2. The drift check with standard drift gauge for tubing should be conducted
    before RIH swabbing string.
3. The total length of stuffing box and lubricators should be at least 2
   meters more than the length from rope socket to swab cup, which should
   be safe and reliable to prevent blowout.

 Operation Procedures:
1.  Link braided line, rope socket and sinker bar, and connect lubricators.
2.  Connect swab mandrel without swabbing cup to sinker bar to check the
   ID of tubing and 6 marks shall be made on the swab line (3 marks when
    the in lubricator, 2 marks after 50m and one marks after 100m from first
   marks).
3.  Link swab cup after POOH sink bar, then put into swabbing head.
   Continue RIH swab after connecting lubricators with the speed no more
   than 150m per min. The submergence should be controlled within 200m.
4.  It should not be run too deep when unclear of liquid depth and be tried
    gradually to deepen.
5. Check the lubricators once every 5 times swabbing and make sure the
   packing of lubricators in good condition.
6.  If blowout is observed while swabbing then POOH until swab enters into
    lubricators and then close swab valve.
7. The pressure difference should not be too large when swabbing for sand
   produced formation and the uplifting speed should be reduced.


                 Squeezing Cement Procedures

1. RIH squeezing string to designed depth.
2.  Connect and flush surface lines, then pressure test 15-20MPa without
   leakage in 5-10 minutes to be qualified.
3.  Circulate down hole fluid, and check whether the surface system in good
    condition or not and to decrease down hole temperature.
4.  Conduct trivial squeezing with clean water to test water absorbing index
   and casing expansion rate (injective test to determine the feed rate).
5. Mix cementing slurry according to design.
6.  Normally displace designed volume of cementing slurry according to
   design requirement.
7.  Normally displace specific volume of displacement fluid according to
   design requirement and the displacement rate should be controlled to
   0.5m3/min.
8. POOH string to the position for reverse circulation.
9.  Reversely circulate to wash out extra cementing slurry.

10. POOH string to designed depth, and assemble stopcock and pressure
   gauge at the top of tubing string.
11. Multi-squeeze until the pressure stable, then close the casing valve to
   wait on cement.

                 Sand Flushing Procedure

1. RIH sand flushing string to tag sand level with one bevel edge at the
   bottom of the string.
2.  Uplift sand flushing bevel edge 3m above sand level. Lower down string
   slowly and smoothly after normal circulation with pump and the pump
    rate should meet the demand of working plan.
3.  Full circulation should be conducted for each joint with flushing time no
    less than 15 min.
4.  Each duty should cooperate with each other closely to control RIH speed
   according to pump pressure and outlet pump rate.
5. When over 5 joints are flushed continuously, the RIH could be continued
   only after flushing one round.
6. When malfunction happens to pump and the pump must be stopped to
    dispose, the string should be uplifted to 10m above initial sand level and
    trip up and down repeatedly.
7. Normal circulation must be kept when troubles happen to uplifting
   equipment.
8. When serious leakage of formation and no sand flushing fluid returning
    to surface are found during sand flushing operation, the flushing should
   be stopped. Uplift flushing string above initial sand level and trip up and
   down repeatedly. The job could be continued with methods such as wax
    ball plug-off, sand flush with united large rate pumps, and sand flush
   with gasified fluid…and so on.
9. The outlet pump rate should be controlled for the sand flush of high
    pressure flowing wells to be balanced with inlet pump rate so as to
    prevent blowout.
10. After sand flushing to designed depth or bottom hole which is the top
    surface of cement plug the pump rate should be kept above 400L per min
    to continue circulation. It is qualified after sand cut less than 0.2 percent.
   Then uplift string more than 20 m, then detect sand level repeatedly after
    waiting for sand settlement 4 hours later and record the depth.

                         Drill Cement Plug Procedures

1. Preparation:
1.1 Equipment
1.1.1 Lifting equipment: Workover rig, Downhole service rig.
1.1.2 Drill tool: hydraulic power swivel, downhole screw driller, or rotary table.
1.1.3 Circulating equipment: Cementing truck, Mud pump.
    1.1.4 Drill bit: OD of the bit (winged scraping bit) should be less 6-10mm
   than ID of the casing. Measure the ID, OD, length of the Bit, thread type.
1.1.5 Killing fluid (high pressure wells)
   Based on the well condition, prepare the killing fluid. The volume is 1.5 or 2
   times of the volume of the well bore.
1.2 Prepare pipe(tubing or drill pipe)
1.2.1 Based on the operation program, prepare the drilling pipe(tubing or drill
      pipe)
1.2.2 Pipe rack height is 0.3m above the ground.
     Measure the pipe 3 times by 3 persons, the difference  is  less than
     0.2m/1000m.
2. Operation:
2.1 Use downhole screw driller to drill cement plug
2.1.1  Before RIH the downhole screw driller, test it on surface.
2.1.2  The BHA as following (from bottom to top): bit (or winged scraping bit)
     + downhole screw driller + DC (heavy weight DP) +Drilling jar +lifting
       sub +tubing (or drill pipe).
2.1.3  The filter unit should be installed at surface in the circulating pipe.
2.1.4  Above 5m of the cement plug level, normally circulated  till stable,
       slowly lower the  string, WOB:5-10KN,  fluid annulus  return speed
       should be more than 0.8m/s.
2.2 Use power swivel to drill cement plug.
2.2.1  The BHA as following(from bottom to top): bit(or winged scraping bit)+
        Drill collar + Drilling jar + lifting sub + tubing(or drill pipe)+ power
       swivel
Above 5m of the cement plug level, normally circulated  till stable, start the
power swivel, after the swivel running normally, lower the string lowly, WOB:5-
10KN, 40-60rpm, fluid annulus return speed should be more than 0.8m/s.
2.3 Use rotary table to drill cement plug
2.3.1 The BHA as following(from bottom to top): bit(or winged scraping bit)+
        drill pipe+ Kelly pipe
2.3.2 Above 5m of the cement plug level, normally circulated till stable, start
      the rotary table, after the rotary table running normally, lower the string
      slowly, WOB:10-25KN, 60-120rpm, fluid annulus return speed should be
     more than 0.8m/s.
2.4 Quality requirement
2.4.1 Record the ID, OD and length of drill tool, pipe string, and draw the
      sketch map.

────────────────────────────────────────────
2.4.2 Pipe thread must be painted by pipe dope, the thread torque is in the range
      of the pipe required.
2.4.3 Be sure that there is no junk on top of cement plug level.
2.4.4  If use downhole screw driller, the drill fluid must be clean to meet the
      requirement of downhole screw driller.
2.4.5 Make a short trip every 3-5m of footage.
2.4.6 Avoid the casing damage.
2.4.7 The drill fluid must be compatible with the formation.
2.4.8 To the designed depth, RIH casing tool gauge or scraper to Drilled
      designed depth, and clean the wellbore with 1.5-2.0 times of wellbore
     volume fluid.

Drill out bridge plug procedures

1.   Preparation:
1.1 Prepare for the drill pipe and drill collar and crossover.
1.2 Prepare for the milling bit: wash over milling shoe, rock bit, flat-bottom
    mill, concave mill
1.3 Well control unit
1.4 Weight indicator, pressure gauge
1.5 Milling fluid.
2. Operation
2.1 Washover methods
2.1.1 BHA assembly (from bottom to top): washover milling shoe + washover
      pipe + junk basket + drill string (DC+DP).
2.1.2 At the very beginning of washover the BP, as following:
     Lower the string, put 10-20KN weight on the BP, marked on the drill pipe,
       pull up string 1-2m to make sure that the milling shoe is not contacted
      with the BP.
      Circulation and rotate the drill pipe at 60-100rpm, the fluid of returning
      speed in annulus is more than 0.8m/s.
      In 15 mins, put 10-20KN weight on BP gradually.
2.1.3 Washover the BP, WOB: 10-20KN, the rotary torque is no more than the
       string required torque.
2.1.4 After released the BP, pushed it to PBTD. If the PBTD is not required to
      leave junks, then RIH fishing tool to take out all junks.

2.2  Milling method
2.2.1 At the very beginning of wash over the BP, as following:
     Lower the string, put 20-40KN weight on the BP, marked on the drill pipe,
       pull up string to original weight.
      Circulation and rotate the drill pipe at 60-100rpm, the fluid of returning
      speed in annulus is more than 0.8m/s.
      In 15 mins, put 20-40KN weight on BP gradually.
2.2.2 Milling the BP, WOB: 20-40KN, the rotary torque is no more than the
       string required torque.
2.2.3 After released the BP, pushed it to PBTD. If the PBTD is not required to
      leave junks, then RIH fishing tool to take out all junks.
2.3 Rock bit (Drilling bit) Method:
2.3.1 BHA (from bottom to surface): rock bit + bit sub + drill collar + drilling jar
  + drill string.
2.3.2 beginning of BP drilling as following:
     Lower the string, put 10-20KN weight on the BP, marked on the drill pipe,
       pull up string 1-2m to make sure that the drilling bit is not contacted with
      the BP.
      Circulation and rotate the drill pipe at 60-100rpm, the fluid of returning
      speed in annulus is more than 0.8m/s.
      In 15 mins, put 10-20KN weight on BP gradually.

2.3.3  Drill out the BP, WOB: 10-20KN, the rotary torque is no more than the
       string required torque.
2.3.4 After released the BP, pushed it to PBTD. If the PBTD is not required to
      leave junks, then RIH fishing tool to take out all junks.

2.4  Quality requirement
2.4.1 If there is junks on top of the BP, it must be fished and make the BP level

2.4.2 The milling fluid must be compatible with the formation.
2.4.3 Before POOH, make a round circulation.

               Tubing Conveyed Perforating Procedures

Tubing Conveyed Perforating is running the perforation gun with tubing.
Procedure :
1. Get the well information from well operation program;
2. Get the perforation request from the well operation program;
3. Draw the Gun String sketch for the operation;
4. Prepare the Gun on surface;
5. Double check the Gun String before sent to wellsite;
6. Confirm the Gun String is well-assembled;
7. Make sure all the safety regulations are well performed;
8. Safety meeting is necessary before lifting the gun;
9. The Safety Space Gun must be long enough to ensure the gun under surface
  when make up the Fire Head;
10.Gun must be RIH with smoothly and keep the RIH speed in 10-15stands;
11.The Tubing and Annular must be  well covered  to  protect something
   unexpected drop in;
12.The fluid height both in Tubing and in Annular are enough to keep the gun
    fire safely;
13.GR/CCL depth correlation logging must be done for confirm the gun face the
   planning information;
14.BOP is well installed on the wellhead, function and pressure test must be
   done on the BOP facilities;
15.Drop bar or Pump to fire the gun with observing on the wellhead and casing
   gauge to detect the perforation on surface.
16.POOH perforated gun with smoothly.

       Downhole Tools Setting

                         Procedures


    [DBP (Drillable Bridge Plug) + CR(cement retainer)](7’’, 9 5/8’’):
   1- Reach to the setting depth put a mark above the holding slips
  Of the tubing and it will be a reference point.
   2- Pick up 2 feet.
   3- Turn to the right 15 turns.
   4- Back again to the setting depth
   5- Over pull 10 Tons in case of 7’’ casing & 15 Tons in case of 9 5/8’’casing
   6- Go back again to the Neutral weight of the Tubing.
   7- Apply down 10 Tons in case of 7’’ casing & 15 Tons in case of 9
       5/8’’casing.
   8- Go back again to the Neutral weight of the Tubing.
   9- Over pull 2 Tons.
   10- Turn to the right 4 turns until the weight indicator release the over pull.
  11-Move the tubing up and down until you sure the tool is free.
   12- Pressure test the Bridge plug to 1000 psi if there is no perforation
      above it.
     For Cement Retainer conduct the same procedures of DBP. To sting in
     go down and Apply between (2-4) tons, and start the injectivity test. To
       sting out apply over pull (2-4) tons.
