1.  General Information

    1.1 Field Introduction
Azadegan South Oil Field is situated in West Karoun province on a large long-axis anticline with N-S
trending with approximate dimensions about 20 km width and 60 km length near to Iran-Iraq border, to
the east of Yaran Oil Field.
The trap area of the south Azadegan is limited to 43.2x17.2 km. The Azadegan Oil field was discovered
in 1999, when Well AZN-1 was drilled vertically down to the Cretaceous Fahliyan Formation with total
depth of 4,643m. The results of this well, revealed the reservoir characteristics of the field. Following to
the result of the well no. AZN-1, another 5 exploration wells was drilled in different parts of this huge
field down to Fahliyan Formation. In the later stage the well No.10 assumed as exploration well for
evaluation of deeper formations. This well was drilled in 2008, down to the depth of 5340m (Neyriz
Fm.), mainly to evaluate the Jurassic formations.
Based on the exploration wells results, carbonate reservoir of Sarvak Formation is broadly developed in
Azadegan Field. This formation was penetrated in all of seven exploration wells (AZN-1, AZN-2, AZN-
3, AZN-4, AZN-5, AZN-6 and AZN-10). Clastic oil-bearing zones of Kazhdumi Formation developed in
main anticline of Azadegan Field, extending from north to center of South Azadegan. The Clastic oil-
bearing zones of Gadvan Formation developed only in crest area and south parts of South Azadegan
Filed, which was discovered by AZN-1 in crest and AZN-4 in south parts. The carbonates of Fahliyan
Formation also developed in Azadegan field, but the oil reservoir of this formation developed in South
part of South Azadegan.
The Azadegan Field is an elongated anticline, trending in N-S direction, belonging to "Arabian Trend".
The stratigraphy sequence of the field includes AghaJari, Gachsaran, Fahliyan, Pabdeh, Gurpi, Ilam,
Laffan, Sarvak, Kazhdumi, Dariyan, Gadvan and Fahliyan Formations, from top to bottom. This
sequence in well no AZN-10, the deepest up to now drilled well of the field, continues with Garau,
Gotnia, Najmah, Sargalu, Alan, Mus and Neyriz formations.
The Dominant lithofacies of Sarvak Formation are Rudist-bearing packstone and partially grainstone,
which contains abundant bioclasts. Some other facies like matrix-dominated wackestone and mudstone
are also found.
The AZADEGAN  Oilfield  is Located  in Khuzestan Province and  is Approximately Located 80
Kilometers West of Ahwaz City, near to Iran-Iraq Border. It is in The Northwest of The YADAVARAN
Field and in The West of JUFEYR Field. The Average Monthly Temperature is 45ºc in summer, and
Highest Temperature is 52ºc. The Lowest Temperature is -4.4ºc in winter. Maximum Rainfall is 78 mm
in February, Winter Season.

    1.6 Formation Description

A brief description of the Sarvak units are as following:

Fahliyan Formation

This formation is start with light gray, light green gray, soft Limestone interbedded with green gray, soft,
sticky Marl, green gray, light gray, soft, Argillaceous Limestone and dark green gray, soft, sticky Clay
stone beds. This formation at continue is consisting of buff white light gray, light brown, brown, medium
hard, hard, densely, slightly argillaceous Limestone and off white, white gray, medium hard Chalky
Limestone beds.

    1.8 Drilling History

The AZNS-010 well was drilled from 25-June-2008 to 23-March-2010 to total depth 5340m and
abandoned with cement plugs as describe in the following.

26" Hole Section Summary:

This section was drilled from surface to 152.5m with 26" bit & Mw 65pcf, cased with 20"(K-55, 133#) at
151m with 240bbl cement & S.W 115pcf, cement return observed, well equipped by 20-3/4" – 3000psi
Wellhead & related BOP & Acc.

17-1/2" Hole Section Summary:

This section was drilled with 17-1/2" bit to 1502m with gradual increase of Mw f/74 to 90pcf because of
tight hole, pressure drop observed several times & a fishing Op. with overshot  at 1264m, took
CR/SONIC/CALLIPER/CDR logs, cased with 13-3/8" (N-80, 72#, 8RD&BTC) at 1502m with S.W
115pcf, return cement observed, well equipped by 13-5/8" – 5000 psi Wellhead & related BOP & Acc.

12" Hole Section Summary:

This section was drilled with 12" bit to 2731m with 0-8BPH losses and Mw 85pcf, waiting due to repair
from GWDC Company, mud loss observed amount of 45 & 50BPH at 2690 & 2731m, loss controlled
with LCM   Pill,  continue  drilling  to  3002m  with  68BPH  losses,  condition   trip &  took
GR/CNT/LDT/HNGS/BHC/HRLA/MSFL logs in two runs, continue drilling to 3857m with 0-6BPH
losses and gradual decrease of Mw f/85 to 84pcf, GR/WSTTCDR and SDL/DTD/GTET/CSNG/DSN and
GR/MSFL/DLL were taken in 3 three runs, 9-5/8" casing (L-80, T-95, 53.5#, SEC) was run to 3854m,

5.  Sequence of Operation

    5.1  Main Objective
Based on test results the decision has been made to complete the well for Fahliyan production. In order to
achieve these, following steps will be performed.
1. Move and spud the rig over well.
2. Check & record all wellhead pressures.
Note #1: If wellhead pressure is not zero, follow the contingency plan
Contingency plan: Release pressure and monitor the pressure, if wellhead pressure is not zero, the
contractor shall inform the client. The remedial plan will be prepare in advance by operators and
followed after the client's confirmation.
Note #3: Ensure that adequate fishing tools are available.
Note #4: Enough mud material, LCM & cement should be available.
Note #5: Ensure that all drilling tools & equipment are on well site & are serviceable condition.
3. Remove 11” blind flange and change wellhead section C with new one.
4.  Nipple up BOPs set up 13-5/8" plug tester and pressure test same. Perform wellhead pressure
integrity test with 9-5/8" cup tester.
 All pressure test shall be performed with both low pressure test, usually 300 psi and high pressure test,
usually to the rated value6500 psi for wellhead and 70% of WP (7500 psi) for annular BOP. All high
pressure tests should be maintained for 10-30 minutes, and low pressure test 5 minutes.
Note #6: When casing cup tester is used, the above test pressure should not exceed 80% of the
internal yield pressure of the exposed casing. Wellhead side outlets below the test tool must be open
during a test to avoid the risk of pressure increase below the test tool if there is a leak.
5.  Displace oil base mud with 106 pcf kill mud.
6. RIH with 8-1/2" BHA & D/O cement plug#1(1378-1502m), continue D/O cement plug#2 (3630-
3707m). Tag 7” liner lap. Circulate till shakers clean, POOH to surface.
Note #7: D/O last 10m of first cement plug by closed system and circulation through the chock
manifold.
7. RIH W/ 5-7/8” Bit and tag cement plug#3 at 4663m, D/O cement plug #3, POOH to surface & L/D
bit.
8. RIH with 5-7/8" Center Cut Bit, put CMT plug from 4663m to top of Garau (base of Fahliyan,
   4392m), tag top of cement plug @ 4392m, Wash Down to Get Hard Cement POOH to surface.
9. RIH 7” & 9-5/8" tandem scraper to 4392m & scrap inside 9-5/8" CSG & 7" liner, CBU, POOH to
surface.
10. RIH with wireline logging tools (GR/CCLCBL/VDL/SBT or CAST or USIT) inside 7" liner, perform
    logging, POOH.

 11.  Waste Management

      11.1 Scope of work

    11.1.1   Drilling waste management design
   The contractor is responsible for designing the optimum practices for treating the produced
   waste during the operation. The initial technical waste management proposal should be
   included the practices for minimizing, treating and recovering of the wastes.
   According to the below categories:
         .  Water based mud containing minerals and natural synthetic polymers, and etc.
         .  Cuttings contaminated with WBM.
         .  Oil Based Mud/ Oil Emulsion Mud.
         .  Cuttings contaminated with OBM.

   The contractor is encouraged to offer possible practices to minimize the drilling fluids waste
   and its regeneration.

     11.1.1.1   WATER BASED MUD CONTAINING MINERALS, NATURALS AND
       SYNTHETIC POLYMERS
     Primary operation

   The proposed primary solids control package should be able  to control  drilling  fluid
    properties, reducing of mud materials usage against conventional solid control equipment's,
   and significantly reduce the number of excess fluids generated throughout drilling each phase
   of the well.

     Secondary Operation

   All excess mud and spillage (water based) should be collected in rig site pits and free surface
    fluid should be treated using a built for propose flocculation station (has to join with
   centrifuge) consisting of integral dosing and pumps along with a centrifuge for efficient,
   quick and controlled separation of the solids before the cleaned fluids is returned to active
   system or buffer tanks.
   This system should be able to remove colloidal sized particles that cannot be removed with
   primary solids control system.
   This system may use to clean up the remaining water-based mud or decrease weight of oil
   base mud at the end of the drilling fluids programmed.

     11.1.1.4    CUTTINGS CONTAMINATED WITH OBM
   Cuttings that are not eligible for spreading should be stabilized with OSEII method to ensure
   a stable mix, then buried and dispose according to environmental policy.
     BPD I Method

  BIOLOGICAL PARD DRILLERS I is a unique Bio catalytic System of preformed multi-
   enzyme liquid concentrate. BPD stimulates and accelerates natural biological reactions.
  When combined with fresh or salt water and oxygen, BPD will cause crude oil and other
   organic substances to rapidly decompose; eventually biodegrading them to carbon dioxide
   and water.
  BPD is non-toxic to humans, animals, plants and marine life. It is nonpoisonous, even if
   accidentally ingested. It is non-irritating to the most sensitive skin. BPD contains no known
   allergens to cause skin, respiratory or other allergic reactions. Birds bathed in BPD should be
   quarantined until their own natural oils are restored.
   Once BPD is applied to a hydrocarbon spill, the enzymes and other product constituents start
   emulsification  and  solubilization  of  the  hydrocarbon  substrate.  Emulsification  and
   solubilization generally take from a few minutes up to a few hours for weathered heavy-end
   hydrocarbons, once BPD is applied with a temperature of 40 degrees F. or greater. Once
   solubilization is completed, the hydrocarbon substrate is less toxic (and the hazard of fire is
   diminished) so the enhanced - naturally occurring bacteria will have a higher affinity for the
   solubilized hydrocarbon substrate.

 11.3.4  High Performance Filter Unit
Filtration plays an important role in the natural treatment of waste water as it percolates through the soil.
It is also a major part of most water treatment. Groundwater that has been softened, or treated through
iron and manganese oxidation, requires filtration to remove floc created by coagulation or oxidation
processes. Since surface water is subject to run-off and does not undergo natural filtration.
it must be filtered to remove particles and impurities. Filtration can be compared to a sieve or micro-
strainer that traps suspended material between the grains of filter media. However, since most suspended
particles can easily pass through the spaces between grains of the filter media, straining is the least
important process in filtration. Filtration primarily depends on a combination of complex physical and
chemical mechanisms, the most important being adsorption. Adsorption  is the process of particles
sticking onto the surface of the individual filter grains or onto the previously deposited materials. Forces
that  attract and hold particles to the grains are the same as those that work in coagulation and
flocculation. In fact, coagulation and flocculation may occur in the filter bed, especially if coagulation
and flocculation before filtration was not properly controlled. Incomplete coagulation can cause serious
problems in filter operation.

    With shut-in casing pressure lower than 1,000psi, while snubbing with annular preventer, drill tools

     After shut off the annular preventer or ram preventer, with the shut-in casing pressure not greater

Treatment of loss of circulation
Before cementing non-target zone or pay zone, if loss of circulation occurs during drilling, POOH shall
be carried to run open ended drill pipe for cement plug job if condition allows. During RIH, mud shall be
filled from annulus to balance formation pressure to avoid blowout.
Treatment of loss of circulation during drilling after production casing cemented
Mud level not at wellhead after loss of circulation. Stop drilling, pick up TDS or Kelly, stop pumps and
observe with moving the drill string and prepare well control related work. Each 10 minutes, mud with
density no lower than the drilling weight shall be reversely filled up from the annulus. Each time, the
filling up shall be 1~2m3, or continuous reserve injecting mud with small speed to maintain that the
column pressure of mud in wellbore is higher than the formation pressure. POOH to casing shoe or
safety interval for observation. Additional treatment shall be made as per the situation of loss of
circulation.
Mud level at wellhead after loss of circulation. Stop drilling, pick up TDS or Kelly, stop pumps and
move the drill string to observe with well control well prepared. When the well is static, pull the bit
upward to the casing shoe or safe interval for observation. If no anomaly is observed, circulate with slow
pump rate. The measures such as plugging back job, POOH or condition mud etc. shall be carried out as
per the situation of loss of circulation. If loss of circulation occurs to pay zone, mud weight shall be
acquired by static pressure. After pulling out to casing shoe and observing for one tripping cycle, when
no more loss of circulation occurs after running to bottom, drilling operation can be resumed.
Well control for E logging, cementing and DST
Before E logging, down hole condition shall be stable and static. If it takes long time for electrical
logging, one more condition trip shall be conducted between logging. Shooting nipple is required for
logging. Logging service shall be furnished with tools for cutting the cables.
Continuously fill the well during performing wireline logging, and keep closely monitoring for the well
via trip tank.
Before running casing, ensure the well is in static condition without any flowing. Pipe ram shall be
changed to casing ram with proper size. Casing circulating head or crossover sub between casing and in
used DP shall be kept at rig floor at all times during casing running.

13.  H2S Monitoring & Prevention Measure

    13.1 Monitoring H2S
 Somewhere on the well site is apt to accumulate H2S, such as mud pit, shale shaker, and drilling
 platform, should be installed one set of. Make sure H2S monitor and alarm system can be used. Alarm
 threshold setting for H2S warning indicator: the first warning level is 10mg/m3, unable warning sound,
  just show present value of H2S content to operators. The second warning level is 20mg/m3, when the
 H2S content in air exceed 20mg/m3, H2S warning indicator can give an alarm automatically, and
 warning sound should be heard by everyone work in well site. The audible alarm should be installed on
 Kelly board.
 Equip enough gas masks on drilling platform, shaker screen (total amount should large than 24), and
 equip 12 sets of 30min positive pressure Air Aspirators 2 sets of portable H2S monitor and appropriative
 H2S warning indicator. Arrange a person specially assigned for taking charge  all the protective
 apparatus. This man must do periodical check to keep these equipment in a good condition.
 H2S monitor should be done weekly check and compulsory examination.
 After drilling into the pay zone, strengthen the detection of H2S content in mud.

    13.2 Measures for H2S prevention
 Windsocks shall be set on the drill floor and in the monsoon direction or/and prevailing wind direction of
 Azadegan oil field, off well site. In emergency, employees must follow the evacuation procedure.
 Explosion-proof blowers shall be installed around the rig, shale shaker, and drilling fluid tanks to blow
 away toxic and flammable gas at the well site.
 The rig crew in the H2S area shall be furnished with H2S monitors and adequate protective apparatus.
 Everybody shall be able to use, maintain, and check the apparatus.
 The technicians of rig crew are responsible for H2S proof training and the tool pusher is in charge of the
 supervision and examination.
 The pH value of drilling fluid in the H2S area shall be kept over 9.5. Pay attention to measurement of
 H2S concentration in drilling fluid. The H2S concentration shall be kept at less than 50mg/cm3 by using
 sulphur removal agents and degassers.
 Personnel safety and protection shall be carried out according to SY/T5087 during operations in polluted
 areas where H2S content exceeds the critical concentration.
 When the rig crew makes decision to bleed and fire because well control cannot be performed due to well
  site conditions, operations shall be performed according to procedures in SY/T5087.
