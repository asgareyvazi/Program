1  WELL SUMMARY


       Location

  The Sepehr-Jufair field (Operation Area) is located in Khuzestan province with about 40km distance in
   the west of Ahwaz City. The Sepehr-Jufair field (Operation Area) covers about 352 square kilometers
   area with approximately 23 kilometers long and 27 kilometers wide, it is located in vicinity of South
  Azadegan and Yadavaran giant fields. The operation area does not have any geological surface
   impression and was explored initially by 2D seismic survey in 1976. The explored oil reservoirs from
   different geological strata show economic hydrocarbon potential, which are developed firmly in the
   Sepehr-Jufair field and nearby oil fields.

   This drilling program will focus on well SPH-05 that is drilled reference to well SPH-01 that was drilled
   in the central part of the Sepehr area. The proposed Well SPH-05 is drilled in the major Sepehr
   culmination as a production well. This well is the fifth well that is drilled by Tadbir company in Sepehr
   area, located in north of well SPH-01 with around 2090 m distance. The main purpose for designing of
   this well is production oil from Lower Fahliyan reservoir.

   Well, SPH-05 has been drilled down to lower part of Fahliyan Formation (4670 meters deep) for
   production of Lower Fahliyan reservoirs.

        Site Description

The nearest well to the proposed SPH-05 well location is SPH-01, which was drilled in 2011 and located in
adjacent with the Jufair village. This appraisal well (SPH-01) was drilled in order to evaluate the
hydrocarbon potential possibility of Bangestan and Khami Groups. This well was drilled from surface
(Aghajari formation) to the near bottom of the Fahliyan formation (Lower Fahliyan reservoir layers) and
finally was penetrated into the depth of 4,577m (FTD). After perforating and conducting the production
tests, respectively from bottom to the top, Lower Fahliyan, Lower Gadvan and Middle Ilam intervals were
evaluated in terms of economic hydrocarbon potential. Based on the achieved results, some layers of the
Lower Fahliyan formation and Lower Gadvan Sands show outstanding capability.

   2   SECTION SUMMARIES


         32ʺ Hole Section

    32” Hole section was planned to be drilled to 110 m with 2 runs; run and cement 26” conductor Pipe.

    However, IN 2 BHA Run, one to drill to 113 m and other as tandem to enlarge section to 32” hole

     section size was done successfully in approximate 4.2 days.


         24” Hole Section

     In next section, plan was to drill 24” hole section to 1529m & run and cement 18 5/8” surface casing.

    However, in 3 BHA Run (BHA #3 to BHA #5), one of them for drill out and clean inside casing 26” and

     drilling to 556m, one of them for drilled as 24” hole to 1503m in an actual and last BHA in this hole

    (BHA #05) as slick BHA for conditioning hole prior to run casing and casing was run to 1498 m. About

    127 hrs time taken to condition hole, which can be the main reason for the lack of mud material such

    as starch & KCl and the next reason for not ream/back-ream each drill stand while drilling hole. The

    program for complete this hole based on drilling program was about 22.5 days. This hole was about

    4 days ahead of the schedule and on the other hand we have progressed about 6 days from the hole

    time schedule. The 24” hole section size was done successfully in approximate 19 days.


         17” Hole Section

     In plan the next hole was to drill 17” hole section to 1963m & run and cement 13 3/8” intermediate

     casing. However, in 2 BHA Runs (BHA #6 and BHA #7), one of them for drill out and clean inside casing

    18-5/8” and drilling to 1963m (STD) and another one as slick BHA for conditioning hole prior to run

     casing. About 108 hrs time taken to condition hole, which can be the main reason for the lack of mud

    material such as starch & KCl and the next reason for not ream/back-ream each drill stand while

     drilling hole. The program for complete this hole based on drilling program was about 23 days. This

    hole was about 8 days ahead of the schedule and on the other hand we have progressed about 6 days

    from the whole-time schedule. The 17” hole section size was done successfully in approximate 15

     days.

  6   DRILLING PROCEDURES

        The plan is to drill out cement plug, Perforation, run completion string, well test operations and
         secure the well. The outlines of workover operations are as follow:

      •  Prepare 1200 bbl MW:115 pcf

      •  Spud Drilling Rig on well# SPH-05.

      •  meanwhile R/B 4650 m 5” DP. Check wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record the wellhead
          pressure; check all annulus pressure.

      •   In case observe pressure:

               1.  Record the pressure.

               2.  Release the pressure and wait for observing any pressure build up.

               3.  In case Pressure Build Up occurred, kill and secure the well by Bull head or Lubricant
              method.

               4.  In case NO pressure builds up occurred, remove Blind Flange.


      •  M/U 8 ½” D/O BHA, RIH by pick up drill pipes joint by joint to TOC plug @ 3360 m.

      •  CBU while Displace MW to 115 pcf.

      •  Commence drill out cement plug f/3360 m to 3510 m w/115 pcf MW, Cont. RIH to top of 7"
           L.Lap.

          Note: Be prepared for potential pressure trap while drilling plug.
      •  Flow check. If NO gain detects, P/U & M/U 5 78” + Clean out BHA, RIH to 4645

      •  CBU, Spot 130 bbls 75pcf NaCl Pill @ BTM, POOH to 3600m, Circulate w/ 115 pcf mud. POOH
          to Surface.

      •  RIH W/ TCP & Perforate Selected Intervals. POOH & L/D Same.

      •  CBU and spot H.Vis brine in 7" liner from ±4400m to comp. PKR depth. POOH to 4100m.
          Displace mud in hole with Completion Fluid (CaBr2).

      •  POOH while L/D all drill collars and drill pipes.
