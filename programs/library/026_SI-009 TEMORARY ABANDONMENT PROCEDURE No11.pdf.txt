                    Temporary Abandonment of the Well SI-009

   The objective of temporary abandonment of the well SI-009 is re-entered safely in

    future and then secured the well using pressure control equipment.

      It is designed to set three cement plugs at depths 2021-1906m, 1050-935m and 600-

    485m.

    Length of each plug is 115m.

    Cement plug program will be issued in due time.

 Procedure:

     1) RIH with 5” O.E.D.P drill pipes to top of plug at 2021m.pull back half a meter.

     2)  Circulate 150% of string capacity. Check mud weight in and out to 149 pcf.

     3) Perform PJSM.

     4) Line up cement pump and test line to 5000psi/15min(consider safety policy).

     5) Pump spacer ahead.

     6) Mix and pump 55 bbl cement slurry as per cement program.

     7) Chase with mud and under-displace cement by 1-2 bbl.
       •  Calculation of Displacement is necessary in order to spot a balanced cement plug

            (hydrostatic heads inside the string and outside in the annulus shall be the same).

     8)  After setting the plug, pull out 150m above the theoretical top of the plug and reverse

         circulate clean 2 times D.P capacity and check cement return.

     9)  Pull back to 1000m and WOC.

    10) RIH with to tag cement plug(Theoretical T.O.C@±1906m).

    11) Test the plug mechanical loading & hydraulically.
       •  Prepare for second cement plug.

    12) Pull back to 1050m.

    13) Set second cement plug as same procedure.

    14) RIH to tag second cement plug (Theoretical T.O.C@±935m).

    15) Test the plug mechanical loading & hydraulically.
       •  Prepare for third cement plug.

    16) Pull back to 600m.
