Work instruction for running fishing 24” stabilizer
To:
Toolpusher, mud engineer, mud logger and waste management
Make up 12 ¾ ‘’ fabricated over shot followed by 1 x 8” DC, fishing jar, 2 x 8” DC followed by drill pipe.
RIH to top of fish. Top of fish if is in wash and ream depth should be +/-870.9m. If bit and stabilizers move down to bottom then top of fish is at +/- 879.9. (fish length is: 23.1m, length of top of fish to stabilizer blade:60cm)
Before tag fish, at +/- 875m (5 m above expected top of fish), slow down trip and pump 40 bbls Hi-Vis pill.
Continue RIH slowly and tag fish. P/U 1m and pump mud with high flow rate. (recommended by drilling supervisor on rig floor)
RIH and engage fish. (Check for SPP and weight indicator, make sure fish is engaged).
POOH to surface. Near to surface make sure trip tank pump is off.
Next Operation: M/U 24” bit and continue drilling 24” hole section.
Work safe + team work = success
Adine / Vaziri
GPTK Drilling supervisors
