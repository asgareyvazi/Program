1.  General Information

 1.1 Field Introduction
The South Azadegan Oil Field is located in Khuzestan province and is approximately located 80
kilometers west of Ahwaz city, near to Iran-Iraq border. The Field has an approximate area of 900 square
kilometers. It is estimated to have 27.937 billion barrels of oil in-place (STOIIP), which makes it one of
the largest developing oil fields in the world in terms of oil-in-place.
COMPANY has a plan to perform work over operation and Convert the 25 Sarvak wells from the natural
flow production to the artificial lift production by installing ESP in the 25 Sarvak wells in the
CONTRACT AREA. The wells are classified as oil producer wells with vertical or directional/horizontal
profiles. Well production rate has been varied in a wide range between different wells distributed across
the field. Initial production of vertical wells was 1400 −3000 BPD while production of horizontal wells
was about 500−4000 BPD. The main objectives of Field Development are highlighted as follow:
     Phase 1: Daily Production rate of 320,000 barrels of crude oil
     Phase 2: Increasing the daily production rate of crude oil to 600,000 barrels (incremental
       Production rate of 280,000 BOPD in phase2)
The development of South Azadegan Oil Filed Phase 1, is awarded to Petropars as General
CONTRACTOR by Petroleum Engineering and Development Company (PEDEC).
The current oil production rate is declined due to high pressure drop as the main drive mechanism of the
Sarvak reservoir is the fluid expansion. In order to keep the stable production rate, the application of ESP
system is highly recommended in Sarvak reservoir of Azadegan oil field.
This scope of work is related to Phase 1 of Field Development.

2.  Geological Information

 2.1 Formation Stratigraphy and Lithology
The CONTRACT AREA is completely covered by the alluvium of Holocene age; the
penetrated Formation from top to bottom is Aghajari, Gachsaran, Asmari, Pabdeh, Gurpi,
Tarbur, Ilam, Laffan, Sarvak, Kazhdumi, Dariyan, Gadvan, Fahliyan and Garau.
1) The Aghajari Formation consists of a repetition of brown to grey calcareous sandstones,
chert debris
and gypsum-veined red marl. A fauna of brackish to freshwater was observed, together with
more
marine fauna.
2) The Gachsaran Formation with a thickness ranging from 400 m to 600 m, thicken towards
the north.
Deposition of the Gachsaran Formation is cyclic. The cycle began with a deposition of
shallow-water
marine carbonates, and then it was overlain by a thick evaporitic phase. The evaporites were
capped by
red to variegated marl, which represented the end of the cycle.
3) The Asmari Formation in Azadegan Field developed a massive sandstone interval, with a
few thin
argillaceous layers in the upper sections. Sands are clear, fine to medium-sorted, and poorly
consolidated. On the other hand, the lower sections consist of intercalation of sandy, chalky,
slightly
dolomitic high-energy limestone with sands layers.
4) Pabdeh Formation is divided into two parts in the Azadegan field. The upper part is made
of grey to
dark-brown argillaceous limestones and marls. Planktonic fauna dates that this part was
deposited in the
Oligocene. The lower part is composed of dark-brown, slightly argillaceous limestone.
Pelagic fauna,
mostly Globigerina and Globorotalia, indicates that this part was formed during the late
Paleocene to
late Eocene in age. It seems that no early Paleocene Formation exists in the Azadegan area.
5) The Gurpi Formation in the Azadegan Field consists of grey to dark-brownish, slightly
argillaceous

6) Tarbur Formation: In the western Abadan Plain about 50 meters thick chalky limestone

7) In the Azadegan Field, the Ilam Formation consists of grey micritic limestone, often
argillaceous, rarelypyritic, limestones. Microfauna, mostly pelagic, including rotalids and
oligostegina assemblage, a few algae, bryozoa, and rudist fragments exist.
8) The Laffan Formation developed a thin greenish-grey pyritic shale layer, capping the
Sarvak Formation. It was encountered in the Azadegan wells and only ostracods were
found. It is a cap rock for the upper part of the Sarvak reservoir.
9) The Sarvak Formation developed a carbonate unit which was gradually overlying the
Kazhdumi
Formation, and it was abruptly terminated by an erosion phase and covered by various
late Cretaceous sediments. A Typical geological section of the Sarvak Formation in
Azadegan reveals that dark grey fine grained nodulary argillaceous limestone interbedded
with thin marls in the bottom, and graded upward into a white-to-buff massive chalky
limestone with siliceous nodules;it was developed a very massive tan-to-brown limestone
containing abundant rudist debris in the middle part; the upper part is made up of rubbly
limestone with red staining and leached zones of breccia and the top of Sarvak Formation
is very ferruginous.

5.  Sequence of Operation

 5.1 Snubbing Unit operation TBD Wells with retrievable Packer
    (Sarvak Producer)
 1.  Held PJSM. Spud the Snubbing Unit.
 2. Check the wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record well head pressures; check all
     annulus pressure, check the return fluid & Bleed off any annulus pressure to Zero. Kill
     the well w/ bullhead or circulation method, upon client's approval and recheck the well
      status.
 3.  Secure the well by close 3SV and X-Mass Tree Valves, Set TWCV inside tubing hanger
   & pressure test, N/D XMT & N/U BOPs, open tie down screws, perform BOP pressure
     Test.
 4.  Retrieve TWCV, R/U casing running equipment, RIH with TBH retrieving tool &
    engage with TBH, apply over pull to un-set packer, Displace completion fluid & oil in
     hole with wtr or mud. R/U s.line install plug & Prong on ssd.
 5.  Pull out and lay down completion string precautionary due to probable trapped pressure
    below plug & Prong. While POOH monitor the well and circ cross well head from

10.  Health, Safety and Environments

  6.1 WELL CONTROL
 All the drilling operation shall obey the requirement of Well Control Manual of MSA and contractor’s
 well control manual; under no circumstances should the deviation from well control policy be permitted
 without prior approval through MSA. If the contractor’s well control manual or anywhere has conflict
 with Well Control Manual of MSA, take Well Control Manual of MSA as the standard.

   6.1.1 WORK Requirement of Well Control Equipment
    With shut-in casing pressure lower than 1,000psi, while snubbing with annular preventer, drill tools
      shall have 18-deg shoulder tool joints, and the snubbing velocity shall not be greater than 0.2m/s.
     After shut off the annular preventer or ram preventer, with the shut-in casing pressure not greater
     than 2,000psi, drill tools are allowed to pull up and down with the velocity less than 0.2m/s,
     however, rotating or tool joint passing through are forbidden.
    While drilling through pay zone and thereafter, BOP function (close-open) test for the BOPs shall be
     performed regularly.
     Kill manifolds shall not be used for drilling fluid fill-in.
    The maximum allowed shut-in casing pressure shall be remarkably indicated on choke manifolds.
     All gate valves of well control manifolds shall be tagged with serial numbers, and be indicated with
      on/Off.
     In case shear rams were used, after shearing the drill pipe with shear rams, retrieve the upper sheared
       drill pipe, lower a drill pipe joint with TIW valve installed into the hole and close pipe rams on it to
      prevent the possibility of shear ram’s failure.

   6.1.2 Management of Well Control Equipment
    Well control equipment used by the drilling crew shall be managed and operated by designated
     personnel with clear post responsibilities.
    Well  site  shall have specified component storeroom and rubber element storeroom with  air
      conditioners  respectively. The temperature of storerooms  shall meet storage requirements of
     components and rubber elements.
    At least 1 set is available as back-up for every type ram assembly in the well site. Ram preventer
      side door seals and ram rubber, every type gasket and bolt etc. should be enough for backup.

Main technical measures for prevention of kicking and blowout during tripping.
Before POOH, circulation duration of mud in wellbore shall not be less than one and a half cycles. The

Prior to POOH (keep string rotation at 10 rpm for preventing stuck pipe)
POOH to last casing shoe
Before POOH BHA through BOP stack

during POOH, mud shall be filled full for each joint or continuous filling shall be conducted and trip

11.  H2S Monitoring & Prevention Measure

  7.1 Monitoring H2S
 Somewhere on the well site is apt to accumulate H2S, such as mud pit, shale shaker, and drilling
 platform, should be installed one set of. Make sure H2S monitor and alarm system can be used. Alarm
 threshold setting for H2S warning indicator: the first warning level is 10mg/m3, unable warning sound,
  just show present value of H2S content to operators. The second warning level is 20mg/m3, when the
 H2S content in air exceed 20mg/m3, H2S warning indicator can give an alarm automatically, and
 warning sound should be heard by everyone work in well site. The audible alarm should be installed on
 Kelly board.
 Equip enough gas masks on drilling platform, shaker screen (total amount should large than 24), and
 equip 12 sets of 30min positive pressure Air Aspirators 2 sets of portable H2S monitor and appropriative
 H2S warning indicator. Arrange a person specially assigned for taking charge  all the protective
 apparatus. This man must do periodical check to keep these equipment in a good condition.
 H2S monitor should be done weekly check and compulsory examination.
 After drilling into the pay zone, strengthen the detection of H2S content in mud.

  7.2 Measures for H2S prevention
 Windsocks shall be set on the drill floor and in the monsoon direction or/and prevailing wind direction of
 Azadegan oil field, off well site. In emergency, employees must follow the evacuation procedure.
 Explosion-proof blowers shall be installed around the rig, shale shaker, and drilling fluid tanks to blow
 away toxic and flammable gas at the well site.
 The rig crew in the H2S area shall be furnished with H2S monitors and adequate protective apparatus.
 Everybody shall be able to use, maintain, and check the apparatus.
 The technicians of rig crew are responsible for H2S proof training and the tool pusher is in charge of the
 supervision and examination.
 The pH value of drilling fluid in the H2S area shall be kept over 9.5. Pay attention to measurement of
 H2S concentration in drilling fluid. The H2S concentration shall be kept at less than 50mg/cm3 by using
 sulphur removal agents and degassers.
 Personnel safety and protection shall be carried out according to SY/T5087 during operations in polluted
 areas where H2S content exceeds the critical concentration.
 When the rig crew makes decision to bleed and fire because well control cannot be performed due to well
  site conditions, operations shall be performed according to procedures in SY/T5087.
