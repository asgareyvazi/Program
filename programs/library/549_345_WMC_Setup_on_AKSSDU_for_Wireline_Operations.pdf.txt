Subject: WMC Setup for Wireline Logging

On the 13th of December 2010, a mock rig up for the wireline Wave Motion Compensator
system was made on Amir Kabir SSDU. The attendants were as follows:

    1-  Raja S. Hamady (Schlumberger Wireline Engineer)

    2-  John Hunter (KEPCO Drilling Superintendent)

    3-  Francois Nerriere (NDCO OIM on AK SSDU)

The WMC setup was done using the following components:

A - Provided by Schlumberger Wireline:

    1-  11 Ton x 1in groove diameter Compensating Sheave (Qty:1)

    2-  1.5 meter x 10 Ton Sling (Qty:1)

    3-  5 meter x 10 Ton sling (Qty:1)

    4-  1 meter x 10 Ton sling (Qty:1)

    5-  8 Ton Weak Link (Qty:1)

    6-  11 Ton SLB Shackle (Qty:3)

    7-  12 Ton Shackle (Qty:6)

    8-  10 Ton x 1in groove diameter Snatch Block (Qty:1)

    9-   4ft x 14.5 Ton Synthetic Safety Sling (Qty:1)

B – Provided by KEPCO:

    1-  Temporary (and spliced) 1.25in x 10 Ton compensating line (Qty:1)

    2-  10 Ton Shackle (Qty:3)

The rig up was completed successfully with the following notes to be taken into account:

    1-  Hydraulic elevator cannot be used as the T-bar on the compensating sheave will not be
        set properly. The manual elevator (available on the rig) has to be used when rigging up
       the WMC for wireline operations.

    2-  The pad eyes used for the WMC on the rig are not certified and need to be certified (to
      10 Tons) before the logging operation. (refer to pictures for details)

    3-  The rig needs to provide a certified compensating line (1in x 10 Ton x 80m) for the
        logging operation. (Logging cannot be done with the compensating line used for the
      mock rig up).

    4- A second mock rig up is required to after the risers are installed to calculate the exact
        length of the compensating line needed for the operation.

    5-  The diameter of the compensating sheave is too small compared to the wireline sheave.
        This will cause the wireline sheave to contact the compensating line in case the sheave
        rotates. During the “Wireline Logging” operation, the sheave will not rotate and the
       operation can be done safely. However, attention must be paid if attempting a different
         rig up (eg. Coil tubing, lubricators, etc…) as the sheave may come in contact with the
       compensating line.

    6-  There is no pad eye on the slip joint to connect the other end of the compensating line
      on the risers (after they are installed). A certified pad eye needs to be made.

Below is a basic guideline on how to setup the WMC for wireline logging on Amir Kabir SSDU:

A - Weak Link Assembly:

             1.  SLB Shackle (Qty:1)

             2.  1.5m x 10 Ton Sling (Qty:1)

             3. 5m x 10 Ton Sling (Qty:1)

             4.  8 Ton Weak Link (Qty:1)

             5.  12 Ton Shackles (Qty:4)

As shown in the picture below:

               -   The SLB shackle at one end of the 1.5m sling should be connected to the pad
             eye (needs certification) on the rig floor, and to the weak link on the other end

               -   Connect the 5m safety sling on the weak link inner eyes (using a shorter sling is
              not safe)

               -   The whole assembly should be connected to the compensating line on the other
             end using a 10 Ton shackle provided by the rig.

         (Make sure you use safety pins on all the shackles and keep the whole assembly as it
                 is, meaning do not disassemble it, after the job)
