# Executive Plan

## Purposes
The facilities for South Azadegan Project Early Production consist of only temporary devices which have a lot of functional deficiencies. Particularly, there is no effective and precise manner for measuring single well oil and gas production rate. Current way for determining each single well production rate is through a choke valve formula by applying a key factor, GOR, which was defined long time ago when well test was carried out during drilling period, and obviously needs to be rectified for present utilities.
During a comparatively long time of production, no regular reservoir survey has ever been implemented, leaving some uncertainties to current reservoir studies and production optimizations.
More importantly, there is no reliable definition to indicate when an artificial lifting production approach shall be applied, because of lack of essential down-hole and surface production data. Some dynamic simulations were fulfilled before, but the conclusions shall be revised based on current reservoir conditions. This issue is a key topic which relates to the sustainability of the existing wells and of which the results even can be considered as references to Phase One and Phase Two of South Azadegan Project.
Therefore, this first time of systematic production tests on all existent Early Production wells will mainly focus on the followings:
Measure real GORs of each production well under at least three applicable choke sizes.
Measure down-hole flowing pressure/temperature relating to each choke size.
The tested production data shall be analyzed and explained by applying appropriate software and/or dynamic simulation model, and optimization approaches shall be recommended.
Artificial lifting timing shall be suggested for this well.

# Operational Procedures for Surface Surveys
This part explains the operational procedure which will be carried out by OTS Co.

## Surface equipment preparation and pressure test
General points regarding rig-up of surface well test equipment are as follows:
Rig-up surface well test equipment package as per layout plan.
Where pipework crosses walkways, pipe should be made clearly visible (with danger tape) and tripping hazards minimized. Pipework should be close to the ground.
Tag the instrument air supply valve to the separator to ensure continuous supply so that testing operations are not interrupted unintentionally.
Erect barriers, display pressure test signs and make announcements to warn personnel when pressure testing is about to commence.
After getting a work permit, pressure test the surface test equipment as per the CNLC procedures and record all pressure tests on a chart recorder.
During pressure testing check the following:
The orifice meter sliding valve holds pressure
The choke manifold isolation valves are tested independently to ensure choke changes and inspections can be made.
The following should be included as part of pressure testing surface well test equipment:
Flush all surface lines and equipment from upstream SSV.
P/T entire system to 800 psi against flow line valve (plugged).
P/T against oil and gas manifold to 800 psi.
P/T separator gas outlet & bypass, oil outlet & bypass plus water outlet to 800 psi.
P/T upstream of choke manifold & HP side of choke manifold to the 5000 psi.
P/T downstream of choke manifold to separator inlet and oil & gas bypass to 800 psi.
At the end of pressure testing ensure all barriers and signs are removed. Also inform personnel and sign off permits.

### Choke Manifold
Prepare the choke manifold as outlined below:
Pressure test the choke manifold as per OTS procedures to 5,000 psi.
Pressure is to be bled-off between each test.
Check all service breaks.
Prepare the necessary fixed choke and check condition as per OTS standard.
On completion of pressure testing all valves should be left open. Drift the choke manifold to 3.00”.

### Function Test for ESD System
Requirements and points to note for the ESD system are as follows:
Remote shutdown points for the ESD system shall be installed at the test separator and outside the lab cabin.
The separator instrument air supply will be an integral component of the ESD system. Air supply interruptions will activate the ESD system and shut-in the well at the flow line. The ESD system is to be function tested. The function test will be witnessed by the Well Test Engineer.

### Surface Sampling Equipment
Prepare sample bottles according to OTS procedures.
Gas sample bottles need to be evacuated prior to commencing sampling operations.
All sample containers are to be clearly labeled and a sample list produced. Labels should include client company name, well name, test interval, test number, flow period, sample number, sampling date & time, sampling point and sample type/nature.

### Calibration and Check
The following calibration checks of surface well test equipment should be carried out:
Calibrate differential pressure gauges using a manometer.
Surface pressure gauges shall be calibrated at the OTS base prior to mobilization using a dead weight tester.
Check flow meter calibrations by pumping water through each meter. Obtain two consistent results for each meter.
Check condition of measuring instruments, etc. thermometers, hydrometers etc.
Ensure all orifice plates & choke beans are in good condition and an adequate supply is on hand.

### Separator Condition
Separator pressure would be set according to the measured downstream pressure of each choke while the separator is bypassed and control valve at inlet of the cluster kept fully open. / Max differences between the set points are 15%.

### Pressure Test for Surface Test Equipment
Test all surface test equipment and surface test lines to minimum of anticipated operating pressure (all tests should include a low pressure test of approximately 500psi where applicable). All tests should include be held for 10 minutes.

#### Main flow test
Opened well with 8/64" adjustable choke and increased to 32/64" gradually. The hydrocarbons will be diverted to production line by-passing the test separator.
Switch flow from 32/64" adjustable choke to 32/64" fixed choke. Monitor FTHP and BS&W. Measure salinity and pH of any produced water if needed.
Once sediment below approx. 0.5%, divert flow through the test separator. Send produced gas and oil to production line. ( If not; the supervisor must be informed in advance)
Establish stable flow conditions and produce well at constant flow rate through the separator. The Well Test Engineer will determine the duration of the flow period. Duration of 8-10 hours (depend on well response and decided by well testing supervisor) is expected.
The choke sizes for the multi-rate test will be selected based on the results of well response during flow period. The choke sizes given in this program can therefore change accordingly.
Measure the stable flow rate of 32/64″choke size.
Switch from 32/64″ fixed to 32/64″ adjustable choke. Gradually decrease adjustable choke to 28/64″.
Switch flow from 28/64" adjustable choke to 28/64" fixed choke. Monitor FTHP and BS&W. Measure salinity and PH of any produced water if needed.
Establish stable separator conditions and continue to flow at this rate for approximately 8-10 hours (duration to be determined by the Well Test Engineer depending on well behavior.
Measure the stable flow rate of 28/64″choke size.
Before change of choke size (when the WHP stabilized), take a set of separator PVT samples & liquid samplers (gas, oil and water) as per reservoir engineer requirement.
Switch from 28/64″ fixed to 28/64″ adjustable choke. Gradually increase adjustable choke to 32/64″.
Switch flow from 32/64" adjustable choke to 32/64" fixed choke. Monitor FTHP and BS&W. Measure salinity and PH of any produced water if needed.
Establish stable separator conditions and continue to flow at this rate for approximately 8-10 hours (duration to be determined by the Well Test Engineer depending on well behavior).
Measure the stable flow rate of 32/64″choke size.
Switch from 32/64″ fixed to 32/64″ adjustable choke. Gradually increase adjustable choke to 36/64″.
Switch flow from 36/64" adjustable choke to 36/64" fixed choke. Monitor FTHP and BS&W. Measure salinity and PH of any produced water if needed.
Establish stable separator conditions and continue to flow at this rate for approximately 8-10 hours (duration to be determined by the Well Test Engineer depending on well behavior).
Measure the stable flow rate of 36/64″choke size.
Switch from 36/64″ fixed to 36/64″ adjustable choke. Gradually increase adjustable choke to 40/64″.
Switch flow from 40/64" adjustable choke to 40/64" fixed choke. Monitor FTHP and BS&W. Measure salinity and PH of any produced water if needed.
Establish stable separator conditions and continue to flow at this rate for approximately 48 hours (duration to be determined by the Well Test Engineer depending on well behavior).
Measure the stable flow rate of 40/64″choke size.
One hour before the end of the main flow period, take a final set of separator PVT samples & liquid samples (gas, oil and water) as per reservoir engineer requirement.
Throughout main flow, measure and record the following at regular intervals. Surface data acquisition will be used to monitor some of these items simultaneously.
Flowing tubing pressure and temperature.
Separator pressure and temperature
BS&W
Water chlorides and concentrations of any tracers.
Oil and gas rates and calculated gas-oil ratio (verify orifice plate used).
Oil and gas gravities.
Gas H2S.
as required, divert separator oil line output to surge tank for oil rate measurement and establishment of combined shrinkage/meter factor.
Note: Carefully monitor for evidence of sand at surfaces.
Utilize methanol injection as necessary to prevent hydrates at choke manifold.

#### Take oil sample from choke manifold (C/M)
The operation procedure to take the oil sample from choke manifold:
Take the barrel and open it, check it, and make sure it is clean inside.
Take the barrel and plastic hose to the choke manifold, and put them near the sample point on the C/M.
Connect the hose from sample point to the barrel.
One operator who with the H2S detector, wearing SCBA (Self Contained Breathing Apparatus) to protect himself, and stand in the upwind direction, then open the fan to enforce the air to circulate strongly.
Give information to remind the people who working in the field and release all persons in the downwind direction.
Then open the needle valve on the sample point slowly, check the situation of the sample barrel.
When we are sure the oil sample is enough to do the analysis then close the needle valve and open all the hoses connected to the barrel, at last transfer the sample to another plastic barrel.

#### Take oil & gas sample from separator
Generally at least three matched sets of pressurized samples are taken from the separator for each zone. Normally, 20 liters of gas per 600 cc oil sample are taken for recombination. In addition, a quantity of non-pressurized (atmospheric) 1 liter samples of oil and formation water will be required. The precise number of samples to be taken should be determined by the Company Petroleum Engineer prior to mobilization in order to ensure that sufficient sample bottles/containers are available.
Unless there is a separate sampling flow period, the well test program will usually call for surface PVT samples to be taken towards the end of the main flow period to ensure the well is fully cleaned up. However, for back-up purposes, it is worth collecting at least one additional set of samples as soon as conditions stabilize.
Correct sample labeling and logging of surface conditions, flow rates, etc. during sampling is essential. Data sheets for each set of samples are to be completed and copies sent with the samples.
A typical sequence for surface sampling is as follows:
Flow well until stabilized wellhead conditions are achieved and solids content is less than 1.0%.
Direct flow through test separator, monitor and record process parameters.
Cease upstream chemical injection (if possible).
Connect one 20 liter evacuated gas sample bottle via sampling manifold to sampling tap on separator gas run. Connect one 600 cc positive displacement sample bottle via a sampling manifold to the sampling tap on the separator oil line.
Open the sample point valve(s) on the oil and gas lines and purge the lines and manifolds.
Open the inlet valves on both sample bottles and proceed with sampling operation. It should take 30 - 45 minutes to obtain one set of recombination samples. The rate of displacement will be controlled by the operator. The samples should then be labeled as a matched pair before starting on the next set.
Validation of the gas sample is determined by checking the bottle pressure prior to disconnection of the sampling manifold. Validation of the oil sample is determined by checking bottle pressure and volume of fluid displaced.
The sample bottles should have plugs installed to prevent leakage during shipment to the laboratory.
Completed data sheets for each sample should be included in the transport case, referencing bottle serial number, well' details, flowing conditions and parameters, time taken etc.

# Operational Procedures for Down-hole Surveys

## Flowing Pressure and Temperature Survey Procedure
Pre job safety meeting on site.
Prepare slick line string such as: Rope socket, Stem, Jar, Memory Gauge, Swage,…
Prepare control pressure equipment such as: B.O.P, Lubricator, Stuffing Box,….
Rig up B.O.P and lubricator on well.
Make slick line tool string for Tag Bottom of well.
Open swab valve and RIH to find tag bottom of well.
Identify tag bottom of well for survey.
POOH slick line string after find tag bottom of well.
Make slick line string with memory gauge.
Rig up B.O.P and lubricator on well for take pressure and temperature survey.
Open swab valve and stop at lubricator for 5 Min- Flowing.
Go to 300 M with speed of 60 m/Min.
Stop t 300 M for 5 Min – Flowing.
Repeat 12, 13 items at 600, 900, 1200, 1500 Meter depth.
Go to 1800 M with speed of 60 M/Min.
Stop at 2100M for 5 Min – Flowing.
Repeat 15, 16 items at 2400, 2700, 3000, 3300 Meter depth.
Stop at tag bottom depth and stay there while the surface well-test takes place.
After the surface well-test period is finished start to pull up the gauge.
After reach to surface, close swab valve.
Start to bleed lubricator pressure.
Take the gauge from lubricator.
Transfer data from gauge to PC.
If result was OK, rig down from well.

H2S levels will be monitored cleanup & well testing during. Low levels of H2S (<20ppm) may be encountered.
BA systems will be available during testing in the following locations: wellhead, choke manifold, and designated safe area(s). Samples should be taken and checked for H2S as soon as gas & fluids are produced at surface and at regular intervals during the testing.
A H2S leakage, emergency muster and protection drill with each crew shall be conducted prior to any testing.
All breathing apparatus shall be inspected and ready for emergency use. All concerned personnel on location during testing shall have H2S and SCBA training with certification.

### First Aid
The following first aid procedure applies to all H2S victims:
remove the victim to fresh air, upwind or crosswind from the suspected leak source
isolate the leak/source if possible
commence artificial respiration and/or administer oxygen as required
summon qualified medical assistance
keep patient quiet, comfortable and warm
if eyes are affected, wash thoroughly with water for 15 minutes – ice compresses may also be applied
Patients must be kept under medical observation until they are declared fit to return to work or they are evacuated. The possibility of delayed pulmonary oedema up to 24 hours after exposure to H2S should not be overlooked.
