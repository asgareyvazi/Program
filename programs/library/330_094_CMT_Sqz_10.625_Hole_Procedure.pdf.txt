    CMT SQUEEZE 10 5/8” HOLE PROCEDURE
                       Goldfinger

1. POOH L/D BHA.

2.  P/U Side Entry sub, TIW, and pup joint. (Lay out on Skate)
    • Mix 100 BBL of Spacer in Add #2. No surfactant until after PJSM.

3.  P/U FASDRIL Retainer W/Ball Catcher.

4.  TIH to + 13,291’. Watch flex joint, BOP, Well head, TOL etc.
    •  Trip speed 1.5-minutes a stand.

5.  Locate Annular-Space out tool joint 25’ Maximum below. (P/U Side Entry Assy.)

6.  Set FASDRIL as per Halliburton. Test backside to 2000-psi.

7.  Establish injection rates @ .5, 1, 3, & 6 BPM. “Annular Closed”
    •  Sting out of FASDRIL. Close IBOP Hold 500-psi on top.
    • Pump 70-BBL spacer (13.6-ppg)
    • Pump 189-BBL CMT (1000-sx) (130-BBL without Fiber. 30-BBL with Fiber. 30-BBL without
       Fiber.)
    • Pump 10-BBL Spacer. (Hold + 1400-psi on Annulus at the end of pumping CMT. Communications
     W/Rig critical.)
    • Pump 15-BBL Seawater and sting in. Add 2# of sugar to each mix tank of seawater.
    • Pump 90-BBL of seawater.
    • Pump 149-BBL mud.
    •  Hesitate, Squeeze 10-bbl of mud, hesitate, squeeze 10-BBL mud.

8.  Sting out of FASDRIL.
    •   Reverse out 1-1/2 DP Volumes. (Mud Engineer to monitor returns and dispose of seawater.)
    •  Do not exceed 2000-psi on CSG.
    •   Insert wiper ball in drill string and pump to ball catcher.

9. POOH L/D BHA. (Last 2000’ 0f 21.90 DP look for CMT)

10. P/U Power drive Assy.
