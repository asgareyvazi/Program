Well Status
1.  7'' liner hanger was set.
2.  Stable well filled with 106 PCF mud.
3. 11” short wear bushing installed in wellhead.

B.1. Objective

The objective is to run the clean out BHA to top of perforation and Circulation
to displace completion fluid.

 B.2. Risk Evaluation

  Junk falls in hole.
  Debris on top of liner hanger.
 BHA stuck in the hole.
   Bit or BHA stuck due bad liner joint(Small ID or collapsed casing/liner)in
     liner or casing.

B.3. HSE Focus Points

  Well has to be stable before running the BHA.
  Monitor well for loss/gain over the trip tank.
  Good communication among all parties responsible for well control.
  Pre-job meeting will focus on well control, contingency procedures to be
    understood by all involved parties.
  Volume and well control shall be focused upon throughout running/pulling.
       Fill out kick sheet prior to run in hole.
  On a positive indication of a kick, unexpected pit gain or flow return rate
     increase, well shall be shut in without performing flow check.
  A kick stand shall be readily available at all times.
  Run with BHA slowly & carefully inside liner In the First.

D.1. Objectives

The objective is to install the upper completion tools, including anchor latch
seal (anchor is removed), landing nipple, production packer, expansion joint,
SSD, chemical injection mandrel, chemical injection valve, TRSSSV, 4 1/2” & 3
1/2” production tubing, tubing hanger and X-mas tree.

D.2. Risk Evaluation

  Fluid left in tubing is not properly inhibited.
  Losing control line clamps in hole.
  Junk falls in the well before or during completion.
  Over torque tubing connections.
  Leak in TRSSSV control line when pressure testing .
  Damage to TRSSSV control lines from manual slips.
  Taking kick/inflow during completion.
  Damage to tubing hanger when running through riser/BOP.
  Poor make up graphs due to poor supervision.

D.3. HSE Focus Points

  Maintain good housekeeping on the drill floor at all times.
  The well shall be lined up to the trip tank. Keep the trip tank pump running.
  Perform pre job meetings with all involved personnel at critical stages of
    the operation.
  Perform kick drill prior to commencement.
    It is strongly advised to have emergency cable cutters (tong) for TRSSSV
    control line ready on rig floor.
  Observe that well is stable before running completion.

D.4. Technical

  TRSSSV should be drifted to minimum ID prior to shipment.
  Verify that BPV has been physically checked to fit the TH no-go profile and
    that it has been drifted through the X-mas tree.
  The tubing will be measured, checked, drifted and cleaned from inside.

Storage compounds are not suitable as running compounds, so they must be
removed and the connections cleaned prior to application of thread running
compound for make-up.
  Never use barite or wire brush to clean connections as damage may be
    occurred.
  Never use diesel to clean the connections as this will leave a film that can
   cause make up problems or affect the performance.
  Use steam cleaner or water jet to clean the threads.
Rack one row of tubular on pipe rack, open the protectors, clean the box and
pin connections out of storage dope, hand tight the clean protectors and rack
another row of tubular on top. Continue the above procedure till all connections
are cleaned and racked consecutively.
Rack the tubular in staggered configuration with suitable pipe in between.
Connections should be completely dry before application of thread compound.
All compounds conforming to API 5A2 and API 5A3 are acceptable as running
compounds.
These API modified thread compounds are harmful to the environment and
can cause skin problems.
Do not use expired running thread compounds. If excess thread compound
has been left from a previous job and it has been stored properly, then it is
acceptable to use. Ensure that the compound is free of foreign particles.
Never use drill pipe compounds for tubing and casing and vice-versa.
Thread compounds are all required to be regularly mixed during use.
Evenly apply the correct quantity of compound (as per below table) to all the
thread seal and shoulder areas with fine brush. The compound must reach the
bottom of the thread. Thread compound must not extrude from the connections
during make up as this indicates extra dope applied.
