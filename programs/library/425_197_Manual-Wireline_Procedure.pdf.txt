                          SAFETY PRECAUTIONS


OBJECTIVE

    This procedure describes the main precautions to take care before and during a
    wire-line operation.


GENERAL RULES

    1- An inspection of the work area must be carried out prior to any wire-line work.
        Particular caution should be paid to missing or damaged deck plates/hand
          rails, faulty safety equipment,  etc...  Any irregularities should be reported
       immediately to wire-line chief operator and POGC supervisor .

    2- The extinguishers must be accessible and have a valid inspection label.

    3- The POGC supervisor should hold a meeting to review the responsibilities for
       the operations to be performed and to discuss the program.

    4- Welding operations and other hot works should not be performed during wire-
         line operations.

    5- Any simultaneous  wire-line  activities must be performed under  specific
       procedures.

    6- Communication equipment should be in good working order before starting
        operations.


OPERATIONS

    1- Any discharge of large quantities of condensate or gas to the atmosphere
       should be to a safe area, preferably on the downwind side of the well.
       Disposal of oil spills to the sea is prohibited.

    2-  All equipment should be located so that the Chief Operator can see the wire-
         line operators working on the lubricator.

    3-  Wire-line unit should be secured to prevent any unwanted movement of the
         unit when load is taken on the line.

    4- Rope should not be used to tie off the wire-line clamp. The clamp should be
         tied off with chain.

    5- The POGC supervisor should use his own judgment to decide whether or not
       the lubricator and mast can be rigged up safely and securely in strong wind
        conditions.

    6- Only personnel essential to perform wire-line operations should be permitted
       near the pressurized lubricator and X-mas tree.

                           WIRELINE STRING SELECTION


GENERAL

   The wireline tool string is necessary for the efficient surface control during running
    or pulling operations with slickline for subsurface control devices.

   An assembly of wireline tools connected as wireline string is used to deliver
    surface controlled impact  (jar  action), upwards or downwards to manipulate
    devices within the well bore.


DESCRIPTION

   A wireline string is composed of following items :

            - Rope Socket: Slick line, WL stranded line, electric stranded line.

            - Sinker Bar: Normal stem, Roller stem

            - Jar: Spang, tubular, hydraulic, spring

            - Specific Component: Knuckle  joint, Knuckle  jar, quick lock, centralizer,
         stretch simulator


STRING SELECTION

        •  Length

       The length of a sinker bar is given by the well head pressure which dictates
        the weight required to offset the ejecting force exerted on the wire by WHP.

         Diverting good impact with the jar is the other parameter to decide minimum
     WL string weight .

          Impact = ½ M V2 ( M: weight of stem in well fluid,V2 : square of speed )

       The wireline string must not have less than 5 ft of 1 7/8” stem or 7 ft of 1 ½”
       stem when using 0.108” wire.

        •  Outside Diameter

          All WL string components must have the same OD and identical fishing neck.

         String OD is driven by OD of pulling tool required to fish the string if it is lost
          in hole.

       The pulling tool must pass through the tubing and through the completion
          restrictions that the WL string may pass through when lost in hole.

       The string OD should be as large as possible to ease the fishing operations
        unless operational and well reasons such as crooked tubing,piston effect in
        viscous effluent dictate a smaller string OD.

       The pulling tool standard OD must be large enough to avoid by passing of the
         fishing neck in the completion ID.

                       WIRELINE EQUIPMENT INSPECTION


PURPOSE

    Before starting a new contract, AMOS shall ensure that all equipment complies
    with the client requirements and the required specifications.  All along the life of
    contract, regular routine checks will be carried out to ensure the integrity and the
    conformity of equipment with the contract and the specifications.


WIRELINE EQUIPMENT INVENTORY

   The inventory will be carried out the first time at the beginning of contract and will
   be repeated every 3 months for all the contract duration.


WIRELINE SURFACE EQUIPMENT TEST

    This surface equipment test will be carried out the first time before the beginning
    of the contract, under a third party witness. This test will be repeated once a year
    (always witnessed by an approved third party), for all the contract duration. Any
    additional equipment  required  during  operations  or   if  other equipment are
    transferred to the field should undergo same test.

   A routine test of the surface pressure equipment will be performed before the first
    run of every job, using the pressure test unit. The equipment will be pressure
    tested up to at least 110% of the Maximum expected SIWHP.


WIRELINE HYDRAULIC UNITS TEST

    Hydraulic power packs, triplex pumps, and winch units tests will be carried out the
      first  time  at  the acceptance  tests  factory  or  during  the commissioning  of
    equipment. They will be repeated once a year to ensure the equipment keep
    specification requirements.

   A copy of the test document will be sent to POGC.


LIFTING EQUIPMENT

     All lifting equipment will be examined by a certifying authority within the preceding
   6 months period and a report made with copies sent to POGC.

     Lifting equipment should follow COMPANY color coding regulations.

                        RISER & LUBRICATOR & ACCESSORIES


GENERAL

   The riser is an element of piping working under pressure when the X-mas tree is
   opened.    It is the necessary pipe extension between the X-mas tree and the
    working level.

   The  riser  is from X-mas tree to wireline deck  for operations on well head
    platforms.

   The riser is from well head to rig floor during rig operations.

   The lubricator is the piping assy, permitting the access to the well bore (for the
     tools), when it is equalized to the well pressure. The number of lubricators and the
     total length of them depend on the wireline string length.

   These sensitive under pressure equipment must be third party certified every
    year.


PRESSURE CONTROL EQUIPMENT SELECTION

        •  Wellhead shut in pressure

       The pressure rating of PCE is normally identified to the X-mas tree pressure
          rating.

       The PCE must be capable to contain the expected WHP plus a 15% safety
        margin.

        For practical reasons and maintenance cost ,  it is best to select the lowest
        range compatible.

        •  OD of the tool to be run or retrieved

      A tapered ID lubricator is used to perform WL operations to save weight and
       an easier rig up.

       The upper sections of lubricator have a reduced ID to accommodate only WL
         string OD.

       The lower section of lubricator, BOP, riser and well head cross over must
       have a larger ID than tool OD.

        •  Length of the WL string run or retrieved

       The lubricator must be at least one foot longer than total WL string length (
         with jar in open position).

       The bottom lubricator with large ID must be larger than the large OD retrieved
         or run and must accommodate the longer wireline tool or equipment used in
        the well.

                    CABLE & ACCESSORIES CONTROL


GENERAL

   The line or cable is one of the most critical item in wireline operations.

    This guide can not dictate the exact rules which will eradicate breaking.

   A great part of the wire assessment is still based on WL chief operator and POGC
    supervisor judgment and can not be replaced by rules.

   The slick line size which will be used on South Pars field phases 1, 2, and 3 is the
    0.108". It should be a special type alloy to work in H2S and CO2 environment.

   The 0.125" slick line is used for heavy job like fishing, requiring pulling weight until
   2000 lbs.  Specifications are the same as for 0.108" wire but upgraded to "high
    strength".


WIRELINE CARE AND MAINTENANCE

    In order to get good service and maximum life from wireline it is necessary to take
   some precautions in handling and usage.

    Gripping the lines with tools such as pliers, hardened jaws should be avoided
   because of damaging the surface and possibly causing failure resulting from
    stress when the line is subjected to tensile and bending stresses.

    Uncontrolled  slack and  resulting  kinking  should  always be  avoided when
    respooling the line into the reel as it is retrieved from a well.  It should be cleaned
    of well fluids and given a coat of protective oil.

WIRELINE PROCEDURE

        •  General

         a) Both static and pulling weights must be checked before carrying out any
        operation below 1000 meters.   If  the weight exceeds 500  lbs  wireline
         authorities should be contacted.

         b) If a tool string becomes stuck and remains so after 3 hours jarring, the wire
       must be cut as close as possible to the tool string.

         c) The wire is to be removed from around the measuring wheel (and the wire
        flagged on) when heavy jarring is expected.

         d) A log book must be available to record the wire working time and the
        remaining length on the drum.

       e)The maximum time to jar at the same depth is limited to 3 Hrs.

       Above such time the wire or cable passing on the hay pulley and stuffing box
       wheel is subject to rapid embritlement. POGC supervisor must give the order
          prior to cut the wire at the tool string rope socket.

        •  Wires

         a) Wireline will be cut off and disconnected in the following circumstances :
             ∗  Before each operation - 30 m
             ∗  When using jar every 3 hours - 50 m
             ∗   In case of line parted - 50 m or more

         b) Jarring with the unit is not allowed above 500 m unless using a stretch
         simulator.

         c) When working in fluid a wireline wiper is to be used and the drum greased
      when operation is completed.

         d) The safe length of wire to remain on the drum at working depth is 500 m
       minimum. If this condition can not be met to perform an operation the wire
        should be replaced.

         e) To avoid loss of jar action, particularly at shallow depth, ensure that the
        wire being run is well lubricated.

                             RIG UP PROCEDURE


PURPOSE

    This is main features for the rig up / rig down procedures.


RIG UP ON X-MAS TREE

               1- Receive X'mas tree from production (ensure work permit is validated
            and production / control room is informed of operational intervention).
          Swab valve should be closed and pressure above to be bled  off.
            Connect DHSV control line to portable test unit after isolating from
              panel.

          One man from production should be in alert during rig up operations.


               2- Open needle valve on tree cap. Monitor for leak. Remove tree cap.

             Caution : use only a copper or brass faced hammer.


               3- Remove hatch cover from B.O.P. deck.  Erect signs and barriers to
            warn other personnel of possible danger.


               4- Assemble B.O.P. :Ensure B.O.P. hydraulic lines are secured.


               5- Function test B.O.P. (BOP test performed once a month).  Connect
              high  pressure  line  to  pressure/  bleed   off  manifold.    Ensure
              pressure/bleed off manifold is open.


              6- Check condition of stuffing box packings, feed wireline cable through
                stuffing box.


              7- Make up  lubricator  sections,  place  wireline  tool  string  inside
                lubricator.


              8- Make up stuffing box to the top section of lubricator, secure stuffing
            box hydraulic hose, rope  to stuffing box.  Fix wireline clamp to bottom
              section of lubricator and wire. Ensure sufficient slack wire is spooled
              out prior to picking up lubricator with crane.


               9- Pick up BOP and make up it on the top of X-mas tree.


               9- Pick up lubricator with the B.O.P.'s.  Fix load cell and hay pulley to
                 riser.  Secure wire to hay pulley, pick up slack wire on wireline unit.

                         BHP SURVEY


GENERAL

   Many types of surveys may be performed: static / flowing bottom hole pressure
    survey, simple gradient survey, P.L.T. and T.D.T. surveys.

    Special precautions have to be taken :

            - Running pulling speeds slow and constant

            - Handling gauges / instruments with extreme care

            - The sequence of ALL events, WHP, times are recorded ACCURATELY.


RUNNING BHP SURVEY GAUGES

   Note : A drift run should be performed prior to run BHP survey gauges.

    1- Make up tool string.

    2- Check and prepare running tool, shock absorber, gauges (with centralizers  if
    necessary).

    3- Make up running tool, shock absorber and gauges to wireline tool string.

              - Care must be taken when handling and installing gauges into lubricator.

    4- Pressure test lubricator x 15 min. Depressurise lubricator slowly.

    5- Perform 15 min lubricator stop. Accurately record WHP. Production incharge to
    record WHP.

   6- RIH gently to 100 meter above the first station depth that is identified by POGC
   as per their program (perform 15 min. gradient stops.  Normally 300 and 100 m
   above the first station depth).

              - Maximum speed 35 m/min.  Caution must be exercised when passing
          through any sub-assemblies in the completion string.

    7- Record pick up .

    8- RIH gently to the first station depth.

    9- Stop at the first station for time instructed by POGC and record BHP.

    10- RIH gently to next stations depth respectively and record BHP for time
    instructed by POGC and record BHP.


RETRIEVING BHP SURVEY GAUGES

    1- POOH string to 100 meter below X-mas tree.
