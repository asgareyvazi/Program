Screw Procedure
Fish length = 549.65 m
Expected TOF = 4086.75 m
Weight of the fish (BHA + DP) = 41/35 Klb in air/mud
RIH PIN DOWN W/BHA SCREW (1 JT 3-1/2” HWDP + 4-3/4” F.JAR + 2 JTs 3-1/2” HWDP ) FOLLOW BY 78 JTs 3-1/2” DP WHILE DRY M/UP 60 JTs & CONT. RIH W/4 STDs 3-1/2” DP;
COUNT THE ACTUAL TURN OF DP (3-1/2” DP);
F/UP STRG & CIRC. AS 1.5 TIMES OF STRG CAP;
CONT. RIH W/5” DP WHILE RETIGHT ALL JTs;
RIH TO 4060 M (1 STD ABOVE TOF) & M/UP TDS
CBU TO SHEAR MUD IN HOLE;
RECORD SPP;
RECORD PUHL/SOHL W/O ROTATION;
RECORD PUHL/SOHL W/20 RPM;
RECORD TRQ W/20 RPM
SLOWLY RIH W/O ROTATION & TAG TOF WHILE PUMPING W/150 GPM;
P/UP STRG 2 M;
SLOWLY RIH W/20 RPM;
STOP PUMPING & RELEASE PRESS. AFTER ENGAGE W/FISH;
ATTEMPT TO SCREW INTO THE TOF W/MAX 10 KLBS IN STEPS WHILE TRANSFER TRQ W/280-380 KLBS SOHL/PUHL ON WT INDICATOR;
