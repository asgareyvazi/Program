Objectives: Drilling pilot hole

             1. Hold on safety meeting before start job.
             2. RIH with 12-1/4” Drilling BHA same as last BHA in last pilot hole. Without guide ropes.
             3. Shallow test MWD & LWD equipment as per SLB instructor.
             4. When bit reach to seabed ROV must on seabed & monitoring & record all things that
              happen.
             5. Stop 30 mt TVDRT from bottom and break circulation with all three pumps in 30,60,90
         SPM record & report pressure loss
             6. Slowly & carefully stab into TGB (without pumping) with surveillance by ROV take
              survey & report result to OIM & Co.man. Adjust or move rig to stab the bit in the center
               of TGB if necessary
             7. Tag seabed (pump off) verifies depth versus depth record in depth check on 12-Aug.
             8. Start to drill by low flow rate and low RPM and low WOB and gradually increase drilling
              parameter to plan parameter (max. WOB=20 KLb RPM=80-120 GPM=750 ) and so keep
           Max.ROP 15 mt/hr
             9. Keep drilling to 860 mt while drilling pump 50 BBL Hi-Vis pill each half stand drilling,
               take survey each stand keep monitoring seabed by ROV.take care about seawater flow &
              shallow gas flow by LWD & ROV.take survey as required.
           10. Incase if observe flow (seawater or shallow gas) stop drilling, ahead & keep circ inform
          OIM & Co.man ASAP & flow check by ROV .and issue order about flow in time by
             Co.man.
           11. In depth of 860 mt circulate 100 bbl Hi-Vis pill clean hole flow check hole by ROV 2 Hr
           12. If well was static pump and displace sea water by 400 bbl 16 ppg mud & check by ROV
                for any losses.(use ahead of 16 ppg mud a few volume of color mud as per indicator ).
           13. Check well by ROV for 2 Hrs .if everything was OK continue drilling to 1410 mt (TD of
                 pilot hole) by keep care about flow & shallow gas.

     NOTE:IF ANY CHANGING PLAN INFORM BY CO.MAN ASAP
     NOTE:IF ANY PROBLEM MADE INFORM OIM AND CO.MAN ASAP
