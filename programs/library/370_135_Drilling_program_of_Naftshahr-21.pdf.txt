                              General Information


1  Introduction

  1.1    Program Introduction
         This program covers the drilling of well#21 in Naft Shahr field, Iran. The well is
          intended to produce oil from Asmari formation. The well will be drilled vertically to a
         planed total depth of 1035m(-838m), ±18m inside Kalhur Formation.

         Note-1: The exact TD will be determined based on the logging results.
         Note-2: This program has been written with respect to the geology forecast no.
        ٣٢٧٣٥ / ا ط م  dated: 1385/11/23 and letter no.٥٨ / ٨٧١٥٥ / ا ط م dated: 1385/11/30.

  1.2     Naft Shahr Structure

         Naft Shahr is an asymmetrical anticline with North West – South East axis trend. The
          dip of structure in the south west is more than the other parts. A thrust fault has
          crossed the Gachsaran Fm and caused the thickness of Gachsaran Fm to be increased
           in south west flank of the structure.
        The approximate coordinates of NAFT SHAHR-21 are:
        U.T.M, International: E: 545, 886m  N: 3, 764, 841m

9 Pre Spud

      P.P.Z prepared a pre-spud checklist and distributed it in contractor rigs. The checklist will

      be performed based on the aforementioned  checklist. In addition to the  checklist,

      following matters should be checked:

   ¾ Check & observe the alignment of the rig. This check will be performed in four stages;

           rig levelling, derrick verticality, derrick alignment with rotary table, rotary table

         alignment with conductor pipe. To do this pick up 3 joints of 9 3/4” DC and check the

           verticality of the derrick.

   ¾  Mechanical facilities for mud treatment such as :

         Desander, Desilter, mud cleaner, proper size of shaker–screens, hoppers, mud guns &

          mixers, should be all available on rig and functioning properly.

   ¾  All mud tanks, mud transfer lines & connection should be checked by filling them

         with water & cross wellhead circulation.

   ¾ Mud  engineer  laboratory should be equipped with a complete  set  of  testing

         equipment. The calibration should be done under supervision of well site drilling

          supervisor before starting the operation.

   ¾ Weight indicator, rotary speed gauge, any smart drilling instrumentation, Stroke

          meter, pressure gauges on manifold and in the drilling console, loss / gain gauge and

         any other gauges should be checked and calibrated.

   ¾  Stand pipe manifold valves & connection must be checked & pressure tested.

   ¾  All motors should be checked under load prior to start drilling.

   ¾ Check & control mud stock & cement stock every day & if there is any necessity  to

          request mud material, cement or any drilling tool, it should be prepared & requested

           at proper time.

                                    Geology


  1  Geological Overview

Following Formations will be drilled in NAFT SHAHR-21 as follows:

Aghajari Fm . as 353.4m vertical thickness ( Surface - 361m )
Is composed of gary to grayish brown Sandstone, fine grained, angular with greenish gray& redish
brown siltstone and red to brown & grayish green, soft, sticky Claystone.
The KB-A  with 4m thickness is at 288.5m depth.
The Aghajari Fm. is conformable with Gachsaran Fm.

Gachsaran Fm . as 605m vertical thickness ( 361m - 966m)
The  Gachsaran Fm. Is  conformable  with asmari Fm. In naft-shar field and is divided to
following members:

► Gachsaran Fm. Mbr 7-5 as 304m vertical thickness (361m-665m)
Is consisted of mainly anhydrite with interbeded of greenish gray and sticky Marl with some oolitic
Limestone.
► Gachsaran Fm. Mbr 4 as 123.5m vertical thickness (665m-788.5m)
Is composed of mainly thick salt beds which are interbeded with beds of Anhydrite & brown to
grayish brown Claystone.
►Gachsaran Fm. Mbr -3 as 126.5m vertical thickness (788.5m - 915m)
Is composed of thick anhydrite beds with some red to gray and sticky Marl or clay stone & with
some Salt beds in middle part of this Member.
► Gachsaran Fm. Mbr-2 as 26m vertical thicknesses (915m-941m)
Is consisted of two thick a Salt bed with a red to brown & calcareous marl between them, the lower
Salt bed is thicker than upper.
► Gachsaran Fm. Mbr-1 as 25m vertical thicknesses (941m-966m)
This member is known to Cap rock as Asmari reservoir and composed of interbeded of thick
Anhydrite beds which are interbeded with some grayish to brownish gray of argillaceous Limestone
or calcareous Marls. the upper part of this argillaceous. Limestone is known to limestone as G-2 in
last classification. With 3-7m from the top of Cap rock.

Asmari Fm . as 51m vertical thickness ( 966m- 1017m )
The Asmari Fm. Is reservoir rock in Naft-shahr field and consisted of light gray to gray & oolitic
Limestone , some Dolomitic , fine grained, fractured and saturated with organic matter.

Evaporate member of Kalhur ( 1017m - 1035m)
This member begins with alternation of anhydrite and dolomite layers which its anhydrite component is
predominant. After passing anhydrite layers, the middle part which includes dominantly of thick beds of salt
begins. The lower part of Kalhur Mbr. Consists of a thick bed of anhydrite in upper part and alternation of
anhydrite and argillaceous limestone layers in lower part. The base of Kalhur Mbr. Is considered by the last
anhydrite layers.

                    Sequence Of Drilling Operation

1.3  Sequence of Operation

     •  Make up 26” BHA as per BHA design, drill formation down to 150m (18-5/8”
         casing point in Aghajari formation). Use LCM pill if encountered heavy losses. If
          loss couldn’t be cured by LCM pill use cement plug(s) to cure same.

     Note:
       - 26” TD is based on 150m inside Aghajari formation.
       - TD should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of rat hole under casing shoe.
       - The exact TD will be determined based on 18-5/8” casing tally.
       - Do not forget to strap pipe while POOH.
       - Drop Totco prior to POOH to evaluate the verticality of hole.
       - Use following parameters to drill this hole section:
        -WOB = Maximum available
         -RPM = 90-130
        -GPM = 600-1000

     •  At TD, Circulate, clean hole. POOH, P/U 26” N.Bit stabilizer or 26” Reamer, RIH
        and condition the hole to section TD. Check for any fill. CBU on bottom.

     •  POOH. If observed any indication of drag, run back to bottom. Wash and ream to
       make sure that the hole condition is good.

     •  R/U 18-5/8” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

     •  Run 150m (Approx. 13 Jts) 18-5/8", 87.5 ppf, J-55, BTS casing with stab in-type
        guide shoe to bottom as per casing program. Casing shoe should be adjusted in such
        a way to have 2 meters of rat hole and enough stick up (1.5 m) on rig floor. Fill
         casing by non LCM fresh mud joint by joint.

     Note:
       -  Take care when running casing not to set down excessive weight if tight hole or
         ledges are encountered.

     •  Run stinger on 5” DP’s using the “C” Plate & 5” x 18-5/8” centralizer. Sting in.
      M/U & test cementing lines. Circulate for one casing annulus volume. While
          circulating, check the DP’s annulus for any return. Use all DP’s weight on the
          string, if observing any minor leakage through DP’s annulus.

    •  Cement casing as per program. Any minor adjustment regarding the corrected depth
          will be done in due time. Displace with 7 bbls mud (2 bbls Short). Sting out. Check
         the float. Circulate to clean the DP. POOH.

                    Sequence Of Drilling Operation

2.3  Sequence of Operations

     •  Make up 17 ½” BHA as per BHA Design. RIH, Tag top of cement. Drill out cement
     & shoe and rat hole cement.

     •   Drill ahead 17 ½” hole section with bit # 2 down to 480m, bit life, ROP: 5-6 m/hr.

     •   Circulate, clean hole, POOH and P/U new bit, RIH to bottom.

     •   Drill formation down to Casing Point @ 650m, bit # 3, ROP: 2-4 m/hr (289m inside
        Gachsaran Fm, Mbr.7-5). Use following parameters to drill this hole section:

         -WOB = 40- 60 klb
          -RPM = 90-130
          -GPM= 800-1000

     Note:
       - Adjust mud weight with respect to hole condition.
       - Drop Totco on each bit trip.
       - TD should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of rat hole for probable fillings.
       - The Section TD maybe changed based on formation tops detected by the well site
        geologist.
       - Do not forget to strap pipe while POOH.

     •  At section TD, circulate, clean hole. Perform wiper trip. Rest the hole for one hour
        and observe same when @ 18-5/8” shoe. RIH. Check for fills. CBU on bottom.
        Spot 100 bbl water loss controlled hi vis. pill @ bottom.

     •  POOH, If observed any indication of drag, run back to bottom. Wash and ream to
       make sure that the hole condition is good.

     •  R/U 13 3/8” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

                            Completion Program


1  Completion Surface Tests


      1.1   Drift and measure OD, ID and length of all completion accessories.

      1.2   Set dummy in 3SV L/nipple, connect Control line and test against dummy to 5000
             psi.

      1.3   Set plug in no-go L/nipple and test to 4500 psi, leave the plug set. (Check it to be
            retrievable).

      1.4  Open Expansion Joint fully and test to 4500 psi, set shear pins.


2  Completion Running Procedure


     - Remove wear bushing.

     - Rig up completion running tools.


      2.1  RIH with Mule shoe_Top no-go L.Nipple and…...etc. as per attached tally (with plug
            in no-go L/nipple) up to 3SV L/nipple.

   Note: Fill up 100m on top of no-go plug with Hi-Vis polymer pill (≥120sec) and the rest
   each 10 jt's with fresh water without debris.


      2.2   Connect control line to 3SV L/nipple and test to 5000 psi against dummy. Band
           control line straps to tighten same against tubing.


      2.3   Continue RIH as per tally, Pick up tubing hanger, Connect Control line to same and
             test to 3000 psi.


      2.4  P/U 7” Landing joint, connect to tubing hanger.


      2.5  R/U Pump unit.


      2.6   Flush and clean tubing head spool with water (or by air).

                         Completion Program



  2.7  Land tubing hanger and just touch tubing head spool.


  2.8   Space out and pull out the string 0.5ft up (for total stroke of 1ft).


  2.9   Pressurize tubing to 500 psi (3 min), 1000 psi (3 min), 1500 psi (3 min), 2000 psi (3
       min), 3000 psi (15 min) and monitor tubing anulus at same time (open tubing head
       spool side outlets).

Note: While pressurizing inside tubing, some back flow will be observed through tubing
head spool side outlet valves, it should be stopped after a while.


  2.10  Release pressure and over pull the string with 20,000 lb's.


  2.11  Apply proper weight down ward to shear off expansion joint shear pins, then set
       tubing hanger at its position in tubing head spool.


  2.12  Tighten tie down screws.


  2.13  Test inside tubing to 3000psi (15 min), close top rams and test Hanger to 3000 psi.


  2.14  Test annulus to 2000 psi (15 min), and back off Landing Joint.


  2.15  Set two-way check valve inside tubing hanger. Pressure test to 3000 psi (15min).


  2.16 N/D BOP's.

     dummy.


  2.18  Nipple up wellhead as per program. Pressure test same.
