5 Basic Well Requirements

  1) The vertical amplitude of swing between designed trajectory and the actual trajectory

     should be less than 2.0 m, while the lateral amplitude of swing should be no more than 10m;

  2) The rate of overall angle change before the KOP should be less than 2.5 °per 30m;

8  Drilling Operation Procedure
 8.1  Preparation and Drill 17-1/2" Hole Section (22-300m)

    1)  The 20"SOL X 21-1/4"2K should be installed before spud as Wellhead engineer
       recommends. And install 21-1/4"2k upper flange welded 20" casing nipple on the
        wellhead.
           M/U 1 stand DP +26" Bit to sweep the soil in 30” conductor and drill to about

      24m (a joint 20"casing below the cellar bottom).
           POOH and run 20" casing string to bottom and space out the 20" SOL x 21-

        1/4"2k wellhead as required .
           Cement the annular between 30" conductor and 20" casing.
            Cut the 30" conductor and 20" casing as required, grind the top end of 20"casing

       and 30"conductor, install the 20" SOL x 21-1/4"2k wellhead and Bowl protector.

              Install a 21-1/4" 2k upper flange welded and bell nipple.

    1)  Drill the first and second stands to avoid washing out the conductor. Pump Hi-Vis pill to
       sweep hole when every stand drills down.

    2)  Go on drilling to section TD 300 m.

    3)  Wiper trip should be conducted 150-200m or as per hole condition.

    4) POOH to surface and run back to bottom, circulation again.

    5)  Pump Hi-Vis pill. Circulate hole clean. Drop single shot survey Tool.

    6) POOH to surface, retrieve single shot Tool.

    7)  Run 13-3/8" casing as per casing tally. Set the 13-3/8" casing hanger in 21-1/4" 2K
        casing head, but release the hang weight of the casing not more than 1ton to prevent the
        20" conductor sink in the earth, the hook must hang the other weight of the casing string.

    8)  Conduct cementing job as per cementing program. If the cementing plug is bumping,
         increases pump pressure to 2000 psi (Green Pressure Test).

    9) WOC. N/U 21-1/4" × 13-5/8" casing head. N/U BOP stack and Conduct BOP and
        casing head Pressure test.
 8.2  Drill 12-1/4" Hole Section (300-1562m)

    1)  RIH BHA with PDC to tag TOC. Drill cement to top of float collar. Circulate bottoms
         up.

   3)   If the plug-bumping failed, pressure  test 13-3/8" casing to 300psi/5mins & 2,000

   8)  WOC. N/D BOP, N/U 13-5/8" × 11" × 5 K casing head. N/U BOP stack, BOP and
       casing head pressure test.
8.3  Drill 8-1/2" Pilot Hole Section (1562-3141m)

   1)  RIH to tag TOC carefully. Drill CMT to top of float collar. Circulate bottoms up. If the
       plug-bumping failed, pressure test 9-5/8" casing to 300psi/5mins & 3,000 psi/30mins @
       1.20sg mud, record the results in DDR.

   2)  Drill with KCl-Polymer mud to section TD @ 3141 m MD RKB. Take survey every
       200-400 m.

   3)  Wipe trip every 200-400m or according to hole condition, drill to TD, wiper trip and
        circulation. POOH to last casing shoe and run back to bottom, circulation again.

   4)  Drop Multi shot and POOH.

   5)  Perform wireline logging as per logging program.

   6)  Run 5" DP to 3130m and circulate the holeclean and set cement plug about 350m of
        length which should from the Bottom of S5 to the Top of S1. The depth will be confirm
       with RE in order to seal off the waterleg of Sarvak.

   7) POOH to 2750m, circulate the hole clean and set 5m3 Hi-vis drill fluid at bottom.

   8) POOH to 2710m, circulate the hole clean and set cement plug about 200m of length.

   9) POOH to 2480m and circulate the hole clean.

 8.4   Drill 8-1/2" Horizontal Section (2610-4140.42m (MD))

   1)  RIH the build-up BHA to the sidetrack depth, side drill the directional hole form the
        pilot hole.

   2)  Directional drill to the end of first build-up section and make sure this depth is about
     30m (TVD) above S1 or when drill into the Lafan formation, POOH and change from
    MWD to LWD.

   3)  Continuing directional drill to target A, then POOH change Build-up BHA to holding
     BHA.

   4)  Continuing directional drill to TD.

   5)  The directional well engineer will decide to adjust the borehole trajectory scheme
       according to changes of the borehole trajectory. Drill the hold-on section with the single
       bending screw of combination drilling method. Adjust the drilling method and control
       changes of the deviation and azimuth according to inclination survey data from time to
        time, so as to ensure the trajectory is smooth.

   6)  For kick-off section and hold-on section, MWD/LWD is adopted for deviated well
        section survey. If the borehole trajectory is greatly deviated from the design trajectory,
           it is necessary to enhance the frequency of inclination survey and adopt measures in
        time.

   7)  Control the drilling speed to avoid pressure surge and stabilize the borehole wall. Adjust
     mud properties according to the drilling design and borehole conditions

   18) Drill out cement and RIH to TD. Circulate the horizontal hole clean.

   19) Displace 1.15±0.05SG Mud below Target A.

   20) POOH to 7” casing shoe, circulated with 1.32±0.02SG Mud.

   21) RIH 7" scraper to scrape the interval for setting 4-1/2" liner hanger and 7" production

   23) Set the liner hanger & release running tool.

   24) Before pick up the running tool, pump 2m3 spacer fluids, pick up one stand and then
       displace original mud with completion fluid reversely.

   25) POOH the running tool to surface.

9.1 Run completion string

      1) P/U & M/U 3-1/2 mule shoe, shear out ball seat, selective landing nipple w/no-go,

      3) Install injection line protector across tubing coupling during RIH.
      4) Drift the tubing and pressure test each 1000m.
      5) Keep the SSD closed during RIH.
      6) When run SPM, the dummy valve should be installed at surface.
      7) When M/U TRSCSSSV, install ¼" control line to TRSCSSSV and pressure test control
          line. Keep pressure applied to ensure TRSCSSSV open. Install TRSCSSSV protector
         for the injection line & control line.
      8) Reverse-circulate 1 round when completion strings run to bottom.

9.2 Space out
      1)  Space out as required with pup joints, to sting mule shoe into 4-1/2" liner hanger 2m±.
      2)  Pick up & M/U tubing hanger with double pin crossover. M/U landing joint.
      3)  Cut control line and injection line about 2-3m above tubing hanger, wrap the lines
        below the tubing hanger and feed control line & injection line through hanger.

9.3 Run Gauge Cutter
      1)  Rig up the slick line unit.
      2)  Open TRSCSSSV.
      3)  Drift wellbore to 2900m (≤45º) with gauge cutter which OD is 2.8". POOH.
      4)  R/D slickline unit.

9.4 Land tubing hanger
      1) Land tubing hanger into tubing head spool.
      2) Lock all tie-down bolts.
      3) Close variable Pipe Ram BOP.
      4) Open gate valves on tubing head spool.
      5) Pressure to 5000psi /15min in annulus through kill manifold to test Tubing Hanger Seal,
        observe any pressure loss.
      6) Monitor if any fluid leak from gate valve.

9.5 Set production packer
     1)  Drop ball into tubing.

     2)  Pump the ball to ball seat.

     3)  Pump pressure to 4600psi to set packer.

     4)  Close annular BOP. Pump 3000psi from annulus to verify the packer setting. Bleed off

         pressure slowly.

     5)  Pump pressure to 5000psi to shear the ball seat.

     6)  Land down landing joint.

9.6 N/D BOP and N/U X-mas tree
    1)  Set BPV

    2)  N/D bell nipple and BOP stack.

    3)  N/U tree adapter and X-mas tree.

    4)  Feed control line and CILs and cable through tree bonnet, install fittings and needle

         valves. Pressure test control line 5000 psi/15 mins, bleed off pressure to zero. Pressure

          test CIL 2000 psi/15 mins, bleed off pressure to zero.

    5)  Retrieve BPV and install TWCV.

    6)  Pressure test tree adapter cavity with test pump to 300psi/5mins, 5,000psi/15mins.

        Pressure tests the X-mas tree body to 300psi/5 mins, 5,000psi/15 mins.

    7)  Function test SSV actuator.

    8)  Pull out TWCV.

    9)  R/U Lubricator. Pressure test lubricator to 5000psi.

    10) Run slickline tools to open SSD.

    11) Displace completion fluid with water in tubing and diesel in annular above SSD.

    12) Close SSD.

    13) Pump pressure (Annulus) to 3000psi to test SSD.

9.7 Rig move
    1)  Close swab valve &wing gate valves on sides, open upper master valve, lower master

        valve and SSV above master valve.

    2)  Open SCSSSV.

    3)  Record SIWHP.

    4)  Bleed off control line pressure to close TRSCSSSV.

    5)  Bleed off WHP, and measure return. And observe WHP for 30 min,  if pressure is

         positive, remedial plan will be discussed.

    6)   Install BPV.

    7)  Close upper master valve. And keep gate wing valves in close position.

    8)  Check the annulus pressure; bleed off to zero if there is any.

    9)  Rig down and move out. Any damage to the X-tree will be forbidden during rig down

       and move.

9.8 Clean up test
    1)  Retrieve BPV.

    2)  Open adjustable choke slowly to flow-back the well,  if can’t flow back naturally,

         nitrogen or fresh water will be considered to inject by CTU.

9.9 Acidizing
Reservoir and production departments will decide when or whether perform the acidizing job.

11 Well Control Requirements

    Drilling contractor must strictly follow IPS, Drilling Operations Manual (YAP1-GGGGGG-
   DRDE-MAOP-1002-G01), Well Control Manual (YAP1-GGGGGG-DRDE-MAWC-1001-
    G00), SIPC petroleum and  natural gas industry standards 《Well control Technique
    Specification for Well Drilling》, SY/ T6426-2005 and《Well Control Equipment Match,
    Fixing and Maintenance》, SY/ T5964-2006.
11.1 Well Control during POOH, RIH and Wire-line Logging

   The well control while running, pull drill pipe and wire-line logging is important and
     critical, best practices shall be followed, including but not limited to:
      Wipe trip and flow check before pull out drill pipe, monitor holes condition by trip tank
       during all of the drilling process.

      Control running drill pipe speed to avoid any risk of losing in weakness formation.

       Wire-line BOP should available in wire-line logging operation.
11.2 Well Control during Running Casing

   The well control while running casing is important and critical, best practices shall be
    followed, including but not limited to:
      Change out pipe size rams with casing size rams prior to running 7"liner for expected
       hydrocarbon bearing formation.
      The crossover for casing connection to  drill pipe must available on floor. A good
        practice is to keep a joint of DP with casing crossover at lower end and full opened
        safety valve at upper end to be ready for picking up on V-door.
11.3 Requirements of weighting material storage

    Barite should be used as weighting material. The stimulation will be conducted to reduce the
    affection of Barite to the reservoir.
   The kill fluid should be prepared before spud in, and settling stability should be less than
    0.03 g/cm3. The kill fluid density should be increased if the active mud weight changes.
