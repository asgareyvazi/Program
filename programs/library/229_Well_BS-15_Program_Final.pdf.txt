1.  Introduction

This document covers Workover#1 of well BS-15 of Bahregansar field, I.R.Iran. This
well was drilled as a development well to produce oil from Burgan "B" reservoir on
WHP-6 platform, but due to stuck 13 5/8" casing at 1000m plan was changed and
well was drilled to 2300m and completed as oil well in Ghar reservoir, but was
suspended due  to lack of T.H.S w/5000  psi working pressure and completion
equipments.



Main Objective:

  The main objective is to change tubing head spool, drill out cement plug, clean hole

   to TD, run completion string, perforate 7" liner & flow the well.


Location map:

The well BS-15 was drilled in Bahregansar field, Iran, and is suspended now.

                Surface Location:  E: 371,950 m

                               N: 3,313,050m

The main headlines of this program are as follows:


1. Move & skid Rig SHENGLI-10 on well BS-15.

2.  Centre the rig, record air gap, RKB-MSL and report same.

3.  Connect kill and choke lines to well head and test same to 2500psi.

4.  Record wellhead and Ann. Pressures. (If possible, bleed off pressure).

5.  Circulate through wellhead & observe well. Confirm the well is static.

6. Remove 11"x4  1/16" -3K  adaptor  flange. N/D 11"x11"-3K T.H.Spool &

   13 5/8"x11"-3K adaptor flange & 13 5/8"-5k x 3k DSA. N/U 13 5/8" x 11"-5K

  DSA & 11"x11"-5K T.H.Spool.

7. N/U BOP stack and pressure test.

8. RIH 8 1/2" bit to 1645m, displace well w/69 pcf salt water mud, drill out cement

   plug to 7" L.lap, POOH.

9. RIH 6 1/8" bit to 2292m, circulate & clean hole, POOH.

10. Run 7"x9-5/8" tandem scraper and clean packer setting depth.

11. Displace well with non solid mud.

12. Run CBL/VDL/CCL/GR log in 7” liner as TLC.

13. Perform perforations as per program as TCP and check for injectivity with 1500

    psi.

14. R/U & run completion string as per completion department program .

15. Secure the well, N/D BOP, N/U well head and test.

16. R/U Coil TBG & RIH. Acidize well as per production Dep. instructions, flow the

   well & let the well be cleaned.

Secure the well and Release the rig.


Attentions:
   •  The detailed program is available in this document under the heading
      “Well Securing Procedure” & “Workover Program”.
   •  All depths in this program calculated considering 26.5m  RKB-MSL,
       therefore a correction depth should be considered for the new RKB-MSL.

 3. Drilling History


26” hole section:

Rig Sheng Li-10 was spudded on 1390/10/25 @ 14:00. Sea bed was tagged at 49.5

m by 26" bit, hole was drilled to 150m, the hole swap and conditioned, 20" casing

was run to 148m and cemented by stinger  , casing was fixed on platform, N/U 20

3/4"-3k C.H.H.,17 1/2" bit was run, cement and rat hole was drilled to 150m.


17 1/2” hole section:

17 1/2" hole was drilled with sea water to 170m, hole displaced by 75 pcf mud and

drilling continued to 756m with 75-76 pcf mud and 15-25 BPH loss, POOH for bit

change with tight hole and over pull, string got stuck at 370m, lost rotation and

circulation, worked on  stuck with over  pull and applying torque  to  establish

circulation and rotation, finally string became free. Drilling was continued to 1375m

and mud weight was increased to 81 pcf to overcome on tight hole, POOH to 1100m,

POOH was continued with over pull and back ream to 1000m, POOH to surface and

changed bit, some pieces of bit cones was broken and left in hole, drilling vertically

was continued to 1572 m and mud weight was increased to 85 pcf at 1541m due to

entrance to Gachsaran formation. Hole was condition to running casing. 13 5/8"

casing was run to 1000m, circulate and made shoe and collar to float position, casing

was stuck. Over pull, pipe lax was un-successful to free the string, mud weight

decreased and let the well flowing with no results, much efforts was done to free

stuck, no success, cement casing at this depth and rig was released at 21:00 on

22/11/1390.


Shengli-10 rig came back on BS-15 well and was spud at 08:00 on 30/03/1391.
Made two holes on 13 5/8" casing and pulled  it to make center with casing head

housing. Set 20 3/4" x 13 5/8" casing slips difficulty, casing neck was final cut and

grid to reduce to 13 5/8", N/Up 20 3/4"-3k x 13 5/8"-5k Casing Head Spool, N/U

BOP and pressure tested same. 12 1/4" bit was run to 960m, cement and shoe track

was drilled, last hole washed and reamed down to 1270m with 82 pcf mud, cement

plug was set at 1270m and hole was side track from 1200m.

12 1/4” hole section:

12 1/4" hole directionally was drilled from 1200 to 1262m with 82 pcf mud, 4.5 BPH

bbls of caustic pill,  drilling was continued to 2105m with 130-132 pcf mud, string

got stuck while backed ream, many efforts was made to free the string with no

success, mud weight was decreased to let the well flowing but there was no results,

finally string twisted off at 1719m from body of 5" drill pipe, 8 1/8" overshot was run

& worked on string with no result, string mechanically backed off POOH and 216.3m

of fish was retrieved(170 m of fish was left in hole and top of fish at 1933.3m that

included 1 stand 5" drill pipe, 4 stands 5" HWDP, X/O, 8" SNMDC, 8" TM sub,

8"NMDC, 8" Pony NMDC, 8" F.Sub, 8" DC, bit sub and 12 1/4" PDC bit), tried to

retrieved remain fish with negative result, run FPIT tools to 1602m and checked the

free position stretch was 75% & TQ was 92%, @ 1808m.

Shoot bar was run to 1888m, string electrically was backed off, POOH to surface and

378 m of string was left in hole, top of fish was 1727m. Hole was condition with bit,

string was stuck again, worked on  string, string came free with over-pull and

pumping pipe lax and caustic pills, POOH and L/D bit, string pin down run to and

screwed in fish, many efforts to release the fish but with no success, finally string

electrically backed off at 1727m and 50 bbls of cement was set at top of fish and hole

was side tracked at 1643m.

9.  Well Operation Procedure

    1. Move & skid Rig SHENGLI-10 on well BS-15.

    2.  Centre the rig, record air gap, RKB-MSL and report same.

    3.  Connect kill and choke lines to well head and test same to 2500psi.

    4.  Record wellhead and Ann. Pressures. (If possible, bleed off pressure).

    5.  Circulate through wellhead, observe and make sure the well is static.

    6. Remove 11"x4 1/16" -3K adaptor flange. N/D 11"x11"-3K T.H.Spool &

      13 5/8"x11"-3K adaptor flange & 13 5/8"-5k x 3k DSA. N/U 13 5/8" x 11"-

     5K DSA & 11"x11"-5K T.H.Spool, inject plastic packing to pack 9 5/8"

       casing neck & test p.seals.


           Note: Supply 9 5/8", 47# pup joint on wellsite to weld & extend 9

           5/8" casing neck in case of need.


    7. N/U BOP stack and pressure test.

    8. RIH 8 1/2" bit w/5" DPs to 1645m, displace well w/69 pcf salt water mud,

         drill out cement plug to 7" L.lap (1910m), circulate & clean hole, POOH to

        surface.


           Note1: 69 pcf mud will impose 200 psi over pressure to Ghar

            reservoir @ 7" liner shoe (2300m,MD / 2103.9m,TVD).


           Note2: In case of observe any flow while drilling cement plug,

            displace well w/72 pcf mud.


    9. RIH 6 1/8" bit w/3 1/2" & 5" DPs to 2292m, circulate & clean hole, POOH to

        surface.

    10. Run 7"x9-5/8" tandem scraper and clean packer setting depth.

    11. Displace well with 69 pcf non solid mud, POOH to surface.

    12. R/U logging tools, run CBL/VDL/CCL/GR log in 7” liner W/DPs as TLC.

  TCP and check for injectivity with 1500 psi.


        Note: Perforation depth will be issued in due time based on result of

          log.


14. R/U & run completion string as per completion department program, set the

15. Secure the well, N/D BOP, N/U well head and test.

16. R/U Coiled tubing & RIH , Acidize well as per production Dep. instructions ,

   flow the well & let the well clean, Record flowing pressure.

17. Secure the well and Release the rig
