    The SPD9 16” Section’s Pitfalls





                  Avoid unnecessary sliding

          Be aware of losses in Fahliyan Formation

       Break circulation carefully according to procedure

            Do not exceed 1.15 SG mud weight

            Reduce ROP in Fahliyan cherty Zone

Be aware of potential HC layers in Dariyan Fm. Avoid swabbing when POOH

1   Dispensations

       Cementing to surface instead of 200m inside 18 5/8” Csg.

2  Geology and Formation Evaluation

2.1  TD / Casing points
       The 16” hole will be drilled 20-25mMD into Hith formation.
2.2   Cuttings sampling
      No cutting sampling

2.3 LWD / MWD
      DIR/GR

3   Operational Sequence

        1.  Prepare the 1.13 SG KCl /Glydril mud.
        2.  Install the 21 ¼” BOP
        3.  Test the BOP to 113 bar and function test same if not tested on stump.
        4.  Install the wear bushing
        5.  Pressure test the 18 5/8” casing, if not already done (113 Bar)
        6. MU 16” BHA on 5 ½” DP and RIH. P/U DP as necessary.
        7.  Perform Well Control and H2S Drill
        8.  Drill shoe track and 3 m of new formation while displacing to 1.13 sg KCl /Glydril /Polymer
        mud
        9.  Perform FIT to 1.30 sg
       10. Drill 16” hole to 20-25 m MD into the Hith Fm.
       11. Circulate hole clean and POOH
       12. Retrieve wear bushing (No need to wash WH after pulling WB)
       13. Run 13 3/8” casing and fill every joint with 1.13 sg.
       14. Circulate and cement 13 3/8” casing
       15. Test casing to 240 bar when bumping the plug
       16. Flush WH
       17. Set and test seal assembly and test same to 125 bar
       18. Nipple down the 21¼” BOP and move to next well
       19. Install the Unitized well head assembly (not on rig time)

4   Directional Drilling

4.1  BHA
        •   The plan is to drill the section with 1 BHA run
        •   The primary BHA will be a 9 ½’’ AKO X-treme motor assembly with 0,8 deg bend.
             Increase bend if low inclination in 24” section.
        •   All BHA components and DP to be drifted to 2 5/8’’.
        •   Use a Corrosion Ring in a DC connection.


4.2  Max hours on rotation BHA string components
         8’’ Jar /Accelerator               : 100 rotation hrs
      X/O-DC                              : 300 rotation hrs
      DP etc                                 : 1000 rotation hrs
       9 ½” MWD                         : 150 rotation hrs / 200 circulation hrs



4.3   Bits and nozzles
       •  TFA: 1.37-1.50 in2
       •  Baker – 16” HCM605 - w/10x14/32" nozzle
        •  Smith – 16” MGR75HSPX - w/7x16/32" nozzle

      Backup bits:
       •  Smith - 16” MGGH+
       •  Smith - 16” MGS01B

5  Mud

       •  KCl /Glydril / Polymer mud system will be used
       • MW 1.13sg - 1.15 sg below Kazhdumi fm.
         Keep 1.15 sg drilling Kazhdumi fm.
       Max MW: 1.15 sg
       •  Use 255 mesh screens and two centrifuges.
       •  Use fresh water in premix to lower weight
       •  Use 3% Glycol
       •  Continuous add corrosion inhibitor and oxygen scavenger

       •  In case of loss, Ref. table and decision tree.

6  Casing and Cementing

6.1  Casing

        •  13 3/8” / 68ppf  / L-80 / Antares ER
        •  Measure casing capacity
        •  Well will be tested to 240 bar after bump plug.
        •  Seal assembly to be tested to 125 bar.


6.2   Centralizers

        •   2/jnt on the 3 first joints (shoe, int., float)
        •   1/jnt from 2 joints inside previous casing to bottom
        •  Special 17” x 13 3/8” centralizers in open hole
        •  2 spiral centralizers inside 18 5/8” shoe to divert the cement flow all around casing.

6.3  Casing Running

        •  Use Flush mounted spider
        •  Use safety clamp when string weight is less than 13 MT
        •   La-fleur to be used if preferred by the rig.
        •  Focus on communication between Weatherford and driller when opening FMS.
            Ref. two earlier incidents where casing slips was opened before elevator was latched.
       •   Casing running speed casing/OH: 60 sec/jnts.
       •  Oil or grease FMS regularly to avoid jamming.

6.4  Cementing

        •  Cement to surface
        TOC to be reported in DDR
        •  Displace well to 1.13 sg mud after csg at TD. Start pumping with low rate, increase in steps.
            Stable return in each step before increasing to next step
        •  Pump 16 m3 SW with H2S scavenger
        •  8 m3 1.13 sg Spacer with H2S scavenger
        •  1.44 sg lead cement with 0% open hole excess with TOC at well head
        •  10 m3 1.90 sg tail cement
        •  Displace cement with maximum loss free rate (not below 1500lpm) or max 2000 lpm.
          Use 2000lpm when 1.90sg tail is going out of shoe.
        •  Max pump efficiency to be used: 95% (expected efficiency 97% )
        •  Measure losses after cement is starting to go out of shoe and report in DDR.
        •  Flush WH properly after finished displacing.
        •  Evaluate stage tool (MSIPC) if large losses have been experienced during drilling.
         One set with MSIPC and FC w/Baffle plate will be available with A-ER pin and box.

                           STANDARD
                            LOST CIRCULATION RECIPE

                      SEEPAGE LOSS SOLUTIONS ( < 3 M3/ HR )
            Initial attempts to solve losses should be made by pre-treating the system with CaCo3 F&M at 10
        kg/m3 each and 6 kg/m3 of Mix II F or EPI Seal XF. If losses continued, then pump concentrated pill
          of the followings:

                          During drilling Loss < 15 and >3: 8 m3 Pill @ 60 kg/m3
                        Product            Concentration ( kg/m3 )
                          CaCo3 F                     10
                          CaCo3 M                     10
                           Mix II F                     10
                          Mix II M                     10
                             Mica F                      5
                            EPI Seal XF                    10
                         Kwik Seal F                    5

                            While POOH and High Loss Rate ( >15 m3/ HR )
        8 m3 Pill @ 114 KG/M3 ( maximum, as per BHI MWD/Motor Limitation )
                        Product            Concentration ( kg/m3 )
                          CaCo3 F                     20
                          CaCo3 M                     20
                           Mix II F                     20
                          Mix II M                     20
                             Mica F                      5
                            EPI Seal XF                    25
                            KwikSeal F                     5

Procedure
       •  Spot in open hole, Pumping rate through MWD/Motor: 1000 lpm
       •   Pull above loss zone and circulate to squeeze bridging material to fractures.
       •  Slowly increase circulation to minimum rate required for drilling.
       •  Increase to 2000 lpm to clean the tool

   Note:

    1. Mud Engineer to supervise the mixing of LCM pills.

     2.  Mud Engineer to visually inspect all LCM being used.

     3.  Mud Engineer to make a clear instruction to the rig mixing crew that if they see any change in size of a par-
         ticular LCM grade confirmed initially they must STOP mixing and inform the mud engineer. Would prefer to
        put laminated instruction at mixing area.

     4.  Company representative, mud engineer and MWD/DD engineers need to agree on the size of LCM mate-
           rial and the above concentrations prior to pumping to avoid plug tools.

     5.  Mud Engineer to ensure no coarse LCM to be mixed in any pills, unless a circulation sub is put in the BHA.
        Otherwise, ONLY fine and Medium grades are required.

     6.  To avoid confusion, Mud engineer to backload any Coarse LCM if circulation sub will not be used.

     7.    If circulation sub is used, coarse materials such as Nut Plug, Kwik Seal M and CaCo3 can be utilized. This
          is only during severe downhole losses.
