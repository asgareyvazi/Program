HSE Focal points:

     Special attention to HSE focuses must be paid to dry test operation since
          Fahliyan formation contains high H2S content. Prior to break tool, HSE
           officer must be on rig floor and review Job Safety Analysis (JSA) and make
          sure all personnel fully understands the risks of the job specially H2S.
     Safety and operations meeting must be held prior to operation.
     Personal safety/H2S Safety equipment.
     Refer to safe handling/ lifting instruction.
