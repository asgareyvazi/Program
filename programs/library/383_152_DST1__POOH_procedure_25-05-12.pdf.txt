                                 KEPCO                        DST #1


      MEA / IRG / KSH          DST & Sub sea POOH procedure                  25-May-2012


        DST&SUBSEA TOOLS PULL OUT OF HOLE PROCEDURE


    Current condition of string as bellow:

    The IRDV TV is in close position and packer is unseated and lubricator valve
    plus 20 joint are out of hole (the packer is on 2223 meter depth).


     1. Safety meeting should be held on the rig floor prior to POOH DST tools.
         Do not turn the string to the left or right in under any circumstances during POOH
               in 7 inch liner.
          The SLB subsea engineer to be present on subsea unit to activate and close
             sentree valve in case of emergency.

   1.   Set the packer at its current depth under SLB DST supervisor instruction.
   2.    Install safety valve on the tubing (In open Position).
   3.   Close Lower pipe Ram.
   4.   Apply pressure in annulus in order to open and lock open IRDV TV.
   5.   Apply sufficient pressure in annulus in order to activate down hole safety valve
       (PFSV) to lock closed.
   6.  Open lower pipe ram.
   7.   Unset the packer.
   8.  POOH and lay down tubing in single joint.
   9.   After POOH 30 Joints, For break out each joint, first turn joint slowly ½ turn to
       observed any leakage and then break out completely.
  10.  Remove diverter bag one stand prior to reach sentree on surface.
  11.   L/D santree.
  12.   Continue POOH to surface.
