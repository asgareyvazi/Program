    2. Job Procedure

                                  Job Procedure

                                                 Pre Job Procedure

       1-Clean up tanks (pump truck's tanks) and preparing chemicals for mixing fluid.

       2-Perform pressure test cement line to 500 psi for 5 minutes and the slowly increase the pressure to 3000 psi and hold for 5
       minutes. Then release the pressure to the pump trucks.

        3- Flush Cement silos hoses, mud pits and pump trucks unit lines properly.

        4- Check the rig mud balance with fresh water and make sure it’s calibrated.

        5- Prepare pit management design and Hold Pre job safety meeting with involved personnel and arrange with mud engineer to
      measure mud & cement loss/gain during cement job.

                                               Job Procedure

       1-Prepare spacer and slurry mix fluid in mud tanks..

          2-Fill tanks with Drill water due to dead volume tanks for mixing fluids.

        3- start mixing cement additives and preparing mixing fluids as per company man"s instruction in following steps:

         * Spacer Mixing procedure:
          • Add Fresh Water to Mud tank.(without dead volume we need 76.2 bbl)Fresh Water
          • Add XC Polymer to fresh water and allow mixing for 15 minutes.
          • Add Spacer to fresh water and allow mixing for 15 minutes.
          • Add Solvent to fresh water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.
          • Add Surfactant to fresh water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.
          • Add Anti Foam to fresh water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.
          • Add Barite to fresh water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.

         * Cement Solution mixing procedure:
       Lead cement mixing procedure:
           • Add Drill Water to Mud tank.(without dead volume we need 408 bbl) Drill Water
           • Add Antifoam to fresh water and allow mixing for 5 minutes .
           • Add Bentonit and allow mixing for 45 minutes .
           • Add Retro120A HT and allow mixing for 15 minutes .
           • Add Turbo-1B and allow mixing for 15 minutes .

         Tail cement mixing procedure:
          • Add Drill Water to Mud tank.(without dead volume we need 214 bbl) Drill Water
          • . Add Salt and allow mixing for 45 minutes
          • Add Antifoam to fresh water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.
          • . Add O-R12 HT and allow mixing for 15 minutes
          • . Add FLC and allow mixing for 15 minutes
          • . Add Turbo-1B and allow mixing for 15 minutes

        4- Before running casing, check cement head specially plunger of released cement plug and put the top plug.

        5- After running Casing, install circulating swage, start circulation at least 1.5 casing cap volume.

        6- -Stop circulation, connect 13 3/8" cement head and cement line.

      7-pump 40 bbl CHEMICAL WASH with 4 bpm with mud pump .

      8-pump 100 bbl SPACER with 6 bpm with mud pump .

       9-Mix and pump 600 bbl lead cement slurry ( 105 pcf – pump rate :10 bpm)

       10- Mix and pump 412 bbl tail cement slurry.( 125 pcf- pump rate : 6 bpm)

        11- Switch to mud pump and start displacement with mud . Displace cement with 993 bbl mud with 15 bpm rate and 40 bbl with
      3 bpm , in case of not observing Bumping pressure, pump additional displacement liquid (mud) (25% Shoe track volume).

       12- Rig Down cement lines.
      13 - End of job.

                                                   Caution and Notes

        1- Put slurry cement samples in water bath .Retain 2.5 gallon of mixing fluid from the tanks and 5 kg of the G cement used in
       the job.

        2- Actual displacement volume shall be calculated on rig by company man.

           All numbers & volumes related to drill string capacity, pump rate and well geometry have been assumed based on
                       initial assumptions however actual figures will be calculated and executed as per company plan.
