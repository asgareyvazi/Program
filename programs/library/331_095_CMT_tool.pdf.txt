 TECH NI CAL DE SCRIPTION


 This tool is run in con junc tion with, and fit ted above, the 30" Cam Ac tu ated Run ning Tool
 (re fer to OSP 6068).


 The pur pose of the ce ment sub is to fa cilitate a ce ment top up oper ation with out the require -
 ment to re lease the 30" CART from the con ductor hous ing.


 The cement sub is con fig ured with a 6-5/8" API reg ular box up by pin down and a 2" WECO
 1502 fe male side out let. The sub is fit ted with an in ter nal isolation sleeve which is shear
 pinned in position and the iso lation sleeve seals off the side out let from the through bore.
 The sub has a 2" nom inal through bore for reg ular ce ment ing oper ations.


 The exter nal annular sleeve al lows the main body to rotate nor mally in the drill string.


 Acti vation of the in ner sleeve is achieved by dropping a steel ball down the string. This will
 eventu ally seat on the in ner sleeve. A build up of pres sure of in ex cess of 450 psi behind the
 ball will break the shear pin, al lowing the inner sleeve to move - this will open up the side
 port for diverted circulation.
