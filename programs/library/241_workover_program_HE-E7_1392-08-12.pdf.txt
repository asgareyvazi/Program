2   Introduction

          This program covers Workover of well HE-E7 of Hengam field, I.R.Iran.

         Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information from

         well HE-E7.

         Note-2: H2S equipment and H2S service should be ready on rig site at the start of
         Operation.

3  Main Objective

        The main objective is to pull out completion string due to observe oil & gas leakage

        on completion string & wellhead.

5  Program Main Head lines

     1.    Skid & Spud the rig.

     2.     Kill the well as per workover procedure.

     3.   N/D X-mass tree, N/U BOP stack.

     4.    Pull out completion string to surface and lay down same.

     5.   Clean well to top of completion packer with 8-1/2" bit.

     6.   Run completion string as per completion engineer instructions.

     7.   Secure the well, N/D BOP, N/U X-Mass tree, flow the well.

     8.    Acidize & Nitrogen lift the well with coil tubing then flow the well.

     9.    Secure the well, Release the rig.



          Attentions:
        The detailed program is illustrated here after in this document under the head of

        "Workover Program".

         Important Note: All depths in this program calculated considering 32m RKB-MSL,

         therefore a correction depth should be considered for the new RKB-MSL.

12 Workover Program


     1.    Skid & spud the rig over well HE-E7.

     2.   Centre derrick, Record & report Air gap, RKB to THF and MSL.

     3.   Hook up kill lines to TBG Ann. & TBG. Sides and test same to 5000psi.

     4.   Record wellhead and TBG Ann pressure.

     5.   Check 3SV by releasing pressure of control line, bleed off well head pressure to zero,

         monitor & test well head pressure for any build up to make sure 3SV is functioning.

     6.   Release Ann. pressure & check for any build up while 3SV is closed.

     7.   Check & make sure 3SV can be opened, Kill the well thru tubing with sea water,.

  Note:
   While bullhead well thru tubing monitor pumping  pressure, notice pumping pressure

    not exceed from 2000psi over shut in well head pressures.

   Base on reservoir data 67 PCF WBM will impose 247 Psi over pressure to the Ilam

    Reservoir pressure@ 9-5/8" casing shoe (4067m, MD / 3699m, TVD).
   Base on well history, while drilling 8-1/2" hole section with 77 PCF mud, heavy to

    complete losses occurred @ 4068 to 4082m. try to cure losses was not successful, so

    F/4082m to 4268m(TD) was drilled with sea water with 200 – 300 BPH losses.
   Adjust MW with respect to the hole condition.
   Weight up material (Salt & Limestone) should be ready on board for possible need

    to kill the well.



     8.   R/U wire line lubricator & BOP, pressure test stuffing box & wire line BOP to 2500psi,

         RIH with 3.5" gauge ring to top of 3SV while pumping sea water through tubing.

     9.   Pressure tests Ann to 1000 psi & check pressure from test ports of bottom section of

        X-Mass tree & make sure the tubing hanger sealing is ok.

    10.  Try to cure losses by pumping Hi Vis pill & shell fish pill & make sure the tubing is

            static.

    11.  Released Ann pressure & check for any build in Ann for 3hrs.

    12.  R/U wire line lubricator & BOP, pressure test stuffing box & wire line BOP to 2500psi,

         while pump in control line & open same, RIH with wire line try to install protection

         sleeve inside 3SV, then RIH with string puncher to ±2690m, MD, punch the first

         4-1/2" joint above the SSD, while apply pressure in control line continuously to keep

       3SV open (if protection sleeve failed to install).

    13.   Kill the well thru tubing with sea water then circulate the well through tubing & get

         return from Ann and balance mud weight.

 Note:
   If not success for punch tubing, try to cure losses, make sure the well is static, lubricate

   Ann with 74 PCF and continue operation from item 14 to end.

   (Base on reservoir data 74 PCF WBM will impose 236 psi over pressure to the Ilam

    Reservoir pressure@ completion packer (2731m, MD / 2636m, TVD))
   If could not punch and secure the well with 3SV & BPV and well is on loss from tubing

    side, supply suitable tubing plug to install or tubing cutter on well site
   Circulate across well head to fill the well during from item 14 to 22.

    14.  Close 3SV & install back pressure in tubing hanger.

    15.  Remove kill line from X-mass tree. Do not disconnect kill line from Ann until the BOP

         stack is installed.

    16.  N/D X-mass tree, Check tubing hanger thread, make sure it is ok, N/U 11"-10K * 18-

        3/4" 15-K DSA, riser, BOP stack 18-3/4" 15-K, bell nipple, over shot, diverter system.

    17.  Pump sea water in tubing, Make up 4-1/2", NVAM landing joint and screw in tubing

         hanger, perform body pressure test of BOP, loose tie down screws, Pressure test

         glands to 3500 psi and pull the completion string up to ±5Klbs over pull & rotate

          string 13-14 turns clockwise to Release anchor seal from packer as per completion

        engineer instructions.

 Note:

   In case of shortage of 4-1/2” NVAM landing joint or thread damage of TBG hanger,

   supply  4-1/2” spear on well site to pull completion string.
   In case of parted completion string supply Complete fishing tools for recover fish.

    18.  POOH completion string, L/D tubing hanger, back pressure valve, circulates & flow

         check, Pull out completion string to surface and lay down same.

    19.  RIH with 8-1/2" bit, clean well to top of packer completion @± 2731m, CBU, OBS

          well, POOH to surface, L/D bit.

    20.  P/U new anchor seal and RIH with DPS, sting in completion packer, pressure test seal

        packer to 1000 psi, POOH to surface & L/D anchor seal.

    21.  P/U new anchor seal and RIH with completion string, tag top of packer, perform

        space out, sting in packer, pressure test seal packer to 1000 psi & over pull to 20KLB,

        Shear pin expansion joint & land tubing hanger inside tubing head spool (with back

         pressure valve) then test same to 1000psi, tighten tie down screws, pressure test

         glands, back off landing joint & L/D same.

 Note:
  Completion program will be issued by IOOC completion department in due time.

    22.  Close 3SV, N/D diverter system, over shot, bell nipple, BOP, riser & N/U BTM section

         of X-Mass tree, inject plastic packing, test p-seal to 6500 psi, test control line & 3SV

         to 6000 psi, install top section of X-Mass tree, pressure test same to 6500 psi,

         retrieve back pressure valve.

    23.  Connect co flex hose from X-Mass tree to production chock manifold, test surface

         flow line F/manifold to burner booms to 3000psi, R/U kill line F/rig floor to X-Mass

          tree.

    24.   Bull head the well with diesel then try to flow the well through choke manifold &

        burner boom as per IOOC production department instructions.

    25.   If well did not flow.

    26.  R/U wire line lubricator & BOP, pressure test stuffing box & wire line BOP to 3000psi,

        RIH with running tools, install protection sleeve in 3SV, R/D wire line & BOP and

          lubricator.

    27.  R/U coil tubing riser on X-Mass tree, install coil tubing BOP, injector head, connect

         hydraulic hoses and high pressure Co flex hose from cement unit to coil tubing unit,

         pressure test coil tubing riser & BOP with 3000 psi.
