
16” CSG CMT PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
L/D double of drill pipe from liner running string.
Stab TIW valve in liner running string.
Double check I.D. of TIW valve.
Break single of drill pipe and pup joint off of CMT Stand.
Use workbasket as last resort for this operation.
M/U CMT head on TIW and liner running string.
HALCO to pump spacer and CMT as per primary CMT job program.
HALCO to check floats after primary CMT job.
Bull head 60-bbl mud down backside.
16”-TOL 8366’ (640’ of lap) 48-bbl volume lap.
Rotate 5-rounds to the right in neutral position to release running tool from hanger. Straight P/U after releasing running tool.
Strip up 25’ after release from hanger.
Secondary CMT job bullhead CMT down backside.
Displace CMT through DP 5-bbl below end of pipe and another 5-bbl down backside. (10-bbl Total)
(Cut weight of spacer back for secondary CMT job)
WOC
Pressure up to 1000-psi on backside to confirm good secondary CMT job.
Bleed pressure off of Annulus.
Circulate out influx through choke and kill.
Open well up circulate out the long way.
POOH.
