

# Back Off Procedures
1.	Ensure String has been correctly torqued to the right with circa. 28000 foot pounds
2.	From Recorded String Wts, Up , Down , Rot
3.	Work in 3-4 LH turns at recorded Up wt, work pipe from 100k over up wt to Block 	wt. Ensure a tong line sensor is connected to a rig tong holding the back off torque so 	that transmission of torque can be visually observed.
Once the torque has dissipated down hole, set string at Up wt.
4.	Work in 2 LH turns, work pipe from 135k 	Down to 250k Up. Checking on load cell to ensure torque is being transmitted 	downhole.
Continue to work torque down in 2 turn stages until 8-9,000 ft/lbs approx 7-8 LH turns 	are in the string.
5.	Once satisfied that the torque has been fully transmitted down hole, hold string wt at 	170 k until string shot is in place. Before charge is fired, correlate depth relevant to 	chosen connection (preferably one which was broken on previous trip) and fire charge.
6.	If the back off is complete, correlate CCL to confirm backoff point.
If no positive indication the pipe has backed off, Pooh with wireline, then continue to 	work pipe across neutral point to complete back off.
Once back off is complete evaluate whether to return with a screw in jarring assembly 	or if desired and sufficient window is available below casing shoe a side track option 	may be a better alternative.
