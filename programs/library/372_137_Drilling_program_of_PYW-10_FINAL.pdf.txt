                              General Information


1  Introduction


        1.1  Program Introduction
         This program covers the drilling of well# 10 in West Paydar oil field, Iran. The well is
          intended to evaluate reservoir properties and to produce maximum allowable oil from
        Asmari formation. The well will be drilled directionally to a planed total depth of
       3497m (-3089). the open hole drain is considered to be 449.5m, MD in Cap Rock and
        Asmari formation.
         Note-1: The exact TD will be determined based on the logging results.
         Note-2: The well will be completed in 8-1/2" open hole.
         Note-3: This program has been written with respect to geological forecast and letter
                   no. ٣٢٨٤ / م زن ,dated: ٦٨٣١ / ٢٠ / ٣٠

        1.2  West Paydar Structure

         Paydar west anticline is located in North Dezful area and Khuzestan province about
       140km northwest of Ahwaz city and 35km west of Cheshmeh Khush filed and is a
         shared structure on the border line with Foughi of Iraq. This structure is asymmetric
         and separates with a saddle of Paydar field. Paydar west#10 is located closed to border
           line about 685m northwest of well#7. The Aghajari formation makes up the surface
         geology of this location.
        The approximate coordinates of West Paydar-10 are:
        Lambert: E: 1, 741, 517m, N: 1, 126, 938m  / UTM: E: 741, 836m N: 3, 556, 699m

9 Pre Spud

      P.P.Z prepared a pre-spud checklist and distributed it in contractor rigs. The checklist will

      be performed based on the aforementioned  checklist. In addition to the  checklist,

      following matters should be checked:

   ¾ Check & observe the alignment of the rig. This check will be performed in four stages;

           rig levelling, derrick verticality, derrick alignment with rotary table, rotary table

         alignment with conductor pipe. To do this pick up 3 joints of 9 3/4” DC and check the

           verticality of the derrick.

   ¾  Mechanical facilities for mud treatment such as :

         Desander, Desilter, mud cleaner, proper size of shaker–screens, hoppers, mud guns &

          mixers, should be all available on rig and functioning properly.

   ¾  All mud tanks, mud transfer lines & connection should be checked by filling them

         with water & cross wellhead circulation.

   ¾ Mud  engineer  laboratory should be equipped with a complete  set  of  testing

         equipment. The calibration should be done under supervision of well site drilling

          supervisor before starting the operation.

   ¾ Weight indicator, rotary speed gauge, any smart drilling instrumentation, Stroke

          meter, pressure gauges on manifold and in the drilling console, loss / gain gauge and

         any other gauges should be checked and calibrated.

   ¾  Stand pipe manifold valves & connection must be checked & pressure tested.

   ¾  All motors should be checked under load prior to start drilling.

   ¾ Check & control mud stock & cement stock every day & if there is any necessity  to

          request mud material, cement or any drilling tool, it should be prepared & requested

           at proper time.

                                 Geology


1  Structural Geology

             Paydar west anticline is located in North Dezful area and Khuzestan province
             about 140km northwest of Ahwaz city and 35km west of cheshmeh khush filed
            and is a shared structure on the border line with Foughi of Iraq. This structure is
             asymmetric and separates with a saddle of Paydar field.

2  Stratigraphy

           Agha Jari Fm. (From surface to 2044m)
              Consists mainly of reddish to reddish brown, silty and sandy marls with some
              sandstone layers and some greenish grey to bluish grey marls, Anhydrite & some
                slight Limestone.
            Age: upper & Middle Miocence

            Gachsaran Fm. (From 2044 to 3110.3m)
              Consists of following members as follows:
            Age: Middle to lower Miocene.

           Members: 7-6 (From 2044 to 2324m)
            GS.Fm. Mbrs: 7-6 are undifferentiated and consists interbeds of Anhydrite, grey
              marl, Limestone & some reddish marls.

           Member-5 (From 2324 to 2552m)
              Includes interbeds of Anhydrite, grey marl with some Limestone and rare salt
              beds.

           Member-4 (From 2552 to 2791.1m)
              This part starts with first thick salt and consists interbeds of salt, Anhydrite, gray
             marl & rare red marl.

           Member-3 (From 2791.1 to 2918.4m)
                Starts with first thick Anhydrite beds and consists interbeds of Anhydrites, grey
             marl & thin beds of salt & some slight Limestone.

           Member-2 (From 2918.4 to 3046.4m)
                    It consist mainly of salt and with some Anhydrite & grey marls

          Cap Rock (From 3046.4 to 3110.3m)
              Includes interbeds of Anhydrite, grey marl with some Lst & Dolomitic Lst as key
             beds C, D, E, & F and some bituminous shale as key bed-B.

           Asmari Fm. (From 3110.3 to total depth of 3497m)
              Consists mainly of Limestone cream to brownish I, I, III and whitish-off white II,
                   II/I with some sandstone layers and some slight shale beds.
            Age: Lower Miocene to Oligocene.

                    Sequence Of Drilling Operation


1.3 Sequence of Operation

     •  Make up 26” BHA as per BHA design, drill formation down to 133m (20” casing
         point in Aghajari formation). Use LCM pill if encountered heavy losses. If loss
         couldn’t be cured by LCM pill use cement plug(s) to cure same.

     Note:
       - 26” TD is based on 133m inside Aghajari formation.
       - TD should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of rat hole under casing shoe.
       - The exact TD will be determined based on 20” casing tally.
       - Do not forget to strap pipe while POOH.
       - Drop Totco prior to POOH to evaluate the verticality of hole.
       - Use following parameters to drill this hole section:
        -WOB = Maximum available
         -RPM = 90-130
        -GPM = 600-1000

     •  At TD, Circulate, clean hole. POOH, P/U 26” N.Bit stabilizer or 26” Reamer, RIH
        and condition the hole to section TD. Check for any fill. CBU on bottom.

     •  POOH. If observed any indication of drag, run back to bottom. Wash and ream to
       make sure that the hole condition is good.

     •  R/U 20” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

     •  Run 20", 106.5 ppf, K-55, BTS casing with stab in-type guide shoe to bottom as per
         casing program. Casing shoe should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of
           rat hole and enough stick up (1.5 m) on rig floor. Fill casing by non LCM fresh mud
          joint by joint.

     Note:
       -  Take care when running casing not to set down excessive weight if tight hole or
         ledges are encountered.

     •  Run stinger on 5” DP’s using the “C” Plate & 5” x 20” centralizer. Sting in. M/U &
           test cementing lines. Circulate for one casing annulus volume. While circulating,
        check the DP’s annulus for any return. Use all DP’s weight on the string, if
         observing any minor leakage through DP’s annulus.

     •  Cement casing as per program. Any minor adjustment regarding the corrected depth
          will be done in due time. Displace with 6 bbls mud (2 bbls Short). Sting out. Check
         the float. Circulate to clean the DP. POOH.

                Sequence Of Drilling Operation


Note:
- Based on the results obtained from cementing, decision will be made to perform top-
up jobs.

•  Rest the 20” casing on the elevator until cement surface samples get hard. Check for
    casing to be centred inside 30” conductor pipe. In case of any problem, report
   immediately.

•   If there is no cement return to surface, a top-up job should be performed to fill the
    annulus, stage by stage until cement reaches the surface. Use the same weight as the
   primary cement slurry. Ensure adequate X-over for connecting line pipe to cement
    line is available. The cement should be accelerated with max. 2.2 lb/sxs calcium
    chloride and no other additives. Wait for the top job cement to get hard.

•  Cut 30” conductor pipe from cellar base. L/D and remove same.

•  Rough cut 20” landing joint and L/D same. Dress casing neck suitable to install and
    to weld casing head spool. The distance between its top flange and ground level
   must be 1.73m.

•   Install and weld casing head housing 21 ¼” – 2000 psi on casing stub and test the
    sealing of welded casing (limit pressure to a maximum of 80% of the collapse
    strength of the casing or maximum 600 psi). Outlets to align with cellar stairs.

Note:
- Before installing the casing head housing, open all the test and plastic packing ports
 and clean inside it with water and air.
- Do not forget to check the wear bushing with CHH and tie down flange before N/U
 BOP’s.

•   Install tie down flange plus 21 ¼”x2000 psi spacer spool.

•  N/U 21 ¼”-2000 psi BOP’s and related lines. N/U bell nipple. Test BOP’s as per
  NIOC policy to 2000 psi pressure.

              20” Casing Tally, 20” Casing Report

                Important: The following program is a sample; therefore it should be tested
                 using the field drilling water, cement sample and additives.

                For cementing operation, note the followings:

              ¾  M/U Cement Stinger C/W spring bow 20” x 5” centralizer and RIH to 10m
                       above the shoe using the C-plate.

              ¾  Circulate 60 bbls of mud and wash down to flush the top of the stab in shoe
                         from any debris.

              ¾  Flush and  fill surface lines with water and pressure test to 3,000psi for 5
                         minutes prior to start of the cement job.

              ¾  Stab in to the shoe. Set down string weight.

              ¾  Circulate mud clean casing annulus. Observe DP’s annulus for any leakage.

              ¾  Pump spacer, Mix and pump cement as per program.

              ¾   All samples of mix water and slurry should be stored until the success of the
                       cement job has been verified, then they may be discarded.

              ¾  Displace cement with mud. Displace for 6 bbls (2 bbls Short).

              ¾  Stop pumping, sting out, flush pipe, check for float, POOH. L/D stinger, wait
                       on cement to get hard.
