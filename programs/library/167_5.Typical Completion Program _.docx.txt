# Summary of operation
RIH 7” Liner scraper.
Scrap packer setting area.
Displace mud with Filterated Completion Fluid.
POOH CSG scraper to Surf.
R/U TBG running Equipment.
Retrieve wear bushing
RIH single string completion with WRSCSSV, Expansion joint, SSD, Production packer, landing nipple and 4-1/2 to desired depth.
Set production packer 50m above 7” TOL.
Pull Test /Pressure Test Packer.
Land tubing hanger.
Test the Packer Ann. & HGR seals.
Close TRSCSSV, Cut C/L & Install BPV.
N/D BOP, N/U Xmas Tree.
Test Xmas Tree.

# Quality, Health, Safety & Environment
Well shall be in stable condition prior to running completions string.
Monitor well for loss/gain over the trip tank while RIH completions string.
Good communication among all parties responsible for well control is essential.
Perform kick drill with focus on the use of kick stand (Kick string assembly) prior to RIH completions string.
Pre-job meeting will focus on well control & contingency procedures to be understood by all involved parties.
Volumes and well control shall be focused throughout running completions string.
Fill out kick sheet prior to RIH
On a positive indication of a kick such as unexpected pit-gain or flow return rate increase, well shall be shut-in without performing flow check.
A kick stand shall be readily available at all time on rig floor.
Maintain good housekeeping on the rig floor at all times.
Special care must be taken in running scraper and polish mill to make sure CSG are clear to run and Set production packer.
Perform pre-job meeting with all involved personnel at critical stages of the operation.
Sufficient lighting on Wellhead/ Well cellar is essential.
Scaffolding shall be installed on wellhead for ease of operation during X-Tree installation and tests.
All personnel working on rig floor, BOP & X-Tree shall be wearing safety harness.

# Main Risk Evaluation
Well completion fluid not cleaned prior to running completion string
Losing control line clamps in hole.
Junk falls in the well before or during running completions string.
Over-torque tubing connections.
Leak in DHSV control line when pressure testing.
Damaging DHSV control line with manual slips.
Loosing pressure on DHSV control line while RIH.
Missing to tight all stud bolts on X-Tree bottom section.
Tubing head wear/damage while RIH equipment, due to off centered derrick.
Taking kick/inflow during completions operation (when well is perforated).
Damaging Tubing Hanger while running through riser/BOP.
Unnecessary personnel on rig floor while running completion string.
Use excess amount of Dope on each connection, Use Improper Tong Dices.

# Well Control Case
Well shall be continuously monitored during running completions string in hole. This is the responsibility of driller, tool pusher and company man to prepare a trip sheet and monitor the well during RIH.
There should be two set of tubing safety x-overs and 2 FOSV on rig floor. The scenario will be as per following:
In case of observing well under minor flow(If Applicable):
Monitor the well
Close Annular BOP, increase Hyd. oil pressure of BOP to 1,000 psi.
P/U safety crossover and M/U to string.
Monitor the condition, if annular BOP does not hold the pressure, open Ann. and POOH 1 jt. string and cut control lines, RIH, close variable ram on tubing body.
Close FOSV and monitor for any leakage.
Discuss the situation with town for further action.
Perform a well control drill prior to RIH completion string and after each shift change (rig crew or completion team shift change). Make sure everyone knows his duties, nominate crew for each position such as observer, control line cutter & closing the BOP, etc.

# TBGs preparation:
Use steam cleaner or water jet to clean the tubing threads on the pipe rack.
Write tubing Joint No. & Measurement on tubing Body .Check Pin/Box for any visual thread damages.
Drift all tubing with proper API drift gauge (rabbit).

# Down hole Safety Valve Preparation:
Connect hand pump to DHSV, pressure up to perform function test.(8,000psi for 15min)
Record DHSV start to open, fully open and minimum closing pressure @ surface.
Install control line Fitting (7/16 HIF) to DHSV.

# X-Mas Tree Preparation:
Wash inside X-Mas Tree via jet wash to clean out any lubricant/debris accumulated in valves ID.

# Completion Fluid:
Completing fluid was explained in Drilling fluid program.
The accepting criteria for the completion fluid is as below:
Recovered permeability: ≥90%
Corrosion rate: ＜0.076mm/a
Diameter of solid particle: ≤2μm

# Completion Running Procedure
Run 7" scraper with DP and scrape production packer setting depth inside 7" liner before running completion string.
Displace the well with proper filtered completion fluid.
Hold a pre-Job safety meeting on rig floor to instruct running speed and other critical installation procedures.
Lift all emergency well control subs on the rig floor and make up to cock valve.
Pick-up and make-up Packer Pre-assy. as following:
Note: all the completion equipment are already made up with 1 pup it above and 1 pup it below, torque up and pressure tested.
Note: Use of safety clamps is required when completions string weight is fairly light.
6.	M/U 1 joint 4 1/2" TBG (13.5#, N.Vam, L-80)
Pick-up and make-up Packer Pre-assy. as following:
Check Packer element/slips for any visual damages.
Use strap sling to lift packer assembly to Rig floor.
A tag line is required to assist lifting.
Slowly pick up tubing and observe hook load to confirm Ratch-A-Latch is fully stabbed, and then open slips.
Slack off packer assembly, double check shear pins quantity, remove the tape on the element.
Care shall be taken while packer is passing through BOP and tubing head spool.
Record pickup and slack off weight on the string.
Don't rotate tubing string after packer is in hole.
Maximum circulation rate of 2 BPM around the packer prior to setting is recommended.
8.	M/U 1 joint 4 1/2" TBG (13.5#, N.Vam, L-80)
Pick-up and make up S.S.D assy. (In close position) As following:
Un-screw shear pins on the packer and screw in packer Test pins with strap to hold pins secure.
R/U Slick line and RIH Gauge cutter to Landing Nipple.
Run & Set plug and prong in NO-GO Landing Nipple.
Fill up string, hook up test line and test packer assembly to 500 psi.
Do not increase pressure more than 700psi. (+-50 psi)
Retrieve prong and Plug.
Open strap from packer and Un-screw testing pins (lock nuts), screw back shear pins.
(Double check number of shear pin screws installing back).
Care must be taken not to drop test screws.
RIH gauge cutter to S.S.D landing nipple.
Run plug & prong and set in S.S.D profile.
18.   M/U 1 joint 4 1/2" TBG (13.5#, N.Vam, L-80)
P/U & M/U Test Assy.
Apply 4,500psi pressure to test SSD plug for 15 min.
Pressure Test shall be performed with 500 Psi Increment stages.(Air must be purged through T-Sub connection prior to testing)
Continue RIH completion string as per tally while Plug in S.S.D Profile.
Pick-up and Make-up Expansion joint assy. In full open position.
Double Check Expansion joint & verify number of shear pins prior to RIH.
Record pickup and slack off weight on the string.
RIH Proper No. of 4 1/2" TBGs.
Fill up string with filtered completion fluid every 10 joints.
Perform pressure test (4,500Psi.) on every 15 Joints.
Pressure Test must be performed with 500 Psi Increment stages.
Pick-up and Make up DHSV assy. (Pre-Assembled)
Connect %" control line to SSSV.
Apply 500psi pressure in C/L for 5min. Then gradually increase pressure to 8,000psi & test for 15min. S Keep 5,000 Psi pressure in C/L while RIH.
Keep control line in position (attached to tubing) with Band-it strap.
Monitor DHSV control line and CIL pressure while RIH.
RIH 4 1/2" TBG as required. (4 1/2",13.5#,N.Vam)
Install C/L protector on Each joint.
Pick up and make up 1 sets of Fluted Swage with X-Over As per below table.
Pick-up and Make-up tubing hanger to completion string.
Check the seals on the hanger body.
Apply light oil on the seals body of tubing hanger.
Check pressure in 1/4" C/L, bleed off pressures to zero.
P/U & M/U Landing Joint to TBG Hanger.
S Do not Torque with rig tongue.
S Use Proper 7" Pup Joints to reach desired stick up.
Cut CL approx. 15m or enough to reach to rig floor on spool.
Feed all lines through Tubing Hanger, install 1/4" fitting on CL.
Secure control line on landing joint body in straight shape.
Mark control line position and orientation on landing joint.
Make sure Tubing Hanger orientation matches with X-Tree orientation (X-Tree orientation to be confirmed with Production Eng. dept. in advance)
Mark depth on landing joint to indicate Tubing Hanger has landed on the load shoulder. Mark below TH if decided to double check its landing through tbg. Spool side valve.
Make sure tubing spool is cleaned prior to setting TH.
Apply 5,000psi in CL and observe flow tube movement and DHSV opening.
Land tubing hanger inside tubing head spool body with special care.
Wellhead specialist shall confirm landing of Tubing hanger in Tubing Head spool.
POOH string 2m to place Tubing hanger 3m above tubing head spool.
R/U slick line lubricator. Test all connections with 4500psi.
Pressure up string to 4,000 Psi for 3 Min.
Perform WLRSV inflow test as following:
Close WLRSV by releasing C/L pressure slowly.
Release string pressure through slick line lubricator to 1,000Psi.
Observe pressure for 15min.
If OK, Equalize above WLRSV and open WLRSV by pressuring up C/L and monitoring flow tube movement.
Disconnect test line on Landing joint.
If NOT, release string pressure and repeat above procedure.
RIH Slick Line as Following:
RIH Cutter gauge to top of DHSV Landing Nipple.
RIH & Retrieve WLSV from Landing Nipple.
RIH & Set Dummy in Landing Nipple.
RIH & retrieve prong & plug from S.S.D profile.
RIH, set prong & plug in Top no-go landing nipple below the packer.
Monitor control line pressure during slick line operation and report any pressure drop to slick line operator, completion supervisor and rig company man.
R/D slick line lubricator.
Rig Up surface pressure line, connect to circulating head , and Set packer as per following procedure:
Fill up Surface Lines and make sure air has been purged.
All Surface lines shall be checked for any possible leak.
Start pumping fluid through string slowly to make sure plug is in place.
Pressure up by 500psi increments, hold 2-3 minutes on each stage, and observe pressure.
Hold final Packer setting pressures has to be confirmed by completion's service engineer.
Slowly bleed off pressure to zero.
Rig down surface pressure lines
Over Pull string (Max. 10 Kips) to test packer.
Slack off tubing weight to shear expansion joint. (2 No. Pins, Approx. 10 kips)
Land tubing hanger in Tubing Head Spool by following below Procedure:
Open Tubing Head Spool side valve and connect line to BOP side valve. Circulate filtered completion fluid to wash inside tubing head spool.
Drain BOP stack through Tubing head spool gate valves.(Keep Open)
Keep Tubing Hanger centered by Air hoists wire slings, pass gently thru BOP stacks as per Specialist instructions.
As the Tubing Hanger reaches load shoulder, weight indicator will descend to minimum weight.
Landing joint mark must be in line with the rotary table. This ensures that Tubing hanger has landed fully in THS load shoulder.
Tighten all Lock-Down screws. (Sufficient Lights shall be provided while tightening tie down screws)
N/U surface pressure line and Perform Expansion Joint pressure test. (5,500psi for 15min), N/D Same.
Test Packer and Hanger seals as per below procedure:
Switch surface line to TBG annulus. Pressure up to 2,500psi for 15min to test hanger seals from bottom and packer element from top.
Back off landing joint on TH and lay down same.
Run two way check valve (2WCV).
Nipple-down BOP
Nipple up X-Mas Tree.
Retrieve 2WCV.
R/U slick line lubricator. Test all connections with 4500psi.
RIH slick line, retrieve prong & Plug from NO-GO.
Retrieve Dummy from 3SV.
RIH & Set WLSV in Landing Nipple.
R/D Slick Line Equipment & close Safety Valve by releasing C/L pressure.
Install X.Mas Tree Cap And Close All Valves Except Bottom Master Valve.
