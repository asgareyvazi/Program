# ============================================================================
# WELL CONTROL DRILL (KICK / BLOWOUT DRILL) — EMERGENCY RESPONSE
# Translated from Persian: "دستورالعمل ایمنی مانور فوران چاه یا خطر"
# (Well Control Drill — BOP Drill instruction, company safety advisor)
# English translation — generalized (no company names).
# ============================================================================

## PURPOSE

The purpose of this drill is to create readiness for emergency conditions
of a well kick / blowout hazard, so that personnel are fully prepared and
can secure the rig and the site with the highest safety factor in the
shortest time.

## DRILL PROCEDURE

### A. When Drilling (hole open, pipe in slips):

1. On the kick alarm, the driller immediately places the safety valve in
   accessible position, shuts down the mud pump, and closes the safety
   valve (in the open position below the kelly) using the pre-designated
   crew member with the prepared wrench.

### B. When Pipe is in / out of hole (tripping):

1. The driller pulls the pipe up to the rig floor quickly; if the pipe is
   in open position, the crew member designated beforehand closes the
   appropriate safety valve on the pipe.

### Individual Responsibilities During the Drill:

2. **Derrickman:** On hearing the continuous alarm, immediately lays down
   the pipe, puts on the safety belt, and comes down to the rig floor in
   the shortest time.

3. **Motorman:** Immediately opens the cooling water valves.

4. **Rig Mechanic:** Present on the rig floor, ready to shut down the
   engines, then stands by.

5. **Electrician:** Stands by at the generator location.

6. **Rig Superintendent (Toolpusher):** Through the "4-way" hydraulic
   panel, opens the valves towards the mud side, then slowly closes the
   appropriate rams with the pipe in the hole, and checks the shut-in
   pressure on the choke manifold.

7. **Other rig floor personnel:** Proceed to close the designated
   hydraulic valves.

8. **Mud Logger / Mud Engineer:** Immediately checks the mud tanks
   (level, volume) and reviews the written records; then, in consultation
   with the Toolpusher and the Operation Supervisor, prepares the
   appropriate mud weight and conditions.

9. **Welder:** On hearing the continuous alarm, shuts down his equipment
   and stands by to assist other sections as previously announced.

10. **All drivers:** Park their vehicles, shut down engines, and stand by
    for further instructions.

11. **Radio Operators:** On hearing the alarm, stand by at the
    communication equipment to relay messages to the Toolpusher,
    Operation Supervisor and the Drilling Management / relevant
    authorities.

12. **Cell phones and open flames:** All open flames and cell phones
    must be shut off; personnel involved in the operation must be ready
    to use the fire extinguishers.

13. **Safety Officer & Motorman:** Ensure sufficient clean water is
    available, and fire-fighting equipment (hoses, extinguishers,
    protective clothing) is ready for use.

14. **Visitors / non-essential personnel:** Move to the assembly point
    (against the wind, according to the wind direction indicator).

## END OF DRILL

At the end of the drill, after checking the choke manifold pressures and
the new mud conditions, the Operation Supervisor and the Toolpusher
declare the emergency situation over, and personnel return to their
normal work stations.
