1.0 Purpose

    To establish the procedure to be used to kill a well using the "Wait and Weight"
     method.


1.1 Scope

      All drilling units with surface installed BOP stack.


1.2 Conditions

     Well shut-in.

     Manifold and BOP lined up to direct returns through remote choke and mud gas
      separator.

    SIDPP and SICP stabilised.


1.3 Procedure


1.3.1  Calculate kill mud weight. Round up to nearest 0.1 ppg.

    DO NOT INCLUDE ANY EXTRA FOR TRIP MARGIN ETC.

       Prepare kill mud in active system.


1.3.2  Calculate Initial Circulating pressure (ICP).

1.3.3  Calculate Final Circulating pressure (FCP).


1.3.4  Complete kill sheet and step down schedule and graph.


1.3.5  Once kill sheet completed and mud weight has been raised to kill weight, open
       valve upstream of choke, zero pump stroke counters. Ensure good
      communication between choke operator, pump operator and personnel in pits
      and pump room.

       All events and pressures should be recorded on the Well Control Report.


1.3.6  Bring the pump to kill speed, opening choke, holding casing pressure constant.


1.3.7  Once kill rate is established record actual Initial Circulating pressure (ICP).  If
        similar to calculated ICP continue according to schedule.

         If significantly different, stop pumping, shut in and investigate. It may be
       necessary to recalculate kill sheet using actual ICP.



1.3.8  Hold drillpipe pressure at ICP until kill mud reaches the rig floor, reset pump
       stroke counters.


1.3.9  Follow step down schedule allowing drillpipe pressure to drop from ICP to
      FCP. Once kill mud enters the annulus , hold FCP constant.



1.3.10 Once returns are uncontaminated kill mud. The well will be shut in and
       pressures observed.

      SIDPP should be zero.

      SICP should be zero.


1.3.11  If pressures are zero, flow check well through choke prior to opening BOP.

         If pressures are not zero this should be investigated and an additional plan
       formulated.

1.4 Responsibilities


1.4.1 Rig Superintendent

       Overall responsibility for installation.

       Ensures operations are planned and executed with the highest regard for the
        safety of personnel, the installation and the environment.

       Ensures those persons with a well control function are competent to fulfil that
       function and are aware of the responsibilities.

      During well killing operations, ensures communications with Rig Manager,
       Operator and around installation are maintained.


1.4.2 Toolpusher

       In direct charge of the kill operation.

       Ensures the personnel involved are competent, organised and prepared.

          It is the Toolpushers responsibility, in consultation with the Operators
       Representative if possible, to advise the Rig Superintendent on the appropriate
       procedures to be used.

      The Toolpusher will decide on and check the final kill sheet to be used.

      The Toolpusher, or his designate, will operate the choke during well control
       operations.


1.4.3  Driller

      Once the well is shut in, the Driller is responsible for monitoring pressure and
       maintaining constant bottom hole pressure until ready to kill the well.

      During the kill operation, the Driller operates the pump, also monitoring and
       recording strokes, pressures and pit volumes.


1.4.4  Assistant Driller

      During well control operations provides a direct link between operations on
       the drill floor and the pitroom/pumproom.

1.5 Calculations


1.5.1  Kill Mud Weight (KMW)

          KMW = (SIDPP  TVD  0.052) + OMW

KMW =      Kill mud weight             (ppg)
OMW =      Original mud weight        (ppg)
SIDPP =     Shut in drillpipe pressure     (psi)
TVD  =      Total Vertical depth           (feet)



1.5.2  Initial Circulating Pressure (ICP)

                         ICP = SCRP + SIDPP

ICP   =       Initial circulating pressure    (psi)
SCRP =     Slow circulating rate pressure (psi)



1.5.3  Final Circulating Pressure (FCP)

                      FCP = SCRP x KMW
                      OMW



1.5.4 Surface To Bit Strokes

                      Strokes = DSVolume  Pump Output

DSVolume   =      Drillstring volume    (bbls)
Pump output  =      bbl/stroke
