1. Introduction

     1.1    Program Introduction

              This program covers workover of well No.14, of Sarkhoon gas field, I.R.Iran. The well is
              gas producer from Razak formation right now with 51.2 MMSCFD.

            Problem
            The main problem is the flowing of salt water from both 7" x 9-5/8" and 9-5/8" x 13-3/8"
              annuluses. All evidences show a relation between 7" x 9-5/8" and 9-5/8" x 13-3/8"
              annuluses. The relating fluid is milky salt water.

           Main Objective
           The main objective of this program is to fix the problem and to resume gas production
            from Razak formation.

             Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of
             workover request no. 860/50/ چ ت ب, dated: 1386 0//3 26 petroleum engineering
              department.

           The main head lines of this program are as follow:
                1.     Re-check the pressure of well head and annuluses. Investigate for any relation
                   between the annuluses.
                2.      Kill well using hi-PH water.
                3.      Install plug in top No-Go landing nipple and pressure test inside tubing.
                4.    Open SSD and displace annulus with hi-PH water. Close SSD. Monitor and
                       investigate for any leakage of 9-5/8" casing and any relation between 7"x9-5/8"
                   and 9-5/8"x13-3/8" annuluses.
                5.      Kill the well above packer using proper hi-weight. N/D well head & N/U BOP'S.
                6.      Pull out & L/D completion string to top of packer.
                7.     Try to find out leak point/interval of 9-5/8" casing using RTTS and hi-PH water.
                       (In case of hi-flowing rate of salt water cement squeezing job maybe planed in
                   due time)
                8.     Mill out and retrieve packer and its tail.
                9.     Set a bridge plug inside 7" liner.
              10.    Displace hole with proper mud weight (max 114 pcf).
              11.    Tie beck 7" short liner up to 120m above the top of leakage area.
                            (It is probable to extend 7" liner to surface)
              12.    Clean out inside 7" liner down to bottom.
              13.   Run completion string.
              14.   Flow the well on proper choke sizes & record the flowing pressures.
              15.    Secure well.

              Attentions:

                •  The detailed program is illustrated here after in this document under the
                  head of "Workover Program".

2. Workover Program


   2.1  Sequence of Workover Operation


                1. Move from AGH11 to the well site of SA14, R/U and make  it ready for operation,
               Record rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea level.

                2. The well head spools, valves & P-Seals and x-mas tree must be checked and
                  serviced by well head specialist. Shall it necessary, well head components should be
                  repaired after being L/D on the well site or at AHWAZ workshop.

              Attention: Ref to letter No. 86/114/ چ ت ب dated 86/06/06 of head of completion
               office- South Zagros; the wellhead vales have internal leakage.

                3.  Connect killing & flowing lines to the x-mas tree side outlets and connect return line
                   to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 4500 psi pressure.

                4. Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 7" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x 13-3/8"
                annulus and 13-3/8" x 20" annulus. Release / Bleed off pressures, monitor their build
               up and investigate for the probable relations.

             Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
             while relation investigation.

                5.  Bullhead the well by pumping hi-PH water which its full column imposes 584psi over
                 pressure to the reservoir @ top perforations (2674m). Shall could not able to
                  bullhead, lubricate the well and make sure about well condition.

             Note: Monitor the pressures of annuluses all the time.

                6.  Rig up wireline equipment; test same and lubricators to max 4500psi pressure. Set
                "AF-4.312" top no-go plug @ ±2394m. Test same and inside tubing to max 4500psi.
             Open SSD. Displace the well by fresh water (its full column has 266 psi over
                 pressure against the reservoir pressure @ packer depth (±2382m).

                7.  Monitor tubing annulus flow and pressure build up behaviour and record the flowing
                   rate and the max pressure build up to calculate proper mud weight to kill the well
                (114 pcf mud weight maybe necessary).

                8.  Kill the well using proper mud weight. Secure the well and L/D well Head. N/U
                BOP's.

                9.  Prepare to pull out completion string. Un-Jay anchor seal, pull out and L/D
                 completion string.

              10. RIH with 8-1/2" E.mill and 8-1/2" casing scraper to top of packer. Scrap the 9-5/8"
                 casing from surface down to top of packer.

              11. Run CBL/VDL log inside 9-5/8" casing and interpret its reading in due time.

12. RIH with 9-5/8" RTTS (Suitable for 43.5 – 53.5 # casing). Perform investigation
    operation to find out leakage point/interval by setting RTTS at some depths and
    displacing mud of annulus above circulating valve by fresh water. Record all results
    carefully for accurate decision.

13. Having find out the leakage point/interval perform injectivity test inside damaged
   zone and record the injection result. In case of getting good injection rate (at least
   3bpm / max2000 psi injection pressure), inject neat cement slurry using 9-5/8" RTTS
    to prevent salt water cross flowing inside perforations after being exposed to hi-PH
   water column which its full column has less hydrostatic pressure against salt water of
   damaged zone, but it imposes ±584psi over reservoir pressure @ the perforations.

Note: Shall it impossible to get injectivity inside 9-5/8" casing damaged zone;
continue the operation following up Item No.15.

14. Displace hole by hi-PH water which imposes 266psi to the reservoir @ packer depth
   (±2382m) and ±584psi over reservoir pressure @ the perforations. Drill out cement,
   check inside 9-5/8" casing for any salt water flow. Shall it be static, mill out and push
   down the packer and its tail down to liner lap. Retrieve packer and its tail.

Attention: Heavy mud losses may occur while milling operation. Use hi-vis pill to
cure same.

15. Scrap into 7" liner from 2480m to 2550m. Set an EZ-drill bridge plug @ 2500m.

16. Displace mud in hole by previous high weight mud.

17. Dress inside tie back sleeve, run and cement 7" tie back liner up to 120m above the
    top of damaged area.

Attention: In accordance to the depth of 9-5/8" casing damage, decision maybe
made to extend the 7" liner to surface. It is highly recommended to pre-plan for
this operation in due time.

18. Clean out inside 9-5/8" casing to liner lap and inside 7" liner to top of EZ-Drill plug @
   2500m. Perform liner lap pressure test, max to 2000psi. Dry test liner lap by
    displacing hole using fresh water.

Attention: Considering the packer setting depth, include casing/liner scraper into
clean out string while tripping in.

19. Drill out cement and EZ-Drill using hi-PH water, drop and push same down to
   2770m; top of permanent cement plug.

20. Run completion string as per program which will be offered in due time according to
    the final well condition.

21. L/D BOP"s stack. N/U wellhead. Perform all needed pressure tests.

22. Connect flowing lines and R/U wireline equipments. Pressure-test them all to
   4500psi. Retrieve top no-go plug and let the well do flow. Record flowing pressures
    via different choke sizes.

2.2 Mud program
