     Introduction
      The AZADEGAN Oilfield is Located in Khuzestan Province and is Approximately
Located 80 Kilometers West of Ahwaz City, Near to Iran-Iraq Border. It is in The Northwest
of The YADAVARAN Field and in The West of JUFEYR Field. The Average Monthly
Temperature is 45ºc in summer, and Highest Temperature is 52ºc. The Lowest Temperature is
-4.4ºc in winter. Maximum Rainfall is 78 mm in February, Winter Season.
      The Field is in Flooding Plain Area with Convenient Road Network which Extends to
Ahwaz. Most of The Oil Fields in The Region are Located in The Foothill Zone Zagros Folded
Zone of the NW-SE Trending Zagros Mountain Range, and Have Similar Trends to The Zagros
Mountain Range Elongated Anticline Structure.
       This Document Covers the Drilling of AZNS-??? (S358) Sarvak Development Highly
Deviated Well With pilot hole in Sarvak in South Azadegan Oilfield, Iran. Well S-358 Shall Be
Considered to Produce from the Sarvak Reservoir. Plan is to Drill the Pilot Hole to Depth of
±3058 Mt in 8-1/2” Open Hole, and Perform Logging as program. Then Plug back to ±30 Mt
above Kick off Point.

    S358 Highly Deviated Well with Pilot Hole in Sarvak
   Drilling on Pad-40 Objectives
     As The Previous Exploration/Delineation Drilling in AZADEGAN Oil Field has Proved
Presence of a Very Prolific Hydrocarbon Bearing Sedimentary Column, Particularly within the
Cretaceous Sequences. The Sarvak Formation is the Main Reservoir which is divided to 13
Zones (Sarvak-1 to Sarvak-12 and Sarvak Intra). The Zones of Sarvak-1 to Sarvak-10 are Oil
Bearing with Tilted WOC.
    A Location on Pad-40 has also been selected for Drilling Sarvak Producer Wells with
pilot hole. As offset well, AZNS-8 was Drilled and Completed Previously. This Pad is Located
in the Northern Part of Field in Block-I (Figure1) Shows the Location of Pad-40 on the
Underground Contour Map at the Top of Sarvak 3&4-6 Reservoir Zone.
       This Well will be Drilled As a Highly-Deviated Well in Sarvak and Pilot Hole in Sarvak
Reservoir. This Document Presents the Scope of Work of S358 as Highly Deviated Producer
Well with Pilot Hole.
      The Objective For this Well will Be Evaluating Sarvak formation in pilot hole and
Interest Sarvak 3&4-6 in deviated hole and Providing Maximum Drain Hole for Production, So
Following the Critical Coordination in Trajectory Is Essential.

    Pore and Fracture Press Prediction

       Since the Formation Pressure of Gachsaran Formation is Different between North Area
and South Near and Decreases Greatly and Complete Loss Took Place at all Four Existing
Wells in South Area, the Oilfield is divided into three Areas.
      Area I (North of AZN-10): Area I Located in North of Line1(Y-Coordinate: 3452051),
in this Area, Gachsaran Formation is Characterized with Abnormal High Pressure. For No
Wells have ever been Drilled in the Area between Line1 (Y-Coordinate: 3452051) and Line 3
(Y-Coordinate: 3457993), This Area Exists Greater Uncertainties and is Delimited to the Area
I Temporarily.
      Area II (Between AZN-10 and AZN-11): Area II Located between Line1 (Ycoordinate:
3452051) and Line 2 (Y-Coordinate: 3443549), in this Area, the Formation of Gachsaran
Formation Decreases Greatly.
      Area III (South of AZN-11): Area III Located the South of Line 2 (Y-Coordinate:
3443549), in this Area, the Formation of Gachsaran Formation Decreases Greatly and all
Existing Wells Occurred Complete Loss at Pabdeh Formation.
      The Area Division shall be Adjusted According to Further Actual Drilling Operation,
and Quantity of Wells in Different Areas could be also Adjusted Accordingly.

    Program Outline
          It is planned to drill and complete a Sarvak highly-deviated development well with pilot
hole.
     26" Hole section:
      The 26” Hole Section will be Drilled 154 Mt through Aghajari Formation. Aghajari
Formation Consists of A Marl, Sand-Shale. For Drilling This Section 67 pcf Hi-Vis Bentonite
Mud will be used for Hole Cleaning. Also PHB Pill Will Be Used to Clean the Hole and Since
Bentonite has the Best Cake around the Well Bore, It will make a Good Cake for Hole Stability.
According to Previous Wells in this Field, this Section is almost Problem Free. The 20" Casing
will be set at ±154 Mt and it will be cemented in One Stage with Stinger (Exact CMT Program
will be issued in Due Time). N/U 20-3/4" 3k C.H.H & 21 1/4" 2K Annular BOP and Single
Rams.

      17-1/2" Hole section:
       This Section will be drilled from ±154 Mt to ±1275 Mt through Aghajari Formation.
According to offset Wells tight spots, Hard Back Ream, W&R while RIH and Bit Balling Up
in surface was recorded. For Drilling This Section 70-80 Pcf Kcl/Nacl Polymer Mud will be
Used. Also PHB Pill will be used to clean the Hole. CDR/GR log will be run in open hole. 13
3/8" Casing Shoe will Be Set at ±1275 Mt at the Top of Gachsaran to Case off Full Soft
Unconsolidated Formation of Lower Aghajari and Drill the next Hole Section with different
Mud Density. It will be cemented in two Stage (Lead & Tail - Exact CMT Program will be
issued in Due Time). N/Up C.H.S 20-3/4" 3k x 13-5/8” 5K. N/U 13-5/8" 5K BOP’s Stack (13
5/8" 5K Annular Preventer + 13 5/8" 5K Double Rams + 13 5/8" 5K Single).
     12 ¼" Hole Section:
       This Section will be drilled from ±1275 to ±1577 Mt through Gachsaran. For Drilling
this Section the 95-107 Pcf KCl Polymer Mud can be used. The Risks of Drilling this Section
is Heavy MW & High pressure formation, Low ROP, Tight Hole and Stuck Pipe Based on
Offset Wells. CDR/GR Log Will Be Run in Open Hole. 9 5/8" Casing Shoe will be set at ±1577
Mt at the Top of Asmari to Isolate High Pressure Formation for Drilling to Next Section to TD
with low Mud Weight. It will be cemented in two Stage (Lead & Tail -Exact CMT Program
will be issued in Due Time). CDR-GR-DT & CCL/GR/CBL-VDL Will Be Run. N/U T.H.S 13
5/8" 5k x 11" 5k. N/U 13-5/8” 5K BOP’s Stack. (13 5/8" 5k Annular BOP + 13 5/8" 5k
Double Rams + 13 5/8" 5k Single Ram).

     8 ½” Hole Section (Vertical):
       This Section will be Drilled from ±1577 to ±3058 through Asmari, Pabdeh, Up. Gurpi,
Tarbur, Lo. Gurpi, Ilam, Laffan, Sarvak as vertically pilot hole. 74-80 Pcf Kcl Polymer Mud
Will Be Used For Drilling This Section. The logging will be performed as program in pilot

    Formation Description
    A brief description of the stratigraphic units expected to be drilled in South Azadegan
typical well has been presented in the following breakdown.

    Recent/Aghajari Formation
      The Aghajari Formation consists of a repetition of brown to grey calcareous Sandstones,
chert debris and gypsum-veined red marl. A fauna of brackish to freshwater was observed,
together with more marine fauna.

   Gachsaran Formation
      The Gachsaran Formation with a thickness ranges from 400 m to 600 m, thicken towards
the north. Deposition of the Gachsaran Formation is cyclic. The cycle began with a deposition
of shallow-water marine carbonates, and then it was overlain by a thick evaporitic phase. The
evaporites were capped by red to variegated marl, which represented the end of cycle.

   Asmari Formation
      The Asmari Formation in Azadegan Field developed a massive sandstone interval, with
a few thin argillaceous layers in the upper sections. Sands are clear, fine to medium-sorted, and
poorly consolidated. On the other hand, the lower sections consist of intercalation of sandy,
chalky, slightly dolomitic high-energy limestone with sands layers.

   Pabdeh Formation
      Pabdeh Formation is divided into two parts in the Azadegan field. The upper part is
made of grey to dark-brown argillaceous limestones and marls. Planktonic fauna dates that this
part. was deposited in Oligocene. The lower part  is composed of dark-brown, slightly
argillaceous limestone. Pelagic fauna, mostly Globigerina and Globorotalia, indicates that this
part was formed during late Paleocene to late Eocene in age. It seems that no early Paleocene
Formation exists in the Azadegan area.

   Gurpi Formation
      The Gurpi Formation in the Azadegan Field consists of grey to dark-brownish, slightly
argillaceous micritic limestone with Maestrichtian to Campanian aged fauna. At Well AZN-1,
about 50 meters of chalky limestones were encountered in the middle of the Gurpi formation,
which contains abundant fragments of rudists, bryozoa, corals, and Orbitoides.

    Ilam Formation
       In the Azadegan Field, the Ilam Formation consists of grey micritic limestone, often
argillaceous, rarely pyritic, limestones. Microfauna, mostly pelagic, including rotalids and
oligostegina assemblage, a few algae, bryozoa, and rudist fragments exist.

Drilling Risks & Pit falls

  While drilling in massive salt formation of Gachsaran Formation, be careful of well
        trajectory and the salt’s cave-in/dissolving.
   Formation gas from Asmari, Pabdeh and Sarvak formations containing harmful
      hydrogen sulfide (H2S) has the risks to harm site personnel during the operation. The
     H2S content in Sarvak Formation is 1750 ppm according to the sample analysis. All site
       personnel should pay more attention on H2S hazards, and all H2S equipment should be
        in good condition.
  Be aware of the bit collision, borehole wall sloughing and bit stuck.
  Lost circulation is the main downhole problem in this part of south Azadegan field, such
       as AZN-5 and AZN-1. The losses occurred in Pabdeh, Gurpi, Ilam and Sarvak
       formations.
HSE Concerns

  In the well AZN-06, there was chert in the cutting in Pabdeh Formation from 1900-
     1950m and from 2070 to 2090m. Also there was chert in well AZN-12 in Pabdeh
      Formation from 1950-1975m and from 1570-1585m; and in Sarvak Formation from
      2825-2865m; and in Dariyan Formation from 3450-3475m. Also these cherts are not so

  F.1.1. INTERVAL DISCUSION
    The 26-inch section will be drilled with Lime treated drill water and hi-viscous mud
    sweeps in a close system.
     This is a large diameter hole and the hole cuttings removal mechanism will be a
     combination of high annular velocities and hi-viscous sweeps.
     Bentonite Mud/Hi-Vis Pill Gel has been planned to overcome the drilling problems and
     increase the rate of penetration. Rheological properties of the system will be increased
    and kept in the optimum range by adding GEL API at the concentration of 20 – 30
      lb/bbl.
     Active system Viscosity should be maintained by effect of PHG sweeps between 55-70
      sec/qt!!! Due to low GPM at the start of drilling the section hole cleaning is important.
     Soft dispersive clays and unconsolidated sands can be expected whilst drilling this
      interval. The formation is mainly composed of Marl, Claystone and unconsolidated
     sands. The hole is displaced with viscous mud at the section TD in order to plaster off
     the sands, enhancing bore hole stability and minimizing fill while running the 20’’
     casing.
     This hole is to be cleaned as required but as a minimum, the following should be
     adhered to:
    Every single a 15 bbls hi-viscous pill should be pumped to clean the hole.

     F.2.1 INTERVAL DISCUSSION
     The 17 ½’’ intervals will be drilled with Lime treated salt polymer mud and hi-viscous
    mud sweeps (if necessary).

     This section will be drilled through the Aghajari, Mishan and upper section of Gachsaran
      formations. Lithology of mentioned formation is mainly Marl, Claystone and sand beds.

     (26" hole) can be used in this section too for the first few meters. Salt is used as weighting
      agent and gives some inhibition against Clay swelling. Addition of Lime provides proper
    pH and Calcium ion as well. Calcium ions will minimize swelling and dispersion of
     formation as well.

     F.3.2. INTERVAL DISCUSSIONS
   Mud from previous section will be forwarded to this section and will be treated for desired
     properties to drill this section.
    Gachsaran consists of Marl, Clay, Anhydrite and Salt, therefore mud will be salt saturated
     to minimize hole wash out and to reduce solid particles since this section will be drilled
    with high mud weight.
     Salt water influx with high Calcium ion concentration is expected in Gachsaran formation
    and presence of Calcium in mud due to addition of Lime from beginning will prevent
     drastic changes in mud properties in confrontation with high Calcium influx.
     Salt saturation and addition of KCl (if needed) will minimize Clay and Marl swelling and
     prevent tight point occurrence. If concentration of Calcium ions in mud changes while
     drilling Anhydrite, system should be treated with Soda ash to adjust calcium concentration
    and keep it at desired range.
   Minimum mud weight expected for this section is 107 pcf and it will be achieved by
     addition of Barite to Salt saturated mud.
    Due to drilling Anhydrite and high Calcium influx, flocculation of clay/ marl should be
     considered and enough stock of deflocculant chemicals e.g. Spersene should be kept at rig
      site to treat system and rectify high viscosity.

Well bore and formation related problems are identified as follows:

Hole instability: has been observed in offset wells. Make sure mud properties are in range
and mud weight is adequate to keep well bore stability and prevent tight hole.

Bit balling: This can occur due to Clay and Marl beds and is attributed to different factors
other than mud chemistry, such as bit hydraulics. At times, hydraulics is not adequate to
clean the bit and stabilizer, balling may occur. If hydraulics appears to be adequate, then a
strategy of additions of materials such as drilling detergent or walnut can be adapted in
order to minimize bit balling.

Lost circulation: The concentration of LCM will vary according to losses. Sufficient stocks
of graded LCM should be kept at location. In case of seepage losses, the system should be
treated with fine grade of LCM at 25 - 30 ppb, for moderate to severe losses, prepare pills
at different graded concentrations and spot across the thief zone.

In order to avoid LCM plugging MWD tools, a circulation sub should be installed in the
drill string. The MWD engineer is to advise on the size and concentration of LCM that can
be pumped through MWD Tools. Ensure that the drilling crew displaces all the LCM pill
out of the string prior to shutting down the mud pumps.
In the event of severe losses, crosslinking pills should be pumped to minimize losses.

Salt water influx: make sure mud weight is adequate to prevent well flowing. In case of
influx treat mud system with Soda ash to keep Calcium Ion in proper range, add fluid loss
control agents to keep fluid loss in range.

Density: Drilling fluid density will be controlled by addition of Hematite / Barite or light
premix fluid. Use available solids control equipment to reduce low gravity solids build-up,
thus reducing any dilution requirements for density control. Mud weight to be increased as
hole conditions dictate. Potential problems may encounter if mud weight is not sufficient in
this section, such as;

Salt water flow
Hole instability and tight hole due to soft, plastic lithology

Plastic Viscosity: in a mud with such high weight is expected to be high, but try to keep it
ALAP.
Yield Point: in a mud with such high weight is expected to be high, but try to keep it in the
range.
Fluid loss control, API : The API fluid loss should be kept within specifications using
additions of PAC UL and /or Starch HT.

CEC: To be controlled with solids control equipment and dilution.

Hole cleaning: Although utilizing such a high weight mud should improve hole cleaning
but if any clue of cutting build up in annulus (e.g pressure increase, torque and drag) is
observed, it is recommended to pump low-vis low-weight pill to make turbulent flow and
help cutting removing.

Solids control: Ensure shakers are running immediately prior to and during all periods of
circulation. Shakers to be dressed initially with 80/100 mesh screens and after the mud is
sheared, finer screens of 120/165 mesh should be dressed. Use centrifuges as required to
control MW and LGS.

Dump and dilute strategy: Dilute active system with premixed fluid to maintain volume
and mud properties. The concentration of this fluid should be somewhat higher than in the
initial mix to compensate depletion.

Bit & BHA Balling: Use Surfactant pill, such as Drilling detergent to reduce surface
tension, hence minimize balling. Concentration of pill is between 1 – 3 volume percent.

Bacterial decay: before any pull out of hole, treat mud system with biocide to prevent
bacterial decay.

Foaming: drilling Anhydrite may cause foam due to flocculation of polymer and air
trapping. Treat system with Soda ash and defoamer to treat it.

Circulation and agitation: make sure pit agitators are run all the time to prevent Hematite
and Barite sag. Circulate pits with shear guns periodically to ensure sag prevention and sag
remove.

   Dewatering Process Advantages

     40% reduction of waste volume and transportation.

       the cost of transportation for the water needed for operations.

      Produced water should be tested for different ions.

    Cutting Transfer System
Cuttings transfer system is required to collect all the solids discharge from solids control
equipment. This system is installed under Shale Shaker and Centrifuge Area.

    Cutting Treatment Process
Cuttings treatment is important part of the waste management services which is usually done to
solidification and stabilize and fix the liquid phase movement on the solids.
Solidification refers to techniques that encapsulate the waste in a monolithic solid of high
structural integrity. In this treatment, material is added to reduce free water and possibly reduce
or slow potential contaminant release. Contaminant migration is restricted by vastly decreasing
the surface area exposed to leaching by isolating the wastes within an impervious capsule.
Stabilization refers to those techniques that reduce the hazard potential of wastes by converting
contaminants into their least soluble, mobile, or toxic form. The physical nature and handling
characteristics of the waste are not necessarily changed by stabilization.

    Well Control

(1)  Recording Reference
All BOP tests, drills, function tests, any malfunctions, repair or maintenance to the mud system and
well control equipment shall be recorded in the IADC daily reports and shall be signed by both the
Drilling Contractor's Tool Pusher and Drilling Supervisor on the well site. They shall also be recorded
in the Daily Drilling Report.

(2)  Flow Check Procedure
To avoid a kick situation, it is recommended that a flow check be conducted at specific times during
the drilling operation.
     1)  While drilling(any warning sign)
     2)   Prior to tripping the drill string
     3)  While tripping(any warning sign)
     4)  While out of hole(continuously)
     5)   Trip out:
        After pulling 5% of the drill pipe
        At the midpoint depth of the wellbore
        Prior to pulling the first stand of drill collars
        After all the drill string is out of hole.
     6)   Trip in:
       Upon reaching surface casing shoe
        At midpoint of the wellbore
(3)  Primary Control Reference
It is the responsibility of the Drilling Contractor to keep the hole full at all times by using all the
available equipment. The Drilling Supervisor shall monitor that the correct preventive practices are
being implemented and/or conducted at all times.
Extreme care shall be taken to monitor mud volume, drilling breaks and gas cut mud.
The MACP (Maximum allowable casing pressure) shall be confirmed by the Drilling Supervisor, either
after setting each new casing string or, whenever the density of the drilling mud changes.
The MACP shall be clearly written on a kick control sheet that will be posted near the choke control
panel.

(5)  Kick Detection Procedure While Drilling
If there is an indication of a possible kick occurring, the Driller shall follow the kick detection
procedures outlined below:
Stop drilling with the pump onPick up top drive to a predetermined space-out positionShut off the
pumps and check for well flow by visual examination on the bell nipple or flow line.
     1)    If the well is not flowing:
        Immediately notify the rig manager, Drilling Supervisor and Toolpusher.
       The Drilling Supervisor will decide whether to circulate bottoms up or resume drilling. The
              penetration rate  will be limited as long as the cause of the abnormal situation  is
             undetermined. Always be aware that some hydrocarbons (gas or oil cut mud) may rise to
              the surface. Ensure that the proper monitoring and safety systems all in place.
     2)    If the well is flowing:
        Close the well as per the Shut-in Procedure.
      Immediately notify the Wellsite Drilling Supervisor, rig manager and Toolpusher.

9.1     Monitoring H2S
      1) Somewhere on the well site is apt to accumulate H2S, such as mud pit, shale shaker, and
            drilling platform, should be installed one set of. Make sure H2S monitor and alarm system
        can be used.
      2) Alarm threshold setting for H2S warning indicator: the first warning level is 10mg/m3, unable
         warning sound, just show present value of H2S content to operators. The second warning
          level is 20mg/m3, when the H2S content in air exceed 20mg/m3, H2S warning indicator can
         give an alarm automatically, and warning sound should be heard by everyone work in well
            site. The audible alarm should be installed on Kelly board.
      3) Equip enough gas masks on drilling platform, shaker screen (total amount should large than
          24), and equip 12 sets of 30min positive pressure Air Aspirators，2 sets of portable H2S
         monitor and appropriative H2S warning indicator. Arrange a person specially assigned for
          taking charge all the protective apparatus. This man must do periodical check to keep these
        equipment in a good condition.

      5) The pH value of drilling fluid in the H2S area shall be kept over 9.5. Pay attention to

        on duty at the well site.
      9) After the blowout is controlled, H2S concentration should be measured in the places where
       H2S is possible to gather.
      10) The personnel are not allowed to access until the H2S concentration is below the critical
         concentration.
