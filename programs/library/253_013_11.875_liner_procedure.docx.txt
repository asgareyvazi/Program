
11 7/8” Liner Plan
JANUARY 15TH, 2004

### Procedure
Rig down Schlumberger wire line.
Rig up rig floor to run casing. Have baker lock on rig floor, move shoe joint to the skate and attach lift nubbin.
Hold pre-job safety meeting with all personnel involved in the job.
PU and run the shoe joint, 2 centralizer joints, and the float collar joint.  Baker lock connections.  Check visually to insure auto fill float equipment is functioning.
Run one slick joint, and one centralizer joint, followed by one slick joint and one centralizer joint.
Run 41 joints of 11 7/8” casing as per the tally.  (TOTAL JOINTS RUN 42 SLICK, 4 CENTALIZER JOINTS, 1 SHOE JOINT, 1 FLOAT COLLAR JOINT.  THERE WILL BE 21 JOINTS LEFT ON DECK + 1 CENT JOINT)
After running all the casing, change out elevators to run 5 ½” drill pipe. PU hanger assembly with running tool attached.  Make up hanger and rig down casing tools.
MU bumper jar, 3 stands of drill pipe, and the Allamon tool.
TIH with liner on drill pipe.  Do not boost riser while running pipe due to mud losses.  Be careful running through the BOP’s with the liner hanger.
3 stands off bottom drop deactivation ball for the Allamon tool.
Run casing to depth and space-out on the cement stand to place the shoe at 13,641’, and the liner top at 11,456’.  Need to be able to slack off at least 25’ on cement stand.
Once casing is on depth, get a pick up weight, and shift the Allamon tool.  Increase rate to 8 BPM and observe pressure drop as float equipment converts.  Note:  have liner in place before converting Allamon tool in case of accidental setting of hanger.
If partial losses are observed while circulating, circulate casing capacity.  If total losses occur, prepare for cement job. While circulating casing, rig up cement lines.
Drop ball from the cement stand, verify ball has left. Set liner hanger as per Weatherford Liner Rep.
Set 25-30K back on hanger and begin pumping spacer for cement job.
Pump 20 bbls spacer, test lines to 5,000 psi.  Continue to pump spacer, then mix and pump the slurry (950 sacks).
Once the cement is pumped, pump 3 bbls spacer and release the drill pipe wiper/latch dart followed by 7 bbls of spacer and displace the cement with mud using Halliburton.  (233 bbls to Allamon tool, after shearing through another 6 bbls to launch latch down plug.  Continue displacing with 226 bbls to bump the plug with 500 psi over circulation rate.  TOTAL DISPLACEMENT = 472 BBLS TO BUMP.)
Check to see if the floats are holding once cement is displaced.
Set liner top packer as per Weatherford Rep.
Test the liner top packer to ~1000 psi.
Bleed off and open bag.  Rig down Cement Lines.
Pull and set back cement stand.  Hook up top drive and put 500 psi on drill pipe.
Un-sting.  As pressure drops, stop, and circulate one circulation out the long way.
Dry pipe and POOH.
