                Gunk Pill Pumping Procedure





1-  Keep string at shoe depth
2-  Prepare 100 bbls of Hi-Vis pill in one of the mud tanks by adding HYDRO-ZAN of HYDRO-CAP XP
    at range of 1-1.5 lb/bbl.
3-  Add 54 bbls of diesel in slug tank (make sure the tanks is empty prior to add diesel in the tank)
4- Pump 10 bbls of Hi-Vis by rig pump into drill string
5- Pump 10 bbls of diesel by rig pump into the drill string
6-  Stop rig pimp
7-  44 bbls of diesel will remain in slug tank.
8-  Add 7 MT of Bentonite into slug tank to prepare Gunk Pill, the final volume became as 60 bbls
9-  Keep agitators run to suspend Bentonite in diesel
10- Pump all 60 bbls of Diesel/ Bentonite pill into drill string
11- Dump dead volume and add 10 bbls pump-able of diesel in slug tank
12- Pump 10 bbls of diesel behind the Gunk Pill
13- Follow up by 10 bbls of Hi-Vis Pill and displace by seawater based on surface line volume to
    displace Gunk plus tail spacer from surface line and send spacer into the drill string
14- Close Hydrill to minimize risk of u-tube because of higher weight of Gunk pill compare to the sea
    water.
15- Switch drill string into cement unit
16- Open Hydrill
17- Displace the Gunk by sea water to reach Gunk to the end the string at rate of 4-5 bbls/min
18- don’t disconnect the cement unit from drill string
19- Close Hydrill
20- Connect rig pump to the top kill line
21- Displace Gunk at rate at 4-5 bbls/min and simultaneously pump sea water by rig pump into the
    close annulus with same rate of Gunk pill
22- Pumping should be continued till all the Gunk pill plus tail spacer has been pumped out of drill
    string
23- Keep pumping for 20 min with 2 bpm from both sides to displace the Gunk into the thief zone.
24- Monitor SICPP for any increasing.
25- If it has not been increased it means the thief zone still has not been cured
26- Repeat the above procedures but in step 7 utilize 8 Mt of Bentonite instead of 7 MT.
27- This procedure should be followed until there is some indication of increase pressure into the
    annulus.
28- If pressure increased in close annulus for 100-200 psi, open Hy-drill and pull drill string for 2-4
    stands.
29- Circulate sea water and monitor volume for down-hole losses.
