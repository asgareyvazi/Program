## Completion Fluids Program
Note: materials stock in Location should be more than 30% of amount, Mentioned in table above.
Preparing the Rig Equipment
Preparing includes cleaning, draining& inspecting the surface fluid handling equipment before the completion fluid is introduced.  Pits, mixing & shearing system, mud lines & mud ditches should be thoroughly cleaned & dried.
Inspect pits & dump valves for leaks.
Completion fluid should be protected from contamination while the fluid is prepared.
Mixing Procedure
Fill the pit by required volume of CaBr2.
Add corrosion inhibitor at 0.5- 1 % v/v & allow to mix for 10 minutes
Add H2S Scavenger 0.3 %v/v & allow to mix for 10 minutes.
Add o2 scavenger at 0.3 % v/v & allow to mix for 10 minutes.
Note:
Completion fluid should certainly be without any kind of solid or precipitate.
There should be used submersible pump for pits between Completion Fluid transfers
O2 Scavenger should be added at suction and not through the mixing hopper.
Before pumping completion fluid all agitators should be off.
PH of completion fluid should be 7.

## Mixing Procedures
Wash Pill
Fill pit with 70 bbl Fresh water
Add 1-3  lb/bbl soda Ash for reduce Ca+2  ion under 700 PPM
Add 0.5 lb/bbl Caustic soda.
Add 1-2 lb /bbl Xc polymer.
