1.  Introduction

This program covers the information and procedures required for Re-Entry plan for well F15-05

in Foroozan Field-Persian Gulf-IRAN.


Problem

  The well was shut in since 2012 due to low potential for oil production.


Main Objective:

 The main purpose of drilling well F-15-05-Re3H is to effectively produce Oil from Lower

Burgan-A reservoir in F-2 structure in Foroozan oil field by drilling about 670mMD horizontal

re-entry leg through this reservoir. 8 1/2" open hole in this well will be drilled to L.P. with 79 –

85pcf mud where 7" liner will be set. 6 1/8" hole will be drilled horizontally to T.D. with 85pcf

mud and the well will be completed as Lower Burgan- A oil producer.

The main headlines of this program are as follow:

 1.  Record & report RKB to THF & MSL of new well.

 4. POOH and L/D completion string and retrievable packer.
 5. RIH w/ 8-1/2" E.mill to ±1936mMD. Circ. and clean well.
 6.  Set cement plug @ +/- 1875-2036mMD interval.
 7.  Pressure test 9 5/8” CSG to 1500psi and dress cement to 1933mMD.
 8.  Set 9-5/8" Whipstock and open window @ ±1925mMD against the Ilam formation.
 9.  Dir. drilling 8-1/2” hole to 7” liner point @±2514mMD with 79-85pcf mud.
 10. Run 7” liner, hang & cement same.
 11. Dir. drilling 6-1/8” hole to TD @±3191.58mMD with 85pcf OBM.
 12. Run 4-1/2” Wire Wrapped Screen to TD.
 13. Run completion string.
 14. N/D BOP, N/U X-mass tree.
 15. Perform stimulation job.
 16. Flow the well.
 17. Secure well, Release Rig.

 3. Drilling History

 The original hole of the well F-15-05 was drilled by Jubilee rig and completed as a Burgan

producer from 13th of Sep 1975 and ending at 17th of Oct 1975 and reached to total depth of

2389.2 MD/RT. Work over operation on the well was performed in 1977 for re-perforation

job. Re-entry operation was carried out in 2003 in order to define the OWC in the Burgan”B”

via a pilot hole and then to increase the oil recovery from the Burgan sandstones which could

be reached through a lateral section within the Burgan”A” reservoir.

 26” section: Drilling of 26” hole was carried out to 231.6 MD/RT with sea water mud. 20"

(94 PPF, J-55) casing was run to 230.1 MD/RT and cemented as per program (cement was

not observed at surface). A single top cement job was performed and filled up space between

30” x 20” annulus.

 17 ½” section: Drilling was resumed vertically from 231.6 to 274.3 (KOP) MD/RT, and

continued with directionally from 274.3 to 904 MD/RT with 67.3-71.8 PCF W.B mud (max.

hole inclination was 33 deg based on directional survey report in 1975). 13 3/8” (68 PPF, J-

55) casing was run to 900.6 MD/RT and cemented as per program (observed cement return at

surface).

 12 ¼” section: Drilling was continued from 904 to 2115.2 MD/RT with 65.8 to 71 PCF

water base mud (max. hole inclination was 35 deg based on directional survey report in

1975). Pipes got stuck  at depth of 1816.5 MD/RT. Experienced minor to severe fluid loss at

the  1659.2-1756.5,  1767.7,  1779-1849.1,  1852.5-1932.3,  1932.3-2029.8, 2029.8-2121.3

MD/RT  intervals and  all attempts  to cure  loss by LCM  pill  in  several  stages went

unsuccessful, Even performing gunk cement squeezing  job  in  several  stage was  not

successful and fluid loss was not cured. Subsequently, drilling was continued from 1659.2 to

TD without mud return. Ran 9 5/8" CSG (C-75, 47 PPF) to 2114 MD/RT (9 5/8" CSG shoe)

and cemented as per program no cement return was observed at surface.

 8 ½” section: Drilling was commenced from 2114 to 2435.2 MD/RT with 74-74.8 PCF

mud  (max.  hole  inclination was  32deg  based  on  directional  survey  report  1975),

DST/MSFL/GR and FDC/CNT/GR logs were taken along 2389.2-903.7 and 2389.2-2112.1

MD/RT intervals and ran 7” liner (29# C-75) from 2022.86 to 2377.3 (shoe of 7” liner)

MD/RT and cemented as per program. D/O cement from 1828.1 to 2022.86 (TOL) MD/RT

and 7” liner was cleaned by 6” bit to 2379MD/RT (shoe track was not drilled).

7.  Expected Litho-Stratigraphic Units

The stratigraphic succession in F-15-05-Re3H will be nearly normal from window of

1925mMD  (-1691.9mTVDss)  in  base  of  Ilam  Formation  to  total  depth  of

3191.7mMD (-2037mTVDss) in Burgan-A as follows:



Ilam: From window of 1925mMD (-1691.9mTVDss) to 1935.4mMD (-1700.9mTVDss):

About 10.3mMD and 9mTVD of this formation will be drilled in this well and  it

consists of soft, white and chalky Limestone.



Upper Ahmadi: From 1935.4mMD (-1700.9mTVDss) to 2041.5mMD (-1788.9mTVDss):

About 88mTVD thickness of this member will be drilled in this well which consists

of inter-beds of cream, tan, white, chalky and crystalline Limestone and minor

brown-green fissile Shale.



Lower Ahmadi: From 2041.5mMD (-1788.9mTVDss) to 2059.1mMD (-1802.7mTVDss):

Lower Ahmadi has about 13.8m TVD thickness and mainly consists of white, light

grey, gummy Marl and green to brown fissile Shale with minor Limestone beds.

Mauddud Mbr.: From 2059.1mMD (-1802.7mTVDss) to 2188mMD (-1896.8mTVDss):

Mauddud has about 94m TVD thickness and mainly consists of Limestone, scattered

thin inter-beds of dark grey to black, soft to medium hard sticky Claystone and Shale.



Upper Kazhdumi: From 2188.9mMD (-1896.8mTVDss) to 2270.4mMD (-1951.2mTVDss):

Upper Kazhdumi composed of inter-beds of dark grey to black, soft to medium hard,

Claystone, Marls and Shale sub-ordinate layers of dark-gray, loose, oil-staining. The

sand streaks are not thick enough in this area. The TVD thickness of this unit is about

54.5m.



Dair Lst.: From 2270.4mMD (-1951.2mTVDss) to 2337.3mMD (-1990.1mTVDss):

This unit mainly composed of chalky and crystalline Limestone with inter-beds of

dark  olive  to  black, medium hard Shale and Marl and minor Dolomite. The

Limestone increasing toward the basal part of this member. The TVD thickness of

this member will be 38.9m in this well.



Burgan-A: From 2337.3mMD (-1990.1mTVDss) to T.D. of 3191.7mMD (-2037mTVDss):

Generally the Burgan-A can be divided into two parts as Upper Burgan-A and Lower

Burgan-A. This unit mostly composed of beds of dark grey, loose, oil-saturated

Sandstone and Shale with minor amount of Marl and Dolomite. The Sands are

characterized by fine to medium grain size, sub rounded to angular. The Upper

Burgan-A is mostly Shaly but the Lower Burgan-A mostly consists of three beds of

oil-saturated Sandstone within major Shale body.

The well will be enter in Lower Burgan-A at 2472.5mMD (-2032.7mTVDss) and is

planned to be landed about 2.8mTVD inside the sand bed at -2035.5mTVDss.

8.  Logging Program


8 1/2" Openhole:

  MWD/GR/ROP: The drilling of 8 1/2" deviated hole is going to be controlled by

  MWD/GR tool in order to keep the well path at the planned direction and picking

  up the Formation tops as well as selecting the proper depth for 7" liner.

   Full set (TLC): full set log will be run to observed Kazhdumi reservoir property.

6 1/8" Openhole:

 MWD (only ROP): is going to be run from 7" liner shoe to T.D. in order to keep

   the well path at the planned direction and controlling the horizontal section.


  LWD (GR/Resistivity): in horizontal section is going to be run from 7" liner shoe

   to T.D. for Geo-steering, gathering data and controlling well trajectory.


  CBL/VDL: is recommended to be run for cement bonding quality evaluation.


Rush copy shall be sent to office, after quality control, hard copy in measured depth,

TVD play back, digital data in LAS and DLIS format should be delivered.  It's

important for IOOC to record all of events at the time of logging and corrections shall

be mentioned by logging company in the final hard copy.

9.  Sampling


   Samples from cutting should be taken every 2-4m intervals, except close to the

formation tops which every 1m interval is recommended. One dry sample for each

selected interval should be stored for future references. 2 samples need to be taken

(Washed/Unwashed-100gr) in the Shaly/ Marly layers. In the reservoir section 2 sets

of sample from cutting shall be taken for thin section study. Sample inventory table,

consisting of sample depth, Box No, date, full lithology description and geologist

name shall be prepared by well site geologist.

16. Well Securing Procedure

1. Move the rig from the well F13-04 Re1H (The Foroozan Field) to F15 platform (The

   Foroozan Field).

2.  Skid on the well F15-05 and record & report RKB to MSL and THF of new well.

   Note:

     All depths are referred to RKB-MSL: 28.6m which should be corrected with new

     RKB-MSL measurement of active rig at time.

3. Hook up kill lines & flow lines to THS & X-Mass. P/T same.

4.  Monitor and record wellhead & annulus pressure. In case of any pressure (release pressure

     if possible).

5.   Kill the well by bull heading sea water in TBG. (Ref. to well history) Circulate f/ annulus

    by sea water and get return f/ tubing till mud weight in/out become balance.

6.  Keep circulation CWH during next operations.

7.  In case of unsuccessful bull heading operation circulate out through SPM w/ Sea water

    64.5pcf.

   Note:

     64.5 PCF Mud will impose ±331 psi over pressure to the Burgan-A Reservoir @

      7” window (2335mMD / -2034mTVD).

     Pump Hi Vis pill ahead of sea water in order to sweep and squeeze remaining oil

      and gas into formation.

     Adjust MW with respect to the hole condition.

     Weight up material (Salt & Limestone) and LCM should be available on board to

      be used for balancing mud weight.

     Circulate across well head to fill the well during operation.
