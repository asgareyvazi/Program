Preparation Before Job
While Tripping: Use Casing Dope on Box of Each Joint When All Casings Are on Racks (To Avoid Drop Brush Inside Casing on Rig Floor by Crew Negligence). Ask Tool Pusher to Prepare 13 3/8” * 13 5/8” Casing Running Requirements Including Drifts, Circulation Swage. Ask Tool Pusher to Prepare 13 5/8”-5K psi Spacer Spool+ 13 5/8”-5K BOP Stack & Related Equipment Check BOP Career Performance Before Job & Repair It, If It Has Any Problem. Don't Forget to Call All Related Services (Casing Running Crew, Wellhead Crew, Pump Truck) In Exact Time.  Pick Up Circulating Head, Cement Head and Place in Corner of Rig Floor. Before R/U Casing Running Equipment, Be Sure About Stabbing Board Installation & Check It’s Ready or Not by Casing Running Crew. R/U Casing Running Equipment at Proper time, to start Running Operation ASAP.  Check the 13 5/8” wear bushing, 13 5/8” Plug Tester and 9 5/8” Casing Slip in C.H.S and adopter flange before installing C.H.S (on Surface).  When Out of The Hole: Remove Wear Bushing. Clear Rig Floor. Change Bails to longer ones. R/U 13 3/8" * 13 5/8” CSG Handling tools. Install Side door elevator, Install Spider Slips. Side door elevator will be changed to spider one when having enough casing weight. Hold Pre job safety meeting. Prepare CSG Accessories on rig floor, X-Overs, Centralizers, 13 5/8” & 13 3/8" Circ. Swages, Nails, CSG Dope, Bakerlok. Ensure Hole Is Full. Monitor the Fluid level in well.  Change Top Drive Links to Longer. Change Right & Left Tong Also Change Master Bushing to Proper Size. Install Proper Side Door Elevator  Clean Up Floor Before Commence Casing Running. Kick Assembly: In Case of Encountered Emergency Condition, Use X.O.S (13 3/8" BTC P * 4 1/2" IF B) + 4 1/2" FOSV As Kick Assembly.

Casing Running and Cementing Procedure
Operation Sequences | Parallel Activities / Operation Notes
Hold Pre-Job Safety Meeting For 13 5/8” * 13 3/8" Casing Running Operations on Rig Floor. | Focus on Falling Object & Heavy Equipment. Prepare PJSM Report by HSE & Send It to Main Office. Prepare CSG Accessories on rig floor, X-Overs, Centralizers, 13 5/8” & 13 3/8" Circ. Swages, Nails, CSG Dope, Bakerlok.
R/U 13 5/8” * 13 3/8" Casing Running Equipment. | Check Running Equipment Before Starting the Job Check Elevator and Lock Pins. Check Stabbing Board.
P/U Shoe joint (13 5/8” Casing), M/U Floated Shoe w/ Applying THD lock compound, run same. 3.1 Fix First Joint of Casing on Rack.  3.2 Wash & Clean Pin Thread & Check Visually to Remove Debris. 3.3 Dry Make Up Float Shoe by Crane & Chain Tong, Check Visually to Avoid Thread Damage 3.4 Use Thread Lock Compound for Last 5 Thread & Make Up Shoe on Joint Completely  P/U & M/U Second Jts (13 5/8” CSG), Run Same. P/U Floated collar, clean THDs, M/U & Use THD lock compound for its Box. RIH & Check for function of Collar Filling.    M/U Circ. Swage, Break Circulation to confirm Flow Pass Through. | Make up and torque up the shoe to shoe joints on the pipe rack, applying Bakerlok™ on shoe box. Shoe Float Valves Should Be Inspected and Checked Before Making Up. Visually Inspect Shoe Joint for Debris While Picking Up, One Signalman to Guide Crane Operator to Prevent Breaking Shoe.  Use one Centralizer for all Joints of shoe track. Use one Centralizer for every 3 joints above shoe track. Casing String: 13 5/8" Floated Shoe, 88.2 ppf, BTC 2 Jts 13 5/8" Casing, 88.2 ppf, P-110, BTC 13 5/8" Floated Collar, 88.2 ppf, BTC 13 3/8’’x 13 5/8" Casing string: Surf -1444 m:         13-3/8"CSG, N80, 72#, BTC 1444 - 1744 m:     13-3/8"CSG, NKT-95, 72#, BTC 1744 - ±2200 m:     13-5/8"CSG, P-110, 88.2#, BTC
Run 13 5/8" Casing (88.2# P-110, BTC) as Per Tally (Totally ±465 m). F/U each 3 first joints & Break Circ. every 300m.  Note: Reduce connection time to prevent differential stuck. Pass carefully string through BOP stack. Use safety clamps while CSG string has not enough slack off weight on slips While M/Up, make sure enough torque according to 13 5/8” CSG spec. (Triangle). Install centralizer as per tally Check CSG fill up volume w/ return mud in pit while running CSG to be certain shoe is in differential condition. RIH Speed: 1.5-2 min slips to slips. | Ensure the Tally Is Followed (Make A Note of Centralizers Used and On What Joint). Any Changes or Rejects to Be Noted and WSS To Be Informed Immediately. Do Not Jerk the String at Any Time. Mud Loggers to Prepare Swab / Surge Calculations. Running Speeds Shall Be Controlled to Ensure Surge Pressures Are Not Excessive. Mud Loggers to Monitor and Advise Driller.   Select the Most Suitable Running Speed According to Hole Conditions Take Care When Running Casing Not to Set Down Excessive Weight If Tight Hole or Ledges Are Encountered. Install centralizer as per tally (one every 3 Jts)
Cont. Run CSG String as per Tally Followed by ±300 m of 13 3/8” NKT-95, 72#, BTC & Break Circ. every 300m Cont. Run CSG String as per Tally Followed by ±1444 m of 13 3/8” CSG 72#, N-80 & Break Circ. every 300m. Reduce connection time to prevent differential stuck. RIH Speed: 1.5-2 min slips to slips. | While M/Up, make sure enough torque up according to 13 3/8” CSG spec. (Triangle). Install centralizer as per tally (one every 3 Jts) Check CSG fill up volume w/ return mud in pit while running CSG to be certain shoe is in differential condition. Fill Up string before making up swage to ensure the string is full.
P/U CSG String 0.5 m & Space out.  Circulate at least 150% of the casing volume Circulation test at following flow rates, 200, 300, and max 350GPM, checking for losses, record results in report. Record Press & Losses for 5 to 15 BPM. Cont. Circ. Till get pressure stabilized. Order to make CMT Solution. RIH Speed: 1.5-2 min slips to slips.  R/D Casing Handling Equipment. | Install centralizer as per tally (one every 3 Jts) Check CSG fill up volume w/ return mud in pit while running CSG to be certain shoe is in differential condition Minimize any stopping time in open hole and in case of any extended delay, Cir. w/ Circ. swage.  Fill Up string before making up swage to ensure the string is full. The Swage for washing down must be made up on rig floor to minimize the time. Wash Down Last Two Joint Check Casing Left on Rack with Pipe Tally. Hold pre cement job safety meeting while circulating, subjected to cement job. Casing Tally and Casing Report should be sent to Tehran office as soon as job is done. Note: Final Landing Weight to Be Mentioned in Daily Drilling Report.
Hold pre job safety meeting for cementing operations on rig floor. Agree on job sequence, pressure limits, signals for open/close valves, etc   Make up cement head, and prepare for cement job (Top and Bottom Plug loaded Already).   L/U CMT Lines to rig floor. Flush the lines from CMT unit. | Check Radio communication between rig floor/cement truck/mud pits.  M/U and prepare cement solution, if not done before. Prepare Cement Slurry Solution at Appropriate Time, So That Cementing Job Can Be Started ASAP After Casing Landing. Secure the Casing on Rig Floor to Prevent Sudden Upward Jumping/Movement. Change over lines to cement head. The only time that the casing can be pressure tested is after bumping the plug while cement is still liquid. No pressure test after this time should be performed before cement is fully set to avoid creating of micro channels.
M/U cement hose to Lo-Torque valve on cement head. Line up cement truck and pressure test line against Lo-Torque valve to 500 psi/5 min and 3000 psi/10 min. | If not done before. WSS to witness.
Pump 40 bbls chemical wash (by pump truck) & 100 bbls 102 pcf Spacer (by rig pump) which is prepared already in Mud tanks.  Release/Drop bottom plug | WSS to witness. Derrick man to visually check strokes on mud pump. Mud engineer and cement engineer make sure of exact volume pumped. Cement Program Will Be Issued Separately.
L/U to cement unit, Mix and pump cement as per cementing program. (1504 bbls, 105 pcf as Lead CMT + 165 bbls, 118 pcf as Tail CMT). CMT Job Pumping Rate:  Lead: 10 bbl/min,  Tail: 6 bbl/min. Note: Final volume of cement slurry should be calculated on rig site. Release top plug. Release top plug within maximum 10 min. (in case of happening any issues, don’t open cement head).  5 bbl cement slurry should pump behind top plug to mitigate any washing possibility or cement contamination.  Pump 5 bbls Tail CMT and 2 bbls drilling water behind the plug. Its recommended to M/U a union+ Lo-TQ valve above CMT Head to Pump 5 bbl CMT behind from here. | Reset stroke counter at driller console. While pumping if pressure increased suddenly, do not let it exceed 3000 Psi. Mud engineer and cement engineer make sure of exact volume pumped.  Monitor the returns continuously. Mud engineer to check. All Senior Personnel Should be present while CMT Job.  Cement samples to be taken during cementing operation. If possible, (in case of casing hanger has been not set) reciprocate casing for max 1m. Mud logger to plot pressure. Check string weight at all the time. Mud loggers and mud engineer to monitor mud tank volumes closely while displacing, keep WSS/cementer informed at all times.
L/U CMT Head to mud pump and displace the cement by mud pump with 1051 bbl 100 pcf mud.  Pumping Rate:15 bbl/min and last 40 bbl w/ 3 bbl/min. The 5 bbl CMT which pumped after plug considered in displacing volume.  Take Rig Pumps efficiency before CMT job. All Rig pumps must be serviced & ready for operation. Bump the plug with final circulating pressure and increase in increments to 1000 psi (Green test). Hold pressure for 15 mins. Check back flow from CMT Unit. In case of any abnormal return, close the valve of cement head and monitor pressure while surface samples are getting hard. Open Side Outlet Valve of 21 1/4” CHH, Flush & Clean Side Outlet & Diverter. WOC to get hard. Do not install handy slips around the casing string. Keep the rest of String WT on elevator till surface samples get hard | Volume of displacement is same as cement program and should be recalculated on rig site, rate of displacement may be changed based on hole and loss condition. In case of observe bumping Integrity test (green test) is performed w/ Max. 1000 psi for 15 min. If plug does not bump after calculated displacement volume, continue with half of shoe track volume.
Hold on casing string weight. Set the 13 3/8" casing hanger. Slack off 80% of casing string weight. Pressure test casing hanger. Remove the flow line and N/D diverter. Rough cut and L/D Landing Joint. Dress and bevel casing neck. Install 21 1/4"-2K * 13 5/8"-3K C.H.S. Test P-Seal and Cavity W/Max 80% of Casing Collapse Pressure.  N/U Double 13 5/8" 5K DSA Locking Tie Down Flange + 13 5/8"-5K Spacer Spool + 13 5/8"-5K BOP Stack and related lines and valves and test all as NIOC policy. N/U Bell Nipple & Flow line.  Send FINAL CASING TALLY, CASING REPORT and CEMENT REPORT JOB to Engineering Office. |

Contingency / First Action Plans
Keep in Mind That the Slurries Have Thixotropic Properties (High YP’s) So It Is Important to Keep Pumping, Even If Only at A Low Rate. Once Pumping Has Stopped It Will Be Difficult to Get the Slurries to Move Again. Closely Monitor Drags. Drags Usually Increase as More Casing Is Run in To the Open Hole, A Normal Incremental Trend Should Be Observable for DRAG Parameters. If A Departure from This Trend Is Observed and Drags, Or Pick Up Force of String After Fill Up or Connection, Begin to Become Excessive, Following Actions Are Suggested: Move string upward 4-5m.  Slowly Reciprocate the string to determine if the drags reduce. Do not push the string downward if excessive drags still exist (more than previous joint drag or normal one) If needed, LD last joint, Break circulation slowly, monitoring the pump pressure and returns for signs of packing off. Circulate bottoms up and monitor the shakers (if possible). Note:   If drags or pick up force of string after fill up or connection remain excessive (more than 50% of normal condition) and continues to increase, consideration should be given to pulling out of the hole the casing for a wiper trip.

Safety Recommendation:
WORK SAFE; ALWAYS ASK YOURSELF HOW CAN I GET HURT IF I DO THIS TASK. Safety Standards and Policy of PECO Should Be Followed During Drilling Operation. In Case of Any Deviation or Advise for This Instruction Call Co. Man ASAP. All Rig Crew Should Follow and Cooperate with Job Specialists as Per This Instruction.  Mud Eng. Is Responsible for Monitoring & Report Pit Levels During Operation. There Is Always Time to Rig Up and Operate Safely:  Take “Time-Out”, If Necessary. Pre- Plan the Job Ahead, Do Not Run Afterwards. Make Sure All Areas Involved in Casing Transport and Handling Are Secured to Avoid Unauthorized Entry into Areas. Focus on Falling Objects: Avoid Loose Items on Rig-Floor and All Decks Below; All Equipment Used Above 2m Has to Be Secured with Wire. Before Starting the Job, Cementing Supervisor Should Check Place of Dumping & Washing the Truck Required After the Job with HSE Officer and Company Man to Eliminate Any Environmental Impact. Flush and Pump Out Cellar. Dry Up Cellar as Much as Possible. Waste management senior must prepare coral & ready to storage volume of excess mud & cement slurry during cement job operation. Also check sludge pump on cellar & if it has any problem work on & repair it.
