HSE Focus points:

     There  is always time to  rig up and operate safely:  Take “time-out”,  if
           necessary.
     Pre- plan the job ahead, don’t run afterwards.
     No work at two levels without a barrier in between;
               Acquire work orders signed by driller and all area authorities;
               Use barrier tape
               Keep people away
               Radio communication
               Respond back on communication
     Hold PJSM and make sure all personnel involved are aware of safety issues.

Operation Sequence:

       Continue mill and retrieve first completion packer.

       RIH w/ 7” scraper and scrap inside 7” liner to 3880m.

      POOH to surface.

       RIH with 7” permanent bridge plug, set bridge plug at 3875m.

       Rig up dry test equipment and run to ±3845m, set packer and perform dry test

          (dry test program will be issued separately), POOH and L/D dry test tools.

       RIH w/ open ended DPs to 3845m, Circulate and balance mud weight and add

           inhibitor to mud system.

      POOH to 3700m, Set 35bbl 118pf cement balance plug.

      WOC to get hard, RIH to & tag cement w/ 10-15klb and pumping (TOC @3550m).
      POOH to ±1480m and pump 35bbl cement as 2nd cement plug. (TOC @1330m)

       Wait on cement to get hard, run back and confirm and check top of cement.

       POOH to Surface and L/D DPs.

       N/D BOP stack.

       Install blind flange (11", 10k).

       Secure the well, release the rig.
