                         Workover Program_ Kangan-23


1. Program Introduction

           The well kangan-23 expected to have collapse problem in shallow depth. A lot of seepages
            were observed around the well.
            Kangan-23 was a gas producer well with about 2.5 million cubic meters per day gas flow rate
              with 1900 psi flowing wellhead pressure while shut in tubing pressure was 2800 psi.
           The flowing wellhead pressure suddenly dropped from 1900 psi to 880 psi (9th, Oct. 2005) so
              decision was made to close the well. Wellhead pressure =880 psi, 9 5/8”x7” annulus pressure
           =550 psi, 13 3/8”x9 5/8” annulus =270 psi and Observed seepages and crakes around the well
                (In radius of 150 meters).
               After two days (11th, Oct. 2005) suddenly above mentioned pressures dropped as follows:
            Wellhead pressure =145 psi, 9 5/8”x7” annulus pressure =145 psi, 13 3/8”x9 5/8” annulus =180
               psi
          Run Diff. Gauges Rings, 1 ½” G.R. could not pass down through 66m depth (2 m below the
          3SV landing nipple).
                 All evidences shows there is a severe casing damage between 60-300m so that the 7” tubing
           and other casings in the well maybe damaged and connected to each other and to the behind
              formation, which caused an under ground blow out and the gas from tubing flowed in the
             shallow formation.

     Kangan-23 before accident

                   1.   It was a Gas Producer well
                   2.  Production Rate was 2.5 MSCMD
                   3.  Flow Rate = 130 Bars (#1900 psi)
                   4.  Well Head closed pressure = 2800 psi
                   5. A 5000 psi Well Head & X-Mass Tree is installed.
                   6.  Casing, Liner, Down Hole Completion, Well & Well Head schematics & Sketches are
                  coming Later on.

     Kangan-23 after Accident (9th, Oct. 2005)

                   1.  Flow Rate Decreased suddenly from/1900 psi to 880 psi
                   2.  Decided to closed the well
                   3.  Monitoring the pressers:
                        a) W/H Press. 880 psi
                        b) 9 5/8” x 7” Ann. Press. 550 psi
                          c) 13 3/8” x 9 5/8” Ann. Press. 270 psi
                   4.  Observed see pages and crakes around the well (In radius of 150 meters)
                   5.  After two days (11th, Oct. 2005) Suddenly above mentioned pressures dropped to the s
                       followings:
                        a) W/H press. 145 psi
                        b) 9 5/8” x 7” Ann. Press. 145 psi
                          c) 13 3/8” x 9 5/8” Ann. Press. 180 psi
                   6.  Run Diff. Gauges Rings, even 1 ½” G.R. could not pass through from 66mt depth (2 m
                   below the 3SV landing nipple).

              Important Note:
                                                    It seems that the pipes have been collapsed from this point (66m).

                   7.  From above mentioned date, water, cement and related additives were pumped into
                       well and its annuluses to kill & secure the well periodically but it was not secured and
                           finally the pressures dropped to 70, 76 & 60 psi (W/H, 9 5/8” x 7” & 13 5/8” x 9 5/8”
                   Annuluses)

   Kangan-23B, 23A & 23 Present Status

Kangan-23:
The well is secured by pumping 8 bpm in KGN23B and 5 bpm into 7" tubing of KGN23. The well location
is fixed and water supplying line and burning line is prepared.
Wireline operation:
    •   Tried to run 6-1/16" gage ring, could not pass through dipping valve (valve could not be fully
       opened)
    •  Run 2-1/2" impression block to 65.8m, tag weight. (No mark on bottom, but on body).
    •  Run 1.775" swage to 65.98m, tag weight. (Found 1.625" ring mark).
    •  Run 1.5" stem bar, could not pass through 65.98m.

Kangan-23B: This well was drilled down to total depth of 2500m and was entered into drainage area of
Kangan-23 gas well:

1.  56.5m 6-1/8" open hole has been drilled from 7" liner shoe down to 2500m with 56 pcf oil Emulsion
   mud (55 sec M.F.Viscosity) in Kangan gas reservoir with no mud losses.
2.  Displaced mud by fresh water; caused 80 BPH losses which decreased to 16 BPH. The mud losses
   caused the wellhead pressure of Kangan-23 to start decreasing after one hour.
3.  Performed an injectivity test with an average rate of 500 BPH and 1150 psi pumping pressure, which
   caused a significant pressure drop on Kangan-23 wellhead (total injected water 2000 bbls)
4.  While preparing to supply and store enough water to secure well Kangan-23, remained pumping 130-
   350 BPH for several days. Wellhead pressure of Kangan-23 dropped to ±5 bar and remained almost
    constant.(pressure before mud losses in Kangan-23B ±16 bars)
5.  Inject ± 75000 bbls water via Kangan-23B first relief well with an average rate of 750 BPH and 350-
   550 psi pumping pressure.
6.  Injected 5750 bbls Hi-Vis pill of 120 sec M.F.Viscosity with 200 BPH and 350 psi pumping pressure.
7.  Performed High rate water injection via Kangan-23B. Totally pumped 38760 bbls with 2000 BPH and
   900 psi surface injection pressure.
8.  Spotted (114+120+100+30) bbls 30 lb/bbl Hi-vis, shell fish pill @ 2500m (Relief well). Squeezed
   same into loss zone.
9.  Cleaned hole to 2495m with 10-20 BPH losses. Spotted 50 bbls, 102 pcf magneset @ 2325-2495
    mtr. Pressure tested same to 2000 psi, No injection.
10. Drilled out magneset plug down to 2464m w/4-8 BPH losses.
11. Injected 39760 bbls water via Kangan-23B with 10-60 BPH and 1550 psi max surface injection
    pressure.
12. Pumped 500 bbls colured water inside well Kangan-23 via its wellhead which observed (Sprayed Out)
    with exiting gas after pumping 70 bbls (5 BPM).
13. Perforated open hole of well Kangan-23B from 2463.5m – 2447m three times by 3-3/8 HSD jets (mud
    losses 50 BPH).
14. Pumped and injected 2000 gal's HCL Acid (28%) into perforated open hole, which followed by high
    rate of water injection. Injected 19800 bbl with 80-90 BPH, and 2500 psi surface injection pressure,
    then 2400 bbl with 30 BPH and 1200 psi surface injection pressure which stopped due to getting fire
    of gas seepages. (Fill up Kangan-23B with 3-5 BPM water)
15. Injected 2000 bbl, 100 pcf mud inside well Kangan-23 via its wellhead which followed by 2000 bbls
    water.
16. Pumped and injected 10500 bbls Hi-Vis pill of 120 sec viscosity via Kangan-23B.
17. Injected 2230 bbls cement of 115 pcf slurry weight followed by 87 bbls cement of 100 pcf slurry
    weight. Stopped cement injection due to build up of pumping pressure. (12-24 BPM ~ 500-2000 psi)
18. Drilled three directional legs in different directions:
        •  From 2448m to 2515m
        •  From 2447m to 2624m
        •  From 2448m to 2630m
19. Injected 16300 bbl fresh water w/5-60 BPH and 80-1300 psi surface injection pressure.
20. Plugged back all holes by cement and made another hole at the same direction as that of the first
    hole, but build up inclination up to 85º at 2519m TD.

2. Objectives

           The main objectives are to pass through the expected collapsed interval, to plug back
             the well at bottom and to secure it by cement plugs, to pull out completion string to top
             of packer and to check 9-5/8"casing.

3. Kangan Gas Field Structure

           Kangan anticline located in coastal Fars, southwest of Nar and west of west Assaluyeh
               anticlines and north of Kangan harbour in Busher province. This structure extended northwest –
             southeast direction and divided to 4 parts of A, B, C and D. The Kangan anticline is nearly
            asymmetric and base on structural map has 55Km length and 6Km width. The youngest and
              the  oldest Formations on  surface  are  Bakhtiyari  (Pliocene) and Sarvak (Cenomanian)
               respectively.
           The highest dip angles of 30 and 40 degree were defined for north and south flanks of Kangan
               anticline respectively and two reverse faults cut this flanks parallel to structure elongation.

5. Drilling History

             Well Kangan-23 was drilled by NIDC Rig FA43. Well was spudded on 1372/11/02 in Kangan
               Field. The well was drilled down to total depth of 2584m in Kangan formation. Completion string
           was run, packer was set 2162m, well was secured and Rig was released on 1373/08/17.


     36” Hole Section:

                 Drilling with 26" bit & 36" reamer to 39m with bentonite mud. Due to complete losses, resumed
                 drilling with Air Foam to 60m. R/U & Run 30" Conductor pipe to 28.5m (Due to hole problem, no
           more progress of running casing).Casing was cemented with return.

     26” Hole Section:

           Resumed drilling f/60m t/584m with Air Foam. R/U & Run 20" casing to 571m (Casing Shoe),
            cement casing. Performed cement top jobs & got return.


     17 1/2” Hole Section:

          Resumed drilling f/584m t/1084m with Air Foam & good return. Left fish in hole (@ 1084m),
             recovered same. Cont. drilling to 1975m with Air Foam. R/U & Run 13 3/8" casing to 1975m
             (Casing Shoe). Cement casing, after several times cement top job, got return.


     12 1/4” Hole Section:

                 Drilling with 12 1/4" bit to 2305m with bentonite mud 64-69 pcf ,Complete losses @ 2228m,
             cure losses by LCM pill. R/U & Run 9 5/8" casing to 2305m (Casing Shoe). Cement casing,
             poor cement in annul.(top of cement @ 2180m), try to fill annulus by cement, no success.


     8 1/2” Hole section:

          Resumed drilling with 8 1/2" bit to 2422m with 68 pcf mud &7-10 bbl/hrs losses. R/U & Run 7"
                 liner to 2422m (Liner Shoe), L.Lap @ 2181.5m. Cement Liner. Pressure test and dry test liner
                lap, OK.
                * RIH Packer (type: TPH) with Setting tools, Circ. Mud, set Tie Back Seal in Tie Back Sleave.
         New L.Lap @ 2178.4m

         * Note: This section was performed after reservoir log in 6” hole section.

     6” Hole section & Completion

          Resumed drilling to 2571m with 0-15 bbl/hr losses while pumped LCM pill, Loss increased to 58
                bbl/hr, cont. drilled while pumped LCM pill to 2583m with 0-16 bbl/hr losses. R/U & Run
               reservoir logs. Run completion string, secure well & rig released.

15.  Work over Program

    1. While pumping 8 bpm water in KGN23B, pump 5 bpm continuously through 7"
       tubing inside well "KGN23" and monitor previous seepages for any flow of gas.

    2. The well head valves & P-Seals must be checked and serviced by well head
       specialist and be repaired if needed. Record the wellhead pressures. Release /
      Bleed off same and monitor the pressure build up if necessary.

    3. Connect 7" x 9-5/8" annulus to killing and flowing lines, test same to 4500 psi
      pressure and monitor other annuluses for any pressure increase or gas flow all
       the time. Pump 5 bpm water while operation.

    4. L/D 7-1/6"-5000 dipping valve and 7-1/16"-5000 solid four way cross.

   Note: 6-1/16" gage ring could not pass through 7-1/16" dipping valve. This
   valve could not be fully opened.

    5. R/U wireline equipment on top of 7-1/16" – 5000 safety valve; test same to 4500
       psi pressure. Install 2.750" plug into dummy. Test same to 4500 psi pressure.

    6.  Install 6-5/16" "RN" BPV and test same to 4500 psi. L/D x-mas-tree equipment
       to top of 13-5/8" X 11" – 5000 tubing head spool.

    7.  N/U 13-5/8-5000 BOP's and related lines and test same to 4500 psi pressure as
      per NIOC policy.

   Important Note:

     -  Shear rams should be included.
     -  Rotating head package is suggested to be installed.
     -  Perform safety meeting for all involved crews and people.
     -  Lubricating assembly should be available on well site.
     -  Use ditch magnet to detect milled out metal pieces.
     -  Adequate fishing tools should be available on well site (magnets, wash
      over shoes, impression plug, spears, over shots, …)

    8.  R/U wire line equipments and test same to 4500 psi. Retrieve 6-5/16" "RN" BPV,
     dummy plug and dummy.

    9.  RIH with 3-3/4" taper mill to top of obstruction. Mill out same to reach down the
       collapsed interval to 500m.

   Note:
     -  Use 4-1/8" and 5-7/8" taper mills if necessary. If could not pass thorough
      collapsed interval use 3-3/4" up to 5-7/8" junk mills and attempt to mill and
     pass same.
