Operation:  Cont. Clean out inside 3 1/2'' Liner & displace the well by 2.69"  Junk mill
Pooh CT to surface & L/D 2.69" J.Mill RIH W/ 2.75" turbo scale mill on CT to 3 1/2" LDC Clean out inside 3 1/2" Liner with max 500 lbs weight & 40-60 gpm. M.while clean out inside 3 1/2" LNR Prepare & Make 78 pcf non-solid brine (comp. fluid) as program  B/O connection between X.O.S (5 1/2" FH P * 4 1/2" IF B) & 5 1/2" DP, pass MHA thru same, M/U 2.75" turbo scale mill then M/U X.O.S Zero depth, RIH clean out assy. thru temporary Kick off string,  Pass 5"/3" L.L @ 3497/3949 m gently. Record PU/SOHL every 500m & prior to 5" & 3 1/2" L.Laps M.While pump 2 bbls thru CT to avoid plugging In case of taking WT due to any CMT, Drill out same to LDC @ 4595 m while P/U CT 5m every 50cm drilled to make sure string is free Check off bottom and on bottom pressure continuously Pump 20 bbls Hi-Vis every two hrs. Check & Report Loss & Gain while clean out by Mud Engineer.   Pump 20 bbls Hi-vis pill & CBU till clean shaker. Establish reverse circ. by rig pump thru ann. & got return via kick off strg. M.while reciprocate CT to avoid stuck same Displace the well w/ 78 pcf non-solid brine (comp. fluid) thru CT up to 10 m above temporary kick off strg.  Check & Report Loss & Gain while clean out by Mud Engineer.  POOH CT to surface, L/D MHA & R/D CTU equipment. Cont. displace the well w/ 78 pcf non-solid brine (comp. fluid) by rig pump thru temporary kick off strg. Check & Report Loss & Gain while displace the well by Mud Engineer.   Safety recommendation: Work Safe; always ask yourself how can I get hurt if I do this task In case of any deviation or advice for this instruction call Co. Man ASAP All Rig crew should follow and cooperate with job specialists as per this instruction  Mud Eng. Is responsible for monitoring & report pit levels during operation H2S equipment should be maintained & functional at all-time  CT fishing tools must be ready on site  S.Noori / A.Aghapour
