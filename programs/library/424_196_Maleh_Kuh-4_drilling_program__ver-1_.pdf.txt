1  Introduction


     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers the drilling of well# 4 in Maleh Kuh field, Iran. The well is
             intended to evaluate reservoir properties and to produce oil from Ilam reservoir. The
             well will be drilled vertically to planed total depth of 1547m (about 102m under
           O.W.C) in Ilam reservoir. After reservoir evaluation results, the well will be drilled
               directional- Horizontally.

            Note:
                 - Directional- Horizontal Program will be issued in due time.
                 - The exact TD will be determined based on the logging results.
             -This program has been written with respect to the  letter (Geological Forecast)

   3 Well Objectives

The primary objectives of drilling the well Maleh Kuh - 4 are:

• To perform development of phase#2 of field
• To evaluate reservoir properties.
• To evaluate the fluid contact levels in the reservoir
• To produce oil from Ilam reservoir.
• To gather data and information that allows optimising the
  planning of the next wells.

9 Pre Spud


      P.P.Z prepared a pre-spud checklist and distributed it in contractor rigs. The checklist will

      be performed based on  the aforementioned  checklist. In addition  to  the  checklist,

      following matters should be checked:

   ¾ Check & observe the alignment of the rig. This check will be performed in four stages;

           rig levelling, derrick verticality, derrick alignment with rotary table, rotary table

         alignment with conductor pipe. To do this pick up 3 joints of 9 3/4” DC and check the

           verticality of the derrick.

   ¾  Mechanical facilities for mud treatment such as :

         Desander, Desilter, mud cleaner, proper size of shaker–screens, hoppers, mud guns &

          mixers, should be all available on rig and functioning properly.

   ¾  All mud tanks, mud transfer lines & connection should be checked by filling them

         with water & cross wellhead circulation.

   ¾ Mud engineer laboratory should be equipped with a complete set of testing equipment.

        The calibration should be done under supervision of well site drilling supervisor

          before starting the operation.

   ¾ Weight indicator, rotary speed gauge, any smart drilling instrumentation, Stroke meter,

          pressure gauges on manifold and in the drilling console, loss / gain gauge and any

          other gauges should be checked and calibrated.

   ¾  Stand pipe manifold valves & connection must be checked & pressure tested.

   ¾  All motors should be checked under load prior to start drilling.

   ¾ Check & control mud stock & cement stock every day & if there is any necessity  to

          request mud material, cement or any drilling tool, it should be prepared & requested at

         proper time.

   ¾  Air drilling package must be installed & ready for operation.

       Note: Fill up pre-spud check list and send it to drilling office.

1.3 Sequence of Operation

     •  Make up 26” BHA as per BHA design, drill formation down to 150m (20” casing
         point in Talezang formation). Use LCM pill if encountered heavy losses. If loss
         couldn’t be cured by LCM pill use cement plug(s) to cure same.

     Note:
       - 26” TD is based on 150m inside Talezang formation.
       - TD should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of rat hole under casing shoe.
       - The exact TD will be determined based on 20” casing tally.
       - Do not forget to strap pipe while POOH.
       - Drop Totco prior to POOH to evaluate the verticality of hole.
       - Use following parameters to drill this hole section:
        -WOB = Maximum available
         -RPM = 90-130
        -GPM = 600-1000

     •  At TD, Circulate, clean hole. POOH, P/U 26” N.Bit stabilizer or 26” Reamer, RIH
        and condition the hole to section TD. Check for any fill. CBU on bottom.

     •  POOH. If observed any indication of drag, run back to bottom. Wash and ream to
       make sure that the hole condition is good.

     •  R/U 20” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

     •  Run 20", 106.5 ppf, K-55, BTS casing with stab in-type guide shoe to bottom as per
         casing program. Casing shoe should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of
           rat hole and enough stick up (1.5 m) on rig floor. Fill casing by non LCM fresh mud
          joint by joint.

     Note:
       -  Take care when running casing not to set down excessive weight if tight hole or
         ledges are encountered.

     •  Run stinger on 5” DP’s using the “C” Plate & 5” x 20” centralizer. Sting in. M/U &
           test cementing lines. Circulate for one casing annulus volume. While circulating,
        check the DP’s annulus for any return. Use all DP’s weight on the string, if
         observing any minor leakage through DP’s annulus.

    •  Cement casing as per program. Any minor adjustment regarding the corrected depth
          will be done in due time. Displace with 7 bbls mud (2 bbls Short). Sting out. Check
         the float. Circulate to clean the DP. POOH.
