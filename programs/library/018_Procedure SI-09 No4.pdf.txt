Page 1 of 1                                     Procedure No4 SI-09

           WELL SI-009 New casing point 13-3/8” x 13-5/8” due to flow procedure

     1)   Kill & balance MW @ 2146m (BTM)

     2) POOH to surface (With Back ream to preventing stuck)

     3)  RIH W/ OEDP to 2146m (BTM).

        Note: Move string as much as possible and reduce connection time to decrease risk of

          differential stuck.

     4)  Mix & Pump 100bbls 122pcf cement and displace as balance plug.

     5) POOH 10 stands, Flush string. Observe well for any loss or gain.

     6) POOH to surface.

     7)  RIH W/ 17-1/2” Bit and tag TOC @ +/- 2046m.

     8) D/O cement to 2106m (New casing point)

     9)  Decrease MW to 100pcf stepwise each step 5pcf and observe well each step.

        Note: In case of observe Gain in each step, kill the well and repeat cement job to isolate

        flow zone.

     10) Condition hole prior to run 13-3/8” x 13-5/8” casing.

     11) CBU, POOH to surface & L/D 17-1/2” Bit.

     12) R/U & Run 13-3/8” x 13-5/8” casing. Cement same as per program.

        Note:

            •   Casing running procedure already send

            •  Cement program ready send

            •  GR log will be run inside 13-3/8” x 13-5/8” casing due to abnormal hole condition.

     13) Continue operation as per program.
