

### PREPARATION TO BE DONE BEFORE RUNNING 20”CASING
Make pipe tally (measuring & numbering all the casing joints)
Inspect casing
Check inside the shoe & make sure there is no rubbish in it
Welding 4 lugs for the guide ropes.
Mark casing with white paint every one meter from top of 18 3/4" wellhead housing to 5 meter below
Also from shoe to 5 meter above it
Check housing measurement, diameter & length
Make up cementing assembly and rack in derrick.
Make up the running tool to 1 x 10’ pup joint
Pick up 18 ¾” Wellhead joint to rig floor and install CART with pup jt. In horizontal position.
Pick up 18 ¾” well head jt. into derrick, remove master bushings and lower into rotary table. Install 20” casing bushings. Lower well head and land on bushings.
Remove CART tool and connect to 1 single 5” DP in mouse hole, torque same.
Pick up CART assembly and run inside 18 ¾” housing. Make up CART tool and paint white marks.
Perform pick up test and P/up and lay out 18 ¾” housing and stinger assembly.
Grinder with cutting disk for anti rotation tabs ready on rig floor.
Pipe deck:
2 ropes size ¾” * 20 meter long for tag line
4 slings 6 meter long & 4 shackles with SWL 5 T
1 set of 2 slings 10 meter long 6 T SWL
Cellar Deck:
4 life jackets
2 safety belts
2 stop chute safety line device
1 riding belt
