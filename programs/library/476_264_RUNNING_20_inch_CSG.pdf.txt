        RUNNING PROCEDURE FOR 20” CSG


      •  Conduct toolbox meeting w/ crew & 3rd party.
      •   All PTW completed and signed, prior to toolbox meeting.
      •  Have ROV ready when you have 20”shoe 3m off Well Head.
      •  2 x 20” elevators, one set use as dog collar or safety clamp.
      •   Slips & bushings, CSG tongs set up for 20” (power & manual tongs).
      •  Spare CSG “O“ rings, Oil and rags.
      •  5.“C “ plate for stinger, slips & false rotary using ITS CSG equip.
      •  Connect Ezy torque line to back up tong.
      •  Change to 20” CSG side door elevator.
      •  R/up power tong to m/up snub post, if using.

        Note: Install 20” bushings only when 20” shoe joint is below table.
      •  Place shoe Jt. on catwalk & lift up to rig floor with crane.
      •   Link tilt and latch elevators, release link tilt to normal mode position.
      •  Pick up, tailing in joint with crane, using rope at V door to assist control of casing on

           rig floor. Remove crane and slings. Run into rotary, start pump & check float shoe.
      •  Land joint in slips use 20” elevators as dog collar, until 10 joints are run.
      •  Pick up next joint and stab in.
      •  Put on back up tong & take up the slack on Ezy torque line.
      •  ITS casing crew to install power tong & m/up connections to specs or use manual

         tongs.
      •  Run casing to moonpool and connect guide ropes to guidelines.
      •    Fill CSG every joint after 1st joint is below sea level.
      •  Continue running 20” CSG
      •  Set CSG low enough to use iron roughneck to m/u stinger if possible.
      •   and to make it safe and easy to run stinger.
      •  Rig down ITS power tongs.
      •   Position “C“ plate & false rotary on top of last joint of casing,&
      •   run d/p stinger using 5” elevators, X 2

      •  When stinger is run, change to 5 ½” elevators. Pick up 18 ¾” wellhead with tail pipe

        from the deck. 5” DP inside and connect tail pipe to stinger in casing. Pick up

          stinger and remove “C” plate.
  •  Make up casing connection on pin end of 18 ¾” housing to casing in rotary table.
  •  Check marks on CART tool and make pick up test. Remove 20” bushings.
  •   R.I.H with CSG on landing string. Run to sea level and fill casing, lower man in riding

      belt to close snorkel tube ball valve)
  •   R.I.H with CSG on landing string.
  •    Fill up landing string as per client representative program.
  •
      •  Compensate String Prior To Stabbing Into 30” LP/Well Head.
      •  Have ROV assist you in entering 30” well head. Move rig if required.
      •   After 20” shoe is stabbed into well head, proceed running casing with landing

           string.
      •  Have ROV assist and monitor closely when 20” casing hanger is approaching 30”

          well head.
      •  Land off on well head.
      •  Latch 20” hanger, take over pull with DSC 50 Kips to confirm latch.
      •  Perform cementing according to operators specs, testing surface lines, plug valves

          etc.
      •  When cement job is performed, close plug valve,
      •  Knock of cement line and check for back flow.
      •  When OK Retrieve CART Running Tool From 18 ¾” Wellhead Housing by rotating

         the stem of CART tool 5 turns R/HAND and Continue to pull assembly to surface .
      •  Leave DSC unlocked as you pull out of casing, once out of casing lock DSC.
      •  Commence out of the hole until landing string & running tool is out of hole.
      •  Break off running tool, lay out, clean rig floor and tools.
