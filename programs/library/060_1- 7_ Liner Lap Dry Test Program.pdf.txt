Normal practice:

1-  Prior to start the jobs for the inflow test, it is necessary to scrape 9 ⅝” casing specially 1584 m
   ±20 m ( packer set depth)
2- Lay down 9 5/8" scraper BHA and check  all scraper blades.
3- Remove wear bushing.
4- Assemble Dry DST BHA under instruction of in charge operator.put one set mechanical
   guage in the BTM carrier and one set electronic memeory gauges in the middle carrier.
5-  Start run in hole while Fill string every each 5 stands with cushion to 478 m. Check the level of
   cushion inside string.Any drop level or return must be reported to DST operator ASAP.
6-  Cont. RIH to 1588 m.
7- Pick up and make up surface test equipments including flow head,flowing lines, DST floor
   choke manifold. Test surface equipments up to 3000 psi for 15 min.
8-  Set the packer at 1584 m.
   Note: Dry test packer Not be set on casing coupling
9- Check to observe indication of tester valve opening.check annalus level.
10- Perform initial flow period to 180 minutes.Check bubbles in bucklet. If cushion got on
    surface, terminate flowing period and go to next step of Dry test by closing DCIP valve.
11- Shutt the well from DCIP for 360 minutes.
12- Drop the bar and perform reverse mud circulation from reversing sub in the fifth hour of
    build up period.check return.record water, mud and probable influx.
13- Continue till mud balanced on both sides.
14- Perform direct circulation at least one cycle.
15- Lay down surface tools and unset packer.
16- Perform mud circulation one cycle.Monitor the well for 30 min., If static start, make the
   well secured under supervision of DSV ,then POOH to surace slowly to prevent swabbing.
17- Lay down DST BHA,Read charts, provide final report and inform the base in Ahwaz.
