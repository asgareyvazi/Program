RIG SPECIFIC PROCEDURE:
Suspension of drilling operations due to shallow gas

PERSONNEL INVOLVED: OIM – S.T.P. - Driller – Asst Driller – Derrickman –Drill
crew – Barge Eng. – Crane Op – Roustabouts, ROV and Standby boat.

       MINIMUM CONSIDERATION FOR THE RISK ASSESSMENT
       Operational   Noise  COSHH   Manual    Lighting     Environment
                                      Handling                  Impact

       Yes     Yes                              Yes

PRE TASK ACTIONS:
The planning process is mandatory for all tasks and a THINK Drill is required
    when there are changes or additions to the task

              Toolbox talk          Yes       No
               Permit to Work                  No
                Electrical Isolation                 No
            Mechanical Isolation                 no

Pre Task Actions:

1.   Hold pre spud meeting and discuss potential of shallow gas and reviewing of
     the rig specific procedures.
2.   Perform a ‘THINK DRILL and discuss all the issues that you may run into.
3.   Water deluge systems will be running on all engine exhaust systems including
     the emergency generator.
4.  ROV fitted with sonar capable of detecting any release of gas from the well
     bore to be deployed on the sea bed at all times while drilling, circulating and
      tripping.
5.    Sufficient Kill mud, to be prepared before Drilling commences.
6.   Where applicable all watertight doors and hatches will be kept closed while
     shallow gas procedures are in force.
7.  A spare set of manual elevators to be on Drill floor as an alternative means of
     dropping the Drill string.
8.   Bubble watch to be in moon pool at all times.
9.   There will be no Hot Work performed during this section.
10.  Read the NDCO Well Control manual and Section on Shallow Gas in the
       Drilling manual.
11.  Standby vessel to be notified and will be positioned up wind of any potential
     gas release.
12.  The minimum volume of Kill mud that is required on the rig will be the
      calculated annular volume of section TD. Mud weight will be 1-2 ppg heavier
     than the mud in use.
13.  No access to columns without written permission from OIM (+PTW)

      to release in the event of emergency move off. Anchor winch deluge to be

IF SHALLOW GAS IS ENCOUNTERED:
1.  Driller to increase pump strokes to maximum (do not exceed the maximum pump
   speed or relief valve settings.)
2. Switch pump suction to Kill Mud in reserve pits without stopping the mud pumps
   and zero stroke counter.
3. Raise alarm and inform OIM, STP and Barge dept (Note: - Control room to inform
   Standby vessel shallow gas has been encountered, and the rig is activating
   shallow gas procedures).
4. Attempt to control the flow by pumping kill mud at maximum rate. When ¾ of kill
  mud volume has been pumped, slow down pumps to determine if well is still
   flowing with ROV sonar.
5.  If well still continues to flow, increase to maximum pump rate until all Kill Mud is
   used up, then switch to seawater and prepare to release string.

WHILE DRILLING:- PREPARE TO RELEASE DRILL STRING (IF NECESSARY)
1. Once all the mud has been exhausted and sea water has not bridged the hole
   over, a decision will be made to drop the Drill string.
2. PA announcement to be made to clear cellar deck of all personnel.
3. Inform ROV operator to abandon the vehicle. Shut down unit and go to the
   temporally refuge or Safe Breathing area.
4. Pick up to the lowest connection possible; keep pumping as long as you can.
5. Set the Drill string in the slips and break the connection (twice). Stop pumping.
6. Run back to bottom and set down weight. The TDS should be reverse at this
   stage.
7. Once the drill string is on the bottom, with weight down, spin the Drill string to the
     left. Watch for the loss of weight.
8. When the string has been released, inform Control room to move the rig off
    location. Be careful as the pipe may be able to make contact with the pontoon.
9. Barge Engineer, will skid rig to a safe position.
10. Monitor gas bubble.

WHILE TRIPPING:- PREPARE TO RELEASE DRILL STRING (IF NECESSARY)
1  Rack stand if you have one
2 PA announcement to be made to clear cellar deck of all personnel.
3  Inform ROV operator to recover ROV.
4  Make up top drive to next stand pick up to next tool joint set slips and break the
   connection twice. Space out must be such that when drill pipe is dropped it will
   clear pontoons and cross tubulars.

6  Inform control room that the pipe has been dropped.

ALTERNATIVE METHOD OF DROPPING DRILL STRING.
1.  If time allows, rack back stand of Drill pipe. This can be achieved by use of a set
    of 5 ½” manual elevators.
2. PA announcement to be made to clear cellar deck of all personnel.
3. Inform ROV operator to recover ROV.
4.  Pull tool joint above Rotary table, place a set of elevators around the Drill pipe
   and set down.
5. Break the connection which is sitting in the elevators, back out and rack back the
   stand.
6. Rig up a tugger and snatch block to pull the elevators open.
7. Clear the rig floor except for the tugger operator. With the use of the tugger, open
   the elevators and let the Drill string drop.
8. Inform control room, the Drill string has been released and the Barge engineer
    will move the rig to safe area.
9. Review the Risk specific procedure and temporally refuge area, for any
   improvement opportunities.

        Responsibilities of Personnel During Shallow Gas or Blow-out.

ROV operator.    Advises Driller of well status.
                  Stays on location until all the heavy mud has been pumped.
                    Well, still flowing, advises Driller, abandons vehicle.
                  Then, goes to TR or Safe Breathing area.

Driller.           Raises the alarm, via the Control room.
                      Notifies the Assist Derrickman, to line up 2 pits of Heavy mud.
              Pumps away, the Heavy mud on strokes/PVT.
                Then switches to Sea water on the run.
                  Well still flowing, releases the Drill string on the OIM’s orders.
                      Notifies the Control room, string has been released.
                 Leaves the Drill floor, and switches on the Deluge, on Aft deck.

Assist Driller.    Goes to Emergency muster station.

Derrickman.        In Pit room during the Drilling phase.
               Goes to Emergency Muster station.

Assist Derrickman. In the Pump room during the Drilling phase.
                Changes the pits as directed by the Driller.
               Goes to the TR or Safe Breathing area.

Floormen # 1, # 2 Remains on the Drill floor, Assists the Driller.
               Goes to the TR or Safe Breathing area, switches on Deluge.

Roughneck # 3.   Leaves the Drill floor.
               Goes to TR or Safe Breathing area.

Rig Supt./Pusher. Rig floor assisting the Driller.
                Communicates with Control room.
               Goes to TR or Safe Breathing area.

Company Rep.   Goes to the ROV.
                    Establish comms with the rig floor.
               Goes to the TR or Safe Breathing area.

Bubble Watch.   Goes to the TR or Safe Breathing area. Checks in.
