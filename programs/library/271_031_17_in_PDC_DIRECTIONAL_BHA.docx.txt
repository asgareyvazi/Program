Objectives: 17” Hole Section Directional Drilling
Make up the PDC bit and directional BHA as per BHA list (AKO=1.03º). Bit will be 17” type HCM-605 , IADC CODE M323,(3x16 + 5x13 nozzles) Shallow test mud motor and MWD by Tri Cone Bit as per MWD & DD instruction. Run directional BHA to 983 m take care while running through shoe tracks equipment. No rotate string while pass from the shoe. Pump 50 bbls hi-vis and profile the PDC Bit by 1-2 tons weight, 20 RPM and minimum GPM for 1 hr and then commence drilling as per DD instruction. For the first drill 17 m formation (12 ¼” S.S inside casing) max RPM= 20. Geologist will inform drilling team when getting close to actual top formations. Mud logger should look after top formation while geologist is not on location. Circulate and back ream each stand drilled if necessary. Make sure hole cleaning is good. In case of necessary pump Hi-Vis pill. Keep mud weight uniform 9.4 – 9.5 PPG and viscosity 45 all the time. Run solids controller equips, Disilter, mud cleaner and centrifuge should be run from the beginning of drilling. Control rate while drilling Dariyan & Kazhdumi. (Max ROP=30 m/h). Good communication between drilling team & leaders for the whole operation, special in abnormal operation.
