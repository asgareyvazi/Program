Game plan for drilling 32” Hole section

    •   Pick up 26” conductor pipes from boat, put on cantilever, cut lifting eyes, drift, measure & clean
        thread. Make tally for 26” pipes. Offload bulk , big bag & equipment on boat.
    •  While offloading from boat pick up & make up 10 stands 5 7/8” drill pipe as stand.
    •   Pick up first BHA including:
               23.5 milled tooth bit IADC 115 with 3x 18 nozzles
                  Bit sub with float valve & totco ring.
              32” hole opener with 3x18 nozzles
             3 joints 9.5 DC
            XOS 7 5/8” pin X 6 5/8” BOX
             3 joints 8 ¼” Dcs
            XOS 6 5/8” pin X XT57 Box
             5 7/8” HWDPs
    •   Pass through funnels carefully. Tag bottom with 1-2 tons. Wash down first 5 m with 200 GPM
       with sea water.
    •   Start drilling with 40-60 RPM, 0-2 MT WOB, and 260 GPM. Do not ream the first 10 m.
    •  Pump 40 bbls guar gum pill that has been prepared before.
    •  Cont drill next 20 m with 0-3 ton WOB, RPM 40-60 & 400 GPM.
    •    Drill ahead to about 180 m (The exact depth will be according to casing tally). With 1000 GPM,
       40-60 RPM & 0-5 tons.
    •  Ream the last 10 m once.
    •  Pump 5 m3 hivis pill per each half drilled joint.
    •   After each drilled stand pump 30 m3 hivis pill.
    •   At TD pump sweep hole with 10 m3 guar gum & 10 m3 pre-hydrate bent pills.
    •  Run totco one stand remaining to bottom & on TD.
    •    Fill up the hole with 75 pcf prehydrated gell.(PHG)
    •  POOH to run 26” conductor pipe.


      Good luck,
         Ali
