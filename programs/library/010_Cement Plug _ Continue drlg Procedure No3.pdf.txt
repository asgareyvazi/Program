                             Well SI-009 NEXT PROGRAM No 3

     1) POOH to 640m check for any possible differential sticking indications against sand zone

         interval (600-640m).

     2) POOH to 570m check for any possible differential sticking indications against sand zone

         interval (570-460m).

     3) POOH to 390m check for any possible differential sticking indications against sand zone

         interval (310-390m).

     4)  Continue POOH to surface & L/D 17 -1/2" Bit.

     5)  RIH W/ C.C.Bit to 830m.

     6)  Mix & Pump 100 bbl cement slurry (100pcf) and displace same as balance plug (2 bbls short

          displace).

        Note: Rotate and reciprocate string while cement job to preventing differential stuck.

     7) POOH 10 stand above theoretical (T.O.C @± 730m)

     8)  Flush string, Squeeze 20 bbls of cement with maximum 100psi.

        Note: In case of observe any differential stuck @ depths (640-600m & 570-460m & 390-

        310m), Perform Cement squeeze job against same interval.

     9) POOH to Surface.

     10) RIH 17-1/2" bit & Drilling BHA (Without stabilizer) to TOC & D/O Same to 830m.

        Note: Observe well statically & Dynamically with various GPM for possible any loss before

      D/O last cement plug.

     11) In Case of static well, Continue RIH & D/O Last cement plug F/835m to +/- 950m.

        Note: Monitor well during D/O cement plug for any flow carefully.

     12) Continue wash & Ream from 950m to 1139m

     13) Continue drilling 17-1/2" Hole section to 1170m.
