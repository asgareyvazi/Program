RIG 56 FA, NIDC,AGHAR-9

COMPLETION STRING PROGRAM-(for final approval)


A-Completion Surface Tests:


1- Drift and measure OD, ID and length of all completion accessories.


2- Set 5.950" dummy in 3SV L/nip Baker (BA), connect Cont. line and

    test against dummy to 7000 psi.


3- Set plug 4.312”AF Baker in Top no-go land. / nip and test to 6500 psi,

   leave the plug set.


4- Set plug 4.562"AF in SSD Baker (CMD) and test to 6500 psi, remove

   plug, open and close SSD with shifting tools and test to 6500 psi.


5- Open Expansion Joint fully and test to 6500psi, set shear pins.


6- Set dummy 1 ½” in SPM and test to 6500psi


B-Completion Running Procedure:

 -Remove wear bushing

 -Rig up completion running tools

1- RIH w/Mule shoe, Top no-go…...etc. as per attached tally (with plug

   4.312” AF in top no-go L/nip. and closed SSD) up to SSSV L/nipple

   Note: F/up 100m on top of no-go plug with H-Vis polymer pill (120-

   150sec) and the rest each 15 jts. W / salt water 71 pcf without debris.

    -If complete loss occur, about 300-500m inside TBG. Should not be

    filled, while cross wellhead 2-3BPM

2- Connect Cont. line to 3SV L/nip. And test to 7ooo psi, use band-it

   strap to tight cont.line against TBG.

3- Cont. RIH as per tally, P/up TBG/Hgr, Connect Cont. line to port on

   Hgr.and test to 7000psi.

4- P/up 7” Landing /jt, connect to Hgr.

5- R /up pump unit.

6- Flush and clean T.H.S w/water.

7- Land T/Hgr and just touch TBG. Head Spool.

8- Space out and pull out the string 3 m up (for total stroke 20ft)

9- Pressurise tubing to 500 psi (3 min), 1000 psi (3 min), 1500 psi (3

min),

  2000 psi (3 min),2500 psi (15 min),6500 psi (15 min).





  Note: While pressurizing the tubing some back flow will observe

 through side outlet valves, it should be stopped after a while.

10- Release pressure and over pull the string with 20000 lb.

11- Land TBG/Hgr in T.H.S. and test hanger to 1000psi, tighten tie down

  screws.

12- Test inside tubing to 6500 psi (15 min), close top rams and test

   Hanger to 6500psi

13- Test annulus to 2000 psi (15 min),

14- R/U wire line equipment with BOP and lubricator and test to 6500psi,

  set plug 3.813” in dummy and test to 6500psi, back off Landing Joint.

15- Install two-way 6-5/16” in TBG/Hgr, and test to 6500 psi (15min)

16- N/D BOP

17- Check hanger for any damage and pressure test control line to

   7000psi

18- N/U Bottom master valve and test the ports of sealing as program.

19- N/U Top master valve and solid block and connect to the chock

   manifold, test all to 6500 psi

20- Remove two-way 6-5/16”

21- R/U wire line equipment with BOP and lubricator and test to 6500psi

22- Remove plug 3.813” from dummy, remove dummy 5.950" and flush

  cont. line with hydraulic oil.

23- Open SSD

24- Displace Ann, W/ completion fluid (water PH 11+corrosion inhibitor)

 and inside tubing w/ fresh water.

25- Close SSD

26- Test inside tubing to 6500 and Ann. to 2000psi

27- Put 1600psi pressure inside tubing and remove prong from top no-go

   L/nip, remove plug 4.312” AF

28- Install 5.950" dummy in 3SV L/nip and test to 7000psi.

29- Flow the well through variable choke to reach pure gas.

30- Divert well to the fix choke and record the result on 32/64”& 48/64”

   choke sizes

31- Close the well

32- Remove dummy 5.950”

33- Flush control line with hydraulic oil

34- Install 3SV and test cont. line to 7000 psi
