
13.50” X 17” HOLE SECTION PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
POOH
L/D Diverter sub and Ball Catcher.
Remove Ball Launcher from CMT stand and install Side entry pump in sub, rack stand back in derrick.
P/U BHA. 13.5” PDC Bit, MWD, Reamer.
Surface test MWD at first stand of HWDP.
TIH. Estimated TOC @ 10,220’.
Function BOP’s after midnight.
Wash F/9900’ to TOC (Max flow 600 GPM in CSG)
Boost riser and raise mud weight to 11.9-ppg.
Flush CK & Kill lines with 11.9-ppg mud (Take CLF’s).
Drill out CMT. Start with 80-RPM rotary, 600-GPM maximum flow rates.
Get off bottom torques with and without the pumps prior to drilling CMT. (MONITOR TORQUE)
Do not rotate in one place more than 5-minutes without notifying Company man.
Drill out CMT, Rat Hole and 10’ of new formation to 10,522’.
Pump (100-bbl Hi-Vis. Weighted sweep 14.5-ppg) CBU to above the WH. *Notify mud loggers of sweeps.
Pump up ESD prior to performing LOT/FIT.
Perform FIT 12.7-ppg EMW.
Boost riser while performing FIT.
Circulate up FIT data before continuing.
Drill ahead with above CSG parameters until UR clears shoe.
Verify UR open by pulling up against Shoe.
Drill ahead to interval: TD @ 12014’ MD (12,000’ TVD)
Max SPP or 1200 GPM pump rates.
Maximum rotary = 160-RPM.
Maximum WOB = 15K (Maximum instantaneous ROP= 200 FPH) Start with lightweights monitor surveys for directional considerations.
100 BBL 14.5-ppg Hi-Vis sweeps after drilling first two stands. Then pump sweeps as needed depending on cuttings volume from first sweep.
Back ream each stand and slack off with arms open not rotary to check for ledges.
Surveys every stand if we miss survey drill ahead and get next survey.
Monitor PWD.
Circulate sweep around plus bottoms up at CSG point.
Short trip to shoe and back to bottom.
Circulate bottoms up.
POOH to run CSG.
Run CSG.
