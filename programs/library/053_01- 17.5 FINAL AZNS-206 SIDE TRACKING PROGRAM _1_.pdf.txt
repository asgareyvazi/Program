Open Hole Side-Track With Steerable BHA:

    1.  Set A Cement Plug According Cement Program.

    2. Make Up 17 ½” Rock Bit On 9 5/8" Mud Motor (17 ¼” Sleeve STBZ),

      9.5"B.SUB(3.0˚), 9.5"F.SUB, 9.5" NMDC, 9.5" MWD SUB, 9.5" NMDC,9

      3/4" DC(3JTS), 9 1/2" Jar, X.O.S, 8.5" D.C (3JTS), X.O.S, 5" HWDP(6JTS),

      X.O.S, DPs, Continue RIH While Filling Up String Every 12 Stands To 400m.

    3.  Start Drill Out Cement Plug To Depth Of 405.0m MD And Evaluate The

     Cement Plug Hardness Condition.

    4.  If Cement Is Good Condition And Is Hard Enough, Start Time Drilling.

    5.  If Not, Continue Drill Out Cement To Maximum Depth Of 418.0m MD And

      Evaluate The Cement Hardness Condition. If Found A Good Cement At Any

      Point In This Interval, Start Time Drilling Operation. If Not, Drill Out Cement

     To 467.5m And Pooh To Surface For Repeat No.1 .

    6.  Start To Time Drilling With Directional Driller Supervision With 0.2 To 0.40

     M/Hr Penetration Rate. During Time Drilling: Keep GPM Constant, Don’t

      Rotating String.

    7. After Getting Some More Formation Samples And Side-Tracking Positive

       Indication, The Time Drilling ROP Can Be Increased With Directional Driller

       Instruction.

    8.  Gather Samples From Shaker And Describe Them. Formation Samples Should

     Be Increased By Time To Reach 100%. (Actual Kick Off Point).

    9. When 100% Formation Confirmed By Geologist, Continue Drilling Ahead

     One Joint. Circulate BTM's Up. Pooh To Surface For Change BHA.
