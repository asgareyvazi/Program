                                                   Liner Top Packer Assembly



                                DESIGN AND OPERATION

                This upper Polished Bore Receptacle (PBR) and liner-top packer assembly is one part
                  of a complete liner hanger “Monobore” system.

             The upper PBR is designed to create a well barrier between tubing and annulus with
                the anchor-latch seal assembly which is stabbed into it afterwards. The liner-top packer
                creates an annulus barrier between the liner hanger and the production annulus and
                also resists tensile loading applied to the tubing. The lower seal assembly creates a
                 tubing annulus barrier with the lower PBR on the liner hanger assembly to isolate the
                    liner hanger from production fluid.

             The assembly is run in hole on drill pipe attached to a dedicated liner top packer setting
                    tool. The lower seal assembly stabs through the liner hanger and into the lower PBR.
             The liner top packer bottoms out on top of the liner hanger. As weight is slacked off the
                  setting pins shear and the seal element is displaced outwards to seal off against the
                casing wall. The slip is also forced outwards to bite into the casing wall.

             The setting tool is released by right hand rotation. 6 full turns at the tool release it from
                the left-hand thread in the setting sleeve.
