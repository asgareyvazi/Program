                            Workover Program

The main headlines of this program are as follows:

1.  Skid from NTH-4 and centre on well NTH-1.

2.  Record wellhead and Tubing Ann. Pressures and hook up Kill lines on THS & X-tree

  & P/T same.

3.  Kill and secure well by sea water, N/D X-mass tree, N/U BOP stack and test same.

4.  While circulate cross wellhead POOH completion string and stand back tubing in

    derrick and lay down ESP.

5.  Clean well to 7" liner shoe.

6.  Perform anti scaling / stimulation plan as per IOOC production Engineering dept.

   program.

7.  Observe well.

8. Run new ESP completion string as per completion engineering plan. (The ESP

   completion string detail will be issued in due time).

9. N/D BOP, N/U X-mass tree.

10. Secure well, Release Rig.



Attentions:


•   The detailed program  is available in this document under the heading


    “Well Securing Procedure” & “Workover Program”.



•    All depths are referred to RKB-MSL: 31.08m which should be corrected


     with new RKB-MSL measurement of active rig at time.

 3. Drilling History


   Well NTH-1 is located in Nosrat field, in the southern part of Persian Gulf. The
    original NT-1 well was spud on May 17th , 1981 from the fixed Nosrat platform
   by IOOC to produce Oil from Mishrif member. This well was originally drilled
    directionally by Scanbay rig and reached to total depth 2934m MD. 30", 20, 13
    3/8" & 9 5/8" casing were run to 103.2, 360, 1451& 2934m MD respectively.
    This well was abandoned temporary on May 29th , 1981.

    This well was re-entered and completed by using an ESP on january 1996 in
    Mishrif (Sarvak) reservoir in two horizontal legs. TD of Leg #1 @ 3365m MD &
  TD of Leg #2 @ 3394 m MD & TD of main leg @3590m MD. 7" liner was set at
    interval 2580 (L.Lap)-3157.42m MD(shoe). This well was worked over several
    times primarily to change ESP.

7.  Completion string After Workover:

Completion program will be issued by IOOC completion engineering department in

due time.

8.  Well Operation Procedure:

    1.  Skid rig from well NTH-4 to NTH-1.
    2.  Record & report, air gap, & RKB to THF and MSL.
    3.  Record wellhead and Tubing Ann. Pressures
    4. Hook up lines on Ann. & TBG. P/T same to 3000psi.
    5.  Kill well by seawater & confirm well is static or under loss condition.
      Note: Seawater impose 1153 psi over pressure to the Mishrif Reservoir
   @ 7" liner shoe 3157.42m MD, 2620.9m TVD).
    6.  Close SSSV and confirm  its functioning,  install BPV in tubing hanger.
       Function test apply pressure in control line and function test 3SV.
    7.  While circulate cross well head N/D X-Mass tree, check tubing hanger thread
     N/U BOP and test same to 3000psi.
    8. Make up 4-1/2" Vam top landing joint inside tubing hanger, unset packer by
       applying over pull.
      Note: Keep 4-1/2" spear available on well site in case of tubing hanger
      thread damage.
    9.  While circulate cross wellhead POOH completion string and stand back
       tubing in derrick and lay down ESP.
    10. RIH w/6 " bit and 7" casing scraper and 9 5/8" casing scraper (tandem). Clean
       well to 3157m MD (7" liner shoe).
    11. Perform anti scaling / stimulation plan as per IOOC production Engineering
        dept. program.
    12. Observe well, and make sure of well condition.
    13. Run ESP completion string as per completion engineer instructions.
      Note: The ESP completion string will be issued by IOOC completion
      department in due time.
    14. Secure well. N/D BOP and N/U X-Mass tree and test same.
    15. Secure well & release Rig.
