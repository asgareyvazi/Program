Objectives: preparation for abondon pilot hole

             1. After reach to TD(means 1220 mt) stop drilling.
             2. Circulate 150 bbl Hi-Vis pill to clean hole.
             3. Spud 250 bbl Hi-Vis pill on bottom of hole and displace by sea water.
             4. POOH to surface L/D bit & SLB equipment.
             5. RIH with 6-1/8 cut bit & 6 stand 3-1/2” DP (used bit sub and float valve) as tail and
              follow by 5-1/2” DP to 900 mt.
             6. Hold pre job safety meeting for cementing.
             7. Mix and pump 17 ton cement “A” type and spud as balance plug per SLB cementer
                calculation and operation (displace calculation by SLB cementer).
             8. All time in operation ROV must on sea bed and watch operation on sea bed.
             9. POOH to surface L/D cut bit and rack back 3-1/2” DP. Wait on cement to get hard 12 hrs.
           10. RIH to sea bed by 12-/4 bit and slime BHA for tag cement and confirm cement hardness.
           11. POOH to surface recover TGB and prepare to rig move on new location.
           12. New location coordinate inform on tome.
