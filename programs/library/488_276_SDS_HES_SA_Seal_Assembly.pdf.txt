HES SA Seal Assembly

912SA Seal Assemblies

     he SA Seal Assembly is used to seal off inside
      Polished Bore Receptacles (PBR’s) or liner top Tie-
     back Sleeves. It is a highly reliable, field provenTdesign for life-of-the-well applications suitable for all well
conditions.

The SA Seal Assembly is connected to the bottom of the
production tubing or to the bottom of a packer tailpipe. It
is a single mandrel design with a minimum of six sets of
seals in each direction for long term reliability.
A retainer ring locked to the seal mandrel is fitted
between every three sets of seals to prevent extrusion of
the seals during stabbing. The retainer rings are
manufactured from low friction bearing material.

The Seal Assembly should be run slowly into the PBR or
Tie-Back Sleeve with circulation at as low a rate as
possible. An increase in pumping pressure will indicate
the mule shoe guide has entered the seal bore and
pumping pressure will rapidly increase when the first set
of seals enter the seal bore. Tubing space out may be
carried out at this stage without stabbing the complete
seal stack.

For large seal bores the SA Seal Assembly is fitted with
molded seals to prevent damage to the primary seals.

A variety of premium seals is available to suit all well
conditions. The metal components can be either standard
or CRA material, equivalent to the production tubing
material.
