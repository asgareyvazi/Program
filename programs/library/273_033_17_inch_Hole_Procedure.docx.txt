
17” HOLE SECTION PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
Finish running riser. PU slip joint, bleed down gimble, latch KT ring & test C&K lines 250/10,000 psi.  Test boost line and rigid conduit line 250/3000 psi.  PU landing joint & install storm loops, move rig over location, land and lock.  Test connector with 100K over-pull.  Scope out inner barrel & L/O landing joint.  PU diverter and land-out. Test C&K lines. Check pressures, circulate across stack under the shears (kill to choke) with SBM while rigging up to run drill pipe.
TIH with crossover (4 ½” IF pin X HT-55 box), pup joint, 2 joints DP, and the universal tool.  Open shear rams and slowly lower the drill string until it lands in the EDPHOT.  Set down 2K to 5K on the pin connection; rotate to the right (torque should increase after 4 turns).  EDHOT will begin to turn at about 2,000 ft/lbs.  PU on the drill pipe and observe weight increase on weight indicator.  Pull EDPHOT above the stack, verify space-out and close test rams and upper annular and test connector to 1,000 psi.
Open rams and annular and displace riser to SBM using the boost line with pumps #1 & #4.  Have the standpipe lined up to take u-tubing water from the drill pipe to the cement manifold and overboard.  Review the displacement procedure from MI prior to starting the job.
Test rams and annulars to 250/4,920 psi.
POOH.  Test C&K manifold and surface equipment offline while POOH.  Surface equipment to be tested to 7,500 psi.  Once above stack, test casing for 5 min low/250 psi-30 min high/1,000 against blind shear rams while POOH. L/D  EDPHOT & 16” running tool.  Do not try and set the slips on the EDPHOT (OD is less than 5 inches).  Use the pup joint above to handle and lay down the EDPHOT.
Test top drive valves to 7,500 psi.
PU 10 5/8” bit and 17” BHA and TIH to top of cement (Float collar is at 9,673’).
Slip and Cut drill line.
Wash/drill cement, float collar, and 10’ new formation to 9,850’.
Get static equivalent mud wt from PWD. Circulate bottoms up above BOP’s, Perform shoe test to a max of 12.7 – 12.8  ppg equivalent mw.  Pump up MWD max reading from LOT.  Take LOT down C&K and drill pipe.
Drill 17” hole 12,000’ if LOT allows, otherwise will drill to 10,500’ and set expandable liner.  Do not allow ECD’s to exceed 0.3 ppg from LOT.  Pump 75 bbl sweeps every other stand. Drill interval at approximately 150 ft/hr instantaneous, 75-100 ft/hr real time. Backream each stand to allow hole to clean up.   Flow check every connection.  Take survey every stand.  NOTE:  DO NOT DRILL AT A REDUCED RATE WITHOUT NOTIFYING THE COMPANY MAN.
CBU, POOH to 16” shoe @ 9,818’ working any tight spots.  TIH to bottom and circulate hole clean.
POOH to run casing.
