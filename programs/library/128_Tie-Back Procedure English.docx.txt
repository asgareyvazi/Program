Cont RIH to Get First Seal with Max 500 Psi Press While Record UP& Down String WT.
POOH till Release Press Make Sure Guide of SEAL Nipple Was Inside Tie Back (below 42 cm).
Short CIRC Mud W/MAX 500 PSI Press While M/U CMT Head (pump Down Already Loaded) W/1 MT Pup Joint& Prepare 20 bbl Chemical Wash On Pump Truck.
Stop Circ.
M/U CMT Head and Pup Joint Press Test CMT Line 500/5000 Psi, Check Reverse Circ L/U (Already Connected to Side Outlet Valve.
Pump Chemical Wash.
MIX& Pump 170 bbl CMT Slurry (S. WT:118 PCF)
Drop Pump Down Plug Under Hanger Man Supervision.
Displace by Pump Truck W/112 bbl 103 PCF Mud (Shearing VOL)
Cont Displace BY Pump Truck W/211.5 bbl 103 PCF Mud After Shearing (1.5 bbl Short)
When CMT Reach to Annulus Mud Engineer Should Be Record Any Losses& Inform Co MAN.
RIH and Enter Rest of Seal Nipple (approximately 110 cm) by Applying 140 Kips WT (Total liner WT =132 Kips).
To Release Setting Tools, Rotate 20-27 Round Clockwise.
POOH Until Lose Liner WT (132 Kips) About 2 mt.
L/D CMT Manifold.
POOH 10 STDS and Record Wet STDS No.
Flush String.
POOH 5 Stds More & CBU While Check Mud Return.
If Unable to Release Setting Tools, POOH 2 MT Close Rams & Perform reverse Circ and Shaker Out Cmt.
While Reverse Circ Communicate with Town for Next Plan.
