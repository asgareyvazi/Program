
13-5/8” CSG RUNNING PROCEDURE

## San Jacinto
OFFLINE:
Buckup retrieving tool and wash sub for FMC NBP.
Buckup WEA cmt std and load darts.  (40.9# - Drifted).
Install stop collars on pipe rack.
Clean pit and mix spacer.
Circ wt up to 10.3 ppg.  Short trip to csg shoe.
Have cmt std ready f/btm: single – 15’ pup – TIW- xo – cmt hd – xo – TIW – single.  PU and std back inderrick – loaded with darts (have co man witness).
TIH to BTM.  CIRC hole clean – Thin mud to 19/20 yp.
POOH
Rack Back BHA – download MWD @ Rotary.
Weatherford measure O.D. of CSG jewelry.
Clean pit for spacer.
P/U wash tool & WBRRT  & TIH, pull NBP /use Landing string (WASH PROFILE).
Move CSG equipment & jewelry to rig floor.
Drop rabbit on trip out of hole W/wear bushing.
R/U CSG tool, c/o bails to 15’ 500T.     P/U CSG.
Mix Halco spacer.
P/U guide shoe, 3 JTs Csg,  FC.  Install Turbulizer & Centralizer on each jt w/stop collar.
Baker lock to top of FC.  Max flow rate thru FE is 2-3 BPM.
RIH as per CSG Tally: 141-Jts 13-5/8” total . (16-JTs left out)
FMC Hanger Assembly.
XO – 20’ 5 7/8” PUP – 5 7/8” 47.34# (.075 w) single + XO.
Install OES DP flow back tool.
8-STDs of Rental 27.7# 6 5/8” DP.
75 std of TO 40.9# Landing string.
CMT STD.
CSG is Auto Fill, confirm pipe is filling.
Mud Engineer to document mud losses (Running & Cementing CSG)
TIH w/CSG @ +  3-min/std. Slack off and adjust speed accordingly.  CSG is Auto Fill, confirm Drill Pipe is filling.  *Easy on and off slips.
Land CSG hanger in well head:
Close FC W/5-7 BPM flow rate.
Circulate csg and DP vol – hold PJSM.
R/U Halliburton CMT lines.
Test Lines to 5000-psi.
Pump 00000-bbl spacer, drop dart.
Mix & Pump 000-sx CMT + 10-bbl spacer, Drop Dart #2.
0000 BBL/0000 stks Dart #1 latch up
0000 BBL/0000 stks Dart #1 latch up
6467 stks plug #1 @ FC-Pump 10-12 BPM
7385 stks plug #2 @ FC.
7467 stks includes ½ shoe track volume.
Bump plug with 500-1000-psi.
Set Seal Assembly:
30K DN, Rotate 4-turns to the right.
Drill string will drop 8”.
Break circulation with Halliburton.
Close Pipe rams.
Pressure up to 7000-psi for 2-minutes.
Bleed the pressure off then go to test pressure.
Test upper 2 VBR’s, annulars, & failsafes to 250/2900 psi.
Open rams and go to straight pull on DP.
