                                Workover Program


1. Program Introduction

              This program covers workover of well No.3, of Sarkhoon gas field, I.R.Iran. The well is gas
             producer from Jahrum reservoir right now, producing 1.237 MMSCMD with 1566psi well
             head pressure via 68/64" choke size.

            Problem
            The main problem is the leakages of salt water through C.H.H threads to the open area and
               the flowing of salt water (± 9 litters/min) from 9-5/8" x 13-3/8" annulus which has been
               directed to the mud pit via 1/2" pipe line.

           Main Objective
            The main objectives of this program are to fix the C.H.H leakages, to run 7" casing and to
              stop the  salt water flowing through 9-5/8" x 13-3/8" annulus and then to resume gas
              production from Jahrum reservoir.

             Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of workover
               request no. ٥٨ / ٨٧١ / چ ت ب ,dated: ٥٨٣١ / ٠١ / ٥٢ South Zagros Oil and Gas Company and the
               other available documents.

           The main head lines of this program are as follow:

                1.  Recheck the pressure of well head and annuluses. Investigate for any relation between the
                  annuluses.
                2.  Kill well using hi-PH water.
                3.  Install plug in top No-Go landing nipple and pressure test inside tubing.
                4. Open SSD and displace annulus with hi-PH water. Close SSD. Monitor and investigate
                   for any leakage of 9-5/8" casing.
                5.  Pull out & L/D completion string to top of packer.
                6. Check inside the 9-5/8" casing down to packer depth.
                7.  Set 9-5/8" RTTS @ 30m. Weld on the connection of C.H.H – 13-3/8" casing. Check
               same for any leakage against 300psi of the 9-5/8" x 13-3/8" annulus pressure .
                8.  Replace existed Tubing Head Spool by Casing Head Spool.
                9.  Mill out and retrieve packer and its tail.
              10. Set a magneset plug @ 2470-2670m. Dress same down to 2620m.
              11. Put 20bbl's cement on magneset plug. Dress same down to 2600m.
              12. Run 7" casing to 2600m.
              13. Clean out inside 7" casing down to LDC.
              14. Perform top up cement job into 9-5/8" x 13-3/8" annulus if it would be possible.
              15. Drill out shoe track and clean the hole down to TD.
              16. Run completion string.
              17. Flow the well on proper choke sizes & record the flowing pressures.
              18. Secure well.
              Attentions:

                •  The detailed program is illustrated hereafter in this document under the
                 head of "Workover Program".

14.  Workover Program

   14.1 Sequence of Workover Operation

                1. Move from SA14 to the well site of SA3, R/U and make it ready for operation, Record
                   rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea level. GLE=27m.

                2. The well head spools, valves & P-Seals and x-mas tree must be checked and
                  serviced by well head specialist. Shall it necessary, well head components should be
                  repaired after being L/D on the well site or at AHWAZ workshop.

                3.  Connect killing & flowing lines to the x-mas tree side outlets and connect return line
                    to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 4500 psi pressure.

                4. Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 7" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x 13-3/8"
                annulus and 13-3/8" x 18-5/8" annulus. Release / Bleed off pressures, monitor their
                   build up and investigate for the probable relations if required.

              Attention: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
             while relation investigation.

                5.  Bullhead the well by pumping Hi-PH water which its full column imposes 583psi over
                 pressure to the reservoir @ 9-5/8" casing shoe (2703m). Shall could not able to
                  bullhead, lubricate the well and make sure about well condition.

              Attention: Monitor the pressures of annuluses all the time.

                6.  Rig up wireline equipment; test same and lubricators to max 4500psi pressure. Set
                "XD-4.562" top no-go plug @ 2700m. Test same and inside tubing to max 4500psi.
             Open upper SSD @ 2681m. Displace the well by hi-PH water (its full column has
                 553psi over pressure against the reservoir pressure @ packer depth @ 2691m.

                7.  Close SSD, test same to 4500psf. Monitor 7" x 9-5/8" annulus for any flow. Install
               Back Pressure Valve inside F5-B tubing hanger and test same to 4500psi. Shall it
                 impossible to set BPV, install dummy and plug into 3SV landing nipple and test same
                    to 4500psi.

                8.  L/D well head to top of Tubing Head Spool. N/U 13-5/8"-5000psi BOP's stack and
             made up its related lines. Connect to choke manifold. Test them all as per NIOC
                    policy.

                9.  Retrieve BPV. Prepare to pull out completion string. Un-latch anchor seal. Pull out
               and L/D completion string. It should be done while circulating water cross the well
               head and monitoring the mud volume all the time.

              10. RIH with 8-1/2" E.mill to top of packer @ 2691m. Pull out of hole, run back with
                   9-5/8" casing scraper. Scrap inside 9-5/8" casing from surface down 50m.


              11. Run in hole with 9-5/8" RTTS and a blind end tail of 25klb weight. Set RTTS @ 30m.
                Release setting tools. Test RTTS to 500psi. Pull out and L/D setting tools.

    stack. Install 11" x 11" – 5000psi DSA pack off, 11" x 11" – 5000 Tubing Head Spool
   and related valves. Test them all to 4500psi pressure. N/U BOP's stack and test
   same as NIOC policy.

21. P/U 6-1/8" Rock Bit. RIH to top of LDC. Drill out LDC, cement, shoe and the cement
   and magneset plugs under the shoe. RIH down to Bottom of open hole section.
    Circulate, clean inside the well. POOH, L/D bit.

   maximum 1500psi injecting pressure. Shall it proceed in successful manner; perform
    top up cement job as per cementing program which will be offered in due time.
