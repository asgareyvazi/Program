           17” Hole Section Drilling Procedure
                San Jacinto Prospect
                      April 11, 2004


 1. When done latching up, test casing and chart while rigging down
    •  Lo: 250 psi for 5 min; Hi:2800psi for 30 min
    •  Vol required should be approximately 9 bbls

 2. RD and PU 17” directional assembly
    •  See BHI assembly for components
    • PU jars and 2 jts heavy weight
    • PU 3 joints of .415 wall 29.44 lb 5 7/8” DP and place immediately above HWDP
    •  Offline: LD 1 double of drill collar (2 are currently racked back) while drilling
    •  Test MWD on HW
    • NBP (nominal bore protector) was pre-installed

3. RIH to wellhead and space out .415 wall DP across stack

4. Test BOPs against the 20”
    •   Test stack to 2875 psi
    •   Test choke manifold to 5000 psi

5. Drill out and displace to synthetic base mud
    • TIH to tag top of cement (expected at 11,495MD)
    •  Hole PJSM for displacing to synthetic base per MI procedure
    •  Ensure that we have zero discharge rigged up for cuttings containing cement
    • Mud logger will sample cuttings looking for 100% shale
    •  Rig will remain on zero discharge until notified by CoRep
    •  Start displacement and have coming up backside before starting to drill cement
    •  Begin drilling out cement and float equipment while displacement coming up backside
    •  Start drilling out with 600 – 800 gpm and 40 rpms
    •  Continue drilling out per Company Rep/BHI
    • Once through shoe, clean out rathole and drill 10’ new formation (11,635 MD)

6. Circ BU to wellhead at 1400 gpm

7. Perform LOT
    • Do not begin FIT until displacement is completed
    • Maximum is 11.0 ppg; minimum is 10.7 ppg
    •  Line up to pump down backside and top drive simultaneously
    • Have Halliburton perform LOT at ¼” bpm
    • Once LOT is complete, inform Anadrill and pump up ESD

8. Drill ahead to 13,788 (interval TD)
    • KOP at 12,350 building 2.5/100 to an angle of 30 degrees at 116 AZ
    •  Drill ahead as per BHI / CoRep
    • Flow rates 1200 – 1400 gpm; rotate 60 – 80 rpm
    •  1 pump on riser boost continuously
    •  Secondary sand objective is expected at 13,629TVD 13,705MD
    •  Control drill from 13,400 MD to casing point
    •  At TD; circ 75 bbl lo vis followed by 200 bbl hi vis sweep

9. Circ 2 BU at 1400 gpm – max flow for BHI motor

10. Short trip to 20” shoe
    •   Pull couple stands then pump slug and pull to shoe
    •   Service rig at shoe
    •  RIH to bottom – wash and ream as required

11. CBU and POOH – 2 cases
    •    If secondary sand is wet, CBU then spot 20 bbl 16 lb mud on bottom, POOH
    •    If logging is required, then just CBU and POOH

12. If logging is required, RU for logging

13.  If logging is not required, PU wellhead NBP retrieving tool and TIH
    •   Pull NBP from wellhead per FMC procedure
    •  POOH and prepare to run 13 5/8” casing
