2  Introduction

This document covers Re-Entry#1 program of well D-127P in Dorood Field.


Important Note:
      This program has been written with respect to the letter # 91/ ز پ پ/ 2440,
       Dated: 1391/10/18 (PPZ Reservoir Eng. Department) and geological forecast
     # 91/ ز پ پ/ 2448 Dated: 1391/11/04 (PPZ Geology Eng. Department) &
       available data from this wells. .

3  Main Objective

The main objective is to open window in 9-5/8" casing to Re-entry well and directional
drilling to planed total depth of ±4184 m, MD to produce oil from Yamama reservoir.

  6  Program Main Head lines

                1. Move & centre the rig to well D-127P.
                2.  Kill the well, make sure well is static or under loss condition.
                3.  Secure well, N/D X-mass tree.
                4. N/U BOP stack and test it.
                5. POOH completion string.
                6.  Clean well to @ 2950m.
                7.  Set CMT plug with 2-7/8" DP open end @ 2950m.
                8. RIH 8-1/2" Bit, tag top of CMT, dress cement to 7" liner lap.
                9.  R.I.H 9-5/8" permanent hydraulic Whipstock, Set and test same.
              10. Open window @ 2820m.
              11. Dir drilling 8-1/2" hole down to 7" liner point @ 3465m.
              12. Run 7” liner & cement same.
              13. Dir drilling 6-1/8” hole to TD @ 4184m.
              14. Take full set log by TLC.
              15. Set Magneset plug with 2-7/8" DP open end @ 3850m.
              16. Dress Magneset plug with 6-1/8" bit to LP & 5" liner point @ 3684m.
              17. Run 5” liner & cement same.
              18. Take (CBL/VDL/CCL/GR) log with TLC inside 7" & 5" liner.
              19. Clean well with 4-1/8" E.Mill to TD @ 4184m.
              20. Run 2-7/8" Slotted liner as per completion program.
              21. Run completion string.
              22. Secure well, N/D BOP, N/U X-mass tree.
              23. RIH coil TBG, Acidize and flow the well.
              24. Secure the well, Release the Rig.

             “Well Securing Procedure” & “Re-entry#1 program”.

               therefore a correction depth should be considered for the new RKB-
              MSL.

                 start of Operation.

      20-28 Feb. 2011
       The purpose of work over was to shut the water entry points by setting a plug the existing
        perforated intervals in 4.5" LNR and perforate a new interval in Upper Yamama. So a
      MPBT Plug was set at 3505.5mdd (-3380.1) and up to 3502 mdd (-3378) was cemented
       and the following intervals were perforated in Yamama formation.

     On 11 March 2011, after perforating the above intervals (3456-3497mdd(-3332.73/-
       3373.25)) run coiled tubing in the hole and tag cement at 3461.1 mdd (Schlumberger
       tagged cement at 3500mdd before perforation). Acid stimulation was performed in two
        stages. At stage One, 1000 gallons of 15% acid+additives was injected at rate of 2 bph, and
       pressure between 120-200psi. In second stage of stimulations resumed with 6000 gallons
        of 15% acid at rate of 4.5 BPH with injecting pressure 200 psi.

     On 12 March 2011, for bringing the well in started to run CT in the hole while injecting
      4200 gal Nitogene from 4000 to 9500ft at rate of 3-8gpm and max pressure 3200 psi.
       Started to receive diesel, oil and water and the WHP increased to 450 psi, which decreased
        to 200 at the end of injection. The last sample received was gas. Rig down coiled tubing,
       while waiting for receive Nitrogen to repeat the Nitro lift the WHP was 100 psi and
       checked TD at 3462.4m.

     On 20 March 2011, in another try to bring in the well, CT inject 6500 gal Nitrogen from
      5000 to 11000 ft in 1000 ft intervals. This operation lasted 22 hrs non-stop. The major
        fluid received was water.
        After performing the stimulation job, the flowing pressure was declined to 200 psi which
         final surface-sample captured was showed Gas fluid. Finally, 2500ft of C.T. string was
       twisted off and fallen down into the well. Therefore, the well was not flowed.

14 Well Securing Procedure

     1.   Skid and spud the rig in to well D-127P.

     2.   Centre derrick, Record & report RKB to THF and GLE.
     3.   Hook up kill lines to Ann. & TBG. Sides and test to 2500 psi.

     4.   Record wellhead and Tubing Ann. Pressures (If possible, bleed off pressure).

     5.   Rig up wire line, check 3SV & make sure 3SV is installed in landing nipple; check

      & test control line.

     6.   Apply pressure in control line to open 3SV.

     7.    Bull head the well with 64.5 PCF sea water by pumping down through Annulus &
        TBG, Observe well.

        Note:
               64.5 PCF will impose ±1643 psi over pressure to the Yamama

               reservoir @ top of the perforations (3456m, MD / 3358m, TVD)
               Adjust MW with respect to the hole condition.
              Pump Hi vis pill during bull head the well due to sweep gas & oil in to
               the formation.
              Weight up material (Salt & Limestone) should be ready on board for
               possible need to kill the well.

     8.    Circulate Through Ann. & get return from TBG, balance MW and check flow.

     9.    Circulate cross well head & make sure the well is static, or under loss condition.

    10.  Close 3SV & test same.

    11.  Install B.P.V in TBG hanger. (Camerom "MC-2" snapring 11" x 5" B.P.V)

    12.  Remove kill line from X-mass tree. Do not disconnect kill line from Ann until the

       BOP stack is installed. Keep the well full from Annulus side.
    13.  While circulate cross well head, N/D X-mass tree, Check tubing hanger thread.

        Note:
               Circulate cross well head to keep well full while N/D, N/U and trip.
    14.  N/U BOP stack, pressure test it (do step13 in daylight) & remove BPV.
