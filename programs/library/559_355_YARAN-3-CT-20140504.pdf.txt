1.6  Challenge and Strategy


Because  of the  peculiar formation and  well  conditions, the Challenges  of Acidizing
Stimulation are presented as following:
   •  Micro-fractures and wormholes are the mainly oil storage space in the carbonate
       reservoir. The reservoir is strong heterogeneity along the horizontal and vertical
       direction and how to stimulate the zone with lower permeability is a huge challenge.
      compsite diverting technologies will be tempered

   •  Open hole section pose RIH CT  a huge challenge.

   •   If CT cannot RIH target interval, bullhead to around 1000m long horizontal section
     make acid placement and diversion be a real challenge.The section of pump rates
     and volumes required to achieve a reasonable penetration in these thick treatment
       intervals is a real challenge. Low-friction and retarded acid required to reduce well
     head pressure and protect string and other equipment and the low corrosion for acid
       to iron are real challenges.



2. Acid Fluid and Other Materials Preparation

2.1   Acidizing Design Principle


Following measures can be adopted  in order to enhance the success rate, efficiency of
acidizing and achieve a good effect on increasing productivity.

❖    Matrix acidizing can satisfy the requirement, 15% HCl can be selected.

❖    Select appropriate acid formula. The formula contains corrosion inhibitor, high efficient
      iron control agent, good multi-function surfactant and good anti-sludge agent.

❖      If CT can reach the target zone,25gal/ft acid will be recommended.

❖      If CT can not RIH to target interval, bullheading around 1000m length horizontal section
       will be recommmended, self diverting visco-elastic surfactant (SDVES) will be used as a
      diverting agent in order to make acid distribution evenly.

4. Operation Procedures

4.1  General Requirements

    1) Provide a coordinator to integrate  all activities such as CT operation, acidizing and

       engineering.

    2) The well site access road must be suitable for acid stimulation treatment equipment

       entry and exit.

    3) The well site must be level and smooth, can stand with the weight of all equipment.

    4) Check all equipment on arrival at the wellsite to make sure no damage/missing items.

     Pay special attention to the crossovers to ensure the threads are compatible.

    5) Pay more attention to and check the ID and crossover of the completion string to make

      sure that CTU RIH and POOH freely and safely, if CT operation is required.

    6)  All the tubing should be cleaned.

    7) Prepare enough fresh water in well site for mixing acid fluid and brine.

    8) Prepare adequate chemicals for mixing of brine and acid fluid.

4.2  Preparation work before operation

    1)  Move all the equipments and accessories to wellsite and set them in place according to

       attached equipments layout.

    2) Hold safety meeting with all personnel involved.

    3) Cordon off all high-pressure area with signal tape and forbid anyone but operators in

       high-pressure area.

    4) Check that all wellhead valves are closed.

    5) Rig up all equipments with accessories to X-mas tree according to attached equipments

       layout. All the high pressure equipments should connected isolation valve. Connect one

     pump truck to THS wing valve for balance pressure.

    6) Pressure test injection line up to 5000psi for 10minutes to make sure it is qualified; bleed

         off directly to waster pit.

    7) Pressure test balance pressure line up to 3000psi; bleed off directly to waster pit.

    8) Carry out acid job as below.

7. Job Risk Analysis

7.1  Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)

1) Fresh water and bottled neutralizer shall be available at the worksite.

2)  In the event of acid contact with the eyes, immediately flushes eyes gently with fresh water

   and consult a doctor as soon as practicable.

3) Acid contacts the skin, wash the area thoroughly with fresh water.

4) Remove clothing contaminated with acid solutions or additive solutions.

5) This clothing should not be re-worn until thoroughly washed.

7.2   High pressure

1)  Enclose the high pressure area with warning line;

2)  Plot the dangerous area with warning plate;

3) Keep all personnel but operators far away from high pressure area while operation;

4) Use safety rope between the pipe line connections in case of disconnection;

5) The pump should be shut down immediately once the pipe line bursts.

7.3   H2S

•  Portable H2S detector must be inspected annually.

•   All Air respirators must be inspected function.

•  Everyone on location should use H2S detector.

•  For the places where H2S may concentrate must have ventilation equipments.

•  Wind-vane must be obvious and showed to all personnel before entering well site.

  All personnel must be trained of H2S contingency plan and the use of all related equipments.

8.3   Requirements for safe operation

Acidizing operations shall comply with the following:

   •  A dedicated line shall be used to run the pumping unit to the well. The transfer and

      pumping lines shall be properly secured with snubbing lines.


────────────────────────────────────────────
[ صفحه 12 از 14 ]
────────────────────────────────────────────
   •   Under no circumstances hammers on leaking connections whilst pumping acid. The

         lines shall be pressure tested prior to starting the acid job.

   •  A pressure relief valve shall be installed on the pumping unit with the vent directed

      away from all personnel. The vent area should be flagged off. A non-return valve shall

      be placed in the pumping line as close as possible to the well.

   •  Be aware of the  risk of reaction gases evolved during the wash (primarily CO2)

       unloading the well as they near surface.

   •  Due to the risk of contamination and splashing personnel, live acid shall not be returned

         into open top fluid tanks. All acid returned from the well shall be regarded as live acid.

   •   Extreme care must be taken during high pressure pumping while conducing acid

        stimulation treatments as unexpected leaks on surface lines can develop which could

        release the HCl acid as a fine spray. HCl acid can vaporize in air to form a noticeable

       cloud if released to the atmosphere. If the vapor cloud is concentrated enough it can be

         toxic until sufficiently dispersed.

8.4   Job planning

A.  Acidizing operations should not proceed in the dark unless there is adequate lighting

     available.

B.  The acids used, hydrochloric acid (HCl) is a toxic and dangerous chemical. Refer to the

   MSDS for details of handling, storage and disposal precautions.

C.  Only essential personnel shall be allowed in the vicinity of the acid handling equipment.

    Every effort shall be made, particularly thorough pre-job planning, to ensure that minimum

     intervention by personnel is required during the acidizing.

D.  Safety barriers shall be used as appropriate to isolate the areas of potential hazard.

E.  Fresh water shall be available around the pump truck. A safety shower or alternative option

     for washing off contamination should be available nearby.

F.  Coiled tubing unit and all pumps must be operated by well trained operators, and under the

    supervisor’s monitor.
