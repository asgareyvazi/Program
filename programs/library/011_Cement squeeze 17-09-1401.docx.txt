Well SI-009 NEXT PROGRAM
Squeeze cementing:
RIH with center cut bit to bottom.
Circulate to balance mud weight @±100 pcf to overcome well flowing.
Pump fresh mud 100 pcf inside DP (1.5 times  drillstring cap).
Pull back to 1124m (±15 off bottom) and static check for 0.5 hrs.
Circulated and bottom's up and check bottom's up.
If observed cut mud weight in return then increase mud weight to 101 pcf and static flow check. Ensure well is static.
If no cut mud weight in return, POOH to 950m.
Spot 100 bbl cement slurry (100pcf).
POOH 7 stand above theoretical T.O.C @± 852m
Wait on cement as per program (or surface sample get hard).
POOH into shoe and OBS the well. Ensure well is static. (Check dynamic loss at different GPM). static check for 0.5hr.
RIH to 300 and spot 260bbl cement (sw:100 pcf) as per program.
POOH to theoretical T.O.C @± 50m.
Inject 100 bbl of cement to 20" casing shoe depth.
WOC to get hard.
Run back and tag top of cement.
check dynamic losses at different GPM. If observed any losses repeat for second cement squeeze job.
POOH to Surface.
RIH 17-1/2" bit & clean out BHA.
