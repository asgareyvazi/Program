1.  Introduction

This program covers the information and procedures required for workover operation on

well F-08-00 in Foroozan Field - Persian Gulf - IRAN.


Problem

  The well is shut down due to high water cut.


Main Objective:

  The main objective of workover on this well is to cut down the horizontal drain in

  Arab buy setting CMT plug up to 29m above 5” shoe, & find new potentials in Hith

  and Manifa


Location map:

                             371315m   E

20/12/1392 (IOOC Production, Workover & Completion Eng. Department) & most

available data from this well. Any amendment program will be issued upon receipt of

new data from IOOC.

                                Well F-08-00
                            Workover Program


The main headlines of this program are as follows:

1. Move from F13 platform to F08 and centre on well F8-00.


2.  Record & report RKB to THF & MSL of new well.


3. R/U Kill lines on THS & X-tree & P/T same.


4.  Kill and secure well, N/D X-mass tree, N/U and test BOP.


5. POOH and L/D Dual completion string.


6. RIH W/ 8-1/2" bit to 1318.8mMD (Top of 7” liner lap) Circ. and clean well.

   POOH.


7. RIH W/ 6-1/8" bit to 2070mMD (Top of 5” liner lap) Circ. and clean well.

   POOH.


8. RIH W/ 4-1/8" bit to 3024mMD (5” Shoe) Circ. and clean well. POOH.


9. RIH OE 2-7/8” to 3024m, MD (5” Shoe). Set 12bbls cement slurry as Balance

    plug. POOH to +/- 2700m, MD. Flush DPs. Squeeze cement to 50m above 5”

    shoe. POOH to surf.


10. RIH W/ 4-1/8” ECONO. MILL & D/O CMT to 2995m, MD (29m above 5”

    shoe). Test CMT plug.


11. RIH 9 5/8” scraper to scrape RTTS depths interval.POOH.


12. RIH  tandem  scarper  7” &  5”  to  scrape  perforation  and  packer  depths

   interval.POOH.


13. Set 9-5/8" RTTS, N/D BOP, Change THS, N/U BOP, test same.


14. R/U & Ran CBL/VDL/GR to +/-2800M (48 deg). POOH & L/D Logging tools.


15. In case of good cement quality behind of 5” liner in Manifa formation, Perforate

   Manifa & Hith formations, otherwise Perforate just Hith formation by TCP.


16. Run completion string as per program.

12. Well Operation Procedure:

1.  Skid out cantilver & center on well F-08-00.


2.  Record & report RKB to MSL and THF of new well.


3. Hook up kill and flow lines to Ann & TBG. P/T same.


4.  Record wellhead pressures (If any, bleed off pressure).


5. Pump and squeeze 50 bbls Hi-vis pill followed by 73 pcf SW into 3-1/2" and 2-

   7/8" tubings and observe well.


Note

     73 PCF SW will impose ±245 psi over pressure to the Arab Reservoir @

      5” liner shoe (2835mTVD / 3021mMD).


     Pump Hi Vis pill ahead of 73 PCF brine in order to sweep and squeeze

      remained oil and gas in to formation.


     Adjust MW with respect to the hole condition.


     Weight up material (Salt, Limestone & Barite) should be available on

      board to be applied in balancing mud weight.


      Important Notice:


6.  Circulate through long string & get return from short string, balance 73 pcf SW

   and flow check, Make sure well is static, or under loss condition.


Note:

      If could not squeeze, 73pcf brine inside well, 98pcf mud should be

       displaced in well through 2-7/8" long string.


     98 PCF Mud will impose ±240 psi over pressure to the Arab Reservoir @

         tail of completion long string (1991mMD / 1991mTVD).


     Circulate across well head to fill the well during operation.
