                      9 5/8" CASING BACK-OFF PROCEDURES


1    Make up 9 5/8" Casing Cutting Assembly. Test the cutter at surface.
     Run in the hole and make the cut in the 9 5/8" approximately 5 feet below the collar in first
        joint above the joint that is required to be backed off.

      8 1/4" Pipe Cutter Assembly

      8 1/2" Taper Mill                      4 1/2" Reg Pin
      8 1/4" Pipe Cutter                      4 1/2" IF Box x 4 1/2" Reg Box
       Dressed with 10 3/4" Opening Knives
       5" Drill Pipe

2    Run the Spear Assembly and recover the cut section of 9 5/8" Casing.

      9 5/8" Spear Assembly

      9 5/8" Pack off Tool                    4 1/2" IF Box x Bullnose
      9 5/8" Casing Spear                    4 1/2" IF Pin x 6 5/8" Reg Box
      c/w 8.681" Nominal Catch Grapple
       8" Spear Stop Mandrel                  6 5/8" Reg Pin x Box
      c/w 10 3/4" OD Stop Plate
       8" Bumper Sub                        6 5/8" Reg Pin x Box
      3 x 8" OD Drill Collars                 6 5/8" Reg Pin x Box


3    Run the Casing Dressing Assembly and dress / mill off the casing top.

      Casing Dressing Assembly

      10 3/4" OD Casing Dressing Mill         6 5/8" Reg Pin
      c/w 8 1/2" OD Pilot Mill
      X-Over                              6 5/8" Reg Box x Box
      3 x 8" OD Drill Collars                 6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
      X-Over                              6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       5" Drill Pipe to surface

       Mill off 4" to 6" then circulate bottoms up and pull out of the hole.

                      9 5/8" CASING BACK-OFF PROCEDURES


4    Make up the 8" OD Casing Back-Off Assembly.

      Casing Back-Off Assembly

       8" OD Bullnose Sub                    6 5/8" Reg Box
      9 5/8" Casing Bottom Anchor Section      6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       8" OD Lubricated Slack Joint            6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       8" OD Torque Generator Section          6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
      8” OD Clutch Unit                     6 5/8” Reg Box x Pin
      9 5/8" Casing Top Anchor Section        6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
      Swivel Joint with landing shoulder        6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       8" OD Slack Joint                     6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
      By-Pass Valve                        6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       Circulating sub                        6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       Crossover sub                         6 5/8" Reg Pin x 4 1/2" IF Box
       5" OD Drill pipe to surface


5.    Run in the hole with the Casing Back-off Assembly.

6     Record the string parameters before entering into the casing stump.
      Lower the assembly into the casing until the landing shoulder is resting on the top of the
       stump. Continue to slack off until the upper slack joint has closed and then apply 30,000lb of
       weight on the stump.

7      Pick up the string to put the upper slack joint in mid-stroke leaving the weight of the back-off
      assembly (11,500 lbs) resting on the landing shoulder.

     Make Sure Blocks Are Unlocked for Work String to Be Free to Rotate While Suspended
       in the Elevators. This is a precautionary measure as no rotation should be seen at surface
      due to the Swivel Sub in the tool.

8       Initiate the back-off sequence by bringing in the pumps up to 150-200 Gpm. This will close the
       by-pass valve and allow pressure to be exerted on the anchor sections and torque generator. As
       the pressure increases (Max 5000 psi) breakout of the connection should begin, this may be
       noticed by a fall off in pressure as the connection breaks. At the end of one cycle (1/2 a turn)
       the pressure will increase indicating the end of the tool stroke. Hold this pressure for 5 minutes.

9     Bleed off the pressure to zero. As this is done the tool will reset itself in preparation for the
       next torque cycle.

                      9 5/8" CASING BACK-OFF PROCEDURES


10     Restart the pump and increase the pressure again to activate the torque generator. The pressure
      peak on this cycle should be less than on the previous cycle, however pressure up to 5000 psi
        regardless, as less force will be required to unscrew the connection after the initial breakout.
       Repeat the cycling procedure until eight cycles have been achieved. At this point four full turns
       should have been applied to the casing connection.

11    Drop the circulating sub ball and open the circulating sub with 1500 psi. POOH.

12    Run back in the hole with a standard 9 5/8" Spear Assembly, engage the casing stump and
       complete the back-off. Recover the casing stub to surface.

      9 5/8" Standard casing spear             6 5/8" Reg Box x 4 1/2" IF Pin.
      c/w grapple to suit casing weight
       8" OD Stop Mandrel c/w 10 3/4" Stop     6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       8" OD Bumper Sub                    6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
      3 x 8" OD Drill Collars                 6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       Crossover sub                         6 5/8" Reg Pin x 4 1/2 IF Box
       5" OD S-135 Drill Pipe to surface


13.   Make up new casing string with Drill Quip re-instatement package.
