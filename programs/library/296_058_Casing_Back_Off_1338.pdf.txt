          Procedures for Cutting, Recovery and Backing Off 13 3/8" Casing


1)    Make up casing cutting assembly, run in the hole and cut the 13 3/8" Casing.
     Make the cut 5 feet below the collar in first joint above the one that it is required to
      back off from.

             Assembly:

            12 1/4" Taper Mill               6 5/8" Reg Pin
            11 3/4" ID Pipe Cutter            6 5/8" Reg Box x Box
             X-over                        6 5/8" Reg Pin x 4 1/2" IF Box
             5" Drill Pipe

2)     Recover cut section and hanger with the following assembly.

      Assembly:

            13 3/8" HD Casing Spear          6 5/8" Reg Box x 4 1/2" If Pin
             8" OD Stop Mandrel             6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
            c/w 14 3/4" Stop Plate
             8" OD Bumper Sub              6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
             Crossover                      6 5/8" Reg Pin x 4 1/2" If Box
             5" Hevi-Wate Drill Pipe

       Note: If the hanger is stuck a Jar and Accelerator may be required to free the hanger.
         If so use the following assembly.

      Assembly:

            13 3/8" HD Casing Spear          6 5/8" Reg Box x 4 1/2" If Pin
             8" OD Stop Mandrel             6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
            c/w 14 3/4" Stop Plate
             8" OD Bumper Sub              6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
             8" OD Fishing Jar               6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
            2 x 8" OD Drill Collars           6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
             8" OD Accelerator               6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
             Crossover                      6 5/8" Reg Pin x 4 1/2" If Box
             5" Hevi-Wate Drill Pipe

      Swivel Joint with 13 3/8" OD Shoulder cap 6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       8" OD Slack Joint                     6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
      By-Pass Valve                        6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       Circulating sub                        6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       Crossover sub                         6 5/8" Reg Pin x 4 1/2" IF Box

6)    Run in the hole with the back-off assembly to back-off at the first casing collar below
       the top of the stump. Once the back-off assembly has been lowered into the casing
       continue slacking off until the landing shoulder is resting on the top of the stump, the
       upper slack joint has closed and 30,000 lbs of weight is resting on the stump.

7)     Pick up the string to put the upper slack joint in mid-stroke leaving the weight of the
       back-off assembly (11,500 lbs) resting on the landing shoulder.

          Procedures for Cutting, Recovery and Backing Off 13 3/8" Casing


 Make Sure Blocks Are Unlocked for Work String to Be Free to Rotate While
 Suspended in the Elevators. This is a precautionary measure, as no rotation should be
  seen at surface due to the Swivel Sub in the tool.

8)      Initiate the back-off sequence by bringing in the pumps up to 150-200 Gpm. This will
       close the by-pass valve and allow pressure to be exerted on the anchor sections and
       torque generator. As the pressure increases (Max. 5000 psi) breakout of the
       connection should begin, this may be noticed by a fall off in pressure as the
       connection breaks. At the end of one cycle (1/2 a turn) the pressure will increase
      (Max. 5000 psi) indicating the end of the tool stroke. Hold 5000 psi for 5 minutes.

9)     Bleed off the pressure to zero. As this is done the tool will reset itself in preparation
        for the next torque cycle.

10)    Restart the pump and increase the pressure again to activate the torque generator. The
       pressure peak on this cycle may be less than on the previous cycle as less force will be
       required to unscrew the connection after the initial breakout, however continue to
       pressure to 5000 psi regardless to complete the cycle.

11)    Repeat this procedure until eight cycles have been completed. At this point four full
       turns should have been applied to the casing connection.

12)   Drop the circulating sub ball and shear open the circulating sub with 1500 psi. Pull
       out of the hole.

13)   Run back in the hole with the 13 3/8" Spear assembly, engage the casing stump and
       complete the back-off by applying left hand rotation. There should be very little
       torque required to complete the back off but caution should be exercised. Recover the
       casing to surface.

      13 3/8" Casing Spear                   6 5/8" Reg Box x 4 1/2" IF Pin
      c/w 12.555" Nominal Catch Grapple
       8" OD Stop Mandrel c/w 14 3/4" Stop     6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
       8" OD Bumper Sub                    6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
      3 x 8" Drill Collars                     6 5/8" Reg Box x Pin
      X-Over                              6 5/8" Reg Pin x 4 1/2" IF Box
       5" Drill Pipe to surface
