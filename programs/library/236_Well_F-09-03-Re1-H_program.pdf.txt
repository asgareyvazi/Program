1.   Introduction

This program covers the information and procedures required for workover operation and

Re-Entry plan for well F09-03 in Foroozan Field-Persian Gulf-IRAN.


Problem

  The well was shut in since 1379 due to high water cut.


Main Objective:

  The main purpose of drilling the F-09-03-Re1H well is to effectively produce Oil from

  Yamama (L4 & L2) reservoir in the north west flank of the F-3 structure in the

   Foroozan oil field by setting 7" liner at L.P., drilling about 800mMD horizontal re-

   entry leg through Yamama (L4 to L2) reservoir, and using perforated pipes in 6" open

   hole for completion of the well.

The main headlines of this program are as follow:

1.   Move from F8-00 to F9 platform and centre on well F9-03.

2.   Record & report RKB to THF & MSL of new well.

3.   R/U Kill lines on THS & X-tree & P/T same, meanwhile make 83pcf mud.

4.   N/D X-mass tree, N/U and test BOP.
5.   RIH w/ 8-1/2" bit to top of 1st cement plug at +/-968m. Displace hole w/ 82pcf

     mud.
6.   D/O 1st cement plug to 1200m.
7.   RIH to top of 2nd cement plug at +/-2406m, D/O same to 2489m.

8.   RIH to +/- 2497m (Top of fish).

9.  POOH to surface, RIH OE Dps to +/- 2497m (Top of fish).

10.  Set 12bbls balance CMT plug & D/O same to 2491m.

11.  RIH and set 9-5/8" Whipstock @ 2485m (Window @ Dair).

12.   Dir. drilling 8-1/2” hole to LP & 7” liner point @±3546m.

13.  Run 7” liner, hang & cement same.

14.  Set 9-5/8" RTTS, N/D BOP, Change tubing head spool, N/U BOP, test same.

15.   Dir. drilling 6” hole to TD & Perforated liner point @±4341.5mMD.

16.  Displace OH with non-solid mud.

17.  Run perforated liner to TD.

18.  Run completion string.

19.  Secure well, N/D BOP, N/U X-mass tree.

20.  RIH coil TBG, Stimulate & flow well.

21.  Secure well, Release Rig.


Attentions:
    All depths are referred to RKB-MSL: 23.4m which should be corrected

     with new RKB-MSL measurement of active rig at time.

3. Drilling History

   The well was spudded on 22nd Apr 1985 & was drilled as a deviated well to 2786
  m in Shuaiba formation (K.O.P: 297 m). Perforation was performed in 2694-2705
  m in L.shuaiba. The well was completed with using dual string. The well was
    released on the N/A.

  The 36" section 26” was drilled with sea water to 107.5 m, 26” hole was
    enlarged to 36” hole. 30 casing was run and cemented at 106m without any major
   problem.

  The 26" section was drilled to the depth of 274m with sea water mud. 20
    casing was run and cemented at 266m without any major problem.

  The 17 1/2" section was drilled to 276 m. formation leak off  test was
   performed, pressure drop from 110 to 80 psi in 5 min. Drilling was continued to
   297 m & Directional drilling was performed w/ DYN drill to 903 m. (K.O.P: 297
   m). 13 3/8” casing was run and cemented at 902 m.

  The 12 1/4" section was directional drilled to 2786 m (TD) in Shuaiba
   formation w/ oil base mud & 0-50 bph losses. FDC, DLL, MSFL, CNL, BHC &
  GR logging was performed. 9 5/8” casing was run and cemented at 2770 m.
   Casing was  tested with 1000  psi, ok. N/U BOP.(top of cement at2750m).
    Perforation was performed in 2694-2705m interval in L.shuaiba. Acidizing was
   performed and dual completion string was run. X-mass tree was N/U. The well
   was displaced with diesel. Flow the well. The well was completed and released on
   N/A.
