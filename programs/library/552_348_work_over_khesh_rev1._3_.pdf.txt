2. ANTICIPATED WORKOVER HAZARDS

The main anticipated workover hazards are as follow:

          •  Mud losses in Asmari & Jahrum formation via perforation.
        •  The reservoir fluid contains 1.16 mol% of H2S & 0.89 mol% of CO2 ,so, the

          H2S release must be monitored and company relevant HSE procedures must

             be enforced at all times during the drilling in reservoir section.

3. BEFORE MOVING

  Before moving the drilling rig on KHESHT#1 location, following tasks should be done:
           • Check, service and repair the wellhead valves by wellhead service company.
           • Check and record pressure inside 9 5/8” casing and 13 3/8” – 9 5/8” annulus. If

            any pressure will be recorded, the pressure should be bleed off carefully and

               build up pressure shall be recorded after closing the well.
           • Any build up pressure shall be reported to engineering department to revise the

             workover program before moving the rig.


4. SEQUENCE OF OPERATIONS

   4.1. N/D wellhead & N/U BOP

        4.1.1. Move the rig on location & rig up on KH-1 well

        4.1.2.  Line up and connect the kill lines to the wellhead and test same to 5000 psi

        4.1.3.  Record pressure in annulus and wellhead, bleed off if possible & observe the

          well for 12 hrs (Discharge fluid shall be detected for H2S)


     NOTE: If the well is static the next sequence will be done, otherwise the revised

      program will be issued at proper time.


        4.1.4. N/D wellhead to top of 13 5/8”-5000 X 11”-10000 THS and N/U 13 5/8”-5000

       BOP stack, related lines and chock manifold. Perform full functional and pressure

             test same with 5000 psi as per section 13


     NOTE 1: Proper Spacer spool will be required

     NOTE 2: Measure the RTE to the ground and all drilled depths after first SLM

monitored while drilling in order to assure the highest standard safety level.
