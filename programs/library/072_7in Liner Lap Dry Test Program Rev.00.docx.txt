Basic Information
The following tables are basic information about the South Azadegan 207 well.
Objective
The main objective of drill stem testing to be conducted in AZNS-207 well is to have 7" Liner Lap Dry Test.
Scope of Work
In order to achieve the objectives outlined above, MDST is planned in AZNS-207 well. These information are as follow:
Well Schematic
Overall Job Procedure
The sequence of events is as follows:

## DST
Programming Electronic Memory Gauge & installing them in Bottom & Middle BT (10s for each sample for less 3 days In the presence of client)
RIH DST tools (during RIH all drill pipe ID checked by acceptable rabbit, according DST string configuration-5 Std/hr).
RIH & have about 653m Fresh Water (62.4pcf) and 2500m KCL polymer limestone mud as water cushion, differential pressure from hydro spring valve is about 2,000psi.
Line up & pressure test the lines (2,500psi).
Set the packer (after monitor string weight down and up, make string 2 meter above rotary table, set slips and rotate 5 cycle the string by rotary table to right hand then RIH slowly till decrease 25000 lb from weight indicator) and then close pipe rams and apply 500 psi in to annulus for set packer test, set the slips to the string again.
Open hydrospring, observe string flow from DST chock manifold for 3 hrs. (The hydrospring opened automatically after a short delay time after set packer).
NOTE: If there is flow in string, after view cushion water at surface, go to next step immediately.
Close DCIP (11 cycle the string by rotary table to right hand) and monitor the well for 6 hrs.
Open circulation valve by dropping bar from surface & reversing till balance mud or open DCIP circulation port. (at the start time of reverse, don’t apply pressure in the annulus and just make it full, the DST supervisor allow to increase the reversing pressure after the water cushion seen at surface)
Unset packer and Close hydrospring. (POOH the string, Packer automatically unset and hydrospring closed)
POOH string (POOH DST tools configuration, 5-6 Std/hr).
Take the flowing sample between DCIP & hydrospring.
Disconnect Electronic Memory Gauges.
Caution: Before disconnecting DCIP from hydro spring, take enough care not to be split the existing sample between the tools.
Safety

## Organization
The proper reporting lines have to be defined by OEOC in order to avoid miscommunication & ensure the integrity of the whole operation.
The program need to be followed up & any deviation from the program will have to be communicated to OEOC management in Tehran & written notice for program modification to be issued to avoid misunderstanding at later stage.
The OEOC Supervisor, Client Petroleum Engineer onsite, Drilling Supervisor (Company-Man) & Tool Pusher need to have close communication prior & during the execution of the program. The Drilling Supervisor is responsible for all rig operations and therefore is the Senior Manager at the well site.
Tool box and safety meeting to be held involving OEOC Crew, Drilling Supervisor, Client Petroleum Engineer, Tool Pusher and other party involved prior to spotting and rig up the equipment, pressure testing and prior to opening the well.

## Responsibility
Drilling Supervisor
He has overall responsibility for conducting the test and will liaise with the Company man and Supervisor to issue all instruction to the drilling contractor and Service Company personnel.
Responsibility for:
Safety.
Communicating all instructions (Drilling Contractor /Service personnel).
Transmitting normal data reporting to office.
Coordinate and liaise with OEOC logistics for transport any additional equipment required for the job.
Ensure that all operations are carried out in a safe and efficient manner.
MDST/RTTS Supervisor
Reports & liaises with the company-man & is responsible for:
The organization of the Crew.
Ensuring all relevant data is collected and for checking the quality of the data.
All equipment is prepared correctly and is suitable for MDST operation.
MDST/RTTS Supervisor
Responsible for all actions taking place on the floor, in particular the following:
All operations concerning the annulus and any operation of valves on the Rig manifold.
Monitoring of the annulus pressure at all times throughout the operation.
Monitoring of the string tension throughout program.
Ensuring that the pump room and mud pit area is manned at all times.
Ensuring all annulus pressure monitoring instrumentation is checked on a regular basis.
Operations carried out during the Operation must be cleared through the Driller. The driller must inform all key personnel immediately of any problem or changes to the program.
Direct communication with the OEOC drilling Supervisor, OEOC petroleum Engineer and OEOC MDST/RTTS personnel throughout the test and he is responsible for maintaining a watch on the test equipment and flow lines up to the choke manifold.
OEOC MDST/RTTS Chief Operator
Responsible for the following:
Ensuring all action during the Operation are conducted safely and as per program, and all actions required during preparations prior to the test are all done correctly and safely.
Ensure that the OEOC Petroleum Engineer and the Driller is informed prior to any actions taking place with the MDST/RTTS equipment (well opening, close the well, etc).
All his crew members are aware as to how the Operation will be conducted and of their duties.
Those hazardous operations are conducted with the necessary work permits and as per Drilling Supervisor's instructions.
Ensuring that an OEOC DST technician is onsite at all time when the tool in the hole.
OEOC Memory Gauges Chief Supervisor
Responsible for the following:
Ensuring all action during the job are conducted safely and as per program, and all actions required during preparations prior to the job are all done correctly and safely which cover but not limited to the following:
Ensuring all the sensors and gauges are checked and function tested to run in hole.
All coefficients and calibration files are calibrated.
All the programming of the gauges and reporting of the data are agreed by supervisor.
Ensuring that the regular reporting of the data is in place as requested and agreed by Petroleum Engineer.
Ensuring that technician ensure that the acquisition system are all working and report as necessary should any problem encountered.

The purpose of the meeting is:

Minutes of the meeting should be taken and any concerns should be addressed and relevant changes be made to the program where appropriate.
Special Procedures for H2S Environment
This phase of the operation will normally be the first time at which H2S is produced to surface. Consequently the risk factor is higher. To cope with this eventuality, the following precautions should be taken.
A safety meeting should be held well in advance of testing when all personnel are to be informed that H2S is present or likely to be present.
All testing equipment shell be certified and approved for H2S duty (NACE MRO1-75).
H2S and CO2 will be monitored by the H2S Engineer during observing the well & sampling.
All non –essential personnel should be taken off the rig before commencement of test.
If the well flow will be come to surface after opening the well all personnel shall carry a 15 min BA set at all times & may remove their BA sets as long as the sets are positioned within 5 meters of their normal working position.
A team of two rig flour persons are required when sampling. One person collects the sample while another person stands upwind some distance away observing the first person, ready to lend assistance should the sampler show signs of the effects of H2S gas.
Both persons must wear BA when sampling and if necessary all other personnel in the immediate area
Rig H2S safety procedure will be use throughout the test. If the H2S concentration around the well test area exceeds 10 ppm, special safety instructions in these conditions should be implemented.
MDST Tools
MDST tools are Mechanical type. Tools wellbore size is from 7" to 13 3/8".
This MDST string is used for cased-hole and can run in a hole for a relative long duration the job. It is composed of a circulating valve, test valve (DCIP), retrievable packer (RTTS packer), gauge carriers and so on. These tools can undertake 10,000 psi pressure and be H2S sustainable. This string is operated mechanically.
Tester Valve (DCIP): to open and close the well during operation
Hydro Spring: to keep separate annulus fluid from tubing fluid during R.I.H.
Packer: to isolate formation.
Reversing Circulate Valve: to circulate string after operation.
Gauge Carrier (BT): for testing operation data acquisition.
Hydraulic Jar: to release stuck BHA components if necessary.
Down hole Memory Gauge: to record pressure & temperature of down hole vs time.
