                                         Well: SI-09
                                     Siah Makan Oil Field

Page 1 of 1                                       Procedure SI-009

              WELL SI-009 cement Injection program No 13

     1) RIH W/ C.C.Bit & 3-1/2 DP followed by 5” DP to 2134m (50 m below 13 5/8” shoe).

        Note:

            •   Inside 13-5/8” shoe, Close the well and perform injection test W/ maximum

                1000psi. In case of injectivity above 2 BPM RIH to 2134m & prepare to cement

               job.

     2) Mix & Pump 120bbls 150pcf cement and displace same as balance.

     3) POOH to 1700m (184m above theoretical TOC)

     4)  Close the well and inject 70bbls cement up to 2034m (50m above 13-5/8” shoe) with max

        1000psi and retain pumping pressure till thick cement (20 Hrs).

        Note:

            •   After making sure well pressure is constant and check surface samples is hard,

                 release pressure and open well.

            •   Strip and move string up / down periodically during WOC.

     5)  Release pressure, open the well. RIH and tag TOC. Circulate and uniform MW @ 149pcf.

     6)  Observe well, POOH to surface.

     7) RIH W/ 12-1/4” Bit (Open Nozzles) and related BHA (without stabilizer) to top of cement.

     8) D/O cement to 2035m, CBU and perform integrity test with 1000psi.

        Note:

            •  In case of needed repeat cement job.

     9)  Continue operation as per procedure No 12.
