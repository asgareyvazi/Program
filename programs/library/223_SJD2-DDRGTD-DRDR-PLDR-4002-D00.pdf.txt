1  WELL SUMMARY

       Location
  The Sepehr-Jufair field (Operation Area) is located in Khuzestan province with about 40km distance in
   the west of Ahwaz City. The Sepehr-Jufair field (Operation Area) covers about 352 square kilometers
   area with approximately 23 kilometers long and 27 kilometers wide, it is located in vicinity of South
  Azadegan and Yadavaran giant fields. The operation area does not have any geological surface
   impression and was explored initially by 2D seismic survey in 1976. The explored oil reservoirs from
   different geological strata show economic hydrocarbon potential, which are developed firmly in the
   Sepehr-Jufair field and nearby oil fields.
  The Well JR-08 has been penetrated in the north part of Jufair structure, north-east direction to the Well
   JR-06 as the main reference well with the distance 2700m. The Well location is away 950m from JR-01
   location. The main purpose of this well is production oil from Lower Fahliyan reservoir.

        Site Description
The nearest well to the proposed JR-08 well location is JR-01, located in adjacent with the Jufair village.
well JR-08 was drilled in order to evaluate the hydrocarbon potential possibility of Bangestan and Khami
Groups. This well was drilled from surface (Aghajari formation) to the near bottom of the Fahliyan
formation (Lower Fahliyan reservoir layers) and finally was penetrated into the depth of 4670m (FTD).
After perforating and conducting the production tests, respectively from bottom to the top. Based on the
achieved results, some layers show outstanding capability.

   2   SECTION SUMMARIES

         32ʺ Hole Section
   36” Hole section was planned to be drilled to 110 m with 2 run; run and cement 26” conductor Pipe.
   However, while drilling with 26” bit to 43 m POOH to surface three times because the bit was balled
   up. It was decided to continue drilling & enlarging well to TD section by 24” bit & 36” hole opener. The
   36” hole section size was done successfully in 7 Days.



         24” Hole Section
   24” Hole Section was planned to drill with 5 run BHA; But unfortunately, due to wash out in DP & DC
   and also inability to pass 18 5/8” CSG from 26” Shoe in several times.

         17” Hole Section
   Based on drilling program, 17” Hole Section would be drilled to ±2031.5 m and 13 5/8” & 13-3/8" casing
   would be run to ±2031 m and cemented as per program. Hole section was planned to drilled with 3
  BHA run after drill out shoe track and this section finished in 3 BHA runs (includes D/O BHA). In actual,
   this section was started by 17” M.T.Bit (clean out BHA) for D/O & drilling formation to 1482.5 m. try to
   perform FIT; no success. Rec. another FOSV, Inside Ann. stepwise W/Max 1200psi, press. gradually
   drop 900psi in 15min. POOH to surface & RIH to 310 by new 17” PDC Bit. Stop RIH due to observed
   unusually SPP. POOH & check string; ok. RIH again with same Bit to BTM & drilling to TD section @
   2031.5 m. Run 13 5/8” & 13 3/8” CSG to 2031 m and cemented as per program.

         12 ¼” Hole Section

  was commenced to drilling 12 1/4” hole section to 2032 m. the well was displaced the with 80 pcf
   KCl/Polymer mud. Continue drilling to 2061 m W/NL-NG. POOH to surface & RIH with performance
   BHA. Drilling was continued as performance to 2242.5 m. stop Drilling; due to low ROP. POOH to
   surface. It was observed that protection sleeve of PDM was open & had fallen on shank of the bit. RIH

  6   DRILLING PROCEDURES
      •  Spud Rig on well#JR-08
      •  Retrieve one way. Record wellhead and annulus pressure.
      •  R/U Slick line equipment, install protection sleeve in 3SV. Record pressure.
      •  Open SSD. Connect surface Line, Kill the well w/115 pcf mud. Get contaminated CaBr2 on
          surface.
      •  Close SSD, Test String w/ 5000 psi, Retrieve protection sleeve. R/D Slick line equipment,
      •   Install Two way in tubing hanger. N/D wellhead and N/U BOP. Pressure test BOP.
      •  Retrieve Two way. R/U Slick line equipment, Retrieve Prong & Plug of NO-GO, Set Plug in SSD,
          Test String w/ 5000 psi.
      •  Unlatch completion string from anchor seal. POOH completion string to Surface.
      •  RIH w/7" mill to packer depth at 4006.1m. Mill packer, POOH to Surface.
      •  RIH w/7" Taper tap to BTM. Engauge w/packer. Pull packer to Surface.
      •  P/U 5 7/8" Condition BHA. RIH to bottom to check well condition. CBU. POOH to Surface. L/D
        BHA.
      •  P/U 2 7/8" open end drill pipe, RIH to TOC at 4631m. Spot 10 bbl 118pcf cement as balance
          plug. WOC to get hard. RIH & tag cement. POOH to Surface. L/D BHA.
      •  P/U 5 7/8" D/O BHA. RIH to Tag TOC. D/O CMT to 4544m. CBU. POOH to Surface. L/D BHA.
      •  P/U 7" Scraper BHA. RIH to packer depth. Scrap packer depth. Displace mud with Completion
           fluid (CaBr2). Perform flow check. POOH to surface.
      •  P/U Completion String. RIH & Set packer @ 3900m.
      •  Secure the well. N/D BOP. N/U wellhead and test same.
      •  R/U Well test & stimulation surface equipment and test same.
      •  Open the well and try to flow in one choke sizes as per program, in case of NO flow, perform
           acidizing as program and clean up the well and flow the well in one choke size. R/D well
          stimulation equipment.
      •  Suspend The Well as per Procedure.
       •   Rig Service
