 A) Preparation
1.  Stand Back DP's as Needed for 12 1/4” Hole Section (3313 m). Regarding the rig Condition, WSS Should decide to Stand DPs
    Joint by Joint When Running in Hole or Stand Back Drill Pipe Before Start of Drilling.
2.  Ensure that all Drilling Tools and equipment, as per Section equipment listings, are on board, checked out and in a serviceable
    condition prior to 13 5/8” casing shoe drill out.
3.  Prepare 9 5/8” Casings Tally after preparation and measurement of casing joints length.
4.  Set 13 5/8” Wear Bushing in Place.
5.   Kill sheet and Trip sheet should be regularly updated during this hole section.
6.  Well Control Drill should be conducted prior to drilling this hole section.

 B) Drilling Procedure

                                        BHA #7
1.  P/U & M/U 12 1/4" Drilling BHA as following:

         a)  BHA #7 Configuration:
                                       12 1/4” BHA No.7 (2677-3313 m)
              Description              Size            Connection                        No.
         PDC Bit (M323)          12 1/4”        6 5/8" Reg. Pin Up                    Nozzle: 9*16
             Bit Sub (Float in)          8 1/2”         6 5/8" Reg B x B
             DC                8 1/2”         6 5/8" Reg P x B                     2 Jts
             String Stabilizer          12 1/4”         6 5/8" Reg P x B
             DC                8 1/2”         6 5/8" Reg P x B                     10 Jts
                 Drilling Jar            8 1/2”         6 5/8" Reg P x B
             DC                8 1/2”         6 5/8" Reg P x B                     3 Jts
             XOS               8 1/2”      6 5/8" Reg P x 4 1/2" IF B
            DP 'G'                    5”            4 1/2" IF P x B                    As Req.

         b)  Use new 12 1/4” PDC Bit and Drill down to the depth which ROP drops below the acceptable ROP determined by
                office. If ROP dropped Make a trip to change the Bit to new Bit and RIH with Same BHA Configuration.
          c)  Use safety clamp and proper slip for all DCs & 5” DPs.

2.   RIH W/ 12 ¼” Drilling BHA#7 to ±2682 m.
         a)  Take extra care, FIRST TRIP INSIDE CSG.
         b)  Calibrate & adjust mud logging depth sensor 3 stand before reach to bottom.
          c)  Pass BOP stack gently.
         d)  F/U & break circulation after RIH BHA.
         e)  Record BHA weight below jar
             f)   After RIH drilling jar Circ to check for any probable wash out in Jar mandrel.

3.   Drill Ahead 12 ¼” hole section to ±3002 m.
         a)   Drilling Parameters:
         WOB: 20-40 Kips, GPM: 600-800, RPM: 110-180

            Note: Perform Drill-Off test to Optimize the drilling Parameters according to Procedure.

 C) Contingency / First Action Plans
Loss Strategy:

       Losses less than 10 bbl/hr:
       ➢ No action to be taken, but close monitoring of the loss shall be made
       Losses 10 - 30 bbl/hr:
       ➢ Load Kwik-Seal F in system up to 10 ppb
                     If no success, then:
         ➢   Pump 50 bbl LCM pill containing:
                     ▪  5 ppb Mica Medium
                     ▪  10 ppb Walnut Shells Fine
                     ▪  10 ppb Kwik-Seal Fine
                     ▪  5 ppb CaCO3-M
                     ▪  5 ppb CaCO3-C
                     Note: System is loaded gradually from low concentration to maximum mentioned ranges.
       Losses 30 – 100 bbl/hr:
       ➢ Load CaCO3 Medium & Coarse in active system up to 5 ppb of each
       ➢ Load Kwik-Seal F in system up to 10 ppb
         If needed / required; shaker-man should be ready for managing a part of the return flow toward the mud pits
                   by-pass the shakers to avoid screens get blinded and LCM get wasted. However, this is a matter of
                      operation-dictated decision.
                     If no success, then:
      ➢ Stop Drilling
      ➢ Pull out string above the loss zone
      ➢ Spot 60-90 bbl pill containing:
                ▪ Pre-Hydrated Bentonite 5-10 ppb
                ▪ 6-10 lb/bbl Kwik-Seal F
                ▪ 6-10 lb/bbl Kwik-Seal M
                ▪ 6-10 lb/bbl Walnut-Shells F
                ▪ 6-10 lb/bbl Mica M
                ▪ 20-30 lb/bbl CaCO3-F, M
                ▪ Repeat this pill for two times
    ➢  If LCM pill at above concentration cannot pass through the string (bit nuzzles, etc.), we need to pump the pill through a
          Mule-Shoe while following the procedure of the next state.
       Losses more than 100 bbl/hr to Total Losses:
          In the event of severe losses, Squeeze Pills, Barite or CaCO3 or Cement Plugs, Silicate Pill or Cross-Linking Pills should be
         pumped to minimize losses.

 LCM Pill Application Procedure
  1.  After spotting LCM pill over thief zone, pull the bit above the calculated top of the pill. Wait 30 minutes and monitor the annulus.
      After 30 minutes, attempt to fill the annulus, if not already full.

  First Actions (Best practice):

  Pipe stuck
     ▪  In case of Pack off reduce FR or bleed off press & make sure to keep SPP below 500 psi, TQ. Up string and work
     downward till get normal rotation and circulation.
     ▪  In case of differential stuck If String is Off-BTM, Torque up string and work downward between.
     ▪  Always rotate, reciprocate with pump on whenever operations are stopped.

  Loss
     ▪  P/U string off-BTM, Reduce flow rate.
     ▪  Check all pit levels for any valve leakage.
     ▪   If Downhole Loss confirmed follow the Loss strategy.

  Drilling Break (fast drilling) or Gain
     ▪   If ROP Suddenly increased significantly or Pit Levels Increasing, P/U String off BTM.
     ▪  Slow Circ & Monitor Active Pit Level. If Levels was static, Resume DRLG. If Pit Levels was increasing, Perform Positive
     Flow Check.
     ▪   If well Flowing, Shut in the diverter & Circ out the kick.

  Stand pipe pressure Drop
     ▪  Continuously Monitor Off BTM & On BTM Press.
     ▪  In case of press drop, P/U String off BTM, stop rotation, Decrease Circulation to minimum.
     ▪  Check all surf Lines & Equip. Check Pit room for any Mud transferring or adding any Mud material to Active. Take MW & F.
      Vis in/out. Check Pit Level.
     ▪  Close IBOP & Press test Surf Lines.
     ▪   If Surf OK, Take SCR & Compare same with Last SCR.

  Bit balling
     ▪  P/U String 2-3 ft off bottom.
     ▪  Apply High RPM to bit for 2 min;
     ▪  Jerk the string up and down for 1 min
     ▪  Stop for 1 minute.
     ▪  Repeat above sequence for another 15 min
     ▪ Tag bottom with maximum bit RPM and apply high FR before tagging bottom
     ▪ Resume drilling with lower WOB.
     ▪   If previous actions not succeed, prepare to pump Balled Up Remover pills, leave to soak and try to dissolve balled material.

 D) Section Notes:
•   During drilling be aware of saltwater flow, Cut MW, Mud properties variation, especially hardness.
•    Kill sheet and Trip sheet should be regularly updated during this hole section.
•   Consider BHA component & surface drilling system abrasion due to high solid content of heavy mud, visually inspection for
    corrosion or wear on each connection / trip.
•   Centrifuge should be used mostly in Barite Recovery mode to control excess drill solid/MBT in this section.
•   Conduct SCR on each shift or every 100 m drilled or whenever mud weight or rheology properties changed.
•   Before ending 12 ¼” Hole section and reaching Asmari formation, H2S services equipment should be available on rig and ready
     to operate.
•  Mud engineer is responsible to monitor shaker condition & maintaining enough high-vis pill & mud, mud material, Drill water &
    inform company man any intense changes.
•   For Timing of Each Operation, Refer to Drilling Program (section 1.9.1 Detail Time Breakdown).
•  Mud Logger Should Closely Monitor and Report Drilling Parameters.
•  Mud Engineer Shall Report Volume of Over Flow in Different Flow Rate to Company Man.
•     If frequent tight holes are encountered, care should be given to clear the tight holes with regular Wash & Ream. If tight holes
    not cured, Adjustment of Mud weight and Mud rheological properties should be considered (Based on Office Decision).
•  H2S Service should be Rigged Up at the end of 12 ¼” Hole Section, Before Drill out of 9 5/8” Casing Shoe.

▶  Offset Wells Problems in Gachsaran Formation:
    ▪   Well Flow in GS FM Cured by MW Increase: SI-004, SI-005
    ▪   Tight hole in GS FM cured by W&R and MW increase: SI-004, SI-005
    ▪  Mud Losses (20-80 bbl/hr w/133-135 PCF) at base of GS FM cured by Cement: SI-001, SI-003
    ▪   Swabbing Followed by Well Flow is highly probable in GS FM due to A) tight hole frequency B) Bit Balling Probability

 E) Safety Recommendation:
 WORK SAFE; ALWAYS ASK YOURSELF HOW CAN I GET HURT IF I DO THIS TASK.
•  Safety Standards and Policy of PECO Should Be Followed During Drilling Operation.
•    It is Not Expected to Encounter Shallow Gas, Since No Shallow Gas Has been Observed in Offset Wells of the Field.
•  In Case of Any Deviation or Advise for This Instruction Call Co. Man ASAP.
•   All Rig Crew Should Follow and Cooperate with Job Specialists as Per This Instruction.
•  Mud Eng. Is Responsible for Monitoring & Report Pit Levels During Operation.
•  Choke line and choke manifold must be lined up for SOFT SHUT-IN.

  F) Section Summary:

The 12 ¼’’ Hole Section is Drilled through high pressure zones of Gachsaran Formation From 2677 m to 3313 m and 9 5/8’’
Casing will be set at ± 3313 m in Cap Rock and cemented in one stage. Salt Saturated Polymer Mud (130-137 pcf) is used for
drilling the section. According to previous wells, there are risks of well flow from Gachsaran high pressure zone, tight holes and
swabbing. CDR/GR and SBT logs are performed to evaluate the well path and cement quality behind the 9  5/8’’ casing,
respectively. 13 5/8"-3K * 11''-5K T.H.S and 13 5/8’’-5K BOP Stack are nipple up at the end of the section.
