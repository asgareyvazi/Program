1 Basic Information

Azar oil field is located in west of Iran in Ilam province and Anaran block. This field is shared with

Iraq’s Badra oil field. Azar structure was discovered by 2D seismic acquisition named ANAR‐600

by NIOC in 1970’s. First exploration well was drilled in this field in 2003. By drilling the second

well in 2004‐2005 as well as 3D seismic which was conducted in that area and Changuleh in

2007, the Sarvak reservoir was found to be commercial for production. Development activities

started in July 2013 by drilling well AZR‐003 followed by other delineation and development

wells.

2 Test Objectives and Requirements

2.1 Testing Objectives

Cased hole test program is important to confirm each reservoir’s productivity and evaluate its

parameters. For the Well AZAR-08, the main objectives of testing are as follows:

      Determination of reservoir’s fluid property,

      Determination of reservoir’s productivity,

      Detection of initial reservoir pressure and temperature,

      Collection of down hole representative fluid samples for PVT analysis,

      Collection of surface samples of separated oil, gas and water for analysis,

      Calculation of the skin factor or formation damage ratio,

      Evaluation of formation characteristics (permeability, fractures and layering).

2.2 Testing Requirements

      Before running the DST String, require performance clean up hole, running scraper to
          scraping 7” casing to 3870m by three times, and circulate to make well bore clean.

      After perforation, an initial cleanup is conducted on 16/64” to 48/64” adj. choke, RIH CT
        and displace fluid column by diesel if the well doesn’t flow, and perform injectivity test.

     The initial flow test is conducted on 48/64” fixed choke and measure the oil & gas rate,
        and then shut in the well from choke manifold for initial build up, surface sampling may
        be needed during flow test. The duration of initial flow test and build up will be
         decided on site.

      Matrix acidizing will be required if necessary. CTU is recommended to be used for AC if
         the operation of bullhead is unsuccessful, which will be followed by post acid clean up
        on 16/64” - 48/64” adj. choke.

     Main flow: Flowing the well on 3 choke size (32/64”、40/64” and 48/64”) and taking
          surface dead oil sample and gas sample while flowing on 48/64” choke.

      Shut in the well from down hole for final build up, the duration depends on the
         requirement of client.

     Down hole PVT sampling will be conducted after final build while flowing the well
         through 16/64” choke.

       Kill the well and POOH F.B. DST string.

      Set bridge plug and prepare for the nest perforation zone.

3 Well Test Procedure

3.1 Layout Surface Equipment and Pressure Test

  General points regarding rig-up of surface well test equipment are as follows:

      Rig-up surface well test equipment package as per layout plan (Refer to Appendix 1
        SURFCE EQUIPMENT LAYOUT).

     Where pipe work crosses walkways, pipe should be made clearly visible (with danger
         tape) and tripping hazards minimized. Pipe work should be close to the ground.

      Tag the instrument air supply valve to the separator to ensure continuous supply so
          that testing operations are not interrupted unintentional.

      Erect barriers, display pressure test signs and make announcements to warn personnel
       when pressure testing is about to commence.

      Surface equipment should be tested pressure and record data.

      During pressure testing check the following:

         -The orifice meter sliding valve holds pressure

         -The choke manifold isolation valves are tested independently to ensure choke changes
        and inspections can be made.

     The following should be included as part of pressure  testing surface well  test
         equipment:

             - Flush all surface lines and equipment from rig floor through burner heads and gas
           lines

             - P/T entire system to 1,000 psi against flare pit (if possible).

             - P/T against oil and gas manifold to 1,000 psi.

             - P/T separator gas outlet & bypass, oil outlet & bypass plus water outlet to 1,000 psi.

             - P/T upstream of choke manifold & HP side of choke manifold to the 7,000 psi.

             - P/T downstream of choke manifold to separator inlet and oil & gas bypass to 1,000
           psi.

At the end of pressure testing ensure all barriers and signs are removed. Also inform personnel by
the PA system and sign off permits.

  Choke Manifold

Prepare the choke manifold as outlined below:

      Pressure test the choke manifold as per procedures to 7,000 psi.

      Pressure is to be bled-off between each test.

     Check all unions and flange connections.

      Prepare the necessary fixed choke and check condition.

     On completion of pressure testing  all valves should be left open. Drift the choke
         manifold to 3.00″.

  Function Test for ESD System

Requirements and points to note for the ESD system are as follows:

     Remote shutdown points for the ESD system shall be installed on the rig floor, at the
        choke manifold, at the test separator and outside the lab cabin.

     The separator instrument air supply will be an integral component of the ESD system.
          Air supply interruptions will activate the ESD system and shut-in the well at the flow
            line.

      Function test HP / LP pilots using a hand pump (if applicable).

     The ESD system is to be function tested, and record in the Well Test Report.

   Calibration and Check

The following calibration checks of surface well test equipment should be carried out:

      Calibrate differential pressure gauges using a manometer.

      Surface pressure gauges shall be calibrated prior to mobilization using a dead weight
           tester.

     Check flow meter calibrations by pumping water through each meter. Obtain two
          consistent results for each meter.

     Check condition of measuring instruments, etc. thermometers, hydrometers etc.

      Ensure all orifice plates & choke beans are in good condition and an adequate supply is
        on hand.

  Pressure Test for Surface Test Equipment

Test all surface test equipment and surface test lines upstream of choke manifold to 7000psi step
by step and hold 15 minutes for each step.

  Connect Test Line to Test tree and pressure test lines.

      Line up the surface test line to one producing wing of Test tree, line up production wing
         to flange.

     Check SSV (Actuator) open  if  still closed, Flush flow line as per Service Company’s
         procedure.

      Pressure test surface equipment and line according to service company’s testing
         procedure, pressure test upstream 7,000psi for 15minutes (from Test tree to choke
          manifold), downstream testing as per well testing company instruction.

  TSTV PRESSURE TEST PROCEDURE

     Make sure that the rams are open during the pressure testing against TSTV.

     The pressure test to be conducted after TSTV, RD circulating plus 1 Joint or 1 std of 3½”
       PH6 tubing are RIH.

     Make up circulating head and TIW valve onto the string.

      Line up tubing to rig manifold for return during reversing out.

      Close Hydril and Reverse out 3 to 4 BBLs or more through TSTV, maximum reverse
           circulating pressure 300 psi and maximum pump rate 2 BPM

      After string has been reversed out, bleed off annulus pressure to “0” psi, open the
          Hydril and line up cement test pump to tubing.

      Pressure test surface line against low torque valve to 8000 psi for 10 min

       If the test is good, bleed off pressure to “0” psi.

     Open low torque valve and pressure test against TSTV If the test OK, bleed of pressure.

3.5 DST Tools Design

The well testing will be conducted using APR tool strings (Refer to Appendix 3 APR TESING
STRING) comprising:

      7” RTTS packer (inclusive of bypass, safety joint, jars).

     Gauge carriers.

      Annulus pressure operated circulating valve (RD valve, RDS valve).

      Annulus pressure operated tester valve (LPR-N valve).

      Drain valve.

     The packer will be set above the zone of interest in the 7" casing.

      Underbalanced pressure perforation.

     The planned production testing commences with an initial clean-up and a initial flow
         followed by an initial build-up.

     The second planned production testing commences with an acidizing treatment which
           will be used to increase reservoir permeability. After post-acid clean up flowing the well
        on 3 choke sizes and shut-in to establish the reservoir pressure.

3.6 Function Test and Pressure Test for DST Tools

      Charge nitrogen for LPR-N tester valve according to Azar-08 bottom hole hydraulic
         pressure and temperature, hold pressure 3 hours, No leakage is OK.

      Pressurize and bleed off pressure from LPR-N valve annulus upper and lower port for
         function test according to function testing procedure.

      Pressure testing the LPR-N ball valve using the clean water. Pressure testing 5000psi
        below the ball valve of the LPR- N tester valve and pressure testing 5000psi above the
           ball valve (directed by the company supervisor), Hold pressure for 15 minutes, No
         leakage is OK.

      Function testing the hydraulic by-pass valve, BIG JOHN hydraulic jar according to
         Function testing procedure.

      Pressure testing for the other tools 5000psi (depend on the supervisor’s requirement of
         the company or the condition of well site). Hold pressure for 15 minutes. No leakage is
          ok, and pressure test RTTS packer and safety joint should be careful for the tension
         sleeve of Safety Joint.

3.7 Run DST Testing String

      Install 4 pins onto LPR-N Testing Valve.

      Adjust the Charging Pressure of LPR-N Tester Valve according to the Bottom Hole
          Hydraulic Pressure, Bottom Hole Hydraulic Temperature and Surface Temperature.

      Install rupture disc onto RD. Safety. Cir Valve and RD. Cir Valve, the option of the

         pressure value of the Rupture Disc installed on the RD Safety Cir Valve, RD Cir Valve.

      Install the tension sleeve for RTTS Safety Joint.

      Four gauges must be programmed before running strings on surface as required, then
         put them into gauge carrier.

     Run testing string from bottom up. Refer to schematic string would be provided by
         Contract Well test Supervisor. Every thread must be smeared with greases.

      Record the hang weight after the two slip joints run into hole, it is useful for unsetting
         the packer.

      Pressure testing DST String.

              - Pressure test to 5000psi against closed LPR-N test valve after running in 1st stand 3-1/2"

          tubing above two slip joints.

              - Pressure testing 5500psi, 6000psi, 6500psi and 7000psi after every running 1000m 3-1/2"

          tubing.

      Control running speed about 8 stands 3-1/2" tubing per an hour.

      Require running DST string very slowly when running into 7" casing and don’t turn right
        DST string in order to ensure that packer is not set.

     DST BHA was assembled on 3-1/2" tubing. Fill pipes every stands with water as the
         cushion until surface.

3.11 Initial Flow and Build-up

     Once the first formation hydrocarbons reach surface, diverted flow to flare pit.

      Collect sample from the needle valve of data head, CO2 and H2S dragger tube
        measurements must be made immediately and repeated every 15 minutes until the
          well is stabilized.

      Divert flow to 48/64" fixed choke, monitor FTHP BS&W, PH and water salinity,  If
        sediment below approx.0.5%, divert flow through the test separator.

      Take surface dead  oil and gas samples from separator as requirement of Client’s
          supervisor.

      Shut in the well from choke manifold for initial build up, monitor the WHTP at choke
         manifold. The duration of initial build up will be decided by client supervisor.

The OTS’s WCP firing head uses one electrical detonator to activate the gun string. The electric

6.4.6 Depth control

The WCP depth correlation uses a wireline CCL log run through the casing. The CCL curve allows
identification of components in the casing.

      After RIH the gun string, the CCL tool which locates at the end of the cable head will
         present one CCL log curve on the logging software; the CCL will detect the casing collar
        and present one peak on the log cure.

     Compare the CCL log curve with the CBL or Full set log CCL curve, and then you can
       know the difference between the perforating depths with the real log depth, and then
        do the depth correlation.

      After the depth correlation finished, go down the gun string and then log up to the
         designed depth.

     Now is ready for shooting.

6.4.8 Gun Retrieval

The following safety procedures must be observed when retrieving guns.
When guns are 200ft below surface, stop POOH. Holding safety meeting with all personnel
involved in reverse deployment of guns.

      All non-essential personnel must keep  far away from the well head during gun
         assembly and handling.

     Be sure that all OTS explosive safety procedures are understood and observed.
            a.  As an electric potential could trigger the detonators, So Radio silence is necessary.
                 All the cell phones, big power radios must be keep off.
           b.  Arming procedures must be suspended during an imminent threat of large static
                electricity discharges such as lightning or storms. It is seriously recommending that
                   all the explosive related jobs will be done at daytime.

      Again near Line Lap area decrease the speed to 10 m/min or less.

     About 50 meter near the well head(shooting nipple) again decrease the speed to
       5m/min and tell one operator to rig floor waiting for shake the wire line cable, in case
          of stuck the cable shake the cable to release the stuck. .

      Assign one person to observe the gun shoot or not when come out.

       If misfired, run new guns for this interval.

Note:In all cases, be aware that the firing adaptor and guns may contain trapping pressure.

Therefore, wear protective goggles. Exercise care since there may be pressure trapped in the

adaptor. Trapped fluids can escape and hurt the people.

Note: Suspect that there may be trapped pressure in any connection that is inexplicably hard to

undo. Never stand in line with a connection that is being loosened.

Note: Prior to disconnecting a normally fired gun, make absolutely sure there is no pressure

trapped inside. Using a bent rod, verify that perforation holes in the gun are not plugged. Wear

Note: Be particularly careful when disconnecting misfired or unfired guns. Always assume that

     Second run, third run, fourth run, repeat the above steps.

      After all the intervals perforated, POOH to surface

      Rig down all the tools and equipment.

      Disconnect the BOP and lay down it to truck.

      Clear the well site.

      Deliver the data to the witness.
