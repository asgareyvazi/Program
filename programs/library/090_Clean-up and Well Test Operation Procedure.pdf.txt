        IPON Co. Clean-up and Surface Well Test Operation Procedure

1. Objectives

The objective of every clean-up and well test operation by WT department in IPON Co. on any
hydrocarbon well(s) is to unload the drilling mud or any other undesired fluid such as
completion fluid that has occupied the issued well(s) in any section till reservoir fluid represent
in whole well sections before performing any test or being delivered to any production unit.
This fluid is known as CLEAN flow. Clean flow criteria would be discussed in following pages.

Further objective is to measure total H2S and CO2 concentration in the produced Hydrocarbons
and to measure productivity of the reservoir to ensure how much hydrocarbon in Gas/ Oil or
Condensate production rate will be achieved upon production start.

The well clean-up/test data will also be used to evaluate the expected production performance
of well(s) by client. In case of any acidizing operation performed on issued well(s), clean-up
and well test operation could be used to analyze well(s) performance pre/post acidizing
operation.

3. Method

   3.1.    Sequence of Events

The sequence of events of every clean up and surface well test is generally as follows:

Note: It must be noted that the document provided with considering that the completion
and perforation of issued well(s) has been done already. This plan specify on clean up and
surface well test program.

     -  Rig up the High rate well test/ clean-up package.
     -   Pressure test package as per safety regulation and rig up procedure.
     -  Rig up the slickline/ coiled tubing. (client option)
     -  RIH the slickline/ coiled tubing to perform desired operation. (client option)
     -  POOH the slickline/ coiled tubing. (client option)
     -  Open the well for initial clean up on effective choke sizes. (mandatory)
     -   Divert through separator for measuring the rate with one effective chock size before
       performing acid job. (client option)
     -   Shut in the well. (client option)
     -   Acidizing. (client option)
     -  Open the well for post acidizing clean up on effective choke sizes. (mandatory if
       acidizing has performed by client option)
     -   Divert through separator for measuring the rate with several effective chock sizes.
     -   Intermediate build up. (client option)
     -  Perform flow after flow with several effective chock sizes. (client option)
     -   Shut in the well.

   3.3.   H2S Measurement

H2S concentration should be carefully measured in certain periods specified by client. As it is
one of the prime objectives of the test and would affect the Gas flow rate measuring directly.

Since presence of H2S as a toxic gas required special precautions, in addition to what has been
dictated in IPON WT operation safety regulation document, to cope with this eventuality, the
following precautions are highlighted and should be taken.

     -  A safety meeting should be held well in advance of testing when all personnel are to be
       informed that H2S is present or likely to be present.
     -   All testing equipment shall be certified and approved for H2S duty.
     -  H2S and CO2 will be monitored by the H2S supervisor all the time or as the Test
       Engineer requires for data.
     -   Positive ignition sources will be provided for both oil and gas burners. Flare gun/
      manual fire handling could be used to light burners if ignition system fails. (IPON Co.
       doesn’t recommend hire manual fire handling at all)
     -   All non–essential personnel should be taken off the rig before commencement of test.
     -  No supply boats alongside, all vessels opposite to wind direction.
     -    It is recommended but not mandatory that for the first 2 hrs. After opening the well all
       personnel shall carry a 15 min BA set at all times. Thereafter personnel stationed at the
        separator, choke and the rig floor may remove their BA sets as long as the sets are
       positioned within 5 meters of their normal working position. (IPON WT supervisor is
       authorized to exempt this precaution.)
     -  A team of three persons are required when sampling. One person collects the sample
       while another person stands upwind some distance away observing the first person,
       ready to lend assistance should the sampler show signs of the effects of H2S gas. Third
      guy should be H2S supervisor which mandatorily has to be present every sampling time.
     -   Person who collect sample must wear BA when sampling and if necessary all other
       personnel should do the same.
     -  Rig H2S safety procedures are to be used throughout the test. If the H2S concentration
      around the well test area exceeds 10 ppm, the test will be stopped. During testing
       operations Two H2S supervisors should be on board to give 24 hrs. Supervision and
       monitor levels of H2S and CO2.

   3.5.    Clean Flow characteristics

Considering above Note and regular well test and clean-up operations practice, all issued wells
shall be cleaned first, prior to start well test operation. As it is obvious, any undesired fluid in
production lines to refineries or onshore production facilities is more undesired for IPON
surface well test utilities. Drilling mud, completion fluid, pre/post flush fluid while acidizing
or any other injection operation into a well is consider as undesired fluid.

Thus the purpose of clean-up operation prior to perform well test operation or deliver issued
well(s) to production line is to displace and evacuate undesired fluid as far as possible in
wellbore and replace it with reservoir representative fluid.

Accordingly clean-up operation shall take place in greater effective choke sizes than is decided
to pass flow through separator to measure Gas and Oil/ Condensate production rate.

Criteria for clean flow specified for IPON WT department as follow;

                  BS&W at the choke manifold less than client specified amount.
                   PH above 5 (after stimulation)
                   Stable Flow.

Following criteria shall be used to determine when stable flow has been achieved for clean-up
phase and for each flow rate period. The period of stable flow can only commence when:

                   Well head temperature changes less than 2 deg. F per hour.
                   Well head pressure changes less than 5 psi per hour
                  BS&W at choke should be less than client specified amount.

WT supervisor onboard is not allowed to pass flow through separator while the flow is not
clean according to what has been presented above and in rig up and run procedures, to protect
WT equipment specially separator from any probable damage which might be occur by
unknown undesired fluid in flow.

   3.6.   Emergency Shut Down System (ESD Panel)

The Emergency Shutdown (ESD) system is the primary safety system in the event of an
uncontrolled escape of hydrocarbons at surface. The system consists of a hydraulically and
pneumatically operated to manage three actuators simultaneously (wing valve, master valve
and WT SSV). Panel should be equipped with number of remotely air operated pilot valves
installed in designed areas. When a pilot or the main valve in the panel is actuated, it causes a
loss of air pressure in turn dropping out the main hydraulic valve which releases the pressure
from the flow head ESD valve actuator.

Other pilots may be high or low pressure actuated pilots installed at critical points in the system

   3.7.    Required Data to be Gathered

The following is the procedure of data list gathering while clean-up and well test operation is
in progress:

     -  Monitor all data points with the electronic surface data acquisition system.
     -  Take manual and manifold readings in specified timing requested by client during the
       well test and as directed during clean-up. (in case of software failure)
     -  Take manual H2S and CO2 Dragger readings in specified timing requested by client
       during the cleanup.
     -   Maintain detailed records on all well flow characteristics and operational changes with
        description.
     -  Take BS&W samples in specified timing requested by client.
     -  Mud logger could be asked to perform laboratory analysis on water for chlorides and
      any other ions such as Ca, Mg, sulphates, pH and density. (client option)
     -  Record the specific gravity of the gas, oil and condensate while flow is passed through
       separator before lowering the orifice plate.
     -   Collect samples of true produced formation water in plastic or pressurized containers,
          if instructed by Client for laboratory analysis. (client option)
     -   Foreign or unidentified materials produced from the well should be kept in a marked
      up plastic sample packet for onshore analysis.
     -   All samples must be clearly identified and logged.

       3.8.2. Sampling from Choke Manifold/ 3-Phase Separator

                3.8.2.1.  Sampling from Choke Manifold

There are at least 3 sampling port from choke manifold. One is on Adj. path, another one is
located on fix path and third one should be installed on outlet path of choke manifold which
can cover Adj. or fix path in case of any non-conformity.

To take sample as  it is cleared on IPON safety regulation regarding well test operation
document and also mentioned in this document as well, no matter from which port on choke
manifold or separator sampling has to be done, a team of three persons are required when
sampling. One person collects the sample while another person stands upwind some distance
away observing the first person, ready to lend assistance should the sampler show signs of the
effects of H2S gas. Third guy should be H2S supervisor which mandatorily has to be present
every sampling time. Person who collect sample must wear BA when sampling and if necessary
all other personnel should do the same.

To get sample from any port on choke manifold, note that 100 ml of produced liquid inside
effluent shall be collected. Collected sample get analyze in lab cabin to determine BS&W
(basic sediment and water) by applying centrifugal force in centrifuge apparatus to divide
collected liquid to sediment, water and hydrocarbon liquid (oil or condensate).

According to centrifugal force concept, solids would be sediment in bottom, water is a medium
media and hydrocarbon liquid is an upper media.

                3.8.2.2.  Sampling from 3-Phase Separator

In test procedure while effluent is passed through separator, depends on what kind of sample
is required, several sampling port are installed on separator lines.

To get gas sample for analyzing gas specific gravity and H2S/ CO2 contents, gaspillow should
be used. Prior to use, gaspillow must be checked visually for any tear or damage. After
sampling, specific gravity will be measured by RANAREX apparatus. See Surface Well
Testing Equipment Run Instruction, section 3.15.2.

Also there are at least 2 sample points on oil and water outlet lines for sampling from
hydrocarbon and water representative for measuring liquid API.

For pressurized sampling from oil, gas and water representative, same ports could be used just
by certified specialist.

Regarding “C” Empirical Constant, industry experience indicates that for solid-free fluids
valves of C=100 for continuous service and C=125 for intermittent service are conservative.
For solid-free fluids where corrosion is not anticipated or when corrosion is controlled by
inhibition or by employing corrosion resistant alloys, valves of C=150 to 200 may be used for
continuous service; valves up to 250 have been used successfully intermittent service. If solid
production is anticipated, fluid velocities should be significantly reduced.

Where solids and/or corrosive contaminate is present or where C valves higher than 100 for
continuous service are used, periodic survey to assess pipe wall thickness should be considered.

Also note that according to Well Testing and Fluid Handling, an Industry Recommended
Practice (IRP) for the Canadian Oil and Gas Industry Volume 4- 2012 section 4.0.13.18,
Maximum gas velocity inside downstream piping on sweet gas and sour wells which contains
less than 1% H2S, must not exceed 331.4 m/s (1087 ft./s). also gas velocity inside downstream
piping on sour gas with H2S greater than 1% should not exceed 95.4 m/s (313 ft./s) or be less
than 10.6 m/s (35 ft./s).

   3.8.6. Raise Orifice Plate form Daniel Orifice Box

-  Never place your hand directly above the Daniel orifice box.
-   Equalize Barton instrumentation before starting any work on Daniel orifice box to avoid

-  Open slide valve.

    shaft is observed.
-   Continue raising plate carrier by rotating upper plate carrier shaft counterclockwise till
    further rotation is impossible.
-   Close slide valve.
-   Close equalizer valve.
-  Open bleeder valve and bleed off pressure and make sure slide valve is not leaking.

-  Turn upper plate carrier shaft counterclockwise and lift sealing bar gasket off its seat to
   make sure no pressure is trapped.
