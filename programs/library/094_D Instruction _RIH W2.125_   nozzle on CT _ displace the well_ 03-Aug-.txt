Operation:  RIH W/2.125"   nozzle on CT & displace the well 78 pcf comp.fluid
RIH W/ 2.125" nozzle on CT to 3 1/2" liner lap @ 3949m  Pull test each 500m Pump 2 bbl thru CT each 500m Call company man 50m to 5 & 3 1/2" liner lap @ 3497/3949m in advance Prepare & make up 78 pcf completion fluid as program by mud engineer   Wash down to 4587m ( 2 m off bottom ) Wash down: 40-60 gpm ( max press 3500 psi ) with 77 pcf mud Start displace mud in well with pump 73 bbl 78 pcf completion fluid thru CT. The volume of below kick of string is 45 bbl. POOH CT to surface, L/D CT equipment  Wash & Clean mud pump#3 POOH & L/D 1 Jts 5 1/2" dp, make up 1 Jts 5" on kick off string,  Start pump 78 pcf completion fluid with low rate to get on rig floor M/up TDS & Cont. displace the well with 78 pcf completion fluid thru kick off string. Got 77 pcf mud in well in MI buffer tank    Safety recommendation: Work Safe In case of any deviation or advice for this instruction call Co.
