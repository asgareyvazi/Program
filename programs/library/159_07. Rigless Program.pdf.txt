                                   Rig Less Operation Program

1.1 Introduction

Mansour Abad development project includes 14 drilling well as rig less operation to be performed to
increase production of the wells.

1.2 Objective

The objectives of perforation, acidizing and well testing operations are:

1‐To perform perforations in Asmari formation and acid wash for more productivity

2- To perform well-testing including flow after flow and build-up test.

3‐High Volume Acidizing if required

4‐To evaluate Asmari formations productivity before and after acidizing

5‐Take down-hole and surface samples

1.3. Executive Summary

The activities in this booklet are prepared and proposed by this company as draft and shall not be considered
as firm plan and final program will be revised according to specific scope of work. All activities are supported
by QHSE and logistics team. The highlights of activities are as follows:

    Perforation, High Volume Acidizing, Well Testing and MOT

The following operation will be performed in sequence:

Preparation

   Mobilization

   Displace completion fluid

In order to prepare the well for underbalance perforation, following steps should be carried out: Rig up and
RIH CTU, displacing well completion brine with service water.

Operation

   Perforation (If required)

   Initial clean up and/or perforation tunnel acid spot

  Hanging down hole memory gauge

   Initial flow test

   Injectivity test and/or perforation tunnel acid spot

   Matrix acidizing

   Post acid clean up

   Abide by applicable laws, regulations, standards and other relevant requirements;

    Stress person-based and prevention first principles;

   Encourage all the staff to take part in HSE work;

   HSE management is regarded as one of the responsibilities of management of all levels；

   Offer sufficient resources and conditions to carry out the requirements of the policy;

   Communicate frankly with our employees, customers, subcontractors and other commercial partners;

   Combine HSE risk management method including identification, assessment, control and emergency in
    our business course;

   Give continual improvement of our HSE performance.

For achieving these goals:

    Utilizing a verifiable QHSE management system which incorporates all processes and activities;

   Monitor, evaluate and improve our companies (core) processes and the HSEQ Management system
      itself;

    Setting objectives for improvement which are measured, valued and adjusted  if necessary (Key

    Utilizing appropriate protective and work equipment, to protect people, assets and the environment in

   Openly communicating with regard to our HSEQ performance;

   Creating a culture in which our employees and contractors together with the other stakeholders
    dedicate themselves to achieving the goals which have been set with regard to HSEQ and their core
     tasks.

3.3. Risk Management

HSE risk management is a dynamic uninterrupted course;  it is the core of all HSE management system
elements. The corporation gives plan, implementation and maintenance of necessary preventive actions and
emergency plans according to the identification and assessment results of HSE aspects (including hazards
that may cause person  injury and  property damage, and environmental  aspects  that may  bring
environmental impact); establish, implement and perfect relevant HSE management program and HSE plan
to realize the continual improvement of HSE objectives and targets; the effective operation of operational
control, emergency preparedness, and HSE management program and HSE plan, relies on the coordination
of  supervision, measurement and  analysis, and the implementation  of  corrective,  preventive and
improvement  actions. The above elements  constitute the main  line  of the  corporation's HSE  risk
management control. The assistant elements of the above main line are documents and data and their
control, record control, organizational responsibility and authority, competence, awareness and training,
communication and consultation, which penetrate in all stages of HSE control main line. International audit
ensures the normal operation and conformity of the corporation's HSE management control main line.
Management review (system maintenance) carries out the continual improvement of HSE management
system from higher level. HSE affair control main line, internal audit and management review constitute the
third levels supervisory mechanism of HSE risk management.

In the aspects of corporation, business divisions and operating projects, their annual HSE management
programs give planning and deployment of HSE risk management of different levels. And the five sets of
documents for basic level work designed by the corporation and developed by every specialty (single well
HSE prospectus, HSE position guidance, construction design, HSE record and HSE examination table) offer
guidance for HES risk management of single well construction project, based on defined requirements of
laws and regulations, specific customers and other interested parties. In addition, as the basic data and
management tools of HSE risk management, the corporation has developed relevant preventive actions and
emergency actions for 30 significant HSE incidents on the basis of HSE aspects identification and assessment,
these actions are used as HSE issue toolbox.

The corporation identifies occupational health and safety hazards and environmental aspects, assesses their
risk grade and significance according to applicable laws and regulations and other requirements, and plans
relevant control actions, especially for those intolerable occupational health and safety risks and significant
environmental aspects.

HSE aspects identification and assessment is developing and changing process, it includes making definite
the requirements of laws and regulations and the impact caused by the corporation's personnel change,
business development, technological upgrading, raw material replacement and requirements of interested
parties, etc. The corporation ensures timely upgrading to be consistent with occupational health and safety
hazards and environmental aspects.

Based on the results of hazard identification and relevant risk assessment, the corporation classifies them
into four types: unacceptable risk, high risk, moderate risk, low risk. Different management actions shall be
planned to correspond to the above risk grades. Based on environmental aspects identification, two kinds of
environmental aspects are assessed, significant environmental aspect and general environmental aspect.
Matching management  actions  shall be planned to cope with the above-mentioned two kinds of
environmental aspects.

Be sure that the following responsibilities are clearly understood.

 1.  CONTRACTOR does not operate any equipment that is not owned by CONTRACTOR. These include the
    wellhead valves, SSSV, third party crane, etc. one pressure gauge should be provided by the client to
    monitor the well head pressure and help the crew controlling the well.
 2.   All non-essential personnel must keep far away from the well head during gun assembly and handling.
 3.  Be sure that all CONTRACTOR explosive safety procedures are understood and observed.
           a)  As an electric potential could trigger the detonators, So Radio silence is necessary. All the cell
              phones, big power radios and etc. must be kept off during the detonator assembling. After
              the detonator is installed to the gun string and till the time which it reaches to depth of
                200ft, or during POOH to surface after 200ft keep radio silence. Welding and cutting
              operations is forbidden. During radio silence safety switch must be put on safe condition and
                    its key must be kept by the personnel who connect/disconnect the detonator.
           b)  Arming procedures must be suspended during an imminent threat of large static electricity
               discharges such as lightning or storms. It is seriously recommending that all the explosive
               related jobs will be done at daytime and good weather.
            c)   All personnel not specifically required for gun loading, gun assembly and handling must leave
              the area, as stated previously.
           d)   All the units must have good ground connection.
 4.  Inspections shall be done to make sure that no electric field is generated between the well and the
     logging unit (Maximum allowable potential difference is lower than 2.0 V-DC&AC before ground cable
     connecting to the wellhead and lower than 0.25 V-DC &AC after ground cable connecting to the well
     head, both should be satisfied). In the event this voltage is exceeded, all sources of electrical energy
    must be switched off. If this problem can’t be solved, CONTRACTOR perforating crew can’t do the
     perforating job for this well.
 5.  Contractor’s specialized personnel shall only perform operations involving the use of explosives. The
    number of persons involved shall be as low as possible. Only the Contractor’s operator is allowed to
     control electric circuits, to load and unload guns.
 6.  The explosive shall be stored in a designated locked container or safety simple warehouse, marked with
     international recognized explosive signs, and keep in a safety area on the wellsite. Should never be
     stored in the vicinity of other hazardous materials, e.g. flammable or combustible liquids, compressed
     gases.

 7.  Gun loading should be done in a barricaded area using” explosive danger” or equivalent warning tape,
     with at least 25.0m distance from the other crew working area and non-responsible personnel entry to
      this area is forbidden.

6.1 Initial clean up

To back produce the completion fluid and recycle diesel, the clean-up of well from small choke size to full
choke size 48/64" is conducted. The duration of the clean-up flow period will depend very much on the flow
performance of the well; in any case, stabilized flow conditions and less than 1 % BS&W should be achieved
before shutting-in the well. At the end of the clean-up flow period a contingent set of separator samples will
be taken.

Actual flow times and choke settings will be determined by the Well Test Engineer and will depend on well
response and operational considerations prevailing at the time. The planned test periods and the expected
duration of each test period are different, actual test periods will be determined by the Well Test
Supervisor/Reservoir Engineer according to well response condition in site.

    1.   Carry out H2S emergency drill and hold pre-test safety meeting before firing gun and cleanup for

        well test.

Take oil sample from choke manifold(C/M)

The equipment involved in the job is listed as follow:

1.   An iron barrel, 20L, whose cap is welding with two quick connections through which the fluid can go

     into the barrel, and one is used to connect the sample point, the other is used to connect the release

    hose.

2.  A plastic hose, 30 m one side is fixed with quick connection.

3.  A big fan from rig, used from forcing the air flow circulating fast near the choke manifold.

4.   SCBA(Self Contained Breathing Apparatus)

5.  H2S detector

The operation procedure to take the oil sample from choke manifold:

1.  Take the barrel and open it, check it, and make sure it is clean inside.

2.  Take the barrel and plastic hose to the choke manifold, and put them near the sample point on the C/M.

3.  Connect the hose from sample point to the quick connection on the barrel.

4.   Connect the release hose to the quick connection on the barrel, and extend its end far away to make
sure the gas come out from it can’t reach the people who working on the site.

5.  One operator who with the H2S detector, wearing SCBA(Self Contained Breathing Apparatus) to protect
himself, and stand in the upwind direction, then open the fan to enforce the air to circulate strongly.

6.   Give information to remind the people who working in the field and release person in the downwind
direction.

7.   Then open the needle valve on the sample point slowly, check the situation of the sample barrel.

8.  When we are sure the oil sample is enough to do the analysis then close the needle valve and open all
the hoses connected to the barrel, at last transfer the sample to another plastic barrel.

Take oil & gas sample from separator

Generally, at least three matched sets of pressurized samples are taken from the separator for each zone.
Normally, 20 liters of gas per 600 cc oil sample are taken for recombination. In addition, a quantity of non-
pressurized (atmospheric) 1 liter samples of oil and formation water will be required. The precise number of
samples to be taken should be determined by the Company Petroleum Engineer prior to mobilization in
order to ensure that sufficient sample bottles/containers are available.

Unless there is a separate sampling flow period, the well test program will usually call for surface PVT
samples to be taken towards the end of the main flow period to ensure the well is fully cleaned up. However,
for back-up purposes, it is worth collecting at least one additional set of samples as soon as conditions
stabilize.

Correct sample labeling and logging of surface conditions, flow rates etc. During sampling is essential. Data
sheets for each set of samples are to be completed and copies sent with the samples.

A typical sequence for surface sampling is as follows:

    1. Flow well until stabilized wellhead conditions are achieved and solids content is less than 1.0%.

6.15.10 First Aid

   1.  The following first aid procedure applies to all H2S victims:

   -  remove the victim to fresh air, upwind or crosswind from the suspected leak source

   -   isolate the leak/source if possible

   -  commence artificial respiration and/or administer oxygen as required

   -  summon qualified medical assistance

   -  keep patient quiet, comfortable and warm

   -    if eyes are affected, wash thoroughly with water for 15 minutes – ice compresses may also be

        applied

   2.   Patients must be kept under medical observation until they are declared fit to return to work or they

       are evacuated. The possibility of delayed pulmonary oedema up to 24 hours after exposure to H2S

       should not be overlooked.

8.1 Stimulation Objectives

  Reducing the Mechanical Skin (Sd) as much as possible.

   Stimulation of reservoir by raising formation permeability and relieving formation damage.

   Focusing on obtaining commercial production rate.

   Learning of Acid Stimulation experience for other well’s job.

   Prevent any induced external damages to the reservoir by an optimized stimulation design.

8.2 Challenge and Strategy

Because of the peculiar formation and well conditions, the challenges of acidizing stimulation are presented
as following:

Challenge:

               Micro-fractures and wormholes are very important to produce  oil in the carbonate

                  reservoir. The reservoir is strongly heterogeneous along the interval and stimulation of such

               zones with lower permeability is a huge challenge.
              The pump rates and volumes required to achieve a reasonable penetration in these thick

               treatment intervals shall be high.
               High pressure High temperature oil reservoir requires appropriate operational facilities to

                achieve  the  sufficient  injection  rates and  pressures,  well-tailored  treatment  fluids

                formulations and design to reduce the corrosion effects and prevent the possible non-

                 compatibilities of injection fluids with formation oil.
               Low-friction and retarded acid required to reduce wellhead pressure and protect string and

               other equipment and the low corrosion for acid to iron are real challenges.

Strategy:

              Composite diverting technologies: VDA + resin
               Low-friction fluid (gelled acid) to reduce well head pressure is necessary.
               Optimize high efficient corrosion inhibitor and additives to adapt HTHP oil reservoir.

8.7 Job Risk Analysis

8.7.1 Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)

1.   Fresh water and bottled neutralizer shall be available at the worksite.

2.   In the event of acid contact with the eyes, immediately flushes eyes gently with fresh water and consult
    a doctor as soon as practicable.

3.   Acid contacts the skin, wash the area thoroughly with fresh water.

4.  Remove clothing contaminated with acid solutions or additive solutions.

5.   This clothing should not be re-worn until thoroughly washed.

8.7.2 High pressure

1.   Enclose the high pressure area with warning line;

2.   Plot the dangerous area with warning plate;

3.  Keep all personnel but operators far away from high pressure area while operation;

4.  Use safety rope between the pipe line connections in case of disconnection;

5.  The pump should be shut down immediately once the pipe line bursts.

8.7.3 H2S

1.   Portable H2S detector must be inspected annually.

2.   All Air respirators must be inspected function.

3.  Everyone on location should use H2S detector.

4.   For the places where H2S may concentrate must have ventilation equipment.

5.  Wind-vane must be obvious and showed to all personnel before entering well site.

6.   All personnel must be trained of H2S contingency plan and the use of all related equipment.

8.8 QHSE Requirements

8.8.1 QHSE requests

1.   Irrelevant personnel are strictly prohibited to enter into the construction area.

2.   All personnel on well-site should be equipped with full PPE, including overall, safety boContractor,
    helmet, goggle, gloves etc.

3.   Pre-job safety meeting must be held and recorded, which should contain operation objective, operation
    procedures, potential risk analysis, job assignment, emergency program etc.

    boContractor.

8.8.3 Requirements for safe operation

Acidizing operations shall comply with the following:

1.  A dedicated line shall be used to run the pumping unit to the well. The transfer and pumping lines shall
    be properly secured with snubbing lines.

2.  Under no circumstances hammers on leaking connections whilst pumping acid. The lines shall be
    pressure tested prior to starting the acid job.

3.  A pressure relief valve shall be installed on the pumping unit with the vent directed away from all
    personnel. The vent area should be flagged off. A non-return valve shall be placed in the pumping line as
     close as possible to the well.

4.  Be aware of the risk of reaction gases evolved during the wash (primarily CO2) unloading the well as
    they near surface.

5.  Due to the risk of contamination and splashing personnel, live acid shall not be returned into open top
     fluid tanks. All acid returned from the well shall be regarded as live acid.

    personnel is required during the acidizing.

5.   Fresh water shall be available around the pump truck. A safety shower or alternative option for washing
     off contamination should be available nearby.

6.   Coiled tubing unit and  all pumps must be operated by well-trained operators, and under the
     supervisor’s monitor.

A3.1.Annulus pressure monitoring

Annulus pressure monitoring while operation on different well sections shall be carefully conducted on
annulus valves for annulus pressures build up which can be resulted from annulus in-situ fluid expansion and
or leakage (of wellhead packing, packer, tubing, casing and etc.).

To perform annulus pressure monitoring a dedicated operator should be assigned to record every 30minutes.
If the pressure behavior was abnormal, operator may record every 15 minutes or even less, and if well
behavior was normal recording me sequence may be increased to every 60 mins or more. It should be noted
that if the pressure behavior was abnormal and it was necessary to release the pressure, there would be
high risk of H2S presence in released gas, so all safety precautions shall be followed by dedicated operator.
To release the pressure, Client should be informed and the work shall be conducted in the presence of
secondwitness which shall witness the job from a safe distance. Operator shall be equipped with full face
mask and portable H2S detector.

In the case of any alarmsfrom detector, all operations shall be stopped, the well be closed and secured,
dedicated operator should come out of cellar and finally all possibilities and reasons of leakage and resulted
pressure buildup should be reviewed and evaluated. If related well integrity logs be required it should to be
run and remedial actions and activities shall be performed.

Though the well cellar is defined as confined space and possibility of H2S presence in that area is high, based
on OSHA standard all safety precautions in confine space shall be addressed.

A3.2.Wellhead and X-mas tree monitoring

During any operation the condition of wellhead and x-mas tree will be monitored and  if any hazard,
especially during lifting of equipment (e.g. lifting BOPs and lubricators for logging) was observed, clear
instruction will be given to crane operator, rigger and logging operator. The physical condition of the well
will be monitored and if during any operations oil, grease, dirt and etc. be observed all of them will be
removed by non-flammable cleaner. (Suitable detergent) At delivery time of the well to Client, the well
condition will be the same as delivery time from Client to Contractor.

A3.3.Cellar Monitoring

Cellar shall be free of any rain water, oil, grease and cellulosic material so, before any job it will be cleaned
and if any cellulosic material was observed they will be removed and dumped in a safe waste basket. During
operation if the cellar be contaminated by rain water, oil and grease they will be removed by vacuum truck.
The stair way of the cellar does not have any ladder so,  if any entrance to the cellar be required, the
dedicated operator will be inform and trained about the hazard. Since, presence of poisonous gas (H2S) is
possible in the cellar which is a confined space the operator will be equipped will full face mask and portable
H2S detector. At delivery time of the well to Client, the cellar condition will be the same as delivery time
from Client to Contractor.
