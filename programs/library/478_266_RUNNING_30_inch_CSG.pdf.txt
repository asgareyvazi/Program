      PREPARATION TO BE DONE BEFORE RUNNING 30”CASING

      1. Make pipe tally (measuring & numbering all the casing joints)
      2.  Inspect casing
      3. Check inside the shoe & make sure there is no rubbish in it
      4. Mark casing with white paint every one meter from top of 30” wellhead housing to
        5 meter below
      5. Also from shoe to 5 meter above it
      6. Check housing measurement, diameter & length
      7. Make up cementing assembly and rack in derrick.
      8. Make up the running tool to 1 stand landing string and rack in derrick.
      9.  Pick up LP Housing joint to rig floor and install CART with pup joint for handling
        and lay down.
     10. Set of burning cutting gear on rig floor to remove pad eyes.
     11. Grinder with cutting disk for anti rotation tabs.
     12. Nipple PGB on main deck
     13. Write numbers on guide post with black paint starting from N° 1 guide post is
         starboard side forward and then clockwise
     14. Check slope indicator.
     15. Install 4 slings with 5 Ton SWL, shackles to move it with BOP crane onto trolley.
     16. Move trolley to well center
     17. Connect guide lines into guide post.


  Pipe deck:
                  1. 2 ropes size ¾” * 20 meter long for tag line
                  2. 4 slings 6 meter long & 4 shackles with SWL 5 T
                  3. 1 set of 2 slings 10 meter long 10 T SWL




         Cellar Deck:
                  1. 4 life jackets
                  2. 2 safety belts
                  3. 2 stop chute safety line device
                  4. 1 riding belt

        RUNNING PROCEDURE FOR 30” CSG


      •  Conduct toolbox meeting w/ crew & 3rd party.
      •   All PTW completed and signed, prior to toolbox meeting.
      •  Have ROV ready
      •  2 x 30” elevators, one set use as dog collar or safety clamp.
      •   Slips & bushings, CSG tongs set up for 30” (power & manual tongs).
      •  Spare CSG “O“ rings, Oil and rags.
      •  5.“C “ plate for stinger, slips & false rotary.
      •  Connect Ezy torque line to back up tong.
      •  Change to 30” CSG side door elevator.
      •  R/up power tong to m/up snub post, if using.
      •  Place shoe Jt. on catwalk & lift up to rig floor with crane.
      •   Link tilt and latch elevators, release link tilt to normal mode position.
      •  Pick up, tailing in shoe joint with crane, using rope at V door and racking arm to

          assist control of casing on rig floor. Remove crane and slings.
      •  Rig up false rotary table
      •  Run into rotary, start pump & check float shoe.
      •  Land joint in slips use 30” pick-up elevators as dog collar,
      •  Pick up next joint and stab in.
      •  Put on back up tong & take up the slack on Ezy torque line.
      •   Install power tong & m/up connections to 35000ft/lbs or use manual tongs.
      •  Enter shoe joint into PGB, install tuggers as required and raise PGB to allow the

           installation of guide ropes, lower PGB back on trolley and remove tuggers.
      •    Fill CSG every joint after 1st joint is below sea level.
      •  Continue running 30” CSG.
      •  Change to DP elevators & pick up LP well head off catwalk & make up..
      •   Position “C“ plate & false rotary on top of well head,&
      •  Run d/p stinger using 2 sets of 5” elevators & “C” Plate

  •  Pick up CART tool and make up to stinger and LP housing. Paint white mark. Remove

     1x 2” plug from top of CART. Perform pick up test before removing false rotary.
  •  Run 30” housing and stab into PGB, Wellhead engineer to be in attendance.
  •  Make pick up test on PGB and remove trolley. Continue to run landing string until the

    PGB is just below the water line then pump and fill casing. Observe the 2” on CART for

      returns. Once full pull back to a safe distance above sea level and send man to replace

     missing 2” plug. Continue to run with landing string.

      •  Have ROV assist you in entering hole & activate compensator.
      •   After 30” shoe is stabbed into well, proceed running casing.
      •   Calculate DP space out and insert cementing stand before land off.
      •  Have ROV assist and monitor closely when PGB is approaching TGB.
      •   Activate compensator.
      •  Land off TGB.
      •  Perform cementing according to operators specs, testing surface lines, plug valves

          etc.
      •  ROV to monitor for returns/ cement.
      •  When cement job is performed, close plug valve,
      •  Knock of cement line and check for back flow.
      •  When OK unscrew “30” running tool, wash wellhead.
      •  Leave DSC unlocked as you pull out of casing, once out of casing lock DSC.
      •  Commence out of the hole until landing string & running tool is out of hole.
      •  Break off running tool, lay out, clean rig floor and tools.
