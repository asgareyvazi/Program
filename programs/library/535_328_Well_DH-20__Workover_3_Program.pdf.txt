1. Introduction


     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers workover of well No.20, of Dehluran oil field, I.R.Iran. The well is
              intended to produce oil from Sarvak formation. The well is in closed position right now
             with 1750 psi wellhead pressure.

            Problem

               Salt water production is the main problem of this well.

             Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of
             workover request no. ٦٨ / ٣١/-٣ ٤٠٠ ,dated: ٦٨٣١ /٢٠ / ٤٠ and letter no. 14889 dated:
           ٦٨٣١ / ٣٠ / ٦١ of petroleum engineering department.

           The main head lines of this program are as follow:

                1.    Move and R/U Workover rig.
                2.     Record wellhead and annuluses pressures.
                3.     Complete wellhead.
                4.    Do flow the well and run PSP log.
                5.     Displace hole with proper mud.
                6.     Secure well, L/D wellhead, N/U BOP's.
                7.      Pull out & L/D completion string.
                8.    Check inside and clean well down to TD.
                9.    Run GR log to evaluate top of formations and layers.
              10.    Displace well by gas oil, flow the well and record flowing data.
              11.   Run Completion string.
              12.   L/D BOP's, N/U wellhead.
              13.    Release Rig.

              Attentions:

                •  In case of impossible well flowing or producing high volume of water,
                  complementary program will be offered in due time.

                •  Considering the GR logging result, drilling into 4-1/8" hole-section down to
                   deeper TD may be needed.

                •  The detailed program is illustrated here after in this document under the
                 head of "Workover Program".

2. Workover Program


   2.1  Sequence of Workover Operation


         1 Move workover rig to the well site of DH20, R/U and make it ready for operation. Record
               rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea level. All depths are recorded
            based on GLE=174m & RTE=182m. Do correct depths if necessary.

         2  The well head spools, valves & P-Seals and x-mas tree must be checked and serviced
            by well head specialist and be repaired if needed.

         3  Complete the well head as per after workover-well head sketch. Test same to
              4500psi.

         4  Connect killing & flowing lines to 7-1/16"-5000 dipping valve and swept bend outlet
               respectively. Connect return line to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test
                    all to 4500 psi pressure.

         5  Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 7" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x 13-3/8"
             annulus and 13-3/8" x 18-5/8" annulus.  If necessary, release  / bleed off pressures,
              monitor their build up and investigate for the probable relations.

             Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
             while relation investigation.

         6  Rig up wireline equipment. Perform a TD check operation using wireline facilities.

         7  Open SSD, displace inside tubing by gasoil  if necessary (already the well column
            should be full of gasoil and the well head pressure is 1500psi), excepted the last
          100m which should be displaced by fresh water, close SSD and test inside tubing to
              4500psi.

             Note: Considering existence of a column of 72pcf salt water under SSD to The
            TD; the 2143m column of gasoil creates ±453psi under pressure to the reservoir
       @ top perforations.

         8  Do flow the well and run PSP log (GR + CCL included) simultaneously against the
              perforated interval and into open hole-section to determine the water producing layer
                 for accurate future decision.

             Note: Catch sample for analysis.

         9  Shall the well be static, follow Item No.9; otherwise, bullhead the well by pumping 70pcf
                 salt water which its full column imposes 218psi over pressure to the reservoir @ top
               perforations (3954.8m). Shall could not able to bullhead, lubricate the well and make
             sure about well condition.

             Note: Monitor the pressure behaviour of annuluses all the time.

10 Rig up wireline equipment; test same and lubricators to max 4500psi pressure. Install

11 While Circulating cross well head, install «6-5/16"» two way back pressure valve into
   11" x 9-5/8" neck x 7" VAM "FBB CAMERON" tubing hanger and test to 4500psi
    pressure.

    in tubing hanger, it is recommended to install plug into dummy of 3SV landing
   nipple or to install 3SV in closed position prior to L/D well head.

12 While circulating cross tubing head spool, N/D well head to top of 13-5/8"-3000 x 11" -
   5000 tubing head spool. N/U 13-5/8" BOP's stack; test same to 4500psi against two-
   way. Retrieve 6-5/16" two way back pressure valve from tubing hanger.

   Note: Take care to provide 11"x13-5/8"-5000 adaptor tie down flange to connect
   BOP's to tubing head spool.

13 Connect to tubing hanger, unscrew tie down screws, sting out anchor seal unit model
   «KC22» from « SAB-3 » packer and circulate 90pcf salt water to be balanced in/out
   mud weight. Observe well for any kick or mud losses.

14 Install 2.750" plug into SSD and test same to 4500psi pressure. While circulating cross
    tubing head spool, pull out completion string; check all components for any damage to
   be fixed or replaced by new one on time.

15 RIH with 8-1/2" E-mill to liner lap @ 2133m.

16 RIH with 5-7/8"E-mill to top of packer @ 2158.4m. Mill out same and push it down to
    5"Liner Lap @ 2762m (Take care about mill out extension, one joint 5" tubing & 5"
   x 3-1/2" x/over under packer). Pull out of hole. L/D mill.

   Note: Adjust mud weight in accordance to well condition.

17 RIH with 6" x 3" taper tap to top of the felt down packer. Work on taper tap to engage
    with packer. Pull out same and its tail. L/D them all.

18 RIH with 5-7/8" E.mill to top of 5" liner lap @ 2762m. POOH, L/D mill.

19 RIH with 4-1/8" E.mill to 4000m. Clean hole-section down to TD @ 4010m using 70pcf
   mud.

   Attention: It maybe necessary to plug the probable water producing intervals by
   cement injection or to cover it by 3-1/2" liner which should be decided for in due
   time prior to cleaning down to TD and/or drilling a new interval.

20 Run GR log to confirm the top of layer 3-2 of Sarvak formation.
