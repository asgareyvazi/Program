                Gunk Pill Pumping Procedure





1-  Keep string at shoe depth
2-  Install Kelly Cock safety Valve on string
3-  Close Hydrill
4-  Prepare 100 bbls of Hi-Vis pill in one of the mud tanks by adding HYDRO-ZAN of HYDRO-CAP XP
    at range of 1-1.5 lb/bbl.
5-  Add 54 bbls of diesel in slug tank (make sure the tanks is empty prior to add diesel in the tank)
6- Pump 10 bbls of Hi-Vis by rig pump into drill string
7- Pump 10 bbls of diesel by rig pump into the drill string
8-  Stop rig pimp
9-  44 bbls of diesel will remain in slug tank.
10- Add 7 MT of Bentonite into slug tank to prepare Gunk Pill, the final volume became as 60 bbls
11- Keep agitators run to suspend Bentonite in diesel
12- Pump all 60 bbls of Diesel/ Bentonite pill into drill string
13- Dump dead volume and add 10 bbls pump-able of diesel in slug tank
14- Pump 10 bbls of diesel behind the Gunk Pill
15- Follow up by 10 bbls of Hi-Vis Pill and displace by seawater based on surface line volume to
    displace Gunk plus tail spacer from surface line and send spacer into the drill string
16- Close Kelly Cock Safety valve to avoid u-tube effect because of higher weight of Gunk pill
   compare to the sea water.
17- Switch drill string into cement unit
18- Displace the Gunk by sea water to reach Gunk to the end the string at rate of 4-5 bbls/min
19- don’t disconnect the cement unit from drill string
20- Connect rig pump to the top kill line
21- Displace Gunk at rate at 4-5 bbls/min and simultaneously pump sea water by rig pump into the
    close annulus with same rate of Gunk pill
22- Pumping should be continued till all the Gunk pill plus tail spacer has been pumped out of drill
    string
23- Keep pumping for 20 min with 2 bpm from both sides to displace the Gunk into the thief zone.
24- Monitor SICPP for any increasing.
25- If it has not been increased it means the thief zone still has not been cured
26- Repeat the above procedures but in step 7 utilize 8 Mt of Bentonite instead of 7 MT.
27- This procedure should be followed until there is some indication of increase pressure into the
    annulus.
28- If pressure increased in close annulus for 100-200 psi, open Hy-drill and pull drill string for 2-4
    stands.
29- Circulate sea water and monitor volume for down-hole losses.
