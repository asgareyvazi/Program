Electrical Back off Operation Job Procedure
Rig-O3, AZR#18, 11-Oct-2018
Back off should be done in joint that more than 80% free by torque or tension measurement.
Back off point: 4291.6 m.
Calculate BO weight = Normal weight of string at BO depth in MUD + (5% Excess) + weight of block
BO weight: 313 Klbs.
Maximum safe pull: 380 Klbs.
Applied maximum right hand turn (10 kft.lb) in order to retighten all possible loose connection meanwhile reciprocate string to transfer down.
Select No. Primacord strands from chart, Add 2 more strand extra to give a better shock.
Strand No.: 8-10
Making up the shooting tool string ready.
Hold pre job safety meeting.
Conduct radio silence before arming the explosive material.
Load explosive material.
Measure the length from CCL to middle of the primacord (shot).
RIH with wire line.
Depth correlation by CCL.
Applied 6 Klb.ft Left Hand Turn, 40% less Right Hand Turn and reciprocate string between MSP and BO weight. (380/320 klbs on weight indicator)
Position the gun at the joint which is intended to back off (back off point).
Resume Left Hand Torque to 7 Kft.lb (70% of Max Right Hand) while stay at 313 klbs on weight indicator.
Secure the rig floor & Fire the explosive.
Follow the left hand turn in case of rotating string after shot.
POOH wire line 3-4m from back off point.
Check the string weight in order to back off confirmation.
Pick up string about 1.5m.
Take the log & POOH wire line to surface.
Good luck & be safe
