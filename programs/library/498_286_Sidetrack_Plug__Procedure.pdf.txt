        SIDETRACK PLUG PROCEDURE

    1)  PU 3 ½ diverter and 956.53’ stinger – xo – ball catcher sub. RIH w/155 stds + 15’ pup
      + stinger to +/-15700’.
             •  Slack off spd will be no faster than 30 sec/std.
             •  Stage pumps up 30 SPM/5 min, 60 spm/5 min, etc… up to 600 gpm at 8500’,
                 11000’, 13000’, 14000’ and on btm.
             •  Pump vol above wellhead at ea stage before continuing in hole.
             • CMT head Assy =15’-pup Jt., TIW, Side entry, TIW, 1 or 2 pup joints.

    2)   Circ btms up.  Reciprocate and rotate pipe.

    3)  RU lines and test to 5000 psi.
             •  Hold 500-psi on IBOP.

    4)  Pump 50 bbls 17.8 ppg spacer. Mix and spot a (230 sk) 395’ plug from 15700’ to
        15305’. Place cmt with Halco unit. 10 bbl spacer + 303 bbl mud = top of the 3 ½
          stinger. Lay out pup on cmt assy.

    5)  POOH slow for 5 stds.  If cmt does not slug, pump 3/4 bbl per std for 5 stds.

    6)   Pull 5 more stds to 14830’ (146 stds in hole, count stds). Stage pumps up and circulate
          at 650 gpm until annular volume is above well head . spot 90 bbls of 16# mud in open
        hole from 14830’ to 14010’ (put 285 bbls behind 16#, 2317 stks). Pull 8 more stds to
        13975’ (137 stds in hole, count stds). Stage pumps up and circulate bttms up at 650
       gpm.

    7)  PU cmt assy. Load 5” ball above upper TIW.
             • CMT head Assy. In step 1.
             •  Hold 500-psi on IBOP.

    8)  Pump 30 bbls 13.7 ppg spacer. Mix and spot a (451 sk) 700’ plug from 13975’ to
        13275’. Place cmt with Halco unit – pump 10 bbl spacer, bleed pressure off IBOP, do
        not drain stand pipe. Kick ball out behind Spacer with 2-bbl mud with rig pumps
       and go back to Halco (266 bbl Mud = top of 3 ½ DP). Snub Side entry sub and rotate
        pipe at 30-RPM while spotting cmt.

    9)  POOH slow for 8 stds. Pump 3/4 bbl per std for 8 stds.

   10)  Pull 10 more stds to 12260’. Blow ball into catcher.

   11) Pump slug (easy) and pooh.

Pump Data:  6 ” Liners – Pop Off = 6200 psi.  Pump Factor = 0.123 BPS
