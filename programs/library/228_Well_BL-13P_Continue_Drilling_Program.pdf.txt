1  Introduction

This program covers the information and procedures required for drilling 6‐1/8” hole section

of the well BL‐13P‐RE1 in Balal Field ‐ Persian Gulf ‐ IRAN.



Main Objective:

 The main objective of performing complementary drilling on this well is to drill out cement plug

inside 9-5/8” casing & 7” liner and continue drilling 6-1/8” section  to total depth of ±2390m. 5”

liner is to be run off bottom at LP and complete well with ESP as Arab-6 horizontal oil producer.

    1.  Skid over BL-13P and record RKB-THF & RKB-MSL.

    7. D/O shoe track and 7” shoe @ 1681m,MD.

    8.  Directional drilling 6-1/8” hole below shoe with 74pcf mud to 2058m,MD(Top of Arab-

      A6) with MWD/GR/ROP.

    9. P/U LWD & Cont. Directional drilling 6-1/8” hole F/ 2058m,MD to 2390m,MD (TD).

    10. Take Full Suite Log Arab-A1 to Arab-A6 (TD).

    11. Set Magneset plug F/ 2174m T/1925m & dress to 2074m,MD (5” liner point).

    12. Run & Cement 5” Liner (1582-2074m,MD).

    13. D/O shoe track, 5” shoe and magneset below the shoe. Condition hole to 2390m,MD

    14. Scarpe 7" & 5" LNRs, circulate to clean hole.

    15. Take SBT log from 5" LNR by TLC.

    16. Run Coil tubing and wash magneset in open hole by acid.

    17. Run ESP completion string.

    18. N/D BOP, N/U 3-1/8" 3K X-Mass tree, P/T p-seal.

    19. Start ESP & let the well flow and be cleaned. Secure the well & Release the rig.

11 Well Securing Procedure


    1. Move rig to BALAL platform, skid on well BL-13P.

    2.  Record RKB-MSL & RKB-THF of new well.

    3. Hook up kill lines to Ann. & TBG sides and test same to 2500psi.

    4.  Record TBG & Ann pressure. In case of any pressure, release pressure. Monitor well for
       build -up.

      Note: Based on DDRs BPV is inside hanger, Check same, otherwise set BPV inside
      tubing hanger (11" * 3-1/2", type: CXS) after monitoring pressure.
