4     Operational Sequence

             1. The Unitized well head assembly will be R/U in advance
             2. R/U 13 5/8” 10,000 psi BOP and LP riser
             3.  Test BOP to 240 bar
             4.  Install Long Bore Protector / wear bushing
             5. M/U 12 ¼” motor/ MWD BHA on 5 ½” DP and RIH
             6.  Perform well control and H2S drill prior to drill out of casing
             7.  Drill out casing shoe track and 3m new formation
             8.  Circulate hole and perform FIT to 1.60 SG.
             9.  Drill well until bit trip required
            10. POOH
                    If gas has been observed, consider to pump out.
            11. Change BHA components as required. RIH with HWDP BHA. Take 3 overlapping sur-
                veys.
            12. Drill well to section TD; 25 mMD into the Upper Kangan formation
            13. Circulate hole clean
            14. POOH
                    If gas has been observed, consider to pump out.
            15. Pull Long Bore Protector / wear bushing
            16. Run and cement 9 5/8” x 10 ¾” casing
            17. Test casing to 300 bar when bumping plug
            18. Set and test seal assembly to 285 bar
            19. Install shallow set GT mechanical plug at first 9 5/8’’ casing joint if movable HC is en-
                countered. Test plug to 300 bar from above


5     Stuck pipe

         Differential sticking has occurred both on SPD7 and SPD8 wells
           If differential stuck:
             •  -IMMEDIATELY HIT JAR UPWARDS WITH MAX ALLOWABLE O/P
                -Next step if off bottomÎ Max down weight & torque to get free
             •  Geologist to evaluate maximum pore pressure and potential permable zones to verify
        MW reduction.
             •  Consider to pump SW pill to reduce hydrostatic pressure

        In well SPD9-07T2 the bit got stuck while backreaming. The pipe came free after pumping
      22% HCl acid. Be aware of potential large volume of gas created when acid reacts with carbon-
          ates.

                avoid EMS.

                           STANDARD
                            LOST CIRCULATION RECIPE

                SEEPAGE LOSS SOLUTIONS ( < 3 M3/ HR )
Initial attempts to solve losses should be made by pre-treating the system with CaCo3 FM at 10 kg/m3
each and 6 kg/m3 of Mix II F or EPI Seal XF . If losses continued, then pump concentrated pill of the
followings;

                     During drilling & (3 M3/hr< losses <15 M3/hr)
                                8 m3 Pill @ 60 kg/m3
                 Product            Concentration ( kg/m3 )
                   CaCo3 F                     10
                  CaCo3 M                     10
                   Mix II F                     10
                   Mix II M                     10
                     Mica F                      5
                   EPI Seal XF                    10
                 Kwik Seal F                    5

                  While POOH and HIGH LOSS RATE ( >15 m3/ HR )
       8 m3 Pill @ 114 KG/M3 ( maximum, as per BHI MWD/Motor tools Limitation )
                 Product            Concentration ( kg/m3 )
                   CaCo3 F                     20
                  CaCo3 M                     20
                   Mix II F                     20
                   Mix II M                     20
                     Mica F                      5
                   EPI Seal XF                    25
                    KwikSeal F                     5

Procedure
       •  Spot in open hole, Pumping rate through MWD/Motor: 1000lpm
       •   Pull above loss zone and circulate to squeeze bridging material to fractures.
       •  Slowly increase circulation to minimum rate required for drilling.
       •  Increase to 3700 lpm to clean the tool

   Note:

    1. Mud Engineer to supervise the mixing of LCM pills.

     2.  Mud Engineer to visually inspect all LCM being used.

     3.  Mud Engineer to make a clear instruction to the rig mixing crew that if they see any change in size of a par-
         ticular LCM grade confirmed initially they must STOP mixing and inform the mud engineer. Would prefer to
        put laminated instruction at mixing area.

     4.  Company representative, mud engineer and MWD/DD engineers need to agree on the size of LCM mate-
           rial and the above concentrations prior to pumping to avoid plug tools.

     5.  Mud Engineer to ensure no Coarse LCM to be mixed in any pills, unless a circulation sub is put in the BHA.
        Otherwise, ONLY fine and Medium grades are required.

     6.  To avoid confusion, Mud engineer to backload any Coarse LCM if circulation sub will not be used.

    7.   If circulation sub is used, coarse materials such as Nut Plug, Kwik Seal M and CaCo3 can be utilized. This
          is only during severe downhole losses.
