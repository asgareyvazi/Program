1.0 Purpose

    To establish procedures for carrying out formation tests.


1.1 Scope

      All drilling units.


1.2 Objectives

     The objective of the leak-off test is not to fracture the formation, but to measure
      the pressure at which it begins to take fluid (the formation intake pressure). It is
      important that pumping is stopped when there is a deviation from the trend as
      further pumping could cause formation breakdown, possible permanently
      reducing its strength.


1.3 Action prior to test

      Prior to testing the formation just below the casing shoe the following will have
     been carried out:
       -   Drilled out shoe, rathole.
       -  Mud circulated and conditioned and of uniform weight.
       -   Bit pulled back into shoe.
       -  Well shut in on pipe rams (string hung off on subsea stacks).
       -  High pressure, low volume pump such as cement pump lined up.

1.4 Procedure - Leak-off Test


1.4.1  Pump down annulus or string or both together (Rig Superintendent to decide
      depending on string configuration) in increments of 0.1 to 0.25 barrels.

1.4.2  After each increment pumped, stop and record final pumping pressure. Wait at
        least 2 minutes for pressures to stabilise. Record final static pressure. Plot both
       against cumulative pumped volume and a graph.

1.4.2 and 1.4.3 may be replaced by slow continuous pumping (0.3 - 0.5 bbl/min)
monitoring pressure and volume.

1.4.3  Repeat until the trend of fluid pumping pressure curve deviates from that of
        final static pressure curve.


1.4.4  Monitor final static pressure for at least another 5 minutes.


1.4.5  Bleed off pressure and record volume flow back.

      Compare volume pumped with volume bled off.

1.5 Procedure - Formation Integrity Test


1.5.1  Determine the upper pressure limit for the test, from the maximum mud
       weight for the next hole section.

      Test Pressure psi = (Maximum MW - Current MW) x Shoe TVD x 0.052



1.5.2  Pump slowly (0.3 - 0.5 bbl/min). Monitor pressure build up.


1.5.3  When the required test pressure is reached, stop the pump.


1.5.4  Monitor pressure for 5 - 10 minutes.


1.5.5  Bleed pressure off. Compare volume pumped with volume bled off.

1.6 Responsibilities


1.6.1 Rig Superintendent

       Overall responsibility for the installation.
       Ensures test is carried out as per well plan.


1.6.2 Toolpusher

       In charge of operation.
       Ensures all personnel are aware of procedures and their responsibilities.
      During test is at pump monitoring and recording pressures and volumes.



1.6.3  Driller

       In charge of drill crew.
     On drill floor.


1.6.4 Cementer

       In charge of pump.

1.7 Calculations


1.7.1  Fracture Pressure (psi)

       F.P. (psi) = (Mud Wt (ppg) x Shoe TVD (ft) x 0.052) + LOT Pressure (psi)


1.7.2 Maximum Allowable Mud Weight (ppg)

   MAMW (ppg) = ( LOT (psi) Shoe TVD (ft)  0.052) + Test Mud Wt
       (ppg)



1.7.3 Maximum Allowable Annular Surface Pressure (psi)

    MAASP (psi) = (MAMW (ppg) - Mud Wt (ppg)) x Shoe TVD (ft) x 0.052
