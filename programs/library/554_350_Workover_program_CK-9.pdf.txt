1. INTRODUCTION
  1.1 INTRODUCTION

THIS PROGRAM COVERS WORK OVER OF WELL CHESHMEH KHOSH-9, CHESHMEH KHOSH FIELD, NORTH
OF DEZFUL, IRAN. THE WELL IS PRODUCING OIL FROM ASMARI FORMATION.
THIS WELL WILL REPAIR TO REMOVE OBSTRUCTION IN THE WELL COLUME & OBSERVE 7" LINER LAP &
9-5/8" CASING.
THE WORK OVER PROGRAM IS PREPARED ACCORDING TO LETTER NO.86/13-3/001 (27.1.86) FROM
WEST PRODUCTION COMPANY.
A REMEDIAL PLAN WAS ARRANGED TO REACH BELLOW PURPOSES:

•  RIGGING UP THE RIG ON CK-9 THEN CHECK AND RECORD WELL HEAD PRESSURES.
•  KILL THE WELL BY WATER (63PCF).
•  NIPPLE DOWN WELL HEAD & N/UP BOP.
•  PULL OUT & LAY DOWN URBAN COMPLETION STRING.
•  CLEAN THE WELL TO TOP OF OBSTRACTION @ 2300MT THEN CLEAN WELL TO "TD" WITH
   RECHECK 7"LINER (LAP) & 9 5/8"CASING.
•  RUN URBAN COMPLETION STRING & COMPLETE WELL HEAD AS ATTACHMENT.
•  FLOWING THE WELL ON PROPER CHOKE SIZES & RECORD THE FLOWING PRESSURES.
•  SECURE THE WELL AND RELEASE THE RIG.

NOTE: In case of encountering 9-5/8” casing collapse, the workover of the well will be depend
on preparing of the required equipment.

lb/ft + 90 joints 13-3/8", 72 lb/ft) was run to 1871.1 m and cemented as program (return 50 bbl cement on
surface).
* Drilling problems:
       - There were washout in string at 1164, 1210, 1461, 1520, 1639 & 1778 m.
       - Pipes stuck at 1462 m & get free after work on it.
       - Mud weight increased gradually to 86 pcf due to tight hole at 1164, 1461, 1462 & 1865 m.

12 1/4" Hole Size:
Drilling with 12 1/4" bit and 126 to 146 pcf mud weight to 3448 m. GR/CDR log was run. 9 5/8" casing (249
joints, 53.5lb/ft, 93 Joints, 58.4 lb/ft) was run to 3448 m and cemented as program without return cement
on surface.
* Drilling problems:
       - 40 bbl/hr losses occurred at 3412m.
       - Mud weight increased gradually to 146 pcf due to tight hole at 1937, 2000, 2176, 2346, 2415, 2500
& 2750 m.

8 3/8" Hole Size:
Drilling with 8 3/8" bit and 64 to 75 pcf mud weight to 3520 m. DST test was done from ASMARI formation
and then drilling continued to 4294 m while increased mud weight gradually to 93 pcf. The VSP/DSI/RFT/
CCL/ VDL/ CBL/UBI/OBMI/LDL/BHC/AIT/CNL/NGS/GR logs was run & recorded in hole.Cement plug back
was set at 3992 to 3754m, 3669 to 3514m & 3514 to 3467m.

Azim = 46.3◦, TVD = 3574.35 m) with 75 pcf mud weight. 7" liner (26 joints, 29 lb/ft) was run from 3638 to
3384 m and cemented as program with observing shearing pressure.

       - There was washout in string at 3596m.

1.2.1.2. PRODUCTION TEST HISTORY

After drilling and prepared the well on summery 84, the well started to produce oil with salt.
At date 1385/11/14 production was stopped due to decreasing well head pressure to zero.
Following operation was done to investigate well problem:
   1- At date 1385/11/18 & 19 ran in hole with wire line equipments and indicated
       obstruction at 2300m. It is tried to pass from this point for several times no success.
      Sample got from obstruction and send to NISOC laboratory to analysis its. Test results
       received from NISOC At date 1385/12/21 (for more information refer to production
        field documents)
   2- The well flowed for several times but well head pressure decrease from 2500 – 3000
       psi to zero after 1 min (pure gas observed at each step).
   3- At date 1385/11/30 tried to remove obstruction by using gasoil with 4000 psi
       pressure, no success.
   4- At date 1386/1/4, observed oil leakage from VR-PLUG of 9-5/8"×13-3/8" TBG SPL,
      leakage was eliminate after releasing pressure from 2500 psi to zero, installing 2-
      1/16"-5000 gate valve and fasten VR-PLUG. Other well head equipments checked that
      they were Ok.
   5- During 1386/1/22 to 1386/1/24 coiled tubing was rigged up on well and following
       operations were done:
   5-1- At date 1386/1/22 ran in hole while pumping gasoil to 2275 m and tried to pass with
    injected 150 bbl gasoil and 4000 psi back pressure, no success.
   5-2- At date 1386/1/23 tried to remove obstruction with water injection without any
   progress.
   5-3- According to results of sample analysis, spot 200 gal 15% chloride acid on obstruction
   with applying 4000 psi back pressure on it, but there was not any effective progress on
   obstruction after 1 hr. Due to no more progress diluted chloride acid with 50 bbl water and
   released coiled tubing.

2.7 Preparation

                   It is planned to Kill the well and cure the loss, N/U the BOP and & pull out urban completion
                 string, cleaned hole then run urban completion string as program. Flow the well on proper
             choke sizes, secure the well and finally release the rig.


            2.7.1 Standards and Preparation of the Operations

                     •  Make sure that the whole operation is based on good and safe work over operation
                          practices.
                     •  After bullhead well, BPV plus related running tools, wear bushing and plug tester,
                    8 3/8" J.mill & E.mill with necessary X-Over are on board. For more detail, refer to
                    work over plan.
                     •  Prior to start operation, hold a safety/Pre-spud meeting with rig crews and service
                     hands to review the work over program and potential problems. Note meetings in
                        tour book. Ensure that Oil & gas monitors are set up. Ensure that all safety devices
                       are properly installed and functioning.

                 Note: Before spud the rig follow pre-Spud check list.

                 Note: Before spud the rig make sure the suitable adaptor for nipple up the
             BOP (13-5/8"-5000) above the 13 5/8"-3000×11"-5000 Tubing head spool
                 are available on rig site.

              2.7.2  Pre-Running Checklist

                    •   Pre Completion Meeting to be held with all Rig Personnel.
                    •  The formation could cause mud losses at any time. Mud volume displaced must be
                  checked against the completion displacement and accurate trip records must be kept at
                           all times.
                    •   Although there  is positive plug in string while running in hole, but the correct
                   crossover from tubing thread to 5” IF drill pipe must be available on the rig floor at all
                   times when handling tubing. Also a safety valve are required on the rig floor all time.
                    •   Special precautions need to be taken when running the control line outside tubing
                  (due to difficulty in sealing with the annular preventor) and when running wire line.
                 These  situations must be discussed by the crew &  all responsible personnel  to
               commence running the completion.

2.8 Completion Running Procedure
This program assumes that the packer and tailpipe are run with the rest of the
completion.


                    •  Make up and test pre-assemblies (in workshop as per manufacturer’s procedures).
                    •    All equipment to be checked for any transport damage.
                    •    All OD’s and ID'S to be checked against the  checklist sheets sent from the
                  workshop. All assemblies and tubing to be drifted with respective sizes prior to picking
                up to the rig floor. It is recommended that after testing, plugs and other flow control
                    accessories are installed in the respective flow control nipples to ensure that the plugs
                  can and will be installed properly down hole.
                    •   Prior to running the completion, RIH with 9-5/8” casing scraper to 250 m.
                    •   Install the XN (3.813'') plug into the top No-Go nipple, then the prong and
                   pressure test to 4500psi. plug in SSD "R" & test string against this plug)
                    •   Check  well  control  precautions (see top  of page)  are  in  place. Run Urban
                   completion same as before. Tail pipe length is approx. 14.20 m.
                 Note that the torque requirements for the 4 ½” string (13.5 lb/ft tubing, 13.5 lb/ft
                   crossovers and  completion equipment) and  the  7”  string  (29  lb/ft  tubing and
                   completion equipment) are:
                    •   Torque figures: 4-1/2” 13.5 lb/ft New Vam 75-80-85 KSI
                                       Min. 3780 ft/lbs; opt. 4200 ft/lbs; Max. 4620 ft/lbs
                     Torque figures: 7” 29 lb/ft New Vam 75-80-85 KSI
                                   Min 8460ft/lbs; Opt. 9400 ft/lbs; Max. 10340 ft/lbs
                    •   Also note that the correct API modified thread lubricant must be used. Do not use
                 any more thread lubricant than necessary, the completion eng. should demonstrate
                     correct use of thread lubricant (using paint brush, not dope brush and applying to pin
                 and only the shoulder of the box).
                    •  Make up 9 5/8” packer assembly and set the assembly in the slips.
                    •  Remove shear screws, and install the packer test clamp (segments).
                NOTE: A dog collar clamp or safety clamp should be used at all times until there is
                       sufficient weight on the slips to ensure that the tubing string is held securely in the
                     rotary table during each connection.
                    •    Fill assembly with 63 PCF high PH water.
                    •   Pressure test assembly to 4,500 psi for 15 minutes.
                    •   Bleed off pressure slowly.
                    •   Dismantle surface test fittings and bull plug and install shear screws.
                    •   Clear the rig floor and make ready to run the rest of the 9 5/8” x 7” Urban
                   completion string.
                    •  Run the packer slowly through the BOP’s.Once the packer is in the casing, do not
                  run the packer faster than 30 foot joint -from slips out to slips in - per 4 minute.
                NOTE: Monitor the trip tank continuously while running completion string plugged. Mud
                 volume displaced must be checked against the completion displacement and accurate
                        trip records must be kept at all times. Fluid level must be monitored continuously. A
                    contractor Toolpusher or supervisor must be on the rig floor at all times and must keep
                   the ICOFC Company Man and Completion Engineer notified of any changes in well
                     status.

                    •  RIH  adding  additional  completion equipment and 7”×9  5/8”  tubing  as  per
                   (Completion diagram), fill the tubing with high PH water.

                NOTE: Ensure the 5” elevators and slips are on the rig floor and ready, when the 5”
                    cross over is reached. Recheck well control precautions .
                    •  Make up the Landing nipple and connect the control line. Pressure test the control
                        line against the dummy already installed to 4,500 psi. Bleed off the pressure to 1,000
                      psi and maintain this pressure while running in the hole.

                NOTE: Extra care must be taken when running the tubing from this point to ensure
                     that:
                    •   Well control precautions are  in place. Because of the control  line outside the
                    tubing, the annular BOP may not seal properly if it is needed.
                    •  The control line is not crushed when setting the slips.
                    •  The required depth of the safety valve nipple is to be +/- 64 m.
                    •   Continue to run in hole until at setting depth clamping or strapping the control line
                      tight to the tubing.
                    •   Install first fluted adaptor (Box by Pin) above the upper 7” flow coupling of the
                SSSV landing nipple, the second above two joints of tubing and the last one above
                   another three joints.
                    •  Run last 5 joints of tubing. RIH to the intended packer setting depth of +/- 200m.
                Make up the Pin by Pin cross-over between the last tubing joint and the tubing hanger.
                Make up the tubing hanger. Make up 7” tubing landing joint(s) to tubing hanger as per
              MSP DRILEX procedures. Run the string in to depth where the tubing hanger can be
                 worked on from the rig floor.
                    •   Bleed off the pressure in the control line and terminate. Dress off the cut control
                        line and make up to the tubing hanger. It is recommended that a minimum of three
                 wraps around the tubing are used prior to connecting the control line to the tubing
                   hanger. Connect a temporary control line to above the hanger. Pressure test the
                     control line again to 4,500 psi to make sure  it’s holding pressure OK. Bleed off the
                   pressure and disconnect the control line.
                    •  Run the tubing hanger below the rotary table and run the landing joint through the
                      rotary, stopping with the tubing hanger 10 ft above its final position.
                    •  Make up circulating head to the tubing and pressure up on the tubing to set the
                    packer. The packer should set at +/- 1,500 psi. Increase pressure to 2,500 psi and
                   hold and record for 15 minutes. Then increase the pressure on tubing to 4500 psi for a
                 30 minutes pressure test (potential acid injection pressure).
                    •   Bleed off the pressure from the tubing.
                    •   Prior to landing the tubing hanger, drain the stack and jet out to clean the tubing
                  hanger land off point. Run in hole slowly to land the tubing hanger in the tubing head
                    spool and tighten the tie – down screws.
                    •   Pressure test tubing to 4500 psi for 30 minutes.
                    •   Pressure up on the annulus to 2,000 psi for 30 minutes to confirm the packer is set
                 and holding. Ensure the tubing is open at surface when testing the annulus. Some back
                    flow will be seen from the tubing as the pressure increases due to the tubing being
                  compressed. The flow should stop once the pressure has been reached. A strong flow
                   could indicate leaking tubing, requiring remedial action.
                    •   Closed top Ram, pressure test tubing hanger to 4500 psi.
                    •   Bleed off the pressure. Unscrew and lay out landing joint(s) from tubing hanger.
                    •   Refer to item work over operation program.

DESIGN OVERVIEW
The 'VHR' Packer is a Retrievable, Single, Hydraulic-Set Packer with completion features of a large bore
permanent Packer. The Packer has been designed with a single set of bi-directional Slips below the
Elements. The Packer will hold pressure from above and below. The Packer is pulled by a straight upward
pull with the 'VRR' Retrieving Tool. Refer to the Design Specifications (Engineering Data Sheet) for details
of pressure ratings, tensile strength and related data.

To retrieve the Packer, the Production Seal Unit is pulled from the hole. The Retrieving Tool is then run
and stung into the packer. Refer to the Design Specifications for the correct Retrieving Tool. A RATCH-
LATCH on the top of the Retrieving Tool latches the Top Sub (or Head) of the Packer. An upward pull of
10,000 pounds over neutral weight is then applied to make sure
that the RA TCH - LATCH is latched in. More upward pull shears the top shear screws in the Retrieving
Tool. Next, the Retrieving Tool is lowered to allow the plugs to engage the shifting sleeve from below.
Pulling upward, shears the shear pins allowing the shifting sleeve to shift. This releases the snap ring and
allows the mandrel to move upward. After the shifting sleeve is shifted, a set of shear screws shear in the
Retrieving Tool's spring housing. Further upward pull is then transferred through the Top Sub of the Packer
which is attached to the Mandrel. A shoulder in the middle of the Mandrel picks up the Top Wedge, the Slip
Carrier, the Slips and the Bottom Wedge. A snap ring on the lower end of the Mandrel picks up the Piston,
the Cylinder, the Bottom Sub and the Hang Weight. Once the Mandrel has completed its releasing stroke,
the Packer should be fully released and the pickup weight may be noted on the Weight Indicator. Slow
upward movement for one joint of pipe is advisable to assure complete release.
PLUG FOR TUBING
The tubing string that is to be pressured at a point below the VHR packer must be plugged. Using a catcher
sub-assembly for this purpose requires installing it at the bottom of the tubing string to be pressurized. A no-
go landing nipple can be used with a wire line retrievable plug as an alternate method.
SETTING PRESSURE
Shear screws retain the packer in an unset position. Application of hydraulic setting pressure shears the
screws and the packer sets. The quantity of shear screws installed in a packer can be varied to change the
value of the required setting pressure. This value will vary with the quantity of shear screws installed and
with variations in the actual shear values of the screws.

Refer to the Engineering Design Specifications for setting pressures.

The setting pressure P is equal to:

P = (Shear value per screw, lb) (Quantity of screws) ÷ (Piston area, sq. in.)
