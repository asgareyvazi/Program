 1.   Introduction


1.1   Program Introduction

  This program covers Second workover on well No.4 of Sarkhun gas field, I.R.Iran. The
  well is gas producer from Jahrum formation.

 Problem
 The well Sarkhun-4 WO-2 needs repair due to strong seepage between Completion string
  and production casing annulus. Tubing and annulus pressure, build up to 1800 Psi.
  Primary investigations indicate that tubing has been parted at depth 24.7m, some interval
  above 3SV. The control line is damaged and 3SV can not be operated from the surface.

 Main Objective
 The main objective of this program is to fix the problem, nipple up standard well
  head, complete the well and resume gas production from the Jahrum reservoir.

  Note: This program is issued with respect to the available information of workover
  request no. 8/7 11 6/ چ ت ب , dated: 138/7 08/ 15 Zagros oil & gas production Co.

 The main head lines of this program are as follow:

  1.   Move and rig up the drilling rig.
  2.   Check wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record well head pressures; investigate
        for seepage of all the casing annuluses.
  3.   Connect and test kill, flow and return lines.
  4.   Run gage rings to specify the free pass of the tubings, attempt to perform wire line
        jobs.
  5.    Kill and secure well. ( including wire line operation)
  6.   Test all the p-seals of tubing and casing spools and fix the problems at site.
  7.   N/D well head and N/U BOP's
  8.    Pull out upper section of disconnected completion string below tubing hanger.
  9.   Attempt to perform wire line operation ( if regret to do it in primary stages of job)
  10.  Un latch the anchor seal and pull the rest of string above packer
  11.  Retrieve or mill the packer. POH the remainder of packer and its tail
  12.  Scrap and clean inside 9-5/8" casing and/or 7" liner in the new packer setting area.
  13.  Run 9 5/8” x 7” completion string
  14.  N/D BOP's and N/U well head
  15.  Flow the well
  16.  Secure the well and release rig

1.3   Drilling History
 26" Hole Section:
  Rig SAIPEM 37 spudded on location W090S and  drilling  started with 68-72 pcf
  bentonite mud down to Casing Point @ 116m with NL-NG. 18-5/8" casing were run to
 115m and cemented to surface (SW=115 pcf, 775 sx, Class "A").

  17-1/2" Hole Section:
  Drilling continued from 116m down to Casing Point @ 1409m with 68-76 pcf WBM and
 NL-NG. Run 13-3/8" casing (72#, N-80, BTS) to 1400m and cemented same (Lead: 2100
  sxs, SW=95 pcf,  / Tail: 530 sxs, SW= 115 pcf), reached cement to surface.

  12-1/4" Hole Section:
  Drilling started from 1409m down to Casing Point @ 2930m with 75 pcf WBM which
  increased gradually to 99 pcf due to tight hole while connections and formation water
  flow @ 1953m. Circulated and cleaned hole and took logs.
 Run 9-5/8" casing down to 2930m and cemented same. (SV=600 bbl's, SW=110 pcf,
  2000 sxs), no return cement to surface.

  8-1/2" Hole Section:
  Decreased mud weight to 82 pcf, and drilled out plugs, cement and shoe. Drilling
  continued with 82 pcf WBM from 2930m down to 3047m, stuck pipe @ 3047m, work on
  stuck, increased mud weight to 84pcf; worked on stuck, got free. Continued drilling and
  coring from 3047m down to 3394m with 82 pcf mud weight which increased gradually to
  92 pcf due to tight hole while connections and formation water flow. Set cement plugs to
  stop flow. Performed DST. Drilling continued to liner point @ 3683m with 87 pcf mud.
  7" Liner (32#, C-75) was run to 3681m (Liner lap @ 2826m) and cemented with 115 pcf,
  slurry.
  Perforated liner from 3425m-3445m and performed DST. Perforated new intervals in the
  liner (3215m-3235m, 3285m-3315m) and performed acid job with 1000 gal 28% HCL.
  Displaced acid by water performed DST.
  Tried to unset DST packer, but could not pull the same due to salt crystals piled up on top
  of packer. String was mechanically backed off from 3186m and pulled out of hole. Set
  bridge plug at 3152m, and dumped cement on top of it due to unsuccessful nine days
  attempt to retrieve the rest of string.

1.4   Completion History
  Well was completed by setting 9 5/8" OTIS production packer @ 2538m. 7" completion
  string then was ran and latched into the packer.
 The 7" liner below packer was perforated from 3050-3070m, with no indication of flow
  after perforation. Well was perforated again from 2935m to2990m. Application of 5700
  psi, hydraulically fractured the formation and caused the well flow. Performed Acid job
  (28%) and after clean up process the flowing well head pressure and burning line
  pressure were recorded as 4250 psi, and 200 psi respectively. The initial flow rate was 18
 MMCF/D through 1/2" choke. Rig released 1357/01/04.

1.5   Workover History

  In year 1364 the  first work over was carried out on Well SA-4 to upgrade the
  completion, since no 3SV had been considered in the old design. In this operation the
  old packer and its tail remained in the hole, and the standard completion string was
  run and latched to the packer as per drawing. Failing all attempts to remove the plug
  out of No Go nipple, 4.562” PXN Plug with Rope Socket were left in place. To find
  a mean of production, several attempts were made to open the SSD below packer but
  couldn't open the same. The 5 1/2" tubing below packer was then perforated from
 2567m to 2569.3m and  let well flow through this interval. Rig released  at
  1364/09/30. (Spud date: 1364/08/23)

1.6   production History after 1st work over

 The well was in service till 1387/07/27. While changing the choke size from ¾” to ½”
  with a sudden bang sound (as reported by the operator) the well was automaticity
  shut-down. Primary investigations by well services crew, revealed that:
   • Completion string is partially parted at 24.7m.
   • 4” gauge ring can passes through the parted joint.
   • 3SV can not be operated from surface. Most probably the control line has been
     disconnected or seriously damaged.
   • 7"tubings, 9 5/8"x 7" annulus and control line are open to each other. There is no
     wall between them!

1.7   Perforations:

 now are located below the bridge plug at 3152m so not active anymore. Perforation

2.   Workover Program

1. Move work over rig from PY-2 to the well site of Sarkhun-4. R/U and make it
   ready for operation, Record rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea
    level. (An overall check on well head spools, valves, P-Seals and x-mass tree is
    strongly recommended before rig move. After killing and securing the well, all
   items must be checked and serviced by well head crew and repaired or replaced if
    needed.)

2.  Connect kill & flow lines to the x-mass tree side outlets and connect return line to
   2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 4500 psi pressure.

3.  Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing and 7" x 9-5/8", 9-5/8" x 13-3/8"
   and 13-3/8" x 18-5/8" annuluses. Release  / Bleed off pressures, monitor their
    build up and investigate for the probable seepage.

   Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
   while investigating the communications.

4.  Test control line to 4500 psi and open 3SV. Bleed the hydraulic pressure and set
   3SV in close position.

5.  Rig up wire line equipment and test to 4500psi pressure. Run different sizes of
   gage rings and specify the free pass of tubings above 3SV.

   Regarding the  results  of item 4&  5, the work over program  is
   separated into two options. Option 1 as optimistic, and option 2 as
   pessimistic program.  If could pass through the damaged interval,
   follow the option 1 and if regret to perform wire line operation start
   from option2. READ THE PROGRAM AHEAD OF TIME AND
  PREPARE EVERYTHING REQUIRED TO PERFORM A FAST
  AND SAFE OPERATION.
   Option 1
1.  Retrieve 3SV. Flush control line and Install dummy into 3SV landing nipple
   (Type "RQL") and pressure test same to 4500 psi. Rig down wire line.

2.  Start killing the well by pumping 630 bbls fresh water (at least 1 1/2 volume of
    the tubing and casing capacity) and squeeze the tubing and casing content into the
    reservoir. The well will be under heavy loss since the hydrostatic pressure of full
   column of  fresh water  is 1010  psi over  the formation pressure  at top of
    perforations  (i.e. 2935m). Fill up (lubricate) the annulus with 213 bbls fresh
    water.
   Note: The volume of tubing, 9 5/8" casing below the tubing, and 7" liner to
   plug back depth @ 3152m is almost 420 bbls. The annular volume of tubings
   above packer is 213 bbls.

4.  Mud program
Work over operation
Fresh water applied to kill this well in the last work over at year 1364, but in some
instances they increased mud weight to 84 pcf. Regarding the reservoir information,
and considering 200 psi over pressure as safety factor 50pcf mud required to kill the
well at top of perforations i.e. 2935m. Here fresh water is selected as kill fluid but
viscosifiers and weighting materials should be available at site for emergency cases.
Weighting material should not damage the formation.
Hydrostatic pressure of a  full column of fresh water  is 1010 psi over the
formation pressure at top of perforations (2935m).

The following mud properties are considered as a basis of mud formulation for first
stage of work over operation in this well:


           WBM

                   Viscosity         40-45                 System                     Plastic Viscosity   18-20
                  Yield Point        10-12        Mud
