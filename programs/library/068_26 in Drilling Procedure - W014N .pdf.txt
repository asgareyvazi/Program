 A)    Preparation
1. Review Rig Condition by Pre-Spud Check List, And Ensure Pre-Commission State of The Rig Is OK. (E.G. Power System,
   Hoisting System Sheaves & Blocks, Mud Pumps, Rig Floor Centricity with Conductor, Solid Control System & Etc.), Align the Rig
   with Table & Also Align Mast With 1 Joint 9 ¾” DC, Client Representative Must Accept All Pre-Spud Check List Items.
2. Before Start of Drilling, ensure that all Items of Pre-Drilling Check List (Above Table) have been met.
3. Ensure That All Drilling Tools and Equipment, As Per the Equipment Check List (Mentioned at the End of This Procedure), Are on
   Board, Checked Out and In A Serviceable Condition Prior To Spud.
4. Ensure That Casing Running Tools and Operators Are All on Board Prior To Reaching the Section TD.
5. Prepare Required Mud Material, Mud & High-Vis PHB Pill for Sweeping 26” Hole Section.
6. Starting Bentonite MW 66 pcf, funnel viscosity: 70-80 sec, Sweeping pill funnel viscosity: 120 sec.
7. Ensure Enough Drill Water, CMT Material and LCM Is Available on Rig Site.
8.  Rack Back Drill Collars & Drill Pipe for Drilling. If Possible Before Spud the Rig, Rack Back DP For the Next Section.
9.  Monitor Well Pad Condition as Set Back Weight Increase.
10. For Hole Cleaning Purpose Ensure Mud Pump Providing High Flow Rates (900-1050 GPM) Without Any Problem.

 B)    Drilling Procedure
1. Spud the Rig.
2. Prepare Enough Pre-Hydrate Bentonite Mud & Hi-Vis Pill.
3. P/U & M/U 26” Milled Tooth Bit & Drilling BHA As Following:
                                   BHA No.1
                  Description              Size            Connection                      No.
                Milled Tooth Bit             26”          7 5/8" Reg. Pin Up         4*18 (If CJ) / 3*20 (If W/O CJ)
         Bit Sub (Float In W/Baffle Plate)    9 3/4”         7 5/8" Reg B x B
               DC                9 3/4”         7 5/8" Reg P x B                   2 Jts
                String Stabilizer             26”          7 5/8" REG P x B
               DC                9 3/4”         7 5/8" Reg P x B                   3 Jts
               XOS               9 3/4”     7 5/8" Reg P x 6 5/8" Reg B
               DC                8 1/2”         6 5/8" Reg P x B                   3 Jts
               XOS               8 1/2”      6 5/8" Reg P x 4 1/2" IF B
                  5" DP 'G'                   5”            4 1/2" IF P x B                  As Req.

4. Tag Formation @ ± 14 m.
5. Commence Drilling 26” Hole Section To 150 m (20” CSG Point @ AGHAJARI FM.)

         a)  Recommended Drilling Parameters for the First 20 m:

                  Interval: ±14 - 19 m
            WOB: 1-5 Kips, RPM: 20- 50, FR: 100-150 gpm

       Sweeping Procedure:

          c)  Pump 30 bbl Hi-Vis PHB Pill Each 10 meters While Drilling.

         d)  Pump & Circulate Out 30 bbl Hi-Vis PHB Pill While Reciprocated String Prior To Connection.

         e)  Spot 50 bbl Of Hi-Vis PHB Pill at End of Each Stand Prior To Connection (Make Sure to Displace the Pill Out of String
            Not to Disturb the TOTCO Running).

        Survey:

           f)  Run TOTCO Each 50 m.

6. Perform Condition Trip Prior Run 20” Casing. Note: If Observe Any Drag, Trip Back to BTM W/ Wash & Ream.
7. AT TD, Pump 30 bbl Hi-Vis Pill, Circulate and Clean the Hole. Spot 50 bbl Hi-Vis PHB Pill, Make Sure to Displace the Pill Out of
    String Not to Disturb the TOTCO Running. Run TOTCO, POOH to Surface.
8. Prepare to Run 20” Casing and Perform Cementing Job. Refer to Casing Running and Cementing Instructions.
9.  After Running the Casing and Performing the Cement Job, refer to 20” Drill Out Instruction for Performing 20” Shoe Drill Out.

 C)    Drilling Notes
•  Since the Well Pad has been Constructed by another Party, Monitor Well Pad Condition Regularly as Set Back Weight Increase.
•  Take Care Not to Wash Rig Subbase by Applying High Flow Rate and Drilling with High ROP.
•  Do Not Back Ream Unless Required.
•  At the End of Stand Circulate Enough to Reduce Cuttings Load In Annulus.
•  In Case of Severe to Complete Loss Due to Formation, Try to Reduce Losses With LCM. If Intense Losses Continued, it may be
   needed to perform a cement Plug.
•     If Tight Hole Is Encountered on A Trip, First Assumption Should Be Cuttings. Monitor Hole Conditions Using Torque, Drag and
    Pressure Data to Ensure That ROP Does Not Exceed Hole-Cleaning Rate.

 D)   Contingency / First Action Plans
•   Bit Balling: Can Be Avoided/ Mitigated by Using Low-Vis, High pH Pill, Or Increasing Flow Rate as Much as Possible. Do Not
   Apply Excessive Weight on Bit If "Bit Balling” Or "Bottom Hole Balling” Have Been Identified as Potential Problems.

•  Lost Circulation: Use LCM Pill If Encountered Heavy Losses. If Loss Couldn’t Be Cured by LCM Pill Use Cement Plug(S) To
   Cure Same.
•  As Contingency Plan, In Case of Any Deviation from Vertical (Based on TOTCO Readings), Adjust Drilling Parameters to Drop
   the Angle and Return to Vertical, Such as Decreasing WOB, Controlling ROP, Increasing FR, Etc.
•  Reduce the Applied Weight on Bit And/or Rotary Speed as Necessary to Ensure That the Penetration Rate Does Not Exceed the
   Maximum Allowable to Maintain the Hole Clean and Stable.

 E)   Section Notes:
•  For Timing of Each Operation, Refer to Drilling Program (section 1.9.1 Detail Time Breakdown)
•  No Logging Program Has been Considered For this Hole Section.
•  The Exact TD Will Be Determined Based on Casing Tally.
•  Mud Logger Should Closely Monitor and Report Drilling Parameters.

 F)    Safety Recommendation:
 WORK SAFE; ALWAYS ASK YOURSELF HOW CAN I GET HURT IF I DO THIS TASK.
•  Safety Standards and Policy of PECO Should Be Followed During Drilling Operation.
•    It is Not Expected to Encounter Shallow Gas, Since No Shallow Ga Has been Observed in Offset Wells of the Field.
•  In Case of Any Deviation or Advise for This Instruction Call Co. Man ASAP.
•   All Rig Crew Should Follow and Cooperate with Job Specialists as Per This Instruction.
•  Mud Eng. Is Responsible for Monitoring & Report Pit Levels During Operation.

 G)    Section Information:
•  Aghajari Formation:
   This Huge Clastic/Marly Formation (2044 M Thickness) Is Mostly Composed of Red Marls, Interbedded Sandstone and Red
   Claystone Beds.  It Is Expected That the Red Claystone Intervals Increase from Top to The Bottom of The Formation. The
   Sandstone and Siltstone Layers Are Variably Consolidated and Generally Can Be Described with Light Brown and Light Gray
   Colors, Fine to Medium Sand Grains, Well Sorted and Sometimes Are Dominated by Sub-Angular to Sub-Rounded Quartz
   Grains. The Above-Mentioned Sandstones Are Loose (Un-Consolidated), Friable to Moderately Hard.
   The Marls Are Red, Reddish Brown to Pale Reddish Brown, Generally Sticky and Slightly Silty, Soft to Firm, Grading to
   Claystone. The Clay Stones Are Red Color, Sometimes Show Dark Brown, Generally Soft, Sticky and Amorphous, Locally Some
   Calcareous, Grading to Gray Marl, Generally Non-Silty and Occasionally Mottled.
