     36” Jet-In and 26’ Hole Section Drilling Procedure
                San Jacinto Prospect
                       April 4, 2004


1.  P/U 4500’ + 5 7/8” DP, and all of the rigs 6 5/8’ DP and all of the Specialty Rental tools 6 5/8” DP for a
     total of ~ 11675’. Deepest permitted depth for the 20” shoe is 11675’ MD
       •   Strap 36” and Drill ahead assy while P/U DP
       •  Space out bit 6” or less below 36” shoe
       •  Buck up as much of BHA as possible while P/U DP, BHA will be 26” bit drill ahead assy per BHI
            Directional Driller
       •   Paint bit white.
       •  PU DAT, MU to a single, function test, and LD
       • MU SBLS to H
       •   handling tool
2.  P/U 36” Structural pipe
       •  See csg detail for running order
       •  Use solid body lift sub for 2” wall WH jt
       •  Check for tee pads on WH jt, will be able to cut off before p/u if we use the handling tool and
           support plates
       •   After landing 36’ on support plates l/d handling tool for break out later.
       •  Make sure DAT has right X-O and can be p/u off of deck and will not need to rack back
3.  Rig up false rotary and run 26’ jet-in/drilling assy.
4.  P/U drill ahead tool and m/u to WH hsg .
       •   Verify space out with ROV
       •   Test motor and verify w/ ROV
       •  Run assy thru rotary and MU slope indicator to WH hsg
       •  Take readings off slope indicator in moon pool
5.  TIH to ML.
       •  RKB = 79’
       •   Verify WD according to DP measurements
       •   Verify position w/ John Chance.
       •  Take inclination readings prior to jetting
       •   Notify bridge to keep heading during jet-in
6.  Jet / Wash 36’ pipe down to 344 ft BML per BHI/ Dominion rep.
       •  Leave 12’ stick up AML
       •   Utilize BHI WOB schedule
       •  Soak time 3 hrs
7.  Release DAT, drill 26” interval to ~ 11575’MD per Dominion procedure.
