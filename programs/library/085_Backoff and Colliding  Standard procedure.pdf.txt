     Explosive Operations:

    1.    Always lock the traveling block from turning.

    2.    Use 80 grains/ft primacord, No of primacord strands should be agreed with client /
        company man based upon chart guidelines.

    3.    Making up the string shot / Colliding tool ready:

            a. Put the number of primacord strands according to table. Add one more strand
             extra to give a better shock.

    4.    Hold perforation safety meeting as necessary and follow  all explosive safety
        mentioned in explosive placard. We have to minimize the rig people on the rig
           floor.

    5.    Radio Silence is required before arming the explosive gun.
