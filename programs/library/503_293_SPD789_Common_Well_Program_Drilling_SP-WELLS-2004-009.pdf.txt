5      Health, Safety, Environment and Quality (HSE & Q)


5.1   HSE

       2004 goals
           •  Zero harmful spill to sea
           •  Zero red incidents
           •  Zero dropped objects on the rigs
           •  Zero serious well control situations


5.1.1   Risk assessment

        During the detailed planning of the first well, the different groups involved have prepared risk
        logs for each hole section. The risks are classified using a South Pars risk matrix.

       The risk logs will be checked before the start of each section, and continuously updated if new
         risks are identified. The risk logs shall be used offshore on a daily basis during the operations.

      No shallow gas was detected in appraisal wells SP-9, SP-10 and SP-11. These wells are planned
          utilised as production wells and will be allocated in slot no. 6 at each jacket. With reference to
        Gardline report from 2001 and Statoil’s report of 2003: “There is no evidence to suggest the

6.3     Total Depth (TD)

        Total Depth (TD) for wells will be in the K4M Formation. The TD criteria is based on the gas
        water contact (GWC). TD will be approximately 50 m TVD above the estimated GWC.
     TD of wells will be at the discretion of the well-site geologist and reservoir geologist onshore.


6.4     Pressure Prognosis

        For pressure prognosis refer to figure in Appendix A.


6.5    Temperature

       The temperature gradient is 2.84 DegC/100. At 3100 m TVD MSL the temperature is estimated to
       107 degrees Celsius. The temperature gradient is based on DST temperature from well SP1-6. Ref
         figure in appendix A.


6.6     Geological sampling and formation evaluation


6.6.1    Cuttings sampling

        Cuttings sampling will be performed only in the first two wells on each phase.

      One set of cutting samples will be required at ten meters interval from the first return depth in the
        16’’ section, to 50 m above prognosed top reservoir. From here on, depth samples will be
         collected at five meters interval to TD of the wells.

      One bulk, unwashed wet sample (300 gram) will be collected at each interval. The samples will
       be sent to NIOC exploration storehouse in Rey, via Statoil Base Kish Island.

       Wet, unwashed cutting samples are to be taken in all shales at 10 meters interval. Samples to be
         sent to M-I Dubai for MBT analysis. Shales: Laffan, Kazhdumi, Gadvan, Fahliyan, Neyriz, Upper
     & Lower Sudair and Aghar.

7      Directional surveying


7.1     General

           •  General survey interval is 30 m, max 100 m.
           •  No shallow MWD test to be performed
           •   If changing the BHA/MWD, minimum 3 surveys at overlapping depths of previous run
                 shall be taken to check survey accuracy
           •  Azimuths to be referred to Grid North system
           •  Use minimum curvature calculation method
           •  When drilling close to other wells, the survey requirement is 10 m when the difference
              between the actual well separation, and the minimum allowed distance, is less than 3 m
                   (ref. live wells – WR0436)
           •  All surveys to be sag corrected (WR0436)
           •  Minimum distance from the magnetic sensor package to any magnetic part of the BHA
                 shall be minimum 12 m (WR0436)
           •  Survey error model used: ISCWSA Ellipse

8      Drilling Fluid

       24” Section
       At start of operations, the 26” conductor will be filled with inhibited seawater.
         Drilling of this section will be done with seawater and hi-vis pills.

       Have minimum 50 m3 1.20 SG kill mud ready to be pumped at all times during drilling operation.
        This will be used as displacement mud at TD.

        All pills will be formulated with H2S scavenger to neutralize H2S and minimize corrosion.
        For protection of the drill string, Conqor 202B will be used as a filming agent, this will be sprayed
       on DP when tripping out of hole.

       16” Section
       At start of the operation the 18 5/8” casing will be filled with inhibited seawater. Drilling of this
         section will be done with KCl/Asphasol polymer mud.
        Treating of mud and spray film on DP to avoid twist off due to H2S

       12 1/4” Section
     A KCl/ Asphasol polymer mud will be used.
        Treating of mud and spray film on DP to avoid twist off due to H2S

       8 1/2” Section
     A KCl/ Polymer mud system will be used in this section
        Treating of mud and spray film on DP to avoid twist off due to H2S

12    Hole stability

        Borehole collapse curves have been calculated for inclinations between 0-60 degrees. In the
        carbonates in and above the reservoir the mud density should be designed only for well control
       and lost circulation purposes. Boreholes are predicted to remain stable in the carbonates (without
        cavings) at all mud densities between the pore pressure and the fracturing pressure.

        Borehole collapse will most likely occur in the Sudair and Aghar Shales. It is not possible to
          stabilize the formation with mud weight in high deviated wells. The collapse will occur on each
         side on the wellbore and not from the top and bottom. The mechanism may not be reactive shale
         since both these shales are of Trias age. The reports of suggest either a geo-pressured shale or
         unstable fractured shale.
