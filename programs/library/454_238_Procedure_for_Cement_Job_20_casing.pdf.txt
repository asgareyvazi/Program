Cement Job: 20” casing

    1. Flush and pressure test the lo torque valve, cementing hose and surface lines to
      5000psi (or as Operator Drilling Supervisor instructions).
    2. Line up the spacer and transfer to the cement unit as required.
    3. Line up to transfer the mix water to the cement unit and also to circulate on itself
       to prevent deadheading of the pump.
    4. Mix and pump cement slurry as per the program.
    5. Monitor the returns at the wellhead with the ROV throughout the job.
    6. Take weights and samples as required by the cementer.
    7. Displace surface lines with the cementing unit and continue displacement using
        rig pumps.
    8. When the desired strokes have been pumped release the pressure watching for
       returns.
    9.  If the float is not holding, then pump the volume bled back and hold the shut-in
      pressure until the cement takes its initial set.
   10. Rig down the cement hose and lo-torque valve.
   11. Observing with the ROV, disengage the wellhead running tool from the wellhead,
      as per the Wellhead Service Engineer instruction.
   12. Pull out of the hole and position the end of stinger 3-5m inside the top of the
      wellhead. It is critical that running tool/stinger should be pulled cleanly to avoid
     damage to wellhead gasket profile area.
   13. Flush the wellhead area with seawater at a maximum pump rate.
   14. Ensure the slips are not set when the stinger is near the wellhead and do not
       allow the stinger to contact the top of the wellhead.
   15. Pull out and lay down running tool and cement stinger.
   16. Strap the landing string (SLM) on the trip out and calculate the measurement
      from the rotary table to the top of wellhead housing.
   17. Verify that the ship is at normal drilling draft.
   18. Apply tide corrections and report this depth in the IADC report.
   19. Prepare to run the 16 ¾” BOPs.

Post Task Action:

    1. Flush the cement lines clear of slurry.
    2. Purge the bulk transfer lines to the surge tank.
    3. Vent off the bulk tanks.
    4. Service the mud pump fluid ends and change piston and liner sizes as required
    5. Clean high and low pressure mud pump strainers.
    6. Check the remaining stock of cement.
    7. Ensure that the surge tank is empty and made ready for the next job.
    8. Clean out the tanks used for spacer and mix water.
    9. Flush the mud mix lines.

    1. Cement required (MT) = Slurry required (ltrs) / Yield (ltrs/MT).
    2. Mix Water required (M3) = Cement required (MT) x Mix Water ratio (M3/MT).

Displacement:

    1. Volume of Landing String: Capacity of DP (ltrs/m) x Length of String (m) +
      Capacity of Stinger (ltrs/m) x Length of Stinger (m).
    2. Displacement: Volume String + Volume Stinger + safety margin.
