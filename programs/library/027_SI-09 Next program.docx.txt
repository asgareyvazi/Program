SI-09 Next Program, 11-Jan-23
POOH to surface & L/D 17 -1/2" Bit
***While POOH 17-1/2” D/O BHA, check for any differential stuck in sand zone intervals (640-600m & 570-460m & 390-310m).
RIH W/ 8-3/8” C.C.Bit to 830m (5m above TOC), F/U every 10 Stds.
*** While RIH all Stds connection should be done at minimum possible time.
*** Check differential stuck indication (Pump off) in potential sandstone zones.
CBU & clean hole.
Pump 15bbl fresh water as spacer ahead. Mix and pump 100bbl CMT slurry 100pcf. Pump 1 bbl fresh water as spacer behind. Displace CMT as balance plug (2bbl short).
POOH to 730m. flush string. Cont. POOH to 500m.
Colse annular. Squeeze 20 bbl of CMT w/ max 100psi.
If diff stuck, CBU hi-rate and make sure there is no CMT around the string.
Wait on CMT to get hard and follow differential stuck procedure (Procedure A).
If no differential stuck, POOH to Surface.
*** If intense loss obs. @ each stage, repeat cmt job @ 300m after coordination w/ office.
RIH 17-1/2" bit & Drilling BHA (Without stabilizer) to TOC, Clear tight spots & D/O cmt to 830m.
*** D/O parameters:
WOB: 10-20klbs, Rot: 80RPM, F.R: 700GPM (Due to shaker handling capacity)
B/R: Rot: 50RPM, F.R: 700GPM.
Circ. w/ different flow rates and obs. well for any loss dynamically and statically.
*** If intense loss observed, discuss for next program.
If Static, D/O Last cement plug carefully w/ controlled rate F/835m to +/- 950m.
*** D/O parameters:
WOB: 5-20klbs, Rot: 80RPM, F.R: 700GPM (Due to shaker handling capacity)
B/R: Rot: 50RPM, F.R: 700GPM.
***Monitor well carefully during D/O cement plug for any flow. If flow observed; Circ hi-rate w/ 100pcf mud and try to control the well by uniforming MW to 100 pcf.
*** Keep active MW on 100 pcf.
Continue wash & Ream from 950m to 1139m and monitor pit level carefully.
Commence drilling 17-1/2" Hole section to 1170m.
For 1st 50m drilling w/ controlled ROP and keep GPM @ max shakers capacity. Watch MW & pit levels carefully.
CBU, If well Static; Displace hole with fresh 100pcf mud.
*** Before displace, Pump hi-vis pill and make sure hole is clean.
POOH to surface, P/U New BHA Including PDC Bit (NOV, Used, 6*20/32”)  & 17-1/2" Stabilizer @ 20m & RIH To Bottom while clear tight spots and make connection time as minimum.
Continue drilling 17-1/2" Hole section as per program. Drilling procedure will be announced in due time.
***In case of observe any differential stuck @ sand zone intervals (640-600m & 570-460m & 390-310m), Perform Cement squeeze job against same interval.
*** Check seepages around location all the times and take sample.
Action plans:
Procedure A: In case of differential stucks; Pump some volume of waste water and work on stuck downward with and without TQ in coordination w/ office for volumes and parameters.
Procedure B: If minor or partial loss; Decrease flow rate. Mix & pump 100bbl LCM pill 10ppb (Mica F&M, Kwik seal F) while drilling. If OK, Cont. Drlg w/ increasing GPM gradually to Drlg GPM. If minor loss continues; add 2 sx/hr Mica F & Kwik seal in active mud.
Procedure C: If heavy loss occurs, Keep string rotation while decrease GPM and make distance f/ BTM. Prepare & pump hi-vis LCM pill 20-25ppb (Mica F&M, Kwik seal M, Walnut F&M). Displace w/ low GPM (Never stop pumping) and try to cure loss. If negative; spot 100 bbl hi-vis LCM pill w/ same formula. POOH to top of LCM pill, Circ and let LCM function.
If OK, RIH and Cont. Drlg w/ lower GPM while monitor pit level.
If not OK; Procedure will be announced in due time (maybe cmt plug).
Procedure D: In case of flow; Keep GPM as hi as possible and try to keep MW @ 100pcf.
If OK; Circ and monitor pit level & MW and Cont. controlled Drlg while monitor conditions and increase parameters to Drlg parameters.
If not OK; Increase MW by steps while adding LCM to active system and try to kill the well.
***Always keep 150pcf kill mud pit full.
*** Try to keep surface mud volume as much as possible.
