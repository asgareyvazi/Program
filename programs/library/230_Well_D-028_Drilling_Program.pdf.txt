1  Introduction


Program Introduction

This program covers the information and procedures required to drill new well D-028 from a new

slot on the D-009 platform in Dorood oil field, Persian Gulf, Iran. The well is intended to produce oil

in Yamama Reservoir by drilling an approximately 620m deviated 8-1/2" hole in pay zone.

The 30” Conductor pipe and the 20” casing is to be set and cemented at 110mMD and 600mMD

respectively, the 13-⅜” casing is to be set at 1867mMD inside Asmari Cap rock, the 9-⅝” casing is

to be set at 3840mMD in the Khami formation. Well is going to be nudged at 650mMD and KOP is

at 1870mMD.

The 8-1/2” hole section is going to be drilled initially deviated and continued about 577m inside

Yamama Reservoir to total depth of 4521.41mMD. Well is to be completed 7” liner and perforated.

Dorood Structure


The Dorood offshore oil field has been located in the northern part of the Persian Gulf and partly

underlying the Kharg Island. The structure is an anticline elongated NNE-SSW with 25 km length

and 5 km width.

This field was discovered in 1961 with exploration well IPAC-D-1 (Darius 1).The southern sector of

the field, called Darius, consists of 10 platforms/clusters including wells Darius -1 to Darius-21

which were operated by Iran Pan American Oil Company (IPAC) between 1961 and 1978.

The northern sector of the field, called Kharg, consisting of 18 platforms/clusters with wells of

Kharg-1 through 10) and 1 on-shore single well of Kharg-11 which was operated until 1979 by the

Oil Service Company (OSCO). Finally both sectors came under the operator-ship of the Iranian

Offshore Oil Company (IOOC) and after interruption of production due to Iran-Iraq war(1980 to

1988), Drilling operation was started in both the Darius/IPAC area(Dorood -22H to Dorood -25) and

Kharg/OSCO area (Kharg-12 to Kharg-47) By IOOC.

The Dorood Field was the subject of an Enhanced Oil Recovery program that carried out by Elf

Petroleum Iran, under the conditions of the Dorood Project buy-back contract from 1999 to 2006.

The operation of Elf Petroleum Company was drilling of 30 wells (D-101P to D-130P) from 4

platforms/clusters that three of them are located in the On-shore Kharg Island called North West,

North East and South clusters and one of them has been located in the off-shore the area called PTF-G cluster.

The Lower Cretaceous Fahliyan reservoir contains the main proven pay zones of the Dorood

structure which resulted from deposits on a carbonated platform.

The main Fahliyan reservoir layering is as follows:

- Lower Ratawi and Khami as the seal of the reservoir zone

- Upper Yamama as poor reservoir

- Middle Yamama as main reservoir

- Manifa member (Lower Yamama) as main reservoir but mostly is water-bearing.

The secondary known pay zone in the Dorood structure is the Asmari Formation (Oligo-Miocene),

which shows some oil bearing sandstone reservoir layers near the top, capped by the overlaying

anhydrite beds of the Gachsaran Formation.

1  Geological Overview
The stratigraphic succession in D-028 is expected to be nearly normal (except Upper Ahmadi Mbr.)

from surface to total depth of 4521.4mMD.


Aghajari Fm.

From 79mMD (-47.6mTVDss) to 1363mMD (-1330mTVDss)

The Aghajari Formation consists of Marl: tan to light brown, partly light greenish grey, reddish

brown to greenish grey, partly light beige and light olive grey, soft, sticky, washable, sub-blocky,

slightly   silty,  traces  of  pyrite,  traces  of  microfossils,  grading  to  Calcareous  Claystone,

Sand/Sandstone: glassy, tan, white, off white, cream, light brown, translucent, transparent, medium

hard to hard, rounded, angular, Shale: dark grey to black, soft, calcareous, Claystone: green, light

green, some light gray, gray, soft, medium hard, calcareous and Limestone: cream, off white, I, II,

soft-medium, A, chalky, some argillaceous.


Mishan Fm.

From 1363mMD (-1330mTVDss) to 1570mMD (-1537mTVDss)

The Mishan Formation consists of Limestone: light grey, grayish-white to white, cream, soft to firm,

locally moderately hard, generally argillaceous, locally microcrystalline to very finely crystalline,

traces microfossils and fossils relics, Marl: commonly brown to light brown, reddish-brown, soft to

firm, slightly sticky, sub-blocky to blocky,  silty in part, traces of microfossils and Sandstone:

translucent, brownish, light grey, very fine to fine grained, sub-angular to sub-rounded, poorly sorted,

brittle, friable, grain mainly quartz, litho fragments, aggregates with calcareous cement, locally

becoming sandy Limestone, poor porosity.


Gachsaran Fm.

From 1570mMD (-1537mTVDss) to 1872mMD (-1839mTVDss)

The Gachsaran Formation consists of Anhydrite: transparent, white to off white, brittle, locally milky

white, hard, microcrystalline, putty, soft, some crystalline, hard, glassy, Limestone: grayish-white to

off-white, light grey, moderately hard to hard, microcrystalline to locally very finely crystalline,

traces microfossils and fossils relics, argillaceous in part and locally marly, Shale: light grey to

greenish, grey, firm to compact, occasionally reddish-brown, brown, blocky, sub-flaky, Marl: light

brown to reddish brown, light grey, pale grayish brown, soft to firm, slightly sticky, sub-blocky to

blocky, shaly. Traces of microfossils, slightly calcareous, trace of pyrite, Dolomite: off white to
