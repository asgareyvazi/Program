                            Workover Program

The main headlines of this program are as follows:

1.   Skid from BS-15 and centre on well SR-15.

2.   Record & report RKB to THF & MSL of new well.

3.    Kill and secure well, N/D X-mass tree, N/U and test BOP.

4.  POOH and L/D ESP completion string.

5.   perform pressure test of 9-5/8” casing with max 1000psi

6.   Change Tubing Head Spool.

7.   Wash production screen interval w/2-7/8” tubing.

8.    Inject 5000 gal Surfactant into 2260-2314m (production screen).

9.  OBS well, in case of loss, complete well with new ESP completion string.

10.  N/D BOP, N/U X-mass tree.

11.  Secure well, release rig.


Note:
   After injection surfactant, in case of no loss, re-entry is recommended that will
      be issued in due time



Attentions:
  The detailed program is available in this document under the heading “Well

     Securing Procedure” & “Workover Program”.
   All depths are referred to RKB-MSL: 22.55m which should be corrected with

    new RKB-MSL measurement of active rig at time.

8.  Well Operation Procedure:

    1. Move Shengli-10 rig to Soroosh field, skid and spud on well SR-15.

    2.  Record & report, air gap, & RKB to THF and MSL.

    3.  Record wellhead and Tubing Ann. Pressures

    4. Hook up lines on Ann. & TBG. P/T same to 2500psi.

    5.  Apply pressure on control  line, Open 3SV. Record wellhead and Ann.

        Pressures.

    6. Pump sea water into annulus and get return from tubing. Balance mud weight.

      Confirm well is static or under loss condition.


Note:
  Seawater impose 1085psi over pressure to the Burgan-B Reservoir @ top

       of   perforation (2298.07m MD, 2177.3m TVD, RKB-MSL:22.55m).


    7.  Close SSSV. confirm its functioning. Install BPV in tubing hanger (Cameron

       type “DC-FBB”). perssure test same.

    8.  While circulate cross wellhead, N/D X-Mass tree, check tubing hanger thread

     N/U BOP and test same to 2500psi.

    9. Make up 4-1/2" EUE 8RD landing joint inside tubing hanger, unset packer by

       applying over pull.


Note:
  Keep 4-1/2" spear available on well site in case of tubing hanger thread

      damage.


    10. While circulate cross wellhead POOH completion  string and stand back

       tubing in derrick and lay down ESP.


                                    11

    11. RIH w/8-1/2" Bit & 9-5/8" scraper to top of permanent packer @ 2260mMD,

       scrap 9-5/8” Casing @ B.plug & RTTS depths.

    12. RIH & set 9 5/8” RTTS @ ±2250mMD.

    13. Take Injectivity test.

    14. POOH & L/D RTTS.

    15. RIH & set 9 5/8” Retrievable Bridge plug @ 2250mMD.

    16. Pressure test B.plug & casing to 1000 psi.

    17. RIH & set 9 5/8” RTTS @ 100m.

    18. Change Tubing Head Spool to new one (13-5/8”, 3K * 11”-3K).

    19. Remove 9-5/8” RTTS.

    20. Retrieve Bridge plug & POOH to surface.

    21. RIH w/2-7/8” tubings to 2260mMD (top of “B&W” permanent packer) and

     Wash down to 2314mMD (Bottom of Wire Wrapped Screen & P.B.TD).

    22. POOH to 2260mMD (top of screen packer). Perform Injection test. In case of

        injectivity, mix and inject 5000 gal (119 bbls) Surfactant as per production

       department program.

    23. OBS well, in case of loss, scrap completion packer depth and run new ESP

       completion string as per completion program (The ESP completion string

        detail will be issued in due time).

    24. Install B.P.Valve. N/D BOP, N/U X-mass tree.

    25. Secure well, Release Rig.


Note:

  If OBS no loss after washing with surfactant, re-entry program will be

       issued in due time.
