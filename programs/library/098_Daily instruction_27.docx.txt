Daily Instruction
17-1/2” Hole Section Side tracking
M/U 17-1/2" B.Bit (KD, 535, Open) + 9-5/8” PDM. Adjust AKO on 2.38 deg + MWD.
Perform shallow test of PDM w/ bit + MWD
BHA as: 17-1/2" B.Bit + 9-5/8” D.H.MOTOR (AKO 2.38 deg) + XOS + 8” F.SUB + 8” UBHO + 8” NMDC + XOS + 4 STDs 5” HWDP + XOS” + 2 STD 8-1/2” DC + 8” D.JAR & Flex Jt + 1 STD 8-1/2” DC + XOS
Took check shot to check MWD signal @ 500 & 1000m.
F/U string & break Circ every 15 STD using pipe screen.
RIH to 1197m while adjust TF. Break Circ & Wash Dn last STD.
Commence Time drilling to get 100% formation
Make sure while tag BTM have at least a double above RT
After Drlg a JT, ream & soften Kick-off PT several times.
Make sure connection area is completely free & clean.
After connection, make sure string is free, establish low RPM & increase pump slowly avoiding pack-off.
Drlg parameters will be announced by D.D Sup.
Do not W&R the last 3.
In case of pumping Hi Vis pill, POOH at least 10m, pump pill & reciprocate string till CBU.
In case of abnormal condition as press drop, loss, gain, abnormal drag, call DSV ASAP.
Max usable LCM for MWD is 20 ppb fine to med LCM.
Suitable amount of Hi-Vis pill shall be prepared.
Suitable amount of LCM shall be in hand.
Mud rheology shall be in range as mud program.
In case of loss, Decrease GPM & if in rotate mode, decrease RPM but DO NOT STOP ROTATION.
P/U of-btm & check for surf pit possible leakages. Loss procedure will be confirmed by DSV.
In case of Press Drop, decrease GPM. P/U off-btm. Check recorded SCR, Surf lines & equip & mud cond for any changes. If downhole leakage confirmed, POOH preferably dry as DSV instruction.
In case of gain, Stick up & check choke manifold arrangement on soft shut –in position, flow check & check pit Vol., if positive, follow kill procedure as DSV instruction.
Notes:
Mud logging department has to put comment for any event informed by driller & mud engineer on mud logging screen.
In case of plan change, the revised instruction will be informed in due time.
Don’t hesitate to contact DSV for any clarification.
Check & OBS active system by mud engineer & mud logger for gain & loss.
Good Luck & Be safe.
