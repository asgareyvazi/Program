                  7″ LINER CEMENTING PROCEDURE (Well # AZNS-045)
 1-  Clean up Batch Mixer tanks and preparing chemicals for mixing fluid.
 2-  Fill Batch Mixer tanks with 48 bbl fresh water for mixing fluids.
 3-  Before finished running Liner & 5" Drill Pipe with Co. man approval, start mixing cement additives
    and preparing mixing fluids according to below Main cement Slurry mixing procedure.
Main cement slurry mixing procedure:
         Add Anti Foam to Fresh water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.
         Add CFL-350 and allow mixing for 10 minutes.
         Add O-R12 and allow mixing for 10 minutes.
         Add O-Gas Block and allow mixing for 10 minutes.
         Add CFL-387 and allow mixing until the cement operation will finish.
 4-  Before running Liner, check Plug Dropping Head assembly Specially Pump down Plug release
    Handle.
 5-  Before Liner shoe reach open hole (@ 9 5/8 shoe), prepare circulation at least one annular volume.
 6- When the Liner shoe reached the bottom ,connected Plug dropping head assembly then start
     circulation at least one annular volume.
 7-  While Last circulation, prepare pre job safety meeting with involved personnel and arrange with mud
     engineer to measure drilling fluid & cement loss/gain during cement job.
 8-  Stop circulation, connect cement line.
 9-  Tests cement line W/ 3500 psi.
 10- Pump 15 bbl Salt Saturated Water mix with 45 gal O-Chemical Wash as per flush already prepared in
    cement unit displacement tanks.
 11- Mix and pump 86.5 bbl Main cement slurry.(115 pcf)
 12- Release pump down plug.
 13- Displace with mud at optimum rate. Slow pump rate 10 bbl before Pump down Plug reaches Wiper
    Plug (Drill Pipe Displacement is ……. Bbl) at +/- …….Psi.
 14- Watch for indication of drill pipe plug latching into liner, Wiper Plug on pressure gauge. If this
     pressure indication is seen, recalculate total displacement volume. The new displacement volume will
    be the actual displacement volume for drill pipe plus the calculated liner volume.
 15- Slow pump rate down to 3 bbl/ minute, last 10 bbl of displacement volume.
 16- Land Plug with 1000 psi over final displacement pressure.
      Note: If plug does not bump, do not displace more than %25 of the calculated shoe track. (Optional)
 17- Bleed off pressure and monitor U-Tube return.
 18- Monitoring return line for checking Float Collar and Float Shoe.
 19- Pull out 8 to 10 drill pipe stands or above the top of the cement, flush string drill pipe then
    shut off the well , and hold 50 psi pressure on top of the cement until cement sets. (Optional)
 20- Rig down cement line.
NOTE 1: PUMPS OF EFFICIENCY FACTOR SHOULD BE DETERMINED IN RIG SITE BEFORE
CEMENTING OPERATIONS BY COMPANY MAN.
NOTE 2: THE VOLUME OF DISPLACEMENT SHOULD BE CALCULATED IN RIG SITE BY COMPANY
MAN AND TO ALL THE EXECUTIVE GROUPS TO BE ANNOUNCED.
