Use 870 bbl new cabr2 brine 104 pcf

1.  Make sure all tanks and surface equipment are clean.
2.  Check all connections for any possible leakage.
3.  Use 220 PPB cacl2 (94-97%) for cacl2 brine 85 pcf.
4.  Prepare 120 bbl pumpable cacl2 brine and let it rest for at least 6 hours.
5.  Perform pilot test in mud lab and after mixing 1 bbl of cabr2 with 11% v cacl2 85 pcf, make
    sure the final brine weight is equal to 104 pcf.
6.  Use 450 bbl (360Dr) new cabr2.
7.  Mix 450 bbl cabr2 with 50 bbl cacl2 brine and prepare 500 bbl cabr2 104 pcf.
8.  Mix with 180 bbl (144 Dr) new cabr2 and mix with 20 bbl cacl2 to reach weight of 104 pcf.
9.  Use 170 bbl recovered cabr2 104 pcf from rig 208.
10. Use the excess volume on surface to fill the well during pooh.

Note:

Tank containing recovered cabr2 should be pumped at first.
