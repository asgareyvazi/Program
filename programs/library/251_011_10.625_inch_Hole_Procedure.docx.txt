
10 5/8” HOLE SECTION DRILLING PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
PU BHA (10 5/8” bit, 8” power drive, quad combo LWD) and TIH.  Shallow test MWD.
TIH to 11 7/8” liner top at 11,290’.  Slowly enter liner top with Bit.
Continue TIH to top of float collar at 13,321’.
Wash/drill cement, float collar, rat hole, and 10’ new formation to 13,768’.    Raise mud weight to 13.0 ppg while drilling cement.  Take SPR’s & CLFP’s  before drilling new formation.
Pump 50 bbl Hi-Vis Sweep at 13,768’ and circulate bottoms up to surface. Downlink power drive while circulating.
Pull back to shoe.  Get static equivalent mud wt from PWD.
Line up to pump down the kill line and drill pipe and take pressures up the choke.  Perform shoe test. (Max 14.2 ppg EMW, Min 14.0 ppg EMW.  May be able to live with a 13.9 ppg EMW)  Pump up MWD max reading from LOT.
Drill 10 5/8” hole in to shale at ~15,050’-15,170’ MD.  After evaluating the VSP check shots, it appears that our objective is going to come in higher, cutting approximately 600’ off the MD of the well. The objective is 14,800’ TVD to 15,000’ TVD.  Drill at 100 ft/hr instantaneous to the top of the objective.
When drilling, continue to correlate to Anvil. The target has moved up the hole and could be as much as 150' shallower. It could also be gas.  Thus at ~14,750’ TVD above the target (50' TVD above the targets shallowest possible depth) circulate hole clean.  Simulate a connection or flow check at ¼ and ½ bottoms up.  (As hole cleans up, note ESD after each flow check).  Watch flow checks bottoms up for connection gas when back to drilling.
Control drill thru the event at 50'/hr managing flow rate to control ECD. 500 gpm & 50'/hr generates lowest ECD per MI. If pay sand is logged, pull above it and CBU prior to drilling ahead (pay could be gas).
Time and log flow-backs to develop a trend as well as on/off bottom torque readings.  Communication between mud loggers, shakers, and the rig floor is critical.  Immediately report any variation in trends to the rig floor.  Perform engineers to monitor & communicate hole cleaning with drilling team continuously.  Do not allow ECD’s to exceed 0.2 ppg from LOT.  Do not exceed 800 GPM flow rate to minimize washout to obtain a good LWD log.  The offset Anvil well penetrated the target with a 13.5 ppg mud at 15,600' TVD.
Prior to any trips, ensure MI runs trip log and trip at speeds that ensure shoe is protected and well is not swabbed in.
At TD, pump 100 bbl Hi-Vis sweep and CBU, pump back to shoe working any tight spots.  Test BOP’s
TIH to bottom and circulate hole clean for logging.
POOH to run logs.  Lay out BHA.  Offline, make up 1000’ of 3 ½” cement string in doubles.
If pay is present, 1,000’ of 3 ½” DP stinger will be picked up and run to set open hole plugs in preparation for the sidetrack.  If no pay is encountered, prepare to set a cast iron EZSV at the base of the 11 7/8” casing.
