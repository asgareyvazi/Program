1. INTRODUCTION

       Oriental Oil Kish Company (OOK) as an Iranian, Privately owned company is winner  in a

 tender issued by Oil & Energy Industries Development Company (OEID) for an EPD project

 which is consisting of Drilling of 4 new wells and Re-entry of existing two well in West Paydar

 Oil Field.

       All MC-Work related to OOK’s EPD projects will be handelled by Oriental Engineering &

 Services (OES), which is a private Iranian Registered Company.

      The West Paydar Field is an existing oilfield along the Iraqi border line, located 21 km west
 of the Paydar Field ,200 km north west from Ahwaz,100 km west from Shoosh and 40 kh west
 from Cheshmeh khosh.

      The operation will be supported by operation base in Ahwaz and conducted by main office
 in Tehran.

      The first well,PYW-1,was drilled on 1977 and confirmed oil in Asmari and Bangestan

 formations.The West Paydar anticline is part of the fughi anticline in Iraq.Only a smaller part of

 the structure(less than 20%) belongs to Iran.(Fig-1)

     In this project six wells will be drilled as per scope of work, the drilling of four new wells

(PYW-3B,PYW-6,PYW-7,PYW-8) from three locations and re-entry of two existing wells(PYW-

1&PYW-3A) as horizontal oil producer wells to enhance oil recovery from Bangestan Reservoir

1.1  Well architecture

   This well (PYW-7) will be drilled horizontally in direction S 81 E and horizontal section of

+/- 1110m after deviating hole @ +/- 3757m(KOP) and completed with ESP system as a oil

producer well. The schematic well architecture(fig-2) is as follows:

       •  26” hole for 18 5/8” surface casing
       •  17 ½” hole for 13 3/8” intermediate casing at top of Gachsaran high pressure zone
       •  12 ¼” hole for 9 5/8” production casing at top of Asmari reservoir to cover high

           pressure zone of Gachsaran formation
       •  8 1/2” hole for 7” production liner at LP in Sarvak reservoir
       •  6 1/8” horizontal hole as open hole through the pay zone to TD (+/- 4900 md)

4.ANTICIPATED DRILLING HAZZARDS

      The main anticipated drilling hazards are as follow:

          •  stuck pipe in marl section of AJ formation
          •   losses and gains in high pressure Gachsaran formation
          •   shale instability in Pabdeh & Gurpi formation
          •   differential sticking in Asmari & Sarvak formation
          •   losses in Asmari & Sarvak formation
          •  No significant H2S is reported in this field. A minimum standard of safety

              procedures for H2S hazards and gas detection as per OOK Company Policy will be

               required.

6.1.9.2.1. Prepration

    1.  Drift,tally, number, clean all boxes and pins and prepare all the 18 5/8” casings when

    2.  Visually and mechanically check the duplex shoe thread and sealing area for stinger seal

        nipple.

    3.  Ensure that spiders,slips and elevators are on the rig floor and check them visually for

    4.  Ensure that all cement bulk lines have been checked for blockage and pressurize the

    5. M/U shoe to shoe joint with thread lock.

    6.  Ensure that spare O-rings for stinger are on location.

    7.  Ensure that C-Plate is on the location.

   Running & cementing procedure:

      1.  After RIH with shoe joint, fill the joint with mud .

      2. M/U 18 5/8” casing to the top of shoe joint and RIH o TD(+/-60m)

      3.  Pull back 1m and land the casing on the casing spider.

      4. RIH with cement stinger on 5” DP . Space out 10m above The shoe and circulate the

       volume of 18 5/8” casing.

      Note: Cementing stinger to be made up 5” HWDP (all pre-drifted)

      5.  Ensure that a centralizer is installed on the 5” DP Approx. 1-2m above cement stinger.

      6. R/U circulating lines and cement head +/- 1.5m above rig floor .

      7.  Stab in the cement stinger, fill the annulus with mud, monitor the annulus for leaks

         during cementing, to ensure that stinger seal nipple as in place..

      8.  Circulate 150% of annulus volume

      9. Mix and pump cement, monitor for losses and reduce pumping rate if experiencing any

          losses.

Note: The 18 5/8” casing will be cemented in one stage and cement program will be issued at

due time by cementing service company

7.2. CEMENTING

     Preparation procedures

              Prior to starting or during any job:
         •  Conduct a safety meeting with all personnel involved
         •  The cement unit will be thoroughly checked
         •  The lines will be flushed with mixing water
         •  The lines will be tested to 3500 psi
         •  Volume calculations and displacement will be done by Co. man ,mud Eng. and

            cement Eng.
         •  The cement density will be checked by the mud Eng. on location


      Cementing procedure

           The cementing procedure will be as follows:
         •  R/U cementing head and circulating lines.
         •  Pump the required pre-flush and spacer fluids
         •  Drop bottom plug(for casings) ,Mix and pump the lead and tail slurry  , monitor for

              losses and reduce rate if losses are observed
         •  Drop top plug and displace the cement to collar
         •  Bump the plug

                   Note: record and report final displacement pressure prior to Bump plug

                Max. over displacement is the volume equivalent to ½ shoe track

                        If plug bumps,casing is to be pressure tested just after bumping the plug

                     to 1000 psi for 10 min.
         •  Bleed off pressure and check for back flow
         •  R/D cement head and circulating lines.
