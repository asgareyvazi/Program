    2. Job Procedure

                                  Job Procedure

                                             Pre Job Procedure

       1- Clean up mud tanks and pump truck's tanks, preparing chemicals for mixing fluid.

       2- Pressure test cement line to 500 psi for 5 minutes and the slowly increase the pressure to 3000 psi and hold for 5 minutes.
     Then release the pressure to the pump trucks.

       3- Cement silos hoses, mud pits and pump trucks unit lines needs to be flushed properly.

       4- Check the rig mud balance with fresh water and make sure it’s calibrated.

       5- Pre job safety meeting with involved personnel and arrange with mud engineer to measure mud & cement loss/gain during
      cement job.

                                          Job Procedure

       1- Before start mixing cement additives, get Company man approval.

       2- Prepare slurry mix fluid in mud tanks.

       3- Preparing mixing fluids according to the below:

        * Cement Solution Mixing Procedure:
     Cement Solution mixing procedure:
          • Fill up mud tanks with Drill Water (we need 201.8 bbl D.W with dead volume)
          • Add Antifoam to Drill water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.
          • Add Bentonit to Drill water and allow mixing for 45 minutes.
          • Add Cacl2 to Drill water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.

       4- Preapare CMT Unit Truck , Lin Up to Spoll Side outlet and Press Test.

       5- Mix and pump 300 bbl cement slurry ( 105 pcf - pump rate: 4-6 bpm).

       6- Rig Down cement lines.
       7- EOJ.

                                              Caution and Notes

       1- Put slurry cement samples to water bath.
        Retain 2.5 gallon of mixing fluid from the tanks and 5 kg of the G cement used in the job.
       2- All numbers & volumes related to drill string capacity, pump rate and well geometry have been assumed based on
        initial assumptions however actual figures will be calculated and executed as per company plan.
