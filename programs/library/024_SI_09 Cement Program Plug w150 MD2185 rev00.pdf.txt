    2. Job Procedure
                                                               J ob Procedure

                                  Client   PECO                      Rig Name   207                          Job Type           CMT PLUG

                                   Field     Siahmakan                 Well Name  #09                          Design Date               24-Apr-23

                                                         Pre Job Procedure

        1-Clean up tanks (mud tanks and pump truck's tanks) and preparing chemicals for mixing fluid.

         2- Pressure test cement line to 500 psi for 5 minutes and the slowly increase the pressure to 3000 psi and hold for 5 minutes. Then release the
        pressure to the pump trucks.

         3- Cement silos hoses, solution tanks, mud pits (which were used for making solution and spacer) and pump trucks unit lines needs to be flush
          properly.

         4- Check the loss severity while final mud circulation before cement job.

         5- Check the rig mud balance with fresh water and make sure it’s calibrated.

         6- For any change in the program or volumes please contact to SARVAK Cementing Leader.

        7-Pre job safety meeting with involved personnel and arrange with mud engineer to measure mud & cement loss/gain during cement job.

                                                       Job Procedure

        1-Prepare spacer in mud tanks.

           2-Fill Mud Tank with Drill water (Slurry: we need 57 bbl Drill water(without dead volumes).

        Main cement Solution mixing procedure:
        Add enough Drill Water to the Mud Tank according to its dead volume (we need 57 bbl without dead volume).
        Add Anti Foam to Drill water and allow mixing for 5 minutes.
        Add Salt and allow mixing for 30 minutes.
        Add O-Cfr8 and allow mixing for 10 minutes.

          * Spacer Mixing procedure:
           • Add Drill water to mud tank according to dead volume ( we need 35 bbl without dead volume)

       4-Pump 30.6 bbl spacer as ahead spacer (62.4 pcf with 6 bpm).

         5- Mix 120 bbl Main cement slurry & Pump it in the well (150 pcf with 5 bpm).

       6-Pump 4.37 bbl Spacer as behind Spcer (3 bpm).

         7- Displace with 107.8 bbl mud (with 6 bpm rate).

         8- Pull out of the hole 10 stands drill pipes (the number of wet stands might be changed according to finalized volumes and drillpipes size).

         9- Rig down cement line

         10- WOC

                                                           Caution and Notes

         1- Check the Mud Pumps for its efficiency and line up to ensure Mud pumping before displacement at 500psi by closing low torque on cement
        head. If OK, resume displacement by opening valve.

         2- Put slurry cement samples to water bath .Retain 2.5 gallon of mixing fluid from the tanks and 5 kg of the G cement used in the job.

         3- Displacement volume & spacer behind volume needs to be calculate on rig by company man and Cementer as per update casing tally.
