4      Health, Safety, Environment and Quality (HSE & Q)


4.1   HSE

       The South Pars 6,7,8 goals are:

           •  Zero injuries.
           •  Zero unintended spill to sea.
           •  Zero dropped objects.
           •  Zero serious well control situations.
           •  Rig prepared for H2S.
           •  High standard on housekeeping.


        For theese wells, the special HSE focus points will be:

           •  Safe Job Analysis (SJA) shall be used for planning of all non-standard operations.
           •  Good communication is the key to good teamwork. Radio communication should be used
              between crewmembers to improve communication.
           •  Pre-job meetings shall be held prior to rigging up slickline and MPSP toolstring and prior
                 to commencing the production logging survey.
           •  Rig and crew will be prepared for H2S. Regular drills will be held.
           •  Derrick will be inspected regularly for loose objects or potential falling objects.
           •   Statoil shall report all incidents and non-conformances in Statoil Synergi system.

4.1.1   Risk assessment

        During the detailed planning phase, prepared risk logs have been prepared for each major
         operation. The risks are classified using a South Pars risk matrix.

       The risk logs will be re-visited before the start of each operation, and continuously updated if new
         risks are identified. The risk logs can be used offshore during the operations.



4.1.2   H2S

               •  Reservoir fluid contains H2S.
               •  The rig shall be prepared for H2S (training, procedures and protective gear)
               •  Crew shall be masked up when bleeding off lubricator pressure, fluid sampling during
                   flow and any operation where well content can be released.
               •  Test lines & burner booms are installed and hooked up to X-mas tree.
               •  All equipment; slickline, downhole tools and wireline rig up to be suited for H2S
                      service.



4.2     Quality

       As a part of the Quality Control of the completion programme for the well, a Complete and Test
        Well On Paper session was arranged, March 10, 2004.

        For theese wells, special quality focus points will be:
               •  Ensure that relevant experiences from the previous production wells in the South Pars
                     Field is captured and included in the operations programs and procedures.
               •  Perform production logging operations without well control incidents.
               •   Checklists prepared for all equipment prior to start of operations, with copy to Statoil.
               •  Procedures planned for all operations prior to start of operations, with copy to Statoil.


4.2.1   Quality Rating System

          Statoil’s Quality Rating System has been integrated into the Daily Drilling Report (DBR, version
         5.1) and is a part of the “Operations window”. Down/failure time reported, will be connected to
         responsible company (where applicable), and reference to any Synergi report made on the
         incident.

5    Run MPSP log


5.1     Objective

       Run flowing and shut in passes with MPSP (Memory Production Services Platform) to identify
        producing zones and monitor their individual contribution to total production. A pressure build up
        periode will be planned to monitor flowing and shut-in downhole pressure in order to evaluate
        well productivity.


5.2     Risk Evaluation

               •  Junk falls in the well while R/U WL equipment.
               •  Loss of WL tool string in hole.
               •  Gas w/ high H2S content.
               •  Risk of closing valve on tool or wire.
               •  Unsuccessful acquiring of data.


5.3   HSE Focus Points

               •  Dropped object hazard.
               •  Maintain good housekeeping on the drill floor at all times.
               •  Perform pre job meetings with all involved personnel.
               •  Communication during WL operations. Communicate through driller, Geoservices,
                   Schlumberger, Halliburton and Statoil.
               •  Well control/ continuously log correct valve status.
               •  Gas with H2S in wireline lubricator (gas can migrate to surface).
               •  Avoid operations on two levels without two barriers(use a protective shield around
                      rotary)


        Only essential personnel outside accommodations while flowing well. Planned duration up to 10
         hrs

      RIH WITH MPSP TOOLSTRING



5.5.1   Perform pre-job meeting with involved personnel (can be done off rigtime). Review contingency
        procedures.


5.5.2    Install appropriate fixed choke to achieve 85 MMscf/d. (estimated 64/64”)


5.5.3    Install MPSP tool string


5.5.4   Connect riser. Open swab valve (SV).


5.5.5    Verify that DHSV control line pressure is to 520 bar and DHSV is kept open. Geoservices to
        observe pressure on DHSV C-line as long as W/L is in the well.


5.5.6    Pressure test slick line rig-up to 4350 psi / 300 bar /10 min with Halliburton through TKV against
        closed HMV, Geoservices choke manifold and WL-lubricator. Use 50/50 MEG/water for pressure
          testing.

        Monitor pressure in Geoservices DAS unit. Check valve installed in line to Halliburton.


5.5.7   Bleed pressure to SIWHP 289 bar on Geoservices choke manifold. Close Geoservices choke
        manifold. Leave Geoservices choke closed while RIH with slick line.


5.5.8   Open HMV.


5.5.9    Start RIH with slick line. Run in hole with the tool string. Run slowly through DHSV. (160 - 180m
     MD) Monitor pick up weights according to Schlumberger procedures.


5.5.10   Stop at 40 m above the top of predrilled liner (XXXX m MD). Monitor pick up weight according
         to Schlumberger procedures at this depth.
