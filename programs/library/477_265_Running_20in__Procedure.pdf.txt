        TD 24” Hole and Run 20” Pipe Procedure
                 San Jacinto #2 Prospect

    1. TD well at 11,615’
      ▪   Pump 50 bbl / 16 lb hi vis sweep timed to reach bottom at TD
      ▪   Follow sweep with ½ hole volume 12.5 ppg WBM (straight mud no adds)
      ▪   Follow the WBM with 1 ½ hole volumes of pad mud (lo-fluid loss)
      ▪   Pump at 1500-1700 gpm; rotate 30 rpm; reciprocate 50’ max

    2. Wiper Trip
       ▪   Shut down pumps and flow check
       ▪   Pull into 36” shoe until top stabilizer reaches 36” WH, try to work thru any tight spots w/o rotating and
                if possible ream any tight spots on way back down.
       ▪    If reaming is necessary, rotate 45-50 rpm; flow rate about 1200 gpm; use 12.5 pad mud (lo fluid loss)

    3. POOH
       ▪    If no reaming is necessary during the wiper trip then we will not pump another pad mud treatment
       ▪   Spot 50 bbls of 16 lb mud on bottom (about 75 feet)
       ▪  POOH
       ▪   IF we do pump another pad then pump as follows
       ▪   Pull up to top of pill (75’) and pump 1½ hole volume 12.5 pad mud
       ▪   Shut down pumps and monitor for flow
       ▪  POOH to well head,
       ▪  Wash landing profile in 36” with jet sub
       ▪  POOH until bit is at WH, get mark on DP at rotary and measure w/5’ stick to nearest stand connection
          coming out, calculate WH depth using the DP measurements and BHA length
       ▪  Once out of hole have ROV verify that it is clean
       ▪  Once clear of wellhead, move rig 200’
       ▪  POOH and rack-back BHA for next well

    4. RU to run 20” casing

    5. Run 20” casing
       ▪   Paint white hash-marks every 10 feet as RIH
       ▪   Fill casing every stand
       ▪  Run pipe according to tally with centralizers

    6. RU and run inner string (5 ½ XT 55)
       ▪  Run pipe w/ cement diverter sub on string
       ▪  Run pipe according to tally

    7. PU FMC 18 ¾” hi pressure wellhead RLSA per FMC procedure
       ▪   Fill up casing (per FMC procedure in moonpool) after making up wellhead

    8. Run to Mud Line on DP
       ▪  RIH on DP to approximately 30 feet above wellhead – make sure to fill DP every std w/ fill-up tool and
          screw in every 10th stand and break circulation

    9. RIH
       ▪   Finish moving rig back over location and stab into 36” wellhead housing
       ▪   Ensure heave compensation in active mode
       ▪  DO NOT LOWER DP WITHOUT VISUAL CONFIRMATION!

    ▪  Lower 20” inside 36” no more than 50’
    ▪   Displace DP and casing volume below stinger with 12.5 mud
    ▪  Run casing in hole to land-out at 3-5 mins per std
    ▪    If pumping is necessary, use 12.5 lb pad mud

10. MU cement stand and land out wellhead in 36” casing w/ 25K down
    ▪  Land and lock down well head per FMC
    ▪   Will not preload locking assembly until after the cement job
    ▪   Pull 50K over to verify wellhead locked
    ▪   Set back down maximum of 50K

11. RU and Cement
   ▪    Test lines with Halliburton, or a lesser company if Halliburton is unavailable
   ▪   Pump 200 bbls of 12.5 ppg WBM to verify floats are open (rig pumps) and switch to cementing unit.
      Pump spacer for last 100 bbls
   ▪   Pump cement job per procedure
   ▪    Displace cement w/ water to 50’ below inner string
   ▪   Check floats and set FMC shallow water pack-off and rigid lock wellhead W/ Oceaneering per FMC
   ▪     If floats don’t hold, pump bled-off volume back and hold
   ▪    Release FMC running tool
   ▪  PU cement stand enough to clear WH with RT and displace casing with 12.5 ppg WBM
   ▪   Pump Halliburton Nerf balls to clean DP

12. POOH
    ▪   Clear well head and move rig 200 feet off location
    ▪   Pull to surface (can be started while rig is moving)
    ▪   Inspect wellhead with ROV
