                     IAKSSDU

                                 Drilling without riser shallow gas procedures

A. PREPARATION AND SAFETY REQUIREMENT

    1.  Hold safety meeting for all crew; assess the high risks and declare the procedures in the event that shallow gas is
        encountered. Specify the responsibility for all personnel on board.
    2.   All anchor winches to be in working order with sufficient chain in lockers for winch off..
    3.  Check flammable gas detectors and rig alarm system.
    4.  Check the lifeboats and emergency generators to ensure they are working normally.
    5.  Hold an abandon rig drill, the standby boat will participate the drill
    6.   In the real event unnecessary persons will be evacuated to boat.
    7.  Check the fire system around the cellar deck and spray system at windlass.
    8.   Strictly carry out hot work permit regulations.
    9.  Shut down the watertight doors and hatch covers.
    10. Ensure the top drive, tongs, hydraulic catheads and manual elevators are working properly in case of emergency releasing
          drilling tubular.
    11. Ensure that the mud pumps and mud lines are working normally and mix two times the whole well volume with kill mud at
       any time.
    12. The pits filled with kill mud or hi-density mud besides the other pits full of seawater.
    13. Both deck cranes must prepare personal baskets for possible evacuation.
    14. While drilling the hole, designated personnel assigned to winch duty to move the rig upwind.
    15. Observe sea current, weather condition and wind direction, to determine the direction the rig will be moved.
    16. The stby. boat must standby up-wind and up-current when drilling. BCO to record wind and current from standby boat hourly
    17. Enough radios must be ready for communication.
    18. The offshore helicopter should be standby at base at any time.

B.  DRILLING FORMALITY

    1.  Make up drilling string assembly.
      Use float valve in the string.
    2.  RIH to seabed and tag water depth.
    3.  Run ROV to seabed beside the TGB to observe drilled returns and hole. A designated man will observe the moon pool and
       sea around the rig for any gas bubbles visible at surface.
    4.  Use seawater for drilling ahead, and pumping high viscous mud after every stand drilled.
    5.  Hold rate of penetration at controlled rate.
    6.    If any status like flowing, stop drilling and circulate to observe for 5 to 10 minutes:
    7.  Survey prior to circulate clean, fill hole with hi-vis/hi-wt. mud as required.
    8.   Drill to 8-1/2” pilot hole bottom with seawater, sweep hole clean with hi-vis. POOH prior to displace 1.5 times the whole well
       volume with hi-vis/hi-wt. mud as required.

C  SHALLOW GAS PROCEDURES  ACTIONS

        During drilling  if observed or discovered shallow gas, stop drilling, but keep circulating and observe, pump as soon as
    possible to keep pressure balance. Inform, OIM, Company man and Captain. Estimate well condition immediately, flow being
    small medium large. According to result, procedure is following meanwhile report the condition to the base.
     1.   If emit gas bubbles or mini gas current on the sea surface, POOH one single joint and stop pump to observe; resume drilling
               if no bubble.
     2.   If increased gas bubbles or gas current, circulate the hi-density mud and observe the well for change, then drill ahead slowly.
     3.   If continue to increase gas bubbles, sound the gas alarm, use max pump rates with kill mud to kill gas current. If no high
        density kill mud remains, pump mud and sea water. Report to company man and OIM immediately. Observe the gas current
         situation, if stopped and no gas current coming to surface, resume drilling at controlled rate.
     4.   If there appears massive gas flow/current, very evident on sea surface, and endangering the rig and personnel safety (note:
        only on the instruction from OIM/Companyman):


          (1) Sound General Alarm and make PA announcement for all personnel to report to muster stations upwind. Gas Alert / Alarm
          (2) Inform Standby Vessel and Shore Base of shallow gas and the rig preparing to move off location.

                (4) Drill crew will follow N.D.C.O. Procedure for dropping drill pipe:

           a. Have a spare set of 5 ½ inch manual drill pipe elevators next to rotary table with 2 way sling on ears of elevators and air hoist sling
            running through snatch block attached to back up post to pull elevators from around drill pipe. Have another air hoist attached to latch
              of elevators and running through snatch block at tong back-up post to open elevators.

           b.  Lock DSC and pick up to max. Derrick height with pumps at max. strokes per min.

           c.  Stop pumps, transfer electrical power to winches and spot tool jt. Latch elevators under tool jt.

           d.  Set enough wt. down on elevators to break the connection using rig tongs, put the TDS on neutral then break pipes by rig tongs, (just
            break the connection to some amount)

D   RELEASE ANCHOR CHAIN EMERGENCY PROCEDURE

     1. Open spray system of windlass, prevent from spark while releasing anchor chain.
     2.  Slack off the anchors chain on back wind and back current.
     3.  Pull the opposite direction anchors chain, move the rig towards forward wind and forward current to left location; meanwhile
        release the side anchors chain, keep from rig inclination, and according to condition, ballast properly.
     4.  Fix every anchor chain properly.
