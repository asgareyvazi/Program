1- PRE-TEST   PREPARATION
 1-1 EQUIPMENT PREPARATION ON RIG SITE
 1-1.1 The test equipmentshall be preparedas per OaK, ICOFC, Pishgaman"Specification
for the Rig Site Preparation & testing of  well Testing and Ancillary Equipment".
                                                                                                                                                       \
The test equipment  will be thoroughly checked and inspected by the Pishgaman Testing
Supervisor.
The Rig site check  will ensure  the following  as a minimum;
a) The test equipment  matches  specification.
b) The  certification   is up to date and will remain so during the test.
c) All equipment   is pressure  and function  tested.
d) All meters  have been benched  calibrated  and  relief valves  certified.  e) All gauges  are
within  calibration  period.
f) All crossovers  have been ordered  and inspected.  Ensure  that there  is sufficient  back up
for each crossover.


 1-1.2 The downhole completion equipment will be carefully checked, tested and prepared
in sub-assemblies  at OaK Base at West Paydar. The equipment  will be prepared as pe~
OaK "Specification  for the refurbishment, make-up, testing, storage and handling of well
completion assemblies.

1-2 EQUIPMENT PREPARATION
1-2.1  Completion   string: When  the tubing  arrives on the  rig, off load the crates and racks
       the tubing  separating  each  layer with  at least  three  evenly  spaced  wooden   strips.
     Do not packs  the tubing  tightly  together,  allow  sufficient  room  to  roll the tubing  for
        inspection.
1-2.1  Remove  box and pin protectors,  clean threads and inspect  connections   for damage.
1-2.2   Drift each joint from box to pin with appropriate  tubing  drift.
1-2.3  Measure  the tubing  (total length  less make-up  loss*), and clearly number each  joint.
1-2.4   Refit thread  protectors,  hand  tight.
1-2.5  Check pup joints  for space  out, and inspect and  drift as per full joints.
