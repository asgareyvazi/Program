
Drilling Fluids Program for 17 1/2" Hole Section
After cementing the 20" casing, it is required to prepare the new mud system "PHPA Mud System for 17 1/2" Hole section and displace the hole before drilling the new section.
Mix every batch based on the following formulation:
Depth: 100m – 600m
If ROP is low, Pump Salt Saturated Water. In addition, pump Salt Saturated Water every 150m.
Monitor the shale shaker for cuttings discharge. Make sure that they stick to each other. If they are not sticky add 1 Can for every 20 meter drilling.
To clean the hole, pump Hi-Vis Pill as Required or every 100m by using XC polymer/PAC R
Every 10 meter add 1 Sacks of Torque-Low ( 1-125/300-400 ) from the suction tank.
Depth: 600m – 800m
If ROP is low, Pump Salt Saturated Water. In addition, pump Salt Saturated Water every 150m.
Monitor the shale shaker for cuttings discharge. Make sure that they stick to each other. If they are not sticky add 1 Can for every 10 meter drilling.
Every 10 meter add 1 Sacks of Torque-Low ( 1-125 / 300-400 ) from suction tank.
To clean the hole, pump Hi-Vis Pill as Required or every 100m by using XC polymer/PAC R
Depth: 800m – 1400m
If ROP is low, Pump Salt Saturated Water. In addition, pump Salt Saturated Water every 150m.
Monitor the shale shaker for cuttings discharge. Make sure that they stick to each other. If they are not sticky add 1 Can for every 10 meter drilling.
Every 10 meter add 1 Sacks of Torque-Low ( 1-125/300-400 ) from the suction tank.
To clean the hole, pump Hi-Vis Pill as Required or every 100m by using XC polymer/PAC R
