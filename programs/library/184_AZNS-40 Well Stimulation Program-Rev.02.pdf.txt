1.  Executive Summary

The main objective of this plan is to stimulate (wash method) the producing interval zone in well AZNS-

40 vertically completed into Fahliyan Formation with total depth of 4200 mMD (-4186.55m TVDss).

Finally, the well potential & reservoir characteristics are to be determined via well testing analysis.

Staged-Acidizing is adopted to be run from the bottom of perforation (BOP ≈ 4,112 mMD) to the top of
perforation (TOP ≈ 4,039 mMD). Drag CT from toe to the heel while pumping 191bbl of 15% HCl acid
with suitable additives to spot the toe (49.15gal/ft. as acid index). This is followed by increasing pump rate

as much as possible during operation with pump pressure limited (MAWHP) advised by the Client at

wellsite; then, flow back & POOH CT to surface.

1.5 Objectives of operation

    1.  Reduce formation damage & wash out the remained debris & residuals of completion & drilling
       operations (depth penetration ≤ 2.8m).

    2.  Achieve & ensure uniform distribution of acidizing fluids in the producing interval using CT.

    3.  Achieve good clean up of production string from contaminated fluids & solids.

    4.  Safe operation & special precautions both operationally & technically.

    5.  Increase injectivity through Coiled Tubing acidizing.

    6.  Investigation of stimulation performance by comparing pre- & post-acid parameters.

    7.  All real time data, including coiled-tubing, pumping, nitrogen & testing data shall be transferred

        to production supervisor as per requested.

2.  Operation Job Sequence Details

Important Note: In order to ensure that production packer is not exposed to abnormal pressure during
all stages of operation especially well flowing periods, if annulus pressure increases more than 500psi,

immediately decrease annulus pressure to normal conditions by either decreasing choke size or bleeding

off annulus pressure (based on Client’s decision).

2.1 Preparation & Commissioning Equipment before Job

    1.  Hold pre-job safety meeting (PJSM) & tools box meeting with Production Supervisor, Coiled

      Tubing Supervisor, Slick line Supervisor, Well testing Supervisor & pump operator.

    2.  Monitor Annulus & wellhead Pressure

    3.  Complete/finalize rig up & pressure test testing & stimulation equipment

    4.  Connect Well Testing Flow line to XMT production valve

    5.  Connect all transmitter to Well Testing DAS

2.2 Slickline Operation

    6.  Prepare SL running equipment & rig up lubricators & BOP on X-mas tree based on program.

      Perform the assembling procedure of the surface equipment.

      Rig up Slick Line PCE as per Service Company stack up layout.

    7. RIH SL with 3.300” Gauge Cutter to SSSV depth & then POOH to surface.

    8. RIH SL with P.S. running tools to set the (AF profile, 3.437", ID: 2.9") P.S. on SSSV & then

    POOH to surface.

    9. RIH SL with 2.200’’ or 2.250” Gauge Cutter to maximum depth & then POOH to surface.

2.3 Clean-up & Well Test
    10. Flow the well at different choke sizes for clean-up. Measure & record BS&W, Weight, pH… of

        effluent fluids every 30 min according to approved procedure in this program.

          In case of well does not flow, follow operation from item 13.

2.4 CT-N2 Lifting Operation

    13. Prepare CT running equipment & rig up injector head & BOP on XMT based on program.

      Perform the assembling procedure of the surface equipment.

      Rig up CT PCE as per service company stack up layout.
    14. Make up the connector on CT pipe & pull test  it up to 10,000lb. in presence of Production

       Supervisor, then make up BHA according to the agreement prior to job in the PJSM.

    15. After complete rig up, perform pressure tests according to approved procedure in this program.

    16. After pressure test, deploy Jet nuzzle on CT BHA.

    17. CT Pipe acid pickling according to approved procedure in this program.

    18. Start CT RIH, with minimum speed (<15 ft. /min) while pass the protection sleeve / 3SV.
         Continue RIH with speed 40ft. /min. Decrease CT speed to <15ft. /min 200ft. to target lifting

           depth.

    19. While CT RIH, pump Diesel with minimum rate.

    20. Start to pump N2 at target lifting depth.

    21. CT POOH to surface & rig down CT PCE.

2.5 Well Test & FGS

3.  Fundamental Geology Characteristic

The Fahliyan formation is deepest reservoir among the target reservoirs. The reservoir is shallow marine

carbonates & produces light oil, unlike the north Azadegan, Fahliyan reservoir is important oil bearing

layer in Azadegan. The structural configuration of the Fahliyan is generally similar to the other overlying

Cretaceous reservoir.

          Zone Name/Interval MD (TVDSS)                  Calcite%     Dolomite%   Clay%

           Fahliyan Vertical Well/ 4039-4112                     90            8            2.0

Based on the core study in AZNS for early wells, Fahliyan layers are characterized as follow:

Fah-1: It is characterized by deposition of gray packstone/wackestone, medium-low energy depositional

environment. The rock is linen, tight, oil free. The visible dissolution pore are not contained, developed

horizontal fractures & open fractures contain oil can be observed in local cores.

Fah-2: The component of core is mainly packstone & wackestone which represent medium-low energy

depositional environment. The cores are wrapped with light crude oil, they are homogeneous & have

good oil shows & are with visible dissolution pores.

Fah-3: The component of core is mainly blocky packstone & wackestone which represent medium-low

energy depositional environment. The oil-bearing cores show dark color & make a sharp contrast with

the tight surrounding gray rock which showed heterogeneity, the component of cores is tight & oil

saturation is low.

Fahliyan reservoir distribution showed that compared Fah-1, Fah-2 dos not very much in general & has

better reservoir properties than Fah-1. Besides, Fah-3 has the highest mean PHI & K in Fahliyan

formation. Although, this is contrary to the table-2.

6.3  Strategy of Acidizing

Following measures can be adopted in order to enhance the success rate, efficiency of acidizing &

achieve a good effect on increasing productivity based on elementary acidizing theory & test:

    a)  Considering the temperature of the formation to be treated.

    b)  Select appropriate acid additives, to avoid corrosion, sludge & any problem that damage pay

       zone.

    c) Enhance the injecting pressure & flow rate as high as possible to form the “wormhole” as much

       as possible & deepen its depth.

    d) Design effective pre-flush & Overflush treatment for reservoir conditioning & break any formed

        gel.

6.4 Stimulation Main Challenges

Given the high temperature (>250 oF), reaction rate will be definitely reduced during conventional

treatment. Therefore, a de-efficiency factor (0.8) will be considered for overall stimulation efficiency.

6.7  Stimulation Fluids & Stages Introduction

In aim to achieve the best stimulation results following Stimulation fluids are proposed.

Preflush:  Preflush acts as a spacer between reservoir fluid & main fluid, guarantees the water wet

condition of reservoir rock, eliminates the risk of acid/oil sludge formation & facilitates post acid flow

back process.

Main Acid 15% HCl: HCl is the most suitable treatment fluid for stimulation of carbonate (including

dolomite) reservoirs which can dissolve both rock matrix & damage. The dissolution process leads to

clean up of hydrocarbon flowing path.

Over-Flush: Overflush solution contains mutual solvent acting as a multifunctional non-ionic agent

which is soluble in oil, acid & fresh water, which improves the clean-up of spent acid following

stimulation treatment.

  1.   Have a good pre-job meeting with the whole crew, inform the crane driver & check with him the

       purpose.

       case they may be located within 3 to 7 meters from the well center-line:
     Exhaust with anti-spark device & cooling
     Automatic shutdown by over speed detector system
     Auto-closing of air intake & auto-shut-down in the event gas is detected
     Conspicuous & easily accessible shut-down control

  7.   Ensure that effective firefighting capabilities are in place throughout the slickline operations &

7.7 Well Flow-Back General Guidelines

The maximum initial choke size should not exceed 1/6th the inside diameter of the surface manifold or

the tubing in the well.

Examples:
     3 1/2 tubing - 1/2 inch or number 32 choke
     2 7/8 tubing - 13/32 inch or number 26 choke
     2 3/8 tubing - 5/16 inch or number 20 choke

During flow back as surface pressure decreases, larger chokes may be used to maintain flow. The

manifold downstream of the choke should be as straight as possible to minimize erosion. Always open

the upstream valve first, then the downstream valve.

Always close the downstream valve first, then the upstream valve. Lo-Torc & other stop valves are not to

be used as chokes. Always assume the presence of combustible hydrocarbons.

  1.  On completion of the acid job, open well for flow back to choke manifold (Choke must be closed

        at this stage for initial reading).

  2.    Prior to opening well ensure downstream lines are open to flare boom

  3.    Initial Opening – Open to flare gradually commencing on a 16/64 adjustable choke & increasing

      choke as required. Continue flowing to burner boom until clean.
     Ensure downstream pressure does not exceed pipe specifications (1,000psi as guideline)
     Note: Record all parameters every 5 minutes during the cleanup & then every 15 minutes when

       the well is stable on all choke settings.

Criteria for stable flow:
     Well head temp is changing at less than 2 deg per hour
     Well head pressure is changing less than 5 psi per hour
    BS&W at choke is less than 1%

  4.   Record BS&W every 30 minutes at choke manifold, check for H2S, CO2, pH & Chlorides. (If

       required take & document fluid samples)

  5.   Once the cleanup has been established close the well at the tree & bleed down lines

7.8 CT Acidizing Procedure

    1. RIH CT to the maximum depth as per above procedure.

    2.  Confirm the volume of stimulation fluids with Client representative to be mixed based on the

       access interval in the perforations.

    3.  Stop operations, & have a safety meeting prior to pumping:
     Determine wind direction & nominate a safe assembly point in the event of an emergency
     Discuss chemical & flammable hazards & state the location of the fire extinguishers, first aid kits,

      eyewash stations, & other safety equipment.
     Ensure that MSDS of all chemicals are available onsite.
     Clearly identify maximum pressures for both coiled cubing & injection pressures. CT pump

       pressure should not exceed 6,500 psi as per the line test
     Ensure water hose is accessible in the event of an acid spill.

    4.  Once CT reach to the maximum depth.

    5.  Start mixing the volume of stimulation fluids as per the attached recipe.

     Add Anti-Sludge Agent to the mixing solution & mix thoroughly

     Add Non-Emulsifier Agent to the mixing solution & mix thoroughly

     Add Iron Control Agent to fresh water & mix the solution thoroughly

     Add Corrosion Inhibitor to the solution while continue mixing

     Add H2S Scavenger Agent to the mixing solution & mix thoroughly

      Finally, add raw HCl to the solution & continue mixing for 15 minutes

    6.  Reset the volume counter & start pumping proposed acid treatment as per “Pumping Schedule”

        section.

    7.  Ensure good communication is kept between the pump operator & coiled tubing supervisor &

        that regular check-ins are made regarding pump rate, total volume pumped etc. to ensure program

         is being followed as per design.
