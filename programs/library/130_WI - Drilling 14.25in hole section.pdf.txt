                                  Work Instruction
                       Drilling 14 1/4" * 12 ¼” hole section

      Objective:

        To combined-drilling 14 1/4” and 12 ¼” hole sections from 2729 m to ±3533 m
        MD (The exact TD will be confirmed by geologist based on cutting samples).
         Eliminate 11 ¾” liner

      HSE Focus points:
        We always have time to  rig up and operate safely:  Take “time-out”  if
           necessary
          Plan the job ahead, don’t run afterwards
         Focus on falling objects
          Avoid loose items on rig-floor and all items below rotary table.
          All equipment used above 2m has to be secured with wire.
        Take care handling rig tongs and heavy BHA components, keep back free
        No work has to be performed at two different levels without two barriers
         Two barriers: “silk wire” and cover
         Use barrier tape
         Keep people away
         Radio communication
          Respond back on communication
