                     20″ CEMENTING PROCEDURE (Well no. F2)

    1- Clean up tanks and prepare chemicals for mixing cement solution.

    2- Fill tanks with 200 bbl Fresh Water for mixing cement solution.

    3- Before running 20” conductor pipe finished; with Company man approval, start mixing cement

additives and prepare mixing fluid according below cement mixing procedure:

        Add TA-47 to Fresh Water and allow mixing for 5 minutes

        Add CaCl2 and allow mixing for 30 minutes.

        Add O-CFR3 and allow mixing until the cement operation will finish.

    4- Before running 20” conductor pipe, check the stinger and stab-in shoe & Install stab-in

shoe to end of first casing.

    5- After running 20” conductor pipe install stinger to end of first 5 " drill pipe.

    6- RIH to bottom with 5 " drill pipe, Circulate mud before setting the stinger.

    7- Set the stinger & circulate mud again. If you observe mud flowing in casing, try again

to set the stinger.

    8- Hang 5 " drill pipe.

    9- Pre job safety meeting with involved personnel.

   10- Test cement line W/ 3000 psi.

   11- Pump 70 bbl Fresh water as spacer.

   12- Mix and pump 275 bbl cement slurry. (118 pcf)

   13- Displace cement slurry with … bbl (short displace) drilling fluid.

   14- Sting out and check for float valve working.

   15- Circulate to clean out 20" conductor & Inner string.

   16- At the same time the cementer to wash pump truck and cement line & other things.

   17- Pull out inner string and lay down stinger.

   19- WOC
