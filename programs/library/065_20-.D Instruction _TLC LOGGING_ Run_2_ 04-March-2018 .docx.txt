Operation: Run #2 TLC
CONT POOH to Surf. & R/B all tubular, Meanwhile L/D 4 ¾” D.JAR, 6 1/16” S.STAB, 4 ¾” Bit Sub. B/O & L/D 6 1/8” T.C.I Bit.  Check Jar Body & Mandrel Check F.V after removing F/Bit Sub. Close shear ram as the bit reached to surface. Fill up the well by 74 pcf mud and monitor loss rate carefully.   Retrieve wear bushing    Hold PJSM  P/U & M/U SDL/DNSL/CSNG/CALIPER OTS Logging tools below Down Hole Connector Head (DWCH), surface test the tools (As per Logging supervisor instruction).  Max tools OD: 5 in (Connector Sub), tool length: 16.08 m Latch & unlatch P.W.D.H in Connector sub Logging BHA: Hole Finder: 0.53m, Bull Nose: 0.1m, SDLT: 3.3m, DSNT: 2.95m, CSNG: 2.49m, GTET: 2.6m, DTD: 1.16m, Swivel:0.98 m, Connector Sub:1.98 m Visual check, measure OD, ID, Length all logging tools  Pass pump down head & 2 1/8” rabbit thru all cross over inside string @ surf. Function test of caliper pads. While loading radioactive source, clear the rig floor unnecessary people.    RIH on TLC logging tools on 2 STDs 3 ½” HWDP + 94 STDs 3 ½” DP & followed by 34 STDs 5” DP to +- 3750 m. (latch depth).  Use safety clamp for 1st 15Stds 3 ½” DP. Pass 2 1/8” drift thru all drill pipe & Hammer DP Stds (If the rabbit could not pass any drill pipe, do not use that drill pipe.) Minimal amount of pipe dope brushing on the pin of DP while run in hole Max. running speed 2 min/stand (slip to slips). Pass gently BOP & WH. Took extra caution & RIH Gently while passing 7” L. Lap @1996.5m (call CO. Man two stand in advance) If took WT. Do Not Push more than 1Klb, First Call Company man. Fill up string (Used pipe screen) with 74 pcf fresh mud & break Circ for 15 min each 15 stds (Max. mud circulation pressure should be 300 psi) Put the CAP for each stand after Setting the slips. Set the WL unit behind catwalk  CIRC for 30-60 min with max press 300 - 400 psi on the last stand before Connecting S.E.S  Meanwhile R/U WL Upper & Lower sheaves, Pass the cable through.  Pass PWCH Through CSES and P/U & M/U 5” Cable Side Entry Sub (CSES) ON STRG.   Pass WL cable trough CSES and connect to PWCH prior to M/U CSES Wire line specification: cable OD: 15/32 in”, Max. Tension: 18000 lbs, W.P:4700 lbs.  Running Cable Through DP, Follow OTS Instruction for Latching PWCH to The DWCH.   M/U one STD above CSES, Run PWCH till the PWCH almost 150 m above DWCH [latch depth@3750m], Start pumping. Max SPP:400 psi pressure as per OTS Eng. instruction & procedure. Confirm latch & check the isolation.  Pumping should continue until advised by the OTS Wireline Engineer to stop. This is to insure a good mechanical latch and release 30 m Cable into DP. Test logging tools signal: The OTS Logging Engineer shall check insulation of cable phases and confirm that there is a good electrical and mechanical latch prior to continuing with the program.  Once latch is confirmed, slowly decrease the mud pump until zero while OTS engineer checks the latch electrically and performs a tool operational check. The SES cable clamp is installed and pull test, the rig floor must be cleared for this test. Pull test cable with 4500 lb tension and hold for 3 min.  Log down F/3750 to 4685 m (10 m off bottom). The drill pipes and rotary table must not be rotated. (TDS & Rotary table should be locked) Downhole compression/tension of OTS tools will be monitored by OTS Engineer.  When connecting/disconnecting the drill pipes, make sure the logging cable is not crushed between the DP and the slips. Cable should seat in the groove of the slips to avoid damaging the cable. String needs to be filled mud after latching and during logging to avoid possible unlatching. (every 8-10 stands)   Max. running speed 9 m/min.  Log up to 7” liner shoe @ 3750m. Rig floor workers should use the hammer to push the slips, because the tools  calipers are open during POOH, pushing the slips inside can keep the logging  tool calipers safe. After finishing the log, open cable clamp & weak points, unlatch the pump-down head and remove the cable. Max. Pulling speed 9 m/min.  Pull PWCH from latch point by WL to Surf. Pull out speed: 60m/min – 5 m/min   L/D 5” CSES, R/D WL Sheaves & Equipment  POOH to surf. & R/B all tubular & L/D all SDL/DNSL/CSNG/CALIPER logging tools. Max. pull out speed 2 min/stand (slip to slips) once cable & SES is removed from the DP Close shear ram as the logging tools reached to surface While removing radioactive source, clear the rig floor unnecessary people.       Notes:  No Crane operations are scheduled which would pass over the wireline while rigged up. If the string took weight, p/u string & call OTS supervisor & Company man. While taking log & POOH, Use hammer to place the DP slips correctly Do not apply more than 1000 lb weight on tools. One logging contractor personnel with portable radio to stay on rig floor during the entire operation. The tools do not tag the bottom of the hole. Good communications between rig personnel and wireline operator.  Ensure all fishing/strip over tools are on location prior to start of logging. After latching drilling crew should be careful about wireline cable specially  when putting the slips.  Safety recommendation: Work Safe; always ask yourself how can I get hurt if I do this task In case of any deviation or advice for this instruction call Co. Man ASAP All Rig crew should follow and cooperate with job specialists as per this instruction  Mud Eng. Is responsible for monitoring & report pit levels during operation H2S equipment should be maintained & functional at all-time due to drilling thru reservoir  SAED WSS
