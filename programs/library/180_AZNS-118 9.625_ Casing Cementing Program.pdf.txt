5. Cementing Procedure

5.1 PRE-JOB

(1) Prepare Lead cement blending 90t, Tail cement 17t.

(2) Fill up Tanks with 500bbls Fresh Water for mixing Fluid.

(3) Mix 283bbl fluid in water tank for lead slurry and spacer. Mix 98bbl fluid in water tank for tail slurry.
