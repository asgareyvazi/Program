Page 1 of 1                                     Procedure No8 SI-009

                  WELL SI-009 next program No 8

        Short History:
            •  Due to encountered complete loss @ 3084m (119m inside GS-3) and stuck string @

            3060m and due to bad hole condition decided to POOH & change 12-1/4” PDC Bit to

                R.Bit and L/D S.Stabilizer.
            •  During POOH, Observe gain up to 100BPH. Decided to run back to bottom and

               uniform MW to 149pcf, but observe obstruction @ 2217m, hard wash and ream to

            2230m and observed 50 BPH gain.
            •   Trying to killing the well with 149pcf @ 2191m was not successful and increasing

        MW due to mud condition was not applicable.

 Decided to POOH to surface with gain and RIH with smaller size of BHA to botton and killing

 the well as per below procedure:

     1) POOH to surface and L/D 12-1/4” PDC Bit & BHA.

        Note:
            •  During POOH with gain from Gachsaran, for controlling well, periodically close

                the well and bullhead equal to amount of volume gained and continue POOH.
            •  Due to not availability of Hydrill Rubber spare, it’s not recommended to strip

               out / strip in during trip.

     2) RIH W/ 8-3/8” Bit (Open Nozzles) and related BHA (without stabilizer) to bottom.

     3)  Circulate and uniform MW @ 149pcf and make sure well is static.

        Note:
            •  In case of loss, Use LCM Pill / Cement plug to cure loss.
            •  Check hole stability with various GPM.

     4) POOH to surface, L/D 8-3/8” Bit.

     5)  Next Program will be issued in due time.
