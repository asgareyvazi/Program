1. Introduction


     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers workover of well No.19, of Nar gas field, I.R.Iran. The well is gas
              producer from Upper Dalan formation right now.

            Problem
            The tubing annulus (9-5/8”x7”) pressure was checked 1700 psi. Extra long seal nipple
            and anchor seal are the most doubtful leakage areas.

           Main Objective
           The main objective of this program is to fix the problem and resume gas production
            from the same (Upper Dalan) formation.

             Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of
             workover request no. ٣٨/ چ ت ب /dated:١١٠ ,      ٣٨٣١ / ٢٠ / ٨٢ Zagros oil & gas production Co.

           The main head lines of this program are as follow:
                1.  Prior to rig move, check wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record well head
                   pressures; investigate for communication of all the annuluses.
                2. Move and rig up the drilling rig.
                3.  Connect and pressure test kill, flow and return lines.
                4.  Kill and secure well
                5.  Test all the p-seals of tubing and casing spools and fix the problems at site.
                6. N/D well head and N/U BOP's
                7.  Pull out completion string
                8.  Scrap and clean inside 9-5/8" casing to packer depth.
                9.  Pressure Test DAB Packer and inside 9-5/8" casing using 9 5/8" RTTS
              10. Mill out and recover packer and its tail.(if needed)
              11. Run completion string
              12. N/D BOP's and N/U well head
              13. Flow the well
              14. Secure the well and release rig



              Attentions:

                •  The detailed program is illustrated here after in this document under the
                 head of "Workover Program".

                          Well Nar-19_Workover Program



 Second work over

     In order to fix the gas communication problem in well Nar-19, Rig Ghadr 15
    spudded on this well @ 1378/08/15. After primary investigations and pressure
     readings the well was killed through a complicated lubrication procedure and
     repeating wireline works. 71 pcf mud was displaced in both tubing and annulus.
    The hydrostatic pressure of this mud put the well in vacuum.
     After securing the well and N/U BOPs, the landing joint was screwed in tubing
    hanger and pulled out Hanger to rig floor, where observed leakage in bottom
     connection of the hanger. Tubing hanger and a double pin X -over were laid
    down and tubing string tested to 4000 psi OK. Same hanger was made up after
    some remedial jobs but failed under 4500 psi pressure test, due to leakage at
    same area.
   A new 10"x9 5/8"x7" BTC, OMV tubing hanger received from Tehran, after five
    days waiting, but because of 5cm longer neck, it didn't match the p seals of DSA,
    and ports of the control line. The tubing hanger and DSA were sent to Kangan
     refinery and then to Ahwaz main machine shop for modifications.
    Again and after 5 days waiting, received repaired items and made up the same
    on the well head and tubing correspondingly. Several attempts were made to
     stop leakages of the tubing hanger connections (the threads were sharp and the
     thread profile didn't mach the thread profile of the standard tools).
    The tubing hanger was set in the tubing spool but couldn't back off the landing
      joint, so pulled out the same and found thread damage in tubing hanger and
     landing joint both. Ran hanger by 7" spear and set the same in the spool. Tested
     the tubing and annulus OK, and released the rig @1378/09/09.



1.5     Production history:

     Well started production right after perforation @ 1372/10/18. A successful acid
     job was done to cure skin against perforations @ 1379/8/9. The well is producing
     at a rate of 1.536 million cubic meters per day.

2. Workover Program

                 1. Move workover rig from Sarkhun-6 to the well site of NAR-19, R/U and make  it
                ready for operation, Record rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from
               sea level. (An overall check on well head spools, valves, P-Seals and x-mass tree
                      is strongly recommended before rig move. After killing and securing the well, all
                 items must be checked and serviced by well head crew and repaired or replaced if
                 needed.)

                2.  Connect  kill & flow lines to 7-1/16"-5000 x-mas tree side outlets and connect
                   return line to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 4500 psi
                  pressure.

                3. Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 7" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x 13-
                   3/8" annulus and 13-3/8" x 20" annulus. Release / Bleed off pressures, monitor
                     their build up and investigate for the possible communications.

             Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
             while investigating the communications.

                4.  Rig up W/L and test to 4500 psi.Test control line to 4500 psi. Retrieve 3SV.
             Pump  oil into the control line and install Dummy and 2.750 X N plug in the
                  landing nipple of safety valve @ 71m and test to 4500 psi.
                5.  Bleed off the tubing pressure to 500 psi. Observe and record changes in tubing
               and annulus pressures. Retrieve 2.750 XN plug. (Dummy still in the place). R/D
                  wire line.
                6. Pump fresh water at least 1 1/2 volume of the tubing capacity (200 bbls) and
               squeeze the tubing content into the reservoir. The well will go under vacuum
                  since the  full column of fresh water imposes1314 psi over pressure to the
                   reservoir @ top perforations (2100m). If couldn't squeeze, lubricate the well, and
                observe to make sure about well condition.

             Note: Monitor the pressures of annuluses all the time.

                7.  Rig up wireline and test same to max 4500psi pressure. Install plug «OTIS-RN
                 4.313»in no go nipple @1592m. (Under Baker "DAB" packer @ ±1579.5m) .Test
                 plug and tubings to max 4500psi.Install «OTIS – XN 4.560"» into extra long seal
                bore receptacle. Test same to max 4500psi pressure. Monitor tubing annulus
                 pressure changes while testing inside tubing.

                8.  Release 7" x 9-5/8" annulus pressure through the burning line. If charged with
                   kicking gas, lubricate same using fresh water. Observe and Make sure about well
                   condition. Pressurise tubing annulus to max 2000 psi and check  it for any
                 pressure drop and any return from inside tubing.

                9.  While Circulating cross well head, install «RN 6.250"» two way back pressure
                  valve into 10"x9-5/8" neck x7" VAM "OMV" tubing hanger (top box threads were
                completely damaged in last work over) and test to 4500psi pressure.

Note: If B.P.V failed against pressure test or could not be set in tubing hanger,
it is recommended to install 2.750" plug into dummy of 3SV landing nipple

10. While circulating cross tubing head spool, L/D well head to top of 13-5/8"x11" -
   5000 tubing head spool. Check top side threads of the tubing hanger and confirm
       if damaged. N/U 13-5/8" BOP's stack; test same to 4500psi against two-way.
    Retrieve 6.250" RN two way back pressure valve from tubing hanger and 4.560"
  XN plug from extra long seal bore receptacle.

Note: Pre plan to provide 11"x13-5/8"-5000 adaptor flange to connect BOP's to
tubing head spool.

11. While circulating cross tubing head spool, if regret to make up landing joint due to
    failures on the tubing hanger top connection threads, make up and run suitable
   spear (to catch 7" 29#). Set the spear @ +/-3 meters below tubing hanger.
   Unscrew the tie down screws.
12. Sting out extra long seal bore receptacle Baker "HB" and circulate fresh water to
   clean hole. Check hole for any loss/flow. Fresh water imposes 698 psi over
    reservoir pressure @ 1579.5m, packer depth.

13. While circulating cross tubing head spool, pull out completion string; check all
   components for any damage to be fixed or replaced by new one.

14. RIH with appropriate tool and retrieve the mandrel of retrievable seal bore. UN-J
   anchor seal unit from "DAB" packer.

Note: Decision might be made to change tubing head spool in this phase. In
such a case a complementary program will be issued at due time.

15. RIH with 8-1/2" E-mill and 9-5/8" scraper, clean 9-5/8" casing to top of packer @
   1579.5m.

Note: If primary investigations dictates to check casing for possible leakage,
RIH with 9-5/8" RTTS, set same @ 1566m (some 13 m above packer). Test
packer to 2000 psi, then test inside 9-5/8" casing to max 2000 psi pressure for
any leakage. Meanwhile monitor 9-5/8"x13-3/8" annulus.


16. Build up 55 PCF oil emulsion mud and displace same in the well.

Note: 55 pcf oil emulsion mud impose 960 psi over pressure @ 2100m (Top Of
perforation).

17. Plan to recover Baker "A" retrievable "DAB" packer with appropriate retrieving
    tools and pull out the same.

18.  If could not retrieve the packer RIH with 8 3/8" junk mill and mill out the packer
       till it falls down. RIH with taper tap, catch and engage the remainings of packer.
  POH and L/D fish.

19. RIH with 5 7/8" junk mill and junk sub to top of cement plug @ PBTD 2390m,
    circulate and retrieve junks (if any).

20. RIH with 8 1/2" bit and 9 5/8" scraper to the liner lap and scrap the new packer
    setting depth @+/- 1560m ( at least two times).

21. Run completion string as per program. (A complementary program will be issued
    in due time).

Note: Since full column of gasoil has 398psi over pressure to the reservoir at
1579.5m, pre-plan to flow the well using nitrogen and coiled tubing.

22. Pressure test tubing annulus to 2000psi and inside tubing to 4500psi.

23. Shall all pressure tests are OK, set two-way check valve in tubing hanger and
    test same to 4500psi.

24. Nipple down BOP's and nipple up well head and test same to 5000 psi.

25. Remove two way check valve. R/U wireline equipment, test same to 4500psi.
   Remove all the plugs and retrievables from the completion string. Reinstall
  dummy in the SSSV landing nipple.

26. Displace annulus by completion fluid (gas oil + corrosion inhibitor) and inside
    tubing by gas oil prior to latch into packer.

27. Rig up coiled tubing equipment and connect nitrogen vessel to same. Run in
    tubing with coiled tubing and gas lift the well and let it flow on different choke
    sizes, clean the well and record flowing pressures after pure gas reaches to the
    surface.

   Note: It is recommended to inject a volume of gasoil with high rate into
   reservoir through tubing, then stop injection and let the gasoil to loss into
   reservoir and to make under balanced column of gasoil against reservoir
   pressure to cause the well flowing gradually.

28. Remove dummy and flash control line. Install 3SV and check its open/close
    position. Close 3SV.

29. Release TBG pressure above 3SV, test same against well pressure. Fill up same
    with packer fluid.

30. Secure well, L/D pipes, clean cellar and release the rig.
