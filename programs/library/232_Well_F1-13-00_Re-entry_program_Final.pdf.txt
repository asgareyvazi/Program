1.   Introduction

1.1  Program Introduction

This program covers the information and procedures required for workover operation and

Re-Entry plan for well F13-00 in Foroozan Field-Persian Gulf-IRAN.


Problem

  The well is shut down due to low wellhead pressure and increase water cut.


Main Objective:

The main objective of this program is to squeeze off the existing perforation and

and secure the well in order to open window in 9-5/8" casing at 2143mMD (-

2110.7mTVDss) (U.Dariyan) to Re-entry and horizontal drilling to 3841.0m MD

(2472.3 TVD)(816.3m horizontal leg) to produce oil from Yamama (L5) reservoir in

the F-3 area structure in Foroozan oil field near the Iran-Saudi Arabia border line.

3. Drilling History

F-13-0 well was located on the southern flank of the Foroozan  structure, two

kilometres south of the F-3 well. F-13-0 as vertical well was drilled from 2nd June

1973 to 22nd July 1973 and drilling operations was progressed down to the final

depth of 2560.2MD/RT  in the lower cretaceous yamama formation. No major

difficulties were encountered, except some washouts of drill pipe and minor mud

losses in the dolomite and limestone of Qurna formation (U.C.).


F-13-0 well was completed as a yamama producer with a stabilized rate of 3732

BOPD of 31.1° API gravity oil through 3/4" choke after acid treatment.


26" section was drilled to the depth of 230.4MD/RT with, after conditioned hole, 20″

casing was run and cemented at 230.4MD/RT.

17 1/2" section was drilled to the depth of 797.3MD/RT, after conditioned hole 13
3/8″casing was run and cemented at 797.3MD/RT.

12 1/4" section was drilled to 2560.2MD/RT, after conditioned hole 9 5/8″casing was

run and cemented at 2560.2MD/RT.


F-13-0 well was completed as a yamama producer. An open hole DST No.1 was

conducted on 22nd June 1973 in top portion of the Burgan "A" sand member (2057-

2067.6MD/RT) where  set packer  at 2057MD/RT. After reversed  out, collected

surface crude samples with 19.3° API gravity at 60 deg. F. The yamama formation

was perforated from 2449.86 to 2454.13MD/RT and production tested before and

after the acid treatment. flowing through 3/4" choke, recovered 33 API oil with trace

of water and Bs before acidizing. After acidizing the yamama formation was treated

with 3000 gallons of 15% HCL acid and displaced with 50 bbl of diesel subsequent

test produced 31.1 API oil at the rate of 3732 BOPD through 3/4 choke.

7.   Expected Litho-Stratigraphic Units

2143mMD    (-2110.7mTVDss)  in U.Dariyan  to  total depth  of 3841mMD  (-

Upper Dariyan:

From window of 2143mMD (-2110.7mTVDss) to 2200.5mMD (-2167.9mTVDss)

This interval  is made of inter-bedded pyritic Shale, Sandstone, Limestone, and

Dolomite with minor amount of Marl, Clay, and Siltstone. Towards the base,  it

member will be drilled in this well.

Lower Ratawi:

From 2476.4mMD (-2383.7mTVDss) to 2571.8mMD (-2416.1mTVDss)

The Lower-Ratawi member provides the cap-rock seal over the Yammama reservoir.

This unit is mainly composed of inter-bedded argillaceous, mottled, hard, dense,

micro-crystalline, fossiliferous Limestone and Shale with 32.4m TVD thickness will

be drilled in this well. This Limestone facies, is characterized by poor porosity and

permeability, but in some areas may contain hydrocarbon.


Yammama Formation:

From 2571.8mMD (-2416.1mTVDss) to T.D. at 3841mMD (-2440mTVDss)

Gross thickness of the Yammama layer varies from 225 to 275 meters but in this well

24.9m TVD of this Formation will be drilled. Lithologically, this pay-zone consists of

Limestone, micro-crystalline, dense some with chalky at the top. The rest of the

interval is represented by inter-bedded gradational chalky, calcarenitic and crystalline

Limestone with good inter-granular and vuggy porosity and scattered good  oil-

staining.

8.   Logging Program

8 1/2" Hole:

MWD/GR/ROP: The drilling of 8 ½" deviated hole is going to be controlled by

MWD/GR tool in order to keep the well path at the planned direction and picking up

the Formation tops as well as selecting the proper depth for 7" liner.


6 1/8" Hole:

MWD (only ROP): is going to be run from 7" liner shoe to T.D. in order to keep the

well path at the planned direction.


LWD (Full Suite, ADN-Impulse): in horizontal section  is going to be run from

landing point to T.D.

Note: If LWD tools were not available, then MWD (GR/Resistivity) can be used for

this purpose.


CBL/VDL: is recommended to be run for cement quality in 7" liner.

9.   Sampling
