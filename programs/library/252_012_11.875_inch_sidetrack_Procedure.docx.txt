
10 5/8” X 11 7/8” SIDETRACK DRILLING PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
PU BHA (10 5/8” bit, Powerdrive, MWD, 11 7/8 “Rhino) and TIH.  Shallow test MWD and test pins on reamer (650 GPM test did not shear pins).
TIH to top of cement at ~12,875’, wash down the last 2 stands.
Wash/drill cement (estimated 600’ of cement in casing).   5 feet out of the shoe, down link Powerdrive.  Drill cement and kick-off until cuttings & parameters indicate the bit is through the cement.  The following parameters shall be followed:
500 GPM max inside casing. (Rhino tested to 650 GPM)
10K Weight on Bit
40-60 RPM (evaluate if increase is necessary but do not exceed 80 RPM inside casing)
Circulate cuttings above wellhead & boost out.
Take SPR’s when pulling back to shoe.  Get static equivalent mud wt from PWD.
Line up to pump down the choke line and drill pipe and take pressures on choke panel gauge & HES.  Perform shoe test. (Max 13.9 ppg EMW, Min 13.8 ppg EMW.  Pump up PWD max reading from LOT.
Drill 10 5/8” hole until reamer is 10’ below the shoe.  Drop the ball and shear the reamer pins (pump 80%  of strokes and let ball free fall for 5 minutes before pressuring up).  Bump shoe to verify reamer is open.
Drill 10 5/8” X 11 7/8” directional hole to 14,500’ TVD, ~16,500’ MD building angle from 34.4 degrees to 80 degrees.  The last 1200’ feet to the shoe depth will be tangent.  The following parameters and procedures shall be adhered to:
Target ROP is 60’/hr (80-90 ft/hr average instantaneous).  ROP will be based on ECD’s.  Maximum ECD is 13.7 ppg.  If ECD’s are increasing to maximum values, control with ROP first.  Flow rates can be reduced, followed by slow back reaming (25min/stand) if further work is required to lower ECD’s.
Drill interval with 120 RPM and maximum flow rates not to exceed 700 GPM.
After drilling a stand, slowly back ream 45’.  Stop the rotary and slowly slack off to bottom with the pumps on.  After the flow check get PU and SO weights and make a connection.  Be aware cuttings slumping is likely to occur in the 30-60 degree build section.
13.1 ppg mud weight should suffice to TD.  The yield point should be maintained around 18-20.  Pump 25 bbl LCM pills (G seal & premium seal) every stand.  Every third stand spot 100 bbl Hi-Vis sweep down the boost line during connections.  Circulate out when back to drilling.
Maintain 2 pumps on the boost line while drilling.
Time and log flow-backs to develop a trend as well as on/off bottom torque readings, ECD’s, PU and SO weights with & w/o the pumps, and other relevant data.  Communication between mud loggers, shakers, and the rig floor is critical.  Immediately report any variation in trends to the rig floor.  Perform engineers to monitor & communicate hole cleaning with drilling team continuously.
Prior to any trips, ensure MI runs trip log and trip at speeds that ensure shoe is protected and well is not swabbed in.
After reaching TD, back ream out of the hole at 1 stand per hour.  Slow rotary down when bottoms up cuttings are up in the casing.  Continue Back reaming out of the hole until shakers clean up.  POOH to shoe and service the rig.
TIH to bottom and check for fill.  Circulate and back ream out of the hole as indicated above until shakers are clean.
POOH to run 9 5/8” casing.
