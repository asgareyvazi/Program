Drilling Waste Management Plan
Services

# Service and Technical Requirements
This part covers Contractor’s responsibilities to Company to meet the requirement for drilling waste management services and environmental legislations in I.R of Iran during the contract. These requirements are based on the  current  environmental  legislations and  discharge limitations that have been issued by Department of Environment (DOE) of I.R of Iran. Contractor  shall  direct.  Supervise   and  control   wastewater  and  waste   mud  processing equipment  and  associated  services.  In  particular, Contractor  shall  be  responsible  for  the followings:
Submit Drilling waste Management Program for the project
Provide solidification treatment and/ or other proposed methods to dispose the drilling solid waste and liquid waste including used Drilling and Completion Fluids, Drilling Cuttings, Residual Acid, and sometimes-small amounts of oil, described plan and operation to satisfy the requirement of Iran IPS Standards and COMPANY.
Provide treatment methods to processing the wastewater produced from project camp,
the recycling water includes without limitation the waste water from living area, office area.
Provide  equipment,  operating  crew  and  chemicals  for  drilling  waste  management program.
Estimated disposal cost of drilling waste, the quantity of consumed chemicals, the cost of transportation and equipment. Calculated the cost rate
Maintenance and or repair of all the related equipment,
Provision of daily reports showing:
Record of breakdown time
Analysis of any equipment and or service failures
Maintenance and repair performed and. spare parts consumed.
Drilling waste include used drilling fluids, used completion fluids, drilling cuttings, residual acid, oil etc. Drilling pit wastes may contain constituents of environmental concern including salt, hydrocarbons, and biologically available heavy metals.
The Work includes, but is not limited to the following main deliveries:
Pre-Planning and designing Drilling Waste Management Services for the duration of drilling operation,
Site Survey Report for the mentioned contract before starting the drilling operation,
Drilling Waste Pit design with the minimum required capacity to reduce the environmental damage,
Discharge management of the drilling fluids waste, wastewater, and cuttings
contaminated with waste to reduce the overall waste volume
Installing and operating the required equipment for the duration of the contract,    Providing the required personnel to meet the waste management services,
Laboratory analysis of the produced water, treated wastes during the operation on the daily basis,
Cutting Treatment ,handling and discarding,
Measuring and monitoring of the waste treatment practices by the engineers during the contract,
Providing Daily, Monthly and final report for the waste management services during the operation,
Plan for the Rig down and remain waste at the site.
Final Waste Handling or Site discharge in case of meeting the environmental requirement by the contractor.

# Drilling Waste Management Design
The contractor is responsible for designing the optimum practices for treating the produced waste during the operation. The initial technical waste management proposal should be included the practices for minimizing, treating, and recovering of the wastes according to the below categories:
Water Based Mud that contains minerals and natural and synthetics polymers,
Oil Contaminated Mud
Cuttings contaminated with WBM/ Polymer Mud
Cuttings contaminated with hydrocarbons
Acid and oil base materials used for flowing the well.
The contractor is encouraged to offer possible practices to minimize the drilling fluids waste and reuse of it.
The attached Mud Program in this document is the reference for all the calculations and technical waste management program.

# Site Survey Report
It is the responsibility of the contractor to provide the required facility for waste management services on the site by surveying the site before starting the project and anticipating the requirement for the project. It is obvious that after completing the survey, the contractor should provide a report for sharing the information and concerns.

# Waste Pit Design
Although waste pit has been prepared at this time, the contactor is responsible for designing a waste pit with the minimum required capacity. The specification and technical information for the waste pit design should be included in the technical proposal. Such design will be considered for next locations well site preparation.

# Waste Discharge Management
The contractor is responsible for reducing the generated wastes by advising the best practices. This should be monitored by the contractor personnel during the operation. In addition, the fluids discharge on the cuttings, solids control equipment, and direct discharges should be reported in the daily report.

# Personnel
CONTRACTOR shall maintain staffs of highly qualified technical personnel available to support all the SERVICES provided by CONTRACTOR. CONTRACTOR shall at its own cost, provide and maintain suitable qualified laboratory technician(s) to work at laboratory facilities in CONTRACTOR BASE. However, the contractor is responsible to provide the required personnel at site to handle the waste management operation. The following is the minimum requirement for the personnel. Additional labors are not considered for waste management service. For obtaining good performance and in case of need for helpers and local labor work personnel, such labor and local coolies can be provided by Company at rig site.
One On Site Waste Management Engineer
One On Site Waste Management operator/technician
It is the requirement that one waste management coordinator be in charge of the operation in the office.
CVs of senior staff assigned for this project should be submitted in technical offer package.

# Special Provisions Related to Contractor’s Personnel
Before commencement of the Work, CONTRACTOR shall submit, for COMPANY's approval, a list of all personnel appointed or intended to be appointed for the performance of the Work, together with details of qualifications and experience.
Personnel shall be mobilized within 2 weeks notice.
Thereafter, CONTRACTOR shall submit the same information with respect to additional or replacement personnel at least fifteen (15) days prior to appointment of same, for approval by COMPANY’s Representative.
Any change in personnel assigned to the operations without COMPANY’s approval will be
considered as a breach of the CONTRACT by CONTRACTOR.
CONTRACTOR shall at all times be responsible for ensuring that Personnel provided to perform the SERVICES are qualified, hold valid certifications, are adequately trained, and that the validity of such qualifications, certifications and training are maintained throughout the duration of the CONTRACT.

# Laboratory Facility
The contractor is responsible for on-site monitoring of the generated waste, treated waste and produced water. Testing and laboratory activities should be included in the technical proposal.

# Report Formatting and Programs
The contractor should provide the following reporting system and documentation for the duration of the contract;
Daily/Monthly Waste Management Report
Final waste management report including the total generated waste in each section, total treated waste,  total  chemical  consumption,  cost  monitoring  and  other  activity during the operation

# Waste Discharge or Handling
The contractor is responsible for treating all the generated waste in site without any
discharges in environment or damages. It is also the responsibility of the contractor for handling/discharge of generated waste after treatment. accessories Disposal area will be designated by COMPANY and CONTRACTOR shall transfer fixed cuttings to this location at CONTRACTOR’s cost
