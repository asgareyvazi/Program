1  Introduction


     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers the drilling of well# 32 in Tabnak field, Iran. The well is intended
              to produce gas from Kangan & Upper Dalan formation. The well will be drilled
               vertically to a planed total depth of 3034m (-1850). The exact TD will be determined
            based on the logging results. The open hole drain is considered to be 214m.

            Note: This program has been written with respect to the letter (Geological Forecast)
             No.: م ن ز / ٨٣٣٦٣ dated: ٧٨٣١ / ٥٠ / ٥١ .


     1.2    Tabnak Structure

           Tabnak gas field is an exposed anticline located in the subcoastal Fars province among
             Khalfani anticline in the south, Kuh-e Dehnow in the east, Assaluyeh gas field in the
              east and Varavi gas field in the north. The anticline has been previously known as
           Gavbandy or Tarakemeh anticline. The anticline is an asymmetric structure about 80
         km long and 15 km wide at the surface, considering the depth of Dehram Group,
           Tabnak is the deepest field comparing with the other anticlines in Assaluyeh range.

              Coordinates (Lambert): E: 2, 289, 017.3m N: 625, 575.88m

                               Geology Information



1  Introduction:

 Tabnak gas field is an exposed anticline located in the subcoastal Fars province among Khalfani

  anticline in the south, Kuh-e Dehnow in the east, Assaluyeh gas field in the east and Varavi gas

  field in the north. The anticline has been previously known as Gavbandy or Tarakemeh anticline.

 The anticline is an asymmetric structure about 80 km  long and 15 km wide at the surface,

  considering the depth of Dehram Group, Tabnak is the deepest field comparing with the other

  anticlines in Assaluyeh range. Northeastern flank of the structure is steep dipping. The oldest

  outcrops belong to the Fars group in the crest and the north flank of the structure. On the other

  hand, a sequence from Bakhtiary down to Khami group has been outcroped in the south flank. In

  the same flank a thrust fault caused thrusting Asmari over Bakhtiyary Formation. This thrust is

  assumed to be the extension of Jam- Assaluyeh fault, another normal fault has moved Gachsaran

  adjacent Asmari in the exposed core of Tabnak anticline. However several minor faults have been

  reported from surface outcrops which generally die out in Pabdeh or lower incompetent Formations.

 The field discovery was based on surface geology and structural geology and no seismic data were

  available to be implemented. In addition there are two smaller culminations near the main

  culmination, one in the north west extension and the other one is located in the south western part.

2  Structure

 Tabnak anticline is considered to be one of the three culmination  of West Assaluyeh-Gavbandy

  chain anticlines on the Fars platform (Coastal Fars). Forming of these structures apparently has

  taken place before subcoastal and interior Fars structures. Tabnak anticline is about 50 km long and

  8 km wide on the top of Tabnak Fm. (contour -2400 m), the oldest outcrop rock unit belongs to the

  Hith evaporate Formation in the south flank of the structure but general exposed core consists of

 Champeh member  from  Gachsaran  Formation. However  Tabnak  anticline  and  the  other

  culminations of the chain have been located on High Gavbandy which seems to be the extension of

  Qatar Arch. Toward the north, Tabnak and Varavi anticlines have been connected by a large

  syncline where secondary folding has been induced by several thrust faults. The southern part of

 Tabnak is limited by a large thrust fault which lays the structure by the Khalfani anticlines, the

  throw of fault is considerable and caused thrusting Asmari over Bakhtiary of Khalfani. This means

  a vertical displacement around 1000 m has been occurred. The presence of a sulfur spring in the

  west of Gavbandy area may be a good evidence for the thrust fault activities.

3  Stratigraphy of Tabnak Structure



Gachsaran Fm.

The Gachsaran Fm. is consisted of mainly anhydrite interbedded with limestone and marl beds.
Age:Miocene

Asmari / Jahrum Fms.

The Asmari Fm. is composed of dolomite and limestone. The Jahrum Fm. comprises of mainly

dolomite, pale and light grey, argillaceous dolomite is common in this succession.
Age: Oligo Miocene

Pabdeh Fm.

The lithology is consisted of white to off white argillaceous limestone and brownish grey, grey marl
Age: Pleocene - Eocene

Gurpi Fm.
The lithology is mainly consisted of grey, brown, glauconitic argillaceous limestone, interbedded

with marl and shale layers.

Age: Campanian to maestrichtian

Ilam Fm.

The Ilam Formation is composed of white to brown, partly micritic oolitic and pyritic limestone,.
Age: Santonian to Campanian

Laffan Shale Member
This member is consisted of olive gray,medium hard to soft and pyritic shale and some black spotted
limestone.
 Age: Coniacian to Santonian

Sarvak Fm.
The lithology is mainly white to off white and light cream, stylolitic limestone, with hydrocarbon
traces.
Age: Cenomanian to Probable Albian

Kazhdumi Fm.

The Kazhdumi Fm.   is consisted of cream, grey argillaceous micritic limestone and brown, dark

brown shale.

Age: Albian to Cenomanian

Dariyan Fm.

This Formation is mainly composed of white, light grey limestone, but the middle part of the

Formation is argillaceous, the limestone is mostly micritic and bituminous and pyritic.
Age: Aptian

Gadvan Fm.

The Gadvan Formation is divided to three main parts: Upper Gadvan mainly consists of argillaceous

limestone interbedded with shale. The middle part called Khalij member, is composed of cream, light

cream, clean limestone. The lower part of Gadvan is consisted of argillaceous limestone.
Age: Barremian to Aptian

Fahliyan Fm.

Fahliyan Formation is consisted of brown, brownish grey limestone and dolomitic limestone in the

upper part. The middle part of this formation is massive. Some thin chert band has been developed

here in Coastal Fars area.

Age: Neocomian

Hith Fm.

This formation is mainly composed of anhydrite, and some green gray marl. Hith Formation is the

main cap rock, for Arabian reservoirs.in Persian Gulf and its southern countries
 Age: Upper Jurassic

Surmeh Fm.

The Surmeh Formation is very thick and mainly consists of limestone, dolomite and dolomitic

limestone, with argillaceous limestone. This formation has been divided to five biozone. There are

                     1. A minimum of 10 kg dry blend and 10 kg of neat cement should be shipped
                  from the rig to town along with additive samples for QC testing right after
                      receiving cement. During the cement job, 5 litres of a representative mix-water
                   sample should be collected from cement mix water tanks.
                     2. All samples should be stored until the success of the cement job has been
                        verified, then they may be discarded.
                     3. Before running 7” liner, ensure that all liner accessories and liner running
                   equipment are present. Identify liner as ordered, number the joints and check
                         liner condition. Ensure the correct liner drifts are supplied to the rig. Clean and
                      inspect all threads. Measure liner (cross check tally), prepare liner running list
                   and mark all joints to be left out. Check cement. Mix a pilot sample. Hold liner
                    running / configuration meeting with liner engineer.
