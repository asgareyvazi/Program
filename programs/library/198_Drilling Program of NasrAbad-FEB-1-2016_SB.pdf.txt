2. General Information

    Introduction

Over the next decade, a signiﬁcant amount of exploration and ﬁeld developments will take

place in the saltprovinces around the world. Salt is essentially impermeable and are excellent

traps for hydrocarbons.These are found, among others, in the Gulf of Mexico, the NorthSea,
Iran, oﬀshore Brazil and West Africa, and in Kazakhstan. Several challenges arise when

drilling in salt formations. Unlike the adjacent formations,when buried, salt maintains its

density. Due to gravity, salt will start to migrate upwards when its density becomes lower

than the surrounding rocks, creating rubble zones around the salt. Well control is challenging
as predicting pore pressure, fracture gradients and the existence of fractures is diﬃcult. Shock

and vibration levels have also proven to cause problems in these formations. Exploration for

and development of underground storages for natural gas in Iran has been planned as a long-

term project to cope with the requirements of winter peak shaving in gas consumption and to

combat the irregularities and obstructions which may occasionally occur in the operation of

the upstream gas supply systems. Nasrabad, one of the underground structures, with former

discovery background, located at south-southeast of Tehran and on the Iranian Central

Plateau was selected as a likely candidate for such a purpose.

This is the first well which is planned to drill in this area and the Gas reserve formation shall

be under a salt dome between 900 to 2000 m below the surface. Since this is the first well in

this region, required 8.5" size coring operations have been designed to be perform in order to

evaluate capability and qualityof Nasrabad structure for Gas storage purpose.

3. Health and Safety

    Health & Safety

         The overall strategic objectives of HSE management during drilling, logging,

           coring, operations are:

            To ensure the health and safety of personnel.

            To safeguard the integrity of wells, facilities and installations.

            Prevent adverse effects on the environment and third parties.

            Zero Rating

                  -  Dropped objects

                  -  Well control occurrence

            H2S, Diverter and Well Control Certification and drill procedures

            Daily safety meetings and weekly fire and emergency drills

            Housekeeping daily

            HSE will focus on:

            Communication  to   all  personnel.  Enforce  communication  to  create

             involvement  for  all  personnel  based  on  acting  as  a  ‘Team’.  Radio

             communication for entire drilling unit and headphone for specific work in

               areas of high background noise.

            Pre-job safety meetings start of each crew change and recorded IADC report.

              Additional  safety  meeting  for  every key  event  occurring  i.e.  Casing,

              cementing, and BOP setup and pressure test.

            All crews trained H2S. Regular H2S drills will be hold.

7. BOP Test Policy

    Pressure Test Frequency

   After installing stack on well head.

   Any time a new casing string is run and cemented.

   Max 21 days.

   Any time required by Company.

   Any time BOP opens for repairing or replacing RAMS or Bonnet Seal.

    Function Test

   The pipe rams and BOP valves should be operated at least once every shift.

   Blind/shear rams shall be operated every round trip in the hole.

   The annular preventer shall be operated when the scheduled routine BOP tests are

       performed.

    Duration of Test

   The BOP 300-500 psi low-pressure tests will be performed first. They are to be held

    for a min period of 5min.

   High-pressure tests are held for a minimum of 10mins. The maximum acceptable

       pressure drop over this 10 min. period is 100psi.

8. Coring program &Procedure:

    Coring program

This section is of special interest for rock mechanical and solution mining investigations. The

sections above and below are especially of rock mechanical interest. For structural

interpretation coring of the entire well is recommended Coring is considered.

Photos of each core have to be taken by reflected light as well as transmitted light. Pictures

Should be linked to the mud log.

The cores have to be described carefully. Description as well as photos have to be added to

the reports.

For the core orientation and description as well as for photographing, a core container with

Air-conditioning is required. Beside photo-equipment, the container must provide a special

core table for displaying the cores.

Cover rock

The cover rock does not need to be cored. Investigation of lithology is based on cuttings and

Borehole logging. In case there is no salt determined 50 m of depth below the seismically

Interpreted boundary of salt a check core has to be taken.

Salt formation

After identifying the presence of salt (drilling break, first appearance of salt cuttings),

continuous coring is required (8 ½” core barrel).

The continuously cored interval is expected to start at approx. 600 m until 2000 m. All cores

should be drilled with orientation equipment in order to determine directions of dips.

The deviation has to be controlled. If the given limitation is exceeded, the hole has to be

corrected by directional drilling equipment. In this section coring is not possible.

The overall coring length is estimated to be about 1400 m.

The cores should be stored at a location within reach for the site geologists for inspection at

any time. The core storage must fulfil the following requirements: sun and rain protected,

cool and constant climate, sufficient light for core inspection. The total cored length must be

displayed in once during core sample selection, technicians have to handle the core boxes.

   Surface Handling & Processing

Lay Down Cradle Inner tubes are very flexible and bend easily with or without core

inside.  The purpose of the lay down cradle is to lend support to the tube while the core

is being transported from the rig floor to the cat walk.  The cradle will be strapped to

the tube while  it  is  still in the vertical position.  This prevents any bending of the inner

tube as the barrel moves from the rig floor.  The core inside  is held firm  and   thus

prevents  any damage to the core such as fracturing.  The cradle is also fitted with roller

along it’s length, these are use when cutting  the core  into  4’ lengths, the barrel is simply

moved along the length of the cradle on the rollers until the whole inner tube has been cut.

   Core Quick Scan Procedure

       Designate and tape off layout area.

       Clean up Inner Tube.
       Run Gamma Ray Logger.
       Measure and mark the core.
       Cut the core using air saw machine.
       Preserve 30cm wax sample each 3m.
       Drain mud and Stabilize core with Foam.
       Packed core in wooden Box.
      A core lay-out sheet should be sent with the core.
       Core will be sent to the lab at the end of coring job.

9. Wire line logging program

The logging program is targeted at the following:
      to determine the well geometry as diameter, ovality and break outs.
      to determine the litho-stratigraphic classification.
      to determine the lithological inventory.
      to define the rock mechanical behaviour of the salt.
      to identify the structural characteristics of the salt dome.

15. Cement Program

    Key technical measures in execution:

1).Prior to the cementing operation, enough qualified water shall be reserved. The
volume of the water shall be not less than the volume of 1.5~2.0 times calculated. And
also enough mud shall be reserved for the displacement.
2).Drifting after logging shall be performed with excellent mud and high flow rate to
make sure that the well bore is stable with low friction and no leakage.
3).Casing and accessories shall be inspected closely; Float shoe and float collar shall be
reliable, prior to running in the hole diameter drift shall be performed; connections
shall be screwed on everyjoint; lowering speed should be controled.
4).Prior to the operation,  all the equipment and associated shall be tested in good
condition.
5).Prior to operation, a coordinative meeting shall be held to make sure everyone have
his own post and responsibility and emergency steps shall be taken.
6).Designated person shall be responsible for watching slurry return from wellhead. He
must report to the engineer  to take further steps  at once  if he found something
abnormal.
7).Operator shall be responsible for watching slurry density to make sure that  it is
complied with the Requirement.
