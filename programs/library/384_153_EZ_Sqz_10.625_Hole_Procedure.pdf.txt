     EZ SQUEEZE 10 5/8” HOLE PROCEDURE
                       Goldfinger

1. POOH to 655’ above shoe (12713’ – 127 stds , count stds).

2.  Hold Pre-Job Safety Meeting.

3.  Line up Halco on std pipe to monitor placement and do hesitation sqz.

4.  Pull suction screen from #1 mud pump. Have #2 & #3 lined up on the Hole. Break circ with rig pumps,
   and then with Halco. Double check line ups.

5.  From slugging pit, pump available SwellCM – have man on pits strapping tank for amount pumped. Adjust
    figures for over 40 bbl.

6.  Line up to active system and pump 4 bbls mud.

7.  Line up on Add #2 pit and pull as much EZ Sqz as possible – have man on pits strapping tank for amount
   pumped. Adjust figures for over 90 bbl. Line up on active and pump 10 bbls of mud with pump #1 and
    then switch to pumps to try and maintain efficiency, ZERO STK COUNTER.

8.   Displace Sqz to within 10 bbls of bit and close annular with mud using rig pumps. Strapping pits to
    confirm mud pumped.

           •  Disp vol = 266 bbl (Drill String) + 12 bbl (Lines) = 278 bbl.
           •  Pump 124 bbls/1008 stks (40 SCM + 4 mud + 90 Ezs + 10 mud #1 pmp + 124 mud = 268
                bbls) at 500 gpm, close annular.

9.  Displace Sqz out of DP at 2 BPM with rig pumps, Pump 157 bbls/1276 stks additional to put the
   SwellCM + 10 bbl EZ Sqz into the formation. Wait on SwellCM for 2 hrs. Boost riser with #1 pump and
    clean pump.

10. Hessitate/Squeeze as per Turbochem using Halco, record times, vol’s, dp pressures, and csg pressures for
     all phases of operation.

11. Squeeze EZ Sqz to csg shoe or satisfaction is achieved (+/-500 psi on csg).

12. Wait on Squeeze for 4 hrs. Boost riser for final hr of waiting. Release pressure. Record bleed back.

13. Circulate and wash/ream through sqz to 13853’..

14. Circulate 30 min and pull into shoe. Finish circ btms up.

15. Perform FIT test to a 13.9 EMW. Use PWD readings to adjust test.

16. If successful, finigh cleaning out open hole.  If fill or a bridge is encountered, circ clean and retest shoe.
