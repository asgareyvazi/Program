4.  Operation Procedures

4.1    General Requirements
    (1) Provide a coordinator  to  integrate  all  activities such  as CT  operation,  nitrogen pump and

    engineering.

    (2) The well site access road must be suitable for equipment entry and exit.

    (3) The well site must be level and smooth, can stand with the weight of all equipment.

    (4) Check all equipments on arrival at the wellsite to make sure no damage/missing items. Pay special

    attention to the crossovers to ensure the threads are compatible.

    (5) Pay more attention to and check the ID and crossover of the completion string to make sure that

  CTU RIH and POOH freely and safely, if CT operation is required.

    (6)  All the tubing should be cleaned.

4.2    Preparation work before operation
    1)  Move all the equipments and accessories to wellsite and set them in place according to attached

   equipments layout.

    2) Hold safety meeting with all personnel involved.

    3) Cordon off all high-pressure area with signal tape and forbid anyone but operators in high-pressure

    area.

    4) Check that all wellhead valves are closed.

    5)  Rig up all equipments with accessories to X-mas tree according to attached equipments layout. All

4.3   CT displacement procedure

      1) Check wellhead and annulus pressure.

      2) Close top master valve, wing valve, choke, swabbing valve and kill valve of X-mass tree.
       Remove kill valve flange; Line up flare lines, choke manifold to X-mass tree. Bleed off the X-
        mass tree pressure. Remove X-mass tree cap and flange.

      3) Perform BOP function test and install adapter flange and BOP on X-mass tree.

      4) Line up choke manifold and connect to well head and burn line.

      5) Line up the pump unit treatment lines and equipment.

      6) Rig up coiled tubing unit; make up BHA and flush the surface line.

      7) Connect Injection head to BOP.

      8) Measure back from swab valve to bottom of BHA and set depth counters as per schematic
         depth reference.

      9) Open the swabbing valve, install SSV cap to keep SSV open.

      10) Depressurized WHCP hydraulic pressure, Keep TR-SCSSSV pressure above   psi by manual
        pump.

      11) Open kill valve, flush flare line.

      12) Hold safety meeting and ensure that all personnel on site are aware when pressure test starts.
          Pressure test lines from downstream to upstream. Pressurize downstream line at 1500psi for
        15min and check for any leakage for 15min. Pressurize CTU unit (including BOP rams) and
         upstream line at 4500 psi, Stop pumping.

       All pressure tests have to be held for 15 min and charted. Use water.
      Never exceed X-mass Tree MWP= 5kpsi.
      13) Flush all the water in the coil tubing with N2 out of the BHA tool prior to RIH.

      14) Install H2S detector sensors.

      15) RIH. At about 40 to 60 fpm. Low N2 circulation rate, generally from 200 scfpm and open the
          well to B/pit thru. Adjustable choke. Keep flowing w/h pressure.at 50-100 psi.

          Slow down the RIH &POOH speed at ID changing point.

6. Job Risk Analysis

    6.1 High cold

     1) Cryogenic liquid may cause fatal or severe injury, explosion, or suffering for failing to observe
         safety precautions. Operator must wear PPE before operating: helmet, overalls, goggles, loose
        gloves, toe-guard shoes.

     2) Operator must obey operation procedure and be familiar with all the process and potential
        abnormalities during the operation, and operate strictly according to the procedure. All the
        tanks and pipes should cool down step by step.

    6.2 High Pressure

     3) Enclose the high pressure area with warning line.

     4) Plot the dangerous area with warning plate.

     5) Keep all personnel far away from high pressure area while operation.

     6) Use safety rope between the pipe line connections in case of disconnection, all the lines and
        connections (exposed to high pressure) shall be secured and supported by chains.

     7) The pump should be shut down immediately once the pipe line bursts.



    6.3 H2S

     1) Portable H2S detector must be inspected.

     2) All air respirators must be inspected.

     3) Everyone on location should use H2S detector.

     4) Windsock must be obvious and showed to all personnel before entering well site.

     5) All personnel must be trained of H2S contingency plan and the use of all related equipments.

    6.4 HSE

     1) Make sure the emergence communication is available.

     2) All personnel should be equipped with full PPE, including safety boots, helmet, goggle, gloves
          etc.

     3) Isolate the working area with warning line.

     4) Pre-job safety meeting must be held and recorded, which should contain operation objective,
        operation procedures, potential risk analysis, job assignment, emergency program etc.

     5) All the lines and connections exposed to high pressure should be connected and chained with
         safety rope reliably.

     6) Adequate fire-fighting equipment should be prepared and located in specified place.

     7) No smoking and calling on working area.

     8) All jobs should be full time supervised by OTS Supervisor onsite.
