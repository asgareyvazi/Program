             Cement 20” Pipe Procedure
                San Jacinto Prospect
                       April 9, 2004


1. MU cement std and land out wellhead in 36” casing w/ 25K down
    ▪  Land and lock down well head per FMC
    ▪   Will not preload locking assembly until after the cement job
    ▪   Pull 50K over to verify wellhead locked
    ▪   Set back down maximum of 50K

2. RU and Cement
   ▪   Hold Pre-job Safety Meeting w/ Halliburton and CoRep
   ▪  RU Halliburton completely – have Nitrogen up on rig floor
   ▪    Halliburton break circulation with sea water; close lo-torque side pump-in sub and test all lines
        (5000psi), open lo-torque side pump-in sub; break circulation again after test, close lo-torque pump-in
       sub
   ▪   Open upper TIW
   ▪   Add tracer beads to spacer
   ▪   Rig pumps will pump 250 bbl mud to verify floats are open and then rig pumps will pump 100 bbls
        spacer w/ tracer beads
   ▪   Shut down rig pump, close upper TIW and put 500 psi on top
   ▪   Open Halliburton lo-torque side pump-in sub
   ▪    Halliburton will pump cement – see attached detail for amount and slurry mix
   ▪    Halliburton will displace cement w/ water (325 bbls)
   ▪   Bleed back max of 5 bbl while checking floats
   ▪     If floats don’t hold, pump bled-off volume back and hold
   ▪    Set FMC shallow water pack-off and rigid lock wellhead W/ Oceaneering per FMC
   ▪    Release FMC running tool
   ▪  POOH cement stand plus 1 more stand and pump and displace casing with seawater
   ▪   Pump Halliburton Nerf balls to clean DP while displacing at least 1 DP volume of sea water

3. POOH
    ▪   Clear well head and move rig 200 feet off location
    ▪   Pull to surface (can be started while rig is moving)
    ▪   Inspect wellhead with ROV
