              DST OPERATION

DRY TEST PROCEDURE:

1- Before running packer, first scrap casing

2- Remove wear bushing

3- RIH w/DST tools (Fill up string w/cushion as program each 5 stands & observe
cushion level, Max. RIH speed must be 10 stands per hour)

4- After reach packer depth make up head & surface equipment & pressure test

5- Set packer

6- Open the tools for 3 hours

7- Close the tools for 4 hours & drop shearing bar & reverse circulation @ the
forth hour

8- Unset packer

9- Circulate bottom up (CBU)

10- Observe the well

11- POOH
