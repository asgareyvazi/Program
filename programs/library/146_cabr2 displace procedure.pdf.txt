Case No. 1

Use 250 bbl (old cabr2) and 700 bbl new cabr2 totally 950 bbl cabr2 brine 103 pcf

    1.  Make sure all tanks and surface equipment are clean.
    2.  Check all connections for any possible leakage.
    3.  Perform pilot test in mud lab and after mixing 1 bbl of new cabr2 with 35 % v old cabr2,
      make sure the final brine weight is equal to 103 pcf.
    4.  Mix 250 bbl (old cabr2) and 700 bbl new cabr2 in solution tanks totally 950 bbl cabr2 brine
      103 pcf.
    5. Pump 30 bbl hi-vis pill as ahead spacer.
    6.  Displace the well with 103 pcf cabr2.
    7.  Use the excess volume on surface to fill the well during pooh.

Case No. 2

   Use 950 bbl new cabr2 brine 103 pcf

    1.  Make sure all tanks and surface equipment are clean.
    2.  Check all connections for any possible leakage.
    3.  Use 220 PPB cacl2 (94-97%) for cacl2 brine 84 pcf.
    4.  Prepare 150 bbl pumpable cacl2 brine and let it rest for at least 6 hours.
    5.  Perform pilot test in mud lab and after mixing 1 bbl of cabr2 with 17% v cacl2 84 pcf, make
       sure the final brine weight is equal to 103 pcf.
    6.  Use 840 bbl new cabr2.
    7.  Mix 840 bbl cabr2 with 110 bbl cacl2 brine and prepare 950 bbl cabr2 103 pcf.
    8. Pump 30 bbl hi-vis pill as ahead spacer.
    9.  Use the excess volume on surface to fill the well during pooh.
