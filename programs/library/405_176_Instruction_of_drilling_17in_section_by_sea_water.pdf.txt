Objectives: Drilling 17inch section by sea water

Planned Operations :

    1. RIH with directional drilling tools to 969mt (18-5/8”casing shoe) Fill up stands stroke wise
     & don't pressurize string in order to prevent bit rotation inside casing.
    2. Wash down the open hole slowly to bottom with flow rate 300gpm, wash & ream tight spot
       with 40RPM & flow rate 700gpm
    3. The Gamma survey should be take from below casing shoe to bottom then new formation.
    4.  In the case of tag cement plug, drill out it to bottom with 40RPM flow rate:800gpm WOB:2-
      10Klb & sweep the hole with 30bbl PHB at the middle & end of each joint
    5.  Drilling new formation will be done per SLB directional driller with following hole cleaning
       instruction

    •  Pump 30 Bbls guar gum pill in the start of each JOINT & 30 Bbls PHB pill in the middle of
      each drilled joint .
    •   After each drilled stand, sweep the hole by 30bbl guar gum pill then reciprocate stand
      once
    •   In the case of high torque & hole cleaning problem pump 30bbl PHB pill immediately .
    •  Spot 40 Bbls PHB pill prior to each connection.
    •  Try to minimize connection time to get rid of any stuck.
    •   After connection increase SPM gradually (0.5Min from 0 to max)
    •    If pipe is stuck, decrease GPM to 300 - 400, Pull & compress 10000 Lbs above & below
       neutral weight of string, applying up to 30000 ft-lbs torque on string.
