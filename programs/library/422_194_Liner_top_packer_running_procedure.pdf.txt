                            On-Site Preparation (Prior to Running)

1.      Pre-job meeting shall be conducted prior to running in the hole to go over running and setting
        procedure.

2.     Using the Sub-Assembly Data Packs and the equipment manifest, check that all the necessary
       packer equipment, running tools, etc., are on location and are in good condition.

3.     Take all relevant measurements of the equipment - lengths, OD's, ID's, etc. Record all relevant
        information into Sub Assembly Data Packs. Verify the correct quantity and type (Brass) of shears
        pins have been installed in all equipment.

                                Running Procedure


1.      Verify the tool has been correctly dressed with shear screws using the table below.


 Shear   Nominal Shear Value          Shear Screws                   Location on Tool

   #1         10480 lb.             8 off M6x8 Brass                Anti-preset mechanism
   #2         11884 lb.            4 off M10x20 Brass              Below lower slip
   #3         11884 lb.            4 off M10x20 Brass       Between Slip & Packing Element
   #4         41712 lb.            8 off M10x20 Steel              Above upper slip


2.     Ensure that there is a minimum of 50,000 lbs of Drill Collars and/or Heavy Weight Drill Pipe
        available in the derrick for setting the Tie Back Packer. This should be the same string as was
       used for the clean out run.

NOTE: Place the HWDP/DC higher up in the string so that it stays vertical when the top packer
       reaches setting depth and all set down weight is applied.

3.      Pick up the tieback packer assembly. Check all slips for cracks and all seals for cuts or other
      damage.

4.     Take extra care while passing through the BOP stack, Wellhead and 10¾” x 9⅝” casing x-over

5.     Run in hole with packer assembly on drill pipe. The large OD of the packer body gives a small
        annular clearance in the casing. This may force sea water over the top of the drill pipe. Maximum
        running speed is 2-3 minutes per stand. If sea water overflows the drill pipe, slow down running
       speed, run in carefully to avoid pressure surges. Do not rotate to the right and do not pump
      any slugs.

                                       Setting Procedure


1.     Run in hole to 20 ft ± above the top of the liner.

2.     Space out the drill pipe to ensure no tool joint is in the annular preventer or pipe rams when the
          tie back packer is stabbed in, and to ensure a 30' minimum working over stand.

3.      Reciprocate the string slowly while circulating and record up and down free weights.

4.     Stop circulation and ensure drill pipe is full.

5.     Record up and down free weights.

6.      With drill pipe full begin circulating at 1 Bbl/min with minimum pressure.

7.     Very slowly, lower the string into the seal bore.

8.     Watch pressure gauge  for pressure increase. When pressure increase  is observed stop
         circulation and monitor pressure. If pressure bleeds off, slack off additional weight until a 500 psi
       pressure is maintained and monitor flow line for any returns. Do not set down more than 5,000
        lbs on the liner top packer.

9.     Bleed pressure to 0 psi and leave valve open at surface.

10.    Lower the string further to insert the seal stem into the lower seal bore until a positive weight loss
          is observed, and slack off 5,000 lbs note that the seal drag might be as much as 8,000 lbs.

11.     Test seals and lower seal bore by applying tubing pressure and slack off additional weight
        incrementally as per table below. This incremental application of pressure and weight maintains a
      downward weight of approximately 8,000 lbs at the packer and will not allow pumping out of the
        seal stem from the lower seal bore. Ensure pressure is maintained after each step. If pressure
       begins to drop, remove all previously applied slack-off weight quickly to prevent premature setting
         of the packer.

      The lower stinger seal assembly has a seal OD of 7.375”. When stabbed into the lower
     PBR it creates a piston area which tries to lift the packer assembly out of the PBR as
       pressure is applied. This piston area is the difference between the stinger seal diameter
       cross sectional area and the drill pipe ID cross sectional area. This is 42.75 -17.94 = 24.8
       square inches.

            slacked off (lb)                (psi)                     (lb)               force (lb)

                5000                 250              5000                6,204
                7000                 500              12000               12,408
                7000                 750              19000               18,612
                7000                1000             26000               24,816
                7000                1250             33000               31,020
                7000                1500             40000               37,224

12.     Maintain 1,500 psi tubing pressure for 30 minutes.

13.        If step 13  is successful continue with next step.  If a straight line pressure test cannot be
        maintained, remove slack off weight and pressure incrementally (in reverse from step 12), pick up
        out of seal bore and repeat steps 7 to 13. Pick up slowly and bleed pressure to 0 psi and leave
        valve open at surface. Ensure seals do not come out of seal bore.

14.    Bleed off the pressure slowly and maintain slack off weight. This applies 40,000 lbs to the packer
      and will start the setting process. Three of the four separate shears will occur at the packer
        during step 15 and may be visible on the weight indicator as the inner mandrel and packer seal
       stem should move down a few inches.

15.     Increase slack-off weight to 65,000 lbs to fully set the packer (this could also be done before the
          initiation of depressurizing) and hold for 15 minutes. Pick up to neutral weight on the string so
         that there is no weight on the no-go, close the pipe rams and pressure up on the annulus to
       4,000 psi. Hold for 5 minutes.

NOTE: Some hole conditions or well trajectories may require movement of the drill pipe to "work"
       the slack-weight down to the running tool.

16.    Release the pressure and pick up to 40,000 lbs above pick up weight.  If overpull is achieved, this
        indicates that the packer is set. If while during overpull there is any upward movement at all, stop
       immediately and re-apply weight on packer.

17.    Slack off to neutral position.

18.    Close the pipe rams and pressure up on the annulus to 4,000 psi. Hold for 15 minutes to ensure
        seals are holding after overpull has been applied.

19.    Bleed off annular pressure. Slack off 40,000 lbs on tool and pressure down the drill pipe to 4,000
         psi and hold for 15 minutes.

20.    Set down 10,000 lbs at the setting tool, and release the setting tool with 15 right hand turns at the
          tool.

NOTE: Some hole conditions may require movement of the drill pipe to "work" the torque down to
       the setting tool.

21.     Pull out of hole.

22.    Lay down the running tool, flush and secure for back load.




     NOTE: THESE PROCEDURES ARE TO BE USED AS A GUIDELINE ONLY, SINCE HOLE
           CONDITIONS MAY REQUIRE THESE PROCEDURES TO BE ALTERED.
