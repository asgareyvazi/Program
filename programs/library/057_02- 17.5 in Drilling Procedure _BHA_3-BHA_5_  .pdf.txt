 LCM Pill Application Procedure
  1.  After spotting LCM pill over thief zone, pull the bit above the calculated top of the pill. Wait 30 minutes and monitor the annulus.
      After 30 minutes, attempt to fill the annulus, if not already full.
  2.   If the hole can be filled, close the BOP and squeeze 10 bbl of LCM pill into the formation without exceeding 200 psi.
  3.   If the hole remains stable, continue to drill ahead.
  4.   If the LCM pill failed to reduce the losses or the hole cannot be filled, then mix and spot another 50 bbl of LCM pill.
  5.  Attempt to fill the annulus. If successful, close the BOP and squeeze 20 bbl of LCM pill into the formation without exceeding 200
       psi.
  6.   If a squeeze pressure develops, hold pressure for 1 hour before running the string to bottom.

 G) Section Summary:

The 17 ½’’ Hole Section is Drilled to 2677 m as longest section of the well Through Aghajari, Mishan and upper part of Gachsaran
formation. According to Previous Wells in this Field, there are hole problems like bit balling, Tight Hole and Swabbing and hole wash
out which could be due to salt dissolving. The marl and claystone can cause low ROP due to bit balling, also it can lead to tight
holes and swabbing while drilling and trip in/out. 13 3/8’’ and 13 5/8” casings will be set at ± 2677 m in Gachsaran formation
(member 6) and will be cemented in One Stage (Lead: 98-100 pcf & Tail: 115-118 pcf - Exact CMT Program will be Issued in Due
Time). PHB/HV mud would be used for upper parts of the interval (Down to about 800 m) and then Salt Polymer Mud (64-98 pcf)
would be used for deeper parts. CDR/GR log is performed to survey the well path. 21 1/4"-2K * 13 5/8"-3K C.H.S would be nipple
up at this section and 13 5/8"-5K BOP Stack is used for drilling next hole section.
