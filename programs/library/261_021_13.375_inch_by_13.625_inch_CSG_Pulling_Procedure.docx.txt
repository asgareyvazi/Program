
13-3/8” X 13-5/8” CSG PULLING PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
Pump out of Hole to top of 16” line + 8500’.
CBU @ 10-BPM.
2-Pumps on riser.
Stage pumps up on hole. (Monitor For Losses)
Get Weatherford tools to rig floor. (13-5/8” First)
Rig up fill up line and get protectors on floor for 13-5/8” CSG.
Pump + 100-BBL of 14.4-ppg slug.
POOH W/DP
L/D CSG hanger handle with care.
Dump + 2-BBL of base oil inside CSG.
POOH L/D CSG:
Have clean up material ready on pipe racks.
Clean CSG threads on rig floor.
Leave XO on 13-5/8” CSG.
Leave centalizers on pin end of corresponding joint.
Use Temperature gun to heat Baker locked joints to + 550 degrees. (2-Rosebuds)
Stager joints on rack that are marked for repair.
TIH, set NSP & POOH.
P/U & M/U clean out assembly. TIH to + 10,200’.
Clean out CSG to 10,500’
TIH to TD 11,826’
SAFETY:
Be careful walking on CSG and watch for any loose protectors.
