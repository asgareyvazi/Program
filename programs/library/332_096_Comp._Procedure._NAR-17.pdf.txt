RIG 84FA, NIDC, NAR #17
COMPLETION RUNNING PROCEDURE


A-Completion Surface Tests:
1- Drift and measure OD, ID and length of all completion accessories.
2- Set 5.95" dummy in 3SV L/nip BAKER (BA), connect Cont. line and
   test against dummy to 6000 psi.
3- Set plug 4.312"AF in Top no-go land. / nip BAKER and test to 4500
   psi, leave the plug set.
4- Set plug 4.562"AF in SSD BAKER (CMD) and test to 4500 psi, remove
   plug, open and close SSD with shifting tools and test to 4500 psi.
5- Open Expansion Joint BAKER (N-1) fully and test to 4500 psi, set
   shear pins.
6- Set dummy 1 1/2" in SPM Halliburton (MOR-2) and test to 4500 psi

B-Completion Running Procedure:
 ************************Remove wear bushing**********************
  *************************************************************************
 -Rig up completion running tools

1- RIH w/Mule shoe, Bottom no-go…...etc. as per attached tally (with
   plug 4.312"AF in top no-go L/nip. And closed SSD) up to SSSV L/N
   Note:
   -F/up 100m on top of no-go plug with H-Vis polymer pill (120sec) and
   the rest each 15 jts w/ fresh water(Should be confirmed) without
   debris
     -If complete loss occurred about 300m inside TBG should not be
    filled, while circ. Cross well head about 2-2.5 BPM (the static head
   inside and outside TBG should be balanced)
2- Connect Cont. line to 3SV L/nip. And test to 6000 psi, use band-it
   strap to tight cont.line against TBG.
3- Cont. RIH as per tally, P/up TBG/Hgr, Connect Cont. line to port on
   Hgr.and test to 6000psi.
4- P/up 7” Landing /jt, connect to Hgr.
5- R/up pump unit.
6- Flush and clean T.H.S w/water.
7- Land T/Hgr and just touch TBG. Head Spool.
8- Space out and pull out the string 3 m up (for total stroke 20 ft)
 Note: For complete loss pump completion fluid in Ann. as a capacity of
   Ann. just before setting the packer. (Should be confirmed)

9- Pressurise tubing to 500 psi (3 min), 1000 psi (3 min), 1500 psi (3 min),
  2000 psi (3 min),2500 psi (15 min),4000 psi (15 min).
10- Release pressure and over pull the string with 20000 lb.
11- Land TBG/Hgr in T.H.S. and test hanger to 1000 psi
12- Tighten tie down screws.
13- Test inside tubing to 4000 psi (15 min), close top rams and test
   Hanger to 3500psi
14- Test annulus to 2000 psi (15 min).
15- Back off Landing Joint.
16- Install the plug in TBG/Hgr, and test to 4500 psi (15min)
17- N/D BOP
18- Check hanger for any damage and pressure test control line to
   6000psi
19- N/U bottom master valve and test the ports of sealing as program.
20- N/U solid block and connect to the chock manifold, test all to 4500
   psi
21- Remove TBG/Hgr plug
22- R/U wire line equipment with BOP and lubricator and test to 3500psi
23- Remove dummy 5.95" and flush cont. line with hydraulic oil.
24- Remove plug and prong 4.312” AF from top no-go L/nip.
25- Install 5.95" dummy in 3SV L/nip.
26- Bull heads the well with diesel oil if necessary.
27- Flow the well through variable chock to reach pure gas.
28- Divert well to the fix chock and record the result on 32/64”, 48/64”
   choke sizes
29- Close the well
30- Remove dummy 5.95”
31- Flush control line with hydraulic oil
32- Install 3SV and test cont. line to 5000 psi and also flapper test.
33- Release above SSSV pressure to zero
34- Monitor wellhead pressure for any 3SV leakage
35- Fill up above 3SV with completion fluid
36- Close all valves except Bottom Master Valve
37- L/D drill pipes
38- Release the rig

NOTE:
Make up torque 5 1/2", 20#N/V 6500 lb.ft
Make up torque 7", 29#N/V 9400 lb.ft
Cmp. Fl. water PH 11+.1gal/bbl corr. Inhib. (Should be confirmed)
