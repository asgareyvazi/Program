3.  Re-entry Program:


   1.1  8-1/2" Hole Section / 7" Liner

       General

                         7" Liner @+/- 3370m, MD (2421.41m, TVD), Top of
 7" Liner                L.Arab
                         7" Liner lap @1800mMD

 Formation
                         U.Arab, L.Arab
 Risks & Hole problems   Fluid loss in Buwaib
 Deviation control    MWD
 Logging                   Full set log by TLC
                 KCL Polymer Mud
              MW      65-66 pcf

                     PV      5

                  GPM     450

 Drilling Parameters                WOB     (As per DD engineer instruction)

Sequence of Operation:

   Notes:

    •  Due to reservoir department instruction and changing in target TVD, plug

   back hole from 2906m to 2360m.

    •   Set cement plug as per program. W.O.C to get hard. RIH to tag cement.

   C.B.U. POOH.

    1. P/U & M/U 8-1/2" B.Bit and D.Drilling BHA(w/ MWD/GR/ROP). / RIH to top of

      cement plug.

    2.  Start time drilling for Side tracking at 2360m, MD and Cont. D.Drilling 8-1/2" hole

        to 2592m.

    3. POOH to surf. L/D B.Bit & change motor. P/U & M/U New 8-1/2" PDC Bit and

     RIH W/Directional BHA (MWD/ROP/GR) to BTM.

    4.  Cont , D.Drilling 8-1/2" hole to 3425m.(LP point )

    5.  Perform condition trip to logging operation.

    6. R/U TLC Logging tools. Take full set log by TLC in open hole as pre reservoir

       department instruction. R/D TLC logging tools.

    7. RIH w/ OE DP and set 12bbls magneset balance plug F/ 3425 T/ 3325. POOH to

        surface.

    8. RIH w/  8-1/2”  Bit &  Dir BHA. D/O magneset  plug  F/ +/-3325m,MD  to

      3370m,MD. And perform condition trip before run 7” linetr. POOH to surface and

      L/D bit & BHA.

    9. Remove wear bushing. Change top ram to 7". R/U liner running equipment. M/U &

     Run 7" liner (1800-3370m). Perform cementing job of 7" Liner. POOH & L/D

        setting tool.

    10. Change  top  rams  to  2-7/8"-5"  variable  ram.  Perform BOP  Test.  Install

      Wearbushing, RIH w/ 8-1/2" used Bit and clean well to 7" liner lap @1800m.

    POOH & L/D 8-1/2" BHA.

    11. P/U & M/U 6" BHA containing 3-1/2" HWDP. RIH w/ 6" B.Bit , D/O P.O.B. and

       clean out inside 7" liner to top of LDC @ +/- 3346m, MD. POOH to 1800m Close

       top ram, P/T 7" liner to 1500psi/30min.

    12. RIH & Shoe track & Shoe & Magneset to 3373m, POOH to surface & L/D bit and

     BHA.




Notes:
     65 PCF WBM impose ±1317 psi over pressure to the Buwaib Reservoir in

     window depth @ 1900m MD (1900m TVD) and 374 psi over pressure to the

      U.Arab Reservoir @ 2532m MD (2308.5m TVD)
     Adjust mud weight according to the hole condition.
     Weight up material and LCM should be available on board will be applied in

      emergency case.
     Section TD maybe changed based on formation tops detected by the well site

        geologist.

       program).
