 2. 18-5/8” Cement Program
                   Important: The following program is a sample; therefore  it should be
                       tested using the field drilling water, cement sample and additives.
                   For cementing operation, note the followings:

             By Stinger:
              M/U Cement Stinger C/W spring bow 20” x 5” centralizer and RIH
                               to 10m above the shoe using the C-plate.
               Circulate 60 bbls of mud and wash down to flush the top of the stab
                               in shoe from any debris.
               Flush and fill surface lines with water and pressure test to 3,000psi
                               for 5 minutes prior to start of the cement job.
               Stab in to the shoe. Set down string weight

                  Circulate mud clean casing annulus. Observe DP’s annulus  for any
                               leakage.

                  Pump spacer, Mix and pump cement as per program.

                   All samples of mix water and slurry should be stored until the success of
                               the cement job has been verified, then they may be discarded.

                  Displace cement with mud. Displace for 15 bbls (1.78 bbls Short).

                  Stop pumping, sting out, flush pipe, check for float, POOH. L/D stinger,
                                 wait on cement to get hard.

3. 13-3/8” Cement Program
                 Important: The following program is a sample; therefore  it should be
                     tested using the field drilling water, cement sample and additives.

           A minimum of 10 kg dry blend and 10 kg of neat cement should be

                         transferred from the rig to cement lab along with additive samples for

              QC testing right after receiving cement. During the cement job, 5 litres

                        of a representative mix-water sample should be collected from cement

                   mix water tanks.

           Conduct safety meeting prior to start operation.

            Flush all the lines with water. Test the lines to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.

           Mix and pump cement as per program.

            All samples should be stored until the success of the cement job has

                     been verified, then they may be discarded.

            Fill up the cementing operation sheet and send it to drilling office.

5. 7” Cement Program
                 Important: The following program is a sample; therefore  it should be
                     tested using the field drilling water, cement sample and additives.

              For cementing operation, note the followings:
                   1. A minimum of 10 kg dry blend and 10 kg of neat cement should be shipped
                 from the rig to town along with additive samples for QC testing right after
                    receiving cement. During the cement job, 5 litres of a representative mix-
                   water sample should be collected from cement mix water tanks.
                   2. All samples should be stored until the success of the cement job has been
                      verified, then they may be discarded.
                 3. Before running 7” liner, ensure that all liner accessories and liner
                 running equipment are present. Identify liner as ordered, number the
                     joints and check liner condition. Ensure the correct liner drifts are
                  supplied to the rig. Clean and inspect all threads. Measure liner
                  (cross check tally), prepare liner running list and mark all joints to be
                         left out. Check cement. Mix a pilot sample. Hold liner running  /
                   configuration meeting with liner engineer.
