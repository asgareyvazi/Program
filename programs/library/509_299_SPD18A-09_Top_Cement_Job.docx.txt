Objectives: 18 5/8”*13 3/8” Top Cement Job PROCEDURE
While carry H2S detector & SCBA, line up cement line to 18-5/8”Casing Head Housing. Open 2”-5K side outlet vale & observe if the annulus gains or not for half an hour. (during observation test cement line to 500psi) If no gains leave the valve open, perform top cement job by mix & pump cement ‘G-UCC’ with sea water. Slurry weight:  15.8ppg Slurry volume: 100bbl Pump rate: 2bpm for first 70bbl & 1bpm for last 30bbl In the case of annulus gains, first close the side outlet valve then try to inject sea water  with pump rate 1-2bpm maximum pressure 50psi If the injection was done, go to item 3 in order to perform top cement job (open side outlet valve ) Flush cement line & casing head housing with 10bbl sea water, be sure casing head housing & side outlet valve cleaned from any cement slurry. Close 2”-5k side outlet valve & wait on cement for 3hours.
