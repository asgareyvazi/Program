Objectives: cementing 18 5/8” Casing

Planned Operations:

   1. Prepare all lines for cmt manifold and L/U to cmt unit.
   2. Hold pre-job safety meeting.
   3. Press tests the line with 3000psi. Bleed-off  press to zero.
   4. Drop bottom plug in the casing and open the cmt line on cmt manifold.
   5. Mix & pump 245.5 bbl of 12.5 ppg lead cmt slurry solution by cmt unit. Mixing
       fluid required for lead cmt slurry is 189bbl.
   6. Mix & pump 238 bbl of 15.8 ppg tail cmt slurry solution by cmt unit. Mixing fluid
      required for tail cmt slurry is 141 bbl, Pump CMT with 10 BPM.
   7. Note: Mud engineer to be near cmt unit to take the weight of slurry solution.
   8. Note: Shaker man to be on shakers and check for any return and inform
     company man.
   9.  Drop top plug. Close cmt line and open displacement line on cmt unit.
   10. Pump 12.5 bbl sea water by cmt unit.
   11. Switch to rig mud pumps and displace cmt with 933 bbl (……stk) of sea water
     With 5 BPM.
   12. At 920bbls (…….stk) reduce flow rate and bump the plug with 1000psi press.

   13. Note: Mud engineer and pump man should also check the volume of sea water
     which will be used for displacement.

   14. Hold press. Monitor the press. bleed off press and check for back flow.
   15. Disconnect all lines and hoses F/cmt head.
   16. L/D cmt head.
   17. Release starting head running tool as per engineer instruction.
   18. Flush top of wellhead by sea water.
   19. POOH and L/D landing joint and running tool.
