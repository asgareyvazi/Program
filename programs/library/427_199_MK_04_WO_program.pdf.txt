 1.   Introduction


1.1   Program Introduction

  This program covers 1st. workover on well No.4 of Maleh Kuh oil field, I.R.Iran. The
  well Supposed to be an oil producer from Ilam formation.

 Problem
 The well MK-4 needs repair because it doesn't produce oil from the reservoir.

 Main Objective
 The main objective of this program is to plug back the open hole and stop the source
  of water flow, and find new means of oil production by perforating new intervals in
  Ilam formation.

  Note: This program is issued with respect to the available information of workover
  request no. FO-211-02 dated: 138/7 11 1/2 West oil & gas production Co.

 The main head lines of this program are as follow:

  1.   Move and rig up the drilling rig.
  2.   Check wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record well head pressures; investigate
        for seepage of all the casing annuluses.
  3.   Connect and test kill, flow and return lines.
  4.    Kill and secure well. ( including wire line operation)
  5.   Test all the p-seals of tubing and casing spools and fix the problems at site.
  6.   N/D well head and N/U BOP's
  7.    Pull out completion string
  8.   Ensure the free pass of 8 3/8" mill through 9 5/8" casing
  9.   Mill and retrieve packer in 7" liner
  10.  Plug back the open hole interval
  11.  Perforate 7" liner at specified intervals
  12.  Run completion string
  13.  N/D BOP's and N/U well head
  14.  Flow the well
  15.  Secure the well and release rig

1.3   Drilling History

 26" Hole Section:
  Drilling Rig O2 spudded on location E012N at 1386/09/24 (Dec. 15th. 2007), and
  started to drill well MK-04 with 26" bit. Complete loss occurred right after drilling
  the first 20 cm of the formation which controlled to 70 bph loss with Mica pill.
  Drilling then continued with bentonite mud to 36 m with no return.

 Mud flow was observed out of three points of location basement below shale
  shakers. 100 bbls 120 pcf cement slurry was pumped in two stages and cured the
  seepage zones. Drilling then continued to 155m w/ no loss no gain and 20" casing
 was run to 152m and cemented to surface.

  17-1/2" Hole Section:
  Drilling continued with 17 1/2" bit to 746m w/ 65-70 pcf bentonite mud (NL/NG).
 Mud weight was gradually increased below 503m, from 70 pcf to 72 pcf to overcome
  the hole problems but because of ever increasing need to wash and ream, mud weight
  increased another 2 pcf to become 74 pcf at the end of the section. 13 3/8" casing was
  run to 741m and cemented to surface.

  12-1/4" Hole Section:
  Shifted mud system into 68 pcf oil base mud and continued to drill 12 1/4" hole to
 1210m w/ no loss no gain. After running CDR/GR logs 9 5/8" casing was run to
 1209m and cemented to surface.

  8-1/2" Hole Section:
  Pilot hole ( vertical)
  Drilling 8 1/2" pilot hole started at 1210m and continued to 1319m (coring point). 59
  pcf oil emulsion mud was applied in drilling the section. In the beginning loss rate
 was as high as 30 bph but pumping LCM pills stopped the loss. 15m core was cut
  from 1319m – 1334m with 100% recovery. The loss rate while coring recorded as 16
  bph. Performed DST in Ilam formation from 1334m to 1209m, and observedgas cut
 mud during the DST. Displaced mud system with water and continued to drill to
 1403m with heavy loss 120-50 bph. Pumped and spot 130 bbls magneset plug to cure
  the loss. Shifted back to 59 pcf oil emulsion mud and drilled to 1547m (TD) w/ NL,
 NG. Ran Full suit, VSP, XPT logs with poor results. PLT detected indications of gas
  flow. Well was stimulated by injecting 40 bbls of HCL to the interval 1500-1325m.
 The pilot hole was plugged back to 1214m by cement plug due to undesirable results
  of another PLT.

  Directional Hole (leg #1)
  Drilling a directional 8 1/2" hole started from 1214m, Kicked off well @ 1220m and
  continued drilling to 1276m. Drilled interval was plugged back to 1211m because of
  abnormal increase in deviation angle. 59 pcf oil emulsion mud was used in this
  section.

  Directional Hole (leg #2)
  Drilling started with oil emulsion 59 pcf mud F/ 1211m. Well was Kicked off at
 1215m and continued to drill directional hole to 1512m ( 7" liner setting depth 90°
  inclination).Loss started at 1242m at a rate of 25-40 bph. LCM pills were pumped
  periodically to cure the  loss. Shifted mud system to water  at 1328m and loss
  increased to 30-40 bph to the liner point).

  7" liner was run F/ 1511.5-1085.5m and cemented as per program. Cement slurry was
  lost while cement job and cement was squeeze to the liner lap to complete the cmt
  job. Because of the results of dry test, 7" liner was tied back and extended to 964m
  and cemented again. Performed a new dry test and had a good result this time.

  6-1/8" Hole Section:
  Drilling horizontal well started at 1512 m and continued to 1831m. 59 pcf oil
  emulsion mud was used in this section. Different loss rates were observed in the
  section and pumped (8 ppb shell fish) pills to cure the losses. Encountered complete
  loss at TD and stopped drilling. Ran CBL/VDL/CCL/GR F/ 1508-922m to evaluate
  the performance of cement behind the 7" liner.

1.4   Completion History
  Well MK-04 was completed with a combination of 5 1/2", 4 1/2" tubing and 7"
  packer (as per attached tally) right after drilling operation. Well completion started at
  1387/01/26 (14th. April 2008) and finished in a 2 days period on 1387/01/28 (16th.
  April 2008). No problem was reported in this operation.

1.5   production and Stimulation History
 The well could not produce even a drop of oil. Several attempts were made to bring
  the well into production. Well was acidized with 165 bbls 15% hydrochloric acid at
  87/04/17. In a 2 month stimulation operation from 87/04/18 to 87/06/13 in 5 stages,
  123 tones of nitrogen were pumped by coiled tubing and spotted at the 1350m -
 1830m interval. Well was kicked off by nitrogen lifting mechanism, but it produced
  only salt water with weights ranging from 66-67 pcf.


1.6  Work Over history
 The last few meters interval of this well where experienced complete losses in drilling
  operation was doubted to be the source of salt water, and main reason of self killing
  the well. In order to plug this interval, four attempts were made by coiled tubing and
  7.2 bbls (totally in 4 stages) cement slurry spotted and squeezed in the accessible
  bottom section of the horizontal 6 1/8" hole from 1783m-1820m. This operation
  couldn't bring the well into production

1.7   Perforations:
 No perforation in  this well. Well was expected to produce from 271m 6 1/8"
  horizontal drain from 1512m to 1783m.

2.   Workover Program

1. Move work over rig from Sa-4 to the well site of Male Kuhe-4. R/U and make it
   ready for operation, Record rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea
    level. (An overall check on well head spools, valves, P-Seals and x-mass tree is
    strongly recommended before rig move. After killing and securing the well, all
   items must be checked and serviced by well head crew and repaired or replaced if
    needed.)

2.  Connect kill & flow lines to the x-mass tree side outlets and connect return line to
   2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 3000 psi pressure.

3.  Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing and 5" x 9-5/8", 9-5/8" x 13-3/8"
   and 13-3/8" x 20" annuluses. Release / Bleed off pressures, monitor their build up
   and investigate for the probable seepage.

   Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
   while investigating the communications.

4.  Test control line to 4500 psi and open 3SV

5.  Start killing the well by pumping 160 bbls fresh water (1 1/2 volume of the tubing
   and 7" liner) and squeeze the tubing and casing content into the reservoir. The
   well will be under loss since the hydrostatic pressure of full column of fresh water
    is 366 psi over the formation pressure at 7" liner shoe. Fill up (lubricate) the
   annulus fresh water if required.

   Note: The volume of tubing and 7" liner to shoe @ 1511m is almost 110 bbls. The
   annular volume of tubings above packer is 180 bbls.

   Note: Monitor the pressures of annuluses all the time

6.  Rig up wire line equipment and test to 3000psi pressure. Run gage rings and
    specify the free pass of tubings above 3SV.

7. Remove 3SV from landing nipple. Observe well if static or under loss, RIH and
    install plug «2.813" AF « in the top no Go Landing nipple. Run shifting tool and
   open SSD above packer. Break circulation and balance mud in and out. Install
  dummy and plug into 3SV landing nipple (Type "FRQ") and test to 3000 psi.

8.  Observe well. If static install 6 5/16"two way inside tubing hanger (Type MSP
    Drillex) and pressure test same to 3000 psi.

9.  While circulating fresh water cross tubing head spool and monitoring the mud
   volume, lay down well head and N/U 13-5/8"-5000 psi BOP stack. Test BOP and
    related lines, to 4500 psi as per NIOC policy.

    sufficient for working with taper tap.

15. Run proper size of taper tap and retrieve packer and tail.

16. RIH with 5 7/8" E mill and 3 1/2" drill pipes to 1511.5m. Circulate bottom up.
  POH and Lay down mill.

17. RIH with 6 1/8"center cut bit to 1511.5m. Mix and pump class "G" 115 pcf cmt
   and spot at bottom as balance plug. POH 5 stand flush pipe. Circulate clean hole.
   Wait on cement to get hard.

18. RIH and tag top of cement. Repeat cmt job/jobs till could get cement inside 7"
    liner.
   Note: Experiences of loss in this well may dictate repetitive cement jobs to
   plug the open hole.

   Note: Detailed cement program will be issued in due time.

19. Drill out cement and dress to 1470m to have 18m rat hole below proposed
    perforation interval 1400m-1452m. Pressure test casing and 7" liner lap with 2000
    psi.
20. Displace well with 58 pcf oil emulsion mud.

21. Run Perforation guns with TCP tool and perforate liner as per program.

22. Run completion string as per program.

4.  Mud program
History
59 pcf oil emulsion mud was applied initially in drilling 8 1/2" section. Loss started at
1242m (25-40 bph). LCM pills pumped to cure the loss but finally shifted to water as
drilling fluid. As a consequence loss rate increased to 30-40 bph.

6 1/8" hole  drilled with 59 pcf  oil emulsion mud.  Different  loss  rates were
experienced in the section and pumped (8 ppb shell fish) pills to cure the losses.
Encountered complete loss at TD and stopped drilling.

over operation in this well:

5.   Completion Fluid Formulation:

The well supposed to be completed with kick off string after perforation. Well
should be displaced with 58 pcf oil emulsion mud prior to perforation.

Note:
The pressure of reservoir at top of perforations is almost 1620 psi which is the
same as datum pressure. Hydrostatic head of 58 pcf OEM to this depth (1400m
MD, 1375m TVD)  is 200 psi over the reservoir pressure.
