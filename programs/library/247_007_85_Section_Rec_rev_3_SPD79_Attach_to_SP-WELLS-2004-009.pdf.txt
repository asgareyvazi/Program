     The 8 ½” Section Pitfalls





         Differential sticking
                 Keep the fluid loss below 2ml
                    Minimize connection time
                     Start jarring at max hit upwards
        Apply max torque on string and set down max weight
            Always rotate and pump if stop in operations
           Swabbing
                       Follow the trip plan
               Never pull upwards without pumping
           Monitor trip tank volumes and active volumes
     Pump into slips and take high connection drilling / POOH
(M/U topdrive to string directly, without first lifting string with elevator)
              Losses
                Focus on connection procedures
              Follow the decision tree if losses occur
           Packing off
Consider to reduce flow rate to 500lpm when B/R through shale sections

2     Challenges

       Well control
        •  High focus on well control and kick detection
        •   If any well-control situation, Statoils well control procedures have to be followed circulating
            out a kick (Driller’s Method)
        •  Stop drilling if severe losses (ref decision tree)
        •  Avoid swabbing – Pump out of open hole and 400m into the casing
        •  High gas peak up to 84% in SPD8-07
        •  SP-9 (phase 8): Gas was observed (10-50%) when circulating after installing the 7” liner.
          The SP-9 well 8 ½” section was drilled with 1.37-1.39sg mud weight.
        •  Pump into slips and take high connection drilling / POOH
         (M/U topdrive to string directly, without first lifting string with elevator)

         Drills
         Drills while inside the 9 5/8” casing:
        •  H2S drill
        •  Well control drill
        •  Pump over the burners

       H2S
        •  High values of H2S while drilling is not expected based on the offset wells.
        •  During testing & production, the values of H2S in the reservoir can be up to 5000ppm.
        •  Be aware of possible H2S while circulation above RTTS packer (SPD9-11) and pulling it out
              (ref SPD8-14)

         Differential sticking
        •  The K4 reservoir could be depleted with approximately 30 bar, resulting in 70-100 bar over-
            balance. Also, K4 has the highest permeability.
        •   If differential sticking occurs: Evaluate to pull out with the BHA and drill the remaining with
           a rotary BHA without PDM/MWD/LWD
        •  Surveys in K4: One survey in top K4 and one in bottom K4
        •  Keep acid (HCl) on one of the rigs

        Losses
        •  SPD1 & SPD2: Only seepage losses have been reported, except for SPD2-05 where signifi-
            cant losses were observed in the K1
        •  SPD4-1: Significant losses
        •  In the appraisal wells in phases 6-8, only seepage losses in the SP-11 (phase 6) has been re-
            ported
        •  SPD7 first campaign: No losses
        •  SPD8: No losses
        •  Be aware of high ECD due to 5 ½” DP. 1.57-1.65sg ECD was experienced in SPD7-02 & 15

        Packing off
        •  Consider to reduce flow rate to 500lpm when B/R through shale sections

3     Geology

        3.1   TD
             •  The 8 1/2” hole will be drilled to 50 m TVD above the estimated GWC
               However, the GWC varies across the field, and will be given for each individual well.
             •   If the K4 is coming in significantly deeper than prognosis, it will be considered to set
                  the TD closer to the GWC.

        3.2     Cuttings sampling
             •  Phase 8: SPD9-02 & SPD9-13
             •  Phase 6: No cuttings sampling required

        3.3    FIT
             •  FIT: 1.70 SG EMW.

        3.4   LWD / MWD
             •  DIR/GR/RES/DENSITY/NEUTRON
             •  Perform SJA regarding handling of sources (report in DDR)
             •  Max ROP: 60 m/hr due to logging
             •  Expected repeat section: 60m @60m/hr backreaming in K1. Depths to be decided by
                  geologist
             •  Memory data to be checked and verified before continuing operations


4     Rig

       ¾ Be prepared to divert over the burner booms in case of kick
       ¾ Be prepared to rotate string with rotary table in case of top drive failure
       ¾ Check hours on wash pipe
       ¾  Consider to change saver sub prior to section
       ¾ Do not dope DP when POOH before running liner

5     Operational Sequence

             1.  Test BOP (if required, max 21 days)
             2.  Install short bore protector / wear bushing
             3. Only valid for SPD9-11: Retrieve RTTS packer
             4.  Pressure test the 9 5/8” x 10 ¾” casing, if not already done.
             5.  Perform pre-job safety meeting regarding handling of radioactive sources
             6. MU and RIH with 8 ½” motor/MWD/LWD BHA on 5 ½” DP
             7.  Perform well control, choke and H2S drills
             8.  Clean rat hole and drill 3 m of new formation. Ensure good mud properties
             9.  Perform FIT to 1.70 SG EMW.
            10. Drill 8 ½” hole to TD.
            11. Circulate hole clean, min 2 BU. Condition mud for liner running.
            12. Pump out of open hole to avoid swabbing
              Perform repeat sections
                    If B/R, pulling speed is max 6 min/stand
             Flowcheck in shoe
            13. Pump OOH until 400m inside 9 5/8” casing with reduced flow rate.
                (Circulate B/U in shoe)
            14. POOH
                Pull max 2 min/std for the next 600m
                Pull max 1 min/std for the next 1000m
            15. Perform pre-job safety meeting regarding handling of radioactive sources
            16. End of drilling Operations


6      Directional Drilling

        6.1   BHA
           • The BHA will be designed to minimize wall contact and diff sticking (i.e. no DCs)
           • The BHA will include 6 ¾” Extreme motor (0.0 AKO)
           • A PBL sub will be installed in the BHA
           • Flush the motor and MWD with FW prior to racking back

        6.2     Survey
           • Take surveys one single above bottom and with MWD in tension (upweight).
           • If surveys are flagged, continue drilling to next survey point (maximum survey interval is
             100 m)
           • Surveys in K4: One survey in top K4 and one in bottom K4

        6.3    Max hours on rotation BHA string components
           • Jar /Accelerator   :    250 rotation hrs
           • X/O                   :    300 rotation hrs
           • DP/ HWDP        :    1500 rotation hrs
           • MWD                :    250 circulating hrs

7    Mud

        •  Use KCl / Polymer / 3%Glycol mud
        •  CaCO3 will be used to lower fluidloss and minimize differential sticking.
        •  Add corrosion inhibitor and oxygen scavenger
        •  Have Pipelax available
        •  Mud engineer to quality check the LCM sack content before mixing
           Everything mixed into the system shall go through the hopper
        •  Mud weight SPD7 (SD2):     1.41sg
        Mud weight SPD9 (RWO):    1.37sg


8     Liner

          7” Predrilled / 29ppf / 13Cr80 / AMS 28


9     Flow rate and Hydraulics

       Flow rate while drilling:                                2000 lpm
       Flow rate while backreaming:                             500-2000 lpm
       Flow rate while reaming down:                           1200 lpm
      Pump rate MWD tool (Standard):                          1325-2460 lpm
      Pump flow rate for 6 3/4" M1X-P X-treme motor:            1000-2500 lpm
        Experienced ECD:                                           1.57-1.65 SG
        Experienced pump pressure:                               200-250 bar

       Back reaming through shale sections
        Consider to reduce flow rate to 500lpm when B/R through shale sections

       Tripping
         1. Pump out of open hole and 400m into the 9 5/8” casing
         2.  Pull max 2 min/std for the next 600m
         3.  Pull max 1 min/std for the next 1000m

     STANDARD LOST CIRCULATION RECIPE: 8 ½” Section (also use flow chart)

     1.  Acid Soluble Materials
        1.1  SEEPAGE LOSS SOLUTIONS
           Initial attempts to solve losses should be made by pre-treating the system with CaCo3 FM at 28 kg/m3
         each. If losses continued, then pump concentrated pill of the followings;
        1.2  MODERATE LOSS SOLUTIONS
        8 m3 Pill @ 60 KG/M3
                   2   Product            Concentration ( kg/m3 )
                              CaCo3 F                  30
                              CaCo3 M                  30
        2.1  SEVERE LOSS SOLUTIONS (Need to open PBL sub)
        8 m3 Pill @ 114 KG/M3
                   3   Product            Concentration ( kg/m3 )
                              CaCo3 F                  57
                              CaCo3 M                  57


     2.   Partially Acid Soluble Materials
        3.1  SEVERE LOSS SOLUTIONS (Need to open PBL sub)
        8 m3 Pill @ 114 KG/M3
                   4   Product            Concentration ( kg/m3 )
                              CaCo3 F                  28
                              CaCo3 M                  28
                               Mix II F                  15
                              Mix II M                  28
                               EPI Seal XF                  15

4.1.1   Procedur
        •   Spot in open hole, pull above loss zone and circulate to squeeze bridging material to fractures. Slowly increase
              circulation to minimum rate required for drilling. Increase to 2000 lpm to clean the tool
        •   Pumping rate through MWD/Motor: 1000lpm


4.1.1.1    Instructions

    1.  Ensure Homogenize mixing.

    2. Mud Engineer to supervise the mixing of LCM pills.

    3. Mud Engineer to visually inspect all LCM being used.

     4.  Mud Engineer to make a clear instruction to the rig mixing crew that if they see any change in size of a par-
         ticular LCM grade confirmed initially they must STOP mixing and inform the mud engineer. Would prefer to
        put laminated instruction in mixing area.

     5.  Company representative, mud engineer and MWD/DD engineers need to agree on the size of LCM mate-
           rial and the above concentrations prior to pumping to avoid plug tools.

     6.  Mud Engineer to ensure no Coarse LCM to be mixed in any pills, unless a circulation sub is put in the BHA.
        Otherwise, ONLY fine and Medium grades are required.
