                              General Information


1  Introduction


        1.1  Program Introduction
         This program covers the drilling of well# 9 in West Paydar oil field, Iran. The well is
          intended to evaluate reservoir properties and to produce maximum allowable oil from
        Asmari formation. The well will be drilled vertically down to 2417m then directionally
           in the Asmari formation.
         Note-1: The exact TD will be determined based on the logging results.
         Note-2: The Open hole will be plugged back to 20m (TVD) up the Water-Oil contact
           level which its exact depth will be determined according to the reservoir logging
            results.

        1.2  West Paydar Structure

        The West Paydar field is located 140 km to the NW of Ahwaz and 35 km to the west
          of Cheshmeh Khosh structure. The Iran –Iraqi borderline passes through this field.
        The W006N is located at an approximate distance of 643m to the NW of PYW-1 &
       644m to the south of PYW-5. The Aghajari formation makes up the surface geology of
           this location.
        The approximate coordinates of West Paydar-9 are:
         Lambert:
          E: 1, 741, 261m
         N: 1, 127, 450m

9 Pre Spud

      P.P.Z prepared a pre-spud checklist and distributed it in contractor rigs. The checklist will

      be performed based on the aforementioned  checklist. In addition to the  checklist,

      following matters should be checked:

   ➢ Check & observe the alignment of the rig. This check will be performed in four stages;

           rig levelling, derrick verticality, derrick alignment with rotary table, rotary table

         alignment with conductor pipe. To do this pick up 3 joints of 9 3/4” DC and check the

           verticality of the derrick.

   ➢  Mechanical facilities for mud treatment such as :

         Desander, Desilter, mud cleaner, proper size of shaker–screens, hoppers, mud guns &

          mixers, should be all available on rig and functioning properly.

   ➢  All mud tanks, mud transfer lines & connection should be checked by filling them

         with water & cross wellhead circulation.

   ➢ Mud  engineer  laboratory should be equipped with a complete  set  of  testing

         equipment. The calibration should be done under supervision of well site drilling

          supervisor before starting the operation.

   ➢ Weight indicator, rotary speed gauge, any smart drilling instrumentation, Stroke

          meter, pressure gauges on manifold and in the drilling console, loss / gain gauge and

         any other gauges should be checked and calibrated.

   ➢  Stand pipe manifold valves & connection must be checked & pressure tested.

   ➢  All motors should be checked under load prior to start drilling.

   ➢ Check & control mud stock & cement stock every day & if there is any necessity  to

          request mud material, cement or any drilling tool, it should be prepared & requested

           at proper time.

                                 Geology


1  Structural Geology

   The W-Paydar structure which is oriented in the direction of NW-SE, forms part of the
    Foghi asymmetric anticline. Across the border in Iraq based on seismic interpretation, the
    Paydar and W-Paydar anticlines are separated by a saddle in the form of an echelon fold.
   The Bakhtiari conglomerates and soft Agajari fm make relatively smooth & flat topography,
    however it changes into higher elevations towards N-NE which for the Asmari and Sarvak is
    respectively 3036mss & 4040m ss.

2  Stratigraphy


       •  Aghajari

              Lithology: sandy, silty red marl, soft to semi hard. Expands when wet, red to
            brown sandstone, cemented sub rounded to sub angular /interlayers of anhydrite
        & limestone.
            Age: mid to upper Miocene.
                Fossils: Rotallia beccardi, Nonion, Ephidium, Tubucellaria sp


       •  Gachsaran fm:

            The following members make Gachsaran: GS mbr. 7-6:
            Gray marl;  soft,  sticky and expandable. Red marl;  sticky and expandable.
              Anhydrite; white to milky.

         GS mbr 5:
            Gray marl; soft, sticky and expandable with interlayers of limestone. Anhydrite;
              white to milky salt, cream to yellowish, translucent.

          Gs mbr4:
                Salt; cream to yellowish.

          Gs mbr 3:
              Anhydrite; marly, white to milky, grayish, Gray marl; soft expandable and sticky.
             Limestone; thin bedded, chalky gray, white to milky.

         GS mbr-2
                Salt; thick layered, yellow to cream with interlayers of anhydrite.

                          Geology



•  Cap rock:

      This mbr is made up of six key beds:

     Key bed "A": Anhydrite; white, partially crystallized, sandy, radial, translucent.

     Key bed "B": Gray marl; soft, slightly dark, bituminous.

     Key bed "C": Limestone; cream to light brown, relatively hard, dolomitic.

     Key bed "D": Limestone; gray to cream, dolomitic, anhydritic.

     Key bed "E": Limestone; gray to cream, dolomitic argillaceous.

     Key bed "F": Limestone, gray to cream, dolomitic with miliolids.


•  Asmari formation:

      Limestone, cream to light brown, type I and II relatively hard, saccarodial
      dolomitic with good porosity.
      Limestone I&II; white, chalky.
      Shale; silty, red, gray.
      Sandstone; white to gray translucent surrounded to angular.

       Fossils:
      Nummulites, Vascus, Nummulites Intermedius, Peneroplis thomasi, Eulepidina,
      Miogypsina, Alveolina melo curdico, Archaias.
      Age: Lower Miocene.

                    Sequence Of Drilling Operation


1.3 Sequence of Operation

     •  Make up 26” BHA as per BHA design, drill formation down to 130.4m (18-5/8”
         casing point in Aghajari formation). Use LCM pill if encountered heavy losses. If
          loss couldn’t be cured by LCM pill use cement plug(s) to cure same.

     Note:
       - 26” TD is based on 130.4m inside Aghajari formation.
       - TD should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of rat hole under casing shoe.
       - The exact TD will be determined based on 18-5/8” casing tally.
       - Do not forget to strap pipe while POOH.
       - Drop Totco prior to POOH to evaluate the verticality of hole.
       - Use following parameters to drill this hole section:
        -WOB = Maximum available
         -RPM = 90-130
        -GPM = 600-1000

     •  At TD, Circulate, clean hole. POOH, P/U 26” N.Bit stabilizer or 26” Reamer, RIH
        and condition the hole to section TD. Check for any fill. CBU on bottom.

     •  POOH. If observed any indication of drag, run back to bottom. Wash and ream to
       make sure that the hole condition is good.

     •  R/U 18-5/8” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

     •  Run 18-5/8", 87.5 ppf, J-55, BTS casing with stab in-type guide shoe to bottom as
         per casing program. Casing shoe should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters
         of rat hole and enough stick up (1.5 m) on rig floor. Fill casing by non LCM fresh
      mud joint by joint.

     Note:
       -  Take care when running casing not to set down excessive weight if tight hole or
        Ledges are encountered.

     •  Run stinger on 5” DP’s using the “C” Plate & 5” x 18-5/8” centralizer. Sting in.
      M/U & test cementing lines. Circulate for one casing annulus volume. While
          circulating, check the DP’s annulus for any return. Use all DP’s weight on the
          string, if observing any minor leakage through DP’s annulus.

     •  Cement casing as per program. Any minor adjustment regarding the corrected depth
          will be done in due time. Displace with 6 bbls mud (2 bbls Short). Sting out. Check
         the float. Circulate to clean the DP. POOH.

                Sequence Of Drilling Operation


Note:
- Based on the results obtained from cementing, decision will be made to perform top-
up jobs.

•  Rest the 18-5/8” casing on the elevator until cement surface samples get hard.
   Check for casing to be centred inside 30” conductor pipe. In case of any problem,
    report immediately.

•   If there is no cement return to surface, a top-up job should be performed to fill the
    annulus, stage by stage until cement reaches the surface. Use the same weight as the
   primary cement slurry. Ensure adequate X-over for connecting line pipe to cement
    line is available. The cement should be accelerated with max. 2.2 lb/sxs calcium
    chloride and no other additives. Wait for the top job cement to get hard.

•  Cut 30” conductor pipe from cellar base. L/D and remove same.

•  Rough cut 18-5/8” landing joint and L/D same. Dress casing neck suitable to install
   and to weld casing head spool. The distance between its top flange and ground level
   must be 1.73m.

•   Install and weld casing head housing 21 ¼” – 2000 psi on casing stub and test the
    sealing of welded casing (limit pressure to a maximum of 80% of the collapse
    strength of the casing or maximum 500 psi). Outlets to align with cellar stairs.

Note:
