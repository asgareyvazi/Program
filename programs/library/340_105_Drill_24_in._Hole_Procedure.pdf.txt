            24” Hole Section Drilling Procedure
                San Jacinto #2 Prospect

 1. Release DAT, drill 24” interval to ~ 11615’MD per Dominion procedure.
        •   Drill interval with seawater unless flow is encountered
        •    If well flows, kill with 12.5 lb mud then drill ahead with 10.5 lb mud
        •   First 2 stds pump 800-1000 gpm
        •   Control drill 120 - 160 ft/hr
        •  Survey  1st 3 stds after MWD clears shoe
             o  By-pass sub interfering with MWD signal - Bring pumps online as aggressively as
                  deemed safe to get good signal as survey is pumped to surface

 2. Drill rest of interval per Co Rep w/ SW unless flow is encountered
        •  Have ROV perform flow checks every connection
        •   Rotary – 60-80 RPM
        •  WOB 0-5 klb
        •  Flow Rate – 1800- 2000 gpm; to protect motor, drop flow rate to 60% of drilling rate before
           coming off bottom
        •   Monitor PWD – watch for upward trend, control PWD with P-rate. Keep PWD below 8.9 ppg.
        •  Try to maintain 100+ FPH avg ROP
        •  Survey every 500’ and survey again at TD
        •   Section TD 11,615’ – deeper than 11675’ MD requires MMS notification

 3. At section TD we will circulate and clean up hole,
        •  Have 75 bbl 16.0 ppg viscous sweep in DP timed to go out bit when bit reaches TD
        •  Follow the viscous sweep with ½ hole volume 12.5 ppg WBM
        •  Follow the WBM with 1.5 hole volume 12.5 ppg lo-fluid loss pad mud.
        •   Displace pad mud with SW to WH.
        •  Flow check. Prepare to pull out of hole

 4. Wipe hole to 36” shoe. If tight spots are encountered we will attempt to work thru them w/o rotating and
     then ream back to bottom from 36” shoe. If back reaming is necessary we will use pad mud to BR and any
     other reaming that may be necessary.

 5. TIH and spot 50 bbls of 16.0 pad mud POOH above weighted pill followed by 12.5 pad mud and POOH

 6. RU to run casing
