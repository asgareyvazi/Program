Objectives: Drilling 23.5” Phase (Top of Jahrum to TD)
Planned Operations :

   1. Make up rotary assembly:

        • Be careful not to side track the well
        •  If problems to get down, wash down with low flow
        •  If reaming is necessary, always ream on way up(40 RPM)
        • Wash down the last one stands


   2. Drilling parameters from Top of jahrum formation(Complete Loss zone) :

        • WOB:  20~35 Klbs
        • RPM:   70~95
        •  Flow Rate: 1200 GPM

        Note 1: If drilling with high torque, consider rotating and pumping pills at
       same spot until torque decreases

        Note 2: If the pumps have to be stopped for any reason – POOH for 2 or 3
        stands & Spot PHG pills around BHA.


   3. Sweep Plan in Jahrum formation:

        • Pump 30 bbls PHG pill every 3 mt drilled.
        • Pump 40 Bbls guar gum pill every 2th 3 mt drilled.
        •  Reduce Flow rate to 800 GPM when pill enter bit.

4. Connection procedure in Jahrum Formation:

      • Ream & back ream with Low RPM (40) and same drilling GPM.
      • Pump 40 bbls GG and Circ for 15 min.
      •  Spot 60 Bbls PHG around BHA before connection, do not pump water
       Out of bit.
      • Make sure the pipe is free and low torque readings (Below +/-7500 ft-lb)
        before making connection, if not, circulate more pills/SW.

     Note 1: Focus on quick connection.

     Note 2: After Connection Start Rotation and then bring the pump up slowly in
      steps (2 min) to 800 GPM and keep for a while before increasing it further.
