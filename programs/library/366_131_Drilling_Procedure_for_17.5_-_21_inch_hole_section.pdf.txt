                       Contractor: NDCO.
                     Well Type: Exploration
  Procedure for drilling 17 ½” x 21 ” section “IAK”



 1. Run Bops on riser. Testing riser choke and kill lines when running in. land bops on

    18 ¾” well head and latch as per NDCO procedure.

 2. Run isolation test tool as per Vetco procedure and test the BOP and 18 ¾ Well head as

    per commisioning test procedure 15000psi SWP.

   NOTE: during the 15000psi test on BOP connectors, according to drilling program,

   we need to use use 5.5", S-135, 38.01# DP as this has a collaps pressure of 31,798psi

    and the (21.90 lbs/ft has a collaps pressure of 12,679psi) and we don’t want to collaps

     this drill pipe. So we require to use the 38.01 lbs/ft.

 3.  Function test overboard Diverter system and pressure test to 500psi. Pressure test

    300psi low 5 mins and 500psi high 10mins.

 4.  After testing is completed on the Bop, Well head and diverter system . Pooh with the

     isolation test tool and lay out same.

 5. Make up 17 ½” slick BHA #1 and RIH. (None ported float to be run in bit sub).

 6.  Install diverter insert packer and lock in.

 7.  Care must be taken when passing 16” landing ring @ 1093.17m ID 17.57”.

 8.  Continue to RIH and filling pipe every 10 stands with sea water Tag (Top of cement

    +- 1179.37m.)

 9.   Drill out cement inside 20" casing with 17 1/2" bit from 1179.37m to the top of 20"

    shoe @ 1193.37m. using sea water. (Shoe @ 1194.37m).

 10. Perform diverter exersise and follow shallow gas procedures for drilling new hole.

 11. Pump and sweep hole with a hi vis pill and displace hole with 9.63ppg (72 PCF) water

    based mud.

12. Cont drill out cement from 1193.37m and shoe @ 1194.37m and cmt in rat hole to

   1205m. (Take care when reaming out shoe and watch torque)

13. Continue drill new formation from 1205m to 1207m. use high-vis pill to sweep the

   hole clean as required.

14. Observe the well by flow checking one hour.

15. Continue drilling formation from 1207m to 1230m with BHA No. 1. pumping high-vis

    pill to sweep hole as required. NOTE: Drill with low WOB and high RPM to control

    the iclination of the well.

  NOTE: Control trip's speed when going through 16” landing ring @ 1093.17m inside

   20" casing. Inside diamiter ( ID) at this section is 17.57"

16. Circ bottoms up and flow check.

17. POOH and change the BHA.#1 for BHA #2 (Note: Incert packer to be unlocked

    before pulling bit into rotary table.)

18. RIH with BHA No. 2 that consits of 17 1/2" bit, 21" under reamer, Stabs and MWD.

    Install diverter insert packer and take care going through16” landing ring@1093.17m
   Continue drilling new hole from1230m to 1713m TD Bit depth. Adjust Mud weight

  (MW) to suit the well well situation. use high-vis pill to sweep hole as required.

  NOTE: Drill with low RPM from 1230m to 1240m to prevent any damage to the 20"

    casing shoe with stabilizer. Stabilizer shall be above the 20” Csg shoe 6.6m +-. And

    the 21” cutter shall be +- 4.40m below the 20”shoe.

19. Circulate bottoms up and Flow check.

20. POOH and change the BHA #2 for BHA #3 Trip drills to be performed when tripping

    out hole times recorded. (Note: unlock incert packer before the first stab comes

   through rotary table.

21. RIH with BHA #3 install diverter insert packer after last stabilizer run.

22. Continue RIH with #3 BHA and open up the 17 1/2" hole to 21" from 1682m to

   1705.5m+- (under reamer depth) Bit depth 1713m+- with BHA No. 3. use high-vis

    pills to sweep the hole as required. (Geoligest shall confirm the casing point).

23. Circulate bottms up untill shakers are clean.
