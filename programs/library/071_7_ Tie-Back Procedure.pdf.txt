    Screw and baker lock the Seal nipple to the first joint liner.
                                                               Depended Program
    Screw and baker lock the Mechanical LDC to the second joint liner.

     Trip in with liner according a program.

     Rig up setting tools.

     Pin the wiper plug to the shear sub on slick joint.

    Screw the tools assembly to the last joint and make up tool assembly.

     Drift rabbit and SLM drill pipe.

     Trip in with drill pipes until touch Lap liner.

    Check the weight indicator.

     Circulation bottoms up.

    Be sure of string in and sting off.

     Entire the first O-ring seal nipple with 100 psi pressure.

    Turn off pump and put out O-ring seal nipple and exist return mud.

    Be sure a guide of seal nipple was inside tie back.

     Rig up and testing cementing line and trucks according to program.

     Calculate all necessary cementing data such as bumper Vol. and shearing Vol, etc.

    Pumping cement and drop pump down plug and short displeasing with mud observe shearing and

     no observe bumping.

     Enter seal nipple with displeasing pressure.

    Turn the string 20-27 round to the right, setting tools must be released (checking the weight

        indicator, it must show weight of drill pipe.)

     Pick up 4 ft to exit setting dogs from PBR.

   When setting dogs are out of the PBR, slack off 30,000

   POOH to safety point with the highest speed (cement escape).
     In the time of POOH open the drill pipe with air tongs and don’t turn the string in the well to the
        left and right.
   POOH to surface according to program.
    Lay down setting tool at surface and lay down same.
