1.   Introduction


1.1  Program Introduction

This program covers workover of well No.09, of Kangan gas field, I.R.Iran. The well is
gas producer from Upper Dalan formation.

Problem
The tubing annulus (9-5/8” x 7”) is charged with the gas and pressurized to 2103psi
(145bar). Based on the temperature log readings, a weak point in tubing at +/-1920m
depth is the most doubtful leakage area.

Main Objective
The main objective of this program is to fix the problem and resume gas production
from the same formation.

Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of
workover request no. 87/ 610/ اف م , dated: 1387/ 06 /11 Zagros oil & gas production Co.
(Complementary survey on the well file was conducted to clear vague information )

The main head lines of this program are as follow:
1.    Prior to rig move, check wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record well head
      pressures; investigate for seepage of all the annuluses.
2.   Move and rig up the drilling unit.
3.   Connect and pressure test kill, flow and return lines.
4.    Kill the well & secure same by installing plugs and investigate most probable
     leakage area.
5.   Test all P-seals of tubing and casing spools and fix problems at site.
6.   N/D well head and N/U BOP's
7.   Un-latch seal nipple and pull out completion string. Replace doubtful tubing in
     +/-1920m depth interval.
8.  POOH completion string. Check all items and replace with new ones if required.
9.   RIH with appropriate tools and retrieve 9 5/8" Packer
     •   If unable to retrieve packer RIH and mill 9 5/8" packer. RIH  with appropriate
         fishing tools and retrieve remainder of packer and its tail.
10.  Scrap and clean inside 9 5/8" liner in new packer setting area.
11.  Run 9 5/8" x 5 1/2" completion string
12.  N/D BOP's and N/U well head
13.  Flow well
14.  Secure well and release rig

 Attentions:
•   The program which is illustrated here after in this document under the
    head of "Workover Program" could be modified regarding unpredictable
      cases.

2.   Workover Program

  1. Move workover rig from Shanul - 02 to the well site of Kangan - 09, R/U and
    make   it ready  for operation, Record  rotary table and tubing head spool
     elevations from sea level. (An overall check on  well head spools, valves, P-
     Seals and x-mass tree is strongly recommended before rig move. After killing
     and securing the well, all items must be checked and serviced by well head crew
     and repaired or replaced if needed.)

  2.  Connect kill & flow lines to 7-1/16"-5000 x-mas tree side outlets and connect
      return line to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 4500
      psi pressure.

  3. Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 7" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x
     13-3/8" annulus and 13-3/8" x 20" annulus. Release  / Bleed off pressures,
     monitor their build up and investigate for the possible seepages.

     Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of
     pipes while investigating the seepages.

  4.  Rig up W/L and test to 4500 psi. Test control line to 4500 psi. Retrieve 3SV.
    Pump oil into the control line and install 5.963" FRQ Dummy in landing nipple
      of safety valve @ 69m.

  5.  Bleed off the tubing pressure to 500 psi. Observe and record changes in tubing
    and annulus pressures. Retrieve plug. (Dummy still in the place). R/D wire line.

  6. Pump fresh water at least 1 1/2 volume of the tubing capacity (200 bbls) and
     squeeze the tubing content into the reservoir. The well will go under vacuum
     since the full column of fresh water imposes 1030 psi over pressure to the
      reservoir @ top perforations (2480m).  If couldn't squeeze, lubricate the well,
    and observe to make sure about well condition.

     Note: Monitor the pressures of annuluses all the time.

  7.  Install plug «OTIS – 4.313"»in top no go nipple @1949m. (Under Baker "DAB"
     packer @ ±1936m).Test plug and tubing to max 4000psi. Install «OTIS – XN
     4.560"» into extra long seal bore receptacle. Test same to max 4000psi
     pressure. Monitor tubing annulus pressure changes while testing inside tubing.

  8.  Release 7" x 9-5/8" annulus pressure through the burning line. If charged with
      kicking gas, lubricate same using fresh water. Observe and Make sure about
      well condition. Pressurise tubing annulus to max 1000 psi and check it for any
     pressure drop and any return from inside tubing.

  9.  While circulating cross well head, R/U and run wire line. Install «RN 6.250"»
     two way back pressure valve into 10"x9-5/8" neck x7" VAM "OMV" tubing
     hanger and test to 4500psi pressure.
     Note: If B.P.V failed against pressure test or could not be set in tubing
     hanger,  it is recommended to install plug into 5.963" dummy of 3SV
     landing nipple prior to L/D well head.

10. While circulating cross tubing head spool, L/D well head to top of 13-5/8"x11" -
   5000 OMV tubing head spool. Check top side threads of the tubing hanger and
   confirm  if damaged. N/U 13-5/8" BOP's stack; test same to 4500psi against
   two-way. Retrieve 6.250" RN two way back pressure valve from tubing hanger
   and 4.560" XN plug from extra long seal bore receptacle.

   Note: Pre plan to provide 11"x13-5/8"-5000 adaptor flange to connect
   BOP's to tubing head spool.

11. While circulating cross tubing head spool, if regret to make up landing joint due
    to failures on the tubing hanger top connection threads, make up and run
    suitable spear (to catch 7" 29#). Set the spear @ +/-3 meters below tubing
   hanger. Unscrew the tie down screws.

12. Sting out extra long seal bore receptacle Baker "HR" and circulate fresh water
    to clean hole. Check hole for any loss/flow. Fresh water imposes 398 psi over
    reservoir pressure @ 1936m, packer depth.

13. While circulating cross tubing head spool, pull out completion string; check all
   components for any damage to be fixed or replaced by new one.

     • According to workover request most probable leakage area is in +/-1920m

14. RIH with appropriate tool and retrieve the mandrel of retrievable seal bore.
   UN-J anchor seal unit from "DAB" packer.

   Note: Decision might be made to change tubing head spool in this phase.
    In such a case a complementary program will be issued at due time.

15. RIH with 8-1/2" E-mill and 9-5/8" scraper, clean 9-5/8" casing to top of packer
  @+/ -1936m.

   Note:  If primary investigations dictates to check casing for possible
   leakage, RIH with 9-5/8" RTTS, set same @ 1916m (some 20 m above
   packer). Test packer to 2000 psi, then test inside 9-5/8" casing to max
   2000 psi pressure for any leakage. Meanwhile monitor 9-5/8"x13-3/8"
   annulus.
   Amendment to repair leak point will be issued in due time.

16. Plan to recover Baker "A" retrievable "DAB" packer with appropriate retrieving
    tools and pull out the same.

17.  If could not retrieve the packer RIH with 8 3/8" junk mill and mill out the packer
       till it falls down. RIH with taper tap, catch and engage the remaining of packer.
  POOH and L/D fish.

18. M/U 5  7/8" Econo  mill and RIH  to bottom  at 2685m and confirm TD.
   Commence circulation and condition hole. POOH to surface.

19. RIH with 8 1/2" bit and 9 5/8" scraper to the liner lap and scrap the new packer
    setting depth @+/- 1907m ( at least two times).

20. Run completion string as per program. (A complementary program  will be
   issued in due time).

21. Pressure test tubing annulus to 2000psi and inside tubing to 4500psi.

22. If all pressure tests are OK, set two-way check valve in tubing hanger and test
   same to 4500psi.

23. Nipple down BOP's and nipple up well head and test same to 4500 psi.

24. Remove two way check valve. R/U wireline equipment, test same to 4500psi.
   Remove all the plugs and retrievable ones from the completion string.

25. Open SSD and displace annulus by completion  fluid (gas  oil + corrosion
    inhibitor) and inside tubing by gas oil. Close SSD.

26. Pressure test annulus to 2000psi/15min and inside TBG to 4500psi/15min.

27. Retrieve plug from top NoGo. Flow well and record all flow parameters.

28. Secure well, L/D pipes, clean cellar and release the rig.
