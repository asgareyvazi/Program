Objectives:Condition Trip


   1- Cont. wash and ream to 1150 mt by 350 GPM and 60-70 RPM and pump 20 bbl HI-
     VIS pill per each stand.
   2- After reach to 1150 mt circ by sea water till BTMS UP circ 50 bbl HI-VIS pill to
      clean hole spot kill mud as per hole volume .
   3- POOH to surface.
   4- RIH w/ cementing assembly include 6-1/8” cut bit + bit sub (float valve in)+10
      stand 3-1/2” DP follow by 5” DP & 5-1/2” DP to 1050 mt (depend on to find hole
     by ROV).
   5- Line up cement line & test to 3000 psi .
   6- Mix & pump cement “G” type as per SLB cementer plan.
   7- POOH to 750 mt flush pipe wait on cement to get hard.
