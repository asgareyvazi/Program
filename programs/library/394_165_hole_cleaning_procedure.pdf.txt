The following procedure shall be used to clean a moderate to high angle hole after
reaching total depth. This procedure minimizes hole washout by staging down pipe RPM
in relation to the bottoms up cuttings reaching the vertical component of the cased well
bore. Once the cuttings have been brought above the wellhead, down hole flow rate is
reduced to minimum while all remaining resources are put on the boost line.

    1.  After the well is drilled to TD, pump a 100 bbl Hi-Vis sweep and begin
        circulating bottoms up with maximum rotation and flow rate. During this phase
       the bit is to be left on bottom (No pipe reciprocation).
          •  Mudloggers: Monitor mud loss or gain
          • MWD: Establish starting ESD, ECD, from PWD readings and
              monitor/record same.
          •  Mud Engineers: Develop trip plan for upcoming short trip
          •  Perform Engineers: Have torque/drag model made and available for short
                  trip.
    2.  Continue circulating at max RPM and flow rate until 2 – 2 ½ open hole volumes
       are up in the casing above the high angle interval (less than 30 degrees).
          •  Mudloggers: Monitor mud loss or gain
          • MWD: Determine change in ESD, ECD, from PWD readings.
          •  Mud Engineers: Discuss weighting up to counter downward shift in ESD.
    3. Slow rotation to 10-15 RPM and maintain maximum flow rates. Reciprocate the
       pipe until the second open hole volume is above the BOP’s.
          •  Mudloggers: Monitor mud loss or gain
          • MWD: Determine change in ESD, ECD, from PWD readings.
          •  Mud Engineers: Discuss weighting up to counter downward shift in ESD.
          •  Rig Floor: Keep pipe moving. Prepare to line up another pump on the
               boost.
    4. Slow GPM to a minimum and put all remaining resources on the boost line.
    5. Once shakers have cleared up, pull back to the shoe.  If drag is encountered, screw
        in with the top drive and slack-off ~ 30-50 feet before staging up on the pumps.
       Pull back to where the drag was encountered, if there is no indication of drag than
        there are still cuttings beds.  If there is still drag, back-ream and work through the
        tight spot.
          •  Perform Engineers: Plot actual drag verses predicted and communicate
              hole cleaning effectiveness for each stand.
          •  Mud Engineers: Communicate calculated trip speed to the rig floor to
              prevent swabbing.
          •  Rig Floor: Maintain trip sheet and confirm hole is taking proper fill.
    6.  Trip back to bottom. Check for fill, and if present slowly wash to bottom. Repeat
       procedure from step 1 and POOH.
