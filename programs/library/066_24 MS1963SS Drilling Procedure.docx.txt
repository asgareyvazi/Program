Running in the Hole
Washing to bottom by rotating at approximately 60 RPM, 700 GPM and a maximum of 3klbs WOB;
When reaming near bottom (2-3m), reduce RPM to 30RPM and slowly tag bottom, after tagging bottom, pick up about 0.3-0.5m off bottom and flow at full GPM for 10 minutes;
Drilling
Break in bit. Rotate at approximately 60 RPM and lower to bottom, increase the WOB to 3-5Klbs. Continue to drill at 3-5Klbs for 1-1.5m. Then increase the WOB to 7-8tons (15-18Klbs), RPM to 130 RPM to drill ahead.
The formation is not hard; however, much attention should be paid to the bit balling because the marl is soft and sticky, so the low WOB (15-18Klbs) and high RPM (130 RPM) is proposed.
Pick up off bottom and use maximum flow rate and RPM to circulate when drilling about 10m interval. This can prevent the bit from balling.
Pay attention to the torque fluctuation, downhole vibration, and adjust the parameters accordingly.
Adjust mud properties to inhibit the marls from balling-up if necessary.
While drilling if there is a sudden drop in ROP, reduce the RPM. Consider the following:
Fluctuating torque or decreasing torque could mean hard stringers. Be patient and keep a low RPM while gradually increasing WOB to the planned level;
If SPP increases and the torque drop very low, the bit might be balled up.  Pick up off bottom and use maximum flow rate & high RPM to circulate and spin;
If the ROP increases after a hard section, gradually increase the RPM to the planned max RPM.
