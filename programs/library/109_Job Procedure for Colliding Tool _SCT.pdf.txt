     Job Procedure for
Severing Colliding Tool (SCT)

                              1

────────────────────────────────────────────
────────────────────────────────────────────
                   - Safety Documents: Hazard Analysis & Risk Controls
WSI operation-related safety documents for the upcoming operation include below HARCs
(Hazard Analysis & Risk Controls). These documents will be submitted to Rig HSE Officer
and Tool pusher before the operation to get an approved and proper work permit on the
wellsite.
    -  Wireline Rigup.
    -  Safety for arming Explosive devices.
    -   Operation Recommendations.
Operation Sequence on Wireline Conveyance method is as follows:
-Safety meeting with Wireline and rig crew.
-Rig up Wireline Sheaves.
-Pass cable and logging head through Sheaves.
-Rig up tools string and hot check.
-Safety meeting for arming Procedures.
-Arming and Connect the Colliding Tools after confirm the Type with Client.

-RIH to free point.
-A cut must always be attempted at or above the free Point.
-Correlate the depth and log up for shooting depth.

                                         2

────────────────────────────────────────────
────────────────────────────────────────────
-It is recommended to always put the drilling string under maximum safe tension and with
maximum safe right hand torque.
-Before attempting a Cut make sure the rig floor is Clear from Personnel stress this point
during the Pre-Job safety meeting.
-Cut and start POOH .
-Explosive Safety Procedure will be follow when the Tool at surface.
-Lay down the Colliding tools after Confirm the tools has been detonated.
-Logging head break down.
-Lay down Sheaves.
-Ask the driller to work on the drill pipe and confirm if the drill pipe gets free.
-Rig down complete if the drill pipe get free if not wait next client instructions.
