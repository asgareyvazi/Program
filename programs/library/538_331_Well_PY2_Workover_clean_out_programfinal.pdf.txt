1.   Introduction


1.1  Program Introduction

This program covers workover of well No.2, of East Paydar oil field. The well is oil
producer from Sarvak formation.

Problem
The production rate of well PY2 is low and the water cut is high. Several stimulation jobs
were done by Coil tubing but no considerable improve has been experienced after these
attempts. The final well shut in pressure recorded as 1000 psi.

Main Objective
The main objective of this program is to clean the open hole and investigate and plug
back the source of water which influences the oil production in this well.( If regret to
achieve this goal, an amendment to this program will be issued to drill a re entry well
to Sarvak formation, not too far from the existing well and resume oil production from
the same formation).

Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of
workover request no. 8/7 13- 3/ 24 , dated: 138/6 06/ 31 West oil & gas production Co.

The main head lines of this program are as follow:
1.    Prior to spud the rig, check wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record well head
      pressures; investigate for communication of all the annuluses.
2.   Move and rig up the drilling rig.
3.   Connect and pressure test kill, flow and return lines.
4.    Kill and secure the well.(Installing plug in NOGO and Displace mud via SSD)
5.   Test all the p-seals of tubing and casing spools and fix the problems at site.
6.   N/D well head and N/U BOP's
7.    Pull out completion string
8.   Mill out packer and retrieve same.
9.   Clean out open hole to TD with bi centre bit.
10.  Perform formation evaluation tests. Investigate and plug back water zone.
11.  Run completion string
12.  N/D BOP's and N/U well head
13.  Flow the well, monitor and record flow parameters.
14.  Secure the well and release rig

Attentions:

•   The detailed program is illustrated here after in this document under the
    head of "Workover Program".

Perforations

1.4   Production history:

Well was tested right after releasing the rig at Esfand 1384 and stimulated by acid job
in Farvardin 1385. One month later because of high water cut, PSP test was carried
out by Schlumberger to locate the source of water, but couldn't reach TD due to
obstruction in depth 3765m. According to PSP results in the only 20m accessible
interval below the 5" liner shoe, well was producing oil from the top 5m of this zone
(in the base of Ilam), and the bottom 15m interval comprised water.
After running, down hole memory gage in Azar 1385, the water level in the bore hole
was detected at 3352m (above 5" liner lap). In Azar 1385 and Shahrivar 1387, two
work over jobs were done on this well by coiled tubing unit. In these operations well
was cleaned out to the depths near TD and after one month acid stimulation jobs were
done to improve the well production rate. To stop water, well was plugged back by
cement from 3828 to 3830m ( only 2 meters) in the last work over.

   1.6   Reservoir Data
   Upon running 7" liner, 6 1/8" hole was drilled with 102-104 pcf OBM to TD @
   3992m.Drill stem test tools were run in hole and 7" packer was set in 3630m to
    evaluate Ilam and Sarvak. A total of 60 bbls of oil and gas was diverted to the burning
    line. According  to DST  results, well was considered  to have the  potential of
   producing oil, gas and water. Down hole sample was composed of 56 pcf-65 pcf oil
   and water. Final closed in pressure after 72 hours and at the depth 3637m was
   recorded as 6720 psi.
   Another DST was carried out in this well after running 5" liner at 3398m-3765m
    (Sarvak formation and Base of Ilam). Here the packer set in 7" liner at depth 3370m,
   28m above the 5" liner lap. Well flowed by strong bubble show, and shifted to 8-13
    bbls/hr pure 59 pcf oil with gradual increase of water cut.
   Well was static with 72-73 pcf mud in hole all the way through performing the
    last DST, and while running completion string.

2.   Workover Program

1. Move workover rig from Kangan-25 to the well site of PY2, R/U and make it
   ready for operation, Record rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea
    level. (An overall check on well head spools, valves, P-Seals and x-mass tree is
    strongly recommended before rig move. After killing and securing the well, all
   items must be checked and serviced by well head crew and repaired or replaced if
    needed.)

2.  Connect kill & flow lines to 7-1/16"-5000 x-mass tree side outlets and connect
    return line to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 4500 psi
    pressure.

3.  Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 5" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x 13-
    3/8" annulus and 13-3/8" x 18 5/8" annulus. Release / Bleed off casing –casing
    pressures, monitor their build up and investigate for the possible communications.
  Do not bleed off tubing pressure.
   Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
   while investigating the communications.

4.  Test control line to 4500 psi. Operate 3SV. Bleed the hydraulic pressure and set
   3SV in close position. Rig up W/L and test to 4500 psi. Retrieve 3SV. Pump oil
    into the control line and install Dummy in the landing nipple of safety valve @
   81.9m and test to 4500 psi..

5. Run gage ring to get sure of free pass of «RPT 3.00"» plug. Install plug « RPT
   3.00"»in no go nipple @2860m. (Under "MHR" packer @ ±2845.78 m) .Test
   plug and tubings to 4500 psi.

6. Run shifting tool and open Otis "RPD" SSD at 2831m. Start killing the well by
   pumping 95 pcf mud in the tubing and receiving return from the annulus through
    the choke to the burning pit.  (Volume of hole including tubing capacity and
    annular volume between tubing and casing is almost 630 bbls).
   Note: Monitor the pressures of annuluses all the time.

7.  While Circulating cross well head, install 4 3/8" two way back pressure valve into
   11" x9 5/8"x5" BDS (Box up and down) "KAVERNER" tubing hanger and test to
   4500psi pressure.
   Note: If B.P.V failed against pressure test or could not be set in tubing
   hanger, it is recommended to install 3.385" plug into dummy of 3SV landing
   nipple prior to L/D well head.

8.  While circulating cross tubing head spool, L/D well head. Check top side threads
    of the tubing hanger for possible damage. N/U 13-5/8" x 5000 BOP's stack; test
   same to 4500psi against two-way. Retrieve 4 3/8" two way back pressure from
    tubing hanger.

   Note: Pre plan to provide 11"x13-5/8"- 5000 adaptor flange to connect BOP's

9.  While circulating cross tubing head spool, make up the landing joint on the top
   connection of tubing hanger and test tubing to 4000 psi. Unscrew the tie down
    screws.

10. Pull and un-latch the tubings out of the packer as per procedure. Circulate 95 pcf
   mud to uniform in and out. Check hole for any loss/flow.


11. While  circulating  cross  well  head,  pull  out  completion  string;  check  all
   components for any damage to be fixed or replaced by new one.

12. RIH with 8 3/8" J-mill to 2787m (7" liner lap depth) and check easy pass through
   2215m. CBU and POH.

13. RIH with 6 1/8" J-mill, displace hole volume with oil base mud and mill the
   packer @ 2845.78m. Push the packer to top of 5" liner lap at 3398m.

14. Run proper size of taper tap and retrieve tail of packer. Check all components for
   any damage to be fixed or replaced by new one.

15. RIH w/ 4 1/8" mill and clean out BHA, tag and record TD.

16. RIH w/ 4 1/8"-6" bi centre bit to TD and clean intervals of formation suspected to
   be contaminated with cement or magneset.

17. Observe and make sure well is static. Pull out and lay down bit.

18. Perform reservoir evaluation  tests, and/or appropriate production tests as per
   program. Flow the well and run PLT to find out the water zone. (Formation might
   be perforated prior to performing the tests).

19. Plug back and confirm TD.

20. Run 7" scraper and scrape 7" completion packer depth and displace well with non
    solid 72 pcf mud.

21. Run completion string and set packer, pressure test tubing and annulus (the
   completion program will be issued in due time.

22. N/D BOP and Install X mass tree and test same to 4500psi.

23. Remove plug and prong and flow the well and record flowing parameters.
24. Secure the well and release the rig.

4.  Mud program
102-104 pcf oil base mud has been used to drill 6 1/8" section in this well which was
exposed to Gurpi, Ilam, and Sarvak formations. During DST, drill out and completion
operation, well was displaced with lighter fluids as low as 72 pcf water base mud.
Any how regarding the last information gained through the results of DST, the
following mud weight  is primarily required for  kill/ secure operation. The mud
properties may be modified based on what would dictated by the well.
