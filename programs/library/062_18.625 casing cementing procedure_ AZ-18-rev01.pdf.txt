                                  Work Instruction
                       185/8" Casing Cementing

      Well: Azar-18

       Rig: O3

        Objective:

        To cement 18 5/8” casing (TOC to be at surface)

        Status:

        18 5/8" casing run to ± 2076 m MD;
        26" shoe @ 156 m;
       Mud weight: 85 pcf.

   HSE Focus points:
     We always have time to  rig up and operate safely:  Take “time-out”  if
           necessary
     Plan the job ahead, don’t run afterwards
     Make sure all areas involved in casing transport and handling is being
           secured to avoid unauthorized entry into areas.
     Focus on falling objects
                  Avoid loose items on rig-floor and all decks below
                   All equipment used above 2m has to be secured with wire
     No work at two levels without a barrier between
                  Important with work orders signed by driller and all area responsible
                  Use barrier tape
                  Keep people away
                  Radio communication
                  Respond back on communication
     Hold PJSM and make sure all personnel involved are aware of safety
            issues regarding to run casing.

Preparation prior to 18 5/8" casing cementing:

       Equipment/ procedure                                               Verified
        Justify everyone that HSE issues are superior to any other issues
          and hold PJSM before each job.
        Ensure all personnel involved in the job have proper PPE.
        Personnel on the rig floor should use chin strap to secure the
           helmet and prevent it from falling.
         All falling and loose objects should be secured on the rig floor.
        Take samples from dry cement and mix water prior to the cement
            job.
        Calculations of cement quantities and volumes should be checked
           by both the WSS and the cementing operator. Compare job
            duration with thickening and compressive strength development
            times. Check design temperature.
        In winter time, check that everything is in place to comply with
           the cold weather procedure, with particular attention to the air
           humidity for the cement displacement from the silos.
        Ensure a cement program for a top up job is available on the rig.
        The cement mix  fluids for the lead and  tail  slurries  will be
           prepared in two separate tanks.
        Keep in mind that the slurries have thixotropic properties (high
            YP’s) so it is important to keep pumping, even if only at a low
             rate. Once pumping has stopped it will be difficult to get the
              slurries to move again.
        Check seals on cement head in good condition and have backup
             available.
        Prepare to pump mud or water w/casing fill-up hose at rotary
             table.
        Supply pressure for bulk cement set to 40 psi.

        Volume and material calculations and inventory checked.

        Sampling and confirmation testing carried out.

        Cement truck, lines and rig pump operational checked.

        Review Contingency  plans  for  losses and packing  off  during
           cementing, etc.

        Leak test up to Lo-Torque valve on cement hose to 4000 psi.

        Conduct pre-job safety meeting with all involved personnel prior
            to job start-up.
        Solution tanks are ready and cleaned for cement job.

        Have a T-Line on cement head.
        While draining cement  (if cement sent from yard  is in sacks)
