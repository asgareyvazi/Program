         All the drill pipes must have a minimum drift of 2.4 in.(Rig should provide 2.4” Rabbit )

      Not using of plastic coated drill pipe while TLC.

      Accurate pipe tally is necessary for depth control; we will require the tally before the
logging.

      Upper sheave wheel must be rigged up with the Schlumberger fishing sling on a derrick
crossbeam; before rigging up the tools.

     Make up all required pipe needed for the job before Schlumberger rig-up.

     No jars and drill collars to be used.

       RIH and POOH speed is 3.5 minutes per stand, slip to slip. Elevator/Rotary must be locked.
Use minimum dope, and only dope Pin ends while RIH. String weight should be monitored by driller
while operation and should not exceed 1500lb more than normal weight.

       Pipe must not be rotated. (Tighten pipes using both tongues)

       Ensure pumps can supply enough pressure (500 PSI) for a proper latch. (Max 600 GPM)

     Make sure the X-Over are having proper ID and OD (If it is above CSES) and no internal
shoulder

        Circulate (with Screen/strainer) every 10 stands for 15 minutes after observing returns (max
600 GPM). Inform SLB of any kicks in pressure. Do not stop before getting returns.

       Circulate 2 string-volumes once you reach latch point.

       Proper pressure/flow rate must be maintained as directed by TLC specialist.

        String needs to be filled after latching and during logging to avoid possible unlatching.

       Set pipe gently inside slips to avoid tool damage. Maximum compression of Schlumberger
tools is –1500 lbs.

       Anything dropping into the pipe (rags / rabbit / rope of rabbit / etc.) will result in complete
P.O.O.H and may damage the DWCH the down hole latch.

       Latch depth will be confirmed before job starting, we will stop when the bottom of the tool is
at this point to install the CSES
