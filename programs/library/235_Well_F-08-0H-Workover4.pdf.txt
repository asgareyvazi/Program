9. Work Over Operation Procedure:

1. Move from F-09 platform to F-08 and centre on well F8-00H.

2.  Record & report RKB to MSL and THF of new well.

3. Hook up kill and flow lines to Ann & TBG. P/T same.

4.  Record wellhead pressures (If any, bleed off pressure).

5.  Bullhead well with 50 bbls Hi-vis pill followed by 72 pcf SW into tubing and
    anuulus.



Note:
     72 PCF brine will impose ±200 psi over pressure to the Arab/Hith/Manifa
    & Reservoirs @ top of perforations (2787.7mMD / 2746.4mMD).

     Pump Hi Vis pill ahead of 72 PCF brine in order to sweep and squeeze
      remained oil and gas in to formation.

     Adjust MW with respect to the hole condition. It is allowed to increase
   MW up to 90 pcf by the aid of Lime Stone Powdre.

     Circulate across well head to fill the well during operation.



6.  Release pressure from control line & close 3SV.

7.  Install BPV inside Hanger (Tubing  Hanger, Type "MSA" , 11" x 4 1/2" VAM
  TOP BOX With 4" BPV).

8.  Disconnect kill line from X-mass tree. Do not disconnect kill line from Ann until
    the BOP stack is installed.

9. N/D X-mass tree. N/U BOP stack, P/T same.

10. Remove BPV from tubing hanger.

11. Make up suitable 4-1/2” Vam Top landing joint and screw in tubing hanger, close
   pipe rams, apply pressure to make sure landing joint is completely screwed in
   tubing hanger, loose tie down screws, Pressure test glands to 1500psi.
