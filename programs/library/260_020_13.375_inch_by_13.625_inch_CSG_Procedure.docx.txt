
13-3/8” X 13-5/8” CSG RUNNING PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
POOH
L/D BHA below Reamer
Anadril check for plugged nozzles.
Weatherford measure O.D. of CSG jewelry.
Clean pit & mix spacer.
Build slug for CSG.
P/U wash tool & WBRRT  & TIH, pull Wear bushing/use 24.7# DP (WASH PROFILE).
Move CSG equipment & jewelry to rig floor.
Drop rabbit on trip out of hole W/wear bushing.
Get Weatherford hydraulic unit out for back up.
R/U CSG tools & P/U CSG.
P/U Mud master shoe (Length 48’), Cent. JT CSG, Cent. JT CSG, FC, Cent. JT CSG, Cent.
Baker lock to top of FC.
RIH as per CSG Tally: 51-Jts 13-3/8” total not including Mud Master. (22-JTs left out)
Cross over. 13-3/8” X 13-5/8”.
99-JTs of 13-5/8” CSG (9-JTs left out)
FMC Hanger Assembly.
3-STD of DP.
Allamon Tool.
59-STDs of DP.
CMT STD.
CSG is Auto Fill, confirm pipe is filling.
Mud Engineer to document mud losses (Running & Cementing CSG)
TIH w/CSG @ +  3-min/std. Slack off and adjust speed accordingly. (WELL CONTROL ALLAMON)
Drop Allamon ball with 13-3/8” CSG @ open hole. (STD #45 DP)
*Get slow pump rate before dropping ball.
*Easy on and off slips.
Boost through lower most kill line.
Land CSG hanger in well head:
Close Allamon W/2400-psi.
Close FC W/5-7 BPM flow rate.
R/U Halliburton CMT lines:
Hold PJSM
Test Lines to 5000-psi.
Pump 75-bbl spacer, 600-sx CMT + 10-bbl spacer, turn over to rig.
97 BBL/789 stks Dart #2 @ Allamon (Slow to 4-BPM)
104 BBL/841 stks Dart #2 latch up (Zero stroke counter)
6467 stks plug #1 @ FC-Pump 10-12 BPM
7385 stks plug #2 @ FC.
7467 stks includes ½ shoe track volume.
Bump plug with 500-1000-psi.
Set Seal Assembly:
30K DN, Rotate 4-turns to the right.
Drill string will drop 8”.
Break circulation with Halliburton.
Close Pipe rams.
Pressure up to 7000-psi for 2-minutes.
Bleed the pressure off then go to test pressure.
Open rams and go to straight pull on DP.
