                               Drilling Fluid Program

1. Objectives

     This program has been designed by Pars Drilling Fluids Co. (PDF) for the

      provision of drilling fluids engineering and operational services to drill

     Asmari Oil Producer Well in Mansour Abad Oil Field, operated by PEDC.

     This program  is based on the provided information by PEDC Drilling

     Department for the normal operation.

     In case of having contingency or emergency situations variation from the

     program is required based on the hole conditions and dictation to obtain

     optimum  drilling  fluid  parameters  until  returning  to  the  normal

      condition.

     This program has been design based on 50% / 50% Dewatered and Drill

     Water. In case of using 100% Dewatered chemicals, deviation from the

     program should be considered and will be justified to be economical.

2. Engineering Guidelines

    Mud Weight          Maintain mud density at recommended weight with dilution and efficient
                          use of the solid control equipment. Increases in mud weight to be
                           achieved by the addition of salts for all intervals, BARA-WATE (barite)
                          (and HEMA-WATE in case of more weight requirement) for the first
                               intervals and CARBO-WATE F (Calcium Carbonate) should be used for
                           the last two intervals to avoid formation damage, unless an emergency
                               situation arises.

      Plastic Viscosity        Plastic Viscosity should be maintained as low as possible by minimizing
                                       drill  solids and maximizing use  of  solids  control equipment, and
                            maintaining dilution rate.

      Yield Point          The Yield Point should be maintained in the programmed ranges once
                           the mud has sheared through the bit. The hole should be monitored for
                              signs of poor hole cleaning, quantity and quality of the cuttings on the
                             shaker, especially after  trips. Additions of BIO-XCD (Xanthan Gum)
                          and/or PAC UHV should be used to maintain or increase rheology.

      Gels / 6 RPM          Gels can be increased if necessary by additions of BIO-XCD (Xanthan
                        Gum).

       Filtration            The API/HPHT filtrate should be maintained in the programmed ranges
                         by using PAC LV-TG and AMYLOSE LV.

    pH               pH should be maintained between 9.5 and 10.5 with CAUSTIC SODA and
                           LIME, depending on hole section.

     Calcium              Maintained, preferably at less than 400mg/l by addition of SODA ASH
                                in all  sections.

      KCl                              If cuttings quality and/or hole conditions indicate, the concentration of
                            KCl R-UPG will be increased.

      Chlorides            The chloride content will be kept in the programmed range.

      M.B.T.              To be maintained  well below 35 Kg/m3  (10  lb/bbl)  (equivalent
                            Bentonite) by maintaining a satisfactory dilution rate and an accurate
                          usage of the solids control equipment. It is very important to determine
                   MBT of fresh mud and monitor MBT increasing during drilling.
      Centrifuges          The centrifuges should be run judicially to control mud weight and
                            buildup of low gravity solids.

      Shakers            shaker screen’s inventory should be reported at the end of each week.

       3.1.2. Interval Key Issues (Potential Risks and Mitigations)

           Loss Zone: There is a possibility of complete loss in this interval with No Return mud,

           so LCM pill should be prepared before drilling of this section as contingency.

          Hole Cleaning: Hole cleaning problems could be avoided by proper use of viscosifiers,

              e.g. BENTO-GEL API.

       3.1.3. Engineering Outline & System Maintenance

       The Plastic Viscosity value should be minimized by optimum usage of the solids control

     equipment and by dilution with pre-mix, where applicable, to generate new volume and / or

     reduce mud rheology.

       Yield point should be kept high (the program range) to reduce the annular flow rates

      required and gel value are required to reach to maximum hole cleaning.

       It is essential to remove drilled solids rather than dilute with water to acceptable levels.

      Therefore every effort must be made to maximize the efficiency of the solids control

     equipment. Where possible all centrifuges should be used to process the active mud and

      Desilter discharge.

          3.2.1.        Drilling Operation Overview

   To solve problems associated with this interval and increase the ROP, CLAY-BOND FLUID SYSTEM has

   been designed to drill the formation and inhibit clay and marl problems in this section.

   CLAY-BOND L has also been successfully used to drill clay interbeds in both saline and non-saline

   systems. It is very important to determine MBT of mud during drilling and keep it in the program

   range. Controlling of MW could be done by running SCE and dressing the shakers with finest mesh

   screens, start with 65 pcf MW and increase using salts till 75 pcf (This can be achieved by adding

   saturated salt water to system) and BARA-WATE API for >75 pcf.

   Note: Due to the importance of drilling this section, before each connection and continuing drilling

   wash and ream should be done completely.

       3.2.3. Drilling Fluid Formulation

After cementing 20" casing,  it  is required to prepare the new mud system “CLAY-BOND FLUID

SYSTEM " for 17 1/2" Hole section and displace the hole before drilling the new section.

Mix every batch based on the following formulation:

         Mixing should be done with waste water, inform company man if waste water condition

                 is not good.
           If ROP is low, Pump Salt Water. In addition, pump 50 bbl Salt Water (69-70 pcf) every

           stand by company man order (Optional).

          /PAC UHV (Hi-Vis Pill viscosity should be 65 sec/qt.)

      M100 from the suction tank

Note: To adjust pH in this interval (Gachsaran formation) use LIME instead of CAUSTIC SODA.

      3.2.5. Interval Key Issues (Potential Risks and Mitigations)

          Loss Zone: There is a possibility of complete loss in this interval with No Return mud, so

        LCM pill should be prepared before drilling this section as contingency.

          Bit Balling: Due to the clay and marl characteristic of the formation, bit balling is one of

           the main problems. It is required to use KCL R-UPG and CLAY-BOND L according to the

          above mentioned to provide cutting encapsulation during drilling.

          Clay & Marl Inhibition: Tight hole might be experienced in this interval due to existence

            of clay & marl in the formation. Good inhibition will be helpful to provide stable hole

            condition.

          Salt Water Flow: There is a possibility of salt water flow. Therefore close monitoring is

           required to maintain Ca++ and pH level.

     3.3.2. Interval Key Issues

         Salt Water Flow: Gachsaran formation has the potential of salt water flow. The hardness

          of this water is between 35000-53000 mg/l, so any increase in the hardness of mud

        would be an indication of possible salt water flow. Therefore, during drilling this section,

         hardness of the system must be checked and monitored three times per day for any

          increase in Ca++ ions.

          Losses: In the upper part of Gachsaran a weak zone has been experienced. Layers

         interbedded in the salts in Gachsaran Members 2 & 3 can have normal pressure, and

          therefore a reduced fracture gradient compared to the salt layers. Heavy losses might be

         experienced in Gachsaran. Losses have also been seen in the first shale layer of Cap Rock,

             its second layer from the top, so  it  is very important not to  drill deeper than

         approximately one meter into this Member.

        Stuck Pipe: Tight hole / stuck pipe in the 12 1/4" hole section is typically isolated to the

          Shales/Clay stones and salt beds. The stuck in Shales and clay stones as far as the MW is

         adjusted correctly and FL is tight and the salinity is saturation is not a matter of concern.

   3.3.3. Engineering Outline & System Maintenance

Mud Weight

 In the initial mixing, salt would be the first weighting agent to Saturation level, then BARA

 WATE I and HEMA WATE I will be used as per above instruction to reach to 105-125 pcf as hole

 conditions dictate. Controlling the mud weight meanwhile drilling would be done by running all

 SCE and dump and dilution. Pore pressure is expected to be marginally above the normal

 pressure in the Jurassic / Upper Triassic.

 Mud weight selection is critical in this interval. In some offset wells, excessive losses may occur

 in the Gachsaran member 3 when the mud weight is higher than the optimum. On the other

 hand, in some offset wells, water flow could be encountered when the mud weight is allowed

 to fall down below optimum MW.

  It is important to drill this section with the mud weight being kept as per the guide line, also by

 running all solids control equipment and dressing the shakers with finest mesh screens; it is

 possible to minimize the ECD and the potential for induced losses.
PH
 The viscosity of a clay suspension is lowest in the pH range of 7 to 9.5 so in this section, pH

 should be kept at the range of 9-9.5.
Shale Inhibition
 Salt would be used for shale inhibition to combat the Gachsaran Marl/Shale’s. However, the

 very tight FL would be the other shale inhibition property of the system. Controlling the salinity

 saturation is vital to drill into Gachsaran formation against troublesome Shales/Marls.
Rheological Properties

    PV is always to be kept as low as possible by reducing the drilled solids concentrations of

     mud using the proper installation of the solids control equipment so that the hydraulics is

       optimized. High gel values are required to support the high quantity of weighting agents

       while tripping, making connections and during survey times. PV should be kept in the

       range mentioned in the drilling fluid program.

    A thin salt bed in Gachsaran member 2 is a red flag for Geologist that means reaching to

      cap rock is imminent, salinity below the saturation will cause the salt samples to be

       dissolved in the system resulting in no salt sample over the shakers.

          3.4.1.2.    Interval Key Issues

 Swab possibility

Special care must be taken when the BHA is inside the previous casing. In contrary to popular belief,

pressure does travel easily in drilling fluids, so tripping speed must be controlled to avoid swabbing.

 Stuck Pipe

The combination of different size of CARBO-WATE incorporated into the mud will form a thin and

slick filter cake in borehole and prevent the risk of stuck pipe.

 Drilling Issues

When drilling through reservoir, swabbing of hydrocarbons can be experienced. Influx due to

overpressure vs. influx due to swabbing must be differentiated.

 Losses

It is recommended to drill with as low as possible mud weight through the reservoir to prevent any

losses.

 H2S & CO2 Gases

There  is possibility for existence of H2S and CO2 Gases in the reservoir zone (Asmari),  it  is

recommended to keep the pH around 9.5 to 10 and also using H2S SCAV P to prevent mud properties

from being changed.

4. Completion Fluids Design and Program

   4.1.     Clean up and Completion Program

This program describes the procedure to clean up and displace the cased hole from water based mud

brine as safely and effectively as possible prior to running the completion equipment.

Clean working practices and good housekeeping as always are important.

All rig pits, ditches, and lines (including gun lines) should be cleaned using solvents and detergents.  If it is

practical, lines should be opened to check for any mud solids that might have settled in them.

All the pits, sand traps, and under the shale shakers should be cleaned out and washed down using high

pressure cleaning equipment. In the pit room, all gratings should be cleaned, all the lights and beams

should be washed down.

All cleaning should be done well in advance wherever possible. To clean pits and lines, 15 m3 of

water containing 1 to 2 drums of CLEAN SURF should be mixed up and circulated through all lines,

gun lines, mixing lines etc. This can also be used to clean the tank system initially.

4.2.     Completion Fluids Design and Formulation

          The completion fluid for this section will be fresh water treated with a scavenger,

            inhibitor and biocide.

            Solids-Free Nacl Fluid Systems

            This fluid is recommended to make from fresh water or is solutions made with water

         and the addition of one or more electrolytes. These fluids do not contain either

          suspended particles or viscosifiers, which mean neither filtration control nor viscosity

            control, are provided.

           Corrosion Control:

        Some NaCl is corrosive and when corrosion inhibitors are required, compatibility

            studies should be performed. The corrosion problem can be solved by adjusting the

        pH of the solution to above 10.5 - 11; however, pH measurements may be less

           important than measuring and controlling the. It may be necessary to use a corrosion

            inhibitor to achieve lower corrosion rates. In some cases, an oxygen scavenger might

         be used in conjunction with a filming amine inhibitor.

             Filtration Process:

             Filtration provides clean fluids that  will prevent damage to the formation, the

            reservoir and its permeability. It does this by removal of solids and particulates from

           the  fluid that may otherwise block the open space within the rock formation

           containing the hydrocarbon fluid within the production zone.

         Any solids and contaminates in the completion fluid will reduce its effectiveness. An

             efficiently filtered and completely solid free completion fluid will add value to both

           the productivity and the dependability of the well in the long term.

          Dual pod filtration cartridge unit is recommended for filtering brines (5 microns and

         25 microns filter cartdriges) and produced water in completion, stimulation, and

          workover operations. The pods are connected by a manifold, providing staged

              filtration capability. Staged filtration utilizes cartridge filters with different ratings to

          maximize filtration efficiency. Cartridge filtration  is designed for use with fluids

           containing a low percent by volume of suspended solids. Cartridge filtration is not

          economic when fluids are contaminated with oil or other substances.

   4.4.     Displacement Procedure

To ensure effective mud displacement and water wetting of the casing, the displacement pill needs to be

designed carefully. The use of a weighted push pills with a viscosity greater than that of the mud, will

minimize channeling and ensure good cleaning of the low side of the well bore.

The best fluid for thinning the mud is the continuous phase of the mud. In addition, SPERSEEN CFL could

be used to reduce the yield point and gel strengths. The yield point should be between 8 to10 Pa and the

10 minute gel to <10 Pa. The actual quantity of material required to do this must be determined by the

pilot testing performed by the mud engineer to determine the most cost-effective treatment.

Throughout the drilling fluid conditioning process, the flow rate used should be the maximum practical

rate.

       The drill string should be reciprocated and rotated during the operation. The combined effect of

      scrapers being run in the hole, the high flow rate and the reciprocation/rotation will help to

      remove/disperse large quantities of mud solids and general debris. The reciprocation stroke

      should be +/- 10 m. and the rotation speed should be +/- 60 RPM

       During this operation, as much of the surface volume of mud as possible should be removed

     from the pits to accommodate the inhibited water and the well clean up pills. Once the mud has

     been back-loaded, surface preparation can be done.
