EQUIPMENT

   30" HYDRAULIC LATCH (or PIN CONNECTOR)

TECHNICAL TOOL DESCRIPTION

   The ABB Vetco Gray 30" Hydraulic Latch provides a means to connect the marine riser
    to the conductor wellhead housing. This is accomplished by dog segments which are
   driven radially outward by hydraulic cylinders acting on a tapered drive sleeve. The dog
   segments engage a matching profile inside the conductor housing, locking the 30" latch
    in position. O-ring seals prevent circulation between the 30" latch and conductor
   housing. The top of the 30" latch is available with an ABB Vetco Gray mandrel H-4
    profile, a clamp hub or flange connection. These connections allow mating with either
   the lower marine riser package H-4 connector or a separate flex joint. The 30" latch
   and marine riser allow drilling fluid returns to the vessel, thereby providing a means of
    control should shallow gas be encountered.

SPECIFICATIONS AND CAPACITIES

   •  Refer to drawing for dimensions.
   •  Hydraulic fluid - A 50/50 mixture of water/ethylene glycol or a waterbase control
        fluid with inhibitor.
   •  Hydraulic system normal working pressure - 1,500 psi (103 bar).
   •  Maximum Pressure - 3,000 psi (207 bar).
   •  Recommended Lubricants: Assembly - Jet Lube AP-5 or equivalent molybdenum
       disulfide bearing grease. Maintenance - Chevron Polyurea EP-2 or equivalent
      grease.

ROUTINE OPERATION

                    PRE-OPERATIONAL CHECKS

    1.   Thoroughly clean the assembly using either a steam cleaner or high pressure
        water blast. Pay particular attention to the dog segments, the main body above
        the dog drive sleeve and the area between the dog drive sleeve and stop ring.
       The effectiveness of the cleaning method will be improved if the dog drive sleeve
          is cycled while being cleaned.
    2.   Inspect the stainless steel lined seal surface at the top of the latch for damage,
            i.e.: AX, RX, or BX ring groove. If any damage is discovered, further inspection
       should be made using non-destructive testing techniques  such as
       magnaflux, dye penetrant or ultra-sonic. If these tests cannot be performed
      on the vessel, the hydraulic latch should be moved to a shore base where
        the inspection can be performed.
    3.   Apply 1,500 psi (103 bar) to the unlock function (rod retracted) and check for leaks
         at the cylinders and fittings. With the cylinders retracted, verify that the dog
       segments are free to move in. Apply 1,500 psi (103 bar) to the lock function (rod
        extended) and check for leaks at the cylinders and fittings.  If desired, the
        hydraulic system may be tested at pressures up to 3,000 psi (207 bar) at this
        time. Before commencing the test, verify all hose and fittings (if other than
     ABB Vetco Gray supplied) are suitable for 3,000 psi (207 bar) service and
      remove personnel from the test area.
    4.   Inspect the O-ring and replace if damaged. Apply a light coat of grease to the main
        body, dog drive sleeve, dog segments and the O-ring seal.


                     RUNNING PROCEDURE

    1.   Position the 30" latch and its handling/storage skid (if provided) on the spider or
       moonpool beams. Depending on the top configuration connect either the lower
       marine riser package, a flex joint, or a joint of marine riser. Ensure that the
        hydraulic latch control hoses are properly secured to the marine riser when
        running.


 CAUTION: Do not attempt to lift the 30" latch with the four padeyes located on
           topofthedrivesleeve.Thesepadeyesaretobeusedonlyasamanual
            overrideincaseofahydraulicfailure.

    2.   Pressure the "unlock" line to 500 psi (35 bar) and hold. Run and land the 30" latch
          in the conductor wellhead. Vent pressure on the "unlock" line.
    3.   Pressure the "lock" line to 1500 psi (103 bar). To ensure the 30" latch is properly
       secured  to  the  conductor  wellhead,  exert a  uniform  "overpull"  load  of
        approximately 20,000 pounds (9072 Kg) over the running string weight.


                        RECOVERING

    1.   Recover the marine riser and 30" latch by releasing the "lock" pressure and
        applying 1,500 psi (103 bar) to the "unlock" line. The effective tension at the 30"
        latch location should be reduced or neutral to aid in the release process.
    2.   Carefully pickup on the running string until certain the 30" latch is free. Recover
        the running string and 30" latch. During the recovery operation, cycling the 30"
        latch several times (lock and unlock) will free it of some entrapped sand or
           silt thereby reducing clean up time on deck.
    3.   In case of hydraulic failure, some 30" latches are equipped with padeyes located
       on top of the drive sleeve.  If the hydraulics fail, carefully bleed the hydraulic
        system. Latch on to the padeyes with cable from the deck and recover the latch
       as recommended in step 2.


                POST OPERATIONAL MAINTENANCE

    1.   Thoroughly wash the unit with fresh water.
    2.   Perform steps 1 and 2 of the Disassembly Procedure.
    3.    Lightly grease the OD of the drive sleeve and the ID of the dog segment/ stop ring
        assembly.
    4.    Install the dog segment/ stop ring assembly on the main body and make up. When
        properly made up, the stop ring is even with the bottom edge of the main body.
    3.   Apply a light coat of water resistant grease to all operating surfaces, cycle the unit
        several times, then apply grease to the exposed surfaces of the dog segments
       and install protective covers or place the unit on the storage skid.

DISASSEMBLY/ASSEMBLY PROCEDURE

                         DISASSEMBLY

    1.   The dog segment assembly is threaded to the main body with a right hand, 2-pitch
        stub acme thread. Remove the dog segment assembly by placing chain tongs just
       below the dog segments and back up tongs where possible above the hydraulic
         cylinder mounting flange.
    2.   Position the dog segment assembly so that  it  is setting upright. Using the
        appropriate size hex key, remove the socket head cap screws from the dog
         retainer ring. Remove the dog retainer ring, dog segments and dog segment
        spacer blocks. Inspect the wear ring for excessive wear and replace if required.
          Install new O-ring seals.

 NOTE:    Theupperinsidesurfaceofthedogsegmentshasa30obevel.Makecertain
            thedogsegmentsareinstalledwiththisbevelupasshownontheassembly
             drawing. Using the appropriate size hex key, securely tighten the socket
          headcapscrewsholdingthedogretainerringtothestopring.Checkeach
          dogsegmentforeaseofmovementinandout.Settheassemblyaside.


    3.   Unless the hydraulic cylinders are damaged or leaking, it will not be necessary to
       remove them.


 CAUTION:  Carefullyinspectthehydraulichosesforanydamage,eitherabrasion
          or kinking. In either case the hose should be replaced. Also inspect
           thehosefittingsandadaptersforexcessivecorrosion.Corrosioncan
             greatlyreducethepressureratingofthefittings.


    4.   Mark one of the cylinder rod ends and its mating clevis on the drive sleeve.
      Remove the pins between the cylinder rods and the drive sleeve, then slide the
         drive sleeve from the main body. Clean the OD of the main body and the ID of the
         drive sleeve. Inspect the tapered bottom edge of the drive sleeve for damage.

                         ASSEMBLY

    1.    Lightly grease the OD of the main body. Install a new O-ring on the ID of the dog
         drive sleeve. Pack the annular recess on the ID of the dog drive sleeve with grease.
    2.   Slide the drive sleeve on the main body using the previous marks for correct
        alignment of the four cylinders to the pad eyes on the drive sleeve.
    3.    Install the pins between the rod ends and the pad eyes on the drive sleeve.
    4.    Lightly grease the OD of the drive sleeve and the ID of the dog segment/stop ring
        assembly.
    5.    Install the dog segment/stop ring assembly on the main body and make up. Refer
         to the drawing. When properly made up the stop ring is even with the bottom edge
         of the main body.


                       TEST PROCEDURE

    1.   Attach hydraulic hoses and cycle the unit several times to verify smooth operation.
    2.   The dog segments are not spring loaded and will not move back when the drive
        sleeve is in the retracted (unlock) position; they must be forced back by hand.
