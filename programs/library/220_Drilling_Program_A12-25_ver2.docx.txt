# Introduction

## Program Introduction
This program covers the drilling of well# 25 from a new slot on the A-12 platform in Aboozar field, Persian Gulf, Iran. The well is intended to produce oil from Ghar Sand reservoir by drilling an extended leg of about 834.3m and completing the well by running proper size of pre-pack screen. The objective of A-12-25H is to improve the oil production rate from the Aboozar field. The 13 3/8” casing will be set at 410m MD and the 12 ¼” hole will kick off from 480m to drill directionally towards N66.1E to the depth of 1062.3m MD (-845m TVDss) where the 9 5/8” casing will be set at the landing point in the Ghar reservoir.
The 8 1/2” hole section is going to be drilled horizontally through the Ghar Sand Mbr. For about 835m to total depth of 1897m MD (-845m TVDss).
Note: Due to unavailability of 4 ½” screen at the time which normally was run through 29lbs 7” liner, and necessity of drilling new wells, it has been decided to change the regular casing setting design in the field and set the 9 5/8” casing at the landing point in the Ghar reservoir instead of setting into the Asmari Caprock which was a normal practice.

## Aboozar Structure
The Aboozar Field was previously known as the Ardeshir Field is located some 74Km west of Kharg Island in the Persian Gulf. The field was discovered by drilling the A-2 well in 1968. The first exploratory well was drilled in 1961 which indicated the presence of Hydrocarbon in the Ghar/Asmari Formation. The reservoir rock consists of fractures and porous carbonates, Limestone and Dolomite in the upper and lower parts, and Sandstone in the middle of the Asmari Formation of Oligocene and Miocene age.
The reservoir structure is a NW-SE trending anticline with 1-2 degree in dip & measuring about 24 by 8 Km. The Ghar reservoir is an unconsolidated Sandstone reservoir which has average porosity of 35% with very high permeability in the range of 2-3 darcies.

# Geological Overview

## Lithology
The stratigraphic succession in A-12-25H is expected to be nearly normal from surface to total depth of 1897m MD that will be cut as follow:
1-Upper Fars Group From surface to 585.3m (-560.8m TVDss)
The Upper Fars Group mainly consists interbedding of Marl: light brown to grey colour (occasionally red brown), soft, gummy, calcareous, washable, silty & sandy, Gypsum/Anhydrite: translucent to transparent, amorphous, soft to moderately hard, Gypsum in the shallow section, Anhydrite layers is more frequent in the lower part.
Sand: fine-medium grained (occasionally coarse), unconsolidated, poorly sorted, sub rounded to rounded, translucent to transparent and Limestone: dolomitic Mudstone, light grey to yellow grey colour, occasionally chalky, soft to moderately hard, locally towards bottom.
Note: The content of sand will increase towards the base.
2- Gachsaran Formation From 585.3m (-560.8m TVDss) to 885.3m (-798.8m TVDss)
The TVD thickness of this Formation is about 238m and mainly consists interbedding of massive, white, hard Anhydrite & white, grey Marl.
3- Upper Asmari Cap Rock  From 885.3m (-798.8m TVDss) to 905m (-808.2m TVDss)
The lithology unit of this part mainly consists of about 9.5m TVD massive, white Anhydrite with some interbeded of medium hard crystalline, oil stained Dolomite.
4- Upper Asmari  From 905m (-808.2m TVDss) to 961.5m (-829.5m TVDss)
The entry depth into the Asmari reservoir will be at -808.2m TVDss and entry depth of the Ghar Cap Rock at -829.5m TVDss which will be about 11.5m TVD above the GOC. The TVD thickness of this part is about 21.3m which mainly consists interbedding of massive Anhydrite: white, hard, Dolomite: crystalline, medium hard, oil stained, porous and dolomitic Limestone: crystalline, medium hard, oil stained, porous.
5- Ghar Cap Rock From 961.5m (-829.5m TVDss) to 976.7m (-833.8m TVDss)
The TVD thickness of this unit is about 4.3m which mainly consists interbedding of massive Anhydrite: white, hard, Dolomite: crystalline, medium hard, oil stained.
6- Ghar Sand From 976.7m (-833.8m TVDss) to Total Depth of 1897m MD (-845m TVDss)
The TVD thickness of this part of Ghar Sand is about 11.2m at total depth that mainly consists interbedding of Sand: losse, fine to medium grain, well rounded, fair sorting, Shale and Dolomite. The 9 5/8” casing is going to be set at -845m TVDss about 11.2m TVD inside the Ghar reservoir. The USIT log is recommended to be run for cement quality through 9 5/8'' Casing.
About 833.8m MD of this part will be drilled horizontally from Landing point (at 1062.3m) to total depth of 1897m (869.1m TVD, -845m TVDss). This Sand layer is the main oil producer in the Ghar reservoir. Special cares should be paid in this section to stay in the Sand interval.

# 17 ½” Hole Section / 13-3/8” Casing

## General

## Formation Sampling
On each 2-4 meters one sample would be taken except close to formation tops which every 1m interval is recommended.
One dry sample for each selected interval should be stored for future references.
In addition to this general rule, geologist may decide to take more samples.

## Sequence of Operations
P/U 17-1/2" BHA and RIH, tag sea bed, Drilling 17-1/2" hole to Casing Point @ 410m, MD. Pump Hi-Vis Pill, Circulate clean hole.
For more details Ref. to "Time Breakdown"
Perform Condition trip, Spot 100 bbl hi-vis pill. Drop Totco. POOH to surface.
General Notes:
TD should be adjusted in such a way to have 1.5-2 meters of rat hole under casing shoe.
Drop Totco prior to POOH to evaluate the verticality of hole.
Enough mud materials should be present on board in case of needed.
R/U 13 3/8” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.
Run 13 3/8" casing to bottom as per casing program. M/U 13 5/8”–3000 x 13-3/8"-3000 Unihead on last joint & place it onto 20” Conductor Pipe & circulate mud.
Note:
- Do not exert much load and compressing stress on the shoe if encountered tight hole and ledges.
M/U cementing head. Load the plugs. Test the lines to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.
Welded unihead to 20" Conductor Pipe. Cement the casing as per program, L/D landing joint. Wait on cement to get hard, Wash inside of unihead. Install side outlet Valves While WOC. Use top job to fill annulus if needed.
N/U 13-5/8"-5K BOP's, Perform BOP Test. Install wear bushing into unihead.
P/U 5" DP's & 5" HWDP stand back enough in Derrick. P/U next BHA (Junk Sub Included), RIH & pressure test casing up to 1500 psi. Drill out cement & shoe & 3 m below shoe. Leak off test must conduct w/ 11 ppg equivalent pressure. Displace hole with 70pcf Salt Polymer Mud.

## Logging Program
No logging program is considered for this hole section.

## Hole Deviation
This well is assumed to be vertical. Take TOTCO surveys before each POOH and record the results.

# 12 ¼” Hole Section / 9-5/8” Casing

## General

## Formation Sampling
On each 2-4 meters one sample would be taken except close to formation tops which every 1m interval is recommended.
One dry sample for each selected interval should be stored for future references.
In addition to this general rule, geologist may decide to take more samples.

## Sequence of Operations
Continue vertical drilling from 413m to 480m (KOP). Work on junk sub and POOH to surface.
P/U Directional drilling equipment RIH. Directional Drilling from 480m,MD down to casing point @ 1062.3m, MD.
Note:
- Adjust mud weight with respect to the hole condition.
- The Section TD maybe changed based on formation tops detected by the well site geologist.
- Enough mud materials should be present on board in case of need.
At section TD, circulate, clean hole. Perform wiper trip. Check for any fill. CBU on bottom. Spot 100 bbl hi-vis pill. POOH to surface. Retrieve wear bushing & change top ram to 9 5/8”.Body test BOP to 1000 psi.
R/U 9-5/8” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.
R/U & Run 9-5/8" casing to bottom as per program, M/U 9 5/8” CSG hanger to the last joint, Hang CSG w/ placing hanger into unihead.
(Take care about CSG tally & space out for hanging casing.), Circulate to make
hole clean.
Note:
Do not exert much load and compressing stress on the shoe if encountered tight hole and ledges.
M/U cementing head. Load the plugs. Test the lines to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.
Cement casing as per program. Displace with mud. Monitor for pressures. Record and report the final pressure before bump the plug. Bump the plug with 500 psi over the final circulating pressure. Bleed off pressure. Check for back flow. If any back flow observed, hold the pressure inside for the cement to thick.
L/D landing joint. Install pack off & pressure test up to 2000 Psi after activation of pack off.
N/U 13-5/8"-5K BOP's stock mean while fill the 9-5/8" casing annulus if needed. Perform BOP Test.
P/U 5" DP's & 5" HWDP stand back enough in Derrick. P/U next BHA (Junk Sub Included), RIH, pressure test casing up to 2000 psi and drill out cement D/O shoe track & shoe.
Important Note: H2S equipment and H2S service should be ready on board at the start of Operation.
