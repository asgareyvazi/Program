TECHNICAL DESCRIPTION

   The Vetco Gray 16" Casing Hanger/Seal and
   Running Tool are designed to be used with a
   sub-mudline landing sub welded into the 20"
   casing.

Casing Hanger - Ref. P/N H50320

   The patented expanding serpentine collet landing
    ring, an integral part of the casing hanger
   assembly, is used to suspend the casing string
   weight. It is held in position on the casing hanger
   body during the running procedure by a detaining
    ring,  until the collet landing ring reaches the
   unique dual profile of the landing sub and latches
    into place. The collet landing ring is kept from
    rotating by anti-rotation keys on the OD of the
   casing hanger body, maintaining proper
   alignment of the flow-by slots.

   Reciprocation of the casing string during
   cementing operations does not affect the collet                               3220
    ring latching mechanism.

ROUTINE OPERATIONS

                   PRE-OPERATIONAL CHECKS

Casing Hanger
    1.  Check the seal area.

    2.   Inspect the OD wickers and lightly wire brush to remove all foreign debris. Deburr
        the wicker surface if required. Lubricate the OD and ID of the hanger.
    3.   Ensure serpentine collet is secure in place and free of damage.



                             NOTE:


 Utilizing the 16" Casing Hanger below the mudline will require that the mating landing sub
 be pre-installed (welded into the 20" conductor string). The location of the landing sub
 within the 20" string, relative to the top of the high pressure wellhead housing, is required
 to accurately measure in the hole when running the 16" casing hanger.


Seal Assembly
    1.   Inspect the ID wickers of the packing nut and lightly wire brush to remove all
         foreign debris. Deburr the wicker surface if required.
    2.   Inspect the seal and o-rings.

Running Tool
    1.   Inspect the o-rings on the OD of the test plug and replace if required. The test plug
       should be routinely disassembled from the running tool for inspection and
       replacement of the o-rings on the ID of the plug.
    2.   Ensure that the two (2) spring loaded anti-rotation keys will depress completely
         inside the OD of the cam ring. Disassemble and lubricate as required.
    3.   Verify that the seal retrieval ring and its expansion pins all move freely and are
         lubricated.
    4.   Verify, through the slot in the seal retrieval ring, that the anti-rotation screw (½"-13
      NC) is properly installed into the stabilizer sleeve.
    5.   Ensure that the six (6) 1"-8UNC cap screws (item 15) are torqued to 450-500 ft-lbs
        (610- 678 Nm). This locks the retainer nut to the shaft.
    6.   Hold the cam ring with a strap wrench and rotate (by hand) the tool stem left and
          right to ensure ease of rotation. Lubricate the thread area with light weight oil as
         required.
    7.   Lightly grease the entire surface area of the tool prior to installing it into the casing
        hanger.
    8.    Install the lead impression block Ref. P/N H50332-27 onto tool if desired.

    9.     If the casing hanger is to be run without a packoff (i.e. the packoff to be run later),
        then the running tool must be fitted with P/N H50332-25 (Item 20 - spacer ring).
        This ring is slipped over and secured in place with three (3) set screws (Item 16). A
       measure of 9.25" can be made from the end of the spacer ring to the top of the lock

                    RUNNING PROCEDURE


                            NOTE:


If the automatic seal retrieval mode is not required, the eight (8) retrieval ring actuating
pins (item 10) should be removed prior to interfacing the tool with the hanger. This is
achieved by removing the anti-rotation pin (item 21) through the machined hole in the
retrieval ring (item 8). The retrieval ring is then rotated and the pins removed individually
through the same hole. Upon completion, the retrieval ring should be returned to its
original position and the anti-rotation pin refitted.


           Seal automatic retrieval is outlined in the following section.
  1.   Set the casing hanger in an upright position and install the seal on to the casing
      hanger body. Thread the six (6) shear pins into the holes in the packoff assembly
       body, ensuring that these engage with the mating retaining holes in the casing
      hanger body.


                            NOTE:


There are two sets of holes in the casing hanger body, circular and elongated (slotted).
Ensure that the shear pins are fitted to the circular holes during this phase. This will
ensure that the space-out between the tool and hanger is correct and that the tool lock
ring will interface with the corresponding groove in the hanger body.

  6.   Rotate the stem to the left for a minimum of three (3) turns until a build up of torque
         is noticed. This has the effect of moving the cam ring down and engaging behind

number of shear pins may be used. Each pin is rated for approximately 5000 lbs down. A
minimum of three (3) shear pins may be used, with a corresponding minimum weight

ensure that the tool has returned to its running position from where the release procedure
can be repeated.

             PACKOFF RETRIEVAL PROCEDURES

If  it is necessary to retrieve the packoff immediately after setting the packoff and
performing the pressure test, the following procedure should be used.
1.   With the running tool still in the hole, confirm that the BOP pipe rams are open.

    Apply 10,000 lbs (4 540 kgs) weight down on the running string and rotate the
     running string 6 turns to the right, or until torque is noticed. This indicates that the
    cam has contacted the stabilizer sleeve. Do not exceed 5,000 ft-lbs (6 870 Nm)
     torque.

     This right hand rotation will force the expansion pins outward and lock the seal
      retrieval ring behind the upper lip of the packing nut, allowing retrieval of the seal.
2.  A gradual overpull on the running string separates the wicker engagement and
     pulls the seal free of the casing hanger and seal cavity. The wickers should
    separate at 25,000-35,000 lbs (11 350 - 15 890 kgs) over the running string
     weight. A maximum overpull of 150,000 lbs (68 100 kgs) may be applied  if
    cement, gumbo, etc. is holding the seal in place and preventing retrieval at the
    designed overpull.

        OPERATIONAL SYNOPSIS  - PACKOFF RETRIEVAL
1.   Weight Down - 10,000 lbs

2.   6 RH turns or until torque build up (maximum 5000 lbs)

3.   Overpull 25-35k (maximum 150,000 lbs)

4.   Pull out of hole


   PACKOFF INSTALLATION PROCEDURE (TWO TRIP PROCEDURE)
1.  Run the Mill and Flush tool per Operating and Service Procedure 6071.

2.   Position and secure the running/retrieving tool upside down and rotate (by hand or
     strap wrench) the cam downward (right hand rotation) until the cam has bottomed
     out on the stabilizer sleeve. This should fully expand the seal retrieval ring.

     Rotate the cam, approximately 4 turns (left hand), until the retrieval ring will fully
     collapse into its groove. Record the number of turns for reference when the
    packoff is installed on the tool, and subsequently when the assembly is run
     in the hole.

     Verify that the cam has not engaged the lock ring and that it is fully collapsed.
3.   Ensure that the two (2) anti-rotation keys will depress completely inside the OD of
     the cam.
4.   Position the seal assembly onto the tool ensuring that the seal collet fingers are
     resting on the tool retainer ring.

  5.   Ensure that the seal is fully protected from damage then lay the tool and seal

                           CAUTION:


Ensure all six (6) shear pins are removed from the packoff assembly. Shear pins
are only required for retaining packoff assembly to hanger body for single-trip
running and installation.

  6.   Pick up packoff and running tool and run in hole.

DO NOT rotate string when running in hole. Take particular care NOT to rotate the
landing string while making up the connections as this may cause the running tool
cam sleeve to rotate with respect to the running tool stem.

  7.  A slight left hand rotation of the running string will be required when the tool has
      landed downhole. Additional weight below the tool  will assist in fully
       inserting the tool into the casing hanger and prevent cocking of the packoff.
  8.   With the assembly landed in the correct position, apply 30,000 -50,000 lbs
       (13,620-22,700 kgs) of weight down to set the seal and engage the locking wickers
        to the hanger.
  9.   Close the pipe rams and pressure test the packoff through the choke and kill line.

     An additional ½ turn may be required to initially engage the anti-rotation
      keys with the slots in the casing hanger body.
  11.  Pull the running tool out of the hole.

POST OPERATIONAL MAINTENANCE

16" Casing Hanger Running Tool
    1.  Wash the tool thoroughly with fresh water.

    2.   Inspect the o-rings on the OD of the test plug and replace if required.

    3.   Lightly grease the anti-rotation keys and key sockets.

    4.   Completely lubricate the tool prior to storage. Apply thread dope to both tool joints
       and install the thread protectors. Install and fasten the running tool protector.
