Preparation
Stand Back DP's as Needed for 17-1/2” Hole Section (2610.5 m). Regarding the rig Condition, WSS Should decide to Stand DPs Joint by Joint When Running in Hole or Stand Back Drill Pipe Before Start of Drilling.  Check Junk Sub to be BOX DOWN Type. If Not, Use Bit Sub above Bit. Ensure that all Drilling Tools and equipment, as per Section equipment listings, are on board, checked out and in a serviceable condition prior to 24" casing shoe drill out. Prepare 13 3/8” & 13 5/8” Casings Tally after preparation and measurement of casing joints length.  Be ready to run Packed Hole Assembly for lower parts of the section, in case of need.

[جدول 4]
Drilling Procedure | Parallel activities / items to remember
Hold PJSM P/UP enough 5” DP to be able to drill to TD (+/-2610.5 m)  Drill Out BHA P/U & M/U 17 1/2" Drill Out BHA as following: Drill Out BHA Configuration:  Run W/ 17-1/2” Drill Out BHA to ±150 m. Pass annular preventer gently. F/U & break circulation after RIH BHA. Record BHA weight.  Drill Ahead 17-1/2” hole section to ±180 m using HI-VIS pill. Drilling Parameters:  WOB: 10-25 Kips, GPM: 300-400, RPM: 60-90    Note: Use safety clamp and proper slip for all DCs & 5” DPs. POOH to Surf. L/D BHA and Junk Sub. RIH W/ New BHA (two S.Stab @ ±10m and ±20m) and 17 1/2" R. Bit.  Drilling BHA Drilling BHA Configuration: ** Use 1/8” U.G Stabilizer Cont. Drilling 17 ½’’ hole section down to ±800 m. (Displace the mud with new Salt /Polymer mud). POOH to surface. L/D bit. Change the Bit to new PDC Bit. RIH W/ Drilling BHA and new 17-1/2" PDC Bit. Note:  This hole section will be penetrated into the Aghajari & Mishan formation and 13 3/8” casing shoe will be set at the near top of Gachsaran formation, 10 m in member no. 6, at depth of 2610.5 m (MD). The Mishan formation (about 438 m thickness) with Middle Miocene in age is mainly comprised of alternated marls and limestone beds in this well, overlying the topmost Gachsaran anhydrite layer. 17 1/2” Hole size will be penetrated within this marly/ carbonate formation. Care should be given to avoid Bit Balling by adjusting Hole Cleaning Hydraulics and Mud Properties. Since, there is a risk of Bit Balling, Overpull and Drag Force should be well monitored specially during trips to avoid excess Swab forces caused by balled up BHA.  In wells SI-6 & SI-7 & SI-8 MW increased gradually from 70pcf to 82-85pcf at top of Mishan.   Drill Ahead 17-1/2” Hole section from ±800m to ±2610.5 m. Drilling Parameters: As per Bit Engineer Recommendation WOB: 10-35 Kips, GPM: 800-900, RPM: 120-180  Notes: Change BHA at defined working hours (400 hr respectively).  Circulate & Clean the Hole, Check Hole Inclination Each 250 m (Using TOTCO).  Perform Wiper Trip each 350 m. Condition the hole.  After drilling each stand, BR/RD last joint and make sure to clear slips area.  Perform drill-off test to optimize ROP.  Drilling parameters & BHA design will be modified to Keep hole vertical.  Record PU/SO HL regularly & off-BTM TQ & monitor and report in DDR.  Driller should consider any abnormal behavior of drill string due to probable bit whirl, bit bouncing & etc. in case of such indications immediately inform WSS to adjust drilling parameters.  Shakers must be checked for amount & shape of cuttings.   BR/RD each drilled STD prior to connection. Do not forget to take P/U, S/O weight & rotation weight prior to M/U next Std.  Never go for connection if hi-TQ/hi-pressure observed, keep circulating and reaming till normal situation.  If over pull / pack-off occur when reaming upwards, move pipe down and reduce pump rate immediately to avoid formation breakdown.  Near TD, adjust ROP as per geologist request to have enough time for cutting to come to surface.  Perform Condition Trip prior to (Casing Running & logging). Flow Check at Shoe. Trip back to BTM. Notes: In Case of observe any tight hole, W&R same and clear tight spot. Meanwhile performing condition trip, prepare logging equipment to run CDR/GR.  Spot 100 bbl Hi-Vis Pill at bottom. POOH to Surface. Hold pre-job safety meeting (PJSM). R/U Wireline logging equipment. Run CDR/GR as per logging program. POOH Logging Equipment. Prepare for running casing string.  Note:  BHA designs may be changed based on Hole Survey Results to Maintain the hole Verticality. | Focus on stabbing BHA, hole cleaning, deviation Check radio communication. Check wash pipe, IBOP and saver sub, change before RIH, if required.      Mud engineer will be at shakers to ensure mud is in good shape before drilling out shoe. Work through shoe area several times and clean out rat hole. Watch carefully for any indication of abnormal torque, since there might be a possibility for shoe being backed off. Maximum allowable torque is 3-4 klb.ft. Report any problem immediately. Try not to pollute mud with cement while drilling shoe track. Rig management is responsible for supplying conventional fishing equipment of this hole section. Before drill out shoe check parameter and carefully observe torque to avoid damage shoe or back off      Use safety clamp and proper slip for all DCs & 5” DPs.     Check Hole Inclination Each 250 m (Using TOTCO). Perform Wiper Trip each 400 m. Newly inspected drill pipes are recommended to be used at the top of string (closer to surface).  Record the time and the rest of drilling parameters in IADC report.        To avoid corrosion on mud pump and high pressure hoses and drill string it is recommended to control pH and solid of mud. As per nearby well …., it may be possible to observe increase in Ca2+, but in case of not any side effect in other mud properties, no permission to increasing MW. Mud weight range in this section is expected to be from 64 to 98 pcf. In case of hole conditions dictation, get permission from office for increasing MW. Mud properties (especially Hardness of mud filtrate), should be checked frequently – according to DSV – not less than three times a day to avoid well kicking. Check and maintain pit mud guns regularly. Use LCM pill if encountered heavy losses. If loss couldn’t be cured by LCM pill use cement plug(s) to cure same. To avoid wash out and twist off inspection or replacement of the DC’s and BHA should be done according to normal inspection periods, having available a reasonable amount of spare DC’s and bottom hole assemblies is required. Submit inspection report to engineering department. Wash and ream all these zones of hold up and do not try to push through this zones. Mud properties will be monitored by mud engineer while drilling and if any indication of Salt Water flow is detected, mud weight will be increased by adding barite and hematite for killing the well.  The exact TD will be determined based on geologist confirmation. Double check pipe tally (SLM). Sweep & circulate hole clean, work tight spots of bottom. Call for logging crew and casing running services, so that logging can be immediately started after pulling out the reaming assembly. Check bit/ nozzles and report dull grading. Consider to inspect top drive and derrick for loose items. Consider load capacity of the floor. If necessary lay down extra pipes. Well control policy: hard shut-in Blind rams at bottom and Pipe rams at top of BOP stack. Use wear bushing to protect CHH.                            Based on hole condition, if Observed any indication of improper hole condition meanwhile logging operation, perform a condition trip.
