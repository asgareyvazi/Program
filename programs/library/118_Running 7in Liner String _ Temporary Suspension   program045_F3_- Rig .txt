     Forward Program: Running 7” Liner String & Temporary Suspension
     program.

   Data Summary:

                                    8 1/2” Hole Section

    RTE:                         13 m

     Surface Coordination:            E: 195,087.    N: 3,447,608.

     Formation:              FAHLYAN (3933mt)
     Lithology Description:            Limestone
    7" Liner/ Type/           From 4144 m to 2760 m ,7", L-80, 35#, Hydrill CTS.
     Grade/Thread:

   Mud Type/Weight:             Water base mud / 104 PCF.

   Purpose of this Program

     The main purpose of this program is Run 7” Liner String and Temporary Suspension well
    AZNS 045(F3)

   Sequences of Operation

   A. Run 7” Liner String

        1. RIH with given hole condition BHA to the bottom of the well at depth of 4145m MD
          to check hole condition.

        2. At any depth that condition string got weight, start wash & ream down ward to ensure
           that hole is at good condition for running 7” liner string.

        3. At the bottom of the well, circulate, BTM’s up with mud 104 Pcf to clean the hole and
           also balance mud weight.

        4. Check shale shaker and ensure that after BTM’s up, it is clean and free of any cutting.

        5.  Prepare and spot 40 BBls Hi-Vis Pill with 104 Pcf Mud Weight in open hole.

        6. POOH condition BHA to 9 5/8” shoe at depth of 3880m MD. Check hole condition
          while POOH and ensure that well is at good condition.

        7. Observe the well at 9 5/8” shoe for 15 minutes. If well is static, continue POOH to
           surface and if well is at loss situation, measure and report loss volume to town.

        8. Continue POOH to BHA, and observe again the well for 15 minutes. Follow Item 7
          procedure.

        9. Continue POOH to surface. break out & L/D Bit. Report bit dull condition in DDR.

       10. Remove wear bushing from THS.

        11. Check top BOP Rams, and change them to 7” Rams according to NIDC tool pusher
          procedure.

       12. Rig up NIDC casing running equipment and check tools functionality.

       13. Prepare a job safety meeting with all related and key personnel.

       14. Pick up and make up 7” Float shoe (7”, L-80, 35#, Hydrill CTS) under the first 7” liner
            joint (7”, L-80, 35#, Hydrill CTS) and 7” Float Collar (7”, L-80, 35#, Hydrill CTS)
         above second liner joint (7”, L-80, 35#, Hydrill CTS). Then make up LDC (7”, L-80,
         35#, Hydrill CTS) above third liner joint (7”, L-80, 35#, Hydrill CTS). Make up them
          to optimum make up Torque.

       15. Use casing baker lock to all of shoe, Float and LDC Joints.

       16. Check 7” Float Shoe and 7” Float Collar functionality test and if Ok, RIH to the well.

       17. Continue Pick up and Make up 7” liner (7”, L-80, 35#, Hydrill CTS) to depth of 385m
     MD as per tally sheet while fill up inside liner string periodically. (fill up each 5 joints)

       18. Short circulate mud to confirm filling string.

       19. Pick up and make up 7” Hydraulic Set Liner Hanger with related accessories and
            liner hanger running tool and make up them to liner string.

       20. Liner hanger man should check the liner hanger and also running tool before RIH.

       21. Continue RIH 7” liner string followed by 5” DP to 9 5/8” casing shoe at depth of
        3880m MD.

       22. Note: Suitable Rabbit for 5”, 19.5# DP should pass while RIH each stand of DP’s and
           also fill up each 10 stands of 5” DP.

       23. At 9 5/8” shoe, short circulate mud as one volume of 7” string.

       24. Continue RIH 7” liner by 5” DP’s to open hole carefully. Wash down at any point in
         which string got weight.

       25. Tag well bottom gently at depth of 4145m MD. Mark on 5” DP. POOH 1.0m as rath-
          hole to depth of 4144m MD. (7” Shoe should be at depth of 4144m MD).

       26. Circulate mud as one volume of casing string at depth of 3921.0m MD.

       27. Line up cementing surface lines carefully and pressure test it to 3,000 Psi with water.

       28. Drop Liner Hanger setting ball as per Liner Hanger Man Procedure and pump to seat
            ball correctly. Set Liner Hanger as per Liner Hanger Man Procedure. Record the
           setting pressure.

       29. Mix and Pump 86.5 BBls cement slurry 115 PCF as per given cement program and
           displace  it as per given program. Record and observe the Shearing and Bumping
          pressure in DDR. Measure and record mud and cement loss into formation while
        pumping and displacing cement slurry.

       30. POOH 5 stands above liner lap and Flush String.

       31. POOH 5 stands more and Direct Circulate Mud as per 1.5 volume of annulus.

       32. Continue POOH 5” DP’s with running tool to surface and L/D running tool.

   B. Clean Cement Out of Well

         1. P/U & M/U 8-1/2” Clean up BHA#1:

       BHA#1: 8-1/2” Bit(214 or EQ., 3*18) + Bit Sub w/ float valve + 6-1/2” DC(12Jts) +

         6-1/2” Drilling Jar + 6-1/2” DC(3Jts) + 5” HWDP(9Jts)

        2. RIH with 5” DP to tag TOC carefully. D/O cement in 9-5/8” casing to top of 7” L.
          Lap. Circulate BTM’S UP and Clean Hole.

        3. POOH to Surface and L/D 8-1/2” Bit.

        4. P/U and M/U 5 7/8” BHA#2:

       BHA#2: 5 7/8” Bit(214 or EQ., Open) + Bit Sub w/ float valve + 4-3/4” DC(15Jts) +

         4-3/4” Drilling Jar + 4-3/4” DC(3Jts) + 3-1/2” HWDP(15Jts).
        5. P/U 19Jts of 3-1/2” DP and RIH with BHA#2.

        6. Continue RIH with 5” DP to tag 7” L. Lap Carefully, Continue RIH to tag P.O.B,

      D/O P.O.B.

        7. Continue RIH to TOC. Drill out Cement & CMT Plug to top of LDC.

        8. Pump 20 BBLs Hi-vis Pill. Circulate BTM’s up and Clean Hole.

        9. POOH to 30m above of 7” Liner Lap.
   C. Liner Lap. 7” Positive Test:
        1.  Prepare a job safety meeting with all related and key personnel.

       2. L/U CMT Line & Test to 3000 psi

       3. Close Well & Perform Liner Lap. 7” Positive Test to 2000 psi (Step by Step, Each
           Step500psi).
       4. Record Pumped Volume, Pressure (Pressure Recorder) and Bleed Back Volume.
         Report all them to the Town.
       5.   If Liner Lap. 7” Positive Test is NOT OK, Next Program will be Issue Later.
       6. POOH to Surface L/D 5 7/8” Bit.

   A. Logging:

      Rig up wireline Equipment and conduct Neutron/GR and CBL/VDL/CCL/GR Log. Rig
     down wireline Equipment.

   B. Liner Lap 7” Dry Test:

         1. P/U & M/U 8 ½” Bit, 9 5/8” Scraper & 6 ½” DC(9Jts) & RIH to 20m above Liner

        Lap 7”, Scrape 20-50m above Liner Lap. 7” Several Time.

        2. POOH to Surface & L/D 8 ½” Bit, 9 5/8” Scraper.
        3. P/U & M/U Dry Test Tools and RIH to 30m above Liner Lap. 7”, Test Liner Lap. 7”

           as attached Program.

        4. POOH to Surface & L/D Dry test Tools.

        5.  If Dry Test Result is NOT OK, Next Program will be Issue Later.

    6. Sequences of Temporary Suspension

        1. P/U & M/U 5 7/8” C. Cut Bit, 10 STDs 3 1/2” & 5" DP’s and RIH to Liner Lap. 7”.

        2.  Circulate, BTM’s up.

        3. Spot 25 BBLs Hi-vis Pill.

        4. POOH to 100m above Liner Lap. 7”.

        5.  Prepare a job safety meeting with all related and key personnel.

        6. Mixed and Pump 35BBLs Cement Slurry as per Given Cement Program and displace it

           as cement balance plug. POOH 5 stands above estimated top of cement plug and flush

          the string.

        7. POOH to 3200m and Wait on CMT to get hard (according to cement program).

        8.  If CMT surf sample get hard continue RIH till string get weight. Stop RIH and start

         wash downward carefully. Observe returned mud and cuttings to surface, Continue

        Wash down until get weight.

        9. POOH to Surface L/D 5 7/8” C. Cut Bit.

       10. P/U & M/U 8 ½” Bit & BHA#1 RIH W/ 5” DPs to TOC, D/O 10m of Hard

        Cement.

        11.  Circulate BTM’s up.
       12. Displace Mud System by Hi-PH Mud (MIN PH: 10).

       13. POOH to Surface L/D 8 ½” Bit.

       14. P/U & M/U 5 7/8” C. Cut Bit, 10 STDs 3 1/2” & 5" DP’s and RIH to 1200m.

       15. Spot 25 BBLs Hi-vis Pill.

       16. POOH to 1100m.
       17. Prepare a job safety meeting with all related and key personnel.

       18. Mixed and Pump 35 BBLs Cement Slurry as per Given Cement Program and displace it

           as cement balance plug. POOH 5 stands above estimated top of cement plug and flush

          the string.

       19. POOH to 900m and Wait on CMT to get hard (according to cement program).

       20. If CMT surf sample get hard continue RIH till string get weight. Stop RIH and start
         wash downward carefully. Observe returned mud and cuttings to surface, Continue

        Wash down until get weight.

       21. POOH to Surface L/D 5 7/8” C. Cut Bit & DPs.

       22. L/D BOP and Install 11” * ½” Blind FLG 10K on CHS.

       23. Install Pressure Gauge on Blind FLG.
       24. Test B.FLG to 2000psi for 15 Minute.

       25. Release Rig.
