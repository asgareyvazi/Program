                             On-Site Preparation (Prior to Running)



1.     Using the Sub Assembly Data Packs and equipment manifest, check that  all the necessary
       equipment is on location and is in good condition.

2.     Take  all relevant measurements of the equipment  - lengths, OD's,  ID's, etc.  Make sure  all
       accessories (mills, scrapers) have compatible threads.

3.     Ensure that there is a minimum of 50,000 lbs of Drill collars and /or HW Drill Pipe available in the
         derrick. . The pipe used in this run should be the same string as is going to be used for the setting of
        the Tie Back packer.




                                  Running Procedure


1.     Run in the hole with 6" bit/scraper/mill/scraper assembly. Note: Have the assembly spaced out so
        the No-Go on the top dress mill locates on top of the 7" liner and that the 6" bit is 20 to 30 feet above
        the landing collar at this point. In the event of a triple scraper assembly, ensure the 13 3/8 scraper is
      20 to 30 feet (approximately) above the top of the 9 5/8" liner.

2.     Make up one trip mill assembly (consisting of 9 5/8” scraper c/w top dress mill, spacer and lower
        polish mill).


3.     Run in hole until the mills are ±20 ft. above the top of the liner, or the bit tags cement, whichever
      comes first.

4.     Break circulation 30 - 50 SPM and record pick up and slack off weights.

5.       Start rotating at 20 - 30 rpm, and record torque value.

6.     Run in hole and tag the liner top with no-go on the top dress mill assembly. Mark the pipe at the
        rotary table. Pick up 7ft and mark pipe again. Start rotating and reciprocate the string between the
       two marks. Set down 5,000 lbs. Rotate the mills slowly for 6-10 revolutions to polish the top of the
          liner and to mark the indicator ring on the top dress mill.

7.     Stop rotation and pick up mill assembly from liner top. This will prevent false marking of the mill
      when pulling out. Ensure that polishing mill does not come out of liner top.

8.     Repeat steps 6 and 7 a further two times making sure that the liner top dress mill locates at the
      same depth each time.


9.      Pick up until lower polish mill is above the top of the liner (Approximately 10 feet).

10.   Pump hi-vis sweep and flow check. Continue circulating until clean returns are seen at surface.
       Flush all surface lines followed by safe surf pill. Function BOP’s. Continue to circulate until NTU

12.    Lay down the mill assembly. The dress off mill has a wear ring which should indicate it located on
        the top of the liner (the underside of the no go has been painted to highlight the wear).
