
Welling recommendation for 100 M cement plug pilot hole

# Subject : Summery of the cement program and design  for 100 m of pilot hole
Open hole size= 8.5”
Open hole depth = 100 meters
Volume in 100 meters= 23 bbls
Considering 100% Excess = 46 bbls
Cement type= Class G Neat
Slurry density = 15.8 ppg
Required Yield= 1.15 ft3/sk
46bbl= 258 Ft3
Dry cement required  = 258/1.15= 222 sk  = 224 x 94 lbs=21088 lbs= 9.6 MT
Considering dead volume and other waste= 15 MT cement is required
So to have double in the offshore rig based on standard = 30 MT cement is required
Approximate water requirement is = 5 gal/sks =27 bbls water (these number is approximate based on experience, proper lab test is needed based on actual rig cement sample plus water amount has to be adjust via accurate density)
required chemical
13-1 – make sure 0.02 gal /sk of antifoam is mixed , even MI type
_ no more chemical is needed unless less than 6-7 hrs TT is needed , so in this case you can add 1% BWOC CaCL2 or 3% BWOW NACL  ( Again I am insisting that this concentration are all assumption and need proper lab test with actual chemical and cement )
CS will achieve at least 24 hrs due to low temperature
