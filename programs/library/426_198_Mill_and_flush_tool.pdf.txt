TECHNICAL DESCRIPTION


   The Vetco Gray MS Flush Tool is a cylindrical assembly with a 4-1/2" IF box up and a
   ported overshot type body with scrapping edges down; the ID of which is equipped with
   a bronze bearing plate and lead impression blocks.

   The Flush Tool is designed to remove debris, loose cement, formation cuttings or
  gumbo from the annulus seal area between the casing hanger and the wellhead
   housing. It is not designed to grind out the MS-1, MS-E or SG-TPR seal assembly
   or any other metal which may have collected in the seal pocket.

   Vetco Gray places emphasis on the need to flush sufficiently while using a minimal
   amount of rotation and working of pipe (up and down movement).

ROUTINE OPERATION


                   PRE-OPERATIONAL CHECKS

    1.   Inspect the OD and ID of the tool for gouges and dents. If any are found, file or
        grind smooth.

    2.   Verify the bronze landing plate is installed correctly, with the tapered side looking
      down and all eight screws securely fastened. Verify that all other fasteners are
        secure.

    3.   Note the color and condition of the landing plate as you will need to compare it after
        the flushing procedure to confirm that the tool has landed on the hanger.

    4.   Connect a pressure sub or the kelly to the tool joint connection and pump through
        the tool to verify that the fluid courses are clear. Stay clear from the path of the
         jets to avoid injury.


                             NOTE:


 The four ¼" holes in the bronze landing plate allow for the installation of special lead
 babbits which extend below the landing plate, serving as an indicator of how far the tool
 reaches into the seal pocket.


                     RUNNING PROCEDURE

    Prior to running this tool verify the seal assembly is not in position and has been
   removed from the wellhead.

    1.  Make up the tool on drillpipe and run into the hole. Measure pipe going in the hole
         to verify the tool position against measurements previously obtained when
         retrieving the seat protector/wear bushing.

    2.   Prior to passing through the BOP with the tool, verify the space out. Make up the
        preferred circulation method to the running string.

    3.  Open the motion compensator and adjust it to place a maximum of 2,500 lbs
        (1 134 kgs).

    4.   Slowly lower the tool through the BOP. Due to the close tolerances between the
        tool OD and the BOP ID, a considerable amount of drag may be encountered
      when passing through annular preventers. It may be necessary to slowly
        rotate the tool to the left in these areas.

5.   Prior to landing out, begin flushing and build up to approximately 800 psi (55 bar).
    Land the tool on the casing hanger. Pick up approximately to the top of the
     wellhead (2’-4’ dependant on the casing hanger in the hole) and begin to rotate 3-5
   RPM while flushing. Slowly lower and land on the casing hanger. Monitor for
     torque build up. Maintain minimum torque. Remain on the casing hanger only
      briefly (a minute or so) and pick tool back up off of the hanger. Repeat this process
     several times, remaining on the hanger only briefly (a minute or so) keeping torque
    and rotation to a minimum. Continue to flush at all times. Vetco Gray places the
    emphasis on the flushing action with assistance from rotation to remove
    debris from the seal pocket so it can be lifted out with fluid. With assistance
     from the choke and kill lines, circulate riser clean until returns contain a minimum
    amount of debris (a minimum of twice the riser volume). During rotation, be alert
     for any sudden torque build up. Should this happen - stop the rotary table,
    mark the pipe and carefully raise the string.

    Vetco Gray recommends the choke and kill line be opened and used to
     assist in flushing any debris from the casing hanger/wellhead bore and riser
    during the operation of the Flush Tool. The setting of a “Gel Pill” in the
    wellhead prior to retrieving the Flush Tool is recommended.

6.   After pulling out of the hole, inspect the landing plate inside the tool for marks
     indicating that the tool has landed on the casing hanger. This check will indicate
    whether all the foreign material has been removed from the seal area or if the tool
     should be run again. A smooth, complete circle marked on the plate indicates that
     the tool has landed on the hanger and has cleaned the entire seal pocket. As
     required and  if not already utilized, the lead babbits may be installed, again
     running the tool to confirm how close the tool came to full depth within the seal
     area.

              POST-OPERATIONAL MAINTENANCE

1.  Wash running tool with fresh water. Check condition of landing plate for excessive
     wear. Replace if necessary.

2.   Flush the fluid courses with fresh water until clean.

3.   Lubricate before storing.

DISASSEMBLY/ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES


                        DISASSEMBLY

    1.   Lay the tool on its side and remove the eight screws securing the bronze plate.


                            CAUTION:


 The removal of the screws will allow the plate to shift and fall out. Caution must be
 exercised to prevent injury.


    2.  Remove the jet nozzles.

    3.  Remove the pipe plugs on the OD of the tool.

                        ASSEMBLY

    1.   Ensure that the fluid courses are clear by flushing fresh water through the bore of
        the stem. Then install the four pipe plugs around the OD using a 3/8" hex key.
       Continue the flushing to ensure that the fluid courses are clear through to the
        nozzles, then install the nozzles. The four pipe plugs on the OD must not
       extend beyond the OD of the body of the tool.

    2.    Install the landing plate with the tapered side looking down with eight shoulder
        screws. Tighten the screws.

                             TEST

      None required.
