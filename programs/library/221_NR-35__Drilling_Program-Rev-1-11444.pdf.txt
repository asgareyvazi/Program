   2  Introduction

             This program covers the drilling of well# NR- 35 in Nowrooz field, Iran. The well is
             intended  to produce Oil from Dictyoconus Reservoir. The well  will be  drilled
               directionally to a planed  total depth of 2791m MD, TVD: 2612.7m (-2577.4m
            TVDss). The exact TD will be determined based on the logging results.

            Note: This program has been written with respect to the Draft of geological
              forecast, in case of any dispute in this program, amendment program will be
             issued in due time.


   3  Well Purpose & Objectives

                   It is proposed to drill deviated well as a development well to produce Oil from
            Dictyoconus Reservoir.

            •  At section TD, circulate, clean hole. Perform wiper trip. CBU on bottom. Spot 100

                  to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.


            •  Cement casing as per program. Displace with mud. Monitor for pressures. Record
              and report the final pressure before bump the plug. Bump the plug with 500 psi over
                 the final circulating pressure or to 1000 psi max. Bleed off pressure. Check for back
                flow. If any back flow observed, hold the pressure inside for the cement to thick.

            •  Perform top-up cement jobs as per program to fill the annulus if needed.

            •   Hold on casing string weight. Drop 21 ¼” x 13 3/8” casing slip. Slack off casing
                weight. Test same to 500 psi pressure. (Open base flange side outlet valves while
                   testing).

            •  Rough cut 13 3/8" casing.

            •  Remove the 18 3/4" Hydrill.

            •  Cut 20" casing @ the level of wellhead position.

            •  Cut & dress 13 3/8" casing @ proper height.

            •  N/U 21 1/4"-2000 x 13 5/8”-3000 Casing Head Spool on top of 13 3/8" casing & test
                         it w/ 1000 psi.

                13-3/8" casing annulus if needed. Test BOP's.

                  Note: Do not forget to check the wear bushing with CHS and tie down flange
                    before N/U Annular preventer.

      Sequence of Operations

            •  Make up 8-1/2” BHA (Include junk sub) as per BHA design. RIH to top of float

                well flow.

            •   Circulate clean hole, work on junk sub, POOH to surface, L/D bit, P/U & RIH with
              keep angle Assy to bottom

            •  Dir drilling 8-1/2" hole down to 7" Liner Point @ 2754m, MD.
             Note: Adjust mud weight with respect to the hole condition.

            •   Circulate, Clean & Condition hole prior to run liner. Spot 50 bbl hi vis pill and
            POOH.

               run liner hanger and accessories. Follow liner string with 5” DPs (Run drift inside 5”
             D.P & perform SLM while RIH with liner).

      15.1 Casing General Preparation


        ¾  Visually and mechanically check shoe and collar for proper operation.
        ¾  Ensure all connections are thoroughly cleaned using steam or a grease solvent.
        ¾  Ensure that all casing running equipment are available and in good operation
                   condition.
        ¾  Ensure the correct casing drifts are supplied to the rig.
        ¾  Ensure that all casing joints have been drifted.
        ¾  Ensure all cement bulk lines have been checked for blockage with bulk tanks
                   fluffed and pressurised to the required transfer pressure.
        ¾ Check the plugs fins not to be squeezed or damaged.
        ¾  Inspect and pressure test cement manifold.


                 If Using Stinger:
        ¾ Company man to check stab in adaptor matches stab in shoe.
        ¾ Company man to check stab in adaptor matches stab in collar.
        ¾  Visually and mechanically check the stab in guide shoe for proper operation.
        ¾  Ensure spare seals for stab in adaptor are available.


             General Notes for Liner:
        ¾ WSS is to supervise the loading of liner wiper plug in liner running tool, and
                  loading of drill pipe wiper dart in cementing head.
        ¾ The liner hanger engineer is to be on the rig floor at all times that the liner is being
                 run in open hole.
        ¾ Hold pre-job safety meeting. When running-in, apply thread lock to all connections
                  of the shoe track.
        ¾  Drift Drill pipe while run the liner with suitable Drift.
        ¾  If liner top packer didn’t used after finishing the cement displacing, pull the drill
                  pipes 15 stands. Circulate to clean the string. Observe the well. POOH and lay
              down setting tools.
