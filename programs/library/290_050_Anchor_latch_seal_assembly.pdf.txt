                                                 14SSA73700

                                          Anchor Latch Seal Assembly




                                DESIGN AND OPERATION

                This anchor-latch seal assembly is one part of a complete liner hanger “Monobore”
               system.

             The anchor-latch is designed to engage in the thread in the setting sleeve to tie the
                 tubing string to the liner hanger system. The indicator collet is used to indicate when the
                seal assembly is stabbed into the PBR and allow space out of the tubing hanger at
                 surface. The seal assembly creates a tubing/annulus pressure barrier to prevent fluid
               communication.

             The assembly is run in hole on the tubing string. The seal assembly enters the upper
           PBR of the upper PBR and liner-top packer assembly until weight slacks off when the
                  indicator  collet locates  in the  profile of the setting sleeve within the upper PBR
               assembly. Picking up weight verifies that the indicator collet is engaged and the datum
                 point at surface is used to space out the tubing hanger.

            When the tubing hanger is landed the anchor-latch is still not engaged in the mating
                thread on the setting sleeve. An annular pressure test to check the tubing hanger, seal
              assembly and liner top packer creates sufficient reverse ballooning force to extend the
                 tubing string and engage the anchor-latch.

             The anchor-latch can be disengaged by right-hand rotation of the tubing string or a
               200,000 lb. overpull which shears the shear sub and un-supports the anchor latch
                 allowing it to be pulled free.
