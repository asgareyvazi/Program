PARS PETRO ZAGROS Co.

RIG 84 Fath,SARKHOON-4
COMPLETION STRING PROGRAM


A-Completion Surface Tests:
1- Drift and measure OD, ID and length of all completion accessories.
2- Set 5.950" dummy in Baker 3SV L/nip, connect Cont. line and test
   against dummy to 5000 psi.
3- Set plug 4.188”RPT in Otis Top no-go land. / nip and test to 4500 psi,
   leave the plug set.
4- Set plug 4.562" AF in Baker SSD and test to 4500 psi, remove plug,
   open and close SSD with shifting tools and test to 4500 psi.
5- Open Expansion Joint fully and test to 4500psi, set shear pins.
6- Set dummy 1 1/2" in Baker SPM and test to 4500psi

B-Completion Running Procedure:
 -Remove wear bushing
 -Rig up completion running tools

1- RIH w/Mule shoe, Top no-go…...etc. as per attached tally (with plug
   4.188"RPT in top no-go L/nip. and closed SSD) up to SSSV L/nipple
   Note: F/up 100m on top of no-go plug with H-Vis polymer pill
   (120sec) and the rest each 15 jt w/ fresh water (should be confirmed)
   without debris
    -If complete loss occur, about 400m inside TBG. Should not be filled,
   while cross wellhead 2.5BPM
2- Connect Cont. line to 3SV L/nip. And test to 5ooo psi, use band-it
   strap to tight cont.line against TBG.
3- Cont. RIH as per tally, P/up TBG/Hgr , Connect Cont. line to port on
   Hanger and test to 5000psi.
4- P/up 7” Landing /jt, connect to Hgr.
5- R /up pump unit.
6- Flush and clean T.H.S. w/water.
7- Land T/Hgr and just touch TBG. Head Spool.
8- Space out and pull out the string 1.5 m up (for total stroke 10ft)
9- Pressurise tubing to 500 psi (3 min), 1000 psi (3 min), 1500 psi (3 min),
  2000 psi (3 min),2500 psi (3 min),3000 psi (3 min),3500 psi(3 min),4000
psi(3 min),4500 for 15 min.
 Note: While pressurizing the tubing some back flow will observe
 through side outlet valves, it should be stopped after a while.
10- Release pressure and over pull the string with 20000 lb.

18- N/U Bottom master valve 11"x9" Cameron and test the ports of
   sealing as program.

   manifold, test all to 4500 psi

 4500psi

  hydraulic oil.
23- Open SSD; displace Ann. and TBG. w/ inhibited diesel (should be
 confirmed)

27- Flow the well through variable choke to clean up.
28- Divert well to the fix choke and record the result on 32/64”and 48/64”
   choke sizes
29- Close the well and monitor annulus pressure
30- Remove dummy 5.950”
31- Flush control line with hydraulic oil

33- Release above SSSV pressure to zero in two stages.

35- Fill up above 3SV with completion fluid
36- Close all valves except Bottom Master Valve and 2SV
