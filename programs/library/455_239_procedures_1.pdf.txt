                  PROCEDURE FOR RUNNING 20"RL-4S CASING

EQUIPMENT REQUIRED

1) 2 Sets of 20" elevators
2) 2 sets of 20" slips
3) 2 sets of 20" dog collars
4) Power tong and Rig tongs set to 20"
5) Set of 20 " inserts
6) 2 x 16" landing joints
7) 2 of 20" casing shoes pre-painted white with black meter bands
8) Xxx amount of 20" casing to allow for spare
9) 2 of 18 3/4 hp wellheads marked in meter lengths
  Primary wellhead will have pre-installed NSP and 18 3/4 Cam Actuated Running Tool (CART)
10) Spare 18 3/4 Cam Actuated Running Tool (CART )
11) Vetco Anti Rotation Tab shear tool
12) Grinder with cutting disc
13) Oil for connector lub
14) False rotary plate
15) 2 sets of D/pipe elevators for cement stinger make up
16) Spare O-rings PT NO XXXXXXXXXXX
17) 2 of 20 " drifts
18) Centralisers as required
19) 2 of Cement heads and darts as required





RUNNING PROCEDURE

1) Floor to be prepared and ready for equipment
2 )Rig up 20" elevators
3) Remove rotary table and diverter element and fit 20" bowl inserts
4) Have tongs prepared and set for 20" pipe
5) Bring slips and dog collar to floor
6) Pick up shoe joint and tail into floor
 Make sure the slingset is in a position to allow the elevators to latch behind the shoulder
7) Latch elevators to shoe and pick up
  Land shoe joint in slips and fit dog collar
 Shoe will be pre-painted and marked in meters to assist ROV Ensure no trash in pipe
8) Check shoe
9) Pick up the next joint and bring to floor
10) Set in elevators lift and stab into shoe Ensure Orings good and lubricate connector
    Protectors to remain on shoe until joint is suspended
11) Stab and make up joint (RL-4) using tong and back -up (1/4-1/2 turn RH M/UP TORQUE 25000 ftlbs)
12) Cut anti rotation tab then pickup test
13) Remove slips and RIH
14) Continue to run casing as above filling as required to wellhead joint

[ صفحه 2 از 10 ]
────────────────────────────────────────────
AT 18 3/4 WELLHEAD

15) With last joint in table
16) Release bales from elevators leaving elevators around casing as safety collar
17) Fit false rotary plate and make up cement stinger using second set of D/pipe elevators
  Change out elevators each stand down
18) Make up open ended D/pipe cement stinger to desired length
19) Pick up 18 3/4 CART and lift and stab, m/up drillpipe cement connection
   Ensure the whole assembly rotates as one unit
20) Remove false rotary and lower and stab wellhead joint to last connection
21) Make up wellhead RL-4C Connection 1/4 -1/2 turn RH to 25000ftlbs
22) Pick up test then Remove slips and run through floor     Fit rotary
23) Run to below waterline and fill pipe
   Tool will have vent port open( pumps can be used at low rate to fill)
24)When full pick up and fit /close plug
25) Continue to run and with ROV witness shoe enter LP housing
   Adjust rig position to suit
26) Pick up cement stand and make up to string
27) Run and land 18 3/4 wellhead in 30" LP housing
28) Confirm lock down with 50000 O/Pull
29) Prepare to circ and cement

30) On completion of cement job recover tool with 5 k o/pull by releasing tool 5 Turns Rh
31) Break out tool and preserve
32) Prepare for next section

            PROCEDURE FOR RUNNING 13 3/8 CASING

Equipment Required

1) 2 x 13 3/8 shoe joint
2) 2 x 13 3/8 float collars
3) 2 X landing shoulders
4) 2 x 13 3/8 hangers
5) 13 3/8 pipe ( X joints) +5 and 10 ft pups pups
6) Centralisers (x)
7) 2 of Drifts
8) Klepo protectors
9) 2 of 13 3/8 pick up elevators
10) 2 of 13 3/8 stabbing guides
11) 2 of sets of bushing inserts
12) 2 of sets of elevators
13) 2 of sets of slips
14) 2 of dog collars
15) 2 of power tongs
16) 2 of PADPRTS in shipping skids
17) 2 of sets cement plugs
18) 2 of cement equalising subs
19) 2 x cement heads
20) 2 of 4 1/2 if pin x 4 1/2 if 8 rnd pin x overs
21)2 0f 13 3/8 wear bushings
22) 2 of MS-1 SEALS
23) 1 of MS Emergency seals
24) 2 of cement heads
25) 2 sets of 500 ton air powered slips
26) 2 sets of 500 ton air powered elevators


Procedure for making up hanger to PADRT with seal and plug launcher
Take the cement head prepared with darts to the floor
Make up to landing stand and rack back


The PADPRT can be made up to the hanger offline using a hole in the deck or other suitable place

Take the PADPRT to the floor and torque a 15 ft pup joint above
Take the 4 1/2 IF Pin x 4 1/2 IF 8 RND Pin to the floor along with the cement equalising sub and
torque together in the roughneck
Bring tool and sub to the work area
Pick up the hanger using single joint elevators or slings in a basket hitch and stab in the deck hole
Remove the hanger protector and fit the seal over the hanger
Pick up the tool and make up the equalising sub 8 rnd connection to the bottom of the tool
using chain tongs (awkward)
Fit the plug set to the plug launcher
Grease the plugs and soak the inside of the casing
Stab the plug set into the casing and lower until the PADPRT seal thread is at a working height
Pick up the seal and screw up on to the tool thread When fully made up use a punch to ensure it
will not come loose


────────────────────────────────────────────
[ صفحه 4 از 10 ]
────────────────────────────────────────────
Continue to lower the the tool and just prior to the tool entering the hanger fill the pipe with water
Align the anti rotation tab on the tool with the hanger profile and stab in
Make the tool up to the hanger with 4 TURNS RH
Mark a line on the tool and check the standoff from the bottom of the seal to its landing shoulder
Fit the protector around the seal and lift the assembly to the storage area until needed

Running Casing

Ensure all pipe is drifted
Rig up to run 13 3/8 casing
Pick up shoe and set in table
Fit float collar
Pick up second joint and make up to the collar
Fit centralisers as necessary
Continue to run casing to the Hanger joint fitting centralisers and filling as required
Change to 500 ton slips and elevators as necessary
At the hanger joint change out the elevators to 5 1/2
Pick up the hanger joint and tool assembly and latch in elevators
Lower and stab into the last joint
Make up the connection ensuring the whole assemby rotates as one unit
Run the tool and hanger through the table to land out in the wellhead
Use the Reference line from the outer barrel of the slip joint to ensure correct position
Commence circulating and cementing
When cementing completed set the seal (4 turns RH +pressure )and test the seal and stack
On completion release the tool with 4 turns RH and recover to surface
Check the lead blocks
Lay the tool down for service and rig up to drill next section
Strip clean and re-cock PADPRT

                  PROCEDURE FOR RUNNING 30" CASING

EQUIPMENT REQUIRED

1) 2 Sets of 30" elevators
2) 2 sets of 30" slips
3) 2 sets of 30" dog collars
4) Power tong and Rig tongs set to 30"
5) 2 x 30 " baasch ross bowls
6) 2 x 30" elevators
7) 2 x 30" slips
8) 2 x 30" dog collars
9) 2 x 30"shoes painted white with meter bands
10) 2 x 30"LP Wellheads
  Primary will have 30" CART tool with handling pup above installed ashore
  Spare CART to be made up in derrick
11) 30" Casing as required
12) 2 sets of 40-60 ft slings (capable of 12 joints weight
13) 6 of shackles for above
14) Vetco Anti Rotation Tab shear tool
15) Grinder with cutting disc
16) Lub oil for connectors
17) Spare O-rings PT NO XXXXXXXXXXX
18)Both Vetco tool skids and all tool spares
19) Burning gear to remove pad eye if not previously done
20) Plate to act as false rotary
21) 2 x Permanent Guide Base (PGB)
22) 2 x Darron Cement Sub complete with hoses and connections ( 1 made up and racked back)
23) 2 x sets of regan posts (8) Posts will be identified with paint rings
24) Spelter sockets/shackles

Procedure
  PGB will be in position under the rotary table with 4 posts marked and fitted
  G/Lines attached

1) Remove bales and fit 60 ft slings to lugs
2) Fit shackles to lower eyes of slings (may need to be doubled to allow orientation)
3) Connect shackles to first set of 30" elevators and secure
4) Spare elevators to be on floor
5) Remove rotary table and fit 30" Baasch Ross bowl
6) Have power tong and rig tong set to 30" for back up
7) Bring slips and dog collar to floor
8) Pick up shoe joint and bring to Vee door joint will be painted white and marked with meter bands
 Make sure the slingset is in a position to allow the elevators to latch behind the shoulder
9) Latch shoe joint into elevators
10) Remove box protector and recheck connection and O-ring Ensure no trash in pipe
11) Pick up shoe and latch in elevators (remove padeye)
12) Lower and land shoe joint in bowl ensure dog collar fitted
13) Check shoe
14) Disconnect slings from elevators leaving them round the 30" pipe as a collar


────────────────────────────────────────────
[ صفحه 8 از 10 ]
────────────────────────────────────────────
15) lubricate connector and refit protector prior to tailing in 2nd joint
16) Connect the slings to the spare elevators and ready for next joint
17) Lift second joint to floor and connect elevators
18) Pick up and stab into shoe connector
19) Fit tongs and make up joint to 35000ftlbs (1/4 - 1/2 turn RH)
20) Cut 2 of anti rotation tabs using vetco tool or grinder with cutting disc
21) Pick up test and remove 1st set of elevators from around pipe
22) Split bowl and run connection through
23) Reconnect bowl and land out intermediate joint in slips
24) Again remove slings from elevators and leave round pipe as collar
25) Reconnect slings to first set of elevators and continue to run pipe
   Cut anti- rotation tabs and fill pipe as required
26) Continue to run 30 " to LP Housing joint



At LP Housing Joint

1) Change out 30" elevators for drillpipe elevators
2) fit false rotary table over last joint of 30"connection
3) Utilising 2 sets of drillpipe elevators make up cement stinger
  This will mean changing out elevators as previous
4) At last connection pick up 30"LP Housing and connect d/pipe connection
  Lower pipe and stab and torque 30" connection to 35000ftlbs
5) Remove handling pup from CART and pick up stand with Darron sub and connect
5) When made up remove false rotary , lower and stab 30" RL-4 connection
6) Torque connection to 35000ftlbs
7) Pick up test and run and land housing in PGB orienting pipe as required
8) Ensure snap ring locates over housing
9) Screw in anti rotation bolts
10)Fit cement jewellery
11) Pick up and run to beneath water
12) Fill pipe ( Pumps can be used to assist)
13) When full close valve on CART
14) Run to stab in pre-drilled hole (ROV jumped earlier )
15) Pick up cement stand and connect to string
15) Continue to run pipe until PGB at set depth above mudline
16) Circulate and cement
17) Hold string to allow cement to cure


To recover tool

  On completion of set time back out CART tool with 5 Turns RH
   Recover tool to flooor
   Prepare for next section
