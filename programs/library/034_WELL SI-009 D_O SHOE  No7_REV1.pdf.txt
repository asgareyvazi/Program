                  WELL SI-009 next program No 7

     1) N/U 13 5/8"-5K BOP stack while perform top job if required.

     2)  Test BOP stack and all related lines as NIOC POLICY.

     3) L/D 9 3/4" Drill Collars.

     4)  P/U 12 1/4'' R. Bit and next BHA & stand DPs as needed.

     5) RIH 12-1/4" R. Bit W/ D/O BHA to top of cement and tag cement above float collar.

     6)  Perform C.I.T to 1500psi/15min.

     7)  Displace hole fluid with 130pcf mud.

     8)  Drill out cement, shoe track & cement below shoe and clean the hole to 2146m (17-1/2-hole

         bottom).

     9)  Circulate bottoms up to clean hole.

        Note:

      ➢  While circulating, use a clean mud.

      ➢  Ensure that the MW in is the same as MW out .

     10) Pull the drilling bit back inside the casing.

     11) Connect the cementing unit lines to the drill pipe. Pressure test.

        Note:

       ➢  use cementing pumps with pressure recorder.

     12) Perform FIT to 140pcf EMW.

         Note: Wait for 15 mins to ensure that the test is held .

       ➢  Bleed off the pressure .

       ➢  Open the BOP

        Note: Record the following:

      ➢  Total drilling fluid volume pumped.

      ➢  The final pumping pressure.

      ➢  The final static pressure.

     13) If formation not held the pressure. Perform remedial squeeze cementing job as below:

      ➢  Perform a pre-job injection test (INJECTIVITY TEST).

      ❖  Note: Record & Report injection pressure and injection rate.

      ❖  Note: According to injectivity test remedial cementing program will be issued.

      ➢ POOH to surface. L/D bit.

      ➢ RIH with 5" OEDP to bottom.
