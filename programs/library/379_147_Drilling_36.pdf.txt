10-1 Drilling 36” Hole Section (Riserless)

Interval                                         :     747m - 878 m MD BRT
30” casing shoe                             :     878 m MD BRT
Mud weight                                   :     S.W. with high-vis sweeps


A 36” hole will be drilled  riserless approximately 130 meter below
  mudline and 30” structural pipe with RL-4 connectors will be run and
  cemented in the pre-drilled 36” hole. A main criterion  is to have
   sufficient support for the 20” surface casing and MS700 full bore
   wellhead in the soft and unconsolidated seabed. The conductor will be
  cemented back to seabed.


Operational guidelines:
1. The pilot hole will be drilled on the final well location.
2. Have available 500 bbls of 1.3 S.G. kill mud.
3. RIH with 36” assembly including 26” bit x 36” hole opener and
   inclination control tool. Tag seabed, record water depth and take a
   deviation survey. Ensure the string is as vertical as possible.
4. Drill 36” hole to 878 m MD BRT (130 meter BML), with seawater in
   combination with high viscosity pills every 10 m or as required.
   Limit weight on bit and keep high RPM to straighten out the hole.
5. After reaching TD, Circulate bottoms up.
6. Displace the hole with high viscosity/ weighted mud..
7. Take deviation Measurement.
8. POOH to 3 meters below the mud line and wait 3-4 hours RIH to TD,
   check for fill.
9. Displace the hole to 1.35 sg weighted bentonite mud at TD.
10.POOH
11.Makeup the 30” running tool into the wellhead housing with drill
   pipe below (cement stinger to be about 18 meters above the float
   shoe).
12.Make up and Lower the 30” conductor, wellhead housing, and
   permanent guide structure on the landing string and stab into the 36”
   hole. Observe hole entry with the ROV. Continue RIH and tag
   bottom.
13.Fill each stand with seawater while running.
14.After landing, check the stick-up and bull's-eye with the ROV. The
