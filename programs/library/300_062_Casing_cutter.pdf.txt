              CASING CUTTER PROCEDURES.


1 )  Check casing collars around cut depth.

2 )   Function test the cutter on surface
     Take note note of the strokes to open the Knives   SPM = _________
     This will be the minimum requirement whatever depth the tool is run.

3 )   Ensure that the knives are tied back securely, so they do not expand prematurely

4 )   Trip in hole to cut depth. Recored WT up _______WT dn_______

5)    Confirm pipe tally for space out of cutter and spear. Engage spear and pick up
string + 5t overpull to confirm spear engaged.

6 )   Bring pump up to required rate, and start cutting operation.

7 )   Casing when cut may Jump or a press drop noticed.

8 )   Pick up string to confirm cut .

9 )  When cut is confirmed cutter bha and casing string is ready to pulled from hole.
