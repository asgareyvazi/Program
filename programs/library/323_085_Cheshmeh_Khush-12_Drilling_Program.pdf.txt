1  Introduction

     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers the drilling of well # 12 in CHESHMEH KHUSH FIELD, Iran.
           The well is intended to produce oil from Asmari formation. The well will be drilled
               vertically down to total depth @ 3527m MD (-3400m, SS). The exact TD will be
             determined based on the logging results. The liner will be perforated in Asmari
             formation, after conventional rig release.


     1.2   Cheshmeh Khosh Structure

           The Cheshmeh Khosh oil field is located on the edge of the Dezful embayment, near
              the southeast of Lurestan border, and 180km to northwest of Ahvaz, between the
           Danan and Paydar oil fields. The reservoir, Asmari structure, is approximately 23.5
         km long and 4.3 km wide at OWC on the northern part of the structure. The terrain is
           somewhat hilly, but the soil provides a suitable foundation for constructing drilling
              platforms.

8 Pre Spud

    IRAN RAZI prepared a pre-spud checklist and distributed  it in contractor rigs. The
       checklist will be performed based on the aforementioned checklist. In addition to the
       checklist, following matters should be checked:
       Check & observe the alignment of the rig. This check will be performed in four stages;
           rig levelling, derrick  verticality, derrick alignment with rotary  table, rotary table
         alignment with conductor pipe. To do this pick up 3 joints of 9 3/4” DC and check the
           verticality of the derrick.
         Mechanical facilities for mud treatment such as :
         Desander, Desilter, mud cleaner, proper size of shaker–screens, hoppers, mud guns &
          mixers, should be all available on rig and functioning properly.
          All mud tanks, mud transfer lines & connection should be checked by filling them
         with water & cross wellhead circulation.
       Mud engineer laboratory should be equipped with a complete set of testing equipment.
        The calibration should be done under supervision of well site drilling supervisor
          before starting the operation.
        Weight indicator, rotary speed gauge, any smart drilling instrumentation, Stroke meter,
          pressure gauges on manifold and in the drilling console, loss / gain gauge and any
          other gauges should be checked and calibrated.
         Stand pipe manifold valves & connection must be checked & pressure tested.
          All motors should be checked under load prior to start drilling.
        Check & control mud stock & cement stock every day & if there is any necessity  to
          request mud material, cement or any drilling tool, it should be prepared & requested at
          proper time.

       Note: Fill up following forms and send to drilling office:

         o Pre-spud check list
         o Location Handed Over
         o Pump Site + Booster and water supply line Schematic.

1  26” Hole Section / 18-5/8” Casing
      1.1 General

       Casing Point             18-5/8” Casing @ 150m
       Formation                Aghajari
        Risks & Hole problems   No Significant Hole Problem
        Deviation control        Totco
       Logging                       ---
                                  Bentonite Mud
                MW    65-68 pcf
        Drilling Fluid
                                     Vis.    90-100 sec
                        PH    11
                   WOB  Maximum Available
        Drilling Parameters    GPM   600-1000
                     RPM   90-130

      1.2 Formation Sampling
             On each 10 meters one sample would be taken. In addition to this general rule,
                   geologist may decide to take more samples.


      1.3 Sequence of Operation

            Make up 26” BHA as per BHA design, drill formation down to 150m (18-5/8”
                casing point in Aghajari formation). Pay enough attention to drill straight hole. Use
         LCM pill if encountered heavy losses. If loss couldn’t be cured by LCM pill use
              cement plug(s) to cure same.


               For more details Ref. to "General Information", item 6-1


           Note:
                - 26” TD is based on 142m inside Aghajari formation.
                - TD should be adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of rat hole under casing shoe.
                - The exact TD will be determined based on 18-5/8” casing tally.

                 Cheshmeh Khosh-12 Loc. W018N
                 Sequence of Drilling Operation

- Do not forget to strap pipe while POOH.
- Drop Totco prior to POOH to evaluate the verticality of hole.

- Use following parameters to drill this hole section:
     -WOB = Maximum available {Pay enough attention to drill straight hole}
     -RPM = 90-130
     -GPM = 600-1000

   At TD, Circulate, clean hole. POOH, P/U 26” N.Bit stabilizer or 26” Reamer, RIH
   and condition the hole to section TD, use Bentonite pill. Check for any fill. CBU on
   bottom.

   POOH. If observed any indication of drag, run back to bottom. Wash and ream to
   make sure that the hole condition is good.

   R/U 18-5/8” casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

   Run ±150m (Approx. 13 Jts) 18-5/8", 87.5 ppf, J-55, BTC casing (CSF= 2.7 ~ 68
    pcf) with guide shoe to bottom as per casing program. Casing shoe should be
    adjusted in such a way to have 2 meters of rat hole and enough stick up (1.5 m) on
    rig floor. Fill casing by non LCM fresh mud joint by joint.

Note:
- Take care when running casing not to set down excessive weight if tight hole or ledges
  are encountered.

  M/U cementing head. Load the plugs. Company representative is to witness the
    loading. Test the lines to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.

   Cement casing as per program. Any minor adjustment regarding the corrected depth
    will be done in due time. Displace with mud. Monitor for pressures. Record and
    report the final pressure. Bleed off pressure. Check for back flow. If any back flow
    observed, hold the pressure inside for the cement to thick.


Note:
- Based on the results obtained from cementing, decision will be made to perform top-up

         Approx. ±354m (±30 Joints) 7”, 29 ppf, N-80, New Vam liner.
          (Est. length for each joint of liner=10m). CSF: 1.38 ~ 64.7 pcf
         Hydraulic set liner hanger + liner top packer (if available).
          Liner setting sleeve C/W liner setting tool connected
          5”, 19.5 lb/ft, drill pipe to surface.


      Centralizers:

  - 1 Bow centralizers for the first 5 joints.
  - 1 Bow centralizer per 3 joint for the rest of the open hole.
  - 1 Bow centralizer per 5 joint for the rest liner joints inside 9 5/8” casing
  - Use Rigid centralizer on the third and forth joint from the top.
  Place each centralizer between stop collar and casing collar. Do not place
  centralizers over casing collars

1. After making up the shoe track, lower through rotary table and check that the
   floats are holding. Fill up with mud and pick up to check float functionality.
   Circulate for 10 min.
2. Run liner in at controlled speed to minimize pressure surges (approx. 12
   joints/hr). Check returns constantly.
3. Fill up each liner joint whilst running in the hole. Fill up liner completely every
  10 joints run.
4. When the required numbers of liner joints have been made up, make up the
   liner hanger & liner top packer, running tool as per liner hanger engineer’s
   instructions.
5. Use Liner setting tool retrievable pack off assembly for positive cement job.
6. Circulate 120% of the liner capacity. (Record Liner weight)
7. Run the liner with 5” drill pipe to the casing shoe. Circulate a minimum of one
   string capacity before running into open hole, RIH in the open hole at 4-5
   min/stand. Liner hanger engineer should be on rig floor while running liner
   into open hole.(check the loss while RIH if need decrease the speed of the
  RIH).SLM & drift the drill pipe while RIH the liner.
8. Work through any tight spots with maximum 50 klbs slack off / over pull and
  wash down  if necessary. Establish full circulation slowly prior to washing
  down.  Immediately call the WSS and Tool pusher to the rig floor, if there is
   tight hole.
Note: before loading the drill pipe wiper plug try to pass it from safety valve.
