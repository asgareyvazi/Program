 A)    Preparation Before Job
1. While Tripping:
     ▪ Use Casing Dope on Box of Each Joint When All Casings Are on Racks (To Avoid Drop Brush Inside Casing on Rig Floor
         by Crew Negligence).
     ▪ Ask Tool Pusher to Prepare 20” Casing Running Requirements Including 20” *5” C-Plate, 4 ½” IF Circulation Swage
     ▪ Ask Tool Pusher to Prepare 21 1/4"-2K * 21 1/4"-2K Double Studded Tie Down Flange + 21 1/4"-2K Spacer Spool + 21
          1/4"-2K Annular Preventer.
     ▪ Check BOP Career Performance Before Job & Repair It, If It Has Any Problem.
     ▪ Don't Forget to Call All Related Services (Casing Running Crew, Wellhead Crew, Pump Truck) In Exact Time.
     ▪  Pick Up Circulating Head, Cement Stinger, C-Plate and Place in Corner of Rig Floor.
     ▪ Before R/U Casing Running Equipment, Be Sure About Stabbing Board Installation & Check It’s Ready or Not by Casing
         Running Crew.
     ▪ R/U Casing Running Equipment at Proper time, to start Running Operation ASAP.

2. When Out of The Hole:
     ▪ Ensure Hole Is Full. Monitor the Fluid level in well.
     ▪ Change Top Drive Links to Longer. Change Right & Left Tong Also Change Master Bushing to Proper Size.
     ▪ Install 20” Side Door Elevator
     ▪ Clean Up Floor Before Commence Casing Running.

 D)   Contingency / First Action Plans
•  Keep in Mind That the Slurries Have Thixotropic Properties (High YP’s) So It Is Important to Keep Pumping, Even If Only at A Low Rate. Once
   Pumping Has Stopped It Will Be Difficult to Get the Slurries to Move Again.
•  Closely Monitor Drags. Drags Usually Increase as More Casing Is Run in To the Open Hole, A Normal Incremental Trend Should
   Be Observable for DRAG Parameters. If A Departure from This Trend Is Observed and Drags, Or Pick Up Force of String After Fill
  Up or Connection, Begin to Become Excessive, Following Actions Are Suggested:
    ▪  Move string upward 4-5m.
    ▪   Slowly Reciprocate the string to determine if the drags reduce. Do not push the string downward if excessive drags still exist

 E)   Safety Recommendation:
 WORK SAFE; ALWAYS ASK YOURSELF HOW CAN I GET HURT IF I DO THIS TASK.
•   Safety Standards and Policy of PECO Should Be Followed During Drilling Operation.
•   In Case of Any Deviation or Advise for This Instruction Call Co. Man ASAP.
•    All Rig Crew Should Follow and Cooperate with Job Specialists as Per This Instruction.
•  Mud Eng. Is Responsible for Monitoring & Report Pit Levels During Operation.
•   There Is Always Time to Rig Up and Operate Safely: Take “Time-Out”, If Necessary.
•   Pre- Plan the Job Ahead, Do Not Run Afterwards.
•  Make Sure All Areas Involved in Casing Transport and Handling Are Secured to Avoid Unauthorized Entry into Areas.
•   Focus on Falling Objects:
       ▪   Avoid Loose Items on Rig-Floor and All Decks Below;
       ▪    All Equipment Used Above 2m Has to Be Secured with Wire.
•   Before Starting the Job, Cementing Supervisor Should Check Place of Dumping & Washing the Truck Required After the Job with
   HSE Officer and Company Man to Eliminate Any Environmental Impact.
•   Flush and Pump Out Cellar. Dry Up Cellar as Much as Possible for Safety While Welding.
