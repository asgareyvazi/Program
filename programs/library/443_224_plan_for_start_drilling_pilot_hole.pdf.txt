Objectives: preparation for drilling

             1. Hold on safety meeting before start job.
             2. RIH 12-1/4” bit to measure the sea level and tag sea bed and record & report.
             3. ROV check the sea bed around well location and must on position to watch everything.
             4. Prepare TGB and run it by specialist guide to sea bed.
             5. Mark the guide lines for further operation.
             6. Pick up and Make up 12-1/4” string as per last well BHA.function test LWD.
             7. RIH to tag sea bed and check sea bed. Inform the standby boat following shallow gas
               procedure.
             8. Start drilling pilot hole and continue to 860 mt.
             9. While drilling pump hi-vis 50 bbl per half stand and take survey each stand.
           10. All time while drilling must check by ROV and LWD about flow & shallow gas.
           11. Stop drilling flow check displace sea water in hole by 16 ppg new mud (red color).
           12. Continue observe well for return of red color mud (show well hold 15.8 ppg CMT slurry
              without loss).
           13. If everything was OK continue drilling to 1410 mt to finish pilot hole
