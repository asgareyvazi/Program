
14 1/2” HOLE SECTION DRILLING PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
PU 12 1/4” bit and 14 1/2” BHA and TIH.
Before going through stack, close shear rams and test casing.
Continue TIH, Set wear bushing in well head with FMC stabilizer.
TIH to top of float collar at 11,625’.
Wash/drill cement, float collar, rat hole, and 10’ new formation to 11,836’ with reamer closed.  Take SPR’s & CLFP’s  before drilling new formation.  While drilling cement, use conservative parameters (minimum flow rates of 600 GPM with 40-50 rotary RPM).  Be aware that Rhino reamers can become stuck in the shoe track due to pack-off while drilling out (close OD/ID tolerances).  Raise mud weight to 12.1 ppg while drilling cement.
Circulate bottoms up above the wellhead and boost out. Get static equivalent mud wt from PWD. Downlink power drive while circulating.
Pull back to shoe.
Line up to pump down the kill line and drill pipe and take pressures up the choke.  Perform shoe test.  Pump up MWD max reading from LOT.
Drill ahead from 11,836’ to 11,882’.  This will position the Rhino reamer 10 feet below the 13 3/8” shoe. Drop 2” ball and shear pins in reamer (ball will drop at 1000’/min).  Bring up pumps to deploy reamer blades a few feet below the shoe.  Verify reamer is open by bumping the shoe.
Drill 12 1/4” X 14 ½” hole to 13,699’.  Do not allow ECD’s to exceed 0.2 ppg from LOT.  Make sure that the ESD is 0.2 ppg greater than the estimated pore pressure.  Pump sweeps as needed. Drill interval at approximately 200 ft/hr instantaneous.  Backream each stand to allow hole to clean up.   Flow check every connection.  Take survey every stand.  NOTE:  DO NOT DRILL AT A REDUCED RATE WITHOUT NOTIFYING THE COMPANY MAN.  Time and log flowbacks to develop a trend as well as on/off bottom torque readings.  Communication between mudloggers, shakers, and the rig floor is critical.  Immediately report any variation in trends to the rig floor.
CBU, POOH to 13 3/8” shoe  working any tight spots.
Slip and Cut drill line.
TIH to bottom and circulate hole clean for logging.
POOH to run wire line log.
