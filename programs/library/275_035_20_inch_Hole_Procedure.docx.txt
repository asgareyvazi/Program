
20” HOLE SECTION PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
Make up kick-off assembly and TIH to top of cement (look for cement around 8567’).  Drill out cement and sidetrack as per directional driller.
Drill 10’ of new formation.  Get static equivalent mud wt from PWD. Circulate above BOP’s, Perform shoe test to a max of 11.7 ppg equivalent mw.  Pump up MWD max reading from LOT.  Take LOT down C&K and drill pipe.
Drill cement and 20” hole to 9840’.  Do not allow ECD’s to exceed 0.3 ppg from LOT.  Pump 75 bbl sweeps every other stand. Drill interval at approximately 150 ft/hr instantaneous, 75-100 ft/hr real time. Backream each stand to allow hole to clean up.   Flow check every connection.  Mud Engineers to treat mud as needed, allow mud weight to come back to 10.8 ppg.  Take survey every stand.
CBU, POOH to 16” profile.  Drop dart to activate PBL sub and wash profile. (May evaluate need for wiper trip).
POOH to run 16” casing.
