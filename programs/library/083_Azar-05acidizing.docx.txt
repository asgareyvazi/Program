## Reservoir Properties
Reservoir Lithology
Ilam Formation comprises of buff, yellowish grey, pale yellowish brown and light olive grey micritic, argillaceous, glauconitic and locally pyretic limestone interceded with dark. Green grey, dark brown to dark grey marl and clay stone in upper part. Buff, cream to very light grey clean, moderate hard limestone in lower part. Presence of micro pyrite as lamination/ nodule/pellet/aggregate in olive grey to pale grey limestone reported (about5 to 10m below the top of Ilam Formation) could be consider as a key bed. Ilam Formation contains both pelagic and neritic faces.
Reservoir Temperature and Pressure
Reservoir Temperature: 107ºC@ 4025mMSL (Highest recorded Temp. from well test)
Reservoir Pressure: 8754 psi@4025mMSL

## Stimulation Objectives
Stimulation of reservoir by raising formation permeability and relieving formation damage.
Focusing on obtaining commercial production rate.
Learning of Acid Stimulation experience for other well’s job.

## Challenge and Strategy
Because of the peculiar formation and well conditions, the Challenges of Acidizing Stimulation are presented as following:
Micro-fractures and wormholes are very important to produce oil in the carbonate reservoir. The reservoir is strong heterogeneity along the interval and how to stimulate the zone with lower permeability is a huge challenge. Composite diverting technologies will be tempered
The pump rates and volumes required to achieve a reasonable penetration in these thick treatment intervals shall be high.
Low-friction and retarded acid required to reduce well head pressure and protect string and other equipment and the low corrosion for acid to iron are real challenges.

# Acid Fluid and Other Materials Preparation
Acidizing Design Principle
Following measures can be adopted in order to enhance the success rate, efficiency of acidizing and achieve a good effect on increasing productivity.
Matrix acidizing may satisfy the requirement, 15% HCl will  be selected.
Select appropriate acid formula. The formula contains corrosion inhibitor, high efficient iron control agent, good multi-function surfactant and good anti-sludge agent.
Acid Recipe and Volume
Main Acid Recipe: 15%HCl+1%Corrosion Inhibitor+3% Corrosion Inhibitor intensifier+1%Iron Control+1%Multi-function Surfactant+1% De-emulsion+1% Anti-sludge Agent+1%H2S scavenger+0.2%friction reducer.
SDVES Acid Recipe: 20%HCl+1% Corrosion Inhibitor for VES +8% Self diverting Surfactant+2% Iron Control +0.2%friction reducer.
Isolation Fluid： (Asphalt Solvent) 20bbl.

# Operation Procedures
General Requirements
Provide a coordinator to integrate all activities of acidizing and well testing engineering.
The well site access road must be suitable for acid stimulation treatment equipment entry and exit.
The well site must be level and smooth, can stand with the weight of all equipment.
Check all equipment on arrival at the well site to make sure no damage/missing items. Pay special attention to the crossovers to ensure the threads are compatible.
All the tubing should be cleaned.
Prepare enough fresh water in well site for mixing acid fluid and brine.
Prepare adequate chemicals for mixing of brine and acid fluid.
Preparation work before operation
Move all the equipment and accessories to well site and set them in place according to attached equipment layout.
Hold safety meeting with all personnel involved.
Cordon off all high-pressure area with signal tape and forbid anyone but operators in high-pressure area.
Check that all wellhead valves are closed.
Rig up all equipment with accessories to X-mas tree according to attached equipment layout. All the high pressure equipment should connected isolation valve. Connect one pump truck to THS wing valve for balance pressure.
Pressure test injection line up to 7000psi for 10minutes to make sure it is qualified; bleed off directly to waster pit.
Pressure test balance pressure line up to 3000psi; bleed off directly to waster pit.
Carry out acid job as below.

# Job Risk Analysis
Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)
Fresh water and bottled neutralizer shall be available at the worksite.
In the event of acid contact with the eyes, immediately flushes eyes gently with fresh water and consult a doctor as soon as practicable.
Acid contacts the skin, wash the area thoroughly with fresh water.
Remove clothing contaminated with acid solutions or additive solutions.
This clothing should not be re-worn until thoroughly washed.
High pressure
Enclose the high pressure area with warning line;
Plot the dangerous area with warning plate;
Keep all personnel but operators far away from high pressure area while operation;
Use safety rope between the pipe line connections in case of disconnection;
The pump should be shut down immediately once the pipe line bursts.
H2S
Portable H2S detector must be inspected annually.
All Air respirators must be inspected function.
Everyone on location should use H2S detector.
For the places where H2S may concentrate must have ventilation equipment.
Wind-vane must be obvious and showed to all personnel before entering well site.
All personnel must be trained of H2S contingency plan and the use of all related equipment.

# QHSE Requirements
QHSE requests
Irrelevant personnel are strictly prohibited to enter into the construction area.
All personnel on well-site should be equipped with full PPE, including overall, safety boots, helmet, goggle, gloves etc.
Pre-job safety meeting must be held and recorded, which should contain operation objective, operation procedures, potential risk analysis, job assignment, emergency program etc.
Smoking and calling are strictly prohibited on well-site.
During transporting and making up fluid, fluid should not be spilt out of the tank. Residual liquid and chemicals are forbidden to be dumped anywhere other than designated place.
Hoisting equipment shall be guide by director.
Adequate fire engines shall standby on site and sufficient firefighting equipment shall be located in designated position before operation.
First aid box shall be equipped on site with sufficient medicines.
During pressure test, high-pressure region is forbidden to enter in.
Placement of equipment should be complied with location standards.
Ensure an emergency eye washes station and shower station is available
Well site and personnel entry should be controlled in accordance with H2S operation procedure.
All jobs should be full time supervised by a SIPC Well Services supervisor on location.
PPE
Minimum PPE requirements for all personnel working in the vicinity of the pumping operation shall be available (Hard Hat, Chemical Safety Goggles or Face Shield, Safety Boots, Gloves, including rubber gloves for handling acids, work gloves for rigging up/down equipment).
Personnel involved in the handling of acid shall be equipped with full protective clothing. This shall consist of Respirator and Mask, Full length rubber gloves (no pin holes), Rubber apron, Rubber safety boots.
Requirements for safe operation
Acidizing operations shall comply with the following:
A dedicated line shall be used to run the pumping unit to the well. The transfer and pumping lines shall be properly secured with snubbing lines.
Under no circumstances hammers on leaking connections whilst pumping acid. The lines shall be pressure tested prior to starting the acid job.
A pressure relief valve shall be installed on the pumping unit with the vent directed away from all personnel. The vent area should be flagged off. A non-return valve shall be placed in the pumping line as close as possible to the well.
Be aware of the risk of reaction gases evolved during the wash (primarily CO2) unloading the well as they near surface.
Due to the risk of contamination and splashing personnel, live acid shall not be returned into open top fluid tanks. All acid returned from the well shall be regarded as live acid.
Extreme care must be taken during high pressure pumping while conducing acid stimulation treatments as unexpected leaks on surface lines can develop which could release the HCl acid as a fine spray. HCl acid can vaporize in air to form a noticeable cloud if released to the atmosphere. If the vapor cloud is concentrated enough it can be toxic until sufficiently dispersed.
Job planning
Acidizing operations should not proceed in the dark unless there is adequate lighting available.
The acids used, hydrochloric acid (HCl) is a toxic and dangerous chemical. Refer to the MSDS for details of handling, storage and disposal precautions.
Only essential personnel shall be allowed in the vicinity of the acid handling equipment. Every effort shall be made, particularly thorough pre-job planning, to ensure that minimum intervention by personnel is required during the acidizing.
Safety barriers shall be used as appropriate to isolate the areas of potential hazard.
Fresh water shall be available around the pump truck. A safety shower or alternative option for washing off contamination should be available nearby.
All equipment must be operated by well-trained operators, and under the supervisor’s monitor.
