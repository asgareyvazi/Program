
17” HOLE SECTION DRILLING PROCEDURE
Goldfinger
PU 10 5/8” bit and 17” BHA and TIH until BHA is clear of test rams.
Test rams and annulars to 250/4,920 psi. Test top drive valves to 7,500 psi.
Slip and Cut drill line.
TIH to top of cement (Float collar is at 9,673’).  Be careful going through 16” liner top @ 8,526’.
Wash/drill cement, float collar, rat hole, and 10’ new formation to 9,850’.  While drilling cement, use conservative parameters (minimum flow rates of 600 GPM with 40-50 rotary RPM).  Watch torque and have patience, remember the last 16” casing string was backed off above the float collar.  Raise mud weight to 11.6 ppg while drilling out.  Record the amount of metal shavings recovered from the magnets.
Take SPR’s & CLFP’s
Get static equivalent mud wt from PWD. Circulate bottoms up above BOP’s, line up to pump down the kill line and drill pipe and take pressures up the choke.  Perform shoe test to leak off  (should be around 12.7 – 13  ppg equivalent mw).  Pump up MWD max reading from LOT.
Drill 17” hole 12,000’ if LOT allows, otherwise will drill to 10,500’ and set expandable liner.  Do not allow ECD’s to exceed 0.3 ppg from LOT.  Pump 75 bbl weighted sweeps every other stand or more if hole is not cleaning up. Drill interval at approximately 150 ft/hr instantaneous, 75-100 ft/hr real time. Backream each stand at 40 RPM and ream back down to allow hole to clean up.   Flow check every connection.  Take survey every stand.  NOTE:  DO NOT DRILL AT A REDUCED RATE WITHOUT NOTIFYING THE COMPANY MAN.  Be aware that a pressure ramp is anticipated at ~10,750’.  Time and log flowbacks to develop a trend as well as on/off bottom torque readings.  Communication between mudloggers, shakers, and the rig floor is critical.  Immediately report any variation in trends to the rig floor.
CBU, POOH to 16” shoe @ 9,818’ working any tight spots.  TIH to bottom and circulate hole clean.
POOH to run casing. Will have to pull NBP and wash well head prior to running 13 5/8”.
