1  Introduction

  1.1    Program Introduction
         This program covers the drilling of well#23 in Naft Shahr oil field, Iran. The well is
          intended to produce oil from Asmari formation. The well will be drilled vertically to a
         planed total depth of 1018m (-820m), ±58m inside Asmari Formation.
        The primary objectives of drilling the well NSH 23 are:

            • To gather more information that allows optimising future plan for the next wells.
            • To produce oil and maintain the production rate of the field.

         Note-1: The exact TD will be determined based on the logging results.
         Note-2: This program has been written with respect to the letter (Geological Forecast)
         No.:  ٧٨ / م زن / ٩٣٤٤٠٨ dated: ٧٨٣١ / ٢١ / ٨٢ .

  1.2     Naft Shahr Structure

         Naft Shahr is an asymmetrical anticline with North West – South East axis trend. The
          dip of structure in the south west is more than the other parts. A thrust fault has
          crossed the Gachsaran Fm and caused the thickness of Gachsaran Fm to be increased
           in south west flank of the structure.
        The approximate coordinates of NAFT SHAHR-23 are:

       UTM (International)       : E: 547, 080.5m       N: 3, 760, 949.5 m

       UTM (WGS84)                : E: 547, 077.72m      N: 3, 760,773m

  10 Geological Overview


INTRODUCTION

Naft Shahr structure is located in Lurestan area at the west and southwest of Darvana and is a shared
field with Iraq country. It is a geophysical structure with northwest - southeast trend with high
southwest dips. The Gachsaran Fm. in southwest flank is thicker than north part probably due to a
thrust fault effects.
Naft Shahr field was discovered with commercial hydrocarbour in Asmari reservoir and developed
to 22nd well in same. Well # 23 is situated on the southeast part of the field about 600m. southeast of
well#13.  It will be drilled vertically to total depth of 1018 (-820)m in Asmari Fm. Reservoir
evaluation, XPT and FMI logs are planned in this well.
The south flank wells in Naft Shahr field had drilling problem. Oil & gas kick was tested in NSH#13
while drilling at 162.5m. depth in Aghajari Fm. and was cured with increasing mud weight to
maximum 128 pcf. NSH#23 is closed to NSH#13, therefore the same problem may happened
probably.

STRATIGRAPHY OF NAFT SHAHR STRUCTURE
The stratigraphic sequence in Naft Shahr#23 will be nearly normal from surface (Aghajari Fm.) to
total depth of 1018m with exception of members 7 to 3 of Gachsaran Formation that probably can
not be recognized same as Well#13
The detailed lithologies of this stratigraphy succession are as follows:

Aghajari Formation (surface – 369m.)
This Formation consists of light grey – grey Sandstone, light brown Siltstone, red brown Claystone
with some white - off white Anhydrite beds. In the lower part of this Formation reduced from the
Sandstone and increased of Claystone sediments.

Gachsaran Formation (369 – 960m.)
The Gachsaran Formation consists of grey and red - bluish green – bluish grey Marl, white - off
white Anhydrite, Salt and some beds of Limestone. The Gachsaran Formation  is divided to
following members:

Members: 7 – 3 (369 – 916m.):
These members consist of red, bluish green, grey, bluish grey Marl and white to off white Anhydrite
with some Limestone in the upper part and interbeds of Anhydrite and grey Marl, with some Salt and
red Marl beds in the lower part.
Member - 2 (916 – 931m.):
This member consists of thick bed of Salt and a red Marl bed near top of this member.

Cap rock (Member - 1) (931 – 960m.):
This member consists of an Anhydrite bed as key bed: "A" in the top, a grey marl bed in the top
overlaid a Dolomite bed as key bed: "B" (G2) and some carbonate beds (Dolomite & Limestone) as
key beds: C, D, E & F that are interbeds and separated with some anhydrite beds.

Asmari Formation (960 to total Depth of 1018m. )
The Asmari Formation mainly consists of dolomitic Limestone, cream – light cream with some
hydrocarbon.

                    Sequence Of Drilling Operation



1.3  Sequence of Operation

     •  Make up 26” BHA as per BHA design, drill formation down to 100m (20" casing
         point in Aghajari formation). Pump LCM pill  if encountered heavy losses. Set
        cement plug(s) if couldn’t cure the loss with LCM pill.
     Note:
       - Drill 2 meters more than the planned casing setting depth and fix the TD with tally.
       - Drop TOTCO and record the deviation angle prior to POOH to run casing.
       - Do not forget to strap pipes while POOH.
       - Apply following parameters in drilling this section:
        -WOB = Maximum available
         -RPM = 90-130
        -GPM = 600-1000

     •  At TD, Circulate, clean hole. POOH, P/U 26” N.Bit stabilizer or 26” Reamer, RIH
        and condition hole to section TD. Check for any fill. CBU on bottom.

     •  POOH. If observed any indication of drag, run back to bottom. Wash and ream to
       make sure that the hole condition is good.

     •  R/U 20" casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

     •  Run 20", 106.5 ppf, K-55, BTC casing with stab in type shoe to bottom as per
         casing program. Shoe should be set 2 meters off bottom and have enough stick up
         (1.5 m) above rig floor. Fill casing by fresh mud (NO LCM) joint by joint.

     Note:
       - Do not exert much load and compressing stress on the shoe if encountered tight hole
      and ledges.

    •  Run stinger on 5” DP’s using the “C” Plate & 5” x 30” centralizer. Sting in. M/U &
           test cementing lines. Circulate for one casing annular volume. While circulating,
        check the DP’s annulus for any return. Apply all the drill pipe weight on the stinger
          to stop seepage.

    •  Perform casing cement job as per program. Minor volumetric adjustments regarding
         the confirmed TD and shoe depth will be done in due time. Displace cement. Sting
          out. Check the float. Circulate to clean the DP. POOH.

     •  Complementary cementing might be  required  if observed no cmt.  return  in
        performing the primary job. If so, pump cement slurries with accelerating agents in
         stages and  fill up the annulus. (Add 2% calcium chloride  to  accelerate the
         thickening time of cement slurry). Ensure of connecting line pipe to cement line
        with appropriate X-over. Wait for the top job cement to get hard.

2.3  Sequence of Operations

     •  Make up 17 ½” BHA as per BHA Design (Junk Sub Included). RIH, Tag top of
        cement. Drill out cement & shoe and rat hole cement. (Mud type: Hi vis bentonite
            pill).

     •   Drill ahead with hi vis Bentonite pill down to 124m (24m bolow 20" casing shoe).

      •  Take special care for abnormal torques in drilling the shoe. This will minimize the
          risk of shoe back off. Report abnormalities to the base immediately.

     •  Displace mud in hole with 75pcf salt saturated WBM.

     •   Drill ahead 17 ½” hole section down to Casing Point @ 450m, inside Gachsaran
       Fm.

        For more details Ref. to "General Information", item 7-1

       Use following parameters to drill this hole section:

         -WOB = 40- 65 klb
          -RPM = 90-130
          -GPM= 800-1000

     Note:
       - Adjust mud weight with respect to the hole condition and as per offset wells
       experiences.
       - Drop Totco on each bit change trip.
       - Drill 2 meters more than the planned casing setting depth and fix the TD with tally.
       - The Section TD maybe changed based on formation tops detected by the well site
        geologist.
       - Do not forget to strap pipe while POOH.

     •  At section TD, circulate, clean hole. Perform wiper trip. Leave the hole for one hour
        and observe same @ 20” shoe. RIH. Check for fills or any minor kick. CBU on

     •  POOH. Perform wiper trips in case of drag. Wash and ream to make sure of a good

     •  R/U casing running gear. Perform Safety meeting.

        bottom and have enough stick up (1.5 m) above rig floor. Fill casing by fresh mud
      (NO LCM) every 10 joints. (Float collar should be installed one joint above the
         shoe).

                    Sequence Of Drilling Operation

     •  M/U cementing head. Load the plugs. WSS should witness the loading. Test the
          lines to 3000 psi for 10 minutes.

     •  Cement casing  as per program. Minor volumetric adjustments regarding  the
        confirmed TD and shoe depth will be done in due time. Displace with mud. Monitor
         the pressures. Record and report the final pressure before bumping the plug. Bump
         the plug with 500 psi over the final circulating pressure or to 1500 psi max. Bleed
          off pressure. Check for back flow. Observed any back flow, hold the pressure inside
         the casing and let the cement thick.

     •  Hold on casing string weight. Drop 21 ¼” x 13 3/8” casing hanger. Slack off casing
         weight. Test same to 500 psi pressure. (Open base flange side outlet valves while
          testing).

     •  P/U 21 ¼”-2000 BOP’s, Rough cut 13 3/8” landing joint. L/D same and BOP’s.
        Dress and bevel 13 3/8” casing neck.

     •   Install 21 ¼” x 2000 - 13 5/8” x 3000 psi Casing Head spool. Pack & Test p-seals
        with 1500 psi.

     •   Install 13 5/8”x3000 - 13 5/8” x 5000 psi tie down flange. N/U 13 5/8” x 5000 psi

     Important note:

     The following program should be considered as a general guideline in performing
       the cementing job of the casings. There might be minor variations in cement slurry
       properties regarding the composition of field drill water, bulk cement nature and
       purity of the additives. It is recommended to perform pilot tests prior to cmt jobs.

      For cementing operation, follow this procedure:
        ¾ M/U Cement Stinger C/W spring bow 20” x 5” centralizer and RIH to 10m
                above the shoe using the C-plate.
        ¾  Circulate 60 bbls of mud and wash down to flush the top of the stab in shoe
               from any debris.
        ¾  Flush and  fill surface lines with water and pressure test to 3,000psi for 5
                 minutes prior to start of the cement job.
        ¾  Stab in to the shoe. Set down string weight.
        ¾  Circulate mud clean casing annulus. Observe DP’s annulus for any leakage.
        ¾ Pump spacer, Mix and pump cement as per program.
        ¾  All samples of mix water and slurry should be stored until the success of the
                cement job has been verified, then they may be discarded.
        ¾  Displace cement with mud. Displace for 7 bbls (2 bbls Short).
        ¾  Stop pumping, sting out, flush pipe, check for float, POOH. L/D stinger, wait
               on cement to get hard.
        ¾  All samples of mix water and slurry should be stored until the success of the
                cement job has been verified, then they may be discarded.
