The main headlines of this program are as follows:


1.  1. Move rig and spud over well NTH-4.

2.  Record wellhead and Tubing Ann. Pressures and hook up Kill lines on THS & X-tree

  & P/T same.

3.    Kill and secure well by sea water, N/D X-mass tree, N/U BOP stack and test same.

4.  While circulate cross wellhead POOH completion string and stand back tubing in

    derrick and lay down ESP.

5.  Clean well to 5" liner shoe.

6.  Perform anti scaling / stimulation plan as per IOOC production Engineering dept.

   program.

7.  Observe well.

8. Run new ESP completion string as per completion engineering plan. (The ESP

   completion string detail will be issued in due time).

9. N/D BOP, N/U X-mass tree.

10. Secure well, Release Rig.



Attentions:


•   The detailed program  is available in this document under the heading


    “Well Securing Procedure” & “Workover Program”.



•    All depths are referred to RKB-MSL: 29.2m which should be corrected


     with new RKB-MSL measurement of active rig at time.

 3. Drilling History


   Well NT-4 is located in Nosrat field, in the southern part of Persian Gulf. The
    original NTH-4 well was spud on December 11th, 1985 from the fixed Nosrat
    platform by IOOC to produce Oil from Mishrif member. This well was originally
    drilled directionally by Rajaee rig and reached to total depth 2775.6m MD.
   maximum hole inclination was recorded 16 degree at 1631.9m MD. 30", 20, 13
    3/8" & 9 5/8" casing were run  to 128.2, 362.4, 1444.2 & 2774.2m MD
    respectively. This well was completed using an ESP on May 7th, 1986.

    This well was re-entered from 21st August 2001 to 17th November 2001 in Mishrif
    (Sarvak) reservoir by Sakhalinskaya rig (RKB-MSL: 29.2m) in two horizontal
    legs. TD of Leg #1 @ 3306.3m MD & TD of Leg #2 @ 3172.3 m MD, Maximum
    hole inclination Leg-1 & Leg-2 were 91.77 & 88.60 degree respectively. Set a
   whipstock at 2250.5m MD and opened window from 2244 to 2247.9m MD. 7"
   and 5" liner were set at intervals 2124 (L.Lap)-2873.8  , 2836 (L.Lap) -2940m
   MD(shoe) respectively. This well was worked over several times primarily to
   change ESP.

8.  Well Operation Procedure

    1. Move and approach rig alongside Nosrat platform in Nosrat field.

    2.  Jack up and skid mast over well No NTH-4, Record & report, air gap, & RKB

        to THF and MSL.

    3.  Record wellhead and Tubing Ann. Pressures

    4. Hook up lines on Ann. & TBG. P/T same to 3000psi.

    5.  Kill well by seawater & confirm well is static or under loss condition.

      Note: Seawater impose 1153 psi over pressure to the Mishrif Reservoir

   @ 5" liner shoe 2940m MD, 2620.9m TVD).

    6.  Close SSSV and confirm  its functioning,  install BPV in tubing hanger.

       Function test apply pressure in control line and function test 3SV.

    7.  While circulate cross well head N/D X-Mass tree, check tubing hanger thread

     N/U BOP and test same to 3000psi.

    8. Make up 3 1/2" EUE landing joint inside tubing hanger, unset packer by

       applying over pull.

      Note: Keep 3-1/2" spear available on well site in case of tubing hanger

      thread damage.

    9.  While circulate cross wellhead POOH completion string and stand back

       tubing in derrick and lay down ESP.

    10. RIH w/6 " bit and 7" casing scraper and 9 5/8" casing scraper (tandem). clean

       well to 2836m MD (5" L. lap).

    11. RIH w/4 1/8" bit or econo mill and clean well to 2940m MD (5" liner shoe).

    12. Perform anti scaling / stimulation plan as per IOOC production Engineering

        dept. program.

    13. Observe well, and make sure of well condition.

    14. Run ESP completion string as per completion engineer instructions.
