                        PARS POOLAD AGHAEI CO.
                                          Procedure of Running S.N Packer with
                      Sop title
                                                  Mechanical Released
                      Sop number       PPA/TBS         Application date  Dec 2020
  PARS POOLAD AGHAEI    Revision           00                Validity date     3 years

➢   RIH with Seal Nipple Packer on drill pipe to 10 ft above TOL. RIH speed 2-3

     minutes per stand (use drill collar for put weight on packer).

➢    At liner top break circulation on for 15 mins at 2-3 BPM.

➢    Stop circulation and record pickup / slack off weights.

➢    Circulate at 1 BPM and slowly lower string while monitoring pressure until

      pressure increase is indicated.

➢    Entire the first O-ring seal nipple.

➢   Be sure a guide of packer was inside tie back.

➢   Enter packer completely inside tie back.

➢   Turn off pump and release pressure.

➢   Slack off to~20,000 lbs and wait for 15 minutes to ensure good packer

     element packing. Watch for shear indication on weight indicator.

➢   Return to neutral weight, close BOP hydrill and test packer from annulus at

     500-1500 psi.

➢   Bleed off pressure, continue slacking off weight to ~ 40,000 lbs to set the

       slips packer. Watch for shear indication on weight indicator.

➢   Over pull 20,000 lbs above pick up weight. If over pull is achieved, this

     indicates that the packer is set. If while during over pull there is any upward

    movement at all, stop immediately and re-apply weight on packer.

➢   Set down 10,000 lbs at the setting tool, and release the setting tool with 25

      right hand turns at the tool. If torque is not transferred due to inclination

    and drag torque the pipe – hold the TDS brakes and work the string to

     transfer the torque downhole.

➢  POOH without any overpull on weight indicator.

➢  POOH to surface.
