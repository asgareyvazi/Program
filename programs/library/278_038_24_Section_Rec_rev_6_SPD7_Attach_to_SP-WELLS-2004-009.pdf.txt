          No cutting sampling will be required in the 24” section.

3     Operational Sequence

             1. MU 24” motor/ MWD BHA on 5 1/2” DP and RIH
             2.  Perform H2S drill. Test fill-up device, camera and H2S sensor on TDAD.
             3. RIH with 1st BHA w/1.5 deg AKO motor and drill to top Jahrum (do not drill into Jah-
              rum)
             4.  Circulate and POOH
             5. RIH with 2nd BHA, a rotary hold/build BHA with MWD tool
             6.  Perform H2S meeting before drilling into Jahrum Fm.
             7.  Drill 24” hole to TD
             8.  Perform wiper trip one single into Asmari and back to TD
             9.  Circulate and displace to un-weighted hi-vis mud to top of Jahrum Fm
            10. POOH
            11. Run and cement 18 5/8” casing. Fill every joint with SW to avoid collapse of casing
            12. Test casing to 113 bar when bumping plug with sea water
            13. Perform a top up cement job in annulus as soon as possible

4      Directional Drilling

        4.1     Potential Well Interference
               • BHI will provide travelling cylinder plots.

        4.2   BHA
               • The plan is to drill the section with 2 BHA runs
               • The first BHA will be an 11 ¼” AKO motor assembly with a 1.5 deg bend.
               • No accelerator in 1st BHA
               • The plan is to build up to an inclination of +/-18 deg. On top of jahrum formation
                 Lead angle for right hand walk will be 25 deg.
               • The second BHA will be a rotary BHA:
                  23” string stabilizer, shock sub, 16” Roller Reamer and MWD-DIR.
               •  All BHA components and DP to be drifted to 2 5/8”
               • Never run Jar/Accelerator in neutral point.

        4.3     Survey
               • A BHI Sigma kick off gyro will be used together with an UBHO in the first BHA
               • Take gyro survey each stand drilled during kick off
               • When drilling close to live wells, the survey interval should be (according to
                WR0529):
                                  ·    10 m, when Edge Distance is less than 3 m
                                  ·    30 m, when Edge Distance is between 3 m and 15 m
                 The Edge Distance is the difference between the actual well separation and
                     the minimum allowed distance.
               • The gyro will be changed after 3 shots or max 60m to check gyro for errors
               • 1 gyro stations will be taken inside the 26” Conductor.
               • Run gyro until the well is reaching approximately 5deg or no magnetic interference.
               • MWD survey every 30m and min every 100m.
               •  Focus on minimize the survey time to avoid getting stuck. Skip survey if necessary.

        4.4    Max hours on rotation BHA string components
               •  Jar:                200 rotation hrs or 2 wells, whichever comes first.
               •  Accelerator:        One Jahrum section.
               • X/O-DC:           250 rotation hrs.
               • DP-HWDP:         3 wells .
               • MWD:             150 rotation hrs or 2 wells, whichever comes first.
               •  16” RR:          Two wells

5     Casing and Cementing

        5.1     Casing

        •  18 5/8”: 99 ppf / X-56 / Antares ER/ Casing shoe with eccentric guide nose
        •   Fill casing while running
        •  Be aware of possible collapse during csg running and cementing due to low safety factor.
        •   If working casing down, never rotate the casing with weight on shoe. Work the casing
          up/down with the TDS unlocked. The shoe was twisted off in SPD9-13.
        •  Pressure test csg in steps:
           o  (40-80-113 bar) when bumping plug.
           o  Bleed off immediately if leakage.
           o  Bleed fast down to test float
        •   If plugging during casing cementing, do not exceed 140 bar displacement pressure
        •  Use RMS and Spider elevator. Oil and greases regularly to avoid jamming
        •  Evaluate use of La-Fleur circulation packer
        •   Install one cement basket with one rigid centralizer below, 10m below sea bed
        •  Max casing running speed: Unlimited

        5.2     Centralizers
      There will be no centralizers on the 18 5/8” casing string in open hole

        5.3    Cementing

         •   Circulation rate before cement job: Max 3000 LPM to avoid washing out float equeipment.
        •  Pump 30 m3 treated SW-5 m3 GG-5 m3 PHG-20 m3 treated SW -5 m3 GG -10 m3 PHG-80
        m3 treated SW
        •  Cementing, pump following:
           o  Lead: 15 m3 1.50 sg (Econolite/SW slurry)
           o  Tail: 15 m3 1.87 sg
         •  Displacement rate: 3000 LPM
         •   Displace cement with corrosion inhibited SW.

        5.4    Top-up job (To be performed off rigtime)
        •  Perform the job as soon as possible
        •  Run the spaghetti pipe to cement basket
        •   First job ,Pump 2 m3 neat 1.50 sg 2% CaCl2 cement slurry
        •  POOH and WOC for 12 hrs
        •  Second job ,Pump 3 m3 1,50 sg slurry 2% CaCl2
        •  POOH and WOC for 12 hrs
        •  Top up annulus with water to be able to monitor returns on next job
        •  Attempt to Top up with neat 1,9 sg slurry w /1% CaCl2 until cement returns to surface.
        •   If window in 26” CP is cut, close weld ASAP to avoid cross flow from neighbouring wells
