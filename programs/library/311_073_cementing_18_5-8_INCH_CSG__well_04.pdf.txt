                     DISTRIBUTION (check appropriate box)
COSL             CSG CREW                Scomi              CEMENT ENG
POGC           MUD LOGGING

Objectives: cementing 18 5/8” Casing

Planned Operations:
         1.
   1. Prepare all lines for cmt manifold and L/U to cmt unit.
   2. Hold pre-job safety meeting.
   3. Press test the line with 3000psi. Bleed-off  press to zero.
   4. Pump 20bbl drill water spacer by cmt unit.
   5. Drop bottom plug in the casing and open the cmt line on cmt manifold.
   6. Mix & pump 196.5 bbl of 12.5 ppg lead cmt slurry solution by cmt unit. Mixing
       fluid required for lead cmt slurry is 151bbl.
   7. Mix & pump 446.3bbl of 15.8 ppg  tail cmt slurry solution by cmt unit. Mixing
       fluid required for tail cmt slurry is 265 bbl .
   8. Note: Mud engineer to be near cmt unit to take the weight of slurry solution.
   9. Note: Shaker man to be on shakers and check for any return and inform
     company man.
   10. Drop top plug. Close cmt line and open displacement line on cmt unit.
   11. Pump 10bbl sea water by cmt unit.
   12. Switch to rig mud pumps and displace cmt with 806bbl (     stk) of sea water.
   13. At     stk reduce flow rate and bump the plug with 1200psi press.

   14. Note: Mud engineer and pump man should also check the volume os sea water
     which will be used for displacement.

   15. Hold press for 10min. bleed off press and check for back flow.
   16. Disconnect all lines and hoses F/cmt head.
   17. L/D cmt head.
   18. Release starting head running tool as per engineer instruction.
   19. Flush top of wellhead by sea water.
   20. POOH and L/D landing joint and running tool.
