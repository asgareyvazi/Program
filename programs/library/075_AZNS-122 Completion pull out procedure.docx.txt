Pull-out Short Procedure:
Check wellhead pressure.
Secure the well.
L/U Kill & Choke line to wellhead, L/U from pumping unit to Kill line & section C Gate valve.
NOTE: Record section C annulus pressure, if any pressure recorded L/U to annulus & bleed off pressure to Zero.
Remove X-mass Tree cap (3SV & X-mass Tree valve should be close).
R/U SL & perform all required pressure test.
Equalize top of 3SV with pump unit to shut-in wellhead pressure, Open 3SV & confirm 3SV is open.
RIH w/gauge cutter 3.5" to top of 3SV, POOH.
RIH & set Protection Sleeve on 3SV, POOH.
RIH w/gauge cutter 2.8" to top of SSD. If it is not possible to pass, follow contingency plan RIH w/CT & wash the obstruction with xylene till SSD depth & spud 3bbl xylene.
RIH w/Shifting tools, equalize annulus & string pressure by apply equivalent pressure in annulus, open SSD, POOH & R/D SL. If it is not possible to open SSD, RIH w/CT to top of No-Go & displace the well with heavy mud.
Displace the well with heavy mud thru annulus & get return fluid into separate storage tanks.
Monitor the well, In case of any pressure bullhead with maximum 1,000psi.
Set TWCV on TBG HGR, Perform TWCV pressure test with 5,000psi.
N/D X-mass Tree & N/U BOP, Perform BOP pressure test & retrieve TWCV.
RIH w/Landing JT (including TBG HGR Running tools 4-1/2" TPCQ box * 4" ACME left-hand pin, X-over 4-1/2" TPCQ pin * 4-1/2" IF box, 5" DPs Jt), Engage w/TBG HGR, POOH 1.5m & apply over-pull 100-130 klbs to unset the packer.
NOTE: Landing Jt should be connected to TDS & ready for pumping.
NOTE: To apply any more over-pull to unset the packer should be confirm by town.
Start to CBU to confirm in & out Mw is same.
R/U Casing Running Eq. & start to POOH completion string.
