1. Introduction


     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers workover of well No.6, of Dehluran oil field, I.R.Iran. The well
                  is firstly intended to produce oil from layers 2 & 3-2 of Bangestan group. Shall it
              impossible to produce oil, the well will be completed to be a water disposal one. The
              well is in closed position right now with 1500 psi wellhead pressure.

            Problem

              All evidences show that there is no reasonable interval to be perforated for producing
                 oil from this well. Since the well had produced oil with high volume of water,
              decision was made to do new perforations and to try to isolate oil producers from
              water producers perforations.

             Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of
            Workover request no. ٩٧ / ٣١/-٣ ١٠٢ ,dated: ٩٧٣١ /٢١ / ٥١ and letter No. ٨۵ / ك م / ۵٢١٩۴
               dated: ٨٣١۵ //٢١ ١۶ .

           The main head lines of this program are as follow:

                1.    Move and R/U Workover rig.
                2.     Record wellhead and annuluses pressures.
                3.     Displace hole with proper mud.
                4.     Secure well, L/D wellhead, N/U BOP's.
                5.      Pull out & L/D completion string.
                6.     Clean well down to 4109m.
                7.      Perforate against layers 2 & 3-2 of Bangestan group.
                8.    Flow the well (Acidizing may be needed).
                9.    Run PSP log while flowing the well.
              10.   Run Completion string.
              11.   L/D BOP's, N/U wellhead.
              12.    Release Rig.

             Note: In case of impossible well flowing or producing high volume of water the
              well will be completed as water disposal one.

             Note: The detailed program is illustrated here after in this document under the
            head of "Workover Program".

2. Workover Program


   2.1  Sequence of Workover Operation


            1. Move workover rig to the well site of DH6, R/U and make  it ready for operation,
            Record rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea level.

            2. The well head spools, valves & P-Seals and x-mas tree must be checked and
              serviced by well head specialist and be repaired if needed.

            3.  Connect killing & flowing lines to 7-1/16"-5000 x-mas tree side outlets and connect
               return line to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 4500 psi
              pressure.

            4. Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 7" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x 13-3/8"
             annulus and 13-3/8" x 18-5/8" annulus. If necessary, release / bleed off pressures,
              monitor their build up and investigate for the probable relations. Monitor the well for
           any flow through tubing.

             Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
             while relation investigation.

            5.  Shall the well be static, follow Item No.6; otherwise, bullhead the well by pumping
             70pcf salt water which its full column imposes 256psi over pressure to the reservoir
      @ top perforations (3958m). Shall could not able to bullhead, lubricate the well and
           make sure about well condition.

             Note: Monitor the pressure behaviour of annuluses all the time.

            6.  Rig up wireline equipment; test same and lubricators to max 4500psi pressure. Set
             no-go plug «4.562", PXN». Test same and tubing to max 4500psi. Monitor tubing
             annulus pressure behaviour while testing inside tubing. Open SSD, circulate 82pcf
          mud which imposes 157psi over pressure to the reservoir @ top perforations (Oil of
              0.3 psi/ft gradient is supposed to be under 82pcf mud), and balance in/out mud
              weight. Observe well for any mud loss/flow.

            7.  While Circulating cross well head, install « 6-5/16"» two way back pressure valve
                into 11" x 9-5/8" neck x 7" VAM "FBA CAMERON" tubing hanger and test to 4500psi
              pressure.

             Note: Shall B.P.V fail against pressure test or could not be set in its position in
             tubing hanger, it is recommended to install "2.750" PXN plug into dummy of
          3SV landing nipple or to set 3SV in closed position prior to L/D well head.

            8.  While circulating cross tubing head spool, N/D well head to top of 13-5/8"-3000 x 11"
                    - 5000 tubing head spool. N/U 13-5/8" BOP's stack; test same to 4500psi against
             two-way. Retrieve 6-5/16" two way back pressure valve from tubing hanger.

Sequence of Operations


     •  RIH with 4-1/8" BHA as per BHA design, Drill out MPBT Plug @ 4150m, push it
       down to top of LDC @ 4286.5m and try to mill same (E.Mill#1)

     •   Circulate clean hole, POOH, RIH with two junk subs and E.Mill#1, Circulate clean
          hole, Work on junk subs, POOH, L/D E.Mill#1 & J.subs (2No.), RIH with new
        E.Mill#2 to bottom.

     •   Drill out LDC @ 4286.5m, cement and shoe @ 4300m (E.Mill#2).

     •   Drill formation down to 2 m below the shoe (4302) (E.Mill#2, ROP:0.5m/hr).

     •   Circulate clean hole, POOH, RIH with two junk subs and E.Mill#2, Circulate clean
          hole, Work on junk subs, POOH, L/D E.Mill#2 & j.subs (2No.), RIH with PDC
         bit#3 and Down hole motor to bottom.

     •   Drill formation from 4302m down to 4410m, motor life (PDC bit#3, ROP: 1-
