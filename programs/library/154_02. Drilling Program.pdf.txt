1  General Information

This Document Covers the Drilling of Asmari Producer Well in Mansur Abad Oilfield,
Iran. These wells Shall Be Considered to Product from the Asmari Reservoir. There are
totally six group with deferent combination of Producer and Delineation well and two
categories of Vertical and Directional profile. In general, the 26” hole section will be drilled
to 60 m and 18-5/8” casing set as Surface casing. Drilling in 17-1/2” hole section will be
done to top of Gachsaran formation approximately +/- 1200m and will be cover by 13-3/8”
Intermediate casing. The 12-1/4” hole section will be drilled at Gachsaran High pressure
zone to Asmari Cap rock and then 9-5/8” Production casing will be run and cemented. the
8-1/2” hole section at reservoir zone will be different based on well objective and logging
results . The 7” Liner will be run and finally Completion String will be run. Nipple up Well
Head and Complete the Well.

This program covers the drilling of fourteen wells in Mansur Abad oil field, Iran. There
are six types of well in this proposal.

1st group is including vertical wells with production goals from Asmari and delineation
of oil – water contact at southern crest where the deep of layers are high.  In this
category there are two wells as below description:

     -   Location E 051 S as delineation / producer (vertical) well from Asmari reservoir
     -   Location E 025.1 S as producer (vertical) well from Asmari reservoir

2nd group is including vertical wells with production and delineation goals from Asmari
structure in Western section of Mansur Abad oil field . The deep of layer at this area is
normal. In this category there are four wells as below description:

     -   Location W 076 N as delineation  / producer (vertical) well from Asmari
        reservoir
     -   Location E 010 N as producer (vertical) well from Asmari reservoir
     -   Location W 085 N as delineation  / producer (vertical) well from Asmari
        reservoir
     -   Location W 061 N as delineation  / producer (vertical) well from Asmari
        reservoir

     -   Location E 047 S as delineation / producer (vertical) well from Asmari reservoir
     -   Location E 056 N as delineation / producer (vertical) well from Asmari reservoir

4th group is including only one directional well to product from Asmari resevoir in
Eastern section of Mansur Abad oil field . Drilling at location W 035 N will be started as
vertically down to 450m (1st KOP). Drill the well directionally at the rate of 3.5º/100 ft
down to M.B.U.A of 35º toward N179 º. Directional drilling will be continued at keep
section (Constant angle and azimuth) down to top of Asmari formation (D.A.P).
Dropping the well at the rate of 7º/100 ft down to vertical trajectory and continue to 20
m into Pabdeh formation.
     -   Location W 035 N as producer (directional) well from Asmari reservoir

5th group is including one vertical well for delineation and production of Asmari in
Western section of Mansur Abad oil field where the deep of layers are high.
     -   Location W 026 S as delineation  / producer (vertical) well from Asmari
        reservoir

6th group is similar to 4th and including one directional well to delineate and product
from Asmari structure in Western section of Mansur Abad oil field . Drilling at location
W 029 N will be started as vertically down to 510m (1st KOP). Drill the well directionally
at the rate of 2º/100 ft down to M.B.U.A of 31.5º toward N180º. Directional drilling
will be continued at keep section (Constant angle and azimuth) down to top of Asmari
formation (D.A.P). Dropping the well at the rate of 8º/100 ft down to vertical trajectory
and continue to total depth of 3031 m ( -2400 m.s.s.d) in Pabdeh formation .
     -   Location W 029 N as delineation / producer (directional) well from Asmari
        reservoir

1.2  Introduction

The Mansur Abad  oil field  is located in Khuzestan province, approximately 10
kilometers to the Northeast of Behbahan city. This Field is situated in an area which is
known as Dezful Embayment. This structure is an elongated NW-SE trending anticline
with approximately 600 Meters of closure at the Asmari level. It is measured about 3km
by 21km, and is situated at South of Khaviz structure and is on the same trend
structurally with Lishtar closure of Gachsaran oil field which is at southeast of Mansur
Abad structure.

1.1  Formation Description
A brief description of the stratigraphic units expected to be drilled in Mansur Abad Formation typical
vertical well has been presented in the following breakdown.

Mishan Formation
Mishan Formation mainly composed of grey marl and limestone. This formation in the lower part
consists of a repetition of grey marl and boundstone.

Gachsaran Formation
Deposition of the Gachsaran Formation is cyclic. This formation totally consists of a repetition of
Anhydrite, grey and red marl as well as salt and some limestone beds.

Asmari Formation
Asmari formation is predominantly composed of cream to light brown dolomite and Limestone. It is
worth mentioning that due to Pyrnian orogeny, lower part of Asmari formation is not precipitated in
Mansur Abad, Binak, Gulkhari, and Sulabedar oilfields.

Jahrum Formation
This formation mainly consists of limestone with interbedded of dolomite. There is not a sharp
lithological boundary between Asmari formation and Jahrum formation but the top of this formation
should be recognized by assemblage Zone 53.

Pabdeh Formation
At the beginning of this formation there are pyritic argillaceous brown, gray to brown and brown
limestone and also there are layers of pyritic shale. At the base of this formation argillaceous limestone
and gray marl with gluconate will be appear. There are some cherty zones in base of this formation.

Gurpi Formation
This formation mainly consists of gray marl and argillaceous pyritic limestone. At the base of this
formation gray marls will be soft and sticky.
Ilam Formation
This formation consists of slightly pyritic light brown to white limestone which is very thinh (about
1.5m.) in Mansur Abad oilfield.

    planning phase, QRA has been an integral part of the work. All potential risks identified have been
    evaluated and mitigating actions have been implemented to reduce the risks to acceptable levels.

Potassium interactions with clay surfaces can be traced to two effects: ionic size and hydrational
energy. Potassium ions are of the proper size to fit snugly into the spaces between the two silica
tetrahedral layers which contact each other in the formation of a three-layer clay packet. The ionic
diameter of potassium is 2.66 Å, very close to the available distance of 2.8 Å in the lattice space
of the clay structure. A cation slightly smaller than 2.8 Å is desirable to allow for crystalline
compaction.

Filtration Process:

Filtration provides clean fluids that will prevent damage to the formation, the reservoir and its
permeability. It does this by removal of solids and particulates from the fluid that may otherwise
block the open space within the rock formation containing the hydrocarbon fluid within the
production zone.

Any solids and contaminates in the completion fluid will reduce its effectiveness. An efficiently
filtered and completely solid free completion fluid will add value to both the productivity and the
dependability of the well in the long term.

dual pod filtration cartridge unit is recommended for filtering brines and produced water in
completion, stimulation, and workover operations. The pods are connected by a manifold,
providing staged filtration capability. Staged filtration utilizes cartridge filters with different
ratings to maximize filtration efficiency. Cartridge filtration  is designed for use with fluids
containing a low percent by volume of suspended solids. Cartridge filtration is not economic when
fluids are contaminated with oil or other substances.
