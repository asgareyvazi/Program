1.   Introduction

1.1  Program Introduction

This program covers the information and procedures required for workover operation and

Re-Entry plan for well D-126P in Dorood Field-Kharg Island-Persian Gulf-IRAN.


Problem

  The well is shut down due to low wellhead pressure as consequence of high water

   cut.


Main Objective:

The main objective of this program is to abandon the existing pay zone by cement

plug, re-enter the well at 5" liner at (Yamama zone H) and drill a new horizontal

drainl  to 3950,21m MD (-3386m TVDss) (105m horizontal leg) in Yamama (H1)

reservoir. Window depth will be at 3756mMD (-3375,98mTVDss ) in Yamama.

3. Drilling History


SUMMARY


The D-126P was drilled from July 2004 to September 2005 and reached a total depth

of 4185mMD (-3446.3mTVDss) in the Middle Yamama.

The D-126P-Re1 well was drilled following failing to complete the original hole due

to difficulties during drilling, uncertainties on the liner cementation, unsatisfactory

results on logging and collapsed PBR in original well of D-126P. The Re-entry hole

was drilled from a window in 7”  liner reached a  total depth of 3990mMD  (-

3440.9mTVDss) in Middle Yamama where complete loss occurred and the 4 ½” liner

had to be set. The well was completed in Yamama via perforations over the interval -

3425.2 to -3440.8mTVDss, but was shut down due to increasing water cut.

The D-126P-Re2 well was drilled from 22 Oct, 2012 to January 2013 from a window

at 2696mMD in 9 5/8” casing and reached to a  total depth of 4000mMD  (-

3635.1mTVDss) in Middle Yamama. Based on reservoir logs, the Yamama reservoir

layers found to be watered out which probably was partly due to the effect of two

water injections wells in nearby D-114WI and D-117WI. Therefore the hole was

plugged back and decided to drill Re-entry No.3 in a new direction towards the crest.

The D-126P-Re3 well was drilled from February 2013 to July 2013 from a window at

2578mMD in the 9 5/8” casing and  drilled to a  total depth of 4198mMD  (-

3454.4mTVDss) in Middle Yamama.

It was planned to run 5” liner to T.D for completion of the hole, but failed to do so

and the liner became stuck at 3819mMD. Attempts to release the liner was not

successful, subsequently the liner was set off bottom at this depth and cemented.

Tried to re-drill the open hole section by 4 1/8” bit. This hole was drilled from the

shoe to 3940m and decided to complete the hole at this depth.

The well was completed by using 2 7/8” slotted liner through Middle Yamama.

Drilling operations were performed in several phases that are described briefly in

below.

4. Workover History

D-126P & D-126P Re1 wells:

26” hole section: Was drilled vertically from 34 to 630mMD with 69.3-73pcf

mud and the 20”casing was run and cemented at 556mMD in Aghajari Formation.

17 1/2” hole section: This hole was drilled vertically from 560 to 1850mMD with

71.8-92.4pcf mud. Combination of the 13 5/8” & 13 3/8” casings were run and

cemented at 1849mMD in Asmari Formation.

12 1/4” hole section: This section was drilled from 1850 to 2905mMD with 68.6-

69.9pcf mud, lack of pressure balance of well with Formation pressure causes

flow and losses at 2227mMD. LCM pill and cement plug was spotted to cure

losses, at 2402mMD observed gain of 238 BPH but reduced to 6 BPH. The

9 5/8” casing was run to 2900 mMD in top of Kazhdumi Formation.

8 1/2” hole section: This hole was drilled with 93.6pcf KCL Polymer mud from

2905  to 3374mMD.  Drilling was continued  vertically  to 2905m and then

directionally to reach a maximum inclination of 31.9° at 3142mMD. Slant section

was kept from 3142 to 3200m and then build up was performed from 3200 to

3365m (32° to 35.6°). At 3374m found one cone of the bit left in the well and

milled. The 7” liner was run to 3373mMD in Lower Ratawi Formation and hung

inside 9 5/8" casing at 2748 mMD.

6” hole section: The 6” hole section was drilled directionally with 65.5–64.3pcf

Polymer/Starch mud from 3374 to  total depth of 4185mMD. Build up was

continued to T.D. and reached a maximum inclination of 80° at 3734mMD that

complete loss was occurred. Drilling was resumed to 3760mMD with seawater

and several LCM pills and 3 cement plugs were set without success. Drilling

resumed to T.D. with no returns and at times partial losses (126/189 BPH). The 4

1/2” liner was run at 4184mMD and hung inside the 7" liner at 3331mMD and

cemented under partial losses. Due to difficulties during drilling, uncertainties on

liner cementation and unsatisfying results on logging, it was decided to drilling

sidetrack.

6” Re-entry section: After set whip stock above 4 ½” liner lap, a window was

opened in the 7” liner at 3297mMD. The 6”section was drilled directionally from

D-126P-Re2 well:

8 1/2” hole section: From 2696 to 3637mMD. The rig Fath 42 was spudded on 22

Oct, 2012 (1391-08-01) and directional drilling was started (toward Azimuth of

231.4°) in the 8 1/2” hole by opening a window at 2696m in Upper Sarvak to

2820mMD with 71-70pcf water based mud and 8-250 BPH losses. Due to cure

heavy losses, drilling stopped and the hole was cement several times. After drilled

out hard cement directional drilling unwanted was in Sidetrack-01 from K.O.P. of

2776 to 2858mMD with 70pcf water based mud and 2-90 BPH losses in Mauddud

member while mixed and pumped LCM pill periodically. The hole was cemented

because of high losses. Hard cement was drilled out and directional drilling

started undesirably in Sidetrack-02 from K.O.P. of 2805m to 2880mMD and 6-40

BPH losses. The MWD survey takes after each connection. Resume directional

drilling as slide & rotation mode from 2880 to 3637mMD with 71pcf oil based

mud (displacement mud near the top of Kazhdumi Fm.) and 6-30 BPH losses. The

7” liner was set and cemented in Khami Limestone from 3633.5 to 2593m.

6 1/8” hole section: From 3637 to 4000mMD. Directional drilling was continued

in the 6 1/8” open hole to  total depth of 4000mMD (Inclination: 41.1° &

Azimuth: 254.7°) with 56pcf oil based mud and 0-3 BPH losses while take the

MWD survey after each connection. Due to high water saturation values resulted

from reservoir evaluation logs, the well was not completed and was plugged back

to drill a new re-entry hole (Re3).

D-126P-Re3 well:

8 1/2” hole section: From 2578 to 3389mMD. The D-126P-Re3 well was kicked

off   from   a   window    in    the   9   5/8”   casing    at  2578mMD

in the Gurpi Formation. Directional drilling was started in the 8 1/2” hole section

towards azimuth of 161.5° with 70pcf salt polymer mud and 0-10 BPH losses to

2685m. Losses suddenly increased to 100 BPH (in Sarvak Formation) and hole

was cemented.  After  drilling  out cement,  directional  drilling continued  to

2704mMD with 84-240 BPH losses (complete loss occurred  at 2693mMD).

Performance cement job two times and while drilled out cement the hole was

sidetracked unexpectedly. Directional drilling was started in Sidetrack-01 from

K.O.P. of 2678 to 2733mMD with 30-200 BPH losses and no return. After cure

losses drilling was continued to 2850m and 60-100 BPH losses. To control the

loss zone the hole was cemented several times and while drilling out hard cement

the hole was sidetracked unwantedly. Sidetrack-02 was kicked off from 2780 and

was drilled directionally to 2836mMD with 20-25 BPH losses and suddenly

increased to 100 BPH. Drilling continued with 71pcf salt polymer to 2889 and 10-

30 BPH. Mud was displaced  to 70pcf  oil based and  drilling continued  to

3389mMD with 4-80 BPH while pumped LCM pill periodically. The 7” Liner

was set from 2469 to 3388mMD in Khami Limestone (1m off bottom).

6 1/8” hole section: From 3389 to 4198mMD. Directional drilling started in the 6

1/8” open hole with 52pcf oil based mud and 0-2 BPH losses to 3684mMD while

pumped LCM pill periodically. Directional drilling Resumed as slide and rotation

mode with 58pcf Oil based mud and static well from 3684 to 4067mMD. Mud

weight increased to 59.6pcf and directional drilling continued to 4069mMD with

110 BPH losses. Drilling stopped and displaced mud to sea water and directional

drilling continued to 4082mMD with 120-150 BPH losses. Continued drilling to

total depth of 4198mMD (Inclination: 81.7° & Azimuth: 161.5°) by  sea water

and 80-65 BPH losses. (heavy loss started from 4067 to 4198mMD from J & K

zones in Yamama Formation). As per program a  full suite logs was planned to

be run but due to bad hole condition (heavy losses and tight hole) it was canceled.

15.  Well Securing Procedure




    1.  Centre derrick on well D-126P and record RKB to THF.

    2. Hook up kill lines to Ann. & TBG sides and test same to 5000psi.

    3.  Record TBG & Ann pressure.

    4. Check 3SV by releasing pressure of control line, bleed off well head pressure
        to zero, monitor & test well head pressure for any build up to make sure 3SV
         is functioning.

    5.  Release Ann. & TBG pressure & check for any build up while 3SV is closed.

    6. Check & make sure 3SV can be opened. Kill the well by pumping (1.5 times
       of tubing and hole volume) with 64PCF sea water

Note:

     Mud loss experienced while drilling 4 1/8" hole section with 64 PCF mud.
      64 PCF SW impose 1400 psi over pressure to the Yamama Datum depth
      (±3537m TVD).

     Adjust MW with respect to hole condition.

     Weight up material (Salt & Limestone), Acid soluble LCM should be
       available on site for possible need to kill and cure the well.

    7. Check all p-seals of wellhead for any leakages.

    8. R/U wire line lubricator & BOP, pressure test stuffing box & wire line BOP to
       3000psi, RIH with wire line to retrieve 3SV and install dummy.

    9.  RIH With gauge cutter to last No-Go landing nipple (for check the tubing ID
         is suitable for wire line tools). POOH to surface. RIH with plug and set inside
     NO-GO landing nipple at 2751.66m MD.

    10. Circulate thru SPM and balance MW 64PCF sea water inside & outside of
       tubing.

Note:

      If circulation trough SPM had problem Run string puncher and punch
      with 2NO 1-11/16" shot in 3 ½ “ tubing at 2727m-2737m MD.
