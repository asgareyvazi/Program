Subject: TLC Requirements

  Date: 27-May-2020               Rig: NIDC-65

Please note that the following is required for the upcoming TLC logging program:

      All the drill pipes must have a minimum drift of 2.4 in. (WSI should provide 2.4”
Rabbit)
    Not using of plastic-coated drill pipe while TLC.
    Accurate pipe tally is necessary for depth control; we will require the tally before
the logging.
    Upper sheave wheel must be rigged up with the WSI fishing sling on a derrick
crossbeam; before rigging up the tools.
    Make up all required pipe needed for the job before WSI rig-up.
   No jars and drill collars to be used.
    RIH and POOH speed is 3 to 5 minutes per stand, slip to slip. Elevator/Rotary
must be locked. Use minimum dope, and only dope Pin ends while RIH. String weight
should be monitored by driller while operation and should not exceed 1500lb more than
normal weight.
    Pipe must not be rotated. (Tighten pipes using both tongues)
    Ensure pumps can supply enough pressure (500 PSI) for a proper latch. (Max 600
GPM)
    Make sure the X-Over are having proper ID and OD (If it is above CSES) and no
internal shoulder
      Circulate (with Screen/strainer) every 10 stands for 15 minutes after observing
returns (max 600 GPM). Inform WSI of any kicks in pressure. Do not stop before getting
returns.
     Circulate 2 string-volumes once you reach latch point.
    Proper pressure/flow rate must be maintained as directed by TLC specialist.
     String needs to be filled after latching and during logging to avoid possible
unlatching.
    Set pipe gently inside slips to avoid tool damage. Maximum compression of WSI
tools is –1500 lbs.
     Anything dropping into the pipe (rags / rabbit / rope of rabbit / etc.) will result in
complete P.O.O.H and may damage the DWCH the down hole latch.

    Max. Make-up torque for Cable Side Entry Sub (CSES) is 9000 ft.lbs
     After running in our side entry sub, we can run the rest of the pipes, these pipes
should be maximum 5 in DP.
    Snatch pulley will be installed on this point to keep the cable away from drill pipes.
    Knowing the cable is latched any movement of the drill pipes should be
communicated to the winch-man so he can adjust the cable tension accordingly. while the
cable is latched driller cannot move string without WSI permission.
   When connecting the drill pipes make sure the logging cable is not crushed
between the drill pipe and the slip. Cable should seat in the groove in the slip.
    The logging speed will be communicated to the driller real time using intercom.
Driller speed should be constant while logging, if not the logging quality will not guarantee
by WSI.
   We cannot tag TD; we’ll stop approx. 10 meters above TD.
     After finishing the log PWCH will be unlatched and cable will be removed.
     Pull out speed is 3.5 min. per stand
        If you have any questions/clarifications, please do not hesitate to contact WSI
Engineer.
