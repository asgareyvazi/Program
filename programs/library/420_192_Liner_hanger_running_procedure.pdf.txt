             ON SITE PREPARATION (PRIOR TO RUNNING LINER)


Pre-job meeting should be conducted prior to running in the hole to go over running and cementing
procedure.

1.  Using the Check sheets and equipment manifest, check that all the necessary liner equipment,
    running tools, etc., are on location and is in good condition.

2.  Take all relevant measurements of the equipment - lengths, OD's, ID's, etc.  Record all relevant
    information into the Check Sheets. The liner hanger assembly will have been pressure tested at the
    Viking designated base. Verify the correct quantity of shear pins has been installed in all equipment
   as per inspection data sheets. Also, check the compatibility of the liner wiper plug with the drill pipe
    darts and the liner casing.

3.  Check the compatibility of setting ball and ball seat, and that setting ball goes through the liner wiper
    plug.

4. ENSURE THAT A 1.9” BRONZE BALL IS USED AND THAT IT IS INSTALLED IN THE CEMENT
   HEAD BALL DROP OUTLET.
5.  WITH THE FLAG SUB IN THE 6 O’CLOCK (OPEN) POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE BALL CAN
   BE RELEASED FROM THE BALL DROP SUB BY TURNING THE VALVE. MARK THE EXACT
   POSITION OF THE VALVE FOR THE BALL TO BE RELEASED. ONCE CONFIRMED, INSTALL
   BALL AGAIN AND MAKE UP WECO CONNECTION ON BALL DROP SUB.

6.  Make up the float shoe, float collar, and landing collar as per the tally on the pipe deck. Fit the baffle
    plate directly above the float collar inside the 7” liner.

Float shoe, float collar, and landing collar should be made up on the pin end of the casing joints.
These connections should be thread locked.
Record connection make-up torque.

7.  Rabbit all drill pipe in derrick prior to running liner with a drift having a minimum 2-3/4” OD for 5 7/8"
      drill pipe. It is compulsory that all drill pipe singles, crossovers and pup joints, cement head, full open
    safety valves which will be used, have to be drifted.

8.  NOTE: check for any sharp edges inside subs

9.  Remove OD drill pipe rubbers (if any are being used) on lower 10 stands of drill pipe. This reduces
    the chance of cementing the string in, and allows wash over to be attempted if this happens.

10. Prior to running liner, on wiper trip stop at shoe and record torque/amp reading at 10-12 rpm. This
    torque reading can be used to calculate the maximum drill down torque while running the liner. This
    calculation is done by adding the torque required to rotate the drill pipe at the shoe and adding the

                            RUNNING PROCEDURE

1.     The Cement Head assembly can be made up prior to running liner and racked back in the derrick or
      when the liner is run down to the shoe, prior to entering open hole. The Assembly should be made
      up in the following order (top to bottom):

         *      One joint drill pipe
         *     A full opening drill pipe safety valve
         *      Top Drive Swivel with 2” Lo-Torque valve in the closed position.
         *      Top Drive Cement Head
         *      One joint of drill pipe

        Note:  A pup joint has to be added to make the assembly 90-95 ft long. to allow racking in derrick
               This pup can be placed above or below the plug dropping head assembly as required by drill
               pipe tally. (See Attached Drawings)

        Note: Not all rigs will allow racking back in the derrick.

              Function the flag sub and ensure that  it moves freely. Move the flag sub to the 6
           O’CLOCK (OPEN) POSITION. This will ensure that when the ball is dropped, it will
                freely pass through the cement head and into the drill pipe.
                Later, prior to pumping the dart, the flag sub MUST be moved to the 3 O’CLOCK
            (CLOSED) POSITION. The dart should then be released and the flag sub observed for
              confirmation that the dart has left the cement head.

2.     The drill pipe handling sub and setting tool connections for the hanger assembly will be torqued at
        Viking designated base. Lay assembly on the pipe rack.

3.     Run the shoe track as per the tally:

        •  Float shoe.
        •  One joint of casing.
        •  Float collar, float collar joint.
        •  Landing collar.

      Check the float valves in the shoe by observing that the pipe does not fill up through the shoe, and
         that when filled from the top, mud will flow out of the shoe.

4.     Run in with the liner, filling the casing approximately every 3-5 joints. Centralizers to be installed on
        the deck as specified in the program.
      Make up the TIW valve and crossover onto the liner prior to picking up the hanger. Make up the top
        drive and break circulation. Circulate a minimum of liner contents to check for obstructions and to
      remove air from the liner. There is no limit to circulating rates or pressures at this point as the hanger
          is not made up.

5.      Pick up the hanger assembly with the drill pipe elevator, and make up to the top joint of the liner,
         start the pin thread on the hanger assembly with chain tongs or strap wrench to avoid damage to the
       hanger pin thread.

6.    LEAVING ROTARY SLIPS SET ON LINER JOINT pick up 1ft. to confirm that the liner hanger
        running tool is fully engaged in the liner hanger. There is also an “inspection hole” in the liner hanger
       body that allows a visual confirmation that the tool is engaged correctly. If the tool is not correctly
      engaged and releases from the hanger at this point, lay down the hanger and running tool assembly
      and pick up the back up assembly.

7.     Check hanger for any damage and record upwards and downwards weight figures on the indicator.

       Lower the hanger assembly through the rotary table and set the drill pipe slips on the 5” pup joint.
     Do not set slips on the hanger assembly. Be careful to keep the hanger centered while lowering
       through the table to avoid damage to the slips, etc.,

8.     Make Up top drive. Take care in ensuring that there is no left hand rotation or backlash whilst
      making up the Top Drive.

9.     Break circulation and ensure returns. Limit circulation pressure to 500 psi maximum.

      Take circulating pressures at the following:

      15 SPM - 20 SPM - 30 SPM

        Pick Up Hanger to floor and observe for any leaks.

10.    Record Liner Hanger pick up and slack off weights with and without circulation.

11.    Note the total number of drill pipe stands in the derrick, and number each stand as the liner is being
        run. Run the liner in the well at a rate of 1-1/2 minutes per stand. Take particular care as the hanger
       assembly goes through the B.O.P. stack and the wellhead and the 10¾” x 9⅝” casing cross-over.
           Fill the pipe regularly and run a maximum of 5 stands before filling. Take care that no left hand
         rotation is applied while running in the hole with Liner.

12.    Stop at the casing shoe and break circulation for minimum of 200 strokes to record operating
       parameters. Do not exceed maximum pressure of 500 psi whilst circulating.

      Take circulating rates at the following:

      15 SPM – 20 SPM – 30 SPM


13.   When running in open hole, fill up the drill pipe on the fly while going up to latch another stand (To
       minimize the chances of the liner becoming stuck, do not stop in open hole to  fill up the drill pipe).
      Maximum run in speed, 2 minutes per stand. Run in slowly to place the shoe approximately 20 ft.
       from bottom.

14.    Every ten stands make up top drive and break circulation. Do not exceed 500 psi circulating
       pressure while reciprocating the string. Ensure that returns are seen before continuing in the hole.

       Note: If it is necessary to rotate through a bridge in open hole, break circulation and begin
        rotating at 10-12 RPM. Prior to running liner on wiper trip, if a torque to turn the drill pipe at
       the shoe was established,, this torque reading can be used to calculate the maximum drill
     down torque. This calculation is done by adding the torque required to rotate the drill pipe at
       the shoe and adding the torque limit of the Liner Hanger assembly (11,000 FT/LBS is max.
       torque for 7” 26 lbs/ft Vam Top connections).

            If the torque value was not established during the wiper trip, limit the torque to the torque
         limit of the liner hanger assembly.

      CAUTION :   Care must be taken when releasing build up torque in drill pipe.  Right hand
                    torque should slowly be let out to prevent backlash. Left hand backlash of drill
                    pipe could shift the tool into the mechanical release position.  One way to
                   minimize backlash is, after drilling though the bridge, pick up drill pipe above
                    bridge before releasing torque.

                           SETTING PROCEDURE


1.      Approximately 20 ft. from bottom:

     •  Make up the top drive assembly.
       Record pick-up and slack-off weight figures.
     •   Break circulation at lowest possible rate (10-20 SPM).
     •  Do not allow circulating pressure to exceed 500 psi.
     •  Wash down, do not tag bottom with more than 10,000 lbs, and mark pipe.
     •  Record pick-up and slack-off weights. Pick up to 2 metres off bottom and mark pipe.

     •   Break circulation and circulate until you have a stabilized rate. Do not exceed 500 psi.
     •  Record pressure at different pumping rates.

2.     When stabilized circulation is complete, stop pumps. Confirm the flag sub on the cement head is
         at the 6 O’CLOCK (OPEN) POSITION and release the 1.90" setting ball from the ball drop sub into
         the string.

     •   Chain the brake and watch the pressure gauge closely.

     •   Re-establish circulation, chasing the setting ball down with a maximum pump rate of 5 Bbls/min
         (not exceeding 500 psi) until you are 20 Bbls short of the ball landing. Slow down the pump rate to 2
        Bbls/min for the remaining 20 Bbls of the circulation until the ball seats. This is to ensure the ball
       does not slam into the ball seat and prematurely shear the seat in the setting tool assembly.

3.     To set liner hanger:

     •  When the ball lands slow pumps and increase the pressure to 1,500 psi and hold for a five minutes.

     •  Slack off running string while holding pressure, liner weight should be lost before the shoe reaches
        bottom.

       Note:   If 1,500 psi does not set the hanger, pick up to the original position and increase
              pressure by 200 psi and slack off. Do not bleed the pressure back to 1500 psi before
              slacking off weight.    If liner does not set pick up to original position again and
              increase pressure by a further 200 psi. Repeat this process until a maximum setting
              pressure of 2500 psi is applied.

       Note: The running tool is set to release with 2200psi applied pressure whilst the string is in
              the neutral position (i.e. liner weight slacked off).
          DO NOT SLACK OFF TO NEUTRAL WHILST HOLDING MORE THAN 1500 psi WHILE
           ATTEMPTING TO VERIFY THAT THE HANGER HAS SET.

        • When the liner is hung, set down the entire weight of the liner onto the liner hanger, bleed off
           pressure.

         Record if the hanger hang successfully and weight slacked off into hanger

        •  Set down an additional 20,000 lbs of drill pipe weight.

       Note:  The ball seat will shear at +/- 3,000 psi.  If the ball seat is sheared out and the hanger
                slips have not set, the liner can be set on bottom and cemented in place. To leave
                 liner on bottom, the liner running tool would need to be released by the mechanical
               release procedure detailed in step 14.

4.    Releasing setting tool:

        •  While maintaining 20000 lbs set down weight, increase pressure in work string to 2,400 psi to
           release the setting tool. Hold for two minutes and then bleed off pressure. Leave valve open at
           surface.

        •  Pick up to the neutral/drill pipe weight plus 3 feet to ensure that the setting tool has released from
           the liner. Do not exceed 6 feet of movement at the setting tool (to ensure the slick stinger
           stays in the RSB pack off assembly). If setting tool has not released, place setting tool back in
          20,000 lbs compression and increase pressure in 200 psi increments, checking for release after
         each increase.  If tool has not released when Ball seat shears out, proceed to mechanical release
          procedure later in step 14.

        •  After setting tool is released, increase pressure to shear out the ball seat (3,000 psi), re-establish
            circulation. Once ball seat has been sheared out, circulate a minimum of one full bottoms up at
        maximum circulation rate.

5.      Pressure test cement line at 5,000 psi.

        •  Review steps 6 through 9.
        •  Designate one person to coordinate cementing procedures.

6.    Pump cement as per client procedure:

        • Open Lo-Torque valve on cement head.
        • Pump cement (volume and rate as per program).
        • Move the flag sub to the 3 O’CLOCK Position.
        •  Release pump down plug by rotating lever on ‘TD’ plug dropping head counter-clockwise (left
          hand). Watch for indication of pump down plug passing through flag sub.
        • Open drill pipe safety valve.
        •  Close Lo-Torque valve
        •  Displace with cement unit at the optimum rate. Slow pump rate down to 2-3 bbl/minute, 5-10 bbls
          before pump down plug reaches wiper plug.
        •  Watch for indication of drill pipe plug latching into liner wiper plug on pressure gauge.   If this
          pressure indication  is seen, recalculate  total displacement volume.  The new displacement
         volume will be the actual displacement volume for the drill pipe plus the calculated liner volume.
                  If liner wiper plug shear was not seen, pump calculated volume (do not exceed ½ shoe track
          volume).
        •  Slow pump rate down to 2-3 bbl/minute, 5-10 bbl before total displacement.
        •  Land plugs with 1,000 psi over final displacing pressure (maximum of 3,000 psi). If plug does not
         bump, do not displace more than 1/2 of the calculated shoe track volume.

7.      Bleed off pressure and check for back flow.

8a.        If plug has landed:

     •  Pressure test liner to 3,000 psi for 5 min.
     •  Bleed pressure to zero and keep valve open then pull out with running tool.
     •  Some over pull may be seen when pack-off bushing releases after 9 feet of pick-up.
     •  Pull out with running tool and rack back 5-10 stands.

8b.        If plug has not landed:

        •   Pull out with running tool and rack back 5-10 stands.

9.      Rig up and reverse out until returns are clear of cement and spacer.

10.     Function the cement head valves as the pumps are on. This will clean the inside of the cement head.

11.     Pull out of hole. Do not rotate pipe when pulling out of hole and take care to avoid damaging the
        running tool as it comes out of the hole.

12.    Lay down surface equipment, singles and any pup joints and liner setting tool, flush and secure it for
       back load.

                  MECHANICAL RELEASE PROCEDURE FOR
                        TOP SET SETTING TOOL

13.        If the setting tool does not release hydraulically (Step 4 unsuccessful), left-hand (anti-clockwise)
        torque will have to be applied to the tool. The torque acts to shear the brass screws (4 screws,
     10mm Brass, 1000ft/ lbs each, which is approximately a total of 4000 ft/lbs). The torque value used
         in the setting tool can be adjusted depending in the liner connection make up torque and hole
        deviation.

       Bleed off string pressure and pick up string to neutral, then rotate the string to the right (not
       exceeding the lowest liner connection torque value) to ensure that all connections are made up tight.
       Apply left hand torque as required to shear screws and slack off 10,000 lbs on the liner setting tool.

       Note:  Some hole conditions may require movement of the drill pipe to "work" the torque
           down to the setting tool.

         After the screws have sheared, the body of the setting tool will turn 1/4 turn (left) and drop down
          slightly. Then rotate the string to the right to ensure that all connections are made up tight.  Pick
         straight up 3 feet to ensure the setting tool has released, and to release the collet from the profile in
        the liner top (Note: 10,000 lbs of over pull should be seen when picking up the work string).

     Do not exceed 6 feet of pick up at the setting tool.
