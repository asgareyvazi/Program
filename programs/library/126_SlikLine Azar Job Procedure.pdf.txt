                       Sarvak Azar Slick Line Project

CONTRACTOR                                                                                        CLIENT
                               Azar Filed 05 Procedure
   OEOC                                                                                                  Sarvak

    Rig Up Slick Line

       -Connect BOP To Top Flange 7 1/16" Or 4 1/16"

        -Pick Up Gauge Cutter

        -Rig Up Lubricator

       -Pressure Test Up To 4000 Psi For 10 m Graudally

    1- R.I.H Slick Line For Drifting Tubing To Mule Shoe The P.O.O.H

    2- Close Top Valve And Bleed Off The Pressure

    3- Open Lubricator L/D Gauge Cutter. Tandem-Programmed (Pressure/Temperature)

   PVT Sampling

   To Take Downhole PVT Sample Flow The Well On 8/64" Run Slick Line And Take Sample

    1- P/U and M/U Downhole PVT Sampler

   2-Test Lubricator With 4000 Psi Graudally

   3-Open Top Valve

    4- R.I.H Sampler To Suaitable Depth

    5- Open The Well On 8/64" And Flow The Well

    6- Take Sample And P.O.O.H

    7-Close Top Valve And Bleed Off Pressure

   8-L/D Sampler

    9- Transfer Sample To Bottle
