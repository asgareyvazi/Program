# Introduction

## North Yaran Exploration Project Background
Based on 3D seismic data, with similar objectives as Azadegan and Yadavaran fields, the Yaran expletory well was drilled during late 2009-mid 2010 to a total depth of 4235 meters into the Fahliyan formation.
The tests of the open hole interval of the Fahliyan formation and short perforated interval in Lower Gadvan, proved presence of light oil, but no commercial flows were established due to low reservoir quality even after acidizing. Inversion seismic data also presents low quality reservoir characteristic over these intervals. Upon perforation of the interval 2782-2791.5 m of Upper Sarvak, the formation produced 15.7 °API oil at the rate of 2260 bbls per day with 620 psig flowing well head pressure through 1/2" choke after acidizing. It is worthy of note that the PVT analyses of the samples taken from this well indicated 18-19 degrees API oil, which conforms with the API gravity of Azadegan oil.
Yaran Oil Field Location Map
MDT pressure measurements indicate Sarvak reservoir pressure to be in the order of 4555 psig at 2765 mMD (2751 mss).
A substantial oil water contact, northerly tilt (over ± 300 meters), has been recorded in Azadegan wells which can be considered in Yaran reservoir evaluation as well. It is to be noted that well YRNN-01 has been drilled towards the north of the accumulation.
Evaluation of other oil bearing reservoirs of the area, such as Tarbur, Kazhdumi, Gadvan, and Fahliyan formations requires detailed analysis of 3D seismic attributes combined with regional subsurface geology that has to be carried out in future. At locality of Yaran well NO.1 and based on the test results, it has been concluded that only the Sarvak formation contains producible hydrocarbon accumulation.

## Initial Clean-up
Note :
Prepare flow head for Flowing. Ensure choke manifold inlet valve are closed. Close flow head master valve, and flow line valves.
Prepare Data Acquisition System. Assign an operator to record this data at 10 second intervals during periods of rapid change and at 30 second intervals when stabilized.  The data will be used to calibrate fluid flow and hydrate formation models
Hold Pre-Flow Safety Meeting to ensure all personal are aware of their roles & responsibilities. There will only be a requirement to wait until daylight hours before bringing hydrocarbons to surface for the first DST.
Observe for indication of well pressure and open choke to allow flow back for indication of cushion flow back.
Building-up of well to be conducted gradually starting from 8/64" choke and progressing at 4/64" choke increments with time allowed for flow to stabilizes
Produced fluids must be monitored constantly for the presence of H2S.
Broadcast testing crew in position, and keep radio communication freely.
Inject Ethylene glycol into data head.
With adjustable choke set on 16/ choke, open the front valve on the choke manifold. Monitor downstream choke pressure, and well head pressures.
Monitor the well closely to spot cushion to reach the surface.
Bean up well in 4/ stages as instructed by the ONSITE REP. Take one sample of the completion fluid prior to test (ask mud engineer).
When produced fluids reach the surface, divert flow to the fire pit until the well cleans up.
When hydrocarbons reach the surface, monitor for H2S and CO2 at the choke manifold at 15 minute intervals until levels have stabilized and at half hour intervals thereafter. Any liquids should be checked for water and chlorides.
Note:
If the concentration of H2S exceeds 50,000 ppm in the wellstream or 50 ppm in air, the test should be terminated immediately and the testing plan reviewed.
Monitor annulus pressure and bleed off pressure if the annulus pressure exceeds 500 psi. Monitor the upstream and downstream pressures across the choke for signs of plugging or cutting out.
Continue flowing until the well is considered cleaned up.
Well is considered cleaned up when the following criteria are satisfied:
Fluctuation in FTHP is less than 10 psi/hr or declining, as a log function of time, and FTHT is constant.
BS&W is less than 2% and stabilized
Producing Oil-Gas Ratio (GOR) is relatively constant
Chlorides in produced water is constant
Divert through the test separator at the earliest opportunity
If contents of gauge tank(s) are combustible, pump contents to fire pit.
ump surge tank contents to fire pit during the first build up period.
t the end of the clean up flow, close the well in at the choke manifold for a period at least equal to the clean up period and as long as advised by the ONSITE REP.

## Initial Build Up
Bleed off annulus pressure to zero, close selective tester valve. Bleed down surface tubing pressure by 300 psi to verify that the tester valve has closed.
Shut in the well at the choke manifold. Pump out the surge tanks to the flare. Bleed down pressure at flow head to minimize chances of hydrates build up during shut in.
Leave the well shut in for a period of 6 hrs. in order to allow the initial reservoir pressure to restore. This however will be confirmed by the POGID representative.
OTS DST operator will watch flow-head and annulus pressure constantly during well build-up.

## Main Flow
Note :

Calculate gas/oil/water rate every 30 minute.
Sample and analyze dead oil density, gas gravity, BS&W every 1 hour.
Sample and analyze CL- , gas composition every 1 hour.
While flowing the well, continuously check all process parameters and do frequent safety tours to ensure all equipment is operated within its design envelope.

# SAFETY DURING TESTING OPERATIONS
The testing operation shall be carried out with special consideration for:

## Flow periods
The first flow of reservoir fluids to surface shall not commence during the hours of darkness.
Second and/or subsequent surface flows may commence at night, if considered operationally safe by the POGID Supervisor.
Recovery waste oil dropping on testing area or drill floor using cotton and trash to waste pit.

## Sour Gas
The presence of H2S shall be detected. If H2S is present, the flow period should allow determination of a stabilized H2S level.  Well shall be shut in when H2S is consistently observed above 10 ppm and the procedures re-evaluated in light of H2S presence.
Windsocks no less three and positioning at testing area.
Nobody permit leave life area during flowing except personnel on duty, all doors, hatches and vents in the life area should be closed.
All personnel on duty should wear SCBA and operate on up wind.
Operator will accompany by other personnel in critical operation.
Fluid must be separated by Mud separator during circulating kill well, and burning out separated gas in time.
Dedicated personnel are to be assigned to tour the testing areas with H2S detectors.

## Personnel Safety
The Drilling supervisor is in overall command of the installation and has primary responsibility for rig and personnel safety. Prior to each major element of the test, a general safety drill and meeting will be held and will cover:
The planned operation.
the specific risks and hazards of that operation
communications protocols (important for concurrent operations)
Emergency procedures.
Prior to each major operation, the Drilling Supervisor, Well Test Supervisor will visually inspect all surface lines and equipment required for that operation.
All well testing personnel must has been accepted the international authorization agency HSE training and got certification. include as follow:
Well control
Abandonment vessel and escape
Fire fighting
H2S protection
Pressure control
All key personnel (Drilling Supervisor, Well Test Supervisor) shall ensure that they are completely aware of procedures, equipment and their responsibilities during testing.
All shifts shall be briefed on general well testing procedures, the equipment involved and its basic operation by the OTS Well Test Supervisor. The shifts should be walked around the testing equipment for familiarizations.

## Fire Fighting Equipment
The Rig fire fighting systems shall be active in the vicinity of the surface test equipment and key hazardous areas (e.g. diesel and propane tanks, lines and burners). Portable foam extinguishers shall also be available in these areas.
Drill floor： Portable foam extinguishers  4 each
Mud pit：  Portable foam extinguishers  2 each
Separator：Portable foam extinguishers  4 each
C/M ：    Portable foam extinguishers  2 each
The fire fighting team shall be equipped for hydrocarbon fire fighting and equipped with dedicated BA's. Fire fighting drills for the testing area shall be incorporated in the weekly emergency drills prior to, and during, the well test operation.

## ESD's and SSSV
A drawing and location advice of all ESD devices shall be displayed in prominent locations (Drill Floor, Testing Area, Muster/Emergency Response Point’s etc.). A status board on the rig floor will show the status of all valves from the testing choke manifold, Surface Tree and down hole valves at all times during testing operations. It is the responsibility of the Driller, to ensure the status board is updated after every valve operation. The OTS Well Test Supervisor, DST Operators are accountable to the Driller, and should inform him prior to any valve changes
A OTS DST Operator will be present on the rig floor at all times while tools are in the well. The Data Acquisition lab will also be manned at all times.
Prior to the well being brought on-stream the ESD system will be function tested (witnessed and signed off by Drilling Supervisor and Well Testing Supervisor).

A POGID representative shall be present during all well testing operations.
All test operations will be covered by the rig “Permit to Work” system.
Cranes will not be operated in the test area when the well is open to flow.  Limited crane operations will be permitted during shut in periods at the discretion of the  Drilling Supervisor and the POGID Supervisor after discussion with the OTS Well Test Supervisor and should be covered by a PTW.
The cement unit shall be connected to the kill line on the Flow Head and be ready to start pumping kill fluid immediately, if required.
Either the Tool Pusher or the Driller must be on the rig floor at all times, The Assistant Driller is not permitted to act as the relief for the driller during key operations, unless accompanied by the OTS Well Testing Supervisor.
All hazardous areas shall cord off and restricted to essential personnel only.
All doors, hatches and vents in the vicinity of the test equipment will be closed for the duration of the test.
All flammable material (eg: garbage, rags, cardboard, empty pails, etc.) to be picked up and disposed of safely prior to well flow operations (general rig clean-up).
All pressure test areas are to be cordoned-off whilst pressure testing is in progress.
All gas detection equipment must be operational and properly calibrated.
Dedicated personnel are to be assigned to tour the testing areas with gas detectors, and must deploy breathing apparatus if there is either evidence of H2S, or of lack of a breathable atmosphere.
While rigging up the surface test package and ancillary equipment, order should be obtained from the NIOC supervisor in order to manage any equipment interfaces on the wellsite.
