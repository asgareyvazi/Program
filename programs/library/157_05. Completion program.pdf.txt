                               Completion Program

1.  Summary of operation

      •  RIH 9 5/8” CSG scraper.

      •  Scrap packer setting area.

      •   Displace mud with Filtrated Completion Fluid.

      •  POOH CSG scraper to Surf.

      •  R/U TBG running Equipment.

      •  Retrieve wear bushing

      •  RIH single string completion with WRSCS SSV , Sid e P o c ke t M an d re l, Expansion

            joint, SSD, Production packer, landing nipple and 4 ½” x 7” tubing to desired depth.

      •  Set production packer.

      •   Pull Test / Pressure Test Packer.

      •  Land tubing hanger.

      •  Test the Packer Ann. & HGR seals.

      •  Close WRSCSSSV, Cut C/L

      •   Install BPV.

      •  N/D BOP, N/U Xmas Tree.

      •  Test X-mas Tree.

                               Completion Program

2.    Quality, Health, Safety & Environment

      •  Well shall be in stable condition prior to running completions string.

      •  Monitor well for loss/gain over the trip tank while RIH completions string.

      •  Good communication among all parties responsible for well control is essential.

      •  Perform kick drill with focus on the use of kick stand (Kick string assembly) prior to RIH
         completions string.

      •  Pre-job meeting will focus on well control & contingency procedures to be understood by all
          involved parties.

      •  Volumes and well control shall be focused throughout running completions string.

      •    Fill out kick sheet prior to RIH

      •  On a positive indication of a kick such as unexpected pit-gain or flow return rate increase, well
           shall be shut-in without performing flow check.

      •  A kick stand shall be readily available at all time on rig floor.

      •  Maintain good housekeeping on the rig floor at all times.

      •   Special care must be taken in running scraper and polish mill to make sure CSG are clear to run
        and Set production packer.

      •  Perform pre-job meeting with all involved personnel at critical stages of the operation.

      •   Sufficient lighting on Wellhead/ Well cellar is essential.

      •   Scaffolding shall be installed on wellhead for ease of operation during X-Tree installation and
            tests.

      •   All personnel working on rig floor, BOP & X-Tree shall be wearing safety harness.

      •  Unnecessary personnel should not be on rig floor while running completion string.

                            Completion Program

2.1. Main Risk Evaluation
   •  Well completion fluid not cleaned prior to running completion string
   •  Losing control line clamps in hole.
   •  Junk falls in the well before or during running completions string.
   •  Over-torque tubing connections.
   •  Leak in DHSV control line when pressure testing.
   •  Damaging DHSV control line with manual slips.
   •  Loosing pressure on DHSV control line while RIH.
   •  Missing to tight all stud bolts on X-Tree bottom section.
   •  Tubing head wear/damage while RIH equipment, due to off centered derrick.
   •  Taking kick/inflow during completions operation (when well is perforated).
   •  Damaging Tubing Hanger while running through riser/BOP.
   •  Unnecessary personnel on rig floor while running completion string.
   •  Use excess amount of Dope on each connection, Use Improper Tong Dices.

2.2. Well Control Case

  Well shall be continuously monitored during running completions string in hole. This  is the
   responsibility of driller, tool pusher and company man to prepare a trip sheet and monitor the
   well during RIH.

  There should be two sets of tubing safety x-overs and 2 FOSV on rig floor.
   In case of observing well under minor flow (If Applicable), The scenario will be as per following:
        Monitor the well
        Close Annular BOP, increase Hyd. oil pressure of BOP to 1,000 psi.
       P/U safety crossover and M/U to string.
        Monitor the condition, if annular BOP does not hold the pressure, open Ann. and
         POOH 1 jt. string, close variable ram on tubing body.
        Close FOSV and monitor for any leakage.
        Discuss the situation with town for further action.

  Perform a well control drill prior to RIH completion string and after each shift change (rig crew
   or completion team shift change). Make sure everyone knows his duties, nominate crew for
  each position such as observer, control line cutter & closing the BOP, etc.

          3. Install control line Fitting (7/16 HIF) to DHSV.
