1.   Introduction

 This program covers the information and procedures required for workover operation

and Re-Entry plan for well F13-05 in Foroozan Field-Persian Gulf-IRAN.


Problem

  The well was shut in since 1976 due to low potential of oil production. The attempts to

produce from Burgan-A reservoir were unsuccessful, and well could not flow for

unknown reason, although mechanical or drilling problem is suspected.


Main Objective:

  The main purpose of drilling well F-13-05-Re1H is to effectively produce Oil from

Burgan-A reservoir in the South East part of the F-3 area in Foroozan oil field by drilling

about 806mMD horizontal leg in Burgan-A reservoir. 7" liner will be set at L.P and 4 ½”

wire wrap screen is planned to be run in 6 1/8" open hole prior to completion.

Important Note:

If E.C.P tools provided before reaching to L.P depth, 8 ½” section is going to be

continued as far as possible up to T.D. The well will be completed afterward with 5”

Screen during running 7”LNR by E.C.P.

3. Drilling History

   producer in a time interval starting from 3rd Jan 1975 (13th Day 1353) and ending

    of 2960.7 MD/RT.


   26” section:

   26” hole was drilled from 87m (30" shoe) to 239.2 MD/RT with sea water. 20"

   (94 PPF) casing was run to 234 MD/RT and cemented. Well was temporarily
   abandoned and rig was skidded over well F13-04 on 9th Jan 1975.


   17 ½” section:

    drilling 17 1/2" vertically to 365.7 (KOP) MD/RT, commenced Directional

4. Work Over History

      1st work over operation on this well was performed on January 1977 to convert

   well into a Burgan "A" oil producer by closing Yamama and perforating new
    intervals in Burgan-A  . 2nd workover operation was carried out on Feb 1977

   which new  perforation job was performed  in Burgan "A" above previous
    perforation interval. 3rd workover operation was done on Feb 1977 by Bahram rig

    (Acidizing Job).

   1st Work over history:

   Work over operation was started by Bahram rig on the 22nd Jan 1977 (2nd of

   Bahman 1355) due to low potential of oil production. Well was to be perforated

    in new production intervals as drilling program & converted well from Yamama

    to Burgan "A" producer. The operation commenced by killing the well, X-Mass

   Tree was N/D and BOP was N/U. Pulled out completion string. Set EZSV at 2743

  MD/RT thereafter Squeezed CMT into old perforations. Perforation job was

   performed at the intervals of 2391.6-2392.2 MD/RT with 4SPF. Injectivity test
   was carried out. Set 2nd EZSV plug at 2389.5 MD/RT & squeezed cement into

    perforation. Displaced well with seawater afterwards BOP was N/D and X-tree
   was N/U. released rig on 27th Jan 1977.

   2nd Work over history:

   Work over operation was started by Bahram rig on the 6th Feb 1977 due to low

    potential of oil production. Well was to be perforated in new production intervals

    as  drilling program in Burgan "A" producer. The operation commenced by

    securing the  well, X-Mass Tree was N/D and BOP was N/U. Pulled out

   completion string. Tagged bottom at 2393.4m, Perforation jobs were performed at

    the intervals of 2379.7-2385.8, 2357.2-2359.6, 2350.2-2352.6 MD/RT with 4SPF.

   Ran dual completion string to 2314 MD/RT (end of TBG) and set PKR at 97.6m

    afterwards BOP was N/D and X-tree was N/U. flowed well (4-6 BPH flowing) &
    released rig on 8th Feb 1977.

17.  Well Securing Procedure

1.  Skid Mast from F13-00 and centre on well F13-05.

2.  Record & report RKB to MSL and THF of new well.

3. Hook up kill lines to Ann & TBG. P/T same.

4.  Record wellhead pressures (If any, bleed off pressure).

5.  Bull head well with 50 bbls Hi-vis pill followed by sea water by pumping down

   through 3- 1/2" and 2-7/8" tubings, observe well.



Note
  66 PCF Mud  will impose ±220 psi over pressure to the Burgan-A

      Reservoir @ top of perforations (-2050mTVDss).

  Pump Hi Vis pill ahead of sea water in order to sweep and squeeze

      remained oil and gas in to formation.

  Adjust MW with respect to the hole condition.

  Weight up material (Salt & Limestone) should be ready on board for

       possible need to balance mud.



6.  Circulate through long string & get return from short string, balance MW and

   flow check, Make sure well is static, or under loss condition.



Note:
   Circulate across well head to fill the well during operation.
