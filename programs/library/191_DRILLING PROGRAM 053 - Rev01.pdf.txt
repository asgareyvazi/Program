   1 INTRODUCTION

      1.1   History

           The SAADAT ABAD oil-field is located in 90 Km. south west of Shiraz city and 11

          Km. west of Sarvestan city. This oil field was discovered by drilling the first well at

           1973 and existence of hydrocarbon is proved in Sarvak Formations. This project

             including drilling and completion of 2 new vertical wells and re-entry one existing

              well.

      1.2   Introduction

     This program covers the drilling of Production / Vertical Well in SAADAT ABAD oil field,
      Iran. The well is intended to produce oil from Sarvak Reservoir.  The well will be drilled
      vertically to a planned total vertical depth of 2882m. Run liner and perform perforation as
     program, the well will be completed with Run completion string.

   4  WELL OBJECTIVES

          According to client’s preliminary plan, the well W053N is intended to be drilled and

          cased from 18-5/8” casing to 7” liner and perform perforation and finally completed the

          well

         DSI/Density/GR and CDR/GR logs will be run from 13 3/8" casing shoe to 18 5/8"

           casing shoe before running of 13 3/8” casing.

          DSI/Density/GR and CDR/GR logs will be run from 9 5/8" casing shoe to 13 3/8"

            casing shoe before running of 9 5/8” casing.

   1 INTRODUCTION

      The Saadat Abad oil field is located in the south of Iran in Fars province, about 60kilometers

       south east of Shiraz. It has a saturated oil reservoir with a primary gascap contained in the

      Ilam and Sarvak Formation. The Saadat Abad field was discovered in 1973 (1352 Iranian

       Calendar) by well SAA-1 which encountered a large oil column in Bangestan reservoirs. A

        further appraisal well SAA-2 was drilled down dip (in northwest of north flank) in 1975

      (1354 Iranian Calendar).

      The Saadat Abad field consists of an asymmetrical anticline with trending NE-SW with a

       dip around 22° on the north flank and around 14° on south flank. Based on the Static

       pressure of Zagros Company, Bangestan Reservoir is expected 4020psi in -1280mss at the

      end of year-97. Reservoir temperature is expected to be 179 Fahrenheit in -1280mss based

      on the resources and statistic notebook report.

    GOC and OWC based on Reservoir Engineering Proposal respectively, -1205, -1495mss,

      seven drilled wells in this field until now.

      The gas oil contact could not be observed in SAA-1 although the G.O.C was reported to be

        at - 1205mss and O.W.C was recorded at -1495mss (ICOFC proposal and SAA-3 data) and

       the hydrocarbon thickness is approximately 411m in Saadat Abad field. In Saadat Abad Oil

        field, sour oil is observed and it contains 0.63% H2S and 1.83% CO2 approximately.

     SAA#1 was drilled to total depth of 2821m in Sarvak fm., SAA-2 was drilled as an

       exploration well about 3.8 Km east of SAA-1 well. SAA-2 was spudded on 9March 1975

      and reached a TD of 3480.90m in November 1975. The main objective was to test the Ilam

      and Sarvak formation. No core was taken from Ilam and Sarvak formations, but total of five

       cores were taken over the Sachun and Gurpi formations. The Sachun and Dariyan formation

      were considered secondary objectives.

      According to the last U.G.C map 1390, Saadat Abad-W053N is situated in the western flank

    A normal stratigraphical sequence in Saadat Abad-W053N is expected from surface

       clay gravel and pebble.

                  - Razak Fm. ሺ86m to 1044mሻ drilled thickness 958m:

       This formation consists of altercation of reddish brown to dark greenish gray silty marl and

     medium to coarse grained sandstone, and medium-hard siltstone with subordinate bands and

      sandy limestone toward the base of this formation with some of light gray to light green and

      sandy limestone.

                  - Asmari Fm. ሺ1044m to 1269mሻ drilled thickness 225m:

       This formation consists of thin bedded cream to buff and yellowish cream, argillaceous,

      marly limestone with subordinate intercalations of dark gray to reddish brown shale (upper

        part), and a thick sequence of well bedded gray to dark gray sandy marl, Nummulitic and

       mottled limestone (lower  part)  is  attributed to the Asmari formation. This formation

      conformably overlays the Jahrum formation and the contacts between these two formations

       are known to be gradational.

                  - Jahrum Fm. ሺ1269m to 1700mሻ drilled thickness 431m:

       This formation consists of relatively thick and massive too, well bedded, buff to whitish

       gray colored crystalline, limestone, partly and partly argillaceous and also consists of buff

        light brown, hard dolomite in the middle and lower part of Jahrum. Jahrum formation

      conformably overlies the Sachun formation there isn’t known sharp litho logical change

      between Jahrum and Asmari formation.

   5  Sample Requirement and Procedure

Sampling from cutting and core, Thin Section preparing during drilling of
Geological Formations

   The maximum sampling rate for non-reservoir and shallow Formation requested one sample
      from each 5 meters intervals.
   From the Formations with lithology of Limestone, Dolomite and Sandstone which are not
       considered as reservoirs, sampling should be carried out such as Reservoir Formations (every
      2 meters).
   From reservoir Formation and nearby casing point and liner point for maximum of each 1
       meters for Thin Section is necessary.

      be entered in Data base in specified forms and charts with standard format.

       considered as reservoirs, one thin section should be carried out from each 2 meters intervals.
