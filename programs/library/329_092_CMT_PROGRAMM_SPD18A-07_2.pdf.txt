Cementing Procedure:
Top Plug and Bottom Plug Method
While running in hole 13 3/8″ casing:
   1-  Clean up tanks and prepare chemicals for mixing fluid.
   2-    Fill tanks with mixing water for mixing fluids (Lead & Tail).
   3-  5 hours before reaching casing shoe start mixing Lead & Tail cement
          additives.
   4-  After casing running, make up Cement Head and load Bottom plug,
         connect cement line.
   5-  Perform a Pre-Job safety Meeting with all personnel involved on the
         operation as well as the client representative.
   6-  Ensure communication between DOS Cementer and Rig floor.
   7-  Test cements line W/ 3000 psi.
   8-  Drop bottom plug and install top plug in the cement head.
   9-  Mix and pump 573 BBLS lead cement slurry @ 12.5 PPG.
   10- Mix and pump 178 BBLS tail cement slurry @ 15.8 PPG.
   11- Release top plug.
   12- Pump 10 BBLS Drill water behind the plug.
   13- Switch to mud pump and start displacement with the mud.
   14- Bump the plug, bleed off pressure and check for float shoe.

Important Notes and Considerations:
    1- Dead Volume has been considerered and Cementer needs to confirm that
      and calculate the chemical concentrations again.
    2- Slurry Volume has been calculated based on 30 % Excess for Lead & 50%
      Excess for Tail on open hole annular volume.

Chemical Description:

1) FLC-DA 9=Fluid Loss Controller
2) CFR-DA 3=Friction Reducer
3)CR-DA 4 =Cement Retarder
4) DF-DA 3 =Defoamer
5) Microblock =Extender
6) Paragas =Gas Tight Additive

Mixing Procedure For Lead Slurry:

To be Prehydrated in Mixing Water in Mud Pit
1) Mixing Water : Drill Water=570 bbls
2) Bentonite= 3835 Ibs
3) FLC-DA 9=Fluid Loss Controller= 1246 Ibs
4) Paragas =Gas Tight Additive=245 Gal
5) CFR-DA 3=Friction Reducer= 384 Ibs
6) CR-DA 4 = Cement Retarder= 230 Ibs
7) DF-DA 3 =Defoamer= 41 Gal
8) Microblock =Extender= 816 Gal

To be Mixed on Cement Unit

9) Dry Cement= 64 MT

Mixing Procedure For Tail Slurry:

To be Prehydrated in Mixing Water in Mud Pit
1) Mixing Water : Sea Water=145 bbls
2) CFR-DA 3=Friction Reducer=420 Ibs
3) CR-DA 4 = Cement Retarder= 442 Ibs
4) DF-DA 3 =Defoamer= 23 Gal


To be Mixed on Cement Unit

5) Dry Cement= 36 MT
