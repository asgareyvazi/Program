Objectives: cementing 18 5/8” Casing

Planned Operations:

   1. Prepare all lines for cmt manifold and L/U to cmt unit.
   2. Load bottom plug in the casing & N/up CMT Head, Open the cmt line on cmt
      manifold.
   3. Hold pre-job safety meeting.
   4. Flush the line with 5 bbls DW, Press tests the line with 3000psi. Bleed-off  press to
      zero.
   5. Mix & pump 245.5 bbl of 12.5 ppg lead cmt slurry solution by cmt unit. Mixing
       fluid required for lead cmt slurry is 189bbl.
   6. Mix & pump 232.5 bbl of 15.8 ppg tail cmt slurry solution by cmt unit. Mixing
       fluid required for tail cmt slurry is 138 bbl, Pump CMT with 6-8 BPM.
   7. Note: Mud engineer to be near cmt unit to take the weight of slurry solution.
   8. Note: Shaker man to be on Jacket and check for any return and inform company
     man.
   9.  Drop top plug. Close cmt line and open displacement line on cmt unit.
   10. Pump 10 bbls sea water by cmt unit.
   11. Switch to rig mud pumps and displace cmt with904.5 bbl (……stk) of sea water
     With 8-10 BPM.
   12. At 890bbls (…….stk) reduce flow rate and bump the plug with 1000psi press.

   13. Note: Mud engineer and pump man should also check visually the volume of sea
      water which will be used for displacement.

   14. Hold press. Monitor the press. Bleed off press and check for back flow.
   15. Disconnect all lines and hoses F/cmt head.
   16. L/D cmt head.
   17. Release starting head running tool as per engineer instruction.
   18. Flush top of wellhead by sea water.
   19. POOH and L/D landing joint and running tool.
