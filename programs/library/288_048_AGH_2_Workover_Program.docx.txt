1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 INTRODUCTION
This program covers work over of well AGH-2, AGHAR field located in south west of IRAN. The well is producing 1.7 MMM3/D gases from KANGAN formation.
This program is prepared according to letter No. 84/CH T B/251 (251/ب ت چ/84) from South Zagros Production Company.
Investigation of well condition shows that there is pressure relation between 7" × 4-1/2" annulus and   production tubing via tubing (the leakage is probably between SSD and NO GO nipple), 9-5/8" × 7" and 13-3/8" × 9-5/8" annulus. This program is design to check and eliminate all of annuluses and casing heads leakage. A remedial plan was arranged as below for work over of this well:
Kill well by 75 pcf none solid mud.
N/D WELL HEAD & N/UP BOP.
Lay down completion string.
REPAIR DAMAGED PART OF COMPLETION STRING and checked 7" casing.
RUN GAS WELL COMPLETION STRING.
disPLACE ANNULUS BY high ph water WITH CORROSION INHIBITOR VIA SSD.
Flowing well on proper choke & record the flowing pressures.

## 2.7 Preparation
It is planned to Kill the well and cure the loss, N/U the BOP and pull out completion string (change destroyed equipment) & cleaned hole then run completion string as program. Flow the well on proper choke sizes, secure the well and finally release the rig.

### 2.7.1 Standards and Preparation of the Operations
Make sure that the whole operation is based on good and safe work over operation practices.
After bullhead well, we require wire line lubricator, BPV plus related running tools, wear bushing and plug tester, 5 7/8" j.mill and necessary X-Over are on board. For more detail, refer to equipment list.
Prior to start operation, hold a safety/Pre-spud meeting with rig crews and service hands to review the work over program and potential problems. Note meetings in tour book. Ensure that gas monitors are set up. Ensure that all safety devices are properly installed and functioning.
Note: Before spud the rig follow pre-Spud check list.
Note: Before spud the rig make sure the suitable adaptor for nipple up the BOP (11"-10000) above the 11"×11"-10000 Tubing head spool are available on rig site.
2.7.2    Pre-Running Checklist
Pre Completion Meeting to be held with all Rig Personnel.
The formation could cause mud losses at any time and a small drop in mud level could cause rapid flow of gas or setting the packer due to differential pressure in wrong place. So the hole must be monitored continuously. Mud volume displaced must be checked against the completion displacement and accurate trip records must be kept at all times.
Although there is positive plug in string while running in, but the correct crossover from tubing thread (variety size as completion string) to 3-1/2” IF drill pipe must be available on the rig floor at all times when handling tubing. Also a safety valve are required on the rig floor all time (5” pup joint VAM or x-over sub 5” N.VAM pin x 3-1/2" IF box & 4-1/2” N.VAM pin x 3-1/2" IF box excess on rig floor must be exist). If flow is detected, these must be installed immediately and the well must be shut in.
Special precautions need to be taken when running the control line outside tubing (due to difficulty in sealing with the annular preventor) and when running wire line. These situations must be discussed by the crew & all responsible personnel to commence running the completion.

## 2.8 Completion Running Procedure
This program assumes that the packer and tail pipe are run with the rest of the completion.
Make up and test pre-assemblies (in workshop as per manufacturer’s procedures).
All equipment to be checked for any transport damage.
All OD’s and ID'S to be checked against the checklist sheets sent from the workshop. All assemblies and tubing to be drifted with respective sizes prior to picking up to the rig floor. It is recommended that after testing, plugs and other flow control accessories are installed in the respective flow control nipples to ensure that the plugs can and will be installed properly down hole.
Prior to running the completion, RIH with 7” casing scraper to 3560 m.
Install the AF (2.562'') plug into the top No-Go nipple, then the prong and pressure test to 6500 psi. Leave the plug and prong set.(plug in SSD CMD 2.750 & test string against this plug)
Check well control precautions (see top of page) are in place. Run completion as per Completion Diagram. Tail pipe length = approx. 22.81 m.
Note that the torque requirements for the 4 ½” string (12.6 lb/ft tubing, 12.6 lb/ft crossovers and completion equipment) and the 5” string (18 lb/ft tubing and completion equipment) are:
Torque figures: 4-1/2” 13.5 lb/ft New Vam 75-80-85 KSI
Min. 3780 ft/lbs; opt. 4200 ft/lbs; Max. 4620 ft/lbs
Torque figures: 5” 18 lb/ft New Vam 75-80-85 KSI
Min 5220 ft/lbs; Opt. 5790 ft/lbs; Max. 6360 ft/lbs
Torque figures: 3-1/2” 9.2 lb/ft New Vam 75-80-85 KSI
Min 2610 ft/lbs; Opt. 2900 ft/lbs; Max. 3190 ft/lbs
Also note that the correct API modified thread lubricant must be used. Do not use any more thread lubricant than necessary, the completion eng. should demonstrate correct use of thread lubricant (using paint brush, not dope brush and applying to pin and only the shoulder of the box).
Make up 7” packer assembly and set the assembly in the slips.
Remove shear screws, and install the packer test clamp (segments).
NOTE: A dog collar clamp or safety clamp should be used at all times until there is sufficient weight on the slips to ensure that the tubing string is held securely in the rotary table during each connection.
Fill assembly with 75 PCF none solid mud.
Pressure test assembly to 6,500 psi for 15 minutes.
Bleed off pressure slowly.
Dismantle surface test fittings and bull plug and install shear screws.
Clear the rig floor and make ready to run the rest of the 7” x 4 1/2” completion string.
Run the packer slowly through the BOP’s. Fill up the first 10 joints with Hi-Vis pill and the rest with 75 pcf none solid mud to make sure that no debris pumped in. Once the packer is in the casing, do not run the packer faster than 30 foot joint -from slips out to slips in - per 4 minute.
NOTE: Monitor the trip tank continuously while running completion string plugged. Mud volume displaced must be checked against the completion displacement and accurate trip records must be kept at all times. Fluid level must be monitored continuously. A contractor Toolpusher or supervisor must be on the rig floor at all times and must keep the ICOFC Company Man and Completion Engineer notified of any changes in well status.
RIH adding additional completion equipment and 4''/4 ½” / 5” tubing as per (Completion diagram) Fill the tubing with fresh water with inhibitor every 10 joints.
NOTE: When picking up the model ‘M’ travelling joint, check that shear ring is installed. The travelling joint will be run fully open (20 ft stroke). The string will be suspended with the tubing hanger 10 ft above its final position so that after the packer has been set, the hanger will be lowered to close the travelling joint half way.
Run in hole with the tubing.
Important NOTE: Since the string does not test while running in, using a jam unit to make up as per optimum make up torque and record the value is strongly recommended. Otherwise, special care and precautions should be taken into consideration to make up the tubing properly.
NOTE: Ensure the 5” elevators and slips are on the rig floor and ready, when the 5” cross over is reached. Recheck well control precautions .
Make up the model ‘BA’ safety valve nipple and connect the control line. Pressure test the control line against the dummy already installed to 6,000 psi. Bleed off the pressure to 1,000 psi and maintain this pressure while running in the hole.
NOTE: Extra care must be taken when running the tubing from this point to ensure that:
Well control precautions are in place. Because of the control line outside the tubing, the annular BOP may not seal properly if it is needed.
The control line is not crushed when setting the slips.
The required depth of the safety valve nipple is to be +/- 60 m.
Continue to run in hole until at setting depth clamping or strapping the control line tight to the tubing.
Install first fluted adaptor (Box by Pin) above the upper 5” flow coupling of the SSSV landing nipple, the second above two joints of tubing and the last one above another three joints.
Run last 4 joints of tubing. RIH to the intended packer setting depth of +/- 3523m.  Make up the Pin by Pin cross-over between the last tubing joint and the tubing hanger. Make up the tubing hanger. Make up 5” tubing landing joint(s) to tubing hanger as per MCEVOY procedures. Run the string in to depth where the tubing hanger can be worked on from the rig floor.
Bleed off the pressure in the control line and terminate. Dress off the cut control line and make up to the tubing hanger. It is recommended that a minimum of three wraps around the tubing are used prior to connecting the control line to the tubing hanger. Connect a temporary control line to above the hanger. Pressure test the control line again to 6,500 psi to make sure it’s holding pressure OK. Bleed off the pressure and disconnect the control line.
Run the tubing hanger below the rotary table and run the landing joint through the rotary, stopping with the tubing hanger 10 ft above its final position.
Make up circulating head to the tubing and pressure up on the tubing to set the packer. The packer should set at +/- 1,500 psi. Increase pressure to 2,500 psi and hold and record for 15 minutes. Then increase the pressure on tubing to 5,000 psi for a 30 minutes pressure test (potential acid injection pressure).
Bleed off the pressure from the tubing.
Prior to landing the tubing hanger, drain the stack and jet out to clean the tubing hanger land off point. Run in hole slowly to land the tubing hanger in the tubing head spool and tighten the tie – down screws.
pressure test tubing to 6500 psi for 30 minutes.
Pressure up on the annulus to 3,000 psi for 30 minutes to confirm the packer is set and holding. Ensure the tubing is open at surface when testing the annulus. Some back flow will be seen from the tubing as the pressure increases due to the tubing being compressed. The flow should stop once the pressure has been reached. A strong flow could indicate leaking tubing, requiring remedial action.
Closed top Ram, pressure test tubing hanger to 6500 psi.
Bleed off the pressure. Unscrew and lay out landing joint(s) from tubing hanger.
Refer to item work over operation program.

## 2.9 Milling Procedure
2.9.1 Items to be checked prior to sending tools to the rig
1.	OD, ID and length of all bottom hole assembly items.
2.	Ensure the cylinder is long enough to accept the body of the packer.
3.	Ensure the length from the bottom of the milling shoe to the catch sleeve is the correct length and that it will go all the way through the packer to be milled.
4.	Ensure the assembly on the catch sleeve rotates freely.
WARNING: The spring above the catch sleeve is very strong. Watch your fingers.
5.	Confirm the correct catch sleeve is used.
6.	Tighten the safety sub below the catch sleeve. If this rotates off while milling the packer, then the packer cannot be retrieved in one trip.
2.9.2 Milling the packer
Make up the tool string and run in hole slowly. Care should be exercised when running the tools through the BOPs to ensure that the milling tool does not damage this equipment.
Run in hole at a moderate speed
Slow down as packer setting depth is reached. If the packer setting depth is known, then the Kelly can be made up prior to tagging the packer. Ensure there is at least 10 feet of string up when commencing milling operations. This is to ensure that the tool does not have to be released, to add a single while milling the packer. Once the catch sleeve is released, the tool has to come out of the hole to be redressed.
Tag the packer with the shoe, pull back and space out. Ensure there is at least 10 feet of string up when commencing milling operations.
Prior to stinging into the packer, check up and down weights with the pumps on and off. Also check rotating weights with the pumps on and off. Pump rate should be 2 - 3 Bbls a minute and rotating speed should be 70 - 80 RPM.
Stop rotating and slowly go down to tag the packer with the pumps running. As the stinger enters the packer bore a pressure increase will be seen. Back off on the pumps to the same pressure that was seen, when circulating above the packer. Tag the packer with the milling shoe and place 5,000 lbs of string weight on the packer.
Stop pumps.
Pick up to bring the catch sleeve inside the bore of the packer. Slowly rotate the string 2 times. This is to un-jay the catch sleeve and set it in the catch position. Slack-off and place 5,000 lbs of string weight on the packer.
Pick up until an over-pull is seen. Pull 15,000 lbs over string weight to ensure the catch is well seated. If no over-pull is seen after 3 feet of picking up, go back and repeat steps 6, 7 & 8 until an over-pull is seen.
Slack off and place 5,000 lbs of string weight on the packer.
Pick back up until the milling shoe is just above the packer. Do not pick up too far as the string will shorten when the string is turned. This can bring the catch sleeve in contact with the bottom of the packer and may release the catch sleeve.
Start pumps up and bring the pressure up to the same as in step 5 and 6. Start the rotary and build up the RPM to the same as in step 5. Ensure rotary speed and pump pressures are the same as in step 5
Slack off and place 1,000 lbs of string weight on the packer and allow to mill off. Keep doing this and gradually increasing the weight until 5,000 lbs of string weight is on the packer. This should be done over a time frame of 30 minutes. A 7” model FAB packer should take around 4 - 5 hours to mill.
NOTE: Problems may be encountered when milling through the extrusion rings that back up the packing element. These rings may begin to rotate. The pumps may be stopped for 5 minute intervals to try to burn through these rings if they do rotate. A close eye must be kept on the torque at this point to prevent twist off.
Once the packer has fallen, stop the rotary immediately. This is to stop the safety sub from being accidentally being backed out and the catch sleeve releasing. Go down as far as possible. Pick back up as high as possible and circulate at a high rate. Repeat this 3 or 4 times to ensure that the packer is free.
Circulate at a high rate, at least bottoms up before pulling out of the hole.
POOH.
Note: If for some reason the milling tool has to be released from the packer, then following should be followed. Stop pumps and rotary.
Pick up and pull 10,000 lbs over string weight. Rotate the string 10 times to the right. If the tool does not release, increase the pick up to 15,000 lbs over string weight and rotate the string until the tool releases.
POOH.
When the tools reach surface, the Baker Engineer must be on the rig floor to inspect the tools as they come through the rotary table.
