                                         Well: SI-09
                                     Siah Makan Oil Field

Page 1 of 1                                    Procedure No10 SI-009

              WELL SI-009 cement Injection program No 10

     1) RIH W/ C.C.Bit to 1884m (200m above shoe).

     2)  Close the well and perform injection test W/ maximum 2000psi. In case of injectivity above 2

     BPM prepare to inject cement.

     3) Mix and pump 150bbls cement as per program and inject same up to 2034m (50m above 13-

         5/8” shoe) and retain pumping pressure till thick cement (24 Hrs).

        Note:

            •   Inject 160bbls from string then 18bbls from annulus to push down probable

              cement around string.

            •   Strip and move string up / down periodically during WOC.

     4)  Release pressure, open the well. RIH and tag TOC. Circulate and uniform MW @ 149pcf.

     5)  Observe well, POOH to surface.

     6) RIH W/ 12-1/4” Bit (Open Nozzles) and related BHA (without stabilizer) to top of cement.

     7) D/O cement and condition hole to bottom while CBU and uniform MW @ 2300m & 2600m

     & 2900m.

        Note:

            •  In case of loss, Use LCM Pill / Cement plug to cure loss.

            •  Check hole stability with various GPM.

            •  In case of needed repeat cement job.

     8)  In case of possibility, continue Drilling 12 ¼” hole to casing point.

     9) POOH to surface, L/D 8-3/8” Bit.

     10) Next Program will be issued in due time.
