Objectives: Running 7” Liner

    1. After POOH wiper trip BHA, R/up cement manifold on rig floor, inspect same and

      load dart. M/U cement head with lower and upper X-over + safety valve on top+

     pup jt. R/D cement manifold and keep on the cantilever.

    2. R/U 7" ITS handling tools. Provide proper Allen-key to install stop collars.

    3. Be cautious for handling, not to hurt liners while handling.

    4. P/U shoe and m/up to joint # 36 of 29 lb/ft liners, baker lock and torque up same,

     check float operating, fill up and check for drainage.

    5. P/U & M/U float collar, joint # 35 and 34, and landing collar. Use backer lock on

      connections.

    6. M/up all CRA liners with power tong and low RPM. Make sure torque graph is

      acceptable. USE STAB IN GUIDE. If jam unit graph is not ok, do not go for more

      torque, open joint & clean up, check and if OK, make up again.

    7. P/U and M/U joint # 33 and 32. Apply Dope with soft brush only a film on pin (DO

    NOT USE TOO MUCH DOPE ON THREADS).

    8.  Fill up string. Pull string for 20 meter and lower it back. Check for drainage.

    9. RIH with 7” liner 29 lb/ft, L-80 13% CR as per tally. Fill up string every 5 jts liner.

   10. RIH with 7” liner Vam Top P-110 28% CRA as per.

   11. CTR hanger man should prepare 7" liner HGR on catwalk ready to pick up.

   12. Before p/up hanger check remaining liners with pipe tally.

   13. P/U and M/U 7” Liner hanger. Connect Top Drive to String and Circulate liner

     volume (90 bbls). DO NOT EXCEED PRESS MORE THAN 450 PSI. Start pump with

     low stroke. During Circ, keep hanger above RT and check for any leakage.

   14. Record pick up and slack off weight of liner string. No rotation after p/up hanger.

   15. While passing liner hanger from BOP and well head, run slowly and carefully. Also

     be careful while running 7" HGR from 9 5/8" x 10 3/4" X-over at 230 m.

16. RIH on 5 7/8" DP to 9 5/8" shoe at 2639 m with 2 min/stand running speed. Pass

   2.5" Drift from all DP. DO NOT FORGET TO RECOVER DRIFT. Circulate string

   volume (285 bbls). DO NOT EXCEED PRESS MORE THAN 500 PSI. Start pump with

   low stroke. HANGER MAN SHOULD BE ON RIG FLOOR.

17. Fill up every 5 stand. Check the drifts at the end and make sure no drift lost.

18. Call CTR hanger man before 9 5/8” shoe. Record pick up and slack off weight of

    liner string in the shoe with and w/o pump.

19. RIH with Liner into open hole as per DP tally, CTR Hanger man should be on the

   Rig Floor.

   Note:

   ▪  Make DP connection as fast as possible & never keep string stationary while

        in open hole.

   ▪  Move string while filling up in open hole.

20. Wash down if necessary with maximum 500 psi. In case of any tight spot in open

   hole, inform the Co-man.

21. Tag bottom. P/U 2m. Mark the string. Circ string volume w/ max 500psi.

   ▪  While circulation on bottom, reciprocate string 2 m.

   ▪  Record pressure reading in 3 different strokes as per CTR hanger man. Record

     P/U & S/O weight with and w/o pumping.

22. Drop the ball and P/U and M/U CMT Head assembly.

23. Circulate till ball land (circ press not more than 300 psi). During circulation, shoe

   should be in final position, 2m off bottom.

24. Press up string to 1800 psi and set Hanger as per Hanger man. Keep press for

   2 min slack wt and confirm HGR is set. If no indication of hanger setting, increase

   pressure in 200 steps up to 2600 psi as per Hanger man.

25. After setting hanger, increase pressure to 4200 psi to shear ball seat, keep for 2

   min. setting tool should be released while shearing. P/U string max 1.5m and

   confirm running tool is free.

26. Circulate bottoms up, Hold PJSM. R/U cement line, Prepare for cement job and

   prepare for cement job. Pressure test cement line to 5000 psi.
