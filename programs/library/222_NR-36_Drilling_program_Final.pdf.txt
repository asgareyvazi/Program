                     NR-36 Drilling Program


1  Introduction


   1.1 Program Introduction

              This program covers the information and procedures required to drill new well #36
            from a new slot on the NR-14 platform in Nowrooz field, Persian Gulf, Iran. The well
                 is intended to produce oil from Kazhdumi Sand-B reservoir by drilling about 660m
              horizontal drain.
            20” casing will be set at 155m MD, 13 ⅜” casing will be set at 1437 m MD inside
            Jahrum, 9 ⅝” casing will be set at 2994 m MD at top of Kazhdumi Sand-B.
        A 600m 8 1/2” hole section is going to be drilled near horizontal and horizontal
             through Kazhdumi Sand-B to total depth of 3597m MD. Well will be completed with
             Pre-pack screen.
            20” & 13-3/8” & 9-5/8” casings will be set @ 155m & 1437m & 2994m, MD
               respectively.



   1.2 Nowrooz Structure

            Nowrooz Field is located some 90km WNW of Kharg island, some 50km NNW of
            Soroosh Field and some 20km NNW of Aboozar Field. Field NOWROOZ was
             discovered by AGIP in 1966 and the first exploration well NR-1 (originally named
              Palinurus-1) was drilled by AGIP in 1964/65 down flank on Nowrooz structure and
             encountered Kazhdumi Sand-B Reservoir. In 1970 production started with offshore
            equipment and facilities designed to handle 60,000 bbl/d of 22 to 24° API oil. The
           Nowrooz Field  is a NNE-SSW trending, elongated anticline, with dimensions of
             approximately 20km * 5km. The structure has a pronounced southern culmination and
                 is faulted along its crest. A significant crestal collapse is present over the northern
             culmination that has a lower structural relief than the southern one. The faults do not
             extend across the entire structure and reservoir continuity and connectivity is expected
              to be maintained within the oil column resulting in a field wide common OWC and
              pressure regime. The minor displacement along the faults combined with the high sand
             content limit the potential for the development of fault seals. Some faults may seal and
            most faults are expected to form partial flow barriers resulting in tortuous flow paths
              across faults.

               200 bbl hi-vis pill. POOH to surface.
