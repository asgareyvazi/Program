       1 INTRODUCTION

           The Saadat Abad oil field  is located in the south of Iran in Fars province, about 60

             kilometers south east of Shiraz.  It has a saturated oil reservoir with a primary gascap

             contained in the Ilam and Sarvak Formation. The Saadat Abad field was discovered in 1973

            (1352 Iranian Calendar) by well SAA-1 which encountered a large oil column in Bangestan

              reservoirs. A further appraisal well SAA-2 was drilled down dip (in northwest of north

              flank) in 1975 (1354 Iranian Calendar).

           The Saadat Abad field consists of an asymmetrical anticline with trending NE-SW with a

             dip around 22° on the north flank and around 14° on south flank. Based on the Static

             pressure of Zagros Company, Bangestan Reservoir is expected 4020psi in -1280mss in year-

             97. Reservoir temperature is expected to be 179 Fahrenheit in -1280mss based on the

             resources and statistic notebook report.

        GOC and OWC based on Reservoir Engineering Proposal respectively, -1205, -1495mss,

            seven drilled wells in this field until now.

           The gas oil contact could not be observed in SAA-1 although the G.O.C was reported to be

               at -1205mss and O.W.C was recorded at -1495mss (ICOFC proposal and SAA-3 data) and

             the hydrocarbon thickness is approximately 411m in Saadat Abad field. In Saadat Abad Oil

                field, sour oil is observed and it contains 0.63% H2S and 1.83% CO2 approximately.

         SAA#1 was drilled to total depth of 2821m in Sarvak fm., SAA-2 was drilled as an

             exploration well about 3.8 Km east of SAA-1 well. SAA-2 was spudded on 9March 1975

           and reached a TD of 3480.90m in November 1975. The main objective was to test the Ilam

           and Sarvak formation. The Sachun and Dariyan formation were considered secondary

              objectives.

            According to the last U.G.C map 1397, Saadat Abad-E014S is situated in the southeast flank

             of Saadat Abad anticline, about 2914m in the east of well#3 and about 2784m in the

            Southwest of well#4. This well in Plan will be drilled directionally from 2160m as a K.O.P.

              to Total Depth of 3464.28m (+68.3m V.Thickness) inside of Sarvak Formation.

         CBL/VDL& CCL/GR log Formation will be run from 7" liner shoe. CDR/Full suite will be

            run in open hole 5 7/8".

            This well will be completed as Sarvak oil producer through 5 7/8" open hole.

       2 GENERAL FORMATION LITHOLOGY DESCRIPTION (In
           Deviation Hole)

        A normal stratigraphical sequence in Saadat Abad –006 (E014S) is expected from K.O.P.

            (Through Gurpi Fm.) to total depth. In Sarvak Formation is as follows:

                         - Gurpi Fm. (From K.O.P.2160 m to 2897.55 m) drilled thickness + 737.55 m:

            This formation consists of a thick sequence of dark gray to greenish gray calcareous silty

             shale and greenish grey, soft and sticky marl, with subordinate layers of grey to dark

             grayargillaceous and marly limestone.

            Thickness of Gurpi formation is variable in Saadat Abad structure.

                         - Ilam Fm. 2897.55 – 3048.89m.) drilled thickness 151.34 m:

           The top of Ilam formation is marked by a change in lithology from marl in to a dark brown,

           brown to light brown limestone. This formation consists of light gray to grayish brown

               crystalline, fossilliferous and porous limestone and as well as in lower part consists of brown

            dark brown argillaceous limestone and in some part marly limestone.

                         - Sarvak Fm. (3048.89m to TD at 3464.28 m) drilled thickness +415.39m.:

                  It consists of a sequence of white-off white chalky and brownish gray well bedded

              crystalline limestone, partly argillaceous and slightly chalky. The 1/3 basal of Sarvak

             formation contains of a series of dark brown dense argillaceous, sparry limestone, which are

              partly bituminous and partly glauconitic. Sarvak formation divided into 8 zones in Saadat

          Abad field.

            Note:

             In Saadat Abad Oil field, sour oil is observed and it contains 0.63% H2S and 1.83% CO2.

            Bangestan Reservoir is expected 4020psi in -1280mss in year-97.

             Reservoir temperature is expected to be 179Fahrenheit at -1280mss. in year-96.

            Losses are probable in Ilam & Sarvak Fms.

           Gas & Oil in Ilam & Sarvak Fms can be present.

              Structural uncertainties for top Formations estimated +/-50m.

       5  Drilling Hazard

       Drilling through Ilam and Sarvak Formations in this field has been accompanied with
            moderate losses and from Gurpi Formation to T.D. losses is expected.
       Drilling in Shale parts of Gurpi Formation in this field has been accompanied with tight holes
           and stuck pipes; special care is recommended for Mud type and drilling in this formation.
      While drilling in Limestone lithology water flow is predicted, so special care is
           recommended while drilling this section.
       Losses are probable in Ilam & Sarvak Fms.
       In drilling Ilam and Sarvak Fms; it possible to have signs of hydrocarbon, Mud cut and flow.
             Especial care is recommended while drilling these Fms.
       In Saadat Abad Oil field, sour oil is observed and it contains 0.63% H2S and 1.83% CO2.
          Due to the existence of H2S in this field, in this regards, safety regulations should be highly
             considered.
       Structural uncertainties for top Fm. estimated +/- 50m.
      Mud Losses and Gas flow maybe occurred in Ilam and Sarvak Fm.

        *So special care is recommended while drilling this section.
