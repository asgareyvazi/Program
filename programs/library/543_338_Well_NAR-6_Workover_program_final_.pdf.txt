1. Introduction


     1.1    Program Introduction

             This program covers workover of well No.6, of Nar gas field, I.R.Iran. The well is gas
              producer from Dalan formation.

            Problem
            The tubing annulus (9-5/8”x7”) is charged with the gas and pressurized to 105 bar (1522
                 psi). Based on the temperature log readings, extra long seal nipple is the most doubtful
              leakage area.

           Main Objective
           The main objective of this program is to fix the problem and resume gas production
            from the same (Dalan) formation.

             Note: This program has been written with respect to the available information of
             workover request no. ٦٨ / ٥٢٢ / چ ت ب , dated: ٦٨٣١ / ٢١ / ٩١ Zagros oil & gas production Co.

           The main head lines of this program are as follow:
                1.  Prior to rig move, check wellhead for any damage or leakage. Record well head
                   pressures; investigate for communication of all the annuluses.
                2. Move and rig up the drilling rig.
                3.  Connect and pressure test kill, flow and return lines.
                4.  Kill and secure well
                5.  Test all the p-seals of tubing and casing spools and fix the problems at site.
                6. N/D well head and N/U BOP's
                7.  Pull out completion string
                8.  Scrap and clean inside 9-5/8" casing to packer depth.
                9.  Pressure Test DAB Packer and inside 9-5/8" casing using 9 5/8" RTTS
              10. Mill out and recover packer and its tail.(if needed)
              11. Run completion string
              12. N/D BOP's and N/U well head
              13. Flow the well
              14. Secure the well and release rig



              Attentions:

                •  The detailed program is illustrated here after in this document under the
                 head of "Workover Program".

 1.4    Completion history:

      After almost 10 years in 1367, the first work over job was done by Rig Ghadr 17.
    Upon drill out cement plugs, 7" Liner lap was tested to 2000 psi OK. DST tool
    was run to perform a dry test where, no indication of leakage was found in the

     packer was set at 1686.4m.
     Well was completed with a combination of 7" and 5 1/2" tubings with down hole
     equipment as indicated in the attached drawings and rig released @ 1367/06/24.

 Perforations

    The well is producing from 272 m interval in a 6" open hole.


 Problems after work over

     1.45 million cubic meters per day. Well flowin and shut in pressures are 1300 psi

2. Workover Program

                 1. Move workover rig from Nar-19 to the well site of NAR-6, R/U and make it ready
                     for operation, Record rotary table and tubing head spool elevations from sea
                     level. (An overall check on  well head spools, valves, P-Seals and x-mass tree is
                  strongly recommended before rig move. After killing and securing the well, all
                 items must be checked and serviced by well head crew and repaired or replaced if
                 needed.)

                2.  Connect  kill & flow lines to 7-1/16"-5000 x-mas tree side outlets and connect
                   return line to 2-1/16" -5000 side valves of tubing head spool. Test all to 4500 psi
                  pressure.

                3. Record the wellhead pressures; those of tubing, 7" x 9-5/8" annulus, 9-5/8" x 13-
                   3/8" annulus and 13-3/8" x 20" annulus. Release / Bleed off pressures, monitor
                     their build up and investigate for the possible communications.

             Note: Take enough care to consider the collapse and burst pressures of pipes
             while investigating the communications.

                4.  Rig up W/L and test to 4500 psi.Test control line to 4500 psi. Retrieve 3SV.
             Pump oil into the control line and install Dummy and 2.750 XN plug in the landing
                  nipple of safety valve @ 70m and test to 4500 psi.
                5.  Bleed off the tubing pressure to 500 psi. Observe and record changes in tubing
               and annulus pressures. Retrieve 2.750 XN plug. (Dummy still in the place). R/D
                  wire line.
                6. Pump fresh water (at least 1 1/2 volume of the tubing capacity (200 bbls) and
               squeeze the tubing content into the reservoir. The well will go under vacuum
                  since the  full column of fresh water imposes 1173 psi over pressure to the
                   reservoir @ 7" liner shoe, top of open hole (2000m). If couldn't squeeze, lubricate
                  the well, and observe to make sure about well condition.

             Note: Monitor the pressures of annuluses all the time.

                7.  Rig up wireline and test same to max 4500psi pressure. Install plug «OTIS-RN
                 4.313»in no go nipple @1683m. (Under Baker "DAB" packer @ ±1670 m) .Test
                 plug and tubings to max 4500psi.Install «OTIS – XN 4.560"» into extra long seal
                bore receptacle. Test same to max 4500psi pressure. Monitor tubing annulus
                 pressure changes while testing inside tubing.

                8.  Release 7" x 9-5/8" annulus pressure through the burning line. If charged with
                   kicking gas, lubricate same using fresh water. Observe and Make sure about well
                   condition. Pressurise tubing annulus to max 2000 psi and check  it for any
                 pressure drop and any return from inside tubing.

                9.  While Circulating cross well head, install «RN 6.250"» two way back pressure
                  valve into 10 3/4" x7" BDS (Box up and down) "GRAY" tubing hanger and test to
                4500psi pressure.

Note: If B.P.V failed against pressure test or could not be set in tubing hanger,
it is recommended to install 2.750" plug into dummy of 3SV landing nipple
prior to L/D well head.

Note: In case of found any problem or failure in tubing hanger, Decision will be
made to change tubing head spool. In such a case a complementary program
will be issued at due time.

10. While circulating cross tubing head spool, L/D well head, what is above tubing
   head spool (13 5/8"x11" -5000). Check top side threads of the tubing hanger.
   N/U 13-5/8" BOP's stack; test same to 4500psi against two-way. Retrieve 6.250"
  RN two way back pressure valve from tubing hanger and 4.560" XN plug from
    extra long seal bore receptacle.

Note: Pre plan to provide 11"x13-5/8"-5000 adaptor flange to connect BOP's to
tubing head spool.

11. While circulating cross tubing head spool, make up the landing joint on the top
   connection of tubing hanger and test tubing to 4000 psi. Unscrew the tie down
   screws.
12. Sting out extra long seal bore receptacle Baker "HR" and circulate fresh water to
   clean hole. Check hole for any loss/flow. Fresh water imposes 782 psi over
    reservoir pressure @ 1670m, packer depth.

13. While  circulating  cross  well  head,  pull  out  completion  string;  check  all
   components for any damage to be fixed or replaced by new one.

14. RIH with appropriate tool and retrieve the mandrel of retrievable seal bore. UN-J
   anchor seal unit from "DAB" packer.

15. RIH with 8 3/8" E-mill and clean 9-5/8" casing to top of packer @ 1670m.

16. Pressure test 9 5/8" casing to 2000 psi.

Note:
It might be required to check 9 5/8" casing for possible leakages or failures. To
do this, after running 9 5/8" scraper to top of packer RIH with 9-5/8" RTTS, set
same @ 1655m (15 m above packer). Test packer to 2000 psi, then test inside
9-5/8" casing to max 2000 psi for any leakage. Repeat pressuring the casing till
find the weak point (if any). Meanwhile monitor 9-5/8"x13-3/8" annulus.

17. Prepare 55 PCF oil emulsion mud and displace same in the well. 55 pcf mud
   imposes 500 psi over pressure to the gas below packer at depth 1670m.

18. Plan to recover Baker "A" retrievable "DAB" packer with appropriate retrieving
    tools and pull out the same.

19.  If could not retrieve the packer RIH with 8 1/2" junk mill and mill out the packer
       till it falls down. RIH with taper tap, catch and engage the remainings of packer.
  POH and L/D fish.

20. RIH with 5 7/8" junk  mill and junk sub to TD, (approximate depth: 2272m)
    circulate and retrieve junks (if any).

21. RIH with 8 1/2" bit and 9 5/8" scraper to the liner lap and scrap the new packer
    setting depth @+/- 1650m ( at least two times).

22. Run completion string as per attached tally.

Note: There might be minor changes in the completion tally items due to stock
availability, and  engineering  considerations.  (A  complementary  running
program will be issued in due time).

23. Pressure test tubing annulus to 2000psi and inside tubing to 4500psi.

24. Shall all pressure tests are OK, set two-way check valve in tubing hanger and
    test same to 4500psi.

25. Nipple down BOP's and nipple up well head and test same to 5000 psi.

26. Remove two way check valve. R/U wireline equipment, test same to 4500psi.
   Remove all the plugs and retrievables from the completion string. Reinstall
  dummy in the SSSV landing nipple.

27. Displace annulus by completion fluid (gas oil + corrosion inhibitor) and inside
    tubing by gas oil prior to latch into packer. (This might be achieved in step 21, in
   completion program)

Note: Full column of gasoil imposes 405 psi over pressure to the reservoir at
1650m.  It is recommended to inject a volume of gasoil with high rate into
reservoir through tubing, then stop injection and let the gasoil to loss into
reservoir making an under balanced column  of gasoil against reservoir
pressure to cause the well flowing gradually.
Pre-plan to flow the well using nitrogen and coiled tubing.

28. Rig up coiled tubing equipment and connect nitrogen vessel to same. Run in
    tubing with coiled tubing and gas lift the well and let it flow on different choke
    sizes, clean the well and record flowing pressures after pure gas reaches to the
    surface.
29. Remove dummy and flash control line. Install 3SV and check its open/close
    position. Close 3SV.

30. Release TBG pressure above 3SV, test same against well pressure. Fill up same
    with packer fluid.

31. Secure well, L/D pipes, clean cellar and release the rig.
