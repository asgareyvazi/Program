                                 Well SI-009 NEXT PROGRAM

                 Isolate flow zone with Barite Plug and cement injection below 20" shoe

        1)  RIH W/ OEDP to bottom @ 1139m

        2)  Circulate and balance mud weight @100 pcf to kill the well.

           Note:

               •  Add LCM inside mud system to cure loss during killing the well

        3)  Circulate with 1000 GPM, 750 GPM,500 GPM, and record the losses.

        4)  Observe well and check static loss for 20 min.

           Note:

               •  Case of observe loss, cure losses by pumping LCM pill.

        5)  Spot 320 bbl Barite Plug (slurry density: 150 pcf) as Below:

       ➢  Mix and pump Barite slurry with drill pipe as close to bottom as possible.

                Note:

               •  DSV & Mud engineer should study attached Barite Plug program and barite

                   plug weight should be checked by DSV before pumping inside well.

               •  Number and type of additive should be mixed under DSV supervision.

               •  The barite plug should be pumped and displaced at a rate higher than the kick

                     rate.

       ➢  Displace slurry with mud at the same rate as balance plug (2 bbls short displace)

       ➢  Immediately begin pull out pipe to 10 stand above theoretical top of barite plug to

                 avoid stuck inside barite plug

       ➢  Monitor the annulus

       ➢  Cont. POOH into 20" shoe after verifying the well is dead.

               Note:

               •   to avoid the risk of settling and plugging the drill string DON’T STOP before the

                     slurry has been completely displaced.

        6)  Wait on Barite to settle as per program (Barite settlement time should be check by pilot

             test at wellsite).

        7)  Observed the well to ensure there is no flow.

        8)  Run back in to the hole and tag top of Barite plug (Barite settlement depth).

        9) POOH to 200m.

       10) Spot 150 bbl 100pcf cement slurry. POOH to top of cement, inject 100 bbl of cement with

         minimum pressure and hesitate method.

       11) POOH to Surface.

       12) RIH 17-1/2" bit & wait on cement to get hard.

       13) Drilled out cement.

       14) RIH & continue drilling 17.5" Hole section.
